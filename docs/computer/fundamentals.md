Computer Course: Computer ki Buniyad
Computer kya hota hai, us ke hisse, memory aur internet ki pehli samajh

Ye course kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me computer ke sawal har saal ate hain, aur ye sawal us hisse se ate hain jise aksar students sab se asaan samajh kar chor dete hain. Full form kya hai, RAM aur ROM me kya farq hai, aik GB me kitne MB hote hain, pehli generation me kya istemal hota tha — ye chhoti chhoti baatein hain, lekin paper me yehi number bante hain. Is lesson me hum computer ko bilkul shuru se samjhen ge, jese pehli dafa koi cheez seekhi jati hai. Pehle ye ke computer hai kya, phir us ke hisse, phir memory, phir generations, aur aakhir me internet ki buniyad aur full forms ki wo list jo paper me bar bar dohrai jati hai. Jo student ye lesson pakka kar le, one paper ke computer wale hisse ke aadhe sawal us ke liye muft ke number ban jayen ge.

Computer kya hai — pehle tareef samajh lo

Dekho, sab se pehle ye zehen me bitha lo ke computer aik electronic machine hai jo hum se data leti hai, us par humari di hui hidayaat ke mutabiq kaam karti hai, aur phir humein result deti hai. Data wo kacha maal hai jo hum computer ko dete hain, jese numbers, naam, tasveerein ya awazein. Jab computer is kache data par kaam karke use kaam ki shakal de deta hai, to use information kehte hain. Misaal se samjho. Tum ne 40 students ke number computer ko diye, ye data hai. Computer ne bata diya ke sab se zyada number kis ke hain, kitne pass hue aur kitne fail, ye information hai. Paper me ye sawal bohat ata hai ke data aur information me kya farq hai, aur jawab yahi hai ke data kacha hota hai aur information kaam ki bani hui shakal.

Aik aur baat jo paper me poochi jati hai. Computer apne tor par kuch nahi soch sakta. Us ka apna dimagh nahi hota, wo sirf wahi karta hai jo use kaha jaye. Isi liye kehte hain ke computer ghalti nahi karta, ghalti hamesha use hidayaat dene wale insan ki hoti hai. Aur computer ki teen khaasiyat har paper me ghumti hain. Speed, yani wo bohat tez kaam karta hai, aik second me lakhon hisaab kar leta hai. Accuracy, yani us ka jawab durust hota hai. Aur storage, yani wo bohat sara data yaad rakh sakta hai aur saalon baad bhi waisa ka waisa nikaal deta hai.

Computer ke hisse — input devices

Ab computer ke hisson ko samjho. Computer ka kaam teen marhalon me hota hai. Pehle data andar jata hai, phir us par kaam hota hai, phir result bahar ata hai. Jo cheezein data ko computer ke andar bhejne ke kaam ati hain, unhein input devices kehte hain. Sab se mashhoor input device keyboard hai, jis se hum likhte hain. Mouse dusri bari input device hai, jis se hum screen par ishara chalate hain aur cheezein chunte hain. Scanner kagaz par bani tasveer ya likhai ko computer ke andar digital shakal me bhejta hai. Microphone awaz ko andar bhejta hai, aur webcam chehra dikhane ke kaam ata hai. Paper me aksar ye ghumaya jata hai ke printer input hai ya output, to yaad rakho, printer result bahar nikalta hai, is liye wo output hai, input nahi.

Output devices — result kahan se ata hai

Jo cheezein computer ke result ko humein dikhane ya sunane ke kaam ati hain, unhein output devices kehte hain. Monitor sab se bari output device hai, jo kuch hum karte hain wo screen par dikhata hai. Printer kagaz par result chhap kar deta hai, is chhapi hui shakal ko hard copy kehte hain, jabke screen par nazar ane wali shakal soft copy kehlati hai. Ye hard copy aur soft copy wala farq bhi paper me ata hai. Speaker aur headphone awaz sunate hain, is liye ye bhi output hain. Aik aham baat aur. Touch screen wali screen aik hi waqt me input bhi hai aur output bhi, kyunke hum use chhoo kar hidayaat bhi dete hain aur result bhi usi par dekhte hain. ATM ki screen aur mobile ki screen is ki behtareen misaalein hain.

CPU — computer ka dimagh

Ab us hisse ki baat jo sab se zyada paper me ata hai. CPU ko computer ka dimagh kaha jata hai. CPU ka pura naam Central Processing Unit hai. Sari processing, yani sara hisaab kitab aur faisle, yahin hote hain. CPU ke andar do aham hisse hote hain. Pehla ALU, yani Arithmetic Logic Unit. Ye hisaab ka kaam karta hai, jama, tafreeq, zarb, taqseem, aur sath me mantaqi faisle bhi, jese bara hai ya chhota, barabar hai ya nahi. Dusra hisa Control Unit hai, jo pure computer ko control karta hai, kis hisse ne kab kya karna hai, is ka nazm o zabt isi ke pas hota hai. Paper me ye sawal seedha ata hai ke hisaab kitab computer ke kis hisse me hota hai, aur jawab hamesha ALU hai. Aik aur mashhoor sawal ye hai ke computer ka dimagh kisay kehte hain, jawab CPU hai.

Hardware aur software ka farq

Ab aik buniyadi farq jo har paper me kisi na kisi shakal me ata hai. Computer ki wo cheezein jinhein hum chhoo sakte hain, dekh sakte hain aur utha sakte hain, unhein hardware kehte hain. Keyboard, mouse, monitor, printer, CPU ka dabba, ye sab hardware hain. Is ke baraks software wo program hote hain jo nazar to ate hain lekin chhooye nahi ja sakte. Jese Windows, MS Word, koi game ya mobile ki app. Asaan misaal ye hai ke hardware computer ka jism hai aur software us ki jaan hai. Jism baghair jaan ke bekaar hai aur jaan baghair jism ke kahin reh nahi sakti. Computer bhi aisa hi hai. Sirf hardware khareed lene se computer nahi chalta, use chalane ke liye software chahiye. Paper me ye sawal is shakal me bhi ata hai ke in me se konsi cheez software hai, aur options me Windows dekh kar pehchan lena ke yahi jawab hai.

System software aur application software

Software ki do bari qismein hain, aur in ka farq paper ka pasandida sawal hai. Pehli qism system software hai. Is ka sab se bara naam Operating System hai, jisay mukhtasar OS kehte hain. Operating System wo software hai jo computer ko chalata hai, hardware aur user ke darmiyan pul ka kaam karta hai. Jab hum computer on karte hain to sab se pehle yahi chalta hai, aur jab tak ye na chale, koi aur program nahi chal sakta. Duniya me sab se zyada istemal hone wala operating system Microsoft Windows hai. Us ke ilawa macOS Apple ke computers me ata hai, aur Linux aik muft operating system hai. Mobile me Android aur iOS bhi operating system hi hain. Dusri qism application software hai. Ye wo program hain jo hum apne khaas kaamon ke liye istemal karte hain, jese likhne ke liye MS Word, hisaab ke liye MS Excel, presentation ke liye MS PowerPoint, aur tasveeron ke liye Photoshop. Yaad rakhne ka tareeqa ye hai ke operating system pure computer ke liye hai, aur application software kisi aik kaam ke liye hai.

Memory ki ikaiyan — bit se terabyte tak

Ab wo hisaab jo one paper me har dafa ata hai. Computer har cheez ko numbers me yaad rakhta hai, aur us ki sab se chhoti ikai bit hai. Bit ka matlab aik 0 ya aik 1 hai, bas. Aath bit mil kar aik byte banta hai. Aik byte me aik harf, jese A ya B, store ho sakta hai. Is ke baad ikaiyan bari hoti jati hain, aur yahan paper ka asal jaal hai. Aik KB yani kilobyte me 1024 byte hote hain. Aik MB yani megabyte me 1024 KB hote hain. Aik GB yani gigabyte me 1024 MB hote hain. Aur aik TB yani terabyte me 1024 GB hote hain. Gaur karo, har qadam par 1000 nahi, 1024 hai. Yahi wo baat hai jis par aadhe students phas te hain, kyunke rozmarra ki zindagi me hum kilo ka matlab 1000 samajhte hain, lekin computer ki duniya me ye hisaab 2 ki taqat par chalta hai aur 1024 banta hai. Paper me sawal yun ata hai ke 1 GB me kitne MB hote hain, aur options me 1000 bhi hota hai aur 1024 bhi. Sahi jawab 1024 hai. Tartib yaad rakhne ke liye ye jumla zehen me rakho. Bit sab se chhota, phir Byte, phir KB, MB, GB, aur TB sab se bara.

RAM aur ROM ka farq

Ab memory ki do qismein jinke farq par har paper me sawal banta hai. RAM ka pura naam Random Access Memory hai. Ye computer ki woh memory hai jisme wo cheezein rakhi jati hain jin par is waqt kaam ho raha hai. Jab tum MS Word kholte ho to wo RAM me ata hai, jab game chalti hai to wo bhi RAM me chalti hai. RAM ki sab se aham pehchan ye hai ke ye volatile hoti hai, yani bijli band hote hi is ka sara data mit jata hai. Computer band kiya to RAM khaali. Isi liye hum apna kaam save karte hain taake wo kahin pakki jagah chala jaye. Dusri taraf ROM hai, jis ka pura naam Read Only Memory hai. ROM ka data pakka hota hai, bijli jaye ya computer band ho, ye nahi mitta, is liye ise non volatile kehte hain. Is me computer ko start karne wali buniyadi hidayaat rakhi hoti hain, jo company ki taraf se pehle se likhi hoti hain. Paper me sawal yun ata hai ke konsi memory volatile hai, jawab RAM hai. Aur ye bhi poocha jata hai ke computer start karne ki hidayaat kahan hoti hain, jawab ROM hai. Aik jumle me yaad rakho. RAM kaam ke waqt ki meiz hai, cheezein wahan rakhi jati hain aur kaam khatam to meiz saaf. ROM wo almari hai jisme cheezein pakki pari rehti hain.

Storage devices — data pakka kahan rehta hai

Jab hum file save karte hain to wo RAM se nikal kar storage device me chali jati hai, taake computer band hone ke baad bhi mehfooz rahe. Sab se purani aur aam storage device hard disk hai, jisay HDD kehte hain. Is me ghumne wali plates hoti hain aur is ki gunjaish bari hoti hai, aaj kal 500 GB se lekar kai TB tak milti hai. Is se tez aur nayi technology SSD hai, jis ka pura naam Solid State Drive hai. Is me koi ghumne wala hissa nahi hota, is liye ye hard disk se kai guna tez hoti hai aur computer chutkiyon me on ho jata hai. Paper me ye farq poocha jata hai ke SSD aur HDD me kya farq hai, aur jawab yahi hai ke SSD tez hai aur us me moving parts nahi hote. USB flash drive, jisay aam zubaan me USB ya flash kehte hain, jeb me ane wali chhoti storage hai. CD ki gunjaish takreeban 700 MB hoti hai aur DVD ki 4.7 GB hoti hai. Ye do adad paper me bar bar ate hain, inhein pakka yaad kar lo. CD aur DVD ko optical disc kehte hain kyunke inhein laser ki roshni se parha jata hai.

Computer ki generations — paanch dor

Ab computer ki tareekh ko paanch generations me samjho, aur har generation ki pehchan us ke andar istemal hone wali cheez hai. Pehli generation 1940 se 1956 tak ki hai, is me vacuum tube istemal hote the. Ye computers bohat bare hote the, pure kamre me phail jate the, bohat bijli khate the aur bohat garam hote the. ENIAC is dor ka mashhoor computer hai. Dusri generation 1956 se 1963 tak ki hai, is me transistor aya. Transistor vacuum tube se chhota, sasta aur kam garam hota tha. Teesri generation 1964 se 1971 tak ki hai, is me IC yani Integrated Circuit aya, jis me bohat sare transistor aik chhoti si chip par ban jate the. Chauthi generation 1971 se ab tak samjhi jati hai, is me microprocessor aya, yani pura CPU aik hi chip par a gaya. Aaj ke tamam computers isi generation ke hain. Panchvi generation chal rahi hai aur is ki pehchan Artificial Intelligence yani AI hai, computer ka khud seekhna aur faisle karna. Paper me sawal yun banta hai ke microprocessor kis generation me aya, jawab chauthi hai. Aur vacuum tube pehli generation ki pehchan hai. Tartib yaad rakhne ke liye bas ye silsila zehen me rakho. Pehle tube, phir transistor, phir IC, phir microprocessor, aur ab AI.

Computer ki aqsaam

Computer ki aqsaam do hisabon se poochi jati hain. Kaam ke tareeqe ke hisab se teen qismein hain. Digital computer wo hai jo numbers aur gin gin kar kaam karta hai, humara aam computer yahi hai. Analog computer naap tol se kaam karta hai, jese purana speedometer ya thermometer wali soch. Hybrid computer in dono ka mila jula hota hai, aksar hospitals aur science ke kaamon me istemal hota hai jahan naap bhi chahiye aur hisaab bhi. Paper me ye sawal ata hai ke humara rozmarra ka computer konsi qism hai, jawab digital hai. Dusri taraf size aur taqat ke hisab se sab se upar supercomputer hota hai. Ye duniya ke sab se tez aur mehange computer hote hain, mausam ki peshgoi, science ki bari research aur defence ke kaamon me lagte hain. Pakistan ke paper me ye bhi poocha jata hai ke supercomputer kis kaam ke liye hota hai, to mausam ki peshgoi wala jawab zehen me rakho.

Internet ki buniyad

Ab internet ki wo buniyadi baatein jo one paper ka lazmi hissa hain. Internet duniya bhar ke computers ka aik bohat bara jaal hai, jis me sab aik dusre se jure hue hain. Is jaal ke upar jo service sab se zyada chalti hai use WWW kehte hain, yani World Wide Web. Aksar log internet aur WWW ko aik hi samajhte hain, lekin farq ye hai ke internet pura jaal hai aur WWW us jaal par chalne wali aik service hai. Ye farq paper me ata hai. Web par rakhi hui maloomat ko dekhne ke liye jo program istemal hota hai use browser kehte hain, jese Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox aur Microsoft Edge. Aur maloomat dhundne ke liye jo website kaam ati hai use search engine kehte hain, jese Google, Bing aur Yahoo. Browser aur search engine ka farq bohat poocha jata hai. Browser wo darwaza hai jis se hum internet me dakhil hote hain, aur search engine wo rehnuma hai jo humein cheez dhund kar deta hai.

Isi silsile ki baqi baatein bhi pakki kar lo. Har website ka aik pata hota hai, use URL kehte hain, yani Uniform Resource Locator. Jese kisi ghar ka mukammal pata hota hai, wese hi URL website ka pata hai. Internet se jure har computer ki aik khaas number wali pehchan hoti hai, use IP address kehte hain. Website aur browser ke darmiyan maloomat ke len den ke usool ko HTTP kehte hain. Isi ka mehfooz wala roop HTTPS hai, jis me maloomat band aur mehfooz shakal me safar karti hai. Bank aur shopping wali websites par hamesha HTTPS hota hai, aur browser me aik chhota sa tala nazar ata hai. Paper me ye sawal ata hai ke HTTP aur HTTPS me S ka kya matlab hai, jawab Secure hai. Email electronic khat hai, jo internet ke zariye bheja jata hai, aur har email ka aik khaas address hota hai jis me @ ki alamat lazmi hoti hai. Aur Wi-Fi wo technology hai jo baghair taar ke, hawa ke zariye, computer aur mobile ko internet se jorti hai. Wi-Fi ka pura naam Wireless Fidelity mashhoor hai, ye bhi paper me ata hai.

Virus aur antivirus

Aakhir is hisse me wo baat jo har saal kisi na kisi shakal me ati hai. Computer virus aik aisa chhota program hota hai jo khud ko computer me ghusa kar files ko kharab karta hai, data churata hai ya computer ko sust aur bekaar kar deta hai. Bilkul wese hi jese insan ke jism me virus bemari phailata hai. Ye aam tor par ghair mehfooz websites, nakli software aur ajnabi email attachments se ata hai. Is se bachao ke liye jo software istemal hota hai use antivirus kehte hain, jo virus ko pehchanta hai aur khatam karta hai. Paper me ye bhi poocha jata hai ke virus konsi qism ka software hai, to yaad rakho ye aik nuqsan deh program hai, operating system ya application ki aam fehrist me nahi ata. Aur aik aham usool ye hai ke jo email ya file kisi ajnabi ki taraf se aye, use khole baghair sochna chahiye, kyunke virus ka sab se aam rasta yahi hai.

Full forms ki mustanad list

Ab wo list jo one paper me sab se zyada number deti hai. Full forms ko ratta lagane ke bajaye har aik ko us ke kaam ke sath jor kar yaad karo, phir ye kabhi nahi bhoolen gi. Main board par tartib se likh raha hun.

1. CPU ka pura naam Central Processing Unit hai. Ye computer ka dimagh hai.
2. ALU ka pura naam Arithmetic Logic Unit hai. Ye CPU ka wo hissa hai jahan hisaab hota hai.
3. RAM ka pura naam Random Access Memory hai. Ye volatile memory hai, bijli jate hi saaf.
4. ROM ka pura naam Read Only Memory hai. Ye pakki memory hai, start ki hidayaat yahin hoti hain.
5. USB ka pura naam Universal Serial Bus hai. Flash drive aur dusri cheezein isi port se jurti hain.
6. PDF ka pura naam Portable Document Format hai. Books aur forms ki files aksar isi me hoti hain.
7. WWW ka pura naam World Wide Web hai. Ye internet par chalne wali web service hai.
8. HTTP ka pura naam HyperText Transfer Protocol hai. Websites ka pata is se shuru hota hai.
9. HTTPS me aakhir wala S Secure ka hai, yani mehfooz wala HTTP.
10. HTML ka pura naam HyperText Markup Language hai. Web pages isi zubaan me bante hain.
11. Wi-Fi ka pura naam Wireless Fidelity hai. Baghair taar internet jorne wali technology.
12. LAN ka pura naam Local Area Network hai. Aik hi building ya office ke computers ka jaal.
13. WAN ka pura naam Wide Area Network hai. Shehron aur mulkon tak phaila hua bara jaal, aur internet khud sab se bara WAN hai.
14. GPS ka pura naam Global Positioning System hai. Satellite se jagah maloom karne wala nizam, mobile ke maps isi par chalte hain.
15. URL ka pura naam Uniform Resource Locator hai. Ye har website ka pata hai.
16. OS ka pura naam Operating System hai. Windows isi ki misaal hai.
17. SSD ka pura naam Solid State Drive hai. Hard disk se tez wali nayi storage.
18. HDD ka pura naam Hard Disk Drive hai. Purani aur aam storage device.
19. IP ka pura naam Internet Protocol hai. IP address har computer ki number wali pehchan hai.
20. AI ka pura naam Artificial Intelligence hai. Panchvi generation ki pehchan yahi hai.

Aakhir me aik mashwara zehen me rakho. Computer ke sawal one paper me mushkil nahi hote, bas bareek hote hain. 1000 aur 1024 ka farq, RAM ka volatile hona, browser aur search engine ka farq, ye teen chaar jaal hain jin me paper phasata hai. Inhein pakka kar lo to is hisse ke number tumhare pakke hain.
