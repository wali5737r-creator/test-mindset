MS Excel ka Complete Overview
Excel. Hisaab kitab wali sheet, aur paper ke sab se pakke number

Ye overview kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me computer ke hisse me MS Excel par har saal sawal ate hain, aur mazay ki baat ye hai ke Excel ke sawal taqreeban fix hote hain. Rows kitni hain, columns kitne hain, aakhri column konsa hai, formula kis nishan se shuru hota hai, SUM aur AVERAGE kya karte hain. Ye wo sawal hain jo ratta nahi mangte, bas aik dafa screen ko samajh kar dekh lena mangte hain. Jo student is overview ko pakka kar le, wo Excel ke hisse me number nahi katay ga, kyunke yahan ghalti concept ki nahi hoti, chhoti chhoti facts bhoolne ki hoti hai. Isi liye har cheez pehle samjhai jayegi, phir bataya jayega ke screen par wo milti kahan hai, aur aakhir me wo fix fact diya jayega jo paper me seedha poocha jata hai.

Pehle Excel ko samajh lo

Dekho, sab se pehle Excel ko samajh lo, baqi sab khud asaan ho jayega. Apne mohallay ke karyana store wale ka wo bara register yaad karo jisme wo hisaab likhta hai. Khane bane hote hain, har cheez apne khane me, aur aakhir me jama ghata khud hi ho jata hai jab wo calculator nikalta hai. Excel bas usi register ki electronic shakal hai, farq sirf itna hai ke yahan calculator register ke andar hi chhupa hai.

Excel Microsoft Office ka wo program hai jo hisaab kitab ke liye bana hai. Ise angrezi me spreadsheet kehte hain. Spreadsheet ka matlab hai khanon wali bari sheet jis par number likhe jate hain aur un par hisaab hota hai. School ka result banana ho, daftar ka budget ho, dukaan ka mahana hisaab ho ya mulazimon ki tankhwah ki list, jahan bhi numberon ki list aur un par jama, ghata, ausat nikalna ho, wahan Excel kaam ata hai. Word me hum likhte hain, Excel me hum gin-te hain. Ye aik line zehen me bitha lo, aadha concept yahin clear ho gaya.

Tasveer ka khayal: Aik karyana store ke purane hisaab wale register aur us ke sath wali Excel ki screen ka muqabla. Register ke khane aur Excel ke khane aik jaise rang me dikhaye jayen, aur register ke sath rakha calculator Excel ke andar chhupa dikhaya jaye, taake student ko pehli nazar me samajh aye ke Excel asal me hai kya.

File ki pehchan, extension aur default naam

Jab tum nayi Excel file kholte ho to upar us ka naam Book1 likha ata hai. Book1 Excel ki har nayi khali file ka default naam hai, jab tak tum khud save karke apna naam na de do. Ye chhoti si baat paper me poochi ja chuki hai, is liye isay halka mat lo.

Ab extension ki baat. Extension file ke naam ke aakhir me wo chhota sa hissa hai jo batata hai ke file kis program ki hai, jese naam ke sath lagne wala khandan ka naam. Excel 2003 aur us se pehle ke zamane me Excel file ki extension .xls hoti thi. Phir 2007 me Microsoft ne naya format nikala aur extension .xlsx ho gayi. Is x ka matlab hai ke file andar se naye XML wale format me bani hai. Aaj ke Excel 2016 aur Microsoft 365 me default extension .xlsx hi hai, aur paper me jab Excel ki extension poochi jati hai to sahi jawab .xlsx hi banta hai. Lekin khabardar, kuch purani kitabon aur purane papers me .xls bhi jawab ata raha hai, kyunke wo sawal purane zamane ke hain. Usool yaad rakho, 2007 se pehle .xls, 2007 ke baad .xlsx. Sawal me version ka zikr ho to usi hisab se jawab do, na ho to naya wala yani .xlsx chuno.

Tasveer ka khayal: Do file icon sath sath. Aik par purana label .xls aur neeche 2003 likha ho, dusre par naya label .xlsx aur neeche 2007 ke baad likha ho. Darmiyan me aik teer jo purane se naye ki taraf ishara kare, taake saal wala farq aik nazar me zehen me beth jaye.

Workbook aur Worksheet ka farq

Ab wo farq samjho jis par paper sab se zyada phansata hai. Workbook aur worksheet sunne me aik jaise lagte hain, lekin hain bilkul alag cheezain.

Workbook poori file ko kehte hain. Wapas register wali misaal par ao. Poora register aik workbook hai. Aur worksheet us register ka aik warq hai, aik sheet. Aik workbook ke andar kai worksheets ho sakti hain, jese register me kai warq hote hain. Tum neeche dekho ge to sheeton ke naam tabs ki shakal me nazar ayenge, Sheet1, Sheet2 waghera. Kisi bhi tab par click karo, wo sheet khul jati hai. Nayi sheet jorne ke liye tabs ke sath bane plus wale nishan par click karte hain, aur sheet ka naam badalna ho to tab par double click karke apna naam likh dete hain, jese January ka Hisab. Sheet ke naam ki aik fix hadd bhi hai, sheet ka naam zyada se zyada 31 characters ka ho sakta hai. Ye 31 wala number paper me ata hai, yaad rakho.

Ab asal trap suno. Nayi workbook kholne par andar pehle se kitni sheets hoti hain? Aaj ke Excel 2016 aur 365 me jawab hai aik, sirf aik sheet. Lekin purani kitabein teen likhti hain, kyunke Excel 2010 aur us se pehle default teen sheets hoti thi. Dono jawab apni apni jagah sahi hain, farq version ka hai. Paper me agar sirf ye poocha jaye ke nayi workbook me kitni sheets hoti hain aur options me aik aur teen dono hon, to aaj ke hisab se aik wala jawab durust mana jata hai, lekin agar sawal purane Excel ka zikr kare to teen. Yehi wo nuqta hai jahan aadhe students phans jate hain. Aik aur baat, sheets ki zyada se zyada tadaad ki koi fix hadd nahi, ye computer ki memory par munhasir hai, is liye jab paper poochay ke workbook me kitni sheets ho sakti hain to jawab hota hai jitni memory ijazat de.

Tasveer ka khayal: Aik khula register jiske neeche warqon ke kinare tabs ki shakal me bahar nikle hon, aur un par Sheet1, Sheet2 aur January ka Hisab likha ho. Pure register ke gird aik ghaira bana kar Workbook likha ho aur aik warq ko alag rang me dikha kar Worksheet likha ho, taake poori file aur aik warq ka farq kabhi na bhoole.

Rows, Columns aur Cell

Ab Excel ki asal buniyad par ate hain. Excel ki sheet khanon ka aik bohat bara jaal hai. Jo lakeerain left se right jaati hain, unhe row kehte hain. Row ko qatar bhi samajh lo, jese class me benchon ki qatrain. Rows ki pehchan numberon se hoti hai, sheet ke left kinare par 1, 2, 3, 4 likha hota hai. Aur jo lakeerain upar se neeche khari hain, unhe column kehte hain. Column ki pehchan angrezi hurf se hoti hai, upar A, B, C, D likha hota hai. Z, ke baad AA, AB shuru ho jata hai, bilkul wese hi jese 9 ke baad 10 ata hai.

Ab paper ke sab se mashhoor number suno, aur inhe pakka yaad kar lo. Aaj ke Excel me, yani 2007 ke baad wale har version me, aik worksheet me kul 1,048,576 rows hoti hain. Dus lakh se bhi zyada. Aur columns kul 16,384 hote hain. Aakhri column ka naam XFD hai. Yani sheet ka sab se aakhri khana, sab se aakhri cell, XFD1048576 hai. Ab purana wala hisaab bhi sun lo, kyunke paper dono poochta hai. Excel 2003 me sirf 65,536 rows aur sirf 256 columns hote thay, aur aakhri column IV hota tha. Purani kitabein aaj bhi yehi purane number deti hain, aur kabhi kabhi paper bhi purane number ko sahi option me rakhta hai jab sawal me 2003 ka zikr ho. Baghair version ke poocha jaye to hamesha naya number, 1,048,576 rows aur 16,384 columns, XFD.

Ab cell ki baat. Jahan aik row aur aik column apas me milte hain, wahan jo chhota sa khana banta hai, use cell kehte hain. Cell hi Excel ki sab se chhoti ikai hai, saara data cellon me hi likha jata hai. Har cell ka aik pata hota hai jise cell address kehte hain. Pata banane ka tareeqa ye hai ke pehle column ka harf, phir row ka number. Jese B4 ka matlab hai column B aur row number 4 wala khana. Bilkul ghar ke pate ki tarah, pehle gali ka naam, phir makaan number. Jis cell par tum is waqt kaam kar rahe hote ho, us ke gird kala sa mota ghaira ban jata hai, use active cell kehte hain. Aur do chhoti shortcuts bhi yaad kar lo jo paper me ati hain. Ctrl aur Home aik sath dabane se tum seedha A1 par pohnch jate ho, aur Ctrl aur End dabane se sheet ke us aakhri cell par jahan tak data likha gaya hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Excel ki grid ka aik bara hissa jisme aik row ko neele rang se aur aik column ko hare rang se numayan kiya gaya ho, aur jahan dono milte hain wahan cell B4 ko laal ghairay me dikhaya gaya ho. Sath me aik chhota sa khana jis me naya hisaab 1,048,576 rows, 16,384 columns, XFD, aur us ke neeche purana hisaab 65,536 rows, 256 columns, IV, taake dono number aik hi tasveer me yaad ho jayen.

Name Box aur Formula Bar

Ribbon ke bilkul neeche, sheet ke upar wali patti par do cheezain hain jinhe students aksar nazar andaz kar dete hain, aur paper inhi ko poochta hai.

Left taraf chhota sa dabba hai, use Name Box kehte hain. Name Box tumhe batata hai ke is waqt tum khare kahan ho, yani active cell ka address wahan likha nazar ata hai, jese C7. Aur is ka dusra kaam ye hai ke agar tumhe kisi door ke cell par jana hai to Name Box me us ka address likh kar Enter daba do, tum seedha wahan pohnch jao ge. Naam rakha hua cell ya range ho to use bhi yahin se chuna jata hai.

Name Box ke sath lagi lambi patti Formula Bar hai. Formula Bar ka kaam ye hai ke ye active cell ke andar ki asal cheez dikhati hai. Ye farq samjho, ye bohat aham hai. Farz karo aik cell me tumhe 500 likha nazar araha hai. Ho sakta hai andar asal me 500 na ho, andar aik formula ho jis ka jawab 500 aya ho. Cell tumhe sirf jawab dikhata hai, lekin jab tum us cell ko chunte ho to Formula Bar me wo asal formula nazar ajata hai jo andar chhupa tha. Aur koi bhi data ya formula likhna ya theek karna ho to wo bhi yahin Formula Bar me hota hai. Formula Bar ke left kinare par chhota sa fx ka nishan hota hai, ye Insert Function ka button hai, is se functions ki list khulti hai. Paper me ye sawal ata hai ke cell ka asal content kahan nazar ata hai, jawab hai Formula Bar me.

Tasveer ka khayal: Excel ki screen ke upari hisse ka bara sa manzar jis me Name Box ko aik teer se aur Formula Bar ko dusre teer se numayan kiya gaya ho. Aik cell me 500 ka jawab dikhaya gaya ho aur Formula Bar me usi ka asal formula bara bara likha nazar araha ho, taake cell jawab dikhata hai aur Formula Bar asal cheez, ye farq tasveer se pakka ho jaye.

Data ki teen types

Excel ke cell me jo kuch likha jata hai wo buniyadi tor par teen qism ka hota hai, aur teeno ki pehchan paper me poochi jati hai.

Pehli qism text hai. Naam, sheher, cheezon ke label, jo kuch hisaab ke liye nahi sirf pehchan ke liye likha jaye, wo text hai. Text cell me default tor par left taraf chipka hota hai. Dusri qism number hai. Jo adad hisaab me istemal hona hai wo number hai, aur number cell me default tor par right taraf hota hai. Tareekh bhi andar se number hi hoti hai, is liye wo bhi right taraf jati hai. Teesri aur sab se aham qism formula hai. Formula wo hidayaat hai jo Excel ko batati hai ke hisaab kesay karna hai, aur formula ki pehchan ye hai ke ye hamesha barabar ke nishan yani = se shuru hota hai. = ke baghair Excel tumhari baat ko hisaab nahi samjhta, use aam text samajh kar beth jata hai. Jese =A1+A2 likho ge to Excel A1 aur A2 ki qeematein jama karke jawab cell me dikha dega.

Aik pehchan aur yaad kar lo jo paper ki pasandida hai. Jab formula ka hisaab mumkin na ho to Excel jawab ki jagah aik error dikhata hai. Sab se mashhoor error hai #DIV/0!, is ka matlab hai ke tum ne kisi number ko zero par taqseem karne ki koshish ki hai, jo hisaab me ho hi nahi sakta. Sharp wale nishan se shuru hone wala har error isi khandan ka hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Teen khane sath sath. Pehle me kisi ka naam likha left taraf chipka hua aur upar Text ka label, dusre me number right taraf aur upar Number ka label, teesre me = se shuru hone wala formula aur us ke neeche us ka jawab, upar Formula ka label. Neeche aik chhota khana jis me #DIV/0! laal rang me dikhaya gaya ho.

Mashhoor functions

Function pehle se bane banaye formula ko kehte hain. Jese calculator par pehle se aik button bana hota hai jo dabane se ausat nikal ata hai, tumhe lamba hisaab nahi karna parta. Excel me sainkadon functions hain, lekin paper me chand hi bar bar ate hain. Inhe misaal ke sath samjho. Functions Formulas tab me milte hain, aur Home tab ke bilkul right kinare Editing group me AutoSum ka button bhi yahin ka rasta hai. AutoSum ki keyboard shortcut Alt aur = aik sath hai, ye bhi paper me ati hai.

1. SUM. Jama karne ke liye. =SUM(A1:A5) likhne se A1 se A5 tak ke paanchon number jama ho jate hain. Beech wala : ka nishan "se lekar tak" ka matlab deta hai, ise range kehte hain.
2. AVERAGE. Auzat nikalne ke liye. =AVERAGE(B1:B5) paanchon numberon ki ausat dega. Result card ki ausat yahin se banti hai.
3. MAX. Sab se bari qeemat dhundne ke liye. =MAX(C1:C10) das numberon me sab se bara number dega, jese class me sab se zyada number kis ke aye.
4. MIN. Sab se chhoti qeemat ke liye. =MIN(C1:C10) sab se chhota number dega.
5. COUNT. Sirf number wale khane gin-ne ke liye. =COUNT(D1:D10) batayega ke das khanon me number kitno me hai. Khaas baat ye hai ke COUNT text wale khane nahi ginta. Agar text sameit har bhara khana ginna ho to us ke liye COUNTA istemal hota hai. COUNT aur COUNTA ka ye farq paper ka pasandida phanda hai.
6. IF. Shart wala function. =IF(A1>=50,"Pass","Fail") ka matlab hai ke agar A1 ki qeemat 50 ya zyada hai to jawab Pass likho, warna Fail likho. Ye function faisla karta hai, is liye result aur grade wale kaamon me sab se zyada yehi chalta hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Aik chhoti si marks wali sheet jis ke neeche har function ka aik khana bana ho, SUM ke aage jama ka jawab, AVERAGE ke aage ausat, MAX ke aage sab se bara number ghairay me, MIN ke aage sab se chhota, aur IF ke aage Pass ka label. Har function ke sath us ka asal formula Formula Bar wale andaaz me likha ho.

Cell Reference, relative aur absolute ka farq

Ab aik bareek lekin bohat paper wala concept. Jab tum formula me kisi cell ka address likhte ho, jese =A1+B1, to is address ko cell reference kehte hain. Reference ki do baree qismein hain aur in ka farq sirf aik dollar ke nishan ka hai.

Pehli qism relative reference hai. Aam address jese A1. Is ki aadat ye hai ke jab tum formula ko copy karke dusri jagah le jate ho to ye address nayi jagah ke hisab se khud badal jata hai. Jese tum ne C1 me =A1+B1 likha aur use copy karke C2 me laga diya, to wahan wo khud =A2+B2 ban jayega. Ye asal me sahulat hai, isi liye poori list ka hisaab aik hi formula copy karke ho jata hai.

Dusri qism absolute reference hai. Is me column ya row ke aage dollar ka nishan $ lagate hain, jese $A$1. Dollar ka matlab hai taala. Jis ke aage $ lag gaya wo hissa copy karne par nahi badalta, wahi ka wahi rehta hai. Ye tab kaam ata hai jab aik fix qeemat, jese tax ki sharah ya aik fees, poori list par lagani ho aur tum chahte ho ke formula jahan bhi jaye, usi aik cell ko dekhta rahe. Aik teesri darmiyani shakal bhi hai jisme sirf column ya sirf row par taala lagta hai, use mixed reference kehte hain, jese $A1 ya A$1. Aur is ki shortcut bhi yaad kar lo, formula likhte waqt F4 dabane se reference relative se absolute aur absolute se relative ho jata hai. F4 wali ye baat paper me seedhi poochi jati hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Do raaste wali tasveer. Aik taraf aam address A1 jo copy hone par badal kar A2 banta dikhaya gaya ho, dusri taraf $A$1 jis par aik chhota sa taala bana ho aur copy hone ke baad bhi wo waisa ka waisa dikhaya gaya ho. Darmiyan me F4 wali kunji ka nishan.

Roz ke kaam aur un ke tabs

Ab wo cheezain jo Excel me roz karni parti hain, aur paper me in ka sawal hamesha is shakal me ata hai ke falaan kaam kis tab se hota hai. Har kaam ke sath us ka tab zehen me jor lo.

Chart banana Insert tab ka kaam hai. Data select karo, Insert tab me Charts group se Column, Line ya Pie chart chuno, graph khud ban jata hai. Chart ki aik shortcut bhi hai, data select karke F11 dabane se chart alag sheet par ban jata hai. Data ko tartib dena yani sorting aur chhan-na yani filter lagana Data tab ka kaam hai, Sort and Filter group me. Filter ki shortcut Ctrl aur Shift aur L aik sath hai, is se har column ke upar chhota sa teer ajata hai jis se tum sirf matlooba cheezain dikha sakte ho. Bari sheet me pehli row ko rok lena taake neeche jane par headings ghayab na hon, ye Freeze Panes hai aur ye View tab ke Window group me milta hai. Sirf aik hisse ko print karna ho to pehle wo hissa select karke Page Layout tab ke Page Setup group me Print Area set karte hain. Do khano ko aik karna yani Merge and Center, Home tab ke Alignment group me hai, aur text ko khane ke andar lapaitna yani Wrap Text bhi yahi milta hai. Number ki shakal badalna, currency ka nishan ya feesad ka % lagana, ye Home tab ke Number group ka kaam hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Excel ke ribbon ka aik saaf naqsha jis me Insert, Data, View aur Page Layout tabs par un ke kaamon ki chhoti chhoti nishaniyan lagi hon, Insert par graph ki, Data par chhanni ki, View par barf se jami hui pehli row ki, aur Page Layout par print hone wale hisse ke gird ghairay ki. Student dekh kar hi yaad kar le ke konsa kaam kis tab me chhupa hai.

Zoom aur screen ka aakhri hissa

Aakhri baat screen ke size ki. Sheet ko bara ya chhota karke dekhna zoom kehlata hai. Zoom do jagah se milta hai. Aik View tab ke Zoom group me, jahan tum feesad likh kar bhi zoom set kar sakte ho. Dusra aur asaan tareeqa screen ke bilkul neeche right kone me status bar par bana slider hai, use ghaseet kar zoom badlo. Excel me zoom kam se kam 10 feesad aur zyada se zyada 400 feesad hota hai. Ye 10 se 400 wali hadd bhi fix fact hai. Mouse ke sath Ctrl daba kar wheel ghumane se bhi zoom badalta hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Screen ke neeche wali status bar ka bara manzar jis me right kone ka zoom slider numayan ho, aik taraf 10 feesad par chhoti si sheet aur dusri taraf 400 feesad par aik hi cell bohat bara dikhaya gaya ho.

Fix facts ki recap

Ab aakhir me wo tamam fix facts aik sath dohra lo jo is overview se paper ke liye nikalte hain. Inhe bol bol kar yaad karo.

1. Nayi Excel file ka default naam Book1 hota hai.
2. Excel ki nayi extension .xlsx hai, purani .xls thi, farq 2007 ka hai.
3. Workbook poori file hai, worksheet us ka aik warq hai.
4. Nayi workbook me default aik sheet hoti hai, purane Excel me teen hoti thi.
5. Sheet ke naam ki hadd 31 characters hai.
6. Naye Excel me rows 1,048,576 aur columns 16,384 hain, aakhri column XFD hai aur aakhri cell XFD1048576 hai.
7. Purane Excel 2003 me rows 65,536 aur columns 256 thay, aakhri column IV tha.
8. Cell row aur column ke milap ko kehte hain, address pehle column ka harf phir row ka number hota hai, jese B4.
9. Name Box active cell ka address dikhata hai, Formula Bar cell ka asal content aur formula dikhati hai.
10. Formula hamesha = se shuru hota hai.
11. SUM jama karta hai, AVERAGE ausat, MAX sab se bara, MIN sab se chhota, COUNT sirf number wale khane ginta hai, COUNTA har bhara khana, IF shart par faisla karta hai.
12. Absolute reference me $ ka nishan lagta hai, aur F4 se reference badalta hai.
13. Chart Insert tab se banta hai, sort aur filter Data tab se, Freeze Panes View tab se, Print Area Page Layout tab se.
14. Default font Calibri aur size 11 hai.
15. Zoom 10 feesad se 400 feesad tak hota hai.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Aakhir me aik trick jo numberon ko zehen me baitha degi. Naye Excel ke number is jumle me bandh lo. Rows das lakh se upar, columns solah hazar se upar, aur safar XFD par khatam. Das lakh wali baat 1,048,576 ko, solah hazar wali baat 16,384 ko yaad karayegi, aur XFD sunte hi aakhri column zehen me ajayega. Aur purane ke liye ulta jumla rakho. Purana Excel chhota tha, 65 hazar rows aur 256 columns, safar IV par khatam. Naya bara, purana chhota. Version dekh kar number chuno, ghalti nahi hogi.
