MS PowerPoint ka Complete Overview
PowerPoint. Stage par dikhane wali slides, aur paper ke fix facts

Ye overview kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me computer ke hisse me MS PowerPoint par bhi sawal ate hain, aur PowerPoint ke sawal Excel se bhi zyada fix hote hain. Slideshow kis key se shuru hota hai, nayi file ki extension kya hai, default view konsa hai, transition aur animation me kya farq hai. Ye sawal samajh se zyada pehchan mangte hain. Jo student is overview ko aik dafa screen ki tasveer zehen me bana kar parh le, us ke liye ye sawal muft ke number hain. Har cheez pehle samjhai jayegi, phir bataya jayega ke PowerPoint ki screen par wo milti kahan hai, aur phir wo fix fact milega jo paper me seedha poocha jata hai.

Pehle PowerPoint ko samajh lo

Dekho, sab se pehle PowerPoint ko samajh lo. Farz karo tumhe class ke samne aik mozू par bolna hai. Tum board par sab kuch nahi likh sakte, to tum bare bare kagaz ke takhte bana lete ho. Har takhte par aik bara unwan, chand aham nuqaat, aur aik tasveer. Bolte hue tum takhte badalte jate ho aur log dekhte bhi jate hain, sunte bhi jate hain. PowerPoint bas inhi takhton ki electronic shakal hai.

PowerPoint Microsoft Office ka wo program hai jo presentation banane ke liye hai. Presentation ka matlab hai slides ka wo majmua jo screen ya projector par dikha kar kisi mozू ko samjhaya jaye. Har takhte ko yahan slide kehte hain. Daftar ki report ho, school ka lecture ho, kisi nai cheez ki mashhoori ho, jahan baat ko bare unwanon, tasveeron aur charton ke sath dikhana ho, wahan PowerPoint kaam ata hai. Word me likhte hain, Excel me gin-te hain, aur PowerPoint me dikhate hain. Ye teeno jumle aik sath zehen me rakho, teeno programs ka farq kabhi nahi bhoologe.

Tasveer ka khayal: Aik stage ka manzar jis me bolne wale ke peeche bari screen par slides badal rahi hon, aur sath me kagaz ke takhton wala purana andaaz chhota sa dikhaya gaya ho. Har takhte aur har slide par aik hi unwan aur aik hi tasveer ho, taake student samajh jaye ke slide asal me stage wala takhta hi hai.

File ki pehchan, extension aur default naam

Jab tum nayi PowerPoint file kholte ho to upar us ka naam Presentation1 likha ata hai. Presentation1 har nayi khali presentation ka default naam hai, bilkul wese hi jese Excel me Book1 hota hai. Ye baat paper me poochi jati hai, isay fix fact samajh kar yaad rakho.

Extension ki baat wohi hai jo Excel me seekhi. Extension file ke khandan ka naam hai. PowerPoint 2003 aur us se pehle ke zamane me file ki extension .ppt hoti thi. 2007 me naya format aya aur extension .pptx ho gayi. Aaj ke PowerPoint 2016 aur Microsoft 365 me default extension .pptx hai, aur baghair version ke pooche gaye sawal ka jawab .pptx hi banta hai. Version wala usool yahan bhi wohi hai, 2007 se pehle .ppt, 2007 ke baad .pptx. Aik aur extension bhi zehen me rakho jo kabhi kabhi paper me ati hai. Agar presentation ko .ppsx me save karo to wo show wali file ban jati hai, use kholte hi seedha slideshow shuru ho jata hai, pehle editing wali screen nahi ati.

Tasveer ka khayal: Teen file icon aik line me. Pehle par Presentation1 ka default naam, dusre par purana .ppt 2003 ke label ke sath, teesre par naya .pptx 2007 ke baad ke label ke sath. Aik chhota chautha icon .ppsx ka jis se seedha bari screen wali slide nikalti dikhai de.

Slide, Layout aur Placeholder

Ab presentation ki teen buniyadi cheezain samjho, kyunke paper inhi teenon ke gird ghoomta hai.

Pehli cheez slide hai. Slide presentation ka aik safha hai, aik screen bhar ka hissa. Poori presentation slides ka majmua hai, jese kitaab safhon ka majmua hoti hai. Aik slide par unwan hota hai, nuqaat hote hain, tasveer ya chart hota hai.

Dusri cheez layout hai. Layout slide ke andar cheezon ki tartib ko kehte hain, kis cheez ki jagah kahan hogi. PowerPoint me pehle se bane banaye layouts hote hain, kul 9 mashhoor layouts hain. Sab se pehla Title Slide hai, is me sirf bara unwan aur us ke neeche chhota unwan hota hai, aur har nayi presentation ki pehli slide ka default layout yehi Title Slide hota hai, ye fix fact hai. Dusra sab se zyada istemal hone wala Title and Content hai, upar unwan aur neeche nuqaat ya koi bhi cheez. Is ke ilawa Title Only hai jis me sirf unwan hota hai, Blank hai jo bilkul khali slide hai, aur Two Content, Section Header, Content with Caption jese layouts bhi hain. Nayi slide jorna Home tab ke Slides group me New Slide se hota hai, is ki shortcut Ctrl aur M hai, aur bani hui slide ka layout badalna ho to usi group me Layout ka button hai.

Teesri cheez placeholder hai. Slide par jo khali dabbe nazar ate hain jin ke andar halki si tehreer likhi hoti hai, Click to add title ya Click to add text, unhe placeholder kehte hain. Placeholder ka matlab hai pehle se tay jagah, tum bas wahan click karke apni cheez likh do ya laga do. Ye dabbe batate hain ke unwan yahan aye ga, text yahan aye ga, tasveer yahan lage gi. Paper me placeholder ki tareef is shakal me ati hai ke slide par wo dabba jahan click karke text likha jata hai, use kya kehte hain. Jawab placeholder hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Aik slide ka bara manzar jis me upar wale dabbe par Title wala placeholder aur neeche wale par Text wala placeholder teeron se dikhaya gaya ho, aur dono ke andar Click to add wali halki tehreer nazar arahi ho. Sath me teen chhoti slides, Title Slide, Title and Content aur Blank, taake layouts ka farq aik nazar me samajh aye.

Default view, Normal aur baqi views

Jab tum PowerPoint kholte ho to jo screen samne ati hai use Normal view kehte hain, aur yehi PowerPoint ka default view hai. Ye fix fact paper me bar bar ata hai. Normal view ke teen hisse hote hain aur teeno ko pehchan lo. Left taraf aik tung si patti hai jis me tamam slides chhoti chhoti shaklon me nazar ati hain, ise Slides pane kehte hain, yahan se tum kisi bhi slide par aik click me ja sakte ho. Darmiyan me bari jagah hai jahan mojooda slide bara dikhai deta hai, yahi asal kaam ki jagah hai. Aur neeche aik chhoti si patti hai jahan tum us slide ke notes likh sakte ho, notes wali baat aage tafseel se ayegi.

Views sirf Normal nahi hain. View tab ke Presentation Views group me saare views milte hain. Slide Sorter view me tamam slides chhoti shaklon me aik sath nazar ati hain, ye slides ki tartib badalne aur aik nazar me poori presentation dekhne ke liye sab se behtreen hai. Outline View me sirf unwan aur nuqaat tehreer ki shakal me ate hain. Reading View me presentation window ke andar chalti hai lekin editing wale button ghayab ho jate hain. Paper me aksar poocha jata hai ke slides ko chhoti shaklon me aik sath dekhne ke liye konsa view hai, jawab Slide Sorter hai. Aur default view poocha jaye to jawab hamesha Normal hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Normal view ki poori screen ka naqsha jis me left wali Slides pane, darmiyan wali bari slide aur neeche wali notes patti, teeno par un ke naam ke label lage hon. Neeche aik chhoti patti me Slide Sorter ka manzar jisme bohat si chhoti slides aik sath dikhai de rahi hon.

Tabs ka khulasa

Ab ribbon ke bare tabs ka aik khulasa zehen me rakho, taake har kaam ki jagah yaad rahe.

Home tab sab se zyada istemal hota hai. Isi me Slides group hai jahan se New Slide, Layout aur Reset milta hai, isi me Font aur Paragraph groups hain jahan se likhai ka size, rang aur tartib badalti hai. Insert tab se slide ke andar cheezain jori jati hain, tasveer, table, chart, shapes aur video waghera. Design tab poori presentation ki shakal ka tab hai, isi me Themes milte hain jin se aik click me tamam slides ka rang, font aur andaaz badal jata hai, aur isi tab me aage ayegi slide size wali baat. Is ke baad Transitions aur Animations tabs hain jin ka farq agle hisse me tafseel se ayega kyunke paper ka sab se bara phanda wahi hai. Slide Show tab se show ki settings hoti hain, aur View tab se views aur master slide milta hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: PowerPoint ke ribbon ka saaf naqsha jis me Home, Insert aur Design tabs par un ki pehchan wali chhoti nishaniyan lagi hon, Home par slide ki, Insert par tasveer ki, Design par rangon ki pallete ki. Har tab ke neeche aik line me us ka kaam.

Transitions aur Animations ka farq

Ab is overview ka sab se aham hissa, kyunke PPSC ka paper yahin sab se zyada phansata hai. Transition aur animation sunne me aik jaise lagte hain, dono me harkat hai, lekin dono ka maqam bilkul alag hai. Is farq ko aik misaal se pakka kar lo.

Farz karo tum aik theatre me bethe ho. Aik manzar khatam hota hai aur parda girta hai, phir parda uthta hai aur naya manzar samne ata hai. Parda girne aur uthne ka ye andaaz transition hai. Aur manzar ke andar jo adakar chal raha hai, nach raha hai, uchhal raha hai, wo animation hai. Bas yehi farq hai. Transition slide badalne par lagta hai, animation slide ke andar ki cheezon par lagta hai.

Tafseel se samjho. Transition aik slide ke khatam hone aur dusri slide ke ane ke darmiyan ka effect hai. Ye poori slide par aik sath lagta hai. Ise lagane ke liye Transitions tab me jate hain. Mashhoor transitions me Fade hai jis me slide halki si ghul kar ati hai, Push hai jis me nai slide purani ko dhakel kar ati hai, Wipe hai, aur naya mashhoor Morph hai jo PowerPoint 2019 aur 365 me aya aur aik slide ki cheez ko dusri slide me narmi se badal deta hai. Jab kisi slide par transition lag jaye to Slides pane me us slide ke neeche aik chhota sa sitara ban jata hai.

Animation slide ke andar rakhi cheez ki harkat hai. Aik unwan ka urh kar ana, aik nuqte ka aik aik karke zahir hona, tasveer ka ghoomna, ye sab animation hai. Ise lagane ke liye pehle wo cheez select karte hain, phir Animations tab me jate hain. Animations ki baree qism hai entrance, yani cheez ka andar ana, jese Appear, Fade aur Fly In. Is ke ilawa cheez ko numayan karne wale emphasis effects aur bahar nikalne wale exit effects bhi hote hain. Lagi hui animations ki list aur un ki tartib dekhne ke liye Animations tab me Animation Pane kholte hain.

Aik line me farq bandh lo. Transition darwaze ke khulne ka andaaz hai, animation kamre ke andar ki harkat hai. Sawal me agar poocha jaye ke aik slide se dusri slide par jane ka effect kya kehlata hai to jawab transition, aur agar poocha jaye ke slide par mojood text ko urh kar lane ka effect kya hai to jawab animation. Yehi do sawal har dusre paper me ate hain.

Tasveer ka khayal: Do hisso wali tasveer. Aik taraf theatre ka parda girta aur uthta dikhaya gaya ho aur upar Transition likha ho, sath me do slides ke darmiyan teer bana ho. Dusri taraf aik hi slide ke andar unwan urh kar ata dikhaya gaya ho aur upar Animation likha ho. Darmiyan me aik bari lakeer aur us par likha ho, transition slide badalne par, animation slide ke andar.

Slideshow chalana

Presentation banane ka asal maqsad use chalana hai, aur chalane ke tareeqe paper ke sab se fix sawal hain.

Slideshow shuru karne ki sab se mashhoor key F5 hai. F5 dabane se show hamesha pehli slide se shuru hota hai. Agar tum beech ki kisi slide se show shuru karna chahte ho, to pehle wo slide select karo aur Shift aur F5 aik sath dabao, show usi mojooda slide se shuru ho jayega. Ye F5 aur Shift F5 ka jora paper me lazmi ata hai, inhe jor kar yaad rakho. Show khatam karne ke liye Esc dabate hain. Show ke doran agli slide par jane ke liye right wala teer, Space ya Enter chalta hai, aur pichli slide par wapas jane ke liye left wala teer ya Backspace. Do aur chhoti keys bhi yaad kar lo jo paper me ati hain. Show ke doran B dabane se screen bilkul kaali ho jati hai, taake logon ki tawajjo screen se hat kar tum par aye, aur W dabane se screen safed ho jati hai. Dobara wohi key dabane se slide wapas ajati hai. Slide Show tab me From Beginning ka button bhi wohi kaam karta hai jo F5 karta hai, aur From Current Slide ka button wohi jo Shift F5 karta hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Aik keyboard ka hissa jis me F5 wali key hare rang me aur Shift aur F5 ka jora neele rang me numayan ho, sath me Esc wali key laal rang me. Har key ke neeche aik line me us ka kaam, F5 pehli slide se, Shift F5 mojooda slide se, Esc khatam.

Slide ka size, 4:3 aur 16:9

Aik aur fix fact jo zamane ke sath badla hai, aur paper dono zamane poochta hai. Purane zamane me slides ka size 4:3 hota tha. Ise standard size kehte thay, is ki lambai 10 inch aur chorai 7.5 inch hoti thi, aur ye purane motay monitoron aur projectoron ki shakal thi. Phir chaudi screenon ka zamana aya. PowerPoint 2013 se default slide size 16:9 ho gaya, jise widescreen kehte hain. Is ki lambai 13.33 inch aur chorai wohi 7.5 inch hai. Aaj ke TV, laptop aur projector sab 16:9 hain, is liye nai presentation kholte hi slide chaudi ati hai. Slide ka size badalna ho to Design tab ke Customize group me Slide Size ka button hai, wahan se 4:3 aur 16:9 me se chun sakte ho. Paper me baghair version ke poocha jaye to jawab 16:9 hai, aur purane PowerPoint ka poocha jaye to 4:3.

Tasveer ka khayal: Do slides sath sath khari hon. Aik tung aur unchi si jis par 4:3 aur purana zamana likha ho, dusri chaudi jis par 16:9 aur naya default likha ho. Dono ke neeche un ki inch wali paimaish, taake shakal dekh kar hi size yaad ho jaye.

Notes aur Handouts

Ab do cheezain jo dikhai nahi detin lekin paper me lazmi ati hain.

Pehli hai notes. Har slide ke neeche jo chhoti si jagah hoti hai, wahan bolne wala apne liye notes likhta hai. Ye notes wohi baatein hoti hain jo tumhe bolte waqt yaad rakhni hain lekin screen par dikhana nahi chahte. Khaas baat ye hai ke ye notes audience ko nazar nahi ate. Jab tum Presenter View me show chalate ho to tumhari apni screen par slide ke sath notes bhi dikhte hain, lekin projector par sirf slide jati hai. Notes alag safhe ki shakal me dekhne ke liye View tab me Notes Page view bhi hai. Paper me poocha jata hai ke speaker ke notes kahan likhe jate hain, jawab hai slide ke neeche wale Notes pane me.

Dusri hai handouts. Handout wo print hai jo tum sunne walon ko dete ho taake wo presentation ka khulasa apne pas rakh lein. Print karte waqt File tab me Print me jao, wahan Full Page Slides wali setting ki jagah Handouts chun lo. Aik safhe par 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 ya 9 slides print ho sakti hain. In me sab se khaas 3 slides wala handout hai, kyunke us me har slide ke sath khali lakeerain bani hoti hain jin par sunne wale apne notes likh sakte hain. Ye 3 wali khaasiyat paper ka pasandida sawal hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Aik taraf presenter ki screen jis par slide ke sath us ke notes bhi dikhai de rahe hon, aur usi ke sath projector wali screen jis par sirf slide ho. Dusri taraf aik chhapa hua safha jis par teen chhoti slides aur har aik ke sath khali lakeerain hon, upar Handout likha ho.

Master Slide

Ab wo taqatwar cheez jo bare kaam ko aik click me kar deti hai. Farz karo tumhari presentation me 40 slides hain aur tum chahte ho ke har slide par company ka logo upar wale kone me aye, har unwan ka rang aik jaisa ho aur neeche har slide par tumhara naam likha aye. Agar tum ye kaam 40 slides par alag alag karo ge to aadha din lag jayega. Master slide is ka hal hai.

Slide Master View tab ke Master Views group me milta hai. Master slide sab slides ki maan hai. Jo tabdeeli tum master par kar do, wo tamam slides par khud lag jati hai. Master par logo laga do to har slide par logo ajayega, master par unwan ka font badal do to har slide ka unwan badal jayega. Kaam khatam karke Close Master View se wapas aate hain. Paper me sawal is shakal me ata hai ke tamam slides par aik sath tabdeeli karne ke liye kya istemal hota hai, jawab Slide Master hai. Aur ye bhi yaad rakho ke master sirf shakal badalta hai, har slide ka apna text waisa ka waisa rehta hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Aik bari master slide upar aur us ke neeche us se juri hui bohat si chhoti slides, jese aik darakht aur us ki shakhein. Master slide par logo aur neela unwan dikhaya gaya ho aur tamam chhoti slides par wohi logo aur wohi neela unwan nazar araha ho.

Rehearse Timings

Aakhri kaam ki cheez rehearsal hai. Kabhi presentation ko khud bakhud chalana hota hai, jese kisi numaish me lagi screen par slides khud badalti rahen aur koi bolne wala na ho. Is ke liye pehle waqt ki mashq karte hain. Slide Show tab ke Set Up group me Rehearse Timings ka button hai. Use chalane se show shuru ho jata hai aur PowerPoint har slide par tumhara guzra hua waqt record karta rehta hai. Mashq khatam hone par wo waqton ko save kar lene ko kehta hai. Us ke baad show unhi waqton par khud chalti hai, har slide utni hi der rukti hai jitni der tum ne mashq me rukay thay. Paper me poocha jata hai ke har slide ka waqt pehle se set karne ke liye konsa option hai, jawab Rehearse Timings hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Aik slide ke upar bari si stopwatch bani ho aur neeche teen slides aik line me jin ke neeche un ke waqt likhe hon, 10 second, 15 second, 10 second, aur teer dikhata ho ke show khud age barh rahi hai baghair kisi ke dabaye.

Fix facts ki recap

Ab aakhir me wo tamam fix facts aik sath dohra lo jo is overview se paper ke liye nikalte hain.

1. Nayi presentation ka default naam Presentation1 hota hai.
2. PowerPoint ki nayi extension .pptx hai, purani .ppt thi, farq 2007 ka hai. Show wali file .ppsx hoti hai.
3. Presentation slides ka majmua hai, aur pehli slide ka default layout Title Slide hai.
4. Layout slide par cheezon ki tartib hai, mashhoor layouts kul 9 hain, nayi slide ki shortcut Ctrl aur M hai.
5. Placeholder slide par wo pehle se tay dabba hai jahan click karke text likha jata hai.
6. PowerPoint ka default view Normal hai, aur slides ko chhoti shaklon me aik sath dekhne ka view Slide Sorter hai.
7. Transition slide badalne par lagta hai aur Transitions tab me milta hai, animation slide ke andar ki cheez par lagta hai aur Animations tab me milta hai.
8. Slideshow F5 se pehli slide se shuru hota hai, Shift aur F5 se mojooda slide se, aur Esc se khatam hota hai.
9. Naya default slide size 16:9 widescreen hai, purana size 4:3 tha.
10. Speaker notes slide ke neeche Notes pane me likhe jate hain aur audience ko nazar nahi ate.
11. Aik safhe par 3 slides wale handout me notes likhne ki lakeerain hoti hain.
12. Tamam slides par aik sath tabdeeli Slide Master se hoti hai jo View tab me hai.
13. Har slide ka waqt pehle se set karna Rehearse Timings hai jo Slide Show tab me hai.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Aakhir me do chhoti tricks jo do sab se bare sawalon ko hamesha ke liye pakka kar dein gi. Pehli slideshow wali. F5 ko Full Five samjho, full show pehli slide se. Aur Shift F5 ko yun yaad rakho ke Shift matlab yahin se, mojooda slide se. Dusri trick transition aur animation wali hai. Jab bhi shak ho to theatre wala parda yaad karo. Parda yani transition, ye slide badalne par ata hai. Adakar yani animation, ye slide ke andar harkat karta hai. Parda bahar, adakar andar. Bas, farq kabhi nahi bhoologe.
