MS Word ka Complete Overview
Word ki window ka har kona, aur paper ke saare chhote sawal

Ye overview kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me computer ke hisse me MS Word par har saal sawal ate hain. Aur aik baat gaur se sun lo. Ye sawal mushkil nahi hote, ye sawal chhote hote hain. Default font kya hai? Sab se bara font size kitna hota hai? Spelling check ki key konsi hai? File ki extension kya hai? Ye wo sawal hain jo sirf usi ko ate hain jis ne Word ko kabhi gaur se dekha ho. Jis ne kabhi Word khola hi nahi, wo in me phansta hai, chahe wo kitna hi parha likha kyun na ho.

Is overview ka maqsad ye hai ke tum Word ko is tarah samajh lo jese tum asal app ke samne bethe ho. Kaunsi cheez kahan milti hai, kis tab me milti hai, kis group me milti hai. Jab ye naqsha zehen me baith gaya to paper ke sawal khud bakhud asaan ho jayenge, kyunke har sawal isi naqshe se banta hai.

Word kya hai aur kis kaam ata hai

Sab se pehle ye samajh lo ke MS Word hai kya. MS Word Microsoft company ka banaya hua aik word processor hai. Word processor ka matlab hai aisi app jis me tum likhai ka kaam karte ho. Khat likhna ho, application likhni ho, CV banana ho, report tayyar karni ho, kitab ka safha set karna ho, ye sab Word ka kaam hai. Microsoft Office ke package me jo apps ati hain, un me Word sab se zyada istemal hone wali app hai.

Ab file wali baat, kyunke ye paper ka fix sawal hai. Word me jo file banti hai usay document kehte hain. Har file ke naam ke aakhir me aik extension hoti hai jo batati hai ke ye kis app ki file hai. Purane versions me, yani Word 2003 aur us se pehle, extension .doc hoti thi. Word 2007 ke baad se extension .docx ho gayi hai. Docx ke aakhir wala x XML ki nishani hai, kyunke nayi file ka andar ka dhancha XML par bana hai. To ye jumla pakka yaad kar lo: Word 2007 aur us ke baad ki file .docx, us se pehle ki .doc. Paper me aksar yehi poocha jata hai aur aksar student purani wali .doc par nishan laga kar number kata leta hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Is hisse ki diagram me aik file ka icon dikhaya jaye jis ke neeche naam likha ho Report.docx, aur us ke sath doosra purana icon Report.doc, taake student ko dono extensions ka farq ankh se nazar ajaye.

Word ki window ke hisse

Ab Word ko khol kar zehen me rakho aur aik aik hissa pehchano. Jab tum Word kholte ho to samne aik bari screen ati hai. Is screen ke har hisse ka aik naam hai aur paper me inhi naamon se sawal banta hai. Upar se shuru karte hain.

Sab se upar Title Bar hoti hai. Ye wohi patti hai jis par file ka naam likha nazar ata hai, jese Document1 ya tumhari file ka naam. Agar file abhi save nahi hui to wahan Document1 likha hota hai. Title Bar ke right kone par teen mashhoor button hote hain: Minimize, Maximize ya Restore, aur Close wala surkh X. Aur Title Bar ke left hisse me aik chhoti si patti hoti hai jisay Quick Access Toolbar kehte hain. Is par wohi button rakhe jate hain jo tum bar bar istemal karte ho. Aam tor par wahan Save, Undo aur Redo ke button pehle se hote hain. Paper me poocha jata hai ke Quick Access Toolbar kahan hoti hai, to jawab hai Title Bar par, upar left kone me.

Title Bar ke neeche jo bari patti hai, jis par saare button aur options lage hote hain, usay Ribbon kehte hain. Ribbon Word ka dil hai. Saari commands isi par sajti hain. Ribbon ke upar wali line me bade bade naam likhe hote hain: File, Home, Insert, Design, Layout, References, Mailings, Review, View. In me se har naam ko Tab kehte hain. Har tab ko dabane se us ke neeche us tab ki commands khul jati hain. Aur har tab ke andar commands chhote chhote khanon me banti hoti hain, in khanon ko Group kehte hain. Jese Home tab me Font group hai, Paragraph group hai. Har group ke neeche us ka naam bhi likha hota hai. Ye teen lafz pakke kar lo: upar Tab, tab ke andar Group, group ke andar commands. Paper ka aadha naqsha inhi teen lafzon par khara hai.

Har group ke right neeche kone me aik bohat chhota sa teer ka nishan hota hai. Isay Dialog Box Launcher kehte hain. Is ko dabane se us group ki poori settings wali window, yani dialog box, khul jati hai, jahan wo options bhi mil jate hain jo ribbon par jaga kam hone ki wajah se nazar nahi ate.

Ribbon ke neeche, document ke bilkul upar, aik scale type ki patti hoti hai jisay Ruler kehte hain. Ruler se tum margins dekh sakte ho, paragraph ka indent set kar sakte ho aur tab stops laga sakte ho. Ruler aam tor par inches me hota hai. Agar ruler nazar na aye to View tab se use on karte hain.

Beech wala bara safaid hissa Document Area hai. Ye tumhara asal safha hai, yahin tum likhte ho. Is safhe par jo chhoti si kaali lakeer timtimati hai, blink karti hai, usay Cursor ya Insertion Point kehte hain. Tum jo bhi type karoge, wohi se likhna shuru hoga jahan ye timtimati lakeer khari hai.

Screen ke right taraf aur neeche jo pattiyan hoti hain un se tum document me upar neeche aur dayen bayen ja sakte ho. Inhe Scroll Bar kehte hain. Right wali ko Vertical Scroll Bar aur neeche wali ko Horizontal Scroll Bar kehte hain.

Ab sab se neeche wali patti par ao, isay Status Bar kehte hain. Ye chhoti si patti bohat kaam ki khabrein deti hai. Is par page number likha ata hai ke tum kul kitne safhon me se kis safhe par ho, aur sath hi tumhare document ke kul alfaaz ki ginti, yani Word Count, har waqt nazar ati rehti hai. Status Bar ke right hisse me teen chhote button hote hain jinhe View Buttons kehte hain, un se tum document ko dekhne ka andaaz badalte ho. Aur un ke bilkul sath Zoom Slider hota hai, jisay dayen bayen karne se safha bara ya chhota nazar ata hai. Paper me ye sawal bohat ata hai ke word count kahan nazar ata hai. Jawab Status Bar hai. Bas, ye jumla rat lo.

Tasveer ka khayal: Is hisse ki diagram me poori Word window ki tasveer ho aur har hisse par number laga ho — 1 Title Bar, 2 Quick Access Toolbar, 3 Ribbon, 4 Tab, 5 Ruler, 6 Document Area, 7 Cursor, 8 Scroll Bar, 9 Status Bar, 10 Zoom Slider — aur neeche har number ke samne us hisse ka naam likha ho, bilkul wese jese is overview ke sample naqshay me hota hai.

Har tab me kya milta hai

Ab ribbon ke har tab ko aik aik karke pehchano. Ye woh hissa hai jahan se paper ke sab se zyada sawal bante hain, kyunke examiner poochta hai ke falaan cheez kis tab me hai.

File tab sab se pehla tab hai aur ye baqiyon se alag hai. Ise dabane se ribbon nahi khulta, poori aik screen khulti hai jisay Backstage View kehte hain. Yahan file ke bare me kaam hote hain: New yani nayi file, Open yani purani file kholna, Save, Save As, Print, Share aur Export. File ki info bhi yahin milti hai. Yaad rakho, File tab document ke andar ka kaam nahi karta, file ke upar ka kaam karta hai.

Home tab woh tab hai jo Word khulte hi samne hota hai aur sab se zyada istemal hota hai. Likhai ko sajana sanwarna, font badalna, size bara karna, rang lagana, line set karna, ye sab Home tab ka kaam hai. Iski tafseel agle hisse me ayegi kyunke ye tab paper ke liye sab se aham hai.

Insert tab ka kaam hai document ke andar cheezain dalna. Table dalna ho, tasveer dalni ho, shape banana ho, chart, SmartArt, header footer, page number, symbol ya equation, sab kuch Insert tab me milta hai.

Design tab document ki shakal aur sajawat ke liye hai. Themes, rangon ke set, fonts ke set aur page ka background. Aur aik trap yahan pakka kar lo: Watermark, yani safhe ke peeche halka sa likha hua lafz jese CONFIDENTIAL, wo Design tab me hota hai, Insert me nahi. Purane Word 2010 me watermark Page Layout tab me hota tha, lekin Word 2016 aur 365 me ye Design tab me hai aur paper me aaj kal isi naye wale ko sahi mana jata hai.

Layout tab safhe ki setting ke liye hai. Page ka size, orientation, margins, columns, ye sab Layout tab me milta hai. Word 2013 tak is tab ka naam Page Layout tha, 2016 ke baad sirf Layout reh gaya. Paper me agar Page Layout likha aye to ghabrana nahi, wohi Layout tab hai.

References tab lambay aur ilmi documents ke liye hai. Table of contents banana, footnote dalna, citation aur bibliography set karna, ye sab yahan hota hai. Thesis aur kitab likhne wale is tab ko bohat istemal karte hain.

Mailings tab ka khaas kaam Mail Merge hai. Is ki tafseel aage alag hisse me ayegi.

Review tab parhai aur jaanch ka tab hai. Spelling aur grammar ki ghaltiyan dekhna, comments lagana, track changes, aur document ko compare karna, ye sab Review tab me hai.

View tab document ko dekhne ke andaaz ka tab hai. Alag alag views, zoom, ruler on off karna, aur aik se zyada windows ko sath dikhana, ye sab View tab se hota hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Is hisse ki diagram me ribbon ki aik lambi patti dikhayi jaye jis par saare tabs ke naam hon, aur har tab ke neeche aik line me likha ho ke is me kya milta hai, taake student aik nazar me poora naqsha dekh le.

Home tab ki tafseel

Ab Home tab ko khol kar zehen me rakho, kyunke ye tab tum roz istemal karoge aur paper me is par sab se zyada sawal hain. Home tab me aam tor par paanch group hote hain: Clipboard, Font, Paragraph, Styles aur Editing.

Font group me likhai ki shakal badli jati hai. Yahan tum font chun sakte ho, yani likhai ka andaaz, size bara chhota kar sakte ho, aur text ko Bold, Italic ya Underline kar sakte ho. Bold matlab mota likha hua, is ki key Ctrl+B hai. Italic matlab terha likha hua, key Ctrl+I. Underline matlab neeche lakeer, key Ctrl+U. Ye teenon key paper me bar bar poochi jati hain. Font group me hi font ka rang badalne aur highlight karne ke options bhi hote hain. Font wali bari window kholne ki key Ctrl+D hai, ye bhi yaad rakhne wali hai.

Paragraph group me lines aur paragraphs ki setting hoti hai. Sab se aham cheez alignment hai, yani text kis taraf set hai. Alignment chaar tarah ki hoti hai. Left alignment me text bayen taraf se seedha hota hai aur dayen taraf tera mera, ye aam likhai ke liye hota hai, key Ctrl+L. Center alignment me text beech me ata hai, heading ke liye istemal hota hai, key Ctrl+E. Right alignment me text dayen taraf se seedha hota hai, key Ctrl+R. Aur Justify me text dono taraf se seedha ho jata hai, bilkul akhbaar ke column ki tarah, key Ctrl+J. Ye chaar naam aur in ki keys paper ke fix sawal hain.

Isi Paragraph group me line spacing hoti hai, yani do lines ke darmiyan kitna faasla ho. Aur bullets aur numbering bhi yahin milte hain. Bullets matlab points ke aage gole nishan, aur numbering matlab points ke aage 1, 2, 3 wale number. Indent, yani paragraph ko bayen ya dayen khiskana, bhi isi group ka kaam hai.

Styles group me tayyar shuda styles hote hain. Style matlab font, size, rang aur spacing ka pehle se set kiya hua jora. Normal style aam text ke liye hai. Heading 1 sab se bari heading ke liye, Heading 2 us se chhoti heading ke liye. Aik hi click me poore paragraph ki shakal badal jati hai. Paper me poocha jata hai ke heading lagane ke liye konsa group istemal hota hai, to jawab Styles group hai, Home tab me.

Editing group Home tab ke bilkul aakhir me hota hai. Is me Find, Replace aur Select hote hain. In ki tafseel bhi aage ayegi.

Tasveer ka khayal: Is hisse ki diagram me Home tab ka ribbon bara karke dikhaya jaye, Font group aur Paragraph group par ghaira bana ho, aur neeche alignment ki chaar misalein chhote chhote khanon me hon — left, center, right aur justify — taake student ko chaaron ka farq ankh se samajh ajaye.

Font size wali khaas tafseel

Ab wo hissa jo paper ke liye sab se khaas hai. Font size. Dekho, Home tab ke Font group me size wali aik chhoti si khirki hoti hai jis me aik number likha hota hai, aam tor par 11. Us ke sath aik teer hota hai. Teer dabane se aik list khulti hai. Is tayyar list me sizes 8 se lekar 72 tak hote hain. 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 14, 16, is tarah barhte hue 72 tak. Aksar student yehi samajhta hai ke Word me sab se bara size 72 hai. Yehi wo jaal hai jis me paper phansata hai.

Asal baat ye hai ke tum us khirki me khud number type bhi kar sakte ho. Aur type karke tum 1 se lekar 1638 tak koi bhi size de sakte ho. To Word me sab se chhota font size 1 hai aur sab se bara font size 1638 hai. Ye jumla paper ka fix sawal hai, isay zehen me pakka baitha lo. Jab paper me sawal aye ke MS Word me maximum font size kitna hota hai, to options me 72 bhi hoga, 100 bhi hoga, lekin sahi jawab 1638 hai.

Ab default wali baat. Jab tum Word me nayi file kholte ho to jo font pehle se set hota hai usay default font kehte hain. Word 2016 aur 365 me default font Calibri hai aur default size 11 hai. Bohat purane Word 2003 me default font Times New Roman size 12 hota tha, lekin aaj kal ke paper me Calibri 11 hi sahi jawab mana jata hai. Aur aik phanday se bachna. Heading styles me jo bara size nazar ata hai, wo font size ki had nahi hai. Had sirf wohi hai jo type karke di ja sakti hai, yani 1638.

Tasveer ka khayal: Is hisse ki diagram me font size wali khirki bari karke dikhayi jaye, us ki khuli hui list me 8 se 72 tak ke number hon, aur sath aik doosri tasveer ho jis me usi khirki me 1638 type kiya gaya ho aur neeche aik bohat bara sa lafz likha nazar aye, taake student ko yaad rahe ke list wali had asal had nahi hai.

Page setup ki baatein

Ab safhe ki setting samajh lo. Ye Layout tab ke Page Setup group se hoti hai.

Pehli cheez page size hai. Word me do size sab se zyada chalte hain. Aik A4 hai, jo Pakistan me aur duniya ke zyadatar mulkon me standard hai. A4 ki lambai chorai 210 guna 297 millimeter hoti hai, inchon me taqreeban 8.27 guna 11.69. Doosra Letter hai, jo America me chalta hai, 8.5 guna 11 inch. A4 Letter ke muqable me thora lamba aur thora kam chora hota hai. Paper me ye farq poocha jata hai.

Doosri cheez orientation hai, yani safha khara hai ya leta hua. Jab safha seedha khara ho, lambai zyada aur chorai kam, to use Portrait kehte hain. Aam documents portrait me hi bante hain. Jab safha leta hua ho, chorai zyada aur lambai kam, to use Landscape kehte hain. Bari tables aur tasveeron wale safhe landscape me ache lagte hain.

Teesri cheez margins hain. Margin matlab safhe ke chaaron kinaron par chhori gayi khaali jaga. Upar neeche dayen bayen. Word me default margin chaaron taraf 1 inch hota hai. Ye bhi paper ka fix sawal hai. Purane Word 2003 me upar neeche 1 inch aur dayen bayen 1.25 inch hota tha, lekin naye Word me chaaron taraf barabar 1 inch hai, aur paper me yehi jawab chalta hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Is hisse ki diagram me do safhe sath sath dikhaye jayen — aik portrait me khara aur aik landscape me leta hua — aur teesre safhe par chaaron taraf margin wali jaga halke rang se bhari hui ho aur teeron se 1 inch likha ho.

Insert tab aur table ki baatein

Insert tab ka kaam pehle bata chuka hun ke document ke andar cheezain dalna hai. Ab in cheezon ko aik aik karke pehchano.

Table dalna Insert tab ka sab se mashhoor kaam hai. Table matlab khanon wali chart, jese time table hota hai. Table me jo khari lines wale hisse hote hain unhe Column kehte hain, aur jo leti lines wale hisse hote hain unhe Row kehte hain. Row aur column ke milne se jo chhota khana banta hai usay Cell kehte hain. Ye teen lafz pakke kar lo: Row, Column, Cell. Jab tum Insert tab se table dalte ho to ribbon par do naye tab khul jate hain jinhe Table Tools kehte hain, Design aur Layout. In se tum table ko rang de sakte ho, lines badal sakte ho, do cells ko jor kar aik kar sakte ho jisay Merge kehte hain, aur aik cell ko tod kar do bana sakte ho jisay Split kehte hain. Jab poora table select karna ho to table ke upar bayen kone par aik chhota sa plus jaisa nishan ata hai, use dabane se poora table select ho jata hai.

Insert tab se hi tum document me Picture yani tasveer dal sakte ho, Shapes yani gole, chaukor, teer jaisi shaklein bana sakte ho, aur Chart dal sakte ho. Header aur Footer ke buttons bhi Insert tab me hote hain, in ki tafseel thori aage ayegi. Page Number dalne ka button bhi Insert tab me hai. Symbol dalna, yani wo nishan jo keyboard par nahi hote jese copyright wala ya degree wala nishan, wo bhi Insert tab ke aakhir me Symbol se milta hai. Aur hisaab ke sawal likhne ke liye Equation ka option bhi Insert tab me hi hota hai.

Aik baar phir wo trap dohra dun. Watermark Insert tab me nahi hai. Watermark Design tab me hai. Paper me ye jaan boojh kar Insert ke options me rakha jata hai aur bohat se student wahi phanste hain.

Tasveer ka khayal: Is hisse ki diagram me aik chhoti si table dikhayi jaye jis par row par teer, column par teer aur aik cell ke gird ghaira bana ho, taake teeno lafzon ka farq ankh se wazeh ho jaye. Sath Insert tab ke mashhoor buttons ke chhote icon bhi dikhaye jayen.

Find, Replace, Spelling aur Thesaurus

Ab wo tools jo likhai ki jaanch aur tabdeeli ke liye hain. Ye Home tab ke Editing group me bhi milte hain aur in ki shortcut keys paper ki jaan hain.

Find ka matlab hai document me koi lafz dhundna. Is ki key Ctrl+F hai. Ise dabane se bayen taraf aik khana khul jata hai jahan tum lafz likhte ho aur Word tumhe bata deta hai ke wo lafz kahan kahan aya hai.

Replace ka matlab hai aik lafz ko dhund kar us ki jagah doosra lafz rakh dena, pure document me aik sath. Is ki key Ctrl+H hai. Farz karo tum ne pure document me Ahmad likha tha aur ab use Ahmed karna hai. Replace se aik click me har jagah badal jayega. Find aur Replace ka farq paper me bohat ata hai. Sirf dhundna hai to Ctrl+F, dhund kar badalna hai to Ctrl+H.

Ab spelling ki jaanch. Jab tum Word me koi lafz ghalat likhte ho to us ke neeche aik surkh rengti hui lakeer, zigzag line, ajati hai. Is ka matlab hai spelling ki ghalti. Agar grammar ki ghalti ho, yani jumla banaan ke hisab se ghalat ho, to neeche neeli ya sabz lakeer ati hai. Purane versions me grammar ke liye sabz lakeer thi, naye Word me neeli ho gayi hai. Aur aik teesri lakeer bhi hoti hai, neeli wali jo kabhi kabhi jumle ki wazehiyat par ishara karti hai. In lakeeron ko dekh kar hi pata chal jata hai ke ghalti kis qism ki hai.

Poore document ki spelling aur grammar aik sath check karne ke liye F7 dabate hain. F7 Review tab ke Spelling and Grammar wale kaam ko khol deta hai. Ye key paper me lazmi poochi jati hai. Aur is ke sath aik aur key jor kar yaad kar lo: Shift+F7. Shift+F7 se Thesaurus khulta hai. Thesaurus matlab kisi lafz ke hum maani alfaaz, yani synonyms, aur mukhalif maani alfaaz ki fehrist. Jab tumhe kisi lafz ki jagah behtar lafz chahiye ho to Thesaurus kaam ata hai. To ye jora rat lo: F7 spelling aur grammar, Shift+F7 Thesaurus.

Tasveer ka khayal: Is hisse ki diagram me aik jumla dikhaya jaye jis me aik lafz ke neeche surkh zigzag lakeer ho aur doosre hisse ke neeche neeli lakeer, aur neeche likha ho ke surkh matlab spelling ki ghalti aur neeli matlab grammar ki. Sath F7 aur Shift+F7 ki keys keyboard par numaya karke dikhayi jayen.

Mail Merge kya hai

Ab Mailings tab ka wo kaam jo naam sun kar bohaton ko mushkil lagta hai lekin hai bohat asaan. Mail Merge.

Farz karo aik school ko 500 students ke parents ko result ki chitthi bhejni hai. Har chitthi me school ka wohi purana text hoga, bas student ka naam, class aur number alag honge. Agar tum 500 chitthiyan alag alag likho to din lag jayega. Mail Merge ye karta hai ke tum aik hi asal khat likhte ho, aur naamon wali list alag se bana lete ho, phir Word khud har naam ke liye alag chitthi tayyar kar deta hai. Yani aik likhai, bohat se log. Merge ka matlab hai milana — asal khat aur naamon ki list ko mila dena.

Mail Merge Mailings tab me hota hai. Wahan Start Mail Merge se kaam shuru hota hai, phir Select Recipients se naamon wali list jori jati hai, jo aam tor par Excel ki file ya Word ki table me hoti hai. Phir khat me jaga jaga naam wale khaali khane, jinhe merge fields kehte hain, rakhe jate hain. Aakhir me Finish and Merge se saari chitthiyan tayyar ho jati hain.

Paper me Mail Merge is liye ata hai kyunke ye Word ka sab se mashhoor khaas kaam hai aur examiner poochta hai ke ye kis tab me hota hai aur is se kya hota hai. Jawab yaad rakho: Mailings tab, aik hi document bohat se logon ke liye, har aik ke naam ke sath.

Tasveer ka khayal: Is hisse ki diagram me aik taraf aik asal khat dikhaya jaye jis me naam ki jaga khaali khana ho, doosri taraf naamon ki chhoti si list ho, aur beech me aik teer ja kar teen alag alag chitthiyan banata hua nazar aye, taake mail merge ka khayal aik nazar me samajh ajaye.

Header, Footer aur Page Number

Har safhe ke upar wale hisse ko Header kehte hain aur neeche wale hisse ko Footer kehte hain. Header me aam tor par kitab ka naam, chapter ka naam ya school ka naam likha jata hai jo har safhe par khud bakhud ata rehta hai. Tumhe har safhe par alag se likhna nahi parta, aik dafa header me likh do to har safhe par wahi nazar ayega. Footer me aam tor par page number dala jata hai.

Page number dalne ke liye Insert tab me Page Number ka button hai. Wahan se tum chun sakte ho ke number safhe ke upar aye ya neeche, bayen aye ya beech me ya dayen. Header aur Footer me ja kar tum khud bhi number aur text dal sakte ho. Jab tum header ya footer me kaam kar rahe hote ho to asal document halka sa dhundla ho jata hai, aur bahar click karne se tum wapas asal text me ajate ho.

Paper me ye sawal ata hai ke page number kis tab se dala jata hai. Jawab Insert tab hai. Aur header footer kahan hote hain, safhe ke sab se upar aur sab se neeche wale margin wale hisse me.

Tasveer ka khayal: Is hisse ki diagram me aik safha dikhaya jaye jis ke upar wale hisse par ghaira bana kar Header likha ho, beech me asal text halka ho, aur neeche wale hisse par ghaira bana kar Footer likha ho jahan page number 5 nazar araha ho.

Zoom aur Views

Ab document ko dekhne ki baatein. Pehle zoom. Zoom ka matlab hai safhe ko screen par bara ya chhota karke dekhna. Is se asal print par koi farq nahi parta, sirf tumhari screen par safha bara ya chhota nazar ata hai. Zoom status bar ke right kone par slider se badla jata hai, aur View tab me Zoom ka button bhi hota hai. Word me zoom sab se kam 10 percent aur sab se zyada 500 percent hota hai. Ye bhi paper ka fix sawal hai. Aam halat me zoom 100 percent hota hai.

Ab views. View matlab document ko dekhne ka andaaz. Word me paanch views hote hain aur ye View tab se aur status bar ke view buttons se badle jate hain.

Pehla Print Layout hai. Ye woh view hai jo Word khulte hi samne hota hai, yani ye default view hai. Is me safha bilkul wesa nazar ata hai jesa print hokar niklega, margins aur header footer ke sath. Paper ka fix sawal hai ke Word ka default view konsa hai, jawab Print Layout.

Doosra Read Mode hai. Ye parhne ke liye hai. Is me safha kitab ki tarah khul jata hai aur editing ke button ghayab ho jate hain taake tum sukoon se parh sako.

Teesra Web Layout hai. Is me document wesa nazar ata hai jesa kisi website ke safhe par nazar ayega, bina safhon ki taqseem ke.

Chautha Outline hai. Ye lambay document ki headings ko khaaka bana kar dikhata hai, taake poore document ka dhancha aik nazar me samajh ajaye.

Paanchwan Draft hai. Ye saada view hai jis me header footer aur margins nazar nahi ate, sirf text hota hai. Tez likhne ke liye ye acha rehta hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Is hisse ki diagram me aik hi safha paanch chhote khanon me alag alag views me dikhaya jaye — Print Layout, Read Mode, Web Layout, Outline aur Draft — aur Print Layout wale khane par sitara laga ho ke ye default hai. Sath zoom slider ki tasveer ho jis par 10 percent aur 500 percent ke nishan hon.

Print aur Save

Ab aakhir ke do kaam, jo har file ke sath hote hain: save karna aur print karna.

Save ka matlab hai apni file ko mehfooz kar lena taake baad me phir khul sake. Save ki key Ctrl+S hai. Pehli dafa save karne par Word tum se file ka naam aur jaga poochta hai. Agar tum file ko naye naam se ya doosri jaga save karna chahte ho to Save As istemal hota hai, jis ki key F12 hai. Ye F12 wali key paper me bohat poochi jati hai, isay pakka kar lo. Save As se hi tum file ko .docx ki bajaye PDF me bhi save kar sakte ho. PDF aisi file hoti hai jo har computer par aik jaisi khulti hai aur us me tabdeeli asaan nahi hoti, is liye CV aur applicationain aksar PDF me bheji jati hain. File tab ke Export se bhi PDF banayi ja sakti hai.

Print ka matlab hai document ko kaghaz par nikalna. Print ki key Ctrl+P hai. Ise dabane se print wali screen khulti hai jahan tum pehle Print Preview dekh sakte ho, yani print se pehle safha wesa hi dekh sakte ho jesa kaghaz par niklega. Wahan tum chun sakte ho ke kitni copies chahiye, konse safhe print hon, aur printer konsa ho. Ye teen keys ka set aik sath yaad kar lo: Ctrl+S save, F12 save as, Ctrl+P print.

Aur aakhir me word count wali baat aik dafa phir. Tumhare document me kul kitne alfaaz hain, ye har waqt Status Bar par likha nazar ata rehta hai. Agar tumhe poori tafseel chahiye, kitne safhe, kitne alfaaz, kitne characters, to Status Bar ke usi word count par click karne se poori window khul jati hai. Review tab me bhi Word Count ka button hota hai. Paper me poocha jata hai word count kahan dikhta hai, to pehla jawab Status Bar hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Is hisse ki diagram me teen keys bari bari dikhayi jayen — Ctrl+S ke sath Save, F12 ke sath Save As aur Ctrl+P ke sath Print likha ho — aur neeche status bar ki patti ho jis par page number aur word count numaya karke dikhaye gaye hon.

Paper ke fix facts

Ab is pure overview ka nichor aik jagah jama kar lo. Ye wo jumle hain jo paper me seedhe seedhe sawal ban kar ate hain. Inhe is tarah yaad karo jese ye tumhare liye likhe gaye hon.

Word ki file ki extension .docx hai, jo 2007 ke baad se hai, aur us se pehle .doc thi. Nayi khaali file ka naam Document1 hota hai. Word ka default font Calibri hai aur default size 11 hai. Font size ki tayyar list 8 se 72 tak jati hai, lekin type karke 1 se 1638 tak diya ja sakta hai, to sab se bara font size 1638 hai. Default margin chaaron taraf 1 inch hai. A4 ka size 210 guna 297 millimeter hai aur Letter 8.5 guna 11 inch. Zoom sab se kam 10 percent aur sab se zyada 500 percent hota hai. Word ka default view Print Layout hai.

Keys wale facts ye hain. Spelling aur grammar check F7 hai. Thesaurus Shift+F7 hai. Find Ctrl+F, Replace Ctrl+H. Save Ctrl+S, Save As F12, Print Ctrl+P. Bold Ctrl+B, Italic Ctrl+I, Underline Ctrl+U. Alignment ki keys: Left Ctrl+L, Center Ctrl+E, Right Ctrl+R, Justify Ctrl+J.

Jagah wale facts ye hain. Word count Status Bar par nazar ata hai. Quick Access Toolbar Title Bar par hoti hai. Watermark Design tab me hai, Insert me nahi. Mail Merge Mailings tab me hai. Page number Insert tab se dalta hai. Spelling check Review tab me bhi hai aur F7 se bhi khulta hai.

Aur alfaaz wale facts. Table ki khari taqseem Column, leti taqseem Row, aur dono ke milne ka khana Cell hai. Safhe ke upar wala hissa Header aur neeche wala Footer hai. Do cells ko jorna Merge aur aik ko todna Split hai. Spelling ki ghalti par surkh zigzag lakeer ati hai, grammar ki ghalti par neeli lakeer. Jo ye saare jumle pakke kar le, us ke liye Word ke sawal paper ke sab se asaan number ban jate hain.
