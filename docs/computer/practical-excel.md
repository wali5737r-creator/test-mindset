MS Excel me Asal Kaam — Class ka Result Banana
Aik poori result sheet apne haath se banao

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

Excel ka naam sunte hi aksar student ghabra jata hai, kyunke use lagta hai ke ye sirf hisaab walon ka kaam hai. Asal me Excel har us shakhs ka kaam hai jise numberon ko tartib se rakhna hai. Aur is ka sab se mashhoor aur sab se zyada manga jane wala kaam hai class ka result banana. Har school, har academy, har coaching centre me result Excel me banta hai. Agar tum ye aik kaam seekh lo to samajh lo Excel ki aadhi proficiency tumhare haath me agayi.

Is lesson me hum khaali baatein nahi karenge. Hum asal me aik poori class ki result sheet banayenge, pehle khane se lekar print tak. Tum sath sath karte jao. Jahan formula ayega, wahan main pehle ye bataunga ke formula sochta kesay hai, phir likhne ka tareeqa. Kyunke formula ratne wale hamesha phanste hain, formula samajhne wale kabhi nahi phanste.

Qadam 1: Headings likhna

Excel kholo. Samne khanon wali aik bari sheet hai. Yaad rakho, har khane ko cell kehte hain aur har cell ka aik naam hota hai jo us ke column wale harf aur row wale number se banta hai. Sab se upar bayen wala cell A1 hai. Usi par click karo aur pehli heading likho, Roll. Phir Tab key dabao, cursor B1 me chala jayega, wahan Name likho. Isi tarah age barhte jao. C1 me English, D1 me Urdu, E1 me Maths, F1 me Total, G1 me Percentage, H1 me Status. Tab dabate jao aur likhte jao, mouse ko haath lagane ki zaroorat hi nahi.

Ye tartib zehen me rakho. Pehle pehchan wali cheezain, Roll aur Name. Phir har mazmoon ke number. Phir un numberon se banne wali cheezain, Total, Percentage aur Status. Excel me hamesha yehi usool chalta hai, pehle asal data, phir us se nikli hui cheezain.

Tasveer ka khayal: Is hisse ki diagram me Excel ki sheet ka upar wala hissa dikhaya jaye, pehli row me aath headings likhi nazar ayen, aur A1 cell par ghaira bana kar neeche likha ho ke cell ka naam column ke harf aur row ke number se banta hai.

Qadam 2: Data enter karna aur sheet ko saaf karna

Ab dusri row se students ka data bharna shuru karo. A2 me pehla roll number likho, Enter dabao, cursor neeche ajayega, dusra roll number likho. Isi tarah pehle saare roll number bhar lo, phir B column me naam, phir C, D aur E me teeno mazameen ke number. Har mazmoon ke kul number 100 hain, ye baat aage percentage me kaam ayegi.

Data bharte hue aik preshani ayegi. Kisi student ka naam lamba hoga aur cell me pura nazar nahi ayega, aadha chhup jayega. Is ka hal ye hai ke column ko chora kar do. Column wale harf ke dayen kinare par mouse le jao, jab teer dono taraf wala ban jaye to double click kar do. Column khud apne sab se lambe naam ke barabar chora ho jayega. Isay AutoFit kehte hain. Teeno number wale column bhi isi tarah set kar lo.

Ab headings ko numayan karo. Pehli row ko select karo, is ke liye row wale number 1 par click karna kaafi hai, poori row select ho jayegi. Home tab se Bold wala button dabao. Phir usi Home tab me bucket yani rang wali balti wala button hota hai, Fill Color, us se halka sa rang, jese halka neela ya halka peela, pehli row me bhar do. Ab headings alag nazar ayengi aur sheet parhne wale ko foran samajh ayega ke upar wali line naam hain, neeche data hai. Chaho to poori sheet ka data select karke Borders wale button se All Borders laga do, har khane ke gird lakeer ajayegi aur sheet chhapi hui result jaisi lagne lagegi.

Tasveer ka khayal: Is hisse ki diagram me bhari hui sheet dikhayi jaye, pehli row bold aur halke rang me ho, aik lambe naam wala column AutoFit se chora hua dikhaya jaye, aur column ke kinare par double click wali jagah par teer bana ho.

Qadam 3: Total ka formula — SUM

Ab asal Excel shuru hota hai. Formula seekhne se pehle us ke peeche ki soch samajh lo. Formula Excel ko di gayi aik hidayat hai ke is khane me tum khud hisaab karke jawab rakho. Aur har formula hamesha barabar wale nishan se shuru hota hai, yani = se. Ye = hi Excel ko batata hai ke aage likhi cheez aam text nahi, hisaab hai. Agar tum = na lagao to Excel use sirf likhai samjhega aur koi hisaab nahi karega. Ye = wali baat Excel ki sab se buniyadi baat hai, isay kabhi na bhoolna.

Dusri baat, formula me hum number nahi likhte, cell ke naam likhte hain. Kyun? Kyunke agar kal ko kisi student ke number badal jayen to formula khud naya jawab nikaal lega. Agar tum ne formula me seedha number likh diye to number badalne par jawab purana hi rahega aur tumhe pata bhi nahi chalega. Cell ke naam wala formula zinda hota hai, number wala formula murda.

Ab amal karo. Pehle student ke Total wale cell me click karo, ye F2 hoga. Wahan ye likho =SUM(C2:E2) aur Enter dabao. Dekho, teeno mazameen ke number jud kar jawab ajayega. Is formula ko khol kar samjho. SUM ka matlab hai jama karo. C2:E2 ka matlab hai C2 se lekar E2 tak ke saare cell. Darmiyan wala colon, yani do nuqton wala nishan, Excel ki zubaan me se lekar tak ka matlab deta hai. To poora formula bola jaye to ye hai, C2 se E2 tak sab ko jama kar do.

Aik aur tareeqa bhi hai jo tumhe pata hona chahiye. Tum =C2+D2+E2 bhi likh sakte ho, jawab wohi ayega. Lekin jab mazameen teen ki jagah das hon to SUM wala tareeqa hi kaam ayega, das dafa jama ka nishan koi nahi lagata. Is liye SUM seekho.

Tasveer ka khayal: Is hisse ki diagram me F2 cell select hua dikhaya jaye, upar formula bar me =SUM(C2:E2) likha nazar aye, aur C2 se E2 tak ke teen cell kisi rang se range hue hon, taake student ko dikhe ke formula kin khanon ko jama kar raha hai.

Qadam 4: Percentage ka formula

Percentage ka concept pehle zehen me bithao. Percentage ka matlab hai har sau par kitna. Student ne teen mazameen me kul 300 me se number liye hain. Agar us ke kul 210 aye to us ki percentage wohi hogi jo 210 ko 300 se taqseem karke 100 se guna karne par ati hai, yani 70. Bas yahi hisaab formula me likhna hai.

G2 wale cell me click karo aur ye likho =F2/300 × 100 ke bajaye Excel ki zubaan me isay yun likhte hain =F2/300*100 lekin rukho, aik behtar aur saaf tareeqa hai. Sirf =F2/300 likho aur Enter dabao. Jawab 0.7 ajayega. Ghabrana nahi, hisaab sahi hai, bas shakal dusri hai. Ab isi cell ko select rakho aur Home tab ke Number group me % wala button dabado. Jawab foran 70% ban jayega. Asal me % wala button khud hi 100 se guna karke nishan laga deta hai. To usool ye bana, percentage ke liye kul ko kul numberon se taqseem karo, phir % wala button dabao. Agar % wala button na dabao to formula me khud *100 lagana parega, dono tareeqay sahi hain, pehla zyada saaf hai.

Yahan * wala nishan samajh lo. Keyboard par guna ka nishan × nahi hota, Excel me guna ke liye * istemal hota hai, taqseem ke liye / aur jama ke liye +, ghatane ke liye minus wala nishan. Ye chaar nishan Excel ke saare hisaab ki buniyad hain.

Tasveer ka khayal: Is hisse ki diagram me G2 cell me 0.7 likha dikhaya jaye, phir usi cell ki dusri tasveer me % button dabane ke baad 70% nazar aye, darmiyan me Home tab ke % wale button par ghaira bana ho.

Qadam 5: Status ke liye IF ka formula

Ab sab se zyada kaam ki cheez. Status wala column batayega ke student pass hai ya fail. Ye faisla Excel khud karega, tumhe har aik ko dekh kar nahi likhna parega. Is ke liye IF wala formula istemal hota hai. IF ka matlab hai agar. Ye formula Excel ko kehta hai ke agar ye shart sahi hai to ye likho, warna wo likho.

Is ke peeche ki soch ye hai. Hum Excel ko kahenge, agar is student ki percentage 40 ya us se zyada hai to PASS likho, warna FAIL likho. Bas, yahi jumla formula ki shakal me likhna hai.

H2 wale cell me click karo aur ye likho =IF(G2>=40,"PASS","FAIL") aur Enter dabao. Jawab khud ajayega. Ab is formula ko tukron me khol kar samjho. IF ke baad bracket me pehli cheez shart hai, G2>=40, yani G2 ki qeemat 40 ke barabar ya us se bari hai. Ye >= wala nishan barabar ya zyada ka matlab deta hai. Shart ke baad pehla comma, phir "PASS", ye woh jawab hai jo shart sahi hone par ayega. Dusra comma, phir "FAIL", ye shart ghalat hone ka jawab hai. Aur aik bareeki, PASS aur FAIL ko double quotes me is liye likha hai kyunke ye alfaaz hain, number nahi. Excel me jo cheez lafzon me likhni ho use quotes me band karte hain, jo number ho use khula likhte hain. Ye farq na samjho to formula error de dega.

Ek baar ye formula chal jaye to tum ne Excel ka woh darwaza khol liya jahan computer tumhare liye faisle karta hai. Fees ki list me late wale, attendance me kam hazri wale, ye sab isi IF se pakre jate hain.

Tasveer ka khayal: Is hisse ki diagram me H2 cell me PASS likha dikhaya jaye, upar formula bar me poora IF wala formula nazar aye, aur neeche formula ke teen hisse alag rangon me samjhaye gaye hon — shart, sahi wala jawab, ghalat wala jawab.

Qadam 6: Fill handle — formula sab par lagana

Ab woh kaam jo asal proficiency hai. Tum ne formula sirf pehle student ke liye likha hai. Baqi bees students ke liye kya tum bees dafa formula likhoge? Bilkul nahi. Excel ne is ke liye fill handle diya hai.

F2 wale cell ko select karo. Us ke neeche dayen kone par aik bohat chhota sa kala chaukor khana nazar ayega. Yahi fill handle hai. Mouse ko us par le jao, imla nishan aik bare plus se badal kar patla kala plus ban jayega. Ab mouse daba kar neeche ki taraf ghaseeto, jitne students hain wahan tak, aur chhor do. Dekho, har student ka Total khud nikal aya.

Is ke peeche jo jadugari hai wo bhi samajh lo. Jab tum formula neeche ghaseette ho to Excel har row ke hisaab se cell ke naam khud badal deta hai. Dusri row me formula C3:E3 ka ban jayega, teesri me C4:E4 ka. Isay relative reference kehte hain, lekin naam ratne ki zaroorat nahi, bas ye samajh lo ke formula safar karta hai aur jahan jata hai wahan ke khanon ko pakar leta hai.

Yahi kaam G2 ke percentage wale aur H2 ke status wale formula par bhi karo. Teenon columns ko neeche tak bhar do. Chand seconds me poori class ka result tayyar. Aur aik tez tareeqa bhi bata dun, fill handle par double click kar do to formula khud wahan tak bhar jayega jahan tak sath wale column me data hai. Bees ho ya paanch sau students, waqt utna hi lagega.

Tasveer ka khayal: Is hisse ki diagram me aik cell ke neeche dayen kone par bane chhote chaukor khane par bara ghaira bana ho, aur teer neeche ki taraf jata dikhaya jaye, sath teen rows me formula bharne ke baad ke jawabat nazar ayen.

Qadam 7: Class ki report — AVERAGE, MAX aur MIN

Result ban gaya, ab teacher puchega ke class ki ausat percentage kya hai, sab se zyada number kis ke aye, sab se kam kis ke. Ye teeno jawab bhi formula dega.

Sheet ke neeche aik khaali jagah par likho Class Average. Us ke sath wale cell me ye formula likho =AVERAGE(G2:G21) aur Enter dabao. G2:G21 se murad hai doosri row se beesvi row tak ki saari percentages. Apni sheet me jitne students hain, aakhri row ka number wohi likhna. AVERAGE ka matlab hai ausat, saari percentages jud kar un ki tadaad par taqseem, lekin ye saara kaam Excel khud karega.

Isi tarah =MAX(F2:F21) likhne se sab se bara Total ajayega, aur =MIN(F2:F21) likhne se sab se chhota. MAX matlab sab se zyada, MIN matlab sab se kam. Ye teen formula mila kar tumhari sheet sirf result nahi rahi, report ban gayi. Principal ko class ki jo khulasa report chahiye hoti hai, wo yehi teen number hote hain.

Tasveer ka khayal: Is hisse ki diagram me sheet ke neeche teen linein dikhayi jayen — Class Average, Highest, Lowest — aur har aik ke samne formula se aya hua jawab nazar aye, formula bar me AVERAGE wala formula dikhaya jaye.

Qadam 8: Sort karna aur filter lagana

Ab do kaam jo result wale din sab se zyada hote hain.

Pehla sort karna. Merit list banani hai, yani sab se zyada number wala sab se upar. Apne data ke kisi bhi cell me click karo. Data tab par jao aur Sort wala button dabao. Aik window khulegi. Wahan Sort by wali list me Total chun lo, aur Order me Largest to Smallest chun lo, phir OK dabao. Poori sheet palat jayegi, pehle number wala sab se upar. Aik aham ehtiyat, sort karne se pehle hamesha file ko save kar lo, aur sort wali window me agar ye pucha jaye ke selection barhao to Expand the selection wala option chuno, warna sirf aik column paltega aur naam kisi ke aur number kisi ke ho jayenge. Ye ghalti bohat mehngi parti hai.

Dusra filter lagana. Maan lo tumhe sirf FAIL hue students ki list chahiye. Headings wali row select karo, Data tab se Filter wala button dabao. Har heading ke sath aik chhota sa teer ajayega. Status wale teer par click karo, wahan PASS ka tick hata kar sirf FAIL rakho, OK dabao. Ab sirf fail students nazar ayenge, baqi chhup jayenge, gaye nahi, sirf chhupe hain. Saari list wapas lane ke liye Filter wala button dobara dabado ya teer se Select All kar do. Filter chhoti list nikalne ke liye hai, sort tartib badalne ke liye hai, dono ka farq yaad rakho.

Tasveer ka khayal: Is hisse ki diagram me do shaklein hon — aik me merit list bani hui sheet jahan sab se zyada total upar ho, dusri me filter ke baad sirf FAIL wale students nazar aa rahe hon aur Status wali heading par filter wala teer ghaira bana kar dikhaya jaye.

Qadam 9: Print ke liye tayyar karna

Aakhri kaam, sheet ko kagaz par utarna. Excel ki sheet screen par jitni bhi saaf ho, print me aksar bigar jati hai kyunke ye kaam aksar log karte hi nahi.

Pehle Print Preview dekho, Ctrl+P dabao. Agar sheet do safhon me kat rahi hai to pehle orientation badlo. Page Layout tab par jao, Orientation se Landscape chun lo, sheet leti hui ho jayegi aur zyada column aik safhe me ajayenge. Phir bhi na aye to usi tab me Width wali setting ko 1 page kar do, Excel khud sheet ko thora chhota karke aik safhe me fit kar dega.

Agar data zyada hai aur sirf aik hissa print karna hai to woh hissa select karo, Page Layout tab se Print Area dabakar Set Print Area kar do. Ab sirf wohi hissa chhapega.

Aur sab se kaam ki setting, headings ko har safhe par dohrana. Agar result teen safhon ka hai to dusre safhe par headings nahi hongi aur parhne wala confuse hoga ke kaunsa column kis ka hai. Page Layout tab me Print Titles wala button dabao, Rows to repeat wali khirki me pehli row chun lo. Ab har safhe ke upar headings khud ajayengi. Print karne se pehle file save karna na bhoolna.

Tasveer ka khayal: Is hisse ki diagram me print preview wali screen dikhayi jaye jahan landscape me poori sheet aik safhe par fit nazar aye, aur Page Layout tab ke Print Titles wale button par ghaira bana ho.

Aam ghaltiyan jo beginner karta hai

Ab wo ghaltiyan jo main har naye Excel seekhne wale ko karte dekhta hun.

Pehli ghalti, formula se pehle = na lagana. Bina = ke Excel hisaab nahi karta, sirf text samajhta hai. Agar tumhara formula jawab ki jagah waisa ka waisa likha nazar aye to pehla shak isi = par karo.

Dusri ghalti, formula me cell ke naam ki jagah seedha number likh dena. Phir jab data badalta hai to jawab purana rehta hai aur ghalti pakri nahi jati.

Teesri ghalti, percentage me % wala button ya *100 bhool jana, phir 70 ki jagah 0.7 dekh kar ghabra jana. Hisab sahi hota hai, shakal adhuri hoti hai.

Chauthi ghalti, sort karte hue sirf aik column sort kar dena. Is se naam aur number ka rishta toot jata hai aur poora result kharab ho jata hai. Hamesha poora data select karke ya Expand the selection keh kar sort karo.

Paanchvi ghalti, filter laga kar samajhna ke baqi data khatam ho gaya. Filter sirf chhupata hai, mitata nahi. Filter hatate hi sab wapas ajata hai.

Chhati ghalti, file save na karna ya purani file par hi nayi file likh dena. Har term ke result ko alag naam se save karo, jese Result Class 10 December, taake purana record mehfooz rahe.

Khud kar ke dekho

Ab ye mashq khud karo. Excel kholo aur das students ki result sheet banao, naam farzi rakh lo. Headings likho, data bharo, headings bold karke rang do. Phir SUM se Total, taqseem aur % wale button se Percentage, aur IF se Status nikalo. Teeno formula fill handle se neeche tak lagao. Phir AVERAGE se class ki ausat, MAX se sab se zyada aur MIN se sab se kam number nikalo. Us ke baad Total par sort karke merit list banao, phir filter laga kar sirf FAIL wale alag dikhao. Aakhir me sheet ko landscape karke, headings har safhe par dohrane ki setting laga kar print preview dekho. Agar ye sab tum ne aik bethak me kar liya to tum sirf Excel jante nahi ho, tum Excel me kaam kar sakte ho. Yahi asal proficiency hai.
