MS Word me Asal Kaam — Step by Step
Parhne ke sath sath haath se karna bhi seekho

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

Paper ke sawal rat lene se tum number to le loge, lekin jab asal zindagi me kisi daftar, school ya academy me computer ke samne bethna parega to wahan sawal nahi milega, kaam milega. Application likh do, CV bana do, result sheet tayyar kar do. Is lesson ka maqsad ye hai ke tum Word me ye saare kaam khud kar sako, baghair kisi se pooche. Main har kaam ko is tarah bata raha hun jese main tumhare paas betha hun aur tum meri ungli ke ishare par mouse chala rahe ho. Pehle ye dabao, phir ye khulega, yahan ye likho. Bas isi tartib par chalte jao, kaam khud hota jayega.

Aik mashwara shuru me hi de dun. Ye lesson sirf parhna mat. Agar computer paas hai to Word khol kar sath sath karte jao. Jo kaam aik dafa haath se ho jaye, wo zindagi bhar nahi bhoolta. Jo sirf parha jaye, wo paper wale din bhi dhundla ho jata hai.

Kaam 1: Application ya letter likhna

Sab se pehle woh kaam seekho jo Pakistan me sab se zyada manga jata hai. School ke principal ko application, daftar me chhutti ki darkhwast, ya kisi idaray ko letter. Word kholte hi samne jo khaali safha ata hai, wahan se shuru karte hain.

Pehle safha set kar lo. Layout tab par jao. Wahan Size wale button se A4 chun lo, kyunke Pakistan me har daftar aur har photocopy wali dukaan A4 par chalti hai. Us ke sath Margins wale button se Normal chun lo, is se chaaron taraf aik inch ki jaga tayyar ho jati hai. Ye do kaam pehle karne ki wajah ye hai ke baad me page badalne se poori likhai hil jati hai. Pehle ghar ki buniyad, phir deewarein.

Ab likhna shuru karo. Sab se pehle upar bayen taraf se ye teen linein likho. Pehli line me To, likho. Dusri line me jis ko application de rahe ho us ka ohda aur idara likho, jese The Principal, Government High School, Lahore. Teesri line me sirf lafz Subject likh kar us ke aage apni darkhwast ki aik line wali wazahat likho, jese Subject: Application for leave. Subject wali line ko select karke Home tab se Bold kar do, taake parhne wale ko aik nazar me pata chal jaye ke darkhwast hai kis cheez ki.

Phir aik line chhor kar Respected Sir, ya Janab-e-Aala, likho. Phir asal body shuru hoti hai. Application ki body ke hamesha teen hisse hote hain, ye zehen me baitha lo. Pehle hisse me tum apna taaruf aur masla aik do jumlon me batate ho, jese main class 10 ka student hun aur mujhe bukhaar hai. Dusre hisse me tum apni asal guzarish likhte ho, jese meherbani karke mujhe do din ki chhutti di jaye. Teesre hisse me shukriya aur adab wala jumla ata hai, jese main aap ka shukarguzar rahunga. Bas, yahi teen hisse hain. Jo application in teen hisson me likhi ho wo kabhi nakaam nahi hoti.

Body ke baad aik line chhor kar Yours obediently, ya Aap ka farmanbardar, likho. Us ke neeche apna naam, phir class ya ohda, phir roll number ya mobile number jo munasib ho. Aik khaas baat. Apna naam hamesha body ke bilkul aakhir me ata hai, upar kabhi nahi. Upar sirf us ka naam hota hai jis ko application ja rahi hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Is hisse ki diagram me aik tayyar application ka safha dikhaya jaye, us par numbers lage hon — 1 page setup wali jagah, 2 To wali linein, 3 Subject wali line, 4 body ke teen hisse alag alag halke rangon me, 5 aakhir me naam wali jagah — taake student application ka poora dhancha aik nazar me dekh le.

Kaam 2: Formatting — likhai ko sajana

Khali likh lena kaafi nahi hota. Jo document saaf aur saja hua ho, parhne wala usay sanjeedgi se leta hai. Ab wohi application jo tum ne likhi hai, use sajate hain.

Pehle heading ko bara aur mota karo. Heading wali line ko select karne ka sab se asaan tareeqa ye hai ke line ke aakhir par click karke mouse ghaseet-te hue poori line ko neele rang me rang lo, isay select karna kehte hain. Ab Home tab ke Font group me size wali khirki se 14 ya 16 chun lo, aur sath wale B wale button se Bold kar do. Dekho, heading khari ho gayi. Yahi kaam shortcut se bhi hota hai, select karke Ctrl+B dabane se text bold ho jata hai.

Font badalna ho to usi Font group me font wali bari khirki se apni pasand ka font chuno. Urdu mix documents me aksar log Times New Roman pasand karte hain kyunke wo sanjeeda lagta hai. Aam daftar ke kaam me Calibri bhi theek hai. Asal baat ye hai ke poore document me aik hi font rakho, har paragraph ka alag font document ko bazaar ka ishtehaar bana deta hai.

Alignment set karo. Heading ko beech me lane ke liye use select karke Home tab ke Paragraph group me Center wala button dabao, ya Ctrl+E dabao. Body ka text aam tor par bayen taraf seedha rehta hai, yani Left alignment, Ctrl+L. Agar tum chahte ho ke text akhbaar ke column ki tarah dono taraf se seedha ho jaye to Justify kar do, Ctrl+J. Application aur report me justify bohat saaf lagta hai.

Ab line spacing. Do lines ke darmiyan ka faasla. Body ke saare text ko select karo, is ke liye Ctrl+A dabao, poora document select ho jayega. Phir Home tab ke Paragraph group me Line Spacing wale button se 1.5 chun lo. Dekho, lines khul gayin aur parhna asaan ho gaya. Daftar ki applications me 1.5 spacing hi pasand ki jati hai kyunke parhne wale ko darmiyan me likhne ki jaga bhi mil jati hai.

Pehle paragraph ko indent karna seekho. Indent matlab paragraph ki pehli line baqi lines se thora andar se shuru ho. Paragraph ke shuru me click karo aur keyboard par Tab wali key aik dafa dabao. Pehli line andar khisak jayegi. Ye chhoti si cheez document ko parha likha banati hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Is hisse ki diagram me Home tab ka ribbon bara karke dikhaya jaye, Font group aur Paragraph group par ghaira ho, aur neeche aik hi paragraph chaar shaklon me dikhaya jaye — baghair formatting ke, bold heading ke sath, 1.5 spacing ke sath, aur indent ke sath — taake har tabdeeli ka asar ankh se nazar ajaye.

Kaam 3: CV ya Resume banana

Ab woh kaam jo har naukri ke liye lazmi hai. CV woh kagaz hai jo tum se pehle daftar pohanchta hai, is liye ye sab se zyada mehnat wala kaam hai.

Nayi file kholo. Sab se upar apna naam sab se bare font me likho, size 20 ya 22, bold, center me. Us ke neeche chhote font me apna mobile number, email aur sheher likho. Yahi CV ka sarwaraq hai. Naam ke neeche wali line me apni degree ya pesha bhi likh sakte ho, jese B.A, Computer Operator.

Phir headings ki bari ati hai. CV me har hisse ki aik heading hoti hai aur tartib ye hoti hai. Pehle Objective, yani tum kya kaam dhund rahe ho, aik do lines me. Phir Education, yani taleem, sab se nayi degree sab se pehle. Phir Experience, yani kaam ka tajurba, agar hai to. Phir Skills, yani tumhe ata kya kya hai. Aur aakhir me Personal Information, yani walid ka naam, tareekh-e-paidaish, CNIC aur address.

Ab woh trick jo CV ko professional banati hai. Maloomat ko table me likho. Insert tab par jao, Table dabao aur do column wali table banao. Bayen column me labels likho, jese Name, Father Name, Date of Birth. Dayen column me un ke jawab likho. Table banane se har cheez seedhi line me rehti hai, kuch terha nahi hota. Phir table ki lines ghayab karni hain, kyunke CV me khanon wali lines achi nahi lagtin. Poora table select karo, upar Table Tools ke Design tab me Borders wale button se No Border chun lo. Lines gayab, lekin seedh aur tartib barkarar. Yahi professional CV ka raaz hai jo aksar logon ko nahi pata.

Education wale hisse me bhi table kaam ata hai. Wahan chaar column banao. Degree, Board ya University, Year, Marks ya Division. Har degree aik row me. Ye table lines ke sath hi rehne do, wahan lines achi lagti hain.

Aik aham mashwara. CV aik safhe ka ho to sab se behtar hai, do safhon se zyada kabhi na ho. Aur CV me jhoot kabhi mat likho, interview me pehla sawal CV se hi banta hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Is hisse ki diagram me aik mukammal tayyar CV ka safha dikhaya jaye, upar bara naam, neeche headings tartib se, aur aik hisse me woh table dikhayi jaye jis ki borders ghayab kar di gayi hon, sath teer se likha ho ke lines yahan se ghayab hoti hain.

Kaam 4: Table banana aur us me result likhna

Table banana tum CV me seekh chuke ho, ab us ka dusra mashhoor istemal seekho. Class ka result.

Nayi file me pehle heading likho, Result Sheet Class 10, bold aur center. Phir Insert tab se Table dabao. Ab socho ke kitne column chahiye. Roll Number, Name, English, Urdu, Maths, Total. Ye che column hue. Aur rows kitni? Pehli row headings ke liye, us ke baad har student ki aik row. Agar paanch student hain to kul che rows. Table wale khane me mouse ghuma kar 6 guna 6 wala hissa select karo, click karte hi table safhe par ajayegi.

Ab pehli row me headings likho. Pehle cell me click karo, Roll likho, phir Tab key dabao, cursor agle cell me chala jayega. Isi tarah saari headings aur phir saara data bhar do. Table me age barhne ke liye Tab key hi istemal hoti hai, mouse se har cell par click karne ki zaroorat nahi. Aur aik maza ki baat, aakhri cell me Tab dabane se nayi row khud ban jati hai. To agar student barh jayen to ghabrana nahi.

Headings wali row ko select karke bold kar do. Agar table safhe se bahar nikal raha ho to column ki line ko mouse se pakar kar andar khisko, column tang ho jayega. Table ko rang dena ho to Table Tools ke Design tab me tayyar styles hote hain, aik click me poora table saj jata hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Is hisse ki diagram me aik bhari hui result wali table dikhayi jaye, pehli row bold ho, aur sath Insert tab me table select karne wala grid bhi chhota sa dikhaya jaye, taake student ko yaad rahe ke table kahan se ata hai.

Kaam 5: List, page number aur header

Ab teen chhote lekin roz ke kaam aik sath seekho.

Pehla list banana. Farz karo tumhe zaroori kitabon ki list likhni hai. Har kitaab alag line par likho, saari linein select karo, phir Home tab ke Paragraph group me Bullets wala button dabao. Har line ke aage gola nishan ajayega. Agar number wali list chahiye, jese pehla dusra teesra, to us ke sath wala Numbering button dabao. Farq sirf itna hai ke bullets me tartib mayne nahi rakhti, numbering me rakhti hai. Dukaan ki khareedari ki list bullets me, kaam ke steps numbering me.

Dusra page number lagana. Insert tab par jao, Page Number wala button dabao. Wahan se chun lo ke number safhe ke upar chahiye ya neeche, aur neeche me bhi beech me ya kone me. Aam tor par neeche beech me lagta hai. Button dabate hi har safhe par number khud ajayega aur khud barhta jayega. Khud se har safhe par number type karna sab se bari bewaqoofi hai jo beginner karta hai, Word ye kaam khud karta hai.

Teesra header me apna naam lagana. Header safhe ke sab se upar wali woh jaga hai jahan likhi cheez har safhe par khud dohrai jati hai. Insert tab se Header dabao aur Blank chun lo. Ab jo khana khule wahan apna naam ya apne idaray ka naam likh do. Phir document ke beech me click kar do. Dekho, ab har safhe ke upar wohi naam ajayega. School ke notes aur lambay documents me ye bohat kaam ata hai. Neeche wali aisi hi jaga ko Footer kehte hain, page number aksar footer me hi lagta hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Is hisse ki diagram me aik safha dikhaya jaye jis ke upar header me naam likha ho, neeche footer me page number ho, aur beech me bullets wali list ho, teeno par number laga kar neeche naam likhe hon.

Kaam 6: Save karna, PDF banana aur print karna

Kaam kar liya, ab use mehfooz karna aur duniya tak pohanchana seekho. Ye hisa sab se aham hai kyunke yahin sab se zyada nuqsan hota hai.

Save karna. File tab me Save dabao ya seedha Ctrl+S dabao. Pehli dafa save karne par window khulegi. Wahan do cheezain theek karni hain. Pehle jagah, yani file kahan save ho, Documents wala folder chun lo. Dusri naam, wahan Document1 mita kar apna saaf naam likho, jese Ahmed CV ya Leave Application November. Naam aisa rakho ke kal ko dhundne par foran mil jaye. Kaam ke darmiyan har paanch minute baad Ctrl+S dabane ki aadat dal lo. Bijli jane par wohi rota hai jis ne save nahi kiya hota.

PDF banana. Daftar wale aksar kehte hain PDF bhej do, kyunke PDF har mobile aur har computer par aik jaisi khulti hai aur us me tabdeeli bhi asaan nahi hoti. Tareeqa ye hai. File tab me jao, Save As dabao, phir jahan file ki type wali list hai wahan se PDF chun lo aur Save kar do. Tumhari file ki aik PDF copy ban jayegi. Asal Word wali file apni jagah mehfooz rahegi. CV hamesha PDF me bhejna chahiye taake agle ke computer par us ki shakal na bigde.

Print karna. File tab me Print dabao ya Ctrl+P dabao. Samne print wali screen ajayegi, dayen taraf safhe ki jhalak yani preview nazar ayega. Pehle preview gaur se dekho, jo ghalti screen par nazar ajaye wo kagaz par nahi hogi. Agar saare safhe chahiye to kuch karne ki zaroorat nahi. Agar sirf chand safhe chahiye to Pages wali khirki me likh do, jese 1-3 likhne se pehle teen safhe chhapenge, aur 2,5 likhne se sirf dusra aur paanchwa safha. Copies wali khirki me batao ke kitni copies chahiye. Phir Print wala bara button dabao. Aik aur baat, print karne se pehle printer me kagaz aur toner hai ya nahi, ye dekh lena aadhi preshani khatam kar deta hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Is hisse ki diagram me print wali screen dikhayi jaye, preview par ghaira ho, Pages wali khirki me 1-3 likha nazar aye, aur sath Save As wali list me PDF chuna hua dikhaya jaye.

Kaam 7: Spelling check chala kar document saaf karna

Aakhri kaam, document ko bhejne se pehle us ki safai. Ghalat spelling wali application pehle hi minute me tumhari saakh gira deti hai.

Word tumhari madad khud karta hai. Jab tum koi lafz ghalat likhte ho to us ke neeche surkh rengti hui lakeer ajati hai. Us lafz par right click karo, Word tumhe sahi lafz ki peshkash karega, sahi wale par click kar do, lafz theek ho jayega. Neeli lakeer grammar ki ghalti par ati hai, wahan bhi right click se mashwara mil jata hai.

Poore document ki aik sath jaanch karni ho to keyboard se F7 dabao. Bayen taraf aik khana khul jayega jo aik aik karke har ghalti tumhare samne layega. Har ghalti par do raste honge. Agar Word ki peshkash sahi hai to us par click karke badal do. Aur agar lafz theek hai lekin Word use pehchanta nahi, jese Pakistani naamon ke sath aksar hota hai, to Ignore kar do, warna tumhara sahi naam bhi badal jayega. Ye bareeki khaas yaad rakho, spelling check andha saathi hai, faisla tum ne khud karna hai.

Saari ghaltiyan khatam hone par Word bata dega ke check mukammal ho gaya hai. Ab tumhara document bhejne ke qabil hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Is hisse ki diagram me aik jumla dikhaya jaye jis me aik lafz ke neeche surkh lakeer ho, us par right click wala menu khula ho aur sahi lafz par ghaira bana ho, sath F7 wali key bhi chhoti si dikhayi jaye.

Aam ghaltiyan jo beginner karta hai

Ab wo ghaltiyan sun lo jo main har naye student ko karte dekhta hun, taake tum wo na karo.

Pehli ghalti, save na karna. Ghanton ka kaam aik bijli ke jhatke me gaya. Ctrl+S tumhari sab se achi aadat hai.

Dusri ghalti, har safhe par page number khud type karna. Phir jab beech me aik safha barhta hai to saare number ghalat ho jate hain. Page number hamesha Insert tab se lagao.

Teesri ghalti, khali jaga banane ke liye space bar ko das dafa dabana. Is se likhai terhi ho jati hai. Jaga set karni hai to Tab ya alignment istemal karo.

Chauthi ghalti, har paragraph ka alag font aur alag size rakhna. Document rang biranga ho jata hai aur parhne wala sanjeedgi se nahi leta. Aik font, do ya teen sizes, bas.

Paanchvi ghalti, spelling check ki har peshkash ko ankh band karke maan lena. Naam aur Urdu ke alfaaz wahan aksar ghalat kar diye jate hain. Ignore wala button isy liye bana hai.

Chhati ghalti, file ka naam Document1 hi rehne dena. Phir das files me se apni file dhundna azaab ban jata hai. Naam hamesha kaam ke hisaab se rakho.

Khud kar ke dekho

Ab ye mashq khud karo, baghair kisi ki madad ke. Word kholo. Pehle aik application likho apne principal ko aik din ki chhutti ke liye, page A4 set karke, subject bold karke, body teen hisson me. Phir nayi file me apna CV banao, table ke sath, borders ghayab karke. Us ke baad usi CV wali file me neeche paanch kitabon ki bullets wali list banao. Phir file ko apne naam se save karo aur us ki PDF bhi banao. Aakhir me F7 chala kar saari spelling theek karo. Agar ye paanch kaam tum ne aik bethak me kar liye to samajh lo Word ki asal proficiency tumhare haath me agayi. Ab tum sirf paper ke sawal nahi, daftar ka kaam bhi kar sakte ho.
