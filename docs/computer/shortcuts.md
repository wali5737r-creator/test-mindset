Computer Course: Shortcut Keys ka Course
Ctrl wale shortcuts, F1 se F12 tak function keys, aur Windows wale shortcuts

Ye course kyun zaroori hai

One paper me shortcut keys ke sawal har saal ate hain, aur ye sawal un students ke liye muft ke number hote hain jinhon ne keys ko samajh kar yaad kiya ho. Ctrl+S kis kaam ka hai, F7 dabane se kya hota hai, Excel me F2 ka kya kaam hai — ye chhote chhote sawal hain lekin paper me yahi asli farq dalte hain, kyunke zyada tar students inhein parhe baghair ajate hain. Shortcut ka matlab hai mouse ghumane ke bajaye keyboard ki do teen keys aik sath daba kar kaam kar lena. Is course me hum keys ko teen hisson me parhen ge. Pehle Ctrl wale shortcuts jo har jagah chalte hain, phir F1 se F12 tak ki function keys jin ka har aik ka apna khaas kaam hai, aur aakhir me Alt aur Windows wale shortcuts. Har key ke sath ye bhi bataunga ke wo kis app me sab se zyada poochi jati hai, taake paper me sawal dekhte hi jawab zehen me ajaye.

Pehla hissa: Ctrl wale shortcuts

Ctrl key keyboard ke neeche dono taraf hoti hai, aur kisi dusri key ke sath mil kar ye jadoo karti hai. In shortcuts ki khaasiyat ye hai ke ye taqreeban har program me aik hi kaam karte hain, Word ho ya Excel ya browser. Main board par tartib se likh raha hun, har key ke sath us ka kaam samjho.

1. Ctrl+A ka matlab Select All hai. Poori file ka sara text ya sari cheezein aik sath chun li jati hain. Word aur Excel dono me ye bohat poocha jata hai.
2. Ctrl+B text ko Bold karta hai, yani mota aur gehra. MS Word ka sab se mashhoor sawal yahi hai.
3. Ctrl+I text ko Italic karta hai, yani terha jhuka hua. B ke baad I hi ata hai paper me.
4. Ctrl+U text ke neeche line lagata hai, yani Underline. Ye teeno, B, I aur U, hamesha aik sath yaad rakho.
5. Ctrl+C ka matlab Copy hai. Chuni hui cheez ki naqal bana kar yaad rakh li jati hai, asal cheez apni jagah rehti hai.
6. Ctrl+X ka matlab Cut hai. Cheez apni jagah se kat kar chali jati hai. Copy aur Cut ka farq paper me bohat ata hai. Copy me asal rehta hai, Cut me asal bhi uth jata hai.
7. Ctrl+V ka matlab Paste hai. Copy ya Cut ki hui cheez ko nayi jagah par chipka deta hai. Yaad rakhne ki baat ye hai ke Paste ke liye P nahi, V hai, kyunke P pehle hi Print ke liye makhsoos hai.
8. Ctrl+Z ka matlab Undo hai. Jo aakhri kaam kiya tha, use wapas le leta hai. Ghalti ho jaye to ye sab se pehle dabane wali key hai.
9. Ctrl+Y ka matlab Redo hai. Jo kaam Undo se wapas liya tha, use dobara kar deta hai. Z aur Y ko jora samajh kar yaad rakho, aik wapas, dusra dobara.
10. Ctrl+S ka matlab Save hai. File ko mehfooz kar lena. Ye one paper ka sab se zyada dohraaya jane wala sawal hai, har haal me pakka hona chahiye.
11. Ctrl+P ka matlab Print hai. File ko printer se chhapne ka hukum.
12. Ctrl+N nayi file banata hai, yani New. Word me nayi khaali document aur browser me nayi window khulti hai.
13. Ctrl+O pehle se bani hui file kholta hai, yani Open.
14. Ctrl+F ka matlab Find hai. Bari file me koi lafz dhundna ho to yahi dabate hain. Browser me bhi page ke andar lafz isi se milta hai.
15. Ctrl+H ka matlab Replace hai. Aik lafz ko dhund kar us ki jagah dusra lafz rakh deta hai. Word me ye Find ka bara bhai hai aur paper me aksar F ke sath mila kar poocha jata hai.
16. Ctrl+K hyperlink lagata hai. Word aur PowerPoint me kisi lafz ko kisi website se jorne ke liye yehi key hai.
17. Ctrl+L text ko left side par kar deta hai, Ctrl+E darmiyan yani center me lata hai, Ctrl+R right side par kar deta hai, aur Ctrl+J text ko dono taraf se barabar kar deta hai jise Justify kehte hain, akhbaar ke column ki tarah. Ye chaaron alignment wale shortcuts sirf Word me kaam karte hain aur paper me in ka jora bohat ata hai. E ko Center se jor kar yaad rakho, kyunke C pehle hi Copy ke pas hai.
18. Ctrl+Enter Word me page break dalta hai, yani likhai usi jagah se naye page par chali jati hai. Bar bar Enter dabane ke bajaye ye aik key kaafi hai.
19. Ctrl+Shift+barra wala nishan, yani greater than wali key, Word me chune hue text ka font size bara karti hai, aur Ctrl+Shift+chhota wala nishan font size chhota karta hai. Font size wale sawal me ye jora ata hai.
20. Ctrl+Home file ke bilkul shuru me le jata hai aur Ctrl+End file ke bilkul aakhir me. Ye do bhi Word me aksar pooche jate hain.

Dusra hissa: F1 se F12 tak function keys

Keyboard ki sab se upar wali line me F1 se F12 tak ki keys hoti hain, inhein function keys kehte hain. In ka masla ye hai ke har key ka kaam alag hai, aur kuch keys ka kaam alag alag program me badal bhi jata hai. Paper yahi par phasata hai, is liye har key ko us ke mashhoor kaam ke sath yaad karo.

1. F1 har program me Help ki key hai. Kahin phans jao to F1 dabao, madad wali screen khul jati hai. Ye sab se asaan aur sab se zyada poocha jane wala sawal hai.
2. F2 ke do mashhoor kaam hain. Windows me kisi file ya folder ka naam badalne yani Rename ke liye F2 dabate hain. Aur Excel me F2 dabane se cell ke andar likha hua badla ja sakta hai, yani cell edit hota hai. Paper me F2 aksar Excel ke hawale se ata hai.
3. F3 Windows me search aur Find ke kaam ati hai, aur Word me kuch jagah text dhundne me madad karti hai. Ye baqi keys ke muqable me kam poochi jati hai.
4. F4 ka apna seedha kaam pichle action ko dohrana hai, khaas tor par Word aur Excel me. Lekin is ki asal shohrat Alt ke sath hai. Alt+F4 dabane se jo program khula ho wo band ho jata hai, aur desktop par hon to computer band karne wali screen ati hai. Paper me F4 ka sawal taqreeban hamesha Alt+F4 ki shakal me ata hai.
5. F5 ke do bare kaam hain aur yahi is ka jaal hai. Browser aur Windows me F5 ka matlab Refresh hai, page dobara load ho jata hai. Lekin PowerPoint me F5 dabane se slideshow shuru se shuru ho jata hai. Paper me jab F5 PowerPoint ke sath aye to jawab slideshow hai, aur browser ke sath aye to refresh hai. Sawal ko gaur se parhna, yahi is key ka asal imtihan hai.
6. F6 aik hisse se dusre hisse me jane ke kaam ati hai, jese browser me address bar par jana. Ye bohat kam poochi jati hai, bas pehchan ke tor par yaad rakho.
7. F7 MS Word aur baqi Office programs me spelling aur grammar ki ghaltiyan check karti hai. Ye one paper ka fix sawal hai. Spelling check wali key poochi jaye to jawab hamesha F7 hai.
8. F8 ke bhi do roop hain. Computer start hote waqt F8 dabane se Safe Mode khulta hai, ye Windows wala purana aur mashhoor sawal hai. Word aur Excel me F8 selection ko barhane ke kaam bhi ata hai, lekin paper me Safe Mode wala matlab hi ata hai.
9. F9 Excel me tamam formulas ko dobara calculate karti hai. Word me ye fields ko update karti hai. Excel ke paper sawal me F9 ka yahi hisaab wala kaam ata hai.
10. F10 program ki menu bar ko active karti hai, yani upar wale menu khulne ke liye tayyar ho jate hain. Ye bhi kam poochi jati hai.
11. F11 ke do kaam yaad rakho. Browser me F11 dabane se full screen ho jati hai, upar neeche ki pattiyan ghayab ho jati hain. Aur Excel me F11 dabane se chune hue data ka chart aik alag nayi sheet par ban jata hai. Paper me F11 Excel ke chart wale kaam ke sath mashhoor hai.
12. F12 MS Office me Save As kholti hai, yani file ko naye naam ya nayi jagah par save karna. Gaur karo, Ctrl+S seedha save karta hai aur F12 Save As kholti hai. Ye farq paper me bohat pyar se poocha jata hai.

Teesra hissa: Alt aur Windows wale shortcuts

Ab wo shortcuts jo pure computer ko chalane me kaam ate hain, kisi aik program ke nahi. Ye Windows wale sawal hain aur one paper me in ka apna hissa hota hai.

1. Alt+Tab khule hue programs ke darmiyan switch karti hai. Do teen program khule hon to mouse ke baghair un ke darmiyan ana jana isi se hota hai. Ye is hisse ka sab se mashhoor sawal hai.
2. Alt+F4 jo program samne khula hai use band kar deti hai. Desktop par dabao to shutdown wali screen ati hai. Upar F4 wale hisse me bhi ye baat ai thi, yahan phir dohra raha hun kyunke paper me ye sab se zyada ata hai.
3. Windows+D seedha desktop dikha deti hai, tamam khuli windows neeche ho jati hain. D ko Desktop se jor kar yaad rakho.
4. Windows+E File Explorer kholti hai, jahan tamam files aur folders milte hain. E ko Explorer se joro.
5. Windows+L computer ko lock kar deti hai. Apni jagah se uthne se pehle ye dabana achi aadat bhi hai aur paper ka sawal bhi. L ko Lock se joro.
6. Print Screen poori screen ki tasveer le leti hai, phir use Paint ya Word me Ctrl+V se chipkaya ja sakta hai. Laptop par ye key aksar PrtSc likhi hoti hai.
7. Ctrl+Shift+Esc seedha Task Manager kholti hai. Jo program ruk jaye ya hang ho jaye, use yahin se band karte hain.
8. Ctrl+Alt+Delete security wali screen lata hai, jahan se Task Manager, lock aur sign out ke options milte hain. Ye teeno keys aik sath dabti hain aur purane zamane se computer ko dobara chalane ka mashhoor tareeqa rahi hain. Farq yaad rakho. Task Manager seedha chahiye to Ctrl+Shift+Esc hai, aur bari security screen chahiye to Ctrl+Alt+Delete hai.

Aakhir me: paper ke sab se zyada dohraye jane wale 15 shortcuts

Ab wo fehrist jo paper se aik raat pehle bhi parh lo to kaam ban jaye. Ye pandrah shortcuts one paper me ghum phir kar ate rehte hain.

1. Ctrl+S file save karta hai.
2. Ctrl+P print karta hai.
3. Ctrl+C copy, Ctrl+X cut aur Ctrl+V paste karta hai.
4. Ctrl+Z undo aur Ctrl+Y redo hai.
5. Ctrl+A sab kuch select karta hai.
6. Ctrl+B bold, Ctrl+I italic aur Ctrl+U underline hai.
7. Ctrl+F find aur Ctrl+H replace hai.
8. Ctrl+N nayi file aur Ctrl+O purani file kholta hai.
9. F1 help ki key hai.
10. F2 Excel me cell edit aur Windows me rename hai.
11. F5 browser me refresh aur PowerPoint me slideshow hai.
12. F7 spelling aur grammar check hai.
13. F12 Save As kholti hai.
14. Alt+Tab programs ke darmiyan switch hai aur Alt+F4 program band karti hai.
15. Ctrl+Shift+Esc Task Manager kholti hai.

Aik aakhri baat zehen me rakho. Shortcut wale sawal me paper hamesha program ka naam de kar phasata hai. F5 ka jawab browser me aur hai aur PowerPoint me aur. Is liye sawal me pehle program ka naam dekho, phir key ka kaam batao. Ye aik aadat is pure hisse ke number pakke kar degi.
