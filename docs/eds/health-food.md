Khurak, Vitamins aur Bimariyan
Jo khana tum khate ho wo jism ko kya deta hai, aur kami ho to kya hota hai

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

Everyday Science me khurak aur sehat par har saal sawal ata hai. Konsa vitamin aankhon ke liye hai, scurvy kis vitamin ki kami se hoti hai, dengue konsa machhar phailata hai, antibiotic virus par kyun kaam nahi karta — ye sab paper ke fix sawal hain. Lekin student inhe ratta laga kar bhool jata hai, kyunke vitamin naam ki cheez na us ne dekhi hai, na wo janta hai ke ye asal me hota kya hai aur milta kahan se hai.

Is lesson me hum har vitamin ko us ke khane ke sath jor kar dekhen ge. Jab tum zehen me gajar dekho ge to vitamin A yaad ayega, malta dekho ge to vitamin C. Phir bimariyon ko samjhen ge — pehle ye ke bacteria aur virus me farq kya hai, kyunke aadhe sawal wahi se bante hain. Tasveer zehen me hogi to paper me naam dekh kar jawab khud nikal ayega.

Vitamin kya hai, pehle ye samajh lo

Khana jism ko do tarah ki cheezain deta hai. Aik wo jo taqat deti hain aur jism banati hain — roti, chaawal, gosht, daal. Dusri wo bareek cheezain jo miqdar me bohat kam chahiye lekin un ke baghair jism ki machine sahi chalti nahi. Inhi bareek cheezon ko vitamin kehte hain. Inhe angrezi ke hurf se naam mile hain — A, B, C, D, E, K. Har vitamin ka apna aik khaas kaam hai, aur jis vitamin ki kami ho, usi kaam wali jagah par bimari nazar ati hai. Ye usool yaad rakho: kami wahi takleef degi jahan kaam us vitamin ka tha.

Vitamin A — aankhon wala vitamin

Zehen me gajar lao. Wahi lambi, nargisi rang ki gajar. Vitamin A ka sab se mashhoor zariya yehi hai. Is ke ilawa anday ki zardi, doodh, makhan, kaleji aur hare patton wali sabziyan is se bhari hain. Vitamin A ka asal kaam aankhon se hai. Ye roshni ko mehsoos karne wali taqat ko barqarar rakhta hai.

Is ki kami se sab se pehle jo bimari hoti hai wo hai shab-kori, jisay angrezi me night blindness kehte hain. Shab-kori ka matlab hai ke banday ko din me to nazar ata hai, lekin sham hote hi, kam roshni me, usay dikhai dena band ho jata hai. Socho, jese hi andhera hua, aankh andhi. Agar kami aur barh jaye to aankhain khushk ho kar asal me kharab bhi ho sakti hain, aur yehi vitamin ki kami duniya me bachon ke andhe hone ki bari wajah hai. Paper me sawal yun ata hai — raat ko nazar na ana kis vitamin ki kami hai? Jawab vitamin A. Sath ye bhi pakka kar lo ke vitamin A chamri aur jism ki jaraseem se larne wali taqat ke liye bhi zaroori hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Aik meiz par gajar, anda, doodh ka glass aur hari sabziyan dikhayi jayen, un ke upar vitamin A ka label ho, aur sath me aik aankh ki tasveer ho jis ke aadhe hisse par din ki roshni aur aadhe par andhera dikhaya jaye taake shab-kori ka matlab aik nazar me samajh aye.

Vitamin B group — taqat aur aasab wala khandaaan

Vitamin B aik akela vitamin nahi, ye poora khandaan hai. Is khandaan ke bohat se afraad hain, is liye inhe B complex kehte hain. Ye gandum, chaawal ke chilke, daalon, anday, gosht aur doodh me milte hain. In ka kaam khane ko taqat me badalne me madad karna aur aasab, yani nerves, ko sahi rakhna hai.

Is khandaan ka sab se mashhoor banda vitamin B1 hai, kyunke paper isi par phansta hai. B1 ki kami se beriberi naam ki bimari hoti hai. Beriberi me taangain kamzor ho jati hain, sun si ho jati hain aur jism phoolne lagta hai. Ye bimari purane zamane me un ilaqon me bohat hui jahan log sirf safaid polish wale chaawal khate the, kyunke chaawal ki polish ke sath us ka chilka utar jata tha aur chilke me hi B1 hota tha. Kahani samajh aa gayi to fact nahi bhoole ga: chilka gaya, vitamin gaya, beriberi ayi. Paper me sawal seedha ata hai — beriberi kis vitamin ki kami se hoti hai? Jawab B1.

Tasveer ka khayal: Do chaawalon ki tasveer sath sath ho — aik chilke wala bhoora chaawal, aik chamakta safaid polish wala — aur safaid chaawal ke neeche nishan ho ke polish me B1 nikal gaya. Sath me B1 aur beriberi ka label wali aik taang ki tasveer.

Vitamin C — zakham bharne wala, malte wala vitamin

Ab zehen me malta lao, nimbu lao, amla lao. Jo bhi khatti cheez hai, us me vitamin C hai. Amla in sab ka sardar hai, is me malte se kayi guna zyada vitamin C hota hai. Is ke ilawa amrood, tamatar aur hari mirch bhi ache zariye hain. Vitamin C ka kaam jism ke jorne wale hisse ko mazboot rakhna hai — chamri, masoorhe, khoon ki naliyan. Ye zakham bharne me madad karta hai aur jaraseem se larne wali taqat ko bhi barhata hai.

Is ki kami se scurvy hoti hai, aur scurvy ki pehchan bari wazeh hai — masoorhon se khoon ana. Daant dhote hue masoorhe khoon dene lagte hain, daant hilne lagte hain, purane zakham dobara khul jate hain. Purane zamane me ye samandari jahazon ke malahon ki bimari thi. Maheeno samandar me rehne walon ko taza phal sabzi nahi milti thi, to un ke masoorhe khoon dene lagte the. Phir pata chala ke nimbu khilane se ye theek ho jati hai. Paper me sawal bilkul yun banta hai — masoorhon se khoon ana aur scurvy kis vitamin ki kami hai? Jawab vitamin C. Aik bareeki aur: vitamin C pani me ghul jata hai aur garam karne se kharab ho jata hai, is liye ye roz khana parta hai, jism isay jama nahi rakhta.

Tasveer ka khayal: Malta, nimbu, amla aur amrood aik sath dikhayen, un par vitamin C ka label ho. Sath me masoorhe ki aik saaf tasveer ho jis me khoon ane wali jagah par nishan ho taake scurvy ki pehchan zehen me beth jaye.

Vitamin D — sooraj ki roshni wala vitamin

Ye vitamin sab se alag hai, kyunke ye sirf khane se nahi milta. Jab sooraj ki roshni hamari chamri par parti hai to jism khud vitamin D banata hai. Isi liye isay sooraj ki roshni wala vitamin kehte hain. Khane me ye anday ki zardi, machli aur doodh me milta hai. Vitamin D ka asal kaam haddiyon se hai. Ye khane me maujood calcium ko jism me jazb karne me madad karta hai. Calcium ho lekin vitamin D na ho to calcium haddiyon tak pohnchta hi nahi.

Is ki kami se bachon me rickets hoti hai. Rickets me bache ki taang ki haddiyan naram reh jati hain aur jism ke wazan se terhi ho jati hain — taangain bahar ki taraf mur jati hain, bilkul kaman ki tarah. Tasveer zehen me bana lo: naram haddi, upar jism ka wazan, haddi jhuk gayi. Badon me bhi is ki kami se haddiyan naram aur kamzor ho jati hain. Paper ke do sawal fix hain — konsa vitamin sooraj ki roshni se banta hai, aur rickets kis vitamin ki kami hai. Dono ka jawab vitamin D hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Aik taraf chamri par parti sooraj ki kirnain dikhayi jayen jin se vitamin D banta hua dikhe, dusri taraf aik bache ki terhi taangon wali tasveer ho jis par rickets ka label ho. Dono tasveeron ko teer se jora jaye taake kami aur bimari ka rishta saaf nazar aye.

Vitamin E aur Vitamin K — do chhote lekin lazmi naam

Vitamin E jism ke khaliyon ko kharab hone se bachata hai aur chamri aur tolidi sehat ke liye zaroori hai. Ye sabzi ke telon, giri dar mevon, anday aur hare patton wali sabziyon me milta hai. Is ki kami bohat kam hoti hai, is liye paper me ye kam ata hai, bas naam aur kaam yaad kar lo.

Vitamin K ka kaam sirf aik hai, lekin wo aik kaam zindagi bachata hai — khoon ko jamana. Pichle lesson me hum ne parha tha ke platelets khoon jamaate hain, un ki madad yehi vitamin karta hai. Is ki kami ho to chot par khoon behta rehta hai, rukta nahi. Ye hari sabziyon me milta hai, aur hamari aanton me rehne wale ache jaraseem bhi isay banate hain. Paper me sawal ata hai — khoon ke jamne ke liye konsa vitamin zaroori hai? Jawab vitamin K. K ko khoon ke jamne, yani clotting, ke sath jor kar yaad rakho.

Tasveer ka khayal: Aik ungli par chhota sa zakham dikhaya jaye jis par khoon jam kar ruk gaya ho, aur jamne wali jagah par vitamin K ka label ho. Sath me hari sabziyon ki chhoti tasveer ho.

Minerals — haddi, khoon aur gale ke teen naam

Vitamins ke ilawa khane se kuch aur bareek cheezain milti hain jinhe minerals kehte hain. In me se teen paper ke sardar hain.

Pehla calcium. Zehen me doodh ka glass lao, dahi lao, paneer lao. Calcium haddiyon aur daanton ki asal cheez hai — haddi asal me calcium ka dhancha hai. Is ki kami se haddiyan kamzor aur bhurbhuri ho jati hain, badon me zara si chot se toot jati hain. Yahan pichli baat jor lo: calcium akele kaam nahi karta, usay haddi tak pohnchane wala vitamin D hai. Dono ka jora paper me sath sath ata hai.

Dusra iron, yani loha. Is ka kaam khoon se hai. Pichle lesson me hum ne hemoglobin parha tha — wahi jo oxygen le jata hai, wo iron ke baghair banta hi nahi. Iron ki kami se khoon kam ho jata hai, is halat ko anemia kehte hain. Anemia wale banday ka rang peela par jata hai, thakan rehti hai, saans phoolta hai, kyunke khoon me oxygen le jane wale hi kam reh gaye. Iron gosht, kaleji, anday, daalon aur palak jaisi hari sabziyon me milta hai.

Teesra iodine. Ye miqdar me sab se kam chahiye, lekin is ki kami sab se zyada nazar ati hai. Iodine gale me maujood aik ghudood ke liye zaroori hai. Is ki kami se wo ghudood phool jata hai aur gale ke samne aik gilti si ban jati hai — isay goiter, Urdu me galgand, kehte hain. Pahari ilaqon me jahan ki mitti aur pani me iodine kam hoti hai, ye bimari aam thi. Isi liye aaj kal namak me iodine milaya jata hai. Zehen me tasveer bana lo: namak ki theli par likha "iodized" aur gale ki pholi hui gilti — dono aik hi kahani ke do siray hain. Paper me sawal ata hai — goiter kis cheez ki kami se hota hai? Jawab iodine.

Tasveer ka khayal: Teen khane alag alag dikhayen — doodh ke sath haddi ki tasveer aur calcium ka label, palak aur kaleji ke sath khoon ki nali aur iron ka label, aur iodized namak ki theli ke sath gale ki tasveer aur iodine ka label. Har khane ko us ke kaam wale hisse se teer jora jaye.

Ab bimariyan — pehle bacteria aur virus ka farq

Bimariyon ki list se pehle wo farq samajh lo jis par aadha paper khara hai: bacteria aur virus me farq kya hai?

Bacteria aik zinda cheez hai. Ye aik khaliye wali nihayat chhoti makhlooq hai — itni chhoti ke aam aankh se nazar nahi ati, lekin zinda hai, khati hai, barhti hai aur apni taqat par jism se bahar bhi zinda reh sakti hai. Virus us se bhi chhota hai, aur asal farq ye hai ke virus apne tor par zinda cheez nahi hai. Ye jism ke zinda khaliye ke andar ghus kar hi barh sakta hai, bahar ye aik bejaan tukra para rehta hai.

Ab paper ka sab se pyara phanda sun lo. Antibiotic dawai bacteria ko marti hai, lekin virus par antibiotic ka koi asar nahi hota. Kyun? Kyunke antibiotic bacteria ki zindagi wali cheezon ko torta hai, aur virus me wo zindagi wali cheezain hoti hi nahi — wo to kisi aur ke khaliye me chhupa hota hai. Isi liye aam zukam aur flu, jo virus se hote hain, antibiotic se theek nahi hote, aur doctor bila wajah antibiotic nahi deta. Sawal paper me bilkul yun ata hai — antibiotic kis par asar nahi karti? Jawab virus. Ye line pakki kar lo, phir har bimari ko is farq se tolna asaan ho jayega.

Tasveer ka khayal: Do tasveerain sath sath hon — aik taraf gol aur dandi jaisi shaklon wale bacteria jo apne tor par khare dikhaye jayen, dusri taraf us se bhi chhota virus jo aik jism ke khaliye ke andar ghusta hua dikhaya jaye. Beech me aik dawai ki goli dikhayi jaye jo bacteria par to cross lagaye lekin virus par sawal ka nishan ho.

Mashhoor bimariyan aur un ki wajah

Ab har bimari ko us ki pehchan ke sath yaad karte hain — wajah kya hai, phailti kesay hai, aur paper us me se kya poochta hai.

TB, yani tap-e-diq. Ye bacteria se hoti hai. Ye aam tor par phuphron ko kharab karti hai. Lambi khansi, wazan ghatna, sham ko halka bukhar — ye is ki nishaniyan hain. Ye mareez ke khansne se hawa ke zariye phailti hai. Paper me sawal: TB kis se hoti hai? Jawab bacteria.

Polio. Ye virus se hoti hai aur chhote bachon par hamla karti hai. Ye aasab ko kharab kar ke taang ya bazu ko hamesha ke liye kamzor ya mazoor kar deti hai. Is se bachao ke qatray bachon ko pilaye jate hain — polio ke qatray asal me vaccine hai jo munh se di jati hai. Pakistan me polio ke khatme ki muhim isi liye chalti hai. Paper me: polio virus se hoti hai, aur qatray vaccine hain.

Dengue. Ye virus se hoti hai lekin phailata machhar hai — Aedes naam ka machhar, jo saaf khare pani me palta hai aur din ke waqt kaatta hai. Dengue me tez bukhar hota hai aur khoon ke platelets girne lagte hain. Yaad hai platelets khoon jamaate hain? Wahi girte hain, is liye dengue khatarnak ho sakta hai. Paper ke do fix nukta: dengue virus hai, aur phailane wala machhar Aedes hai.

Malaria. Ye na bacteria hai na virus — ye Plasmodium naam ke aik chhote parasite se hota hai, aur isay phailata hai Anopheles machhar ki mada. Malaria me bukhar kaanp kaanp kar charhta hai aur paseena aa kar utar jata hai, ye silsila har dusre teesre din dohraya jata hai. Purane zamane se is ki dawai quinine hai, jo aik darakht ki chaal se banti thi. Yahan wo farq pakka kar lo jo paper me sab ko phansta hai — dengue wala machhar Aedes hai, malaria wala Anopheles hai. Aur malaria ki wajah Plasmodium hai, machhar sirf usay le jane wala hai.

Hepatitis. Ye jigar ki bimari hai aur virus se hoti hai. Jigar kharab ho to aankhain aur chamri peeli parne lagti hai, is halat ko peelia bhi kehte hain. Is ki chand qismein hain — kuch gande pani aur khane se phailti hain, kuch khoon ke zariye. Paper me: hepatitis jigar ki bimari hai aur virus se hoti hai.

Rabies. Ye pagal kutte ke kaatne se hone wala virus hai. Ye dimaagh par hamla karta hai aur nishaniyan anay ke baad ye taqreeban hamesha jaan lewa hoti hai. Isi liye kutte ke kaatne par foran zakham dhona aur teekay lagwana lazmi hai — intezar nahi karna. Paper me: rabies virus hai aur kutte ke kaatne se hoti hai.

Cholera, Urdu me haiza. Ye bacteria se hoti hai aur gande pani se phailti hai. Is me shadeed dast lagte hain aur jism se pani itni tezi se nikalta hai ke banda ghanton me kamzor ho jata hai. Is ka ilaj asal me khoya hua pani wapas dena hai — ORS wala ghol isiliye diya jata hai. Paper me: cholera bacteria hai, zariya ganda pani.

Typhoid. Ye bhi bacteria se hota hai aur gande pani aur khane se phailta hai. Is me bukhar ahista ahista barhta hai aur kayi din rehta hai. Paper me cholera aur typhoid aksar sath rakh kar pooche jate hain — dono bacteria, dono gande pani se.

Tasveer ka khayal: Do machharon ki alag alag tasveerain hon — Aedes par dengue ka label, Anopheles par malaria ka label — taake dono ka farq tasveer se zehen me beth jaye. Sath me aik chhoti list ki jagah har bimari ke sath us ki wajah wala chhota nishan ho: TB bacteria, polio virus, hepatitis virus aur jigar, rabies kutta, cholera ganda pani.

Vaccine kya hai aur kaam kesay karti hai

Polio ke qatray, bachpan ke teekay — ye sab vaccine hai. Lekin vaccine hoti kya hai, ye samajh lo to aadhe sawal wahi hal ho jayen ge.

Zehen me aik sipahion ki fauj ki tasveer banao jo asal jang se pehle mashq karti hai. Vaccine jism ke liye wahi mashq hai. Vaccine me asal bimari ke jaraseem ko kamzor kar ke, ya maar kar, jism me dala jata hai. Ye kamzor jaraseem bimari nahi kar sakte, lekin jism ke sipahi, yani WBC, inhe dekh kar pehchan lete hain aur in se larne ka tareeqa seekh lete hain. Phir jab asal taqatwar jaraseem kabhi hamla kare to sipahi pehle se tayyar hote hain — pehchan bhi mojood, larai ka tareeqa bhi. Bimari honay se pehle hi khatam ho jati hai.

Yahi wajah hai ke vaccine bimari honay se PEHLE lagayi jati hai, ilaj ke tor par nahi, bachao ke tor par. Aur ye bhi samajh lo ke vaccine virus wali bimariyon se bhi bachati hai, jahan antibiotic bekaar tha — kyunke ye dawai nahi, jism ki apni tayyari hai. Paper me sawal ata hai — vaccine ka kaam kya hai? Jawab: jism me bimari se pehle hi us se larne ki taqat, yani immunity, paida karna.

Tasveer ka khayal: Aik chhoti kahani wali tasveer teen hisso me ho — pehle kamzor jaraseem jism me jata hua, phir safed khaliye sipahi usay dekh kar seekhte hue, aur aakhir me asal jaraseem ke hamle par wahi sipahi foran usay khatam karte hue. Har hisse par aik chhota label ho.

Aakhir me safai — sab se sasti dawai

Itni saari bimariyon ke baad aik baat ghor se sun lo. Cholera, typhoid, hepatitis ki kuch qismein, polio — ye sab munh se jism me jati hain, aur munh tak pohnchne ka sab se aam rasta hamare haath hain. Haath din bhar har cheez ko chhoote hain, jaraseem haathon par lagte hain, aur khana khate waqt wahi haath munh ko lagte hain. Khane se pehle aur washroom ke baad sabun se haath dhona is poori zanjeer ko beech se kaat deta hai. Saaf pani peena dusri deewar hai, kyunke ganda pani aadhi bimariyon ka rasta hai.

Paper me sawal ghum kar ata hai — konsi bimariyan gande pani se phailti hain? Ab tum list ratte baghair nikaal sakte ho: cholera, typhoid, hepatitis ki pani wali qismein, polio. Tasveer wahi hai — ganda pani, us me jaraseem, aur munh tak ka rasta. Rasta band kar do, bimari ruk jayegi.

Tasveer ka khayal: Aik taraf gande pani ka glass jaraseem ke nishanon ke sath dikhaya jaye, dusri taraf sabun se haath dhota hua banda, aur beech me aik kati hui zanjeer ka nishan ho jo dikhaye ke safai ne bimari ka rasta kaat diya.

Aakhir me fix facts aik nazar me

Ab wo baatein jo paper me dohrayi jati hain, aik jagah jama kar lo. Vitamin A aankhon ke liye, kami se shab-kori. B1 ki kami se beriberi. Vitamin C ki kami se scurvy aur masoorhon se khoon. Vitamin D sooraj se banta hai, kami se rickets. Vitamin K khoon jamaata hai. Calcium haddi aur daant, iron khoon aur hemoglobin, iodine ki kami se goiter. Antibiotic virus par asar nahi karti. TB aur cholera aur typhoid bacteria se. Polio, dengue, hepatitis aur rabies virus se. Dengue Aedes machhar se, malaria Anopheles machhar se aur us ki wajah Plasmodium hai. Dengue me platelets girte hain. Vaccine bimari se pehle jism me immunity banati hai. In me se har line ke peeche jo tasveer hum ne banayi hai, usay zehen me lao — jawab ratta nahi marega, tasveer bole gi.
