Insani Jism — Andar ki Duniya
Apne hi jism ke woh hisse jo tum ne kabhi dekhe nahi, lekin paper har saal poochta hai

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

Everyday Science ke paper me insani jism par sawal lazmi ata hai. Kabhi poocha jata hai ke jism ki sab se lambi haddi konsi hai, kabhi ke khoon ko lal rang kis cheez se milta hai, kabhi ke universal donor konsa group hai. Masla ye hai ke ye cheezain tum ne kabhi apni aankh se dekhi hi nahi. Dil kaisa dikhta hai, gurda andar se kaisa hota hai, ye sab kitaab ke alfaaz ban kar reh jate hain, aur jo cheez zehen me banti hi nahi wo yaad bhi nahi rehti.

Is lesson me hum har uzu ko pehle dekhen ge. Main alfaaz se us ki tasveer banaunga — us ki shakal, us ka size, us ka rang, wo jism me kahan para hai. Phir samjhen ge ke wo kaam kesay karta hai. Aur aakhir me wo facts bataunga jo paper me dohraye jate hain. Jab tasveer zehen me ban jayegi to fact khud ba khud us tasveer ke sath chipak jayega, aur paper me sawal dekh kar tumhe kitaab nahi, apna jism yaad ayega.

Dil — jism ka pump

Pehle dil ko dekho. Apni mutthi band karo. Bas, tumhara dil takreeban itna hi bara hai, aur wazan me takreeban 300 gram. Ye tumhari chhati me, dono phuphron ke darmiyan, zara sa bayein taraf jhuka hua hai. Upar se ye chaura hai aur neeche se nokdaar, bilkul ulta latka hua aik mazboot sa thela. Ye gosht ka bana hai, lekin aam gosht jaisa nahi — ye wo khaas patha hai jo kabhi thakta nahi, kyunke tum so jao to bhi ye saari raat chalta rehta hai.

Ab isay beech se kaat kar dekho to andar chaar khane nazar ayen ge. Do upar wale chhote khane, jinhe atria kehte hain, aur do neeche wale bare aur mote khane, jinhe ventricles kehte hain. Beech me aik deewar hai jo dayein aur bayein hisse ko bilkul alag rakhti hai. Is deewar ka kaam samajh lo to aadha lesson yahin khatam ho jata hai. Dayein taraf wala hissa poore jism se istimal shuda, ganda khoon wasool karta hai — ganda se murad wo khoon jis ki oxygen khatam ho chuki hai. Dil is gande khoon ko phuphron me bhejta hai, wahan is me nayi oxygen bharti hai, aur saaf khoon bayein taraf wale hisse me wapas aata hai. Phir dil zor se dabta hai aur is saaf khoon ko poore jism me dhakel deta hai. Yani dil aik hi waqt me do kaam kar raha hai — aik taraf ganda khoon andar le raha hai, dusri taraf saaf khoon bahar bhej raha hai, aur beech wali deewar dono ko milne nahi deti.

Dil aik minute me takreeban 72 dafa dharakta hai. Ye number paper ka sab se pyara number hai, pakka yaad kar lo. Dil ki dharkan asal me is dabne aur khulne ki awaaz hai. Doctor jo aala seene par laga kar sunta hai, wo yehi sun raha hota hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Dil ki aik bari tasveer ho jo beech se kati hui dikhai de. Dayein hisse ko halkay neele rang se aur bayein hisse ko lal rang se rangna hai taake gande aur saaf khoon ka farq aik nazar me samajh aye. Chaaron khanon par label hon — right atrium, left atrium, right ventricle, left ventricle — aur beech wali deewar par bhi nishan ho. Dil ke upar aur neeche jane wali bari naliyon par teer ke nishan hon jo khoon ke behne ki simt dikhayen.

Khoon — jism ki delivery service

Khoon ko hum aik lal rang ka pani samajhte hain, lekin asal me khoon aik poora shehar hai jo naliyon me safar kar raha hai. Aik baligh insaan ke jism me takreeban 5 litre khoon hota hai. Is khoon me aik halkay peele rang ka pani sa hissa hai jise plasma kehte hain, aur us pani me teen kisam ke chhote chhote khaliye teir rahe hain. Ye khaliye itne chhote hain ke aam aankh se nazar nahi ate, lekin main tumhe bata deta hun ke wo dikhte kesay hain.

Pehle lal khaliye, jinhe red blood cells ya RBC kehte hain. Ye chhoti si gol plate jaisi shakal ke hain, beech se zara se dabe hue, bilkul chhoti si tikki ki tarah. In ki tadaad sab se zyada hai, lakhs aur karoron me. Khoon ka lal rang inhi ki wajah se hai, aur in ke andar aik cheez hai jise hemoglobin kehte hain. Hemoglobin hi asal me oxygen ko pakar kar jism ke har hisse tak le jata hai. Paper me sawal bilkul yun ata hai — khoon ka lal rang kis ki wajah se hai? Jawab hemoglobin hai. Ye aik line pakki kar lo.

Dusre safed khaliye, white blood cells ya WBC. Ye RBC se bare hote hain aur tadaad me kam. Inhe jism ke sipahi samajh lo. Jab koi jaraseem jism me ghusta hai to ye sipahi us par hamla karte hain aur usay khatam karte hain. Bimari me doctor khoon test karwa kar inhi ki tadaad dekhta hai, kyunke larai zoron par ho to sipahi barh jate hain.

Teesre platelets. Ye sab se chhote tukre hain aur in ka kaam hai khoon ko jamana. Jab kahin chot lagti hai aur khoon behne lagta hai to platelets wahan ja kar jaali si bana lete hain, khoon jam jata hai aur behna ruk jata hai. Agar platelets kam ho jayen to chhoti si chot par bhi khoon rukta nahi.

Ab khoon ke groups. Har insaan ke khoon ka aik group hota hai — A, B, AB ya O. Aur har group ke sath plus ya minus bhi lagta hai. Is me paper ke do fix sawal hain aur dono ko ghalat karna sab se aam ghalti hai. Pehla: universal donor, yani wo group jo har kisi ko khoon de sakta hai — ye hai O negative. Dusra: universal recipient, yani wo shakhs jo har group ka khoon le sakta hai — ye hai AB positive. Donor dene wala hai, recipient lene wala. Lafz par ghor karo, sawal hal ho jayega.

Tasveer ka khayal: Aik nali ke andar khoon behta hua dikhaya jaye jis me teen kisam ke khaliye bare bare nazar ayen — lal tikki jaisi shakal wale RBC, un se bara gol safed WBC, aur chhote tukre platelets. Teeno par label hon. Neeche khoon ke chaar groupon ke chaar khane hon aur O negative par "universal donor" aur AB positive par "universal recipient" ka khaas nishan ho.

Aankh — jism ka camera

Aankh ko bahar se hum bas aik gol cheez dekhte hain, lekin is ki banawat bilkul camera jaisi hai. Aankh aik gol gaind jaisi hai, aur is ke bilkul samne aik saaf sheshay jaisa ubhra hua hissa hai. Us ke peeche wo gol rang wala hissa hai jise iris kehte hain. Kisi ki aankhain kaali, kisi ki bhoori, kisi ki neeli — ye rang iris ka hi hai. Iris ke beech me wo kaala gol surakh hai jise putli, yani pupil, kehte hain. Ye putli chhoti bari hoti rehti hai. Tez roshni me ye sikur jati hai taake andar kam roshni jaye, aur andhere me ye phail jati hai taake zyada roshni andar ja sake. Bilkul camera ke shutter ki tarah.

Putli ke peeche lens hai — aik saaf, naram, ubhra hua shesha. Ye lens bahar se ane wali roshni ko morkar aankh ki pichli deewar par giata hai. Pichli deewar ko retina kehte hain, aur yahi asal screen hai. Yahan aik baat hai jo paper me bar bar ati hai aur sab ko heran karti hai: retina par banti hui tasveer ULTI banti hai. Jo cheez seedhi hai, us ka aks retina par ulta girta hai. Phir dimaagh us ultay aks ko seedha kar ke hamein dikhata hai. Yani hum asal me dimaagh se dekhte hain, aankh bas tasveer bana kar bhejti hai. Aankh se aik taar jati hai jise optic nerve kehte hain, yehi tasveer ki khabar dimaagh tak le jati hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Aankh ko side se kaat kar dikhaya jaye taake andar ke hisse nazar ayen. Samne se ane wali roshni ki lakeerain lens se guzar kar retina par ulti tasveer banati hui dikhayen, aur aik chhoti si teer wali lakeer se dikhaya jaye ke dimaagh isay seedha kar deta hai. Iris, pupil, lens aur retina par lazmi label hon.

Kaan — sunna aur tawazun

Kaan ka jo hissa bahar nazar ata hai wo asal kaan ka sab se chhota hissa hai. Asal kaan khopdi ke andar chhupa hai aur is ke teen hisse hain. Bahar wala hissa wo nali hai jo andar aik bareek jhilli par ja kar khatam hoti hai, us jhilli ko kaan ka parda, yani eardrum, kehte hain. Awaaz is parde se takrati hai aur parda kaanpne lagta hai.

Parde ke peeche darmiyan wala hissa hai, aur yahan jism ki sab se hairan kun cheez rakhi hai — teen nihayat chhoti haddiyan. Ye teeno haddiyan mil kar bhi aik chaawal ke dane se bari nahi. In me sab se chhoti haddi ka naam stapes hai, aur ye poore jism ki sab se chhoti haddi hai. Ye paper ka fix sawal hai — jism ki sab se chhoti haddi konsi hai aur kahan hai? Jawab: stapes, kaan me. Ye teenon haddiyan parde ki kaanp ko barha kar andar wale hisse tak pohnchati hain.

Andar wale hisse me aik ghongay, yani snail, ki shakal ki cheez hai jise cochlea kehte hain. Is ke andar pani bhara hai aur bareek baal jaisi cheezain hain. Awaaz ki lehrein is pani ko hilati hain aur wahan se khabar dimaagh ko jati hai, tab hum sunte hain. Isi andar wale hisse ke sath teen gol ghumti hui naliyan bhi hain jo hamare tawazun, yani balance, ko sambhalti hain. Isi liye kaan ki sakht bimari me insaan ko chakkar ate hain — sunne aur tawazun ka nizam aik hi ghar me rehta hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Kaan ke teen hisse alag alag rang me dikhayen — bahar wali nali aur parda, darmiyan me teen chhoti haddiyan (stapes par khaas nishan aur label ke ye jism ki sab se chhoti haddi hai), aur andar ghongay ki shakal wala cochlea. Awaaz ki lehrein teeron se bahar se andar jati hui dikhayen.

Haddiyan — jism ka dhancha

Agar jism se haddiyan nikal di jayen to insaan gosht ka aik dheela thela ban kar reh jaye, bilkul us imarat ki tarah jis ka lohe ka dhancha nikal diya gaya ho. Haddiyan jism ko shakal deti hain, khara rakhti hain, aur andar ke nazuk hisso ki hifazat karti hain. Khopdi dimaagh ke gird aik mazboot helmet hai, aur pasliyan dil aur phuphron ke gird aik pinjra.

Ab tadaad wale do number pakke kar lo, kyunke paper inhi par ghoomta hai. Aik baligh insaan ke jism me 206 haddiyan hoti hain. Lekin paida hone wale bache ke jism me takreeban 300 haddiyan hoti hain. Ye farq kesay? Bache ki bohat si haddiyan naram hoti hain aur umar ke sath apas me jur kar aik ban jati hain. Is liye bade hote hote tadaad ghat kar 206 reh jati hai. Sawal me agar bacha likha ho to jawab 300, baligh likha ho to 206 — yahi bareeki paper pakarta hai.

Sab se lambi aur sab se mazboot haddi raan ki haddi hai, jise femur kehte hain. Ye ghutne se koolhay tak jati hai aur poore jism ka wazan uthaye rakhti hai. Aur pasliyan? Pasliyon ke 12 joray hote hain, yani kul 24 pasliyan, jo seene ke pinjre ko banati hain. Jahan do haddiyan milti hain usay jor, yani joint, kehte hain — ghutna, kohni aur ungliyan inhi joron ki wajah se murti hain. Aur aik baat jo aksar bhool jati hai: haddi andar se khokhli si hoti hai aur us khokhli jagah me aik naram cheez hai jise bone marrow kehte hain. Wahi hamara naya khoon, khaas kar ke lal khaliye, banati hai. Yani khoon banta haddiyon ke andar hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Poore insani dhanche ki tasveer ho jis me raan ki haddi femur ko khaas rang se numayan karke label lagaya jaye ke ye sab se lambi haddi hai. Seene ke pinjre par pasliyon ka label ho, khopdi par label ho, aur aik chhoti alag tasveer me haddi ko kaat kar andar ka bone marrow dikhaya jaye.

Dimaagh — jism ka control room

Dimaagh khopdi ke andar rakha aik naram sa uzu hai, takreeban derh kilo ke qareeb. Is ki shakal dekhne me aik bare akhrot jaisi hai — upar se jhurriyon wala, gulabi aur sarmai rang ka, aur beech se aik gehri lakeer isay dayein aur bayein do hisso me baanti hai. Ye jhurriyan bewajah nahi. Dimaagh ki upar wali satah jitni zyada ho, utni zyada sochne ki taqat. Jhurriyon ne is satah ko khopdi ke chhote se khane me sama kar kayi guna barha diya hai.

Dimaagh ke teen bare hisse hain. Sab se bara hissa cerebrum hai — ye upar wala bara gumbad sa hissa hai, aur yahi hamari soch, yadasht, seekhne, bolne aur faisla karne ka markaz hai. Tum is waqt jo parh rahe ho, wo cerebrum hi samajh raha hai. Dusra hissa peeche neeche ki taraf chhota sa gol hissa hai jise cerebellum kehte hain. Ye jism ka tawazun aur harkaton ki hamahangi sambhalta hai — cycle chalate hue girne na dena isi ka kaam hai. Teesra hissa neeche aik dandi ki shakal me reedh ki haddi se jurta hai, ye saans aur dil ki dharkan jaisi khudkar cheezain chalata hai, jinhe hamein soch kar nahi karna parta.

Paper ke liye pakka kar lo: dimaagh ka sab se bara hissa cerebrum hai, aur sochne aur yadasht ka kaam isi ka hai. Dimaagh poore jism ko taaron ke aik jaal se control karta hai jinhe aasab, yani nerves, kehte hain. Ye taarain jism ke har hisse se khabar laati hain aur dimaagh ka hukam le jati hain.

Tasveer ka khayal: Dimaagh ko side se dikhaya jaye — upar bara cerebrum, peeche neeche chhota cerebellum, aur neeche dandi jaisa brain stem. Teeno par label hon, aur cerebrum ko sab se numayan rang diya jaye ke ye sab se bara hissa hai aur soch yahin hoti hai.

Paachan ka nizam — khana se taqat tak

Jo khana hum khate hain wo jism me aik lambe safar par nikalta hai. Ye safar aik nali hai jo munh se shuru ho kar takreeban 9 metre lambi hai, aur is safar me khana tukron me tutta, ghulta aur khoon me shamil hota rehta hai. Aao is safar par chalte hain.

Safar munh se shuru hota hai. Daant khane ko chabate hain aur zuban par maujood thook usay naram karta hai. Hazam asal me yahin se shuru ho jata hai. Phir khana aik nali se utar kar maiday me jata hai. Maida pait ke upar bayein taraf aik theli jaisa uzu hai jo phailta aur sikurta hai. Is me aik tezabi pani hota hai jo khane ko aur torta hai aur us me ghule jaraseem ko marta hai. Maiday me khana ghul mil kar aik garhay se pani jaisa ban jata hai.

Phir ye chhoti aant me jata hai, aur yahi hazam ki asal jagah hai. Chhoti aant naam ki chhoti hai, lekin ye takreeban 6 metre lambi hai aur kundli maar kar pait me pari hai. Yahin khana mukammal tor par hazam hota hai aur yahin se khane ki taqat aur ghizaiyat khoon me shamil hoti hai. Jo bach jata hai wo bari aant me jata hai, wahan se us ka pani jazb hota hai aur bacha khucha fazla ban jata hai.

Is nizam ke do bare madadgar khaas taur par samajh lo. Pehla jigar, yani liver. Ye pait ke upar dayein taraf rakha aik bara, gehra lal bhoora uzu hai, aur ye jism ke andar ka sab se bara ghudood hai. Jigar aik poori factory hai — ye khoon ko saaf karta hai, zehreli cheezon ko khatam karta hai, aur aik hariyal sa pani banata hai jise bile kehte hain jo charbi ko hazam karne me madad deta hai. Dusre gurday. Pait ke peeche, reedh ki haddi ke dono taraf, do lobiye ki shakal ke uzu hain. Gurday khoon ko chaante hain. Khoon in me se guzarta hai, gurday fazla aur zyada pani alag kar lete hain, aur wahi peshab ban jata hai. Gurday ke andar lakhon chhoti chhoti chhanniyan hain jinhe nephron kehte hain — khoon saaf karne ki asal ikai yehi nephron hai. Paper me sawal ata hai ke khoon saaf karne wala uzu konsa hai, aur gurday ke andar ki ikai ko kya kehte hain. Jawab: gurda, aur ikai nephron.

Tasveer ka khayal: Aik jism ki tasveer me poora paachan ka rasta teer se dikhaya jaye — munh, khane ki nali, maida, chhoti aant, bari aant — aur sath me jigar aur dono gurday alag rang me numayan hon. Har hisse par label ho, aur gurday ki aik chhoti bari tasveer me nephron dikhaya jaye.

Chamra — jism ki deewar

Chamre ko hum bas jism ki upar wali teh samajhte hain, lekin chamra asal me jism ka sab se bara uzu hai. Aik baligh insaan ki chamri phailai jaye to ye takreeban 2 metre murabba jagah gher legi aur wazan me 4 kilo ke qareeb hogi. Ye jism ko bahar ki dhool, jaraseem aur dhoop se bachati hai, jism ka darja hararat barqarar rakhti hai aur paseena nikal kar jism ko thanda karti hai. Chhoone ka ehsas bhi chamri hi deti hai — garam, thanda, dard, sab ki khabar yahin se dimaagh ko jati hai.

Chamri ki teen teh hain. Sab se upar wali bareek teh wo hai jo hum dekhte hain, usi me wo rang banane wali cheez hai jis se kisi ki rangat saaf aur kisi ki gehri hoti hai. Darmiyan wali teh me paseene ke ghudood, baalon ki jarein aur khoon ki bareek naliyan hain. Sab se neeche wali teh me charbi hoti hai jo jism ko narmi aur sardi se hifazat deti hai. Paper me chamre par sawal aik hi ghumta hai aur wo pakka hai: insani jism ka sab se bara uzu konsa hai? Jawab chamra hai, jigar nahi. Jigar sab se bara andar ka ghudood hai, lekin sab se bara uzu chamra hai — ye farq paper ka pasandida phanda hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Chamri ko side se kaat kar teeno tehain alag rang me dikhayi jayen — upar wali teh, darmiyan wali teh (jis me baal ki jar, paseene ka ghudood aur khoon ki nali nazar aye) aur neeche charbi wali teh. Har teh par label ho.

Sans ka nizam — hawa ka ana jana

Har saans ke sath hum hawa andar lete hain, us hawa se oxygen nikal kar khoon me jati hai, aur jism ki gandi gas, carbon dioxide, bahar nikal jati hai. Ye tabadla phuphron me hota hai. Phuphre seene ke andar, dil ke dono taraf rakhe do naram, sponge jaisi shakal ke uzu hain. Ye andar se khokhle nahi hote, balke lakhon karoron chhoti chhoti hawa ki theliyon se bhare hote hain, bilkul sponge ke surakhon ki tarah. Hawa naak se andar ja kar aik nali se utarti hai, phir ye nali do hisso me bat kar dono phuphron me jati hai, aur andar ja kar chhoti chhoti shakhon me taqseem hoti rehti hai, bilkul darakht ki shakhon ki tarah, yahan tak ke un chhoti theliyon tak pohnchti hai jahan oxygen khoon me aur carbon dioxide khoon se hawa me tabdeel ho jati hai.

Is poore nizam ka asal mazdoor diaphragm hai. Ye phuphron ke bilkul neeche aik gumbad ki shakal ka patha hai. Jab ye neeche ki taraf khichta hai to seena phailta hai aur hawa andar bhar jati hai. Jab ye dhela parta hai to hawa bahar nikal jati hai. Yani hum asal me pet ke is pathay se saans lete hain. Paper ke liye teen baatein pakki kar lo: hum hawa se oxygen lete hain aur carbon dioxide bahar nikalte hain, ye tabadla phuphron me hota hai, aur saans ki harkat ka asal patha diaphragm hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Seene ki tasveer me dono phuphre sponge jaisi banawat ke sath dikhayen, beech se utarti hawa ki nali do shakhon me bat ti hui, aur phuphron ke neeche gumbad ki shakal ka diaphragm khaas rang me. Teeron se dikhaya jaye ke oxygen andar jati hai aur carbon dioxide bahar ati hai.

Aakhir me fix facts aik nazar me

Ab wo saari baatein jo paper me bar bar dohrayi jati hain, aik jagah jama kar lo. Dil me chaar khane hote hain, dharkan aik minute me 72 dafa. Khoon aik baligh jism me takreeban 5 litre hota hai, us ka lal rang hemoglobin ki wajah se hai, aur khoon banta hai haddiyon ke bone marrow me. Universal donor O negative hai, universal recipient AB positive. Retina par aks ulta banta hai. Jism ki sab se chhoti haddi stapes kaan me hai, sab se lambi haddi femur raan me hai. Baligh me 206 haddiyan, bache me takreeban 300, pasliyon ke 12 joray. Dimaagh ka sab se bara hissa cerebrum hai. Jigar andar ka sab se bara ghudood hai, chamra sab se bara uzu hai. Gurda khoon saaf karta hai aur us ki ikai nephron hai. Hazam mukammal chhoti aant me hota hai. Saans ka asal patha diaphragm hai. In lines ko ratta mat lagao — har line ke peeche jo tasveer hum ne banayi hai usay zehen me lao, jawab khud ayega.
