Rozmarra ki Physics aur Chemistry
Bijli, roshni, awaaz, tezab aur wo ajadaat jin hon ne duniya badal di

Ye hissa kyun zaroori hai

Everyday Science ka paper adha unhi cheezon se banta hai jo tumhare ghar ke andar hain. Bulb ki roshni, pankhe ki hawa, kunwein ki ghari, sarouta, ainak, battery ka tezab, thermometer ka para. Problem ye hai ke in cheezon ko hum istemal to roz karte hain lekin kabhi in ke andar jhank kar nahi dekha. Is hisse me har cheez ko pehle tumhare samne khada karenge, kesay dikhti hai, us ke hisse konsay hain, phir wo kaam kesay karti hai, aur phir paper wala sawal. Tasveer bane gi to jawab khud bane ga.

Roshni, sab se tez musafir

Sab se pehle wo baat jo poori science ki buniyad hai. Is kainat me sab se tez cheez roshni hai. Roshni aik second me teen lakh kilometer ka safar karti hai, yani 300,000 kilometer fi second. Zameen ke gird saat chakkar aik second me lag jayein, itni tez hai. Sooraj hum se itna door hai ke us ki roshni ko hum tak ane me taqreeban aath minute lagte hain, yani jo sooraj tum dekh rahe ho wo aath minute purana manzar hai. Roshni hamesha seedhi line me safar karti hai, isiliye saya banta hai, kyunke jahan cheez roshni ko rok le wahan peeche andhera reh jata hai.

Ab aaina samne rakho. Aaine me apna aks dekho. Aaine me tumhara dayein haath bayein taraf dikhai deta hai. Aaine me cheezain ulta is liye dikhti hain ke aaina age peeche ko ulta nahi karta, wo dayein bayein ko palat deta hai. Isi liye ambulance par lafz ulta likha hota hai taake age wali gari ke sheeshay me seedha parha jaye. Paper poochta hai, aaine me aks kesay banta hai? Roshni ke munakis hone se, jisay reflection kehte hain.

Ab lens ki baat, jo ainak aur camera ki jaan hai. Lens sheeshay ka aik tukra hota hai jo roshni ko mor deta hai. Do qismein gaur se dekho. Convex lens darmiyan se mota hota hai aur kinaron se patla, bilkul masoor ki daal ki tarah phula hua. Ye lens roshni ki kirnon ko aik nuqte par ikattha kar deta hai, isiliye is se kagaz par sooraj ki roshni jama kar ke aag bhi jalayi ja sakti hai aur khurdbeen yani magnifying glass me yehi lens chhoti cheez bari kar ke dikhata hai. Concave lens is ke ulta hai, darmiyan se patla aur kinaron se mota. Ye roshni ko ikattha karne ke bajaye phaila deta hai. Ab ainak se jodo. Jis admi ko door ki cheezain dhundli dikhti hain, us ki ainak me concave lens lagta hai. Jis ko qareeb ki kitab dhundli dikhti hai, us ke liye convex lens hota hai. Paper ka fix sawal yahi hai. Door ki nazar ki kamzori, jisay myopia kehte hain, concave lens se theek hoti hai. Qareeb ki nazar ki kamzori, hyperopia, convex lens se.

Tasveer ka khayal: Aik taraf convex lens ki shakal dikhe, darmiyan se moti, aur us se guzar kar kirnein aik nuqte par ikatthi hoti hui, label lage convex lens aur magnifying glass. Dusri taraf concave lens darmiyan se patli, kirnein phailti hui, label lage concave lens. Neeche ainak ki tasveer me dono lenson ki jagah nishan se dikhayi jaye.

Awaaz, lehrain jo sunai deti hain

Awaaz asal me hawa ke kaanpne ka naam hai. Jab ghanti bajti hai to wo kaanpti hai aur apne gird ki hawa ko bhi kaanpne par majboor kar deti hai. Ye kaanpna lehar ban kar tumhare kaan tak jata hai. Awaaz ki raftaar hawa me taqreeban 343 meter fi second hai. Roshni se muqabla karo, roshni aik second me teen lakh kilometer aur awaaz aik second me sirf chand sau meter. Isi liye toofan me chamak pehle aur garaj baad me ati hai, jesa pehle hisse me parha tha.

Do bareek baatain paper ki jaan hain. Pehli, awaaz ko safar ke liye koi cheez chahiye, hawa ho, pani ho ya loha. Khali jagah jahan hawa hi nahi, jisay vacuum kehte hain, wahan awaaz safar hi nahi kar sakti. Chaand par hawa nahi hai, is liye wahan do admi paas khade ho kar bhi aik dusre ki awaaz nahi sun sakte. Dusri, awaaz thos cheezon me sab se tez safar karti hai, phir pani me, aur hawa me sab se sust. Rail ki patri par kaan lagane se train ki awaaz door se sunai dena isi ki misaal hai.

Ab goonj yani echo samjho. Tum kisi bade khali hall ya pahari ke samne zor se awaaz lagao, tumhari awaaz takra kar wapas ati hai. Yehi wapas ane wali awaaz echo hai. Lekin echo sunne ke liye deewar kam az kam taqreeban 17 meter door honi chahiye, kyunke hamara kaan do awaazon ko tabhi alag sunta hai jab un ke darmiyan thoda sa waqfa ho. Kunwein me awaaz lagane par jo jawab sa ata hai, wo bhi echo hai. Paper poochta hai, awaaz khala me safar kyun nahi karti? Kyunke use koi medium chahiye hota hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Aik admi pahar ke samne khada awaaz laga raha ho, us ke munh se lehrain nikal kar pahar se takrati aur wapas ati hui dikhen, fasle par 17 meter ka label lage. Side me chaand par khade do admi dikhaye jayen jin ke darmiyan khamoshi ka nishan ho, label lage vacuum me awaaz nahi.

Bijli, taar ke andar dodti taaqat

Bijli ko teen lafzon se samjho, bilkul pani ki naali ki misaal se. Voltage ko pani ka dabao samjho, dabao zyada to pani tez behta hai. Ampere current ki miqdar hai, yani kitni bijli beh rahi hai, jese naali me kitna pani beh raha hai. Aur watt taaqat ki ikai hai, koi cheez kitni bijli kha rahi hai. Bulb par 100 watt likha hota hai, is ka matlab hai wo har second me itni taaqat khata hai.

Ab wo baat jo har ghar ke bill par ati hai. Ghar ki bijli units me ginni jati hai, aur aik unit ka matlab hai aik kilowatt hour, jisay mukhtasar me kWh likhte hain. Yani aik hazar watt ki cheez aik ghanta chale to aik unit banti hai. Paper ka fix sawal hai, bijli ka unit kis me naapa jata hai? Kilowatt hour me.

Hifazat wali do cheezain gaur se dekho. Fuse aik patli si taar hoti hai jo meter ke paas lagi hoti hai. Jab bijli had se zyada guzarne lagti hai to ye patli taar garam ho kar pighal jati hai aur raasta kat deti hai, yun poore ghar ki mehngi cheezain jalne se bach jati hain. Fuse apni jaan de kar ghar bachata hai. MCB is ka jadeed roop hai, ye taar nahi aik switch hai, zyada bijli ane par ye khud band ho jata hai yani trip kar jata hai, aur theek kar ke dobara on kar sakte ho. Fuse aik dafa ka, MCB bar bar ka.

Aakhir me ye pehchan lo ke bijli kin cheezon se guzarti hai. Jin cheezon se bijli asani se guzar jaye unhe conductor kehte hain, taamba aur loha in me sab se mashhoor hain, isiliye taarein taambe ki banti hain. Jin se bijli na guzre unhe insulator kehte hain, rabbar, lakri aur plastic. Taar ke upar rabbar ka ghilaaf isiliye charhaya jata hai taake haath lagane par jhatka na lage.

Tasveer ka khayal: Aik ghar ka meter aur us ke sath fuse ki patli taar bari kar ke dikhayi jaye, label lage fuse. Us ke sath MCB ka switch on aur trip ki halat me dikhe. Neeche taambe ki taar ka ghilaaf kaat kar andar ki taar dikhayi jaye, taar par conductor aur rabbar par insulator ka label lage.

Simple machines, kaam asaan karne wale purane dost

Insaan ne kaam asaan karne ke liye chand saada cheezain banayi, inhe simple machines kehte hain. Teen mashhoor machinein tumhare samne rakhte hain.

Pehli machine lever hai, yani aik sakht lakri ya lohe ki salakh jo aik nuqte par tik kar ghoomti hai. Is nuqte ko fulcrum kehte hain. Lever ki teen qismein hain aur farq sirf itna hai ke darmiyan me kya hai. Pehli qism me fulcrum darmiyan me hota hai, jese tarazu ka palla, qainchi aur bachon ka jhoola numa seesaw. Tum aik taraf bhaar rakhte ho, dusri taraf zor lagate ho. Dusri qism me bhaar darmiyan me hota hai, jese sarouta yani nutcracker. Saroute ko zehen me lao, akhrot darmiyan me hota hai, aik sira tika hota hai, dusre sire par tum zor lagate ho, aur akhrot toot jata hai. Theli gari yani wheelbarrow bhi isi qism ki hai. Teesri qism me zor darmiyan me lagta hai, jese chimta. Chimte me aik sira jura hota hai, darmiyan me ungliyan zor lagati hain, aur dusre sire par roti pakri jati hai. Belcha bhi isi qism me ginna jata hai, haath darmiyan me zor lagata hai aur mitti sire par uthti hai. Paper teeno qismon ki misaal ghuma kar poochta hai, is liye har qism ko us ki cheez se jod kar yaad rakho. Fulcrum darmiyan to tarazu, bhaar darmiyan to sarouta, zor darmiyan to chimta.

Dusri machine pulley hai, yani ghari ya charakhi. Kunwein par lagi ghari tum ne dekhi hogi. Rassi ghari ke gird lapeti jati hai, aik sire par balti bandhi hoti hai, dusre sire ko tum neeche kheenchti ho aur balti upar a jati hai. Pulley ka asal faida ye hai ke wo zor ki simt badal deti hai. Upar kheenchna mushkil hota, neeche kheenchna asaan, ghari yehi tabdeeli kar deti hai. Jhanda buland karne wali ghari bhi yehi hai.

Teesri machine inclined plane hai, yani dhalaan wala rasta. Seedhi seedhi deewar par bhari cheez uthana mushkil hai, lekin dhalaan par ghaseetna asaan. Hospital aur station par bani hui ramp yehi machine hai. Pahari sarakh seedhi upar nahi jati, morr kaat kaat kar dhalaan bana kar jati hai, taake gari ko zyada zor na lagana pare. Faasla lamba ho jata hai lekin zor kam lagta hai, yehi in tamam machineon ka usool hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Teen alag khane banein. Pehle me tarazu aur fulcrum par label, dusre me sarouta akhrot torte hue, teesre me chimta roti pakre hue, har aik ke neeche us ki qism ka naam. Sath me kunwein ki ghari rassi aur balti ke sath, aur pahar par morr khati dhalaan wali sarakh dikhayi jaye.

Magnet, wo loha jo khud kheenchta hai

Magnet aik aisi cheez hai jo lohe ko apni taraf kheenchti hai. Us ke do sire hote hain jinhe qatb yani pole kehte hain, aik north pole aur dusra south pole. Qanoon seedha hai. Mukhalif qatb aik dusre ko kheenchti hain, north aur south milte hain. Aik jaise qatb aik dusre ko dhakelte hain, north north ko door karta hai. Do magnet haath me le kar ye khud mehsoos hota hai.

Ab compass dekho. Compass ki chhoti si suee asal me aik chhota sa magnet hai jo azaadi se ghoom sakti hai. Zameen khud aik bohat bada magnet hai, is liye compass ki suee hamesha zameen ke north ki taraf rukh kar leti hai. Isi liye samandar ke musafir aur jangalon ke rahi compass se simt maloom karte the. Paper poochta hai, compass ki suee hamesha north kyun dikhati hai? Kyunke zameen aik bara magnet hai aur suee us ke asar me rukh karti hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Ghore ki naal jaisi shakal ka magnet dikhe jis ke dono siro par N aur S ke label hon, us ke paas lohe ki keelain us ki taraf khinchti hui dikhen. Side me compass ki gol tasveer ho jis ki suee north ki taraf ishara kar rahi ho.

Density, barf tairti kyun hai aur loha doobta kyun hai

Aik sawal jo har bache ke zehen me ata hai. Lohe ki chhoti si keel pani me doob jati hai, lekin barf ka bara tukra pani par tairta hai. Jawab density me chhupa hai. Density yani kasafat ka matlab hai ke koi cheez apni jagah ke muqable me kitni bhari hai. Jo cheez pani se zyada ghani hogi, wo doobegi. Jo pani se kam ghani hogi, wo tairegi.

Barf ki kahani bareek hai. Pani jab jamta hai to dusri cheezon ki tarah sikurta nahi, balke thoda phail jata hai. Phailne se wahi wazan zyada jagah me phail gaya, kasafat kam ho gayi, is liye barf pani se halki ho kar upar tairti hai. Yehi wajah hai ke sardion me pipeon ke andar pani jam jaye to pipe phat jate hain. Aur aik pyari baat, jheelon me upar barf jam jati hai lekin neeche pani rehta hai, is liye machhliyan neeche zinda reh jati hain. Loha pani se kahin zyada ghana hai, is liye doobta hai. Lekin lohe ka jahaaz tairta hai, kyunke jahaaz andar se khokhla hota hai aur us ke andar ki hawa mila kar us ki majmooi kasafat pani se kam ho jati hai. Garam hawa bhi thandi hawa se halki hoti hai, isiliye garam hawa ka ghubaara upar uthta hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Aik sheeshay ke glass me pani dikhe, us me barf ka tukra upar tairta hua aur lohe ki keel neeche bethi hui, dono par density ke label hon. Side me khokhla lohe ka jahaaz samandar par tairta hua dikhaya jaye.

Tezab aur base, litmus wala khel

Ab chemistry ki sab se rozmarra baat. Tezab yani acid wo cheez hai jo khatti hoti hai, jese nimbu aur sirka. Base wo cheez hai jo karwi hoti hai aur haath par phisalne wali lagti hai, jese sabun. Dono ko pehchanne ke liye aik khaas kagaz hota hai jisay litmus paper kehte hain. Qanoon yaad kar lo. Neela litmus tezab me dalo to lal ho jata hai. Lal litmus base me dalo to neela ho jata hai. Paper ka sab se mashhoor sawal yahi hai.

Tezab aur base ki taaqat naapne ka aik paimana hai jisay pH scale kehte hain. Ise aik lakeer ki tarah zehen me banao jis par 0 se 14 tak number likhe hain. Darmiyan wala number 7 neutral hai, yani na tezab na base, saaf pani yahin ata hai. 7 se neeche jitna jao ge tezab utna tez hota jayega. 7 se upar jitna jao ge base utni tez hoti jayegi. Maiday ka tezab 1 aur 2 ke paas hai, sabun 9 aur 10 ke paas.

Ab ghar ki cheezon se jodo. Sirke me acetic acid hota hai. Nimbu aur sangtare me citric acid hota hai. Gari ki battery me sulfuric acid hota hai, ye bohat tez tezab hai. Tumhare maiday me hydrochloric acid hota hai jo khana hazam karta hai, jab ye zyada ho jaye to seene me jalan hoti hai jisay tezabiyat kehte hain. Base ki misaalon me sabun, toothpaste aur chuna mashhoor hain. Toothpaste base is liye hota hai taake munh ke tezab ko khatam kare. Aik desi indicator bhi yaad rakho, haldi. Haldi wala rang base me par kar lal bhura sa ho jata hai, sabun ke daagh par kapra haldi se ragro to yahi hota hai. Aakhir me ye baat ke tezab aur base mil jayen to aik dusre ko khatam kar dete hain, namak aur pani ban jata hai, isay neutralization kehte hain. Chinti kaate to us ke tezab par khane ka soda lagana isi usool par hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Aik lambi patti dikhe jis par 0 se 14 tak number hon, neeche walay hisse par tezab ka label aur nimbu battery ki chhoti nishaniyan, darmiyan 7 par pani ka glass, upar walay hisse par base ka label aur sabun ki tasveer. Side me neela litmus kagaz tezab ke glass me lal hota hua dikhaya jaye.

Hawa aur pani, do zaroori cheezain gin kar

Hum jis hawa me saans lete hain wo aik gas nahi, kai gason ka majmua hai. Is ke hisse gin lo. Sab se zyada nitrogen hai, taqreeban 78 feesad. Dusre number par oxygen hai, taqreeban 21 feesad, yehi wo gas hai jis par hamari zindagi hai. Baqi sirf aik feesad me argon, carbon dioxide aur dusri gasain ati hain. Paper hamesha ye phansata hai ke hawa me sab se zyada konsi gas hai, aksar student oxygen likh deta hai, jabke sahi jawab nitrogen hai.

Pani ka formula har student ne suna hoga, H2O. Is ka matlab hai pani ke har hisse me hydrogen ke do hisse aur oxygen ka aik hissa hota hai. Pani 0 degree centigrade par jam kar barf ban jata hai aur 100 degree centigrade par ubal kar bhaap ban jata hai. Ye do number paper ke fix number hain. Aik bareeki ye bhi hai ke pani garam ho ya thanda, wo hamesha upar se jamna shuru hota hai kyunke barf halki ho kar upar a jati hai, jesa density wale hisse me parha.

Tasveer ka khayal: Hawa ke liye aik gol dayra dikhe jis ka bada hissa nitrogen 78 feesad ke label ke sath, dusra hissa oxygen 21 feesad, aur aik bareek si patti baqi gason ki. Pani ke liye thermometer par 0 aur 100 ke nishan lage dikhaye jayen, aik taraf barf aur dusri taraf ubalta pani.

Dhaten, para, tungsten aur zang

Aakhir me chand mashhoor dhaton ki pehchan kar lo. Mercury yani para wohi chaandi jaisa chamakta hua mada hai jo purane thermometer ke andar hota tha. Ye duniya ki wahid dhat hai jo aam darja hararat par maye yani liquid halat me hoti hai, baqi tamam dhaten thos hain. Paper ka fix sawal yahi hai, liquid metal konsi hai? Mercury. Tungsten wo dhat hai jis ka bareek taar bulb ke andar hota hai, isay filament kehte hain. Tungsten is liye chuni gayi kyunke ye sab se zyada garmi bardasht karti hai aur pighle baghair garam ho kar roshni deti hai. Sona is liye qeemti hai ke use zang nahi lagta, hawa aur pani us par asar nahi karte, hazaron saal purana sona bhi chamakta milta hai.

Lohe ko zang kesay lagta hai, ye bhi samajh lo. Zang lagne ke liye do cheezain lazmi hain, hawa ki oxygen aur nami yani pani. Geeli hawa me rakha loha ahista ahista bhoore rang ki teh se dhak jata hai, yehi zang hai jo lohe ko kha kar kamzor kar deta hai. Isiliye lohe par rangat yani paint kiya jata hai taake hawa aur nami us tak na pohanch sakein. Khushk registan me loha der tak zang nahi khata, samandar ke kinare sab se jaldi khata hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Thermometer bari shakal me dikhe jis ke andar chaandi jaisi mercury ki lakeer par label lage. Sath me bulb ka andar wala filament taar jalta hua dikhaya jaye jis par tungsten likha ho. Neeche aik zang lagi keel aur aik chamakti sone ki angoothi ka muqabla dikhe.

Aijadaat aur scientists, cheez aur us ka banane wala

Aakhir me un logon ko pehchano jin ki banayi cheezain tum roz istemal karte ho. Har aik ke sath ye bhi yaad rakho ke wo cheez asal me dikhti kaisi thi.

Bulb Thomas Edison ne banaya. Pehla bulb aj ke bulb jaisa hi gol sheeshay ka gola tha jis ke andar bareek taar jalta tha, lekin roshni peeli aur maddham thi. Telephone Alexander Graham Bell ne banaya, pehla telephone lakri ke dabbe jaisi ajeeb si cheez tha jis par bolna aur sunna aik hi hisse se hota tha. Radio Guglielmo Marconi ne banaya, us ne hawa ke zariye baghair taar ke awaaz aur ishare bhejne ka pehla kamyab tajriba kiya, pehle radio bade bade dabbe aur lambi antena wale hote the. Jahaz yani aeroplane Wright brothers ne banaya, un ka pehla jahaz lakri aur kapre ka bana hua tha aur sirf 12 second ura tha. Steam engine James Watt ne behtar banaya, ye wahi engine hai jo pehle karkhanon aur phir rail gari ko chalata raha, bara sa lohe ka dhacha jis me bhaap pistano ko dhakelti thi. Printing press Johannes Gutenberg ne banaya, is se pehle kitabein haath se likhi jati thi aur bohat mehngi hoti thi, press ke baad kitabein aam ho gayin.

Telescope Galileo ne behtar kar ke asmaan ki taraf uthaya aur chaand ke garhay aur Jupiter ke chaand pehli dafa dekhe, pehli telescope lambi si naali jaisi hoti thi. X-ray Wilhelm Roentgen ne daryaft ki, ye wo roshni hai jo jism ke andar ki haddi ki tasveer bana deti hai. Penicillin Alexander Fleming ne daryaft ki, ye duniya ki pehli mashhoor antibiotic dawa hai aur mazay ki baat ye ke ye aik phaphundi yani mold se mili thi, Fleming ne dekha ke jahan phaphundi lagi hai wahan jaraseem mar rahe hain. Gravity yani kashish ka qanoon Isaac Newton se jura hai, riwayat hai ke darakht se seb girta dekh kar us ke zehen me sawal aya ke seb neeche hi kyun gira, aur yahi soch age chal kar poori zameen aur chaand ko jodne wale qanoon bani. Charles Babbage ko father of computer kehte hain, us ne aik aisi machine ka naksha banaya tha jo hisaab khud kar sake, wo machine us ke zamane me ban na saki lekin aj ka computer usi soch ki aulaad hai. Periodic table Dmitri Mendeleev ne banayi, ye wahi bada sa chart hai jo chemistry ki class ki deewar par laga hota hai jis me tamam anasir tartib se likhe hote hain. Evolution yani irtiqa ka nazariya Charles Darwin ne diya, us ne door daraz ke jazeeron par chidiyon ki chonchon ka farq dekh kar ye samjhaya ke jandaar waqt ke sath mahol ke mutabiq badalte hain. Marie Curie radioactivity par kaam karne wali sab se mashhoor scientist hain, unhon ne do naye anasir daryaft kiye, aur wo wahid shakhs hain jinhe do mukhtalif science ke shobon, physics aur chemistry, me Nobel inaam mila.

Paper in sawalon ko do tarah banata hai. Kabhi cheez de kar banane wala poochta hai, telephone kis ne banaya? Aur kabhi naam de kar cheez poochta hai, Fleming kis liye mashhoor hai? Dono taraf se tayyar raho.

Tasveer ka khayal: Har scientist ke liye chhoti si khana bandi tasveer ho jis me aik taraf us ki ijad ki purani shakal dikhe, jese lakri kapre wala pehla jahaz, dabbe jaisa pehla telephone, naali jaisi telescope, aur neeche naam aur ijad ka label lage.

Aakhir me paper ke fix nuktaat

Poore hisse ki jaan dohra lo. Roshni ki raftaar teen lakh kilometer fi second hai aur ye sab se tez hai. Door ki nazar concave lens se aur qareeb ki nazar convex lens se theek hoti hai. Awaaz ki raftaar hawa me taqreeban 343 meter fi second hai aur awaaz vacuum me safar nahi kar sakti. Bijli ka unit kilowatt hour yani kWh hai. Fuse patli taar hoti hai jo zyada bijli par pighal jati hai. Taamba conductor hai, rabbar insulator. Lever ki dusri qism ki misaal sarouta hai, teesri qism ki chimta. Compass ki suee zameen ke magnet ki wajah se north dikhati hai. Barf pani se kam ghani hoti hai is liye tairti hai. Neela litmus tezab me lal ho jata hai. pH 7 neutral hai. Hawa me sab se zyada gas nitrogen hai, 78 feesad. Pani 0 degree par jamta hai aur 100 degree par ubalta hai. Liquid dhat mercury hai aur bulb ka filament tungsten ka hota hai. Father of computer Charles Babbage hai aur do Nobel pane wali scientist Marie Curie hain.
