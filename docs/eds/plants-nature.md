Pauday aur Qudrat
Roz dikhne wali cheezain, jo asal me hain kya

Ye hissa kyun zaroori hai

Everyday Science ke paper me sab se zyada sawal unhi cheezon par bante hain jo hum roz dekhte hain. Phool har gali me khilta hai, barish har saal hoti hai, baadal roz asmaan par tehalte hain, lekin jab paper poochta hai ke phool ka nar hissa konsa hai, ya chamak pehle kyun dikhti hai aur garaj baad me kyun sunai deti hai, to aksar student ruk jata hai. Wajah ye nahi ke ye cheezain mushkil hain. Wajah ye hai ke hum ne inhe kabhi ghoor se dekha hi nahi. Is hisse me hum har cheez ko pehle alfaaz se tumhari ankhon ke samne khada karenge. Cheez dikhti kaisi hai, us ke hisse konsay hain, phir wo kaam kesay karti hai, aur aakhir me paper wala sawal. Jab tasveer zehen me ban jayegi, to jawab khud yaad rahega, ratta lagane ki zaroorat hi nahi paregi.

Phool ke hisse, ankh se dekho

Sab se pehle aik khila hua phool apne zehen me lao. Gulab ya genda ka phool samne rakho. Sab se bahar jo rang barang bare bare hisse nazar ate hain, jin ki wajah se phool khubsurat dikhta hai, unhe pankhariyan kehte hain, English me petals. Pankhariyon ka kaam sirf khubsurti nahi hai. Ye door se keeron ko bulati hain, bilkul jese dukaan ka board grahak ko bulata hai. Pankhariyon ke neeche, phool ke bilkul buniyadi hisse me chhoti sabz pattiyon jaisi cheezain hoti hain, unhe sepal kehte hain. Jab phool kali hota hai, yehi sabz hisse use lapait kar hifazat karte hain.

Ab phool ke darmiyan me dekho. Darmiyan se chand patli patli salaiyan si nikli hoti hain jin ke siro par chhoti si gathri ya theli bani hoti hai. Ye phool ka nar hissa hai, isay stamen kehte hain. Us theli ko anther kehte hain aur us ke andar peelay rang ka bareek powder bhara hota hai, jisay pollen kehte hain. Pollen hi asal me nar khulye hain, bilkul beej jaisi chhoti si cheez, lekin itni chhoti ke powder lagti hai.

Phool ke bilkul beech me, sab se darmiyan, aik surahi ya bottle jaisi saakht khadi hoti hai. Ye phool ka mada hissa hai, isay pistil ya carpel kehte hain. Is ke teen hisse gaur se dekho. Sab se neeche phula hua hissa ovary hai, us ke andar chhote chhote daanay hain jinhe ovule kehte hain, yehi aage chal kar beej bante hain. Ovary ke upar patli si naali hai jisay style kehte hain, aur naali ke sab se upar chipchipa sa sira hai jisay stigma kehte hain. Pollen ko isi stigma par girna hota hai.

Ab asal kahani suno. Nar hisse ka pollen mada hisse ke stigma tak kesay pohanchta hai? Is safar ko pollination kehte hain. Kabhi ye kaam hawa karti hai, pollen halka hota hai, hawa use ura kar dusre phool tak le jati hai, ghaas aur anaaj ke phool aksar aise hi hote hain. Lekin rang barang bare phoolon ka kaam keere karte hain. Shehad ki makkhi ya titli phool ka meetha ras peene ati hai, us ke jism par pollen chipak jata hai, aur jab wo dusre phool par jati hai to pollen wahan ke stigma par gir jata hai. Isi liye phool rang barang aur khushbu daar hote hain, taake keere door se ayein. Pollen stigma par girne ke baad aik naali si ban kar ovary tak jata hai, ovule se milta hai, is milap ko fertilization kehte hain, aur is ke baad ovule beej ban jata hai aur ovary phal ban jati hai. Aam, seb aur tamatar jo tum khate ho, ye sab kisi zamane me kisi phool ki ovary the.

Paper yahan yun sawal banata hai. Phool ka nar hissa konsa hai, jawab stamen. Mada hissa konsa hai, jawab pistil. Pollen kahan banta hai, jawab anther me. Beej kis hisse se banta hai, jawab ovule se. Phal kis se banta hai, jawab ovary se.

Tasveer ka khayal: App me phool ko beech se kaat kar aadha dikhaya jaye taake andar ke hisse nazar ayein. Pankhari, sepal, stamen ki theli (anther), pollen ke daanay, pistil ke teen hisse stigma, style aur ovary, har aik par us ke naam ka label lage. Aik taraf shehad ki makkhi phool par bethi dikhe jis ke jism par pollen chipka ho.

Photosynthesis, pauda khana kesay banata hai

Ab pauday ki sab se bari khasiyat samjho, wo khasiyat jo kisi janwar ke paas nahi. Pauda apna khana khud banata hai. Tum khana bahar se khate ho, pauda khana apne patton ke andar tayyar karta hai. Is amal ko photosynthesis kehte hain. Photo matlab roshni, synthesis matlab banana, yani roshni se banana.

Tasveer zehen me banao. Aik sabz patta samne hai. Upar se sooraj ki kirnein patte par par rahi hain. Neeche zameen se jarein pani kheench kar tanay ke raste patte tak pohancha rahi hain. Hawa me aik gas hai jisay carbon dioxide kehte hain, jo hum saans chorne par bahar nikalte hain. Patte ki satah par chhote chhote suraakh hain, un se ye gas andar jati hai. Patte ke andar sabz rang ka aik khaas mada hai jisay chlorophyll kehte hain. Yehi sabz mada sooraj ki roshni ko pakarta hai, aur usi roshni ki taaqat se pani aur carbon dioxide ko jod kar cheeni jaisi cheez banata hai jisay glucose kehte hain. Yehi glucose pauday ka khana hai. Is amal me aik gas fazool nikalti hai, aur wo hai oxygen, jo patte se bahar hawa me mil jati hai. Wahi oxygen jo hum saans me lete hain.

Is poori baat ko alfaaz ki equation me yun likhte hain. Carbon dioxide aur pani, sooraj ki roshni aur chlorophyll ki maujoodgi me, glucose aur oxygen ban jate hain. Bas yehi aik line paper ka sab se mashhoor sawal hai. Pauda khana banane ke liye kya kya leta hai? Pani, carbon dioxide aur sooraj ki roshni. Aur kya kharij karta hai? Oxygen.

Ab aik bareek baat, jis par paper phansata hai. Pauday ka rang sabz kyun hai? Chlorophyll ki wajah se. Chlorophyll sooraj ki roshni ke baqi rang jazb kar leta hai lekin sabz rang ko wapas phenk deta hai, is liye patta sabz dikhta hai. Dusri bareeki ye hai ke photosynthesis sirf din ko hota hai, kyunke is ke liye roshni lazmi hai. Lekin saans lene ka amal, jisay respiration kehte hain, pauday me din raat har waqt jari rehta hai, bilkul hamari tarah. Is liye raat ko pauda oxygen nahi banata, balke thodi si carbon dioxide kharij karta hai. Yehi wajah hai ke purani hidayat me bade darakht ke neeche raat ko sone se mana kiya jata hai, halanke din ko wahi darakht oxygen ka karobar karta hai. Paper ka sawal yun banta hai. Din ke waqt pauda konsi gas kharij karta hai? Oxygen. Raat ko konsi? Carbon dioxide. Aur yaad rakho, din ko bhi pauda saans leta hai, lekin photosynthesis itna tez hota hai ke oxygen zyada banti hai aur wohi bahar ati hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Aik bada patta darmiyan me dikhe. Upar se sooraj aur us ki kirnon par "roshni" ka label, side se teer hawa ki taraf se andar jata hua jis par carbon dioxide likha ho, neeche jaron se pani ka teer upar ata hua, aur patte se bahar nikalta hua teer jis par oxygen likha ho. Patte ke andar chhote sabz daanay chlorophyll ke naam se dikhaye jayen.

Beej se pauda, germination ke marhalay

Ab aik beej ko zameen me dabta hua dekho. Chane ya phali ka beej samne rakho, sakht sa, sookha sa, bejaan sa lagta hai. Lekin us ke andar aik chhota sa pauda soya hua hai aur sath us ke liye pehle dinon ka khana bhi rakha hai. Is soye hue pauday ke jag kar bahar ane ko germination kehte hain.

Marhalay tartib se samjho, paper tartib hi poochta hai. Pehla marhala, beej zameen se pani jazb karta hai aur phool jata hai, us ka sakht chilka narm par jata hai. Dusra marhala, beej ke andar se sab se pehle aik safed si cheez neeche ki taraf nikalti hai. Ye jarr hai, isay radicle kehte hain. Gaur karo, sab se pehle jarr nikalti hai, tana nahi, kyunke pauday ko sab se pehle pani aur sahara chahiye. Teesra marhala, phir upar ki taraf hari si konpal nikalti hai, isay plumule kehte hain, yehi aage chal kar tana aur patte banti hai. Beej ke do mote hisse jinhe cotyledon kehte hain, shuru ke dinon me is chhote pauday ko khana dete rehte hain, jab tak ke patte nikal kar khud khana banana na shuru kar dein.

Germination ke liye teen shartein lazmi hain, ye paper ka fix sawal hai. Pani, taake beej jaag jaye. Hawa, yani oxygen, taake beej saans le sake. Aur munasib garmi, na bohat zyada na bohat kam. Roshni shuru ke marhalay me lazmi nahi hoti, beej andhere me bhi nikal ata hai, yehi wo bareeki hai jis par ghalat jawab lagta hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Zameen ki lakeer ke neeche beej ke chaar marhalay aik ke baad aik dikhaye jayen. Pehle sookha beej, phir phula hua beej, phir neeche nikalti jarr (radicle) ka label, phir upar uthti konpal (plumule) ka label, aur beej ke khane wale hisse par cotyledon ka label lage.

Pani ka cycle, wahi pani bar bar

Ab asmaan ki taraf dekho. Samandar, daria aur jheelon ka pani kahan jata hai aur barish ban kar kesay wapas ata hai? Ye aik gol chakkar hai jisay pani ka cycle ya water cycle kehte hain.

Pehla qadam, sooraj ki garmi se samandar aur dariaon ka pani bhaap ban kar upar uthta hai. Is bhaap banne ko evaporation kehte hain. Gaur karo, yehi amal geele kapre sookhne me hota hai. Dusra qadam, upar thandi hawa me ye bhaap thandi ho kar dobara chhote chhote qatron me badal jati hai. Isay condensation kehte hain. Lakhon karoron qatray mil kar baadal ban jate hain. Aaina par bathroom me jo dhund jamti hai, wo bhi yehi condensation hai. Teesra qadam, jab qatray bare aur bhari ho jate hain to zameen ki kashish unhe neeche kheench leti hai, aur barish girti hai. Barish ko precipitation kehte hain, aur oolay aur barf bhi isi ki qismein hain. Chautha qadam, barish ka pani naalon aur dariaon se beh kar dobara samandar me ja girta hai, kuch zameen ke andar utar kar underground pani ban jata hai, aur phir sooraj use dobara bhaap bana deta hai. Chakkar yunhi chalta rehta hai, aur is poore chakkar ka engine sooraj hai. Paper poochta hai, pani ke cycle me pehla marhala konsa hai? Evaporation. Baadal kis amal se bante hain? Condensation se.

Tasveer ka khayal: Aik gol chakkar ki shakal me diagram ho. Neeche samandar, upar uthti bhaap ke teer par evaporation, asmaan par baadal par condensation, barish ke qatron par precipitation, aur pahar se behta daria wapas samandar ki taraf jata hua dikhe. Side me sooraj bana ho jis par "engine" ki tarah garmi ka label lage.

Mausam aur hawa, baadal, barish aur garaj chamak

Baadal asal me hain kya? Upar ja kar samjho. Zameen ke paas ki garam hawa halki hoti hai, wo upar uthti hai. Upar hawa thandi hai, garam hawa ki bhaap thandi ho kar chhote qatron me badal jati hai, yehi qatray baadal hain. Baadal asal me asmaan par tairti hui dhund hai. Jab tum pahar par jate ho aur baadal tumhare aas paas hota hai to wahi dhund hoti hai. Jab qatray itne bare ho jayen ke hawa unhe sambhal na sake, barish girti hai.

Ab garaj chamak wali baat, jo paper ka pasandida sawal hai. Toofani baadalon ke andar barq yani bijli paida hoti hai, aur jab wo chamakti hai to use chamak kehte hain, aur us ke phatne ki awaaz ko garaj. Tum ne dekha hoga, pehle chamak dikhti hai, phir chand second baad garaj sunai deti hai. Aisa kyun? Kyunke roshni ki raftaar bohat zyada hai, aik second me teen lakh kilometer, jabke awaaz ki raftaar aik second me sirf taqreeban 343 meter hai. Roshni takreeban foran tumhari ankh tak pohanch jati hai, awaaz ko ane me waqt lagta hai. Agar chamak ke teen second baad garaj sune to samjho toofan taqreeban aik kilometer door hai. Ye hisaab bhi paper me ata hai.

Ab rainbow, yani qaus-e-quzah. Barish ke baad jab sooraj nikalta hai aur tumhari peeth sooraj ki taraf ho, to samne asmaan par rangon ka aik bada sa aadha gol qaus ban jata hai. Wajah ye hai ke hawa me reh jane wale barish ke qatray chhote sheeshon ki tarah kaam karte hain. Sooraj ki safed roshni qatre ke andar ja kar apne saat rangon me bant jati hai. Saat rangon ki tartib yaad karne ka lafz hai VIBGYOR. Is ke har harf se rang nikalo. V se Violet yani banafshi, I se Indigo yani gehara neela, B se Blue yani neela, G se Green yani sabz, Y se Yellow yani peela, O se Orange yani narangi, R se Red yani lal. Qaus me sab se upar lal hota hai aur sab se neeche banafshi. Paper poochta hai, rainbow me kul kitne rang hote hain? Saat. Aur safed roshni ko rangon me bantne ko dispersion kehte hain.

Tasveer ka khayal: Aik taraf sooraj, dusri taraf barish ke qatray, aur darmiyan me saat rangon ki patti bani hui qaus dikhe, har rang par us ke naam ka label tartib se lage. Neeche garaj chamak wali chhoti tasveer me baadal se chamak pehle aur awaaz ki lehrain baad me pohanchti hui dikhayi jayen.

Greenhouse effect aur global warming

Ab aik kambal wali misaal se bari baat samjho. Sardion me tum kambal orhte ho to jism ki garmi andar ruk jati hai aur tum garam rehte ho. Zameen ke gird hawa ki teh bhi aisa hi kambal hai. Sooraj ki garmi zameen tak ati hai, lekin jab zameen use wapas khala ki taraf phenkti hai to hawa me mojood kuch gasain, khaas tor par carbon dioxide aur methane, us garmi ka kuch hissa rok leti hain. Is amal ko greenhouse effect kehte hain. Greenhouse asal me sheeshay ka wo ghar hota hai jo thande ilaqon me pauday ugane ke liye banaya jata hai, sheesha garmi andar rok leta hai.

Aik baat saaf samajh lo, greenhouse effect bura nahi hai. Agar ye bilkul na hota to zameen itni thandi ho jati ke zindagi mumkin na rehti. Masla tab banta hai jab hum koyla, petrol aur gas jala jala kar hawa me carbon dioxide bohat zyada kar dete hain. Kambal mota ho jata hai, garmi zyada rukti hai, aur zameen ka darja hararat ahista ahista barhne lagta hai. Isi ko global warming kehte hain. Is ke nateeje paper me yun ate hain. Glacier pighalte hain, samandar ki satah unchi hoti hai, mausam bigarte hain, kabhi shadeed garmi aur kabhi achanak barish. Paper ka sawal, global warming ki sab se bari wajah konsi gas hai? Carbon dioxide.

Tasveer ka khayal: Zameen gol bani ho, us ke gird hawa ki teh kambal ki shakal me dikhe. Sooraj ki kirnein andar ati hui aur wapas jati garmi ki kirnon ka kuch hissa kambal se takra kar rukta hua dikhaya jaye. Side me factory aur gari se nikalti carbon dioxide par label lage.

Chaand, sooraj aur grehan, dikhne wali baat

Aakhir me asmaan ki do mashhoor tasveerein. Zameen sooraj ke gird ghoomti hai aur chaand zameen ke gird ghoomta hai. Jab ghoomte ghoomte chaand sooraj aur zameen ke bilkul darmiyan me a jata hai, to chaand ka saya zameen par parta hai aur din ke waqt sooraj chhup jata hai. Isay sooraj grehan kehte hain. Aur jab zameen sooraj aur chaand ke darmiyan a jati hai, to zameen ka saya chaand par parta hai aur chaand ghana ho jata hai. Isay chaand grehan kehte hain. Farq sirf itna yaad rakho, sooraj grehan me chaand darmiyan me, chaand grehan me zameen darmiyan me. Grehan ki mukammal tafseel, chaand ki shaklain aur asmaan ki baqi cheezain Space wale hisse me hain, wahan se ye topic aur gehra ho jayega.

Tasveer ka khayal: Do alag alag saf tasveerein hon. Pehli me sooraj, darmiyan me chaand, phir zameen aik line me, chaand ka saya zameen par parta hua dikhe aur label lage sooraj grehan. Dusri me sooraj, darmiyan me zameen, phir chaand, zameen ka saya chaand par parta hua dikhe aur label lage chaand grehan.

Aakhir me paper ke fix nuktaat

Chalo ab poore hisse ki jaan aik jagah dohra lo. Phool ka nar hissa stamen hai aur pollen anther me banta hai. Mada hissa pistil hai, beej ovule se aur phal ovary se banta hai. Pollen ke stigma tak pohanchne ko pollination kehte hain. Photosynthesis me pauda pani, carbon dioxide aur roshni se glucose banata hai aur oxygen kharij karta hai. Patte ka sabz rang chlorophyll ki wajah se hai. Germination me sab se pehle jarr yani radicle nikalti hai, aur is ke liye pani, hawa aur munasib garmi lazmi hain. Pani ke cycle ka pehla marhala evaporation hai aur baadal condensation se bante hain. Chamak pehle is liye dikhti hai kyunke roshni ki raftaar awaaz se kahin zyada hai. Rainbow me saat rang hote hain aur tartib VIBGYOR hai. Global warming ki sab se bari wajah carbon dioxide hai. Sooraj grehan me chaand darmiyan me ata hai, chaand grehan me zameen.
