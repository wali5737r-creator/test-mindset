Pakistan ki Sarhadein
Har sarhad, us ki lambai, us ki kahani aur paper ke sawal

Ye file kyun zaroori hai

Dekho, one paper me geography ke hisse se har saal sarhadon par sawal ata hai. Sab se lambi sarhad konsi hai, sab se chhoti konsi hai, Durand Line kis saal bani, Khunjerab kahan hai, ye wo sawal hain jo lag bhag har paper me dohraye jate hain. Aur mazay ki baat ye hai ke ye sawal mushkil bilkul nahi hote. Bas chand lambaiyan aur chand tareekhein sahi sahi yaad karni hain. Naqshay par ye sarhadein dekh kar parho ge to ye number zindagi bhar nahi bhoolo ge, kyunke aankh se dekhi hui jagah zehen me beth jati hai.

Pehle pura manzar samajh lo. Pakistan ki zameeni sarhadein chaar mulkon ke sath lagti hain. Maghrib me Afghanistan aur Iran hain, shimal me China hai, mashriq me India hai. Aur junoob me koi mulk nahi, wahan Arabian Sea hai. Kashmir wali taraf jo line hai use international sarhad nahi kehte, use Line of Control kehte hain, aur us ki kahani sab se alag hai. Ab aik aik karke har sarhad ko samajhte hain.

Afghanistan ki sarhad aur Durand Line

Pakistan ki dusri sab se lambi zameeni sarhad Afghanistan ke sath hai. Is ki lambai 2,430 kilometre hai. Kuch kitaabon me 2,640 kilometre bhi likha milta hai, lekin one paper ki mashhoor textbook rakam 2,430 kilometre hi hai, isay hi pakka yaad rakho.

Is sarhad ka naam Durand Line hai. Ye naam aik Angrez afsar Sir Mortimer Durand ke naam par para. 12 November 1893 ko Kabul me Durand aur Afghanistan ke Amir Abdur Rahman Khan ke darmiyan muahida hua aur ye line khanchi gayi. Us waqt hadaf ye tha ke British India aur Afghanistan ke darmiyan aik buffer wali sarhad ban jaye. Yahi wajah hai ke ye line aik hi qaum, yani Pakhtun qabail, ko do mulkon me baant deti hai. Ye baat aksar sawal banti hai.

Ye sarhad Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, purane FATA wale ilaqe aur Balochistan ke shimal maghribi hisse se guzarti hai. Is par mashhoor guzargahen Torkham aur Chaman hain. Torkham Khyber Pass ke qareeb hai aur yehi Pakistan se Jalalabad aur Kabul jane wala sab se bara raasta hai. Chaman Balochistan me hai, us paar Afghanistan ka Spin Boldak hai, aur ye Quetta se Kandahar jane ka raasta hai. Aik aur dilchasp baat yaad rakho. Pakistan aur Tajikistan ke darmiyan koi sarhad nahi lagti, kyunke darmiyan me Afghanistan ki aik patli si patti hai jise Wakhan Corridor kehte hain. Ye bhi paper ka pasandeeda sawal hai.

Is sarhad ka pin hum Torkham par laga rahe hain, kyunke ye is ka sab se mashhoor nuqta hai.
Pin: 34.12, 71.10

Iran ki sarhad

Pakistan ki teesri lambi zameeni sarhad Iran ke sath hai. Is ki lambai 909 kilometre hai. Ye sarhad mukammal Balochistan ke sath lagti hai, jahan dono taraf Baloch aabaadi rehti hai.

Is sarhad ki pehli haddbandi unneeswin sadi me Angrezon ke daur me hui, jis me Goldsmid wali commission ka naam aksar kitaabon me ata hai. Is par sab se mashhoor guzargah Taftan hai. Taftan Pakistan ki taraf ka sheher hai aur sarhad ke us paar Iran ka Mirjaveh hai. Quetta se Zahedan jane wali road aur railway isi raaste se guzarti hai. Ziyarat par jane wale zaireen bhi aam taur par isi guzargah se Iran jate hain. Yaad rakho, Gwadar se Iran ki sarhad zyada door nahi, aur yahi Pakistan ki sarhad ka sab se maghribi kinara hai.

Is sarhad ka pin Taftan par hai, kyunke ye is ki sab se mashhoor guzargah hai.
Pin: 28.60, 61.60

China ki sarhad

Pakistan ki sab se chhoti zameeni sarhad China ke sath hai. Is ki lambai sirf 595 kilometre hai. Lambai me ye sab se chhoti hai, lekin unchai me ye duniya ki sab se unchi sarhadon me se hai, kyunke ye Karakoram ke barfeelay paharon ke upar se guzarti hai. Ye sarhad Gilgit Baltistan ko China ke Xinjiang ilaqe se milati hai.

Pakistan aur China ke darmiyan sarhad ka muahida 2 March 1963 ko hua. Is sarhad ka sab se mashhoor nuqta Khunjerab Pass hai. Khunjerab ki unchai taqreeban 4,693 metre hai aur ye duniya ki sab se unchi paved international guzargah mani jati hai, yani is unchai par pakki sarak wali sarhadi guzargah duniya me aur koi nahi. Mashhoor Karakoram Highway, jo Pakistan ko China se milati hai, isi darre se guzarti hai. Barf ki wajah se ye guzargah saal ke kuch hisse me band bhi rehti hai, lekin ab tunnel aur intezamat ki wajah se ise saal bhar khula rakhne ki koshishein ho rahi hain.

Paper ke liye teen baatein is sarhad se pakki kar lo. Sab se chhoti sarhad China wali hai. Muahida 1963 ka hai. Aur Khunjerab duniya ki buland tareen paved guzargah hai.

Is sarhad ka pin Khunjerab Pass par hai.
Pin: 36.85, 75.42

India ki sarhad aur Radcliffe Line

Pakistan ki sab se lambi zameeni sarhad India ke sath hai. Is ki lambai 2,912 kilometre hai. Jab bhi paper me sab se lambi sarhad poochi jaye, jawab hamesha India hai. Ye sarhad Punjab aur Sindh ke mashriqi kinare par chalti hai.

Is sarhad ka naam Radcliffe Line bhi hai. 1947 me jab Bartania ne Hindustan ko do mulkon me baanta, to Punjab aur Bengal ki taqseem ke liye aik boundary commission bana jis ke sarbarah Sir Cyril Radcliffe the. Unhon ne ye line banayi, aur is ka elan 17 August 1947 ko hua, yani azaadi ke baad. Itni jaldi aur itni kam maloomat ke sath banayi gayi ye line aage chalkar bohat se tanazaat ki jar bani.

Is sarhad par sab se mashhoor guzargah Wagah hai, jo Lahore ke qareeb hai. Dusri taraf India ka Attari gaon hai. Wagah par har sham honay wali parcham utarne ki taqreeb, jise beating retreat kehte hain, duniya bhar me mashhoor hai. Is ke ilawa Ganda Singh Wala, jo Kasur ke qareeb hai, aur Sindh ki taraf Khokhrapar bhi purani guzargahen hain.

Is sarhad ka pin Wagah par hai.
Pin: 31.60, 74.57

Kashmir wali Line of Control

Ab us line ki baat jo sarhad hai bhi aur nahi bhi. Jammu wa Kashmir ke mutanaza ilaqe me Pakistan aur India ke darmiyan jo line hai use Line of Control kehte hain, mukammal naam me ise LoC likhte hain. Is ki lambai taqreeban 740 kilometre hai.

Iski kahani yun hai. 1947 aur 1948 ki pehli Kashmir jang ke baad 1949 me aik ceasefire line ban gayi thi. Phir 1971 ki jang ke baad 2 July 1972 ko Pakistan ke Zulfikar Ali Bhutto aur India ki Indira Gandhi ke darmiyan Simla muahida hua. Us muahide ke baad purani ceasefire line ko naya naam diya gaya, Line of Control. Is liye paper me jab poocha jaye ke LoC kab bani ya kis muahide ke baad bani, to jawab 1972 ka Simla muahida hai.

Aik baat zehen me bitha lo. LoC international sarhad nahi hai. Ye aik aarzi foji line hai jo us ilaqe ko baanti hai jo is waqt jis ke qabze me hai. Ye line Chhamb ke ilaqe se shuru hokar shimal me Siachen glacier ke qareeb aik nuqte tak jati hai, jisay nakshon me NJ9842 likha jata hai. Isi liye is ka pin hum Azad Kashmir ke darul hukumat Muzaffarabad ke qareeb laga rahe hain, jo LoC ke bilkul qareeb ka sheher hai.

Is line ka pin Muzaffarabad par hai.
Pin: 34.37, 74.47

Arabian Sea ki saheli sarhad

Junoob me Pakistan ki koi zameeni sarhad nahi. Wahan Arabian Sea hai, aur Pakistan ki sahel patti ki kul lambai 1,046 kilometre hai. Ye bhi paper ka bohat mashhoor number hai, isay alag se yaad rakho.

Is 1,046 kilometre me taqreeban 750 kilometre sahel Balochistan ke sath hai aur taqreeban 296 kilometre Sindh ke sath. Balochistan wala hissa lamba, khushk aur pahari hai, use Makran coast kehte hain. Sindh wala hissa Indus delta ki wajah se kichar, mangrove janglaat aur chhoti khaadion se bhara hua hai. Sahel ki tafseel, bandargahen aur jazeeray is series ki teesri file me hain, yahan bas ye number pakka kar lo ke kul sahel 1,046 kilometre hai aur us ka bara hissa Balochistan ka hai.

Is sahel ka pin Karachi par hai, kyunke ye Pakistan ka sab se bara saheli sheher hai.
Pin: 24.86, 67.00

Sawal numa baatein aik nazar me

Ab wo tamaam baatein jo paper me seedha sawal bankar ati hain. Inhen aik aik karke bol kar parho.

2. Dusri lambi sarhad Afghanistan ke sath hai, 2,430 kilometre, aur us ka naam Durand Line hai.
3. Durand Line 1893 me bani, muahida Sir Mortimer Durand aur Amir Abdur Rahman Khan ke darmiyan hua.
4. Iran ki sarhad 909 kilometre hai aur us ki mashhoor guzargah Taftan hai.
5. Sab se chhoti zameeni sarhad China ke sath hai, sirf 595 kilometre, aur sarhadi muahida 1963 me hua.
6. Khunjerab Pass duniya ki buland tareen paved international guzargah hai, unchai taqreeban 4,693 metre.
7. India wali sarhad ko Radcliffe Line kehte hain, 1947 me Sir Cyril Radcliffe ne banayi, aur us ki mashhoor guzargah Wagah hai.
8. Line of Control ki lambai taqreeban 740 kilometre hai, aur ye 1972 ke Simla muahide ke baad is naam se bani. Ye international sarhad nahi hai.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Lambaiyon ko yaad karne ka sab se asaan tareeqa tartib hai. Bare se chhote ki taraf ao. India sab se bara, 2,912. Phir Afghanistan, 2,430. Phir Iran, 909. Aur aakhir me China, 595. Bas ye jumla zehen me bitha lo: bara bhai India, darmiyan me Afghan, phir Iran, aur sab se chhota China. Aur sahel ko in se alag rakho, wo zameen nahi pani hai, 1,046. Jab bhi paper me koi lambai poochi jaye, pehle ye tartib dimagh me chalao, jawab khud nikal ayega.
