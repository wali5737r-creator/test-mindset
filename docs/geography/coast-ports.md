Sahel aur Bandargahen
Arabian Sea, Pakistan ki bandargahen, saheli sheher aur jazeeray

Ye file kyun zaroori hai

Dekho, Pakistan ka junoobi kinara samandar hai, aur samandar hi se mulk ki tijarat chalti hai. Mulk ka taqreeban tamam bahri samaan inhi bandargahon se ata jata hai. One paper me is hisse se sawal lazmi ata hai. Pakistan ki sahel patti kitni lambi hai, sab se bari bandargah konsi hai, Gwadar kahan hai, Astola kya hai, Indus delta kahan banta hai, ye wo sawal hain jo har dusre paper me nazar ate hain.

Pehle aik tasveer zehen me banao. Pakistan ke junoob me Arabian Sea hai. Us ke kinare do sobay lagte hain, mashriq me Sindh aur maghrib me Balochistan. Sindh ka sahel kichar aur khaadion wala hai, Balochistan ka sahel lamba, khushk aur pahari hai. Ab aik aik karke sab ko dekhte hain. Har cheez ke sath us ka pin hai taake naqshay par jagah dekh kar yaad ho jaye.

Arabian Sea

Arabian Sea Hindustan ke bare samandar, yani Indian Ocean, ka shimal maghribi hissa hai. Pakistan ki puri sahel patti isi samandar ke sath hai. Mashriq me India, maghrib me Oman aur Yemen, aur shimal me Pakistan aur Iran is ke kinare par hain.

Pakistan ke liye is samandar ki ahemiyat bohat bari hai. Mulk ki tamam bahri tijarat isi se hoti hai. Karachi aur Gwadar dono isi ke kinare hain. Aur aik jughrafiyayi baat yaad rakho, Gwadar se aage maghrib me Hormuz ki aabnaye hai jahan se duniya ke tel ka bara hissa guzarta hai, is liye Pakistan ka sahel duniya ki aham tareen bahri guzargahon ke qareeb hai. Monsoon ki hawaein bhi isi samandar se uth kar Pakistan me barish lati hain.

Is ka pin Karachi ke junoob me khule samandar par hai.
Pin: 24.00, 66.50

Makran Coast aur Pakistan ki sahel patti

Pakistan ki kul sahel patti 1,046 kilometre lambi hai. Is me taqreeban 750 kilometre Balochistan ke sath hai aur taqreeban 296 kilometre Sindh ke sath. Ye number is series ki pehli file me bhi aya tha, yahan dobara is liye de raha hun ke paper me ye sab se zyada poocha jane wala saheli number hai.

Balochistan wale hisse ko Makran Coast kehte hain. Ye Hub darya ke ilaqe se shuru hokar maghrib me Jiwani aur Iran ki sarhad tak jata hai. Ye sahel khushk, pahari aur kam aabaad hai. Yahan barish bohat kam hoti hai, lekin samandar machli se bhara hai, is liye yahan ke logon ka rozgar zyada tar machli pakarna hai. Makran Coastal Highway, jis ka number N 10 hai, Karachi ko Gwadar se milati hai aur is ki lambai taqreeban 653 kilometre hai. Is sarak ke banne ke baad Makran ke chhote sheher mulk se jur gaye.

Sindh wala sahel is ke baraks hai. Wahan Indus delta ki wajah se kichar, chhoti khaadian aur mangrove ke janglaat hain. Dono sahelon ka ye farq paper me aksar sawal banta hai.

Is ka pin Makran ke darmiyan me rakha hai.
Pin: 25.30, 64.00

Karachi Port

Karachi Port Pakistan ki sab se purani aur sab se bari bandargah hai. Ye Karachi ke junoob maghrib me aik qudrati bandargah par bani hai, jahan Manora aur Kemari ke darmiyan pani gehra aur mehfooz hai.

Is bandargah ki buniyad unneeswin sadi me Angrezon ke daur me mazboot hui aur Karachi Port Trust 1887 me bana. Aaj mulk ke bahri samaan ka sab se bara hissa, taqreeban 60 feesad, yahin se ata jata hai. Tel, anaaj, khaad aur containers, sab yahan utarte hain. Karachi sheher ki taraqqi ki sab se bari wajah yahi bandargah hai.

Paper ke liye yaad rakho. Sab se bari aur sab se purani bandargah Karachi hai. Aur Karachi Port mulk ke samaan ka sab se bara hissa sambhalti hai.

Pin: 24.83, 66.98

Port Qasim

Port Qasim Karachi se taqreeban 50 kilometre mashriq me hai, Indus delta ki Phitti khaari par. Ye Pakistan ki dusri bari bandargah hai. Ise 1980 me chaloo kiya gaya tha.

Is bandargah ka naam Muhammad bin Qasim ke naam par rakha gaya hai, jin ke zariye is ilaqe me Islam ki pehli bari pesh qadmi hui thi. Port Qasim is liye banayi gayi thi taake Karachi Port ka bojh kam ho. Aaj mulk ke samaan ka taqreeban 40 feesad hissa yahan se guzarta hai. Is ke qareeb Pakistan Steel Mills aur Bin Qasim ke sanati ilaqe hain, aur koyla aur LNG ke terminal bhi yahin hain.

Dono bandargahon ka farq saaf rakho. Karachi purani aur sab se bari hai, Port Qasim 1980 me bani aur dusri bari hai.

Pin: 24.78, 67.33

Gwadar Port

Gwadar Balochistan ke junoob maghribi kinare par hai, Karachi se taqreeban 460 kilometre maghrib me. Ye Pakistan ki teesri bari bandargah hai, lekin ahemiyat me sab se zyada baat isi ki hoti hai.

Gwadar aik gehray pani wali bandargah hai, yani yahan bare se bara jehaz asani se langar daal sakta hai. Iski jagah is ki sab se bari taqat hai. Ye Iran ki sarhad se taqreeban 72 kilometre door hai aur Hormuz ki aabnaye se taqreeban 400 kilometre ke faaslay par hai. Isi liye China ne CPEC, yani China Pakistan Economic Corridor, ka markaz Gwadar ko banaya hai. Bandargah ki pehli tameer 2002 se 2006 ke darmiyan China ki madad se hui, aur 2013 me is ka intezam aik Chinese company, China Overseas Port Holding Company, ko diya gaya. Yahan free zone bhi ban raha hai jahan sanatein lagengi.

Gwadar ka purana pesha machli pakarna tha aur aaj bhi sheher ki bari aabadi ka yahi rozgar hai. Yaad rakho, Gwadar 1958 tak Oman ki malkiat tha. Pakistan ne ise 8 September 1958 ko Oman se khareeda tha. Ye tareekh paper me bohat ati hai.

Pin: 25.11, 62.34

Ormara

Ormara Balochistan ke Makran sahel par aik chhota saheli sheher hai, Gwadar aur Karachi ke darmiyan. Ye aik hathore jaisi shakal wali zameen ke gird hai, jisay hammerhead kehte hain.

Ormara ka pesha machli pakarna hai. Yahan Pakistan Bahria ka Jinnah Naval Base hai, jo is sheher ki sab se bari pehchan hai. Makran Coastal Highway yahin se guzarti hai aur raste me yahan ke pahari manazir, jin me Princess of Hope jaisi qudrati pathar ki shaklein mashhoor hain, Hingol wale ilaqe me nazar ate hain.

Pin: 25.21, 64.63

Pasni

Pasni bhi Makran sahel ka sheher hai, Gwadar se mashriq ki taraf. Ye Balochistan ke mashhoor machli pakarne wale shehron me se hai. Yahan aik fish harbour hai jahan se machli mulk bhar me aur bahar bheji jati hai.

Pasni ke bare me aik baat khaas yaad rakho. Pakistan ka sab se bara jazeera Astola yahan se taqreeban 25 kilometre door samandar me hai. Pasni hi se kashtiyan Astola jati hain. Is ke ilawa Pasni ke qareeb ke ret ke teelay aur sahel apni khoobsurti ki wajah se mashhoor hain.

Pin: 25.26, 63.47

Jiwani

Jiwani Pakistan ke sahel ka sab se maghribi sheher hai, Iran ki sarhad ke bilkul qareeb. Gwadar se maghrib ki taraf ye aakhri bari saheli aabadi hai.

Jiwani ke qareeb mangrove ke janglaat hain aur ye ilaqa samandari kachhuon ke liye aham mana jata hai. Dusri aalami jang ke zamane me yahan aik hawai adda bhi istemal hua tha, jis ke aasaar aaj bhi milte hain. Jiwani ke bilkul sath sarhad par Gabd ka ilaqa hai jahan se Iran me dakhil hote hain.

Yaad rakho, sahel par safar karo to tartib ye hai. Karachi se chalo to Ormara, phir Pasni, phir Gwadar, aur sab se aakhir me Jiwani. Ye tartib paper me kabhi kabhi pooch li jati hai.

Pin: 25.05, 61.75

Keti Bandar

Keti Bandar Sindh ke zila Thatta me hai, Indus delta ke ilaqe me. Ye Karachi ki tarah bari bandargah nahi, aik chhoti machli pakarne wali bandargah hai.

Lekin is ki tareekh bari hai. Aik zamane me Keti Bandar is ilaqe ki aham tijarati bandargah thi, jahan se Sindh ka samaan bahar jata tha. Waqt ke sath darya ke raaste badle, delta me kichar barh gaya aur ye bandargah peeche reh gayi. Aaj yahan ke logon ka rozgar machli aur delta ke mangrove janglaat se jura hai. Waqtan fawaqtan yahan nayi bandargah banane ki tajweez ati rehti hai.

Pin: 24.14, 67.45

Astola Jazeera

Astola Pakistan ka sab se bara jazeera hai. Ye Pasni ke sahel se taqreeban 25 kilometre door Arabian Sea me hai. Is ki lambai taqreeban 6.7 kilometre hai. Ise Haft Talar bhi kehte hain, jis ke maana hain saat pahariyan, kyunke is par saat chhoti pahariyan nazar ati hain.

Is jazeere par koi mustaqil aabadi nahi. Machli pakarne wale mausam me yahan aakar dera dalte hain. Yahan aik purana lighthouse hai. June 2017 me Astola ko Pakistan ka pehla Marine Protected Area qarar diya gaya, yani ye samandari hayat ke tahaffuz ke liye mehfooz ilaqa hai. Yahan samandari kachhue anday dene ate hain aur ye parindon ke liye bhi aham jagah hai. Purani rivayaton me yahan Kali Devi ke mandir ka zikr bhi milta hai.

Paper ke liye teen baatein pakki kar lo. Sab se bara jazeera Astola hai. Dusra naam Haft Talar hai. Aur ye Pakistan ka pehla marine protected area hai.

Pin: 25.12, 63.85

Bundal Jazeera

Bundal Jazeera Karachi ke sahel ke qareeb hai, Korangi wali khaari ke muhnay par. Ise Bhundar bhi kaha jata hai. Ye Indus delta ke maghribi kinare ke jazair me se hai.

Ye jazeera kam aabaad hai aur is ke gird mangrove ke janglaat hain jo machliyon ki afzaish ke liye bohat aham hain. Karachi ke qareeb honay ki wajah se is par taraqqiati mansoobon ki baatein aksar khabron me ati rehti hain, lekin maahireen e maholiat in mangrove janglaat ke tahaffuz par zor dete hain.

Pin: 24.73, 67.12

Baba aur Bhit Jazair

Baba aur Bhit Karachi ki bandargah ke andar waaqe chhote jazeeray hain, Kemari ke bilkul qareeb. Ye Karachi ki sab se purani aabadiyon me se hain.

In jazair par machli pakarne walon ki bastiyan hain jo kayi naslon se yahan reh rahi hain. Yahan gariyan nahi chalti, ana jana kashti se hota hai. Sheher ke itne qareeb hone ke bawajood in bastiyon ki zindagi aaj bhi purane andaz ki hai. Bhit ko Bhit Shah se mila mat dena, Bhit Shah andaroon e Sindh ka sheher hai jahan Shah Abdul Latif Bhittai ka mazaar hai, ye Bhit aik jazeera hai.

Pin: 24.83, 66.96

Manora

Manora Karachi ki bandargah ke muhnay par hai. Ye asal me aik jazeera tha, lekin ab aik band aur sarak ke zariye sheher se jur gaya hai, is liye ise jazeera numa kahna zyada durust hai.

Manora ki pehchan yahan ka purana lighthouse hai jo 1889 me bana tha aur aaj bhi jehazon ko raasta dikhata hai. Yahan aik purana girja, Saint Paul Church, bhi hai. Manora ka sahel Karachi walon ke liye sair ki mashhoor jagah hai aur haal hi me yahan promenade banaya gaya hai. Bandargah ke daakhle par honay ki wajah se ye jagah foji lehaaz se bhi hamesha aham rahi hai.

Pin: 24.80, 66.97

Churna Jazeera

Churna Karachi ke maghrib me hai, Mubarak Village ke sahel ke samne. Ye aik chhota, ghair aabaad, pathreela jazeera hai.

Churna aaj kal apni samandari sair ki wajah se mashhoor hai. Yahan log snorkeling, scuba diving aur cliff diving ke liye jate hain, kyunke yahan ka pani saaf hai aur patharon ke gird rang barang machliyan aur samandari hayat nazar ati hai. Sardiyon me yahan sair ka khaas mausam hota hai.

Pin: 24.90, 66.60

Indus Delta

Ab is file ki sab se aham cheez. Jahan Darya e Sindh Arabian Sea me girta hai, wahan Indus Delta banta hai. Delta us ilaqe ko kehte hain jahan darya samandar se milne se pehle kayi chhoti shakhon me bat jata hai aur apne sath layi hui mitti bichata hai.

Indus Delta Thatta aur Sujawal ke junoob me phaila hai. Is me 17 bari khaadian, jinhen creek kehte hain, aur wasee mangrove janglaat hain. Ye mangrove duniya ke bare khushk ilaqaati mangrove janglat me se hain. Ye janglaat machliyon aur jheengon ki afzaish ki jagah hain aur saheli aabadi ko toofani leharoon se bachate hain. Indus Delta ko Ramsar list me shamil kiya gaya hai, jo aalmi ahemiyat wali aabi jagahon ki list hai.

Aik pareshan kun baat bhi yaad rakho jo paper me sawal banti hai. Darya ka pani kam honay ki wajah se delta tak meetha pani bohat kam pohanchta hai. Is se samandar ki khaari leharen zameen ke andar ghus rahi hain, mangrove kam ho rahe hain aur delta sikur raha hai. Ise seawater intrusion kehte hain.

Is ka pin delta ke darmiyan me hai, Keti Bandar ke junoob mashriq me.
Pin: 23.95, 67.85

Aakhir me sahel ki tartib yaad kar lo

Agar naqshay par Karachi se maghrib ki taraf ungli chalao to tartib ye banti hai. Karachi, phir Hub ke baad Balochistan shuru, phir Ormara, phir Pasni, phir Gwadar, aur aakhir me Jiwani jahan Iran ki sarhad hai. Aur Karachi se mashriq ki taraf jao to Port Qasim, phir Keti Bandar aur phir Indus Delta. Ye tartib aik dafa naqshay par dekh lo to koi saheli sheher kabhi ghalat jagah par nahi rakho ge. Aur teen number hamesha tayyar rakho. Kul sahel 1,046 kilometre, sab se bari bandargah Karachi, aur sab se bara jazeera Astola.
