Bandh aur Barrage
Pakistan ke dams aur barrages ki mukammal kahani

Ye hissa kyun zaroori hai

Dekho, pehle dam aur barrage ka farq samajh lo, kyunke paper me yehi farq phansata hai. Dam aik unchi deewaar hoti hai jo darya ko rok kar peeche aik bari jheel bana leti hai. Us jheel ko reservoir kehte hain. Dam pani zakheera bhi karta hai aur bijli bhi banata hai. Barrage aik neechi deewaar hoti hai jo pani ko zakheera nahi karti, bas darya ke pani ka rukh mor kar nehron me daal deti hai. Barrage ka asal kaam aabpashi hai, kheton tak pani pohanchana. To qanoon ye bana ke jahan pani rokna aur bijli banana maqsad ho wahan dam, aur jahan pani nehron me baantna maqsad ho wahan barrage. Ab har dam aur barrage ko isi nazar se parho, kis darya par hai, kahan hai, aur us ki wo khaas baat jo paper me ati hai.

Tarbela Dam

Tarbela Pakistan ka sab se mashhoor dam hai aur Indus River par bana hai. Ye Khyber Pakhtunkhwa me Haripur ke qareeb hai, jahan Indus paharon se nikal kar medaan ki taraf ata hai. Tarbela ki sab se bari pehchan ye hai ke ye duniya ka sab se bara earth filled dam hai, yani mitti aur pathar se bhara hua sab se bara bandh. Earth filled wala point paper me itni dafa aya hai ke isay dam ki pehchan samajh lo.

Tarbela 1974 me mukammal hua tha. Is ka kaam do hain, bijli banana aur pani zakheera karna. Iski bijli ki salahiyat waqt ke sath barhti rahi hai aur ab taqreeban 4,888 megawatt hai, jo Pakistan ke kisi bhi aik dam se sab se zyada hai. Jab bhi paper me sab se bara dam ya sab se zyada bijli wala dam poocha jaye, jawab Tarbela hota hai.

Pin: 34.09, 72.70

Mangla Dam

Mangla Dam Jhelum River par bana hai, Azad Kashmir me Mirpur ke qareeb. Ye Pakistan ka dusra bara mashhoor dam hai. Mangla 1967 me mukammal hua tha, aur baad me is ki unchai mazeed 30 feet barhai gayi, jisay Mangla Raising kehte hain. Unchai barhne se jheel me pani bhi zyada zakheera hone laga aur bijli bhi barh gayi, jo ab taqreeban 1,000 megawatt se zyada hai.

Mangla ki khaas baat ye hai ke ye Indus Water Treaty ke baad banne wale pehle bare dams me se hai. Jab teen mashriqi darya Bharat ke hisse me chale gaye to un ke pani ki kami puri karne ke liye Mangla aur Tarbela banaye gaye, inhen replacement works kehte hain. Ye wala point Pak Studies ke liye bohat aham hai. Mangla ki jheel ko Mangla Lake kehte hain aur Mirpur sheher is ke kinare hai.

Pin: 33.14, 73.64

Warsak Dam

Warsak Dam Kabul River par bana hai, Peshawar ke qareeb Warsak ke maqam par. Ye azaadi ke baad Pakistan me banne wala pehla bara dam hai, jo 1960 me mukammal hua. Iski tameer me Canada ne madad ki thi, ye baat bhi kabhi kabhi paper me ati hai.

Warsak ki bijli ki salahiyat taqreeban 240 megawatt hai, jo Tarbela aur Mangla ke muqable me kam hai. Lekin is ki ahmiyat is ke size me nahi, is ke pehla hone me hai. Peshawar ki wadi ki aabpashi aur bijli dono ke liye ye dam buniyad bana. Jab paper me pehla dam poocha jaye to jawab Warsak hota hai, aur jab sab se bara poocha jaye to Tarbela. Dono sawal alag hain, in me confuse mat hona.

Pin: 34.16, 71.36

Chashma Barrage

Chashma Indus River par hai, Punjab ke district Mianwali me. Ise aam taur par Chashma Barrage kaha jata hai, lekin is se bijli bhi banti hai, taqreeban 184 megawatt, kyunke ye low head qisam ka mansuba hai, yani neechi unchai se girne wale pani se bijli banti hai.

Chashma ki sab se mashhoor pehchan is ke qareeb lage hue Pakistan ke atomic bijli ghar hain, jinhen Chashma Nuclear Power Plants kehte hain. Isi liye Chashma ka naam khabron me aksar ata hai. Barrage se Chashma Jhelum Link Canal bhi nikali gayi hai jo Indus ka pani Jhelum ki taraf le jati hai. To yaad rakho, Chashma Indus par hai, Mianwali ke qareeb hai, aur atomic bijli gharon ki wajah se mashhoor hai.

Pin: 32.43, 71.38

Ghazi Barotha

Ghazi Barotha Indus River par bana hua aik khaas qisam ka mansuba hai. Is me koi bari jheel nahi banti. Ghazi ke maqam par, jo Haripur ke qareeb hai, Indus se pani aik lambay channel ke zariye Barotha tak le jaya jata hai, jo Attock ke qareeb hai, aur wahan bijli ghar bana hai. Ise run of the river mansuba kehte hain, yani behta pani seedha istemal hota hai, zakheera nahi hota.

Is ki bijli ki salahiyat taqreeban 1,450 megawatt hai, jo bohat zyada hai. Ye 2003 me mukammal hua. Paper me ye is liye ata hai ke students isay aam dam samajh lete hain, jabke is ki technique alag hai. Yaad rakho, Ghazi Barotha Indus par hai, bijli ke liye hai, aur is me bara reservoir nahi hai.

Pin: 33.78, 72.43

Diamer Basha Dam

Diamer Basha Dam Indus River par ban raha hai, Gilgit Baltistan me Chilas ke qareeb, Diamer ke ilaaqe me. Sab se pehle ye baat wazeh zehen me rakho ke ye dam abhi mukammal nahi hua, tameer jaari hai. Paper me kabhi kabhi jaan bujh kar isay mukammal dams ki list me daal kar phansaya jata hai.

Jab ye mukammal hoga to ye duniya ke unche tareen roller compacted concrete dams me se hoga, is ki unchai taqreeban 272 metre hogi, aur is ki bijli ki salahiyat taqreeban 4,500 megawatt rakhi gayi hai. Ye Tarbela ke baad Indus par dusra meega mansuba hai. Is ka aik bara faida ye bhi hoga ke Indus ka pani zakheera honay se Tarbela ki jheel me mitti bharne ki raftaar kam ho jayegi aur Tarbela ki umar barh jayegi.

Pin: 35.52, 73.74

Guddu Barrage

Guddu Barrage Indus River par hai, Sindh ke shimal me Kashmore ke qareeb, jahan Indus Punjab se nikal kar Sindh me dakhil hota hai. Ye 1962 me mukammal hua tha. Guddu Sindh ka pehla bara barrage hai Indus ke safar me, kyunke Indus sab se pehle yahin se Sindh ke medaan me theek tarah dakhil hota hai.

Guddu se nikalne wali nehrein shimali Sindh ke ilaaqon ko sairaab karti hain. Is ke qareeb Guddu Thermal Power Plant bhi hai jo gas se bijli banata hai. To Guddu ki pehchan teen cheezon se hai, Indus par hai, Kashmore ke qareeb hai, aur Sindh ke darwaze par hai.

Pin: 28.42, 69.73

Sukkur Barrage

Sukkur Barrage Indus River par Sukkur sheher ke qareeb bana hai. Ye Pakistan ke sab se purane aur sab se mashhoor barrages me se hai. Ye 1932 me angrezon ke daur me mukammal hua tha, aur us waqt is ka naam Lloyd Barrage tha. Purana naam paper me bohat ata hai.

Sukkur Barrage ki khaas baat is ki nehrein hain. Is se saat bari nehrein nikalti hain jo Sindh ke bare hisse ko sairaab karti hain, aur apne waqt me ye duniya ke bare tareen aabpashi nizam me se aik tha. Sukkur ke qareeb Indus ke darmiyan aik chhota sa jazeera numa ilaaqa aur Lansdowne Bridge bhi mashhoor hai. Yaad rakho, Sukkur Barrage 1932 ka hai, Indus par hai, aur Sindh ki aabpashi ki rirh ki haddi hai.

Pin: 27.68, 68.85

Kotri Barrage

Kotri Barrage Indus River par hai, Jamshoro ke qareeb, Hyderabad ke bilkul paas. Ye 1955 me mukammal hua tha. Ise Ghulam Muhammad Barrage bhi kehte hain, ye dusra naam bhi paper me ata hai.

Kotri Indus par Pakistan ka aakhri barrage hai. Is ke baad Indus aage barh kar Thatta ke qareeb delta banata hua Arabian Sea me mil jata hai. Kotri se nikalne wali nehrein Karachi aur Thatta samait junubi Sindh ko pani deti hain, aur Karachi ke peene ke pani ka aik hissa bhi yahin se jata hai. To Indus ke barrages ki tartib shimal se junub yun hai, Jinnah, Chashma, Taunsa, Guddu, Sukkur aur aakhir me Kotri. Ye tartib aik line me yaad kar lo, paper me tartib wala sawal banta hai.

Pin: 25.45, 68.32

Taunsa Barrage

Taunsa Barrage Indus River par hai, Punjab ke junub maghrib me, Kot Addu aur Taunsa ke ilaaqe me. Ye 1958 me mukammal hua tha. Taunsa se Dera Ghazi Khan ke ilaaqe ki nehrein nikalti hain aur Thal ke ilaaqe tak pani pohanchta hai.

Taunsa ki aik khaas pehchan ye hai ke is ke qareeb ka ilaaqa Indus Dolphin ke liye mashhoor hai, jo duniya ki nayaab tareen meethay pani ki dolphins me se hai. Guddu aur Sukkur ke darmiyan ka hissa is ki sab se bari panah gah hai, lekin Taunsa ke aas paas bhi ye payi jati hai. Aam GK me ye baat aksar ati hai.

Pin: 30.51, 70.85

Jinnah Barrage

Jinnah Barrage Indus River par hai, Kalabagh ke qareeb. Ye 1946 me mukammal hua tha, yani azaadi se aik saal pehle. Is ka naam Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah ke naam par rakha gaya hai, yehi is ki sab se asaan pehchan hai.

Jinnah Barrage se Thal Canal nikalti hai jo Thal ke registani ilaaqe ko sairaab karti hai. Khushab, Bhakkar aur Layyah ke ilaaqe is se faida uthate hain. Indus ke safar me ye Punjab ka pehla bara barrage hai jab darya paharon se nikalta hai. Tartib phir se sun lo, Indus par sab se pehle Jinnah, phir Chashma, phir Taunsa.

Pin: 32.92, 71.52

Rasul Barrage

Rasul Barrage Jhelum River par hai, Rasul ke maqam par, jo Mandi Bahauddin aur Jhelum ke darmiyan hai. Ye 1968 me mukammal hua tha. Mangla Dam ke baad Jhelum ke pani ko nehron me baantne ka kaam yehi barrage karta hai.

Rasul se Rasul Qadirabad Link Canal nikalti hai jo Jhelum ka pani Chenab ki taraf le jati hai. Link canals wala tasawwur yahan samajh lo. Pakistan me daryaaon ke darmiyan pani muntaqil karne ke liye link canals banayi gayi hain, taake jis darya me pani zyada ho us se kam wale ki taraf bheja ja sake. Rasul Qadirabad Link unhi me se aik mashhoor hai.

Pin: 32.68, 73.52

Qadirabad Barrage

Qadirabad Barrage Chenab River par hai, Hafizabad ke qareeb Qadirabad ke maqam par. Ye 1968 me mukammal hua tha. Chenab par barrages ki tartib me ye Marala aur Khanki ke baad ata hai.

Qadirabad se Qadirabad Balloki Link Canal nikalti hai jo Chenab ka pani Ravi ki taraf le jati hai. Dekho, link canals ka silsila kese banta hai. Rasul se pani Chenab me aya, Qadirabad se wahi pani Ravi ki taraf chala gaya. Isi liye ye teenon barrages, Rasul, Qadirabad aur Balloki, aksar aik sath yaad karaye jate hain.

Pin: 32.32, 73.68

Balloki Barrage

Balloki Barrage Ravi River par hai, Balloki ke maqam par, jo Nankana Sahib aur Kasur ke darmiyan hai. Ye sab se purane barrages me se hai, 1915 me mukammal hua tha. Angrezon ke daur ke is barrage se Lahore ke junubi ilaaqon ki aabpashi shuru hui thi.

Balloki ki pehchan do cheezon se hai. Aik to ye Ravi par hai, aur dusra is ke qareeb Balloki ka mashhoor picnic aur sar seer ka maqam hai jahan Ravi ka pani aur jheel numa manzar hota hai. Qadirabad Balloki Link Canal yahin aa kar milti hai, yani Chenab ka pani Ravi me yahan shamil hota hai.

Pin: 31.22, 73.87

Sulemanki Barrage

Sulemanki Barrage Sutlej River par hai, Sulemanki ke maqam par, jo Okara aur Bahawalnagar ke ilaaqon ke qareeb hai aur Pak Bharat sarhad ke bilkul paas hai. Ye 1927 me mukammal hua tha, angrezon ke daur ke Sutlej Valley Project ka hissa tha.

Sulemanki se nikalne wali nehrein Bahawalnagar aur aas paas ke ilaaqon ko sairaab karti hain. Sarhad ke qareeb hone ki wajah se ye jagah aam khabron me bhi ati hai. Yaad rakho, Sutlej par Pakistan me do aham barrages hain, Sulemanki aur Islam, aur dono Bahawalpur division ke ilaaqon ko pani dete hain.

Pin: 30.38, 73.87

Islam Barrage

Islam Barrage bhi Sutlej River par hai, Hasilpur aur Chishtian ke ilaaqe ke qareeb. Ise Islam Headworks bhi kehte hain. Ye 1927 me hi mukammal hua tha, Sulemanki ke sath hi Sutlej Valley Project ka hissa tha.

Islam Barrage se Bahawalpur ke ilaaqe ki nehrein nikalti hain. Sutlej ke is hisse me pani aam taur par kam hota hai kyunke Indus Water Treaty ke baad Sutlej ka zyada tar pani Bharat ke hisse me hai, aur Pakistan ko is me pani zyada tar sailaab ke mosam me milta hai. Ye wajah samajhna zaroori hai, kyunke paper me poocha jata hai ke Sutlej aur Ravi me pani kam kyun hai. Jawab wahi treaty hai, jo 1960 me hui thi.

Pin: 29.82, 72.72

Panjnad Barrage

Panjnad Barrage Panjnad River par hai, Uch Sharif ke qareeb, Alipur ke ilaaqe me. Pehle Panjnad ka matlab samajh lo. Panj ka matlab paanch aur nad ka matlab darya. Jhelum, Chenab, Ravi, Beas aur Sutlej, ye paanchon darya mil kar jo aik darya banate hain usay Panjnad kehte hain. Ye sangam Mithankot se pehle hota hai.

Panjnad Barrage 1933 me mukammal hua tha. Yahan se nikalne wali nehrein Bahawalpur aur Rahim Yar Khan ke ilaaqon ko sairaab karti hain. Panjnad ke bilkul thora aage ye mila hua pani Mithankot ke qareeb Indus me mil jata hai. To Punjab ke paanch daryaaon ki kahani ka aakhri maqam Panjnad aur Mithankot hai. Ye dono naam jor kar yaad rakho.

Pin: 29.35, 71.02

Khanpur Dam

Khanpur Dam Haro River par bana hai, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa me Khanpur ke qareeb, Haripur ke ilaaqe me. Ye 1983 me mukammal hua tha. Haro aik chhota darya hai jo Abbottabad ke paharon se nikal kar Indus me milta hai.

Khanpur Dam ki khaas baat ye hai ke ye Islamabad aur Rawalpindi ko peene ka pani deta hai. Bijli is ka asal maqsad nahi, pani ki supply hai. Is ki neelay sabz pani wali jheel sar seer ke liye bohat mashhoor hai aur Islamabad ke qareeb honay ki wajah se log yahan kashti rani aur sair ke liye ate hain. Jab paper me Islamabad ko pani dene wala dam poocha jaye to do naam ate hain, Rawal Dam aur Khanpur Dam, aur teesra Simly Dam. Teeno ko aik sath yaad rakho.

Pin: 33.81, 72.93

Rawal Dam

Rawal Dam Korang River par bana hai, Islamabad ke andar. Ye 1962 me mukammal hua tha, yani Islamabad ke banne ke bilkul ibtedai daur me. Korang aik chhota pahari naala numa darya hai jo Murree ke paharon se ata hai.

Rawal Dam Rawalpindi aur Islamabad ko peene ka pani deta hai. Is ki jheel, Rawal Lake, Islamabad ki mashhoor tareen jagahon me se hai, aur Lake View Park is ke kinare bana hai. Dam chhota hai lekin pehchan bohat bari hai, kyunke ye darul hukumat ke andar hai. Paper me sawal aam taur par yun ata hai ke Rawal Dam kis darya par hai, jawab Korang River.

Pin: 33.70, 73.13

Simly Dam

Simly Dam Soan River par bana hai, Islamabad ke mashriq me Simly ke ilaaqe me. Ye 1983 me mukammal hua tha. Ye earth filled qisam ka dam hai, yani mitti se bhara hua.

Simly Dam Islamabad ko peene ka pani dene wala sab se bara zariya hai. Soan River Pothohar ke ilaaqe ka mashhoor darya hai jo Rawalpindi ke qareeb se guzar kar Indus me milta hai. Simly ki jheel lambi aur tang hai aur paharon ke darmiyan hai. To Islamabad ke teen dams ka silsila ye bana, Rawal Korang par, Simly Soan par, aur Khanpur Haro par. Teenon ke darya alag alag hain, yehi paper ka asal sawal hai.

Pin: 33.72, 73.34

Hub Dam

Hub Dam Hub River par bana hai, Sindh aur Balochistan ki sarhad par, Karachi ke shimal maghrib me. Ye 1981 me mukammal hua tha. Hub River Kirthar ke paharon se nikalta hai, ye baat daryaaon wale hisse me parh chuke ho.

Hub Dam Karachi ko peene ka pani deta hai. Karachi Pakistan ka sab se bara sheher hai aur us ki pani ki zaroorat puri karna hamesha aik bara masla raha hai, is liye Hub Dam ki ahmiyat bohat zyada hai. Barishon ke baad jab Hub Dam bhar jata hai to ye khabar ban jati hai. Paper me sawal yun ata hai ke Hub Dam kis darya par hai aur kis sheher ko pani deta hai. Jawab, Hub River par hai aur Karachi ko deta hai.

Pin: 25.25, 67.18

Mirani Dam

Mirani Dam Dasht River par bana hai, Balochistan ke district Kech me, Turbat ke qareeb. Ye 2006 me mukammal hua tha. Dasht River Balochistan ke junub maghrib ka aham darya hai jo Arabian Sea me girta hai.

Mirani Dam Makran ke ilaaqe ke liye pani ka bara zariya hai. Turbat aur aas paas ke ilaaqe is se sairaab hote hain, aur khajoor ke baghaat ke liye ye pani bohat aham hai. Balochistan ke dams me Mirani ka naam is liye ata hai ke ye us khushk ilaaqe me zindagi ka zariya hai jahan barish bohat kam hoti hai.

Pin: 25.94, 62.69

Gomal Zam Dam

Gomal Zam Dam Gomal River par bana hai, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa ke district South Waziristan me. Ye 2013 me mukammal hua tha. Gomal River Afghanistan se ata hai aur Dera Ismail Khan ke ilaaqe me Indus me milta hai, ye safar tum daryaaon wale hisse me parh chuke ho.

Gomal Zam Dam ka maqsad do hain, aabpashi aur bijli, taqreeban 17 megawatt bijli bhi banti hai. Is se Tank aur Dera Ismail Khan ke ilaaqe ki zameenein sairaab hoti hain. Waziristan jaise ilaaqe me dam banna is liye khaas hai ke ye wahan taraqqi aur pani dono ki buniyad hai. Is ka naam Gomal Zam is liye hai ke zam maqami zuban me chashme ya pani ke zariye ko kehte hain, aur Gomal Zam aik purana mashhoor maqam hai.

Pin: 32.03, 69.84

Aakhir me tartib aik nazar me

Ab puri tasveer jor lo. Indus par dams aur barrages shimal se junub yun hain, Diamer Basha jo ban raha hai, Tarbela, Ghazi Barotha, Jinnah, Chashma, Taunsa, Guddu, Sukkur aur Kotri. Jhelum par Mangla Dam aur Rasul Barrage. Chenab par Qadirabad, Ravi par Balloki. Sutlej par Sulemanki aur Islam. Panjnad par Panjnad Barrage. Kabul par Warsak, Haro par Khanpur, Korang par Rawal, Soan par Simly, Hub par Hub Dam, Dasht par Mirani aur Gomal par Gomal Zam. Agar har dam ke sath us ka darya tumhe foran zehen me ane lag jaye to samajh lo ye topic pakka ho gaya, kyunke paper ka sawal hamesha yahi hota hai, dam kis darya par hai.
