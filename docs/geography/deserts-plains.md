Registran, Maidan aur Plateau
Pakistan ke registan, un ki jagah aur khaas baatein, aur do bare plateau

Ye topic kyun zaroori hai

Pakistan sirf paharon ka mulk nahi. Is ke junoob mashriq me bare registan hain aur maghrib aur shumaal me unche plateau. Paper me registanon par sawal aam hai ke konsa registan kis soobay me hai, Cholistan ko aur kya kehte hain, Derawar Fort kahan hai, aur Thal kis ilaqay ko kehte hain. Ye sawal asaan hote hain lekin students ilaqon ko aik dusre me mila dete hain. Har registan ko aik mashhoor jagah se bandh lo, phir ghalti nahi hogi.

Thar Desert

Thar Pakistan ka sab se mashhoor registan hai aur Sindh ke junoob mashriqi hisse me hai, Tharparkar, Umerkot aur Mirpurkhas ke ilaqon me phaila hai. Ye sarhad ke us paar Bharat ke Rajasthan tak jata hai, yani ye aik hi registan hai jo do mulkon me banta hua hai. Is ka kul raqba taqreeban 200,000 murabba kilometre hai, jis ka bara hissa Bharat me hai aur aik aham hissa Pakistan ke Sindh me. Raqba sun kar ye samajh lo ke ye duniya ke bare registanon me se hai, sirf aik chhota sa ret wala ilaqa nahi. Thar ki pehchan ye hai ke ye duniya ke un chand registanon me se hai jahan achi khasi abadi rehti hai, gaon abad hain aur kheti bhi barish ke saharay hoti hai. Is ka mashhoor ilaqa Nagarparkar hai jo Karoonjhar ki pahariyon ke liye jana jata hai, aur Mithi is ka bara sheher hai. Paper me Thar hamesha Sindh ke sath jor kar poocha jata hai.

Pin: 25.36, 69.74

Cholistan Desert

Cholistan Punjab ke junoob me hai, Bahawalpur, Bahawalnagar aur Rahim Yar Khan ke ilaqon me phaila hai. Is ka raqba taqreeban 26,000 murabba kilometre hai, yani ye Punjab ka aik bohat wasee ilaqa hai, sirf chand gaon ke aas paas ki ret nahi. Is ka dusra naam Rohi hai, aur ye naam paper me bohat ata hai. Jab Rohi suno to samajh jao ke baat Cholistan ki ho rahi hai. Cholistan ki sab se mashhoor pehchan Derawar Fort hai, aik azeem shan qila jo registan ke beech me khara hai aur door se nazar ata hai. Qila Nawab of Bahawalpur ke zamane ka hai aur is ke burj bohat unche hain. Cholistan me jeep rally bhi hoti hai jo ab bohat mashhoor ho chuki hai. Yaad rakhne ke liye bas ye jodi pakki kar lo, Cholistan, Rohi aur Derawar Fort, teeno aik hi kahani hain.

Pin: 28.77, 71.33

Thal Desert

Thal Punjab ke darmiyan me hai aur is ki jagah samajhna sab se zyada zaroori hai kyunke yahi paper ka trap hai. Thal darya-e-Sindh aur darya-e-Jhelum ke darmiyan wale ilaqay me hai, yani ye Sindh Sagar Doab ke andar ka registani hissa hai. Is ke ilaqay Bhakkar, Layyah, Muzaffargarh aur Mianwali ke kuch hisse hain. Dekho, Thal koi alag sooba nahi aur na hi ye Sindh me hai. Ye Punjab ke maghribi kinare par do daryaaon ke darmiyan phasi ret wali zameen hai. Jo student doab wala lesson parh chuka hai us ke liye Thal bilkul saaf ho jayega. Thal Canal isay sair karne ki bari koshish hai. Thal ka raqba aam taur par 19,000 se 23,000 murabba kilometre ke darmiyan bataya jata hai. Kitabon me ye figure aik jesa nahi milta kyunke har jagah is ki had bandi aik hi tareeqe se nahi ki gayi, is liye paper ke liye koi aik bareek figure rattne ki bajaye ye range zehen me rakho ke ye taqreeban bees hazaar murabba kilometre ke aas paas ka ilaqa hai.

Pin: 31.63, 71.07

Kharan Desert

Kharan Balochistan ka registan hai aur soobay ke maghribi hisse me Kharan aur Washuk ke ilaqon me phaila hai. Is ka raqba aam taur par taqreeban 48,000 murabba kilometre bataya jata hai, yani raqbe ke aitbaar se ye aik bohat bara registan hai. Is ke bawajood ye Pakistan ke sab se khushk aur sab se kam abadi wale ilaqon me se hai, kyunke raqba bara honay ka matlab abadi zyada hona nahi hota, pani ki kami wahan sab kuch tay karti hai. Is ke qareeb Chagai ki pahariyan hain jahan Ras Koh ke ilaqay me 1998 me Pakistan ne apne johri tajurbat kiye the. Paper me Kharan aksar is sawal me ata hai ke konsa registan Balochistan me hai. Baqi teenon registan Punjab aur Sindh me hain, Balochistan wala sirf Kharan hai. Ye farq yaad rakh lo, aadha sawal yahin hal ho jata hai.

Pin: 28.58, 65.42

Ab do bare plateau

Registan to medani aur ret wale ilaqay hain, plateau is ke ulta hai. Plateau wo unchi aur hamwar zameen hai jo chaaron taraf se neeche ilaqon se unchi ho, jese aik bari si mez. Pakistan me do plateau paper me ate hain.

Pothohar Plateau

Pothohar Punjab ke shumaal me hai, darya-e-Sindh aur darya-e-Jhelum ke darmiyan. Is ka raqba taqreeban 22,000 murabba kilometre hai. Is ke sheher Rawalpindi, Chakwal, Attock aur Jhelum hain. Islamabad bhi is ke kinare par hai. Ye ilaqa Salt Range ke junoob me hai aur yahan ki zameen pathreeli aur lehron wali hai, is liye yahan barani kheti hoti hai, yani barish ke saharay. Pakistan me tail aur gas ke zakheer bhi is ilaqay me mile hain. Paper me Pothohar hamesha Indus aur Jhelum ke darmiyan wali unchi zameen ke naam se poocha jata hai.

Pin: 32.93, 72.86

Balochistan Plateau

Balochistan ka plateau poore soobay par phaila hai aur Pakistan ka sab se uncha aur sab se wasee plateau hai. Balochistan ka kul raqba taqreeban 347,190 murabba kilometre hai, jo Pakistan ke kul raqbe ka taqreeban 44 feesad banta hai, aur is soobay ka zyada tar hissa yehi plateau hai. Is liye is plateau ko samajhna asal me poore soobay ko samajhna hai. Is ki unchai aam tor par 600 se 3,000 meter ke darmiyan rehti hai. Is ke mashriq me Sulaiman aur Kirthar ke silsile khare hain aur maghrib me khushk pahariyan aur registan hain. Quetta isi plateau par waqe hai, is liye wahan ka mosam baqi Balochistan ke medani ilaqon se thanda hota hai. Ye plateau Iran ke plateau ka hi aik hissa samjha jata hai. Paper me jab Pakistan ke sab se bare plateau ka sawal aye to jawab Balochistan Plateau hai.

Pin: 30.18, 66.99

Aakhir me har aik ki aik pehchan

Aakhir me sab ko aik aik line me bandh lo. Thar Sindh ka hai aur Nagarparkar us ki pehchan hai. Cholistan Punjab ka hai, usay Rohi kehte hain aur Derawar Fort us ki nishani hai. Thal do daryaaon ke darmiyan Punjab ke maghrib me hai aur Sindh Sagar Doab ka hissa hai. Kharan Balochistan ka khushk registan hai. Pothohar shumaali Punjab ki unchi zameen hai aur Balochistan Plateau poore mulk ka sab se uncha aur bara plateau hai. Ye paanch linein pakki kar lo, registanon wala sawal kabhi ghalat nahi hoga.
