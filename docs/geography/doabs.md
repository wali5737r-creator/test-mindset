Punjab ke Doab
Do daryaaon ke darmiyan wali zameen, us ke sheher aur naam yaad rakhne ki trick

Ye topic kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me Punjab ke doab har dusre saal pooche jate hain. Sawal seedha hota hai ke falan doab kin do daryaaon ke darmiyan hai, ya falan sheher kis doab me hai. Ye paanch sawal hain aur paanchon ke number pakke ho sakte hain, bas zara sa nizam samajhna hai. Jo student naqshay par ungli rakh kar doab seekh le, wo kabhi Rachna aur Bari me ulta seedha nahi karega.

Doab hota kya hai

Pehle lafz ko samajh lo, baqi sab khud asaan ho jayega. Doab do alfaaz ka jor hai. Do matlab do, aur aab matlab pani, yani darya. To doab wo zameen hai jo do daryaaon ke darmiyan phasi hui ho. Punjab ka matlab hi paanch daryaaon ki zameen hai, aur jahan paanch darya behte hon wahan un ke darmiyan zameen ke tukre bhi bante hain. Yehi tukre doab kehlate hain. Punjab me mashhoor doab paanch hain, aur khushi ki baat ye hai ke un ke naam hi un ka pata bata dete hain.

Naam wala qanoon pehle samajh lo

Dekho, har doab ka naam unhi do daryaaon ke naamon ke pehle harfon ko jodne se bana hai jin ke darmiyan wo hai. Chaj suno to Chenab aur Jhelum yaad karo, kyunke Ch Chenab se aya aur aj Jhelum se. Rachna suno to Ravi aur Chenab, Ra Ravi se aur chna Chenab se. Bari suno to Beas aur Ravi, B Beas se aur ari Ravi se. Bist suno to Beas aur Sutlej. Bas ye aik line zehen me bitha lo, phir koi doab bhool hi nahi sakta. Paper me jab doab ka naam nazar aye to pehle us ke harf toro, darya khud samne ajayenge.

Ab aik aik karke sare doab

Sindh Sagar Doab

Ye sab se bara aur sab se maghribi doab hai. Ye darya-e-Sindh, yani Indus, aur darya-e-Jhelum ke darmiyan hai. Isi liye is ke naam me Sindh saaf nazar ata hai, aur Sagar is liye ke ye sab se wasee hai, sagar yani samandar jitna bara. Is ilaqay me Thal ka registani hissa bhi ata hai, is liye ye doab baqi doabon ki nisbat khushk hai. Is ke mashhoor sheher Rawalpindi, Mianwali, Khushab, Bhakkar aur Attock hain. Pothohar ka plateau bhi isi doab ke shumali hisse me hai. Paper me jab Indus aur Jhelum aik sath ayen to jawab hamesha Sindh Sagar hoga, ye wala sawal sab se zyada dohraya jata hai.

Pin: 32.30, 72.35

Chaj Doab

Naam toro to baat saaf ho jati hai. Chaj darya-e-Chenab aur darya-e-Jhelum ke darmiyan hai. Ye Sindh Sagar ke mashriq me aik nisbatan chhota sa doab hai. Is ke mashhoor sheher Gujrat, Mandi Bahauddin, Jhelum aur Sargodha ka kuch hissa hain. Gujrat apni pankha aur mitti ke bartanon ki san'at ki wajah se mashhoor hai aur yehi sheher is doab ki pehchan hai. Yaad rakhne ke liye bas itna socho ke Chaj me dono darya aik dusre ke bohat qareeb behte hain, is liye ye doab tang hai.

Pin: 32.58, 73.48

Rachna Doab

Ra Ravi se aur chna Chenab se, yani Rachna darya-e-Ravi aur darya-e-Chenab ke darmiyan hai. Ye Punjab ke sab se ghane abadi wale aur sab se zarkhez doabon me se hai, kyunke dono taraf se pani milta hai. Is ke mashhoor sheher Gujranwala, Sheikhupura, Sialkot, Faisalabad ka mashriqi hissa aur Hafizabad hain. Gujranwala pehlwanon aur san'at ka sheher hai aur is doab ka dil hai. Paper me aik trap ata hai ke Faisalabad kis doab me hai. Asal baat ye hai ke Faisalabad Chenab ke kinare par hai aur us ka bara hissa Rachna me hi gina jata hai, jabke sheher ke girdonawa ko aksar Rachna aur Sandal Bar ke hawale se bhi yad kiya jata hai. Sawal me agar Ravi aur Chenab ayen to Rachna par nishan lagao.

Pin: 32.16, 74.19

Bari Doab

Ab sab se mashhoor doab ki bari hai, aur is ko zara khaas tawajjo se parho kyunke Lahore isi me hai. Bari ka B Beas se hai aur ari Ravi se, yani ye darya-e-Beas aur darya-e-Ravi ke darmiyan hai. Is ke mashhoor sheher Lahore, Kasur, Okara, Pakpattan aur Sahiwal hain, aur sarhad ke us paar Amritsar bhi isi doab me ata hai. Lahore Punjab ka darulhukumat hai, Mughalon ka sheher hai, aur paper me jab poocha jaye ke Lahore kis doab me waqe hai to jawab Bari Doab hota hai. Ye sawal itna dohraya gaya hai ke is ko to bas ankh band karke yaad kar lo. Aik aur baat, Beas aaj ke Pakistan ke andar nahi behta, wo Bharat me Sutlej se ja milta hai, lekin tareekhi aur geography ke hisab se Bari Doab ki hadd Beas hi hai. Isi liye kuch students confuse hote hain ke Beas to yahan hai hi nahi. Ghabrana nahi, doab ka naam purani aur asal hadd bandi par hai.

Pin: 31.55, 74.34

Bist Doab

Aakhri doab hai Bist, jo darya-e-Beas aur darya-e-Sutlej ke darmiyan hai. Isay Jalandhar Doab bhi kehte hain kyunke is ka sab se mashhoor sheher Jalandhar hai. Is ke dusre sheher Hoshiarpur aur Kapurthala hain. Aik baat saaf kar dun taake paper me dhoka na ho. Bist Doab ka zyada tar hissa aaj Bharat ke Punjab me hai, Pakistan ke andar nahi. Lekin PPSC aur doosre one paper me ye is liye poocha jata hai kyunke Punjab ke paanchon doab aik mukammal silsila hain aur examiner poori list par sawal banata hai. To isay ye keh kar na chhoro ke ye hamara nahi hai. Naam, darya aur Jalandhar, bas itna yaad kar lo.

Pin: 31.33, 75.58

Aakhir me poori tasveer aik nazar me

Ab zehen me naqsha banao. Maghrib se mashriq ki taraf chalo. Sab se pehle Indus aur Jhelum ke darmiyan Sindh Sagar, phir Jhelum aur Chenab ke darmiyan Chaj, phir Chenab aur Ravi ke darmiyan Rachna, phir Ravi aur Beas ke darmiyan Bari, aur aakhir me Beas aur Sutlej ke darmiyan Bist. Dekha, tartib bhi daryaaon ki tartib hi hai. Jo student ye safar aik dafa naqshay par kar le, us ke liye doab ka sawal zindagi bhar ghalat nahi hota.
