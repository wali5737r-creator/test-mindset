Tareekhi aur Heritage Jagahen
Pakistan ki wo jagahen jin par paper me har saal sawal ata hai

Ye file kyun zaroori hai

Dekho, Pakistan ki sar zameen duniya ki purani tareen tehzeebon ka ghar hai. Yahan paanch hazar saal purane sheher bhi hain aur Mughalon ke banaye hue qile aur masajid bhi. One paper me in jagahon par sawal lazmi ata hai. Konsi jagah UNESCO ki World Heritage list me hai, Mohenjo-daro kis darya ke kinare hai, Rohtas Fort kis ne banaya, Akbar kahan paida hua, ye wo sawal hain jo bar bar dohraye jate hain.

Aik bunyadi baat pehle samajh lo. UNESCO United Nations ka idara hai jo duniya ki aham tareekhi jagahon ki aik list rakhta hai, use World Heritage list kehte hain. Pakistan ki kul che jagahen is list me hain. Is file me jahan UNESCO likha ayega wahan me saal bhi dunga, kyunke saal bhi kabhi kabhi pooch liya jata hai. Jo jagahen list me nahi hain lekin tentative list me hain, yani un ke naam tajweez ho chuke hain, wahan me wazeh likhunga ke ye abhi tentative hai. Dono ko mila mat dena, paper yahi par phansata hai.

Ab aik aik karke tamaam jagahen dekhte hain. Har jagah ke sath us ka pin hai, taake naqshay par dekh kar yaad kar sako.

Mohenjo-daro

Mohenjo-daro Sindh ke zila Larkana me hai, Darya e Sindh ke maghribi kinare ke qareeb. Ye Indus Valley Civilization ka sab se mashhoor aur sab se bara sheher hai. Iski abadi ka zamana taqreeban 2600 qabl maseeh se 1900 qabl maseeh tak mana jata hai, yani ye sheher chaar hazar saal se zyada purana hai. Mohenjo-daro ke maana hain murdon ka teela.

Ye jagah is liye duniya bhar me mashhoor hai ke itne purane zamane me yahan pakki eenton ke makaan, seedhi galiyan, dhaki hui naaliyan aur pani ke intezam ka pura nizaam tha. Yahan ka Great Bath, yani bara ghusl khana, aur bara anaaj ghar is ki sab se mashhoor nishaniyan hain. Yahan se Priest King ka mujasma aur nachne wali larki ki murti bhi mili. Iski khudai 1922 me R. D. Banerji ki nigraani me shuru hui.

Ye UNESCO ki World Heritage list me hai aur 1980 me shamil hui. Ye Pakistan ki pehli jagah thi jo is list me aayi.
Pin: 27.33, 68.13

Harappa

Harappa Punjab ke zila Sahiwal me hai, Darya e Ravi ke purane kinare ke qareeb. Ye bhi Indus Valley Civilization ka bara sheher hai aur Mohenjo-daro ka hum asar hai, yani taqreeban 2600 qabl maseeh ka zamana hai.

Harappa ki khaas baat ye hai ke Indus Valley ki khudai sab se pehle yahin se shuru hui, 1921 me Dayaram Sahni ne yahan kaam kiya. Isi liye is tehzeeb ko doosra naam Harappan Civilization bhi diya jata hai, kyunke pehchan sab se pehle Harappa se hui. Yahan se anaaj ghar, mazdooron ke makaanat aur bohat si mohren mili hain.

Aik aham farq yaad rakho. Harappa UNESCO ki World Heritage list me nahi hai, jabke Mohenjo-daro hai. Paper me aksar dono ko mila kar sawal banaya jata hai aur student yahi ghalti karta hai.
Pin: 30.63, 72.87

Taxila

Taxila Punjab me hai, Islamabad se taqreeban 32 kilometre shimal maghrib me. Ye Gandhara tehzeeb ka markaz tha aur purane zamane me ilm ka bohat bara adda tha. Kehte hain ke yahan ki purani university me door door se talib ilm ate the, aur Chanakya, jise Kautilya bhi kehte hain, ka naam isi sheher se jura hai.

Taxila ki tareekh me bohat se daur aye. Sikandar e Azam 326 qabl maseeh me yahan aya. Phir Maurya, Greek, Kushan aur Gupta daur aye. Sheher ki teen bari bastiyan milti hain, Bhir Mound sab se purani, Sirkap Greek daur ki aur Sirsukh Kushan daur ki. Taxila badh mut ke liye bhi aham tha. Ashoka ke zamane ka Dharmarajika stupa yahan ki sab se mashhoor jagah hai, jahan Buddha ki nishaniyan rakhi gayi theen. Aur Jaulian me aik purani monastery hai jahan talib ilm raha karte the, us ke stupa aur kamre aaj bhi dekhe ja sakte hain.

Ye UNESCO ki World Heritage list me hai aur 1980 me shamil hui, usi saal jis saal Mohenjo-daro hui.
Pin: 33.75, 72.83

Kot Diji

Kot Diji Sindh ke zila Khairpur me hai, Darya e Sindh ke mashriqi kinare ke qareeb. Ye Indus Valley se bhi pehle ki basti hai. Yahan jo tehzeeb mili use Kot Dijian kehte hain aur is ka zamana taqreeban 3500 qabl maseeh ke aas paas mana jata hai, yani ye Mohenjo-daro se bhi purani hai. Iski khudai 1955 me F. A. Khan ne ki. Is se sabit hua ke is ilaqe me sheheri zindagi ki buniyad Indus Valley se pehle par chuki thi.

Kot Diji ke qareeb aik mashhoor qila bhi hai jo Talpur daur me, atharvi sadi ke aakhir me, banaya gaya tha. Usay bhi Kot Diji Fort kehte hain. Dono ko mila mat dena. Aik neeche zameen me dabi purani basti hai aur aik pahari par khara qila hai.

Ye jagah UNESCO ki World Heritage list me nahi hai.
Pin: 27.35, 68.71

Mehrgarh

Mehrgarh Balochistan me hai, Bolan Pass ke qareeb Kachhi ke medani ilaqe me. Ye Pakistan ki sab se purani basti mani jati hai. Is ka zamana taqreeban 7000 qabl maseeh se shuru hota hai, yani aaj se takreeban nau hazar saal pehle.

Mehrgarh ki ahemiyat ye hai ke yahan South Asia me sab se pehle kheti baari aur janwar paalne ke saboot mile hain. Gandum aur jau ki kheti, bakri aur bher ki parvarish, mitti ke bartan aur kachay eenton ke makaan, ye sab yahan se mila. Isi liye ise is ilaqe ki tehzeeb ki maa kaha jata hai, kyunke yahan se shuru honay wala safar aage chalkar Mohenjo-daro tak gaya. Iski khudai 1974 me Jean Francois Jarrige ki sarbarahi me shuru hui.

Ye jagah UNESCO ki World Heritage list me abhi nahi hai, lekin tentative list me shamil hai.
Pin: 29.38, 67.62

Lahore Fort

Lahore Fort ko Shahi Qila kehte hain. Ye Lahore ke purane sheher ke shimal maghribi kone me hai, Badshahi Mosque ke bilkul samne. Is jagah par qila bohat purane zamane se tha, lekin is ki maujooda shakal Mughal badshah Akbar ne 1566 me banayi.

Baad ke badshahon ne is me izafe kiye. Jahangir ne apne hisse banaye, Shah Jahan ne Sheesh Mahal jaisi khoobsurat imaratein banwayin jo 1631 aur 1632 me mukammal huin, aur Aurangzeb ne 1673 aur 1674 me is ka mashhoor Alamgiri darwaza banaya. Qile ke andar Diwan e Aam, Diwan e Khaas, Moti Masjid aur Sheesh Mahal paper ke pasandeeda sawal hain.

Ye UNESCO ki World Heritage list me hai aur 1981 me Shalimar Gardens ke sath shamil hua.
Pin: 31.59, 74.32

Badshahi Mosque

Badshahi Mosque Lahore me hai, Lahore Fort ke bilkul samne, darmiyan me Hazuri Bagh hai. Ise Mughal badshah Aurangzeb ne banwaya tha. Iski tameer 1671 me shuru hui aur 1673 me mukammal hui.

Ye masjid laal retilay pathar se bani hai aur is ke sehan me aik lakh ke qareeb namazi sama sakte hain. Jab ye bani to ye duniya ki sab se bari masjid thi. Iski teen safed marble wale gumbad aur unche meenar Lahore ki pehchan hain. Aik dilchasp tareekhi baat ye hai ke Sikh daur aur Angrezon ke daur me is masjid ko mukhtalif kaam ke liye istemal kiya gaya, aur azaadi ke baad ise dobara masjid ki haisiyat wapas mili.

Ye masjid UNESCO ki World Heritage list me nahi hai, lekin tentative list me shamil hai. Is farq ko khaas yaad rakho, kyunke bohat se students ghalti se ise World Heritage samajh lete hain.
Pin: 31.59, 74.31

Wazir Khan Mosque

Wazir Khan Mosque Lahore ke purane sheher ke andar hai, Delhi Gate ke qareeb. Ise Shah Jahan ke daur me Lahore ke governor Hakim Ilmuddin Ansari ne banwaya, jo Wazir Khan ke naam se mashhoor the. Tameer 1634 me shuru hui aur 1641 me mukammal hui.

Ye masjid apni rang barang kashi kari, yani tile work, aur deewaron par bani naqqashi ki wajah se duniya bhar me mashhoor hai. Iski deewaron par phool, bail butay aur khush khati ke namune hain. Ise Lahore ki sab se khoobsurat aur sab se zyada sajayi gayi masjid mana jata hai.

Ye UNESCO ki World Heritage list me nahi hai, lekin tentative list me shamil hai.
Pin: 31.58, 74.32

Shalimar Gardens

Shalimar Gardens Lahore me hai, Grand Trunk Road ke qareeb. Ise Mughal badshah Shah Jahan ne 1641 me banwana shuru kiya aur ye 1642 me mukammal hua. Ye Farsi tarz ke chaar bagh wale andaz par bana hai.

Is bagh ki khaas baat is ki teen seedhi darjay wali satahen hain, jinhen teh khana ya terrace kehte hain. Upar wali satta se neeche wali tak pani naalon aur fuwwaron se behta tha. Bagh me kul mila kar sainkadon fuwwaray the. Ye Mughalon ke baghbaani ke shauq ki sab se achi zinda misaal hai.

Ye UNESCO ki World Heritage list me hai aur 1981 me Lahore Fort ke sath shamil hua.
Pin: 31.59, 74.38

Jahangir ka Maqbara

Mughal badshah Jahangir ka maqbara Lahore ke ilaqe Shahdara Bagh me hai, Darya e Ravi ke dusri taraf. Jahangir ka inteqal 1627 me hua tha. Is maqbare ki tameer 1627 me shuru hui aur 1637 me mukammal hui, is me Shah Jahan ka bara hath tha aur is ki nigrani ke bare me Noor Jahan ka naam bhi liya jata hai.

Ye maqbara laal retilay pathar se bana hai jis par safed marble ka kaam hai, aur is ke chaar konon par unche meenar hain. Ye aik manzila imarat hai, jo Taj Mahal jaisi do manzila shakal se mukhtalif hai. Bagh ke chaar hisse hain aur darmiyan me maqbara hai.

Ye UNESCO ki World Heritage list me nahi hai, lekin tentative list me shamil hai.
Pin: 31.62, 74.30

Rohtas Fort

Rohtas Fort Punjab me hai, Jhelum ke qareeb Dina sheher ke sath, Grand Trunk Road ke upar aik pahari par. Ise Sher Shah Suri ne banwaya tha. Tameer 1541 me shuru hui aur 1548 me mukammal hui.

Sher Shah ne ye qila do wajah se banaya tha. Aik, Mughal badshah Humayun ko wapas ane se rokna. Dusra, is ilaqe ke Ghakkar qabail par nazar rakhna jo Mughalon ke wafadar the. Ye qila taqreeban chaar kilometre lambi deewaron aur baara darwazon ke sath apne zamane ka na qabil e fatah qila mana jata tha. Mazay ki baat ye hai ke ye qila kabhi bari jang se fatah nahi hua.

Ye UNESCO ki World Heritage list me hai aur 1997 me shamil hua. Ye is list me ane wala Pakistan ka akhri bara izafa hai, ye baat yaad rakho.
Pin: 32.96, 73.58

Derawar Fort

Derawar Fort Cholistan ke registan me hai, Bahawalpur se taqreeban 100 kilometre door. Cholistan ki khushk ret ke darmiyan is ki unchi deewarein aur chaalis burj door se nazar ate hain.

Is jagah par pehla qila nawin sadi me Rai Jajja Bhati ne banaya tha. Maujooda qila Bahawalpur ke Abbasi nawabon ke baani Nawab Sadeq Muhammad Khan Awwal ne 1732 me dobara tameer karwaya. Ye qila Bahawalpur riyasat ki taqat ki nishani tha. Qile ke qareeb Abbasi nawabon ke maqbaray aur aik khoobsurat masjid bhi hai jo Delhi ki Moti Masjid ki tarz par bani hai.

Ye UNESCO ki World Heritage list me nahi hai, lekin tentative list me shamil hai.
Pin: 28.77, 71.33

Ranikot Fort

Ranikot Fort Sindh ke zila Jamshoro me hai, Kirthar ke pahari silsilay ke andar. Ise duniya ke sab se bare qilon me se aik mana jata hai. Is ki deewaron ka kul daira taqreeban 26 kilometre hai. Isi wajah se ise Sindh ki Great Wall bhi kaha jata hai.

Is qile ki asal tareekh par mahireen ka ikhtilaf hai. Kuch ise bohat purana mante hain, lekin maujooda deewaron ki bari tameer Talpur daur me, unneeswin sadi me, Mir Karam Ali Talpur ke zamane me hui mani jati hai. Qile ke andar aik aur chhota qila hai jise Miri Kot kehte hain. Paharion ke beech itni lambi deewar banana aaj bhi hairan kun hai, aur ye sawal ke ye deewar kyun banayi gayi, abhi tak mukammal hal nahi hua.

Ye UNESCO ki World Heritage list me nahi hai, lekin tentative list me shamil hai.
Pin: 25.90, 67.90

Makli Necropolis

Makli Thatta ke qareeb aik pahari par hai, Sindh me. Ye duniya ke sab se bare qabaristanon me se aik hai. Ye taqreeban 10 kilometre ke ilaqe me phaila hua hai aur yahan maqbaron aur qabron ki taadad paanch lakh ke qareeb batayi jati hai.

Yahan chaudiwin sadi se atharvi sadi tak ke hukmaran, soofi buzurg aur ameer log dafan hain. Samma, Arghun, Tarkhan aur Mughal daur ke maqbaray yahan hain. In par pathar ki bareek naqqashi aur neeli tileon ka kaam hai jo Sindh ke fun e tameer ki shandaar misaal hai. Jam Nizamuddin, Isa Khan Tarkhan aur Dewan Shurfa Khan ke maqbaray sab se mashhoor hain.

Ye UNESCO ki World Heritage list me hai aur 1981 me shamil hua. List me is ka naam Historical Monuments at Makli, Thatta ke tor par hai.
Pin: 24.76, 67.90

Shah Jahan Mosque Thatta

Shah Jahan Mosque Thatta sheher me hai, Sindh me. Ise Mughal badshah Shah Jahan ne 1644 me banwana shuru kiya aur ye 1647 me mukammal hui. Kehte hain ke Shah Jahan ne ye masjid Thatta ke logon ke liye tohfe ke tor par banwayi thi.

Is masjid ki sab se bari pehchan is ki neeli aur firozi tileon ka kaam hai, jo andar ki deewaron aur gumbadon par is qadar khoobsurat hai ke ye Sindh ki sab se khoobsurat masjid mani jati hai. Is me kul 93 gumbad hain. Aik aur khaas baat ye hai ke is ki banawat aisi hai ke imam ki awaaz baghair loud speaker ke puri masjid me sunai deti hai.

Ye UNESCO ki World Heritage list me nahi hai, lekin tentative list me shamil hai.
Pin: 24.75, 67.92

Chaukhandi Tombs

Chaukhandi ke maqbaray Karachi se taqreeban 29 kilometre mashriq me hain, National Highway ke qareeb Malir wale ilaqe me. Ye pandarvi sadi se atharvi sadi ke darmiyan ke hain.

Ye maqbaray Baloch qabail, khaas taur par Jokhio qabile, ke logon ke hain. In ki pehchan ret ke pathar par ki gayi hairat angez bareek naqqashi hai. Mardon ke maqbaron par pagri, talwar aur ghore ki shaklein banti hain aur auraton ke maqbaron par zewar ki shaklein. Qabrein aik unche chabutre par seedhi darjay me rakhi jati hain, yehi chaukhandi tarz hai.

Ye UNESCO ki World Heritage list me nahi hai, lekin tentative list me shamil hai.
Pin: 24.87, 67.27

Banbhore

Banbhore Sindh ke sahel par hai, Karachi se taqreeban 65 kilometre mashriq me, Gharo wali khaari ke kinare. Ye aik purana bandargahi sheher tha.

Mahireen ki bari taadad ka khayal hai ke yehi wo jagah hai jise purani kitabon me Debal kaha gaya hai, jahan 711 eisvi me Muhammad bin Qasim ki foj utri thi aur wahan se Sindh ki fatah ka aghaz hua tha. Yahan ki khudai me bandargah ki baqiyat, sheher ki deewarein aur aik bohat purani masjid ke aasaar mile hain, jo South Asia ki sab se pehli masajid me se mani jati hai. Is jagah ko Sikandar e Azam ke daur ki bandargah se bhi jora jata hai.

Ye UNESCO ki World Heritage list me nahi hai, lekin tentative list me shamil hai.
Pin: 24.75, 67.52

Umerkot

Umerkot Sindh ke mashriqi hisse me hai, Thar ke kinare par. Yahan ka qila Sodha Rajput ranon se jura hai aur is ilaqe ki tareekh me bohat aham hai.

Umerkot duniya bhar me aik wajah se mashhoor hai. Yahan 15 October 1542 ko Mughal badshah Akbar paida hue the. Us waqt Akbar ke walid Humayun Sher Shah Suri se haar kar dar badar phir rahe the aur unhon ne Umerkot ke Rana ke yahan panah li thi. Socho, Hindustan ka sab se bara badshah aik chhote se registani qile me paida hua. Ye tareekh aur ye jagah paper me bar bar poochi jati hai.

Ye UNESCO ki World Heritage list me nahi hai.
Pin: 25.36, 69.74

Noor Mahal Bahawalpur

Noor Mahal Bahawalpur me hai. Ye Bahawalpur riyasat ke nawab Sadeq Muhammad Khan Chaharam ne banwaya tha. Tameer 1872 me shuru hui aur 1875 me mukammal hui. Ye Italy ke mehalon wali tarz par bana aik khoobsurat mahal hai.

Is mahal ke bare me aik mashhoor kahani hai ke nawab sahib ne ye apni begum ke liye banwaya tha, lekin begum sahiba ne yahan sirf aik raat guzari aur phir kabhi na ayin, kyunke mehal ki khirki se qareebi qabaristan nazar ata tha. Aaj ye mahal aik museum ki haisiyat rakhta hai aur awam ke liye khula hai.

Ye UNESCO ki World Heritage list me nahi hai.
Pin: 29.38, 71.68

Mohatta Palace

Mohatta Palace Karachi ke ilaqe Clifton me hai. Ise 1927 me aik ameer Hindu tajir Shivratan Mohatta ne apni rehaish ke liye banwaya tha. Is ke designer Ahmed Hussein Agha the. Ye gulabi rang ke Jodhpur wale pathar se bana hai, is liye Karachi ki zyada tar imaraton se mukhtalif nazar ata hai.

Taqseem ke baad ye imarat mukhtalif hathion me rahi. Aik zamane me ye Fatima Jinnah ki rehaishgah rahi aur ise Qasr e Fatima kaha gaya. 1999 se yahan Mohatta Palace Museum hai jahan namayishen hoti hain.

Ye UNESCO ki World Heritage list me nahi hai.
Pin: 24.81, 67.03

Aakhir me UNESCO wali che jagahen aik sath yaad kar lo

Pakistan ki jo che jagahen UNESCO ki World Heritage list me hain, un me se is file me paanch shamil hain. Mohenjo-daro 1980, Taxila 1980, Lahore Fort aur Shalimar Gardens 1981, Makli Thatta 1981, aur Rohtas Fort 1997. Chetti jagah is file me nahi hai, wo Khyber Pakhtunkhwa me Takht i Bahi ke badh mut aasaar hain jo 1980 me shamil hue the. Ye che naam saal ke sath bol kar yaad kar lo, kyunke one paper me ye sawal seedha ata hai ke konsi jagah World Heritage hai aur konsi nahi. Jo jagah is list me nahi, use tentative ya ghair fehrist kehna hi sahi jawab hota hai.
