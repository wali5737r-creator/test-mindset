Pakistan ki Jheelain
Meethay aur khaare pani ki mashhoor jheelain, un ki jagah, un ka pani aur un ki kahani

Ye file kyun zaroori hai

Dekho, jheelain Pakistan ke one paper ka wo hissa hain jahan sawal taqreeban tay hote hain. Sab se bari meethay pani ki jheel konsi hai, Karachi ko pani kahan se milta hai, sab se unchi jheel konsi hai, wo jheel jo aik landslide se bani thi konsi hai, ye sawal har dusre paper me ghum phir kar ajate hain. Masla ye hota hai ke kitabon me jheelain sirf naam ki fehrist bankar reh jati hain aur paper wale din naam idhar udhar ho jate hain.

Is file me har jheel ko us ki jagah, us ke pani aur us ki khaas baat ke sath samjhaya gaya hai. Pehle Sindh ki bari jheelain dekho, phir Punjab aur Islamabad, phir Balochistan, aur aakhir me shimal ki pahari jheelain. Har jheel ke sath us ka pin diya hai taake naqshay par jagah dekh kar yaad ho jaye. Aik baat pehle hi saaf kar dun, Tarbela jheel Darya e Sindh par bane hue dam ki jheel hai, us ki mukammal tafseel dams wali file me hai, yahan usay dobara nahi likha.

Manchar Jheel

Manchar Pakistan ki sab se bari meethay pani ki jheel hai, aur ise Asia ki bari meethay pani ki jheelon me bhi gina jata hai. Ye Sindh me hai, zila Jamshoro aur Dadu ke ilaqe me, Sehwan Sharif ke qareeb. Is ka raqba mausam ke sath badalta rehta hai, khushk mausam me taqreeban 350 murabba kilometre aur barishon ke baad 500 murabba kilometre se bhi zyada ho jata hai. Ye badalta hua raqba hi is ki pehchan hai.

Manchar ko pani do taraf se milta hai. Aik taraf Darya e Sindh se Aral Wah nehr ke zariye pani ata hai, aur dusri taraf Kirthar ke paharon se behne wale barasti naale, jin me Nai Gaj mashhoor hai, is me girte hain. Jheel ke kinare Mohana qabeelay ke log kashtiyon par bane gharoon me rehte hain aur un ka rozgar machli pakarna hai. Ye jheel aalmi ahemiyat wali aabi jagahon ki Ramsar list me shamil hai aur sardiyon me door daraz se aye hue parinday yahan dera dalte hain. Paper ke liye sab se pakki baat ye hai, Pakistan ki sab se bari meethay pani ki jheel Manchar hai, aur ye Sindh me hai, Punjab me nahi. Aksar students ise Punjab se jor dete hain, yahi ghalti number katwati hai.

Pin: 26.42, 67.67

Keenjhar Jheel

Keenjhar ko Kalri Jheel bhi kehte hain. Ye Sindh ke zila Thatta me hai aur Pakistan ki dusri sab se bari meethay pani ki jheel hai. Is ki lambai taqreeban 24 kilometre hai aur raqba taqreeban 135 murabba kilometre hai. Karachi se is ka faasla taqreeban 120 kilometre hai.

Is jheel ki sab se bari khaas baat ye hai ke Karachi jaisay bare sheher ko peene ka pani yahin se milta hai. Keenjhar ko pani Darya e Sindh se ata hai, Kotri ke paas se Kalri Baghar nehr, jisay KB Feeder bhi kehte hain, Indus ka pani is jheel tak laati hai, aur phir yahan se pani Karachi bheja jata hai. Is liye jab paper me poocha jaye ke Karachi ko pani kahan se milta hai to jawab Keenjhar hai. Is jheel ke darmiyan aik chhote se jazeeray par Noori ka maqbara hai, jo Sindh ki mashhoor lok kahani Noori Jam Tamachi se jura hai. Ye bhi Ramsar list me shamil hai aur parindon aur machliyon ke liye aham jagah hai.

Pin: 24.96, 68.03

Haleji Jheel

Haleji bhi zila Thatta me hai, Keenjhar ke qareeb hi, Karachi se taqreeban 80 kilometre door. Asal me ye aik khaare pani ki chhoti si jheel thi, lekin dusri aalami jang ke zamane me Angrezon ne Karachi me mojood foj ke liye pani jama karne ki gharz se ise bara kiya aur is me Darya e Sindh ki nehron se meetha pani dala gaya. Is ka raqba taqreeban 6.6 murabba kilometre hai.

Haleji ki duniya bhar me pehchan parinday hain. Ye Ramsar list me shamil hai aur ise parindon ki sab se bari panah gahon me gina jata hai. Har sardi me Siberia aur dusre thande ilaqon se hazaron parinday, jin me batkhain, baaz aur saras mashhoor hain, yahan ate hain. Paper me Haleji hamesha parindon aur Ramsar ke hawale se ati hai, raqbe ke hawale se nahi, kyunke raqbe me ye chhoti hai lekin parindon me sab se aage hai.

Pin: 24.81, 67.77

Hadero Jheel

Hadero bhi zila Thatta ki jheel hai, Haleji ke junoob me. Ye khaare yani namkeen pani ki jheel hai aur is ka raqba taqreeban 13.7 murabba kilometre hai. Ise barish ke pani aur darya ke selabi pani se hissa milta hai, aur aas paas ka ilaqa Indus delta ke medani hisse jaisa hai.

Ye jheel jangli hayat ki panah gah, yani wildlife sanctuary, qarar di gayi hai aur Ramsar list me bhi shamil hai. Yahan machli pakarna aam hai aur sardiyon me aabi parinday baree tadaad me nazar ate hain. Thatta ki teen jheelon ko aik sath yaad rakho, Keenjhar meethi aur Karachi ke pani wali, Haleji parindon wali, aur Hadero khaare pani wali. Ye teeno aik hi zila me hain aur paper me in teeno ko mila kar sawal banta hai.

Pin: 24.82, 67.90

Uchhali Jheel

Ab Punjab ki taraf ao. Uchhali Jheel Punjab ke zila Khushab ki Soon Valley, yani Wadi e Soon, me hai. Sakesar ki pahari ke daaman me waaqe ye is wadi ki sab se bari jheel hai. Is ka pani khaara hai, is liye is me nahana aur is ka pani peena mumkin nahi, lekin is ki khoobsurti aur parinday ise khaas banate hain.

Uchhali ko pani barish se aur aas paas ki paharion se behne wale naalon se milta hai. Ye Ramsar list me shamil hai aur aalmi satah par is liye aham hai ke sardiyon me yahan nahayat hi khatre se dochar safed sar wali batakh, yani white headed duck, aur flamingo jaise parinday ate hain. Soon Valley ki jheelon ko mila kar Uchhali Complex kaha jata hai. Paper me Soon Valley ka naam aye to zehen me foran Uchhali ajana chahiye.

Pin: 32.57, 72.02

Khabikki Jheel

Khabikki bhi Soon Valley, zila Khushab, ki jheel hai aur Uchhali ki behan samjhi jati hai. Ye bhi khaare pani ki jheel hai aur Uchhali Complex ka hissa hai, is liye ye bhi Ramsar list me shamil hai. Naushera se Khabikki ki taraf jate hue ye sarak ke kinare nazar ati hai.

Ise bhi pani barish aur pahari naalon se milta hai. Sardiyon me yahan bhi hijrat karke ane wale parinday, khaas taur par flamingo, baree tadaad me ate hain aur jheel ka manzar badal jata hai. Uchhali aur Khabikki ko hamesha jora bana kar yaad rakho, dono Soon Valley ki hain, dono khaari hain, dono Ramsar hain. Paper me aksar in dono ke naam options me dekar confuse kiya jata hai, lekin dono ka jawab Soon Valley aur Khushab hi hai.

Pin: 32.62, 72.21

Namal Jheel

Namal Jheel Punjab ke zila Mianwali me hai, Namal ki wadi me, Salt Range yani Kohistan e Namak ke qareeb. Ye qudrati jheel nahi, 1913 me Angrezon ne yahan aik band bana kar barasti pani ko roka tha, aur usi band ke peeche ye jheel bani. Isi liye ise Pakistan ki purani masnoi jheelon me gina jata hai.

Namal ko pani Salt Range ki paharion se ane wale barasti naalon se milta hai, jin me Tarapi naala mashhoor hai. Is pani se aas paas ki zameenain sairab hoti hain aur Mianwali ke ilaqe ko faida pohanchta hai. Jheel ke kinare Namal University waaqe hai jo is ilaqe ki pehchan ban gayi hai. Sardiyon me yahan bhi parinday ate hain. Paper ke liye do baatein pakki rakho, Namal Mianwali me hai, aur ye 1913 ke band se bani hui masnoi jheel hai.

Pin: 32.68, 71.80

Rawal Jheel

Rawal Jheel wafaqi darul hukumat Islamabad me hai. Ye bhi masnoi jheel hai, 1962 me Korang darya par Rawal Dam bana kar banayi gayi. Is ka raqba taqreeban 8.8 murabba kilometre hai.

Rawal ko pani Korang darya se milta hai, jo Murree ki paharion se nikal kar Margalla ke daaman se guzarta hua yahan ata hai, aur chhote chhote naale bhi is me girte hain. Is jheel ka sab se bara kaam Rawalpindi aur Islamabad ko peene ka pani dena hai, aur Rawalpindi ki supply ka sab se bara hissa yahin se jata hai. Kinare par bana Lake View Park Islamabad walon ki mashhoor sair gah hai. Paper me Rawal hamesha Korang darya aur Rawalpindi ki pani ki supply ke sath ati hai.

Pin: 33.70, 73.13

Simly Jheel

Simly bhi Islamabad ki jheel hai, lekin Rawal se mashriq ki taraf, Bhara Kahu se aage. Ye Soan darya par bane hue Simly Dam ki jheel hai. Soan wo darya hai jo Pothohar ke ilaqe se behta hua ata hai.

Simly ki khaas baat ye hai ke Islamabad ko peene ke pani ka sab se bara hissa, taqreeban aadha, yahin se milta hai. Yani darul hukumat ki pani ki zaroorat me Simly ka kirdaar Rawal se bhi bara hai. Simly Dam ko Pakistan ke bare mitti ke bandon me gina jata hai. Dono Islamabad wali jheelon ka farq saaf rakho, Rawal Korang par hai aur Rawalpindi ko zyada pani deti hai, Simly Soan par hai aur Islamabad ko sab se zyada pani deti hai. Yehi farq paper ka sawal hai.

Pin: 33.83, 73.35

Hanna Jheel

Ab Balochistan chalo. Hanna Jheel Quetta ke qareeb hai, Urak ki wadi ke raste par, Zarghoon pahar ke daaman me. Ye 1894 me Angrezon ke daur me banayi gayi masnoi jheel hai, jab barasti pani ko rokne ke liye yahan band banaya gaya tha. Is ka raqba chhota hai, taqreeban 81 acre.

Hanna ko pani Zarghoon pahar ki barf aur barish se milta hai, jo aik nehr ke zariye jheel tak ata hai. Is jheel ki mashhoor pehchan is ki sunehri machli, yani golden fish, hai jo saaf pani me saaf nazar ati hai. Aik waqt aisa bhi aya ke khushk saali ki wajah se ye jheel bilkul sookh gayi thi, phir barishon ke baad dobara bhar gayi. Quetta walon ke liye ye sab se qareebi aur mashhoor sair gah hai. Paper me Hanna hamesha Quetta ke sath ati hai, aur ye Balochistan ki sab se mashhoor jheel hai.

Pin: 30.25, 67.10

Saiful Muluk Jheel

Ab shimal ki pahari jheelon ki taraf chalte hain. Saiful Muluk Khyber Pakhtunkhwa ke zila Mansehra ki Wadi e Kaghan me hai, Naran se aage. Ye samandar ki satah se taqreeban 3,224 metre unchai par hai, yani Pakistan ki unchi jheelon me se hai. Ise pani aas paas ke glacieron aur Malika Parbat ki barf ke pighalne se milta hai.

Is jheel ke samne Malika Parbat khara nazar ata hai, jo Wadi e Kaghan ki sab se unchi choti hai, aur saaf din me is ka aks jheel ke pani me banta hai. Saiful Muluk ke sath Sindh aur Punjab bhar me sunayi jane wali mashhoor lok dastan juri hai, Shehzada Saiful Muluk aur pari Badri Jamala ki kahani, jis ki wajah se ye jheel har Pakistani ke zehen me basi hai. Paper me is se do sawal bante hain, ye Kaghan me hai, aur is ke samne Malika Parbat hai.

Pin: 34.88, 73.69

Attabad Jheel

Attabad Gilgit Baltistan ke ilaqa Hunza me hai, aur is ki kahani Pakistan ki tamam jheelon se alag hai. Ye jheel kisi dam se nahi bani. January 2010 me Attabad gaon ke qareeb aik bohat bara pahari toda, yani landslide, gira aur us ne Darya e Hunza ka raasta rok diya. Darya ka pani rukta gaya aur peeche aik lambi jheel ban gayi.

Ye jheel taqreeban 21 kilometre lambi hai aur is ki gehrai 100 metre se zyada hai. Is ke pani me kayi gaon, jin me Ainabad aur Shishkat mashhoor hain, aur Karakoram Highway ka aik hissa doob gaya tha, jis ke baad logon ka ana jana kashtiyon se hone laga. Baad me 2015 me surangain, jinhen Pak China dosti surangain kaha jata hai, khulne se sarak ka raasta dobara ban gaya. Paper me ye kahani lazmi ati hai, wo jheel jo 2010 ke landslide se bani, jawab Attabad hai.

Pin: 36.34, 74.82

Satpara Jheel

Satpara Gilgit Baltistan ke sheher Skardu ke qareeb hai, samandar ki satah se taqreeban 2,636 metre unchai par. Ye aik khoobsurat neelay pani wali jheel hai jise pani Satpara naalay aur aas paas ki barf ke pighalne se milta hai.

Is jheel ki khaas baat ye hai ke Skardu sheher ko peene ka pani yahin se milta hai. Isi jheel par Satpara Dam bhi bana hai jo bijli bhi paida karta hai aur pani bhi jama karta hai. Yani ye sirf sair ki jagah nahi, Skardu ki zindagi ka sahara hai. Skardu jane wale raaste par ye jheel pehli bari manzil hoti hai. Paper me Satpara hamesha Skardu ke sath ati hai.

Pin: 35.24, 75.64

Lower Kachura Jheel, Shangrila

Lower Kachura ko duniya Shangrila Jheel ke naam se janti hai. Ye bhi Skardu ke qareeb hai. 1983 me yahan Shangrila Resort bana, jis ke baad ye jheel Pakistan ki mashhoor tareen saiyahati jagahon me shamil ho gayi. Resort ka wo hissa jo aik purane jahaaz ki shakal me banaya gaya hai, is ki sab se alag pehchan hai.

Is jheel ka pani saaf aur thanda hai aur kinare par phalon ke bagh, khaas taur par khubani aur seb, lage hain. Ise pani aas paas ke chashmon aur barf ke pighalne se milta hai. Yaad rakho, jab bhi Shangrila ka naam aye to wo Lower Kachura hai aur jagah Skardu hai. Ye naam wala sawal paper me bohat ata hai.

Pin: 35.42, 75.45

Upper Kachura Jheel

Upper Kachura Lower Kachura se taqreeban teen kilometre aage hai, Skardu hi ke ilaqe me. Ye apni behan ke baraks bilkul qudrati aur khamosh jheel hai. Yahan koi bara resort nahi, is liye is ka mahol purana aur asal hai.

Ye Lower Kachura se zyada gehri hai aur is ka pani zyada thanda hai. Yahan trout machli payi jati hai aur machli ke shikar ke shaukeen yahan ate hain. Kinare par khubani ke bagh hain aur garmiyon me maqami log yahin sair karte hain. Dono Kachura jheelon ko jora bana kar yaad rakho, neeche wali Shangrila wali mashhoor, upar wali qudrati aur gehri.

Pin: 35.45, 75.42

Borith Jheel

Borith Gilgit Baltistan ki Wadi e Hunza ke ilaqa Gojal me hai, Hussaini gaon ke qareeb, samandar ki satah se taqreeban 2,600 metre unchai par. Ye aik chhoti si khaare pani ki jheel hai, jo pahari jheelon me is liye alag hai ke zyada tar pahari jheelain meethay pani ki hoti hain.

Ise pani Ghulkin glacier ke pighalne se milta hai. Sardiyon aur bahar me yahan hijrati parinday, khaas taur par batkhain, nazar ati hain aur parinday dekhne wale yahan khaas taur par ate hain. Karakoram Highway se ye jheel qareeb hai, is liye Hunza jane wale saiyah yahan rukte hain. Paper me Borith kabhi kabhi khaari pahari jheel ke tor par pooch li jati hai.

Pin: 36.43, 74.86

Rush Jheel

Rush Jheel Gilgit Baltistan ki Wadi e Nagar me hai. Ye samandar ki satah se taqreeban 4,694 metre unchai par hai. Is unchai ki wajah se ise Pakistan ki sab se unchi jheelon me sab se aage rakha jata hai, aur duniya ki unchi tareen alpine jheelon me bhi is ka naam ata hai.

Ise pani aas paas ke glacieron se milta hai. Yahan se Spantik, jisay Golden Peak kehte hain, aur aas paas ki unchi chotiyon ka manzar nazar ata hai. Yahan tak pohanchna asaan nahi, Hoper ki taraf se paidal safar, yani trekking, karke jana parta hai, is liye yahan sirf wohi log jate hain jo paharon ke asal shaukeen hon. Paper ke liye bas aik line pakki kar lo, sab se unchi jheel poochi jaye to Rush ka naam sab se pehle zehen me lao.

Pin: 36.17, 74.88

Phander Jheel

Phander Gilgit Baltistan ke zila Ghizer me hai, Phander ki wadi me. Ye apne gehre neelay rang ki wajah se mashhoor hai aur ise dekh kar log ise chhota samandar kehte hain. Darya e Gilgit is ke qareeb se behta hai aur isi darya ke pani se ye jheel juri hai.

Phander ki sab se bari pehchan trout machli hai. Thande saaf pani me payi jane wali ye machli yahan baree tadaad me hai aur machli ke shikar ke liye log door door se ate hain. Wadi ke logon ka rozgar bhi is jheel aur machli se jura hai. Gilgit se Chitral jane wale raaste par ye sab se khoobsurat manzil hai. Paper me Phander Ghizer aur trout ke sath ati hai.

Pin: 36.17, 72.93

Karambar Jheel

Karambar Jheel Chitral ke shimal me Broghil ki wadi me hai, jahan se aage Wakhan ka ilaqa shuru hota hai. Ye samandar ki satah se taqreeban 4,272 metre unchai par hai, yani Rush ke baad Pakistan ki unchi tareen jheelon me is ka naam ata hai. Is ki lambai taqreeban 3.9 kilometre hai aur raqba taqreeban 2.6 murabba kilometre hai.

Ise pani aas paas ke glacieron ke pighalne se milta hai aur is ka pani nahayat saaf aur neela hai. Ye ilaqa itna door aur uncha hai ke yahan sirf garmiyon ke chand maheenon me jaya ja sakta hai, aur safar bhi paidal ya jeep ke track se hota hai. Paper me Karambar unchai aur Broghil ke hawale se ati hai, aur aksar Rush ke sath options me rakh kar confuse kiya jata hai. Farq yaad rakho, Rush Nagar me hai aur sab se unchi hai, Karambar Broghil, Chitral me hai aur us ke baad hai.

Pin: 36.88, 73.70

Aakhir me wo number jo hamesha tayyar rakho

Ab is file ke sawal numa number aik jaga jama kar lo. Sab se bari meethay pani ki jheel Manchar hai aur wo Sindh me hai. Karachi ko pani Keenjhar se milta hai, jo Darya e Sindh se KB Feeder ke zariye bharti hai. Parindon ki sab se mashhoor jheel Haleji hai. Khaari jheelon me Soon Valley ki Uchhali aur Khabikki Ramsar ki wajah se aham hain. Islamabad ki do jheelon me Rawal Korang par aur Simly Soan par hai, aur Simly darul hukumat ko sab se zyada pani deti hai. Balochistan ki pehchan Hanna hai jo Quetta ke qareeb hai. Shimal me sab se unchi Rush hai, landslide se bani hui Attabad hai jis ki kahani 2010 ki hai, aur Shangrila ka naam Lower Kachura ka dusra naam hai. Ye chand linein pakki kar lo to jheelon wala koi sawal tumhe pareshan nahi karega.
