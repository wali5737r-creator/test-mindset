Pahari Silsile aur Chotiyan
Pakistan ke pahari silsile, un ki unchi chotiyan aur har silsile ki pehchan

Ye topic kyun zaroori hai

Duniya ki sab se unchi chotiyon ka aik bara hissa Pakistan ke shumaal me hai. Paper me paharon par sawal teen rang ke ate hain. Konsa silsila kahan hai, kis silsile ki sab se unchi choti konsi hai, aur choti ki unchai kitni hai. K2 aur Nanga Parbat par to sawal banta hi banta hai. Aik khushi ki baat ye hai ke Pakistan ke shumaal me teen bare silsile aik jagah aakar milte hain, aur is milne wali jagah ko duniya ki chhat ka garha kaha jata hai. Is tasveer ko zehen me rakh lo, phir har silsila apni jagah khud beth jayega.

Pehle badi tasveer samajh lo

Gilgit-Baltistan ke ilaqay me teen azeem silsile, Karakoram, Himalaya aur Hindu Kush, aik dusre ke qareeb ajate hain aur qareeb hi Pamir bhi khara hai. Isi liye is khitte me duniya ki sab se zyada unchi chotiyan aik hi jagah ikatthi hain. Maghrib ki taraf Hindu Kush hai, darmiyan me Karakoram hai, aur junoob mashriq ki taraf Himalaya hai. Ab aik aik karke har silsile ko dekhte hain.

Karakoram Range

Karakoram Pakistan ka sab se mashhoor silsila hai aur Gilgit-Baltistan me phaila hai. Is ki lambai taqreeban 500 kilometre hai, jo Pakistan aur China ki sarhad ke sath sath chalti hai. Lambai me ye Himalaya se chhota hai, lekin unchai aur glaciers me ye us ke barabar khara hai. Is ki pehchan ye hai ke duniya ki chaar sab se unchi chotiyon me se zyada tar yahi hain aur yahan duniya ke sab se bare glaciers hain jo qatbi ilaqon ke bahir hain, jin me Siachen aur Baltoro sab se mashhoor hain. Karakoram ki sab se unchi choti K2 hai. Jab paper me sab se zyada 8,000 meter se unchi chotiyon wala silsila poocha jaye to jawab Karakoram hai.

Pin: 35.88, 76.51

Himalaya Range

Himalaya duniya ka sab se uncha silsila hai jis ki sab se unchi choti Everest Nepal me hai, lekin is ka maghribi hissa Pakistan tak ata hai. Is silsile ki kul lambai taqreeban 2,400 kilometre hai, jo maghrib me Afghanistan ke qareeb se shuru ho kar Nepal aur Bhutan se hoti hui mashriq me Bharat ke aakhri siray tak jati hai. Pakistan me is ka sirf maghribi sira ata hai, lekin wahi sira Nanga Parbat jaisi azeem choti apne andar rakhta hai, is liye lambai ka zyada tar hissa bahir honay ke bawajood paper me Pakistan wala hissa hi poocha jata hai. Pakistan me Himalaya ki pehchan Nanga Parbat hai jo is silsile ka maghribi akhri bara pahar hai. Gilgit-Baltistan aur Azad Kashmir ke ilaqon me ye silsila phaila hai. Aik trap yaad rakho. Everest Pakistan me nahi hai, lekin Nanga Parbat Pakistan me Himalaya ki sab se unchi choti hai. Paper is farq ko bohat azmata hai.

Pin: 35.24, 74.59

Hindu Kush Range

Hindu Kush Chitral ke maghrib aur shumaal maghrib me phaila hai aur Pakistan ko Afghanistan se alag karta hai. Is silsile ki lambai taqreeban 800 kilometre hai, jis ka aik hissa Pakistan me hai aur zyada tar hissa Afghanistan ke andar chala jata hai. Is ki sab se unchi choti Tirich Mir hai jo Chitral se saaf nazar ati hai aur Chitral ki pehchan hai. Hindu Kush ke daray, jese Lowari aur Dorah, bhi paper me is silsile ke sath juray ate hain. Pehchan ye rakho ke Chitral ka pahar Hindu Kush hai aur us ki choti Tirich Mir.

Pin: 36.25, 71.84

Sulaiman Range

Ab shumaal se junoob ki taraf ao. Sulaiman silsila Balochistan ke mashriq me aur Khyber Pakhtunkhwa ke junoob maghrib me aik deewar ki tarah khara hai aur Punjab ke maidanon ko Balochistan ke plateau se alag karta hai. Ye silsila taqreeban 450 kilometre lamba hai, shumaal me Waziristan ke ilaaqe se shuru ho kar junoob me Jacobabad ke qareeb tak jata hai, is liye ye Pakistan ke lambe silsilon me gina jata hai. Is ki sab se unchi choti Takht-e-Sulaiman hai jis ki unchai taqreeban 3,487 meter hai aur ye Zhob aur Dera Ismail Khan ke darmiyan wale ilaqay me hai. Is silsile ke qareeb hi Gomal aur Waziristan ke ilaqay hain. Paper me Takht-e-Sulaiman aksar is sawal me ata hai ke ye kis silsile ki sab se unchi choti hai.

Pin: 31.62, 69.93

Kirthar Range

Kirthar silsila Sindh aur Balochistan ki sarhad par shumaal se junoob tak phaila hai. Is ki lambai taqreeban 300 kilometre hai, yani ye aik lambi deewar hai jo do soobon ko alag karti hai. Ye wo deewar hai jis ke mashriq me Sindh ke maidan hain aur maghrib me Balochistan ki unchaiyan. Dadu ke qareeb is silsile me Gorakh Hill Station hai jo Sindh ka mashhoor thanda maqam hai aur aksar Sindh ka Murree kahlata hai. Kirthar National Park bhi isi silsile me hai jo Pakistan ke bare mahfooz ilaqon me se hai. Pehchan ye hai ke Sindh ki pahari sarhad Kirthar hai.

Pin: 27.42, 67.13

Safed Koh Range

Safed Koh, jisay Spin Ghar bhi kehte hain, Kurram aur Khyber ke maghrib me Pakistan aur Afghanistan ki sarhad par hai. Ye silsila taqreeban 350 kilometre lamba hai aur puri lambai me sarhad ke sath sath chalta hai. Is ki sab se unchi choti Sikaram hai jis ki unchai taqreeban 4,755 meter hai aur ye Parachinar ke qareeb se nazar ati hai. Naam ki wajah ye hai ke is ki chotiyan baraf ki wajah se aksar safaid dikhti hain. Paper me Sikaram aur Safed Koh ki jodi ati hai, isay yaad rakh lo.

Pin: 34.03, 69.79

Toba Kakar Range

Toba Kakar Balochistan ke shumaal me hai, Pishin, Qila Abdullah aur Zhob ke gird phaila hai. Ye silsila Afghanistan ki sarhad ke sath sath chalta hai aur Balochistan plateau ka shumaali kinara hai. Is ki unchai Karakoram wali nahi, ye aik darmiyani unchai wala khushk silsila hai. Paper me ye aksar Balochistan ke silsilon ki list me poocha jata hai ke konsa silsila shumaali Balochistan me hai. Jawab Toba Kakar hai.

Pin: 30.90, 68.40

Hindu Raj Range

Hindu Raj wo silsila hai jo Hindu Kush aur Karakoram ke darmiyan me hai, Chitral ke mashriq aur Gilgit ke maghrib me. Bohat se students is ka naam pehli dafa sunte hain, lekin paper me ye is liye ata hai ke examiner dekhna chahta hai ke student teen bare silsilon ke ilawa darmiyan wale ko bhi janta hai ya nahi. Is ki sab se unchi choti Koyo Zom hai jis ki unchai taqreeban 6,872 meter hai. Pehchan bas itni rakho, darmiyan wala Hindu Raj hai.

Pin: 36.40, 72.90

Pamir Range

Pamir Gilgit-Baltistan ke sab se shumaal me Wakhan ke sath hai. Isay duniya bhar me Roof of the World kaha jata hai aur hamari zaban me Bam-e-Dunya. Yahi wo ilaqa hai jahan se kai silsile nikalte hain, is liye isay paharon ka garha bhi kehte hain. Pakistan ka Pamir se rabta Wakhan ke tang patte ke zariye hai. Jab paper me Bam-e-Dunya poocha jaye to jawab hamesha Pamir hai, ye sawal bohat purana aur bohat dohraya hua hai.

Pin: 36.90, 73.70

Salt Range

Salt Range Punjab ke shumaal me hai, Jhelum, Chakwal, Khushab aur Mianwali ke ilaqon me phaila hai. Is ki lambai taqreeban 175 kilometre hai, mashriq me Jhelum se shuru ho kar maghrib me Kalabagh ke qareeb Indus tak. Shumaal ke azeem silsilon ke muqable me ye chhota hai, lekin namak ki wajah se is ki pehchan sab se alag hai. Naam ki wajah saaf hai, yahan namak ki kaanen hain aur Khewra ki kaan duniya ki mashhoor tareen namak ki kaanon me se hai. Is ki sab se unchi choti Sakesar hai jis ki unchai taqreeban 1,522 meter hai aur wahan aik mashhoor radar aur hill station wala maqam hai. Kallar Kahar bhi isi silsile me hai. Pehchan ye hai ke namak wala silsila Salt Range hai aur us ki choti Sakesar.

Pin: 32.54, 71.93

Margalla Hills

Margalla Islamabad ke peeche khari wo pahariyan hain jo darulhukumat ki pehchan hain. Ye asal me Himalaya ke silsile ki aik chhoti si shakha samjhi jati hain. In ki sab se unchi choti Tilla Charouni hai jis ki unchai taqreeban 1,604 meter hai. Margalla Hills National Park bhi yahi hai. Unchai chhoti hai lekin paper me Islamabad ke hawale se ye naam bar bar ata hai, is liye isay list se bahir mat samjho.

Pin: 33.74, 73.04

Ab mashhoor chotiyan alag se

Silsile samajh liye, ab un chotiyon ko alag se pakka kar lo jin ki unchai seedhi poochi jati hai. Har choti ko us ke silsile aur aik pehchan wali baat ke sath yaad karo.

K2

K2 duniya ki dusri sab se unchi choti hai, unchai 8,611 meter. Ye Karakoram me hai aur Pakistan aur China ki sarhad par waqe hai. Isay Mount Godwin-Austen bhi kehte hain. K2 ko charhna Everest se mushkil mana jata hai. Paper me dusri sab se unchi choti aur K2 ki unchai, ye do sawal pakke hain.

Pin: 35.88, 76.51

Nanga Parbat

Nanga Parbat duniya ki nawein sab se unchi choti hai, unchai 8,126 meter. Ye Himalaya me hai aur Gilgit-Baltistan ke zila Diamer me waqe hai. Isay Killer Mountain kaha jata hai kyunke is par bohat se koh paima halak hue. Fairy Meadows isi ke samne hai. Pehchan ye hai ke Himalaya ki Pakistani pehchan Nanga Parbat hai.

Pin: 35.24, 74.59

Gasherbrum I

Gasherbrum I duniya ki giyarhween sab se unchi choti hai, unchai 8,080 meter. Ye Karakoram me hai aur isay Hidden Peak bhi kehte hain kyunke ye door se choti chotiyon ke peeche chhupi rehti hai.

Pin: 35.72, 76.70

Gasherbrum II

Gasherbrum II duniya ki terahween sab se unchi choti hai, unchai 8,035 meter. Ye bhi Karakoram me hai aur Gasherbrum I ke qareeb hi hai. Dono ko jor kar yaad karo, pehle I phir II, unchai bhi isi tartib se kam hoti jati hai.

Pin: 35.76, 76.65

Broad Peak

Broad Peak duniya ki barahween sab se unchi choti hai, unchai 8,047 meter. Ye Karakoram me K2 ke bilkul qareeb hai aur is ka naam is ki chorhi choti ki wajah se para. Aik baat zehen me rakho ke Broad Peak unchai me Gasherbrum II se unchi hai, is liye tartib K2, phir Gasherbrum I, phir Broad Peak, phir Gasherbrum II hai. Paper is tartib ko azmata hai.

Pin: 35.81, 76.57

Rakaposhi

Rakaposhi ki unchai 7,788 meter hai aur ye Karakoram me Nagar ki wadi me hai. Is ki pehchan ye hai ke ye Karakoram Highway se bilkul samne nazar ati hai aur baghair kisi rukawat ke maidan se choti tak is ka farq duniya ke bare pahari farqon me se hai. Hunza aur Nagar jane wale har musafir ki pehli tasveer Rakaposhi hi hoti hai.

Pin: 36.14, 74.49

Tirich Mir

Tirich Mir ki unchai 7,708 meter hai aur ye Hindu Kush ki sab se unchi choti hai. Ye Chitral me hai aur poore sheher se nazar ati hai. Jab paper me Hindu Kush ki sab se unchi choti poochi jaye to jawab Tirich Mir hai. Iski jodi Chitral ke sath pakki kar lo.

Pin: 36.25, 71.84

Aakhir me yaad rakhne ki tarkeeb

Chotiyon ko unchai ke hisab se aik seedhi me rakho. Sab se upar K2 8,611, phir Nanga Parbat 8,126, phir Gasherbrum I 8,080, phir Broad Peak 8,047, phir Gasherbrum II 8,035. Ye paanchon 8,000 se unchi hain aur paanchon Pakistan se jurri hain. In ke baad Rakaposhi 7,788 aur Tirich Mir 7,708 ajati hain. Aur silsilon ki pehchan ye hai ke Karakoram glaciers wala, Himalaya Nanga Parbat wala, Hindu Kush Chitral wala, aur Bam-e-Dunya Pamir hai. Bas, paharon ka poora khana tayyar hai.
