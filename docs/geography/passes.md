Pakistan ke Daray (Passes)
Paharon ko paar karne wale raste, un ki jagah, unchai aur tareekhi baatein

Ye topic kyun zaroori hai

Pakistan ke shumaal aur maghrib me unche pahar khare hain, aur un paharon ko paar karne ka wahid tareeqa daray hain. Paper me daray teen tarah se pooche jate hain. Kabhi poochte hain ke falan dara kin do ilaaqon ko milata hai, kabhi sab se unche daray ka naam, aur kabhi kis daray se kon sa tareekhi hamla ya konsi sarak guzarti hai. Ye sattrah daray hain. Zyada lagte hain, lekin har daray ki aik pehchan wali baat hai. Pehchan yaad ho jaye to naam khud yaad rehta hai.

Dara hota kya hai

Dara pahari silsile me wo tang aur nisbatan neechi jagah hai jahan se paidal, janwar ya gaari guzar kar dusri taraf ja sakti hai. Jahan pahar asman se baatein karta ho wahan dara hi darwaza banta hai. Isi liye tareekh me jitne hamla awar aye, wo inhi darwazon se aye, aur aaj ki tijarat aur sarken bhi inhi se guzarti hain. Ab aik aik karke sare daray dekhte hain.

Khyber Pass

Sab se mashhoor dara hai aur paper ka sab se pyara sawal bhi. Khyber Peshawar ko Afghanistan ke sheher Jalalabad aur phir Kabul se milata hai. Is ki unchai taqreeban 1,070 meter hai, yani ye uncha dara nahi hai, aur yahi is ki taqat hai ke ye saal bhar khula rehta hai. Tareekh me Sikandar-e-Azam se lekar Mughalon tak, jitne bare hamla awar is khitte me aye, un ka rasta yahi tha. Is ke munh par Jamrud ka qila aur Bab-e-Khyber hai. Peshawar se Landi Kotal tak jane wali railway bhi isi daray se guzarti thi. Jab paper me hamla awaron wala dara poocha jaye to jawab hamesha Khyber hoga.

Pin: 34.11, 71.10

Bolan Pass

Balochistan ka mashhoor dara hai. Bolan Sibi ko Quetta se milata hai, yani maidanon se Balochistan ke plateau tak jane ka rasta yahi hai. Is ki unchai taqreeban 1,793 meter hai. Is daray se railway line guzarti hai jo apni surangon ki wajah se mashhoor hai, aur Quetta jane wali train isi se chadhai chadhti hai. Yaad rakhne ki baat ye hai ke Khyber shumaal maghrib me hai aur Bolan junoob maghrib me. Aik KP se Afghanistan, dusra Balochistan ke dil tak.

Pin: 29.85, 67.62

Khunjerab Pass

Ye Pakistan aur China ki sarhad par hai aur duniya ki sab se unchi pakee sarhad hai jahan se baqaida gaariyan guzarti hain. Is ki unchai taqreeban 4,693 meter hai. Karakoram Highway, jisay Shahrah-e-Resham bhi kehte hain, isi daray se China ke ilaqay Xinjiang me dakhil hoti hai. CPEC ke hawale se ye dara aaj kal aur bhi zyada poocha jata hai. Baraf ki wajah se ye sardiyon me band rehta hai aur mosam-e-garma me khulta hai. Sab se uncha paved pass wala sawal aye to jawab Khunjerab hai.

Pin: 36.85, 75.42

Gomal Pass

Gomal dara Dera Ismail Khan ke ilaqay ko Afghanistan ke Paktika se milata hai. Is ke sath darya-e-Gomal behta hai aur yahi dara aur darya Waziristan ke junoobi hisse ka darwaza hain. Dera Ismail Khan ki Gomal University ka naam bhi isi darya aur daray par hai. Paper me Gomal aksar Tochi ke sath jor kar poocha jata hai, is liye dono ko alag alag pehchan lo. Gomal junoob me hai, Dera Ismail Khan ki taraf.

Pin: 31.62, 69.83

Tochi Pass

Tochi dara Bannu ko Afghanistan ke sheher Ghazni ke rasta se milata hai aur Shumal Waziristan se guzarta hai. Is ke sath darya-e-Tochi behta hai jis ke kinare Miranshah abad hai. Tochi, Gomal ke shumaal me hai. Aik asaan pehchan ye hai ke Bannu suno to Tochi, aur Dera Ismail Khan suno to Gomal. Dono hi Afghanistan jane ke purane raste hain.

Pin: 33.12, 70.13

Babusar Pass

Babusar Kaghan ki wadi Naran ko Chilas se milata hai, yani ye Mansehra ko Gilgit-Baltistan se jorta hai. Is ki unchai taqreeban 4,173 meter hai. Ye dara sirf garma me chand maheenon ke liye khulta hai, sardiyon me baraf se band ho jata hai. Is ki choti se Nanga Parbat ka dilkash manzar nazar ata hai. Babusar Top par khare ho kar aik taraf hari bhari Kaghan aur dusri taraf khushk Chilas dikhta hai. Mosami dara wala sawal aksar Babusar par banta hai.

Pin: 35.15, 74.05

Lowari Pass

Lowari Dir ko Chitral se milata hai. Is ki unchai taqreeban 3,118 meter hai. Baraf ki wajah se ye sardiyon me band ho jata tha aur Chitral ka poore mulk se rabta kat jata tha. Isi masle ka hal Lowari Tunnel hai jo taqreeban 10.4 kilometer lambi hai aur Pakistan ki sab se lambi surangon me se hai. Tunnel banne ke baad Chitral saal bhar juda rehta hai. Paper me Lowari aur us ki tunnel aksar aik sath pooche jate hain.

Pin: 35.35, 71.80

Shandur Pass

Shandur Chitral ko Gilgit se milata hai aur is ki unchai taqreeban 3,700 meter hai. Lekin is ki asal pehchan unchai nahi, polo hai. Shandur me har saal polo festival hota hai jahan Chitral aur Gilgit ki teamen khelti hain, aur ye duniya ka sab se uncha polo ground kahlata hai. Paper me jab sab se unche polo ground wala sawal aye to samajh jao ke baat Shandur ki ho rahi hai aur jawab bhi Shandur hi hai.

Pin: 36.08, 72.52

Broghil Pass

Broghil Chitral ke sab se shumaali hisse me hai aur Pakistan ko Afghanistan ke Wakhan wale tang patte se milata hai. Is ki unchai taqreeban 3,798 meter hai. Ye bohat door daraz aur kam istemal hone wala dara hai, aam traffic yahan se nahi guzarti. Iski pehchan ye hai ke ye Wakhan corridor ke bilkul qareeb hai, wahi tang sa ilaqa jo Pakistan ko Tajikistan se sirf chand kilometer ke faasle par rakhta hai.

Pin: 36.85, 73.42

Mintaka Pass

Mintaka Gilgit-Baltistan ko China ke Xinjiang se milane wala purana dara hai aur Khunjerab ke qareeb hi waqe hai. Is ki unchai taqreeban 4,709 meter hai. Purane zamane me Shahrah-e-Resham ka karwan yahi se guzarta tha. Jab Khunjerab se pakee sarak ban gayi to Mintaka ka istemal khatam ho gaya. Paper me ye aksar is sawal me ata hai ke Khunjerab se pehle China jane ka rasta konsa tha. Jawab Mintaka hai.

Pin: 36.94, 74.85

Kilik Pass

Kilik bhi Mintaka ke bilkul qareeb China ki sarhad par hai aur is ki unchai taqreeban 4,827 meter hai. Ye bhi purani Resham sarak ke zamane ka dara hai aur aaj kal istemal me nahi. Kilik aur Mintaka ko jor kar yaad karo. Dono Gilgit-Baltistan se China jane ke purane daray hain, aur dono ko Khunjerab ne peeche chor diya. Paper me kabhi kabhi sab se unche ghair istemal shuda daron me is ka naam ata hai.

Pin: 37.08, 74.68

Karakoram Pass

Ye dara Karakoram silsile ke mashriqi hisse me hai aur Ladakh ko China ke Xinjiang se milata hai. Is ki unchai taqreeban 5,540 meter hai aur ye is poore khitte ke sab se unche daron me se hai. Aik baat imandari se saaf kar dun ke ye dara Pakistan ki sarhadi hadd ke andar nahi, ye Gilgit-Baltistan ke mashriq me Ladakh wale hisse me hai, lekin one paper ki kitabon me ye Pakistan ke daron wali list me is liye shamil hota hai kyunke purana Leh se Yarkand jane wala karwan rasta yahi tha aur Siachen ke qareeb hone ki wajah se is ki fauji ahmiyat bohat hai. Naam ki wajah se student isay Khunjerab se mila dete hain, aisa mat karna. Khunjerab sarak wala hai, Karakoram Pass purana karwan rasta hai.

Pin: 35.51, 77.82

Malakand Pass

Malakand dara Mardan aur Chakdara ki taraf se Swat jane ka rasta hai, yani maidanon se Swat ki wadi ka darwaza yahi hai. Is ki unchai zyada nahi, lekin tareekhi ahmiyat bohat hai. Winston Churchill ne apni jang ki pehli reporting yahi ke muhaz par ki thi aur us ki mashhoor kitab The Story of the Malakand Field Force isi ilaqay par hai. Paper me Malakand aksar Swat ke darwaze ke naam se poocha jata hai.

Pin: 34.58, 71.93

Peiwar Kotal Pass

Peiwar Kotal Kurram ki wadi ko Afghanistan se milata hai. Is ki unchai taqreeban 2,596 meter hai. Iski tareekhi pehchan dusri Afghan jang hai. 1878 me General Roberts ne isi daray ko paar karke mashhoor fatah hasil ki thi, aur ye waaqia kitabon me Battle of Peiwar Kotal ke naam se milta hai. Kurram suno to Peiwar Kotal yaad karo, ye jodi paper me ati hai.

Pin: 33.97, 69.87

Shangla Pass

Shangla dara Swat ko zila Shangla se milata hai. Alpuri aur Besham ki taraf jane wali sarak isi se guzarti hai. Is ki unchai taqreeban 2,149 meter hai. Ye dara is liye aham hai ke ye Swat ko Karakoram Highway se jorta hai, yani Mingora se Besham ka sab se chhota rasta yahi hai. Malakand aur Shangla dono Swat se juray hain, farq ye hai ke Malakand maidanon ki taraf khulta hai aur Shangla paharon ke andar Besham ki taraf.

Pin: 34.88, 72.60

Dorah Pass

Dorah Chitral ko Afghanistan ke Badakhshan se milata hai. Is ki unchai taqreeban 4,300 meter hai aur ye Chitral ke unche daron me se hai. Zebak ki taraf jane wala purana rasta yahi hai. Iski pehchan ye hai ke ye Chitral ko seedha Badakhshan se jorta hai, jabke Broghil Wakhan ki taraf khulta hai. Chitral ke daray aksar aik sath pooche jate hain, is liye Lowari, Shandur, Broghil aur Dorah, ye chaaron ko Chitral ke naam par aik khane me rakh lo.

Pin: 36.12, 70.97

Aakhir me yaad rakhne ki tarkeeb

Sattrah daray ratne ki cheez nahi hain. Har daray ko us ke sheher ya us ki pehchan se bandh lo. Peshawar se Khyber, Quetta se Bolan, China se Khunjerab, polo se Shandur, tunnel se Lowari, Chitral se chaar daray, aur Swat se Malakand aur Shangla. Pehchan yaad rahi to naam kabhi nahi bhoolega, aur paper me number bhi pakka hoga.
