Pakistan ke Sobay aur Zilay
Intizami dhancha, har sobay ki pehchan, aur zilon ki taaza tadaad

Ye topic kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke paper me Pakistan Studies ke sab se zyada dohraye jane wale sawalon me aik sawal zaroor hota hai ke falan sobay me kitne zilay hain, ya sab se zyada zilay kis sobay me hain, ya falan sobay ka darul hukumat kya hai. Ye sawal mushkil nahi hote, bas tadaad taza honi chahiye kyunke naye zilay bante rehte hain. Is file me hum pehle pura intizami dhancha samjhen ge, phir aik aik sobay ko us ki pehchan ke sath parhen ge. Jo student ye file aik dafa samajh kar parh le, us ke ye number kabhi nahi kat te.

Pehle intizami dhancha samajh lo

Pakistan aik wafaq hai, yani ikhtiyar markaz aur sobon me banta hua hai. Sab se upar wafaqi hukumat hai jo Islamabad se chalti hai. Us ke neeche sobay hain. Pakistan ke chaar sobay hain, Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa aur Balochistan. In ke ilawa Gilgit-Baltistan aur Azad Jammu-o-Kashmir ke apne intizami nizamon ke sath alag ilaqay hain, aur Islamabad ka wafaqi darul hukumat wala ilaqa hai jo kisi sobay ka hissa nahi.

Ab sobay ke andar ki seedhi samjho. Soba sab se bari ikai hai aur us ki hukumat sobai darul hukumat se chalti hai, jahan wazir-e-aala aur sobai assembly hoti hai. Sobay ko divisions me banta jata hai. Division kai zilon ka majmua hota hai aur us ka sarbarah Commissioner hota hai. Division ke neeche zila ata hai, jo intizam ki sab se aham ikai hai aur us ka sarbarah Deputy Commissioner, yani DC, hota hai. Zilay ke andar tehsilein hoti hain jin ka sarbarah Assistant Commissioner hota hai, aur sab se neeche union council hai jo awam ke sab se qareeb hai.

Bas ye seedhi zehen me bitha lo. Wafaq, soba, division, zila, tehsil, union council. Paper is tartib ko bhi poochta hai aur har satah ke sarbarah ko bhi.

Aik ehtiyaat pehle hi sun lo

Is file me di gayi zilon ki tadaad 2026 ki tadaad hai. Pakistan me sobai hukumatein waqtan fawaqtan naye zilay aur nayi divisions banati rehti hain, is liye purani kitabon me tadaad kam milti hai. 2026 me Balochistan ne apna intizami dhancha naye sire se tarteeb diya, jis se us ke zilay aur divisions barh gaye. Isi tarah Khyber Pakhtunkhwa me bhi naye zilay bane. To jab bhi koi tadaad yaad karo, ye zehen me rakho ke ye mojooda tadaad hai aur naye elan ke baad badal sakti hai. Paper hamesha taaza tadaad ko durust manta hai.

Punjab

Punjab Pakistan ka sab se zyada abadi wala soba hai aur is ka darul hukumat Lahore hai. Is ka raqba 205,344 murabba kilometre hai aur 2023 ki mardum shumari ke mutabiq is ki abadi takhreeban 12 crore 77 lakh hai, jo pure mulk ki abadi ka aadhe se zyada hissa hai.

Punjab me 10 divisions hain aur 41 zilay hain. Divisions ke naam ye hain. Lahore, Gujranwala, Gujrat, Rawalpindi, Sargodha, Faisalabad, Sahiwal, Multan, Dera Ghazi Khan aur Bahawalpur. Gujrat Division nisbatan nayi hai, is liye purani kitabon me divisions 9 milti hain, lekin mojooda tadaad 10 hai.

Paper wali baatein ye hain. Abadi me sab se bara soba Punjab hai. Paanch daryaaon ki zameen yehi hai aur Pakistan ki sab se zyada zarai paidawar yahin se ati hai. Lahore ko Pakistan ka dil kaha jata hai. Aur ye bhi yaad rakho ke Punjab ke 41 zilay taweel arse tak mulk me sab se zyada rahe, lekin 2026 ki tabdeeliyon ke baad Balochistan bhi 41 zilon ke sath is ke barabar agaya hai.

Pin: 31.55, 74.35

Sindh

Sindh ka darul hukumat Karachi hai, jo Pakistan ka sab se bara sheher aur mulk ki maashi jaan hai. Sobay ka raqba 140,914 murabba kilometre hai aur 2023 ki mardum shumari ke mutabiq abadi takhreeban 5 crore 57 lakh hai.

Sindh me 6 divisions hain aur 30 zilay hain. Divisions ke naam ye hain. Karachi, Hyderabad, Sukkur, Larkana, Mirpur Khas aur Shaheed Benazirabad. Karachi sheher khud saat zilon me banta hua hai, ye baat aksar sawal banti hai.

Paper wali baatein ye hain. Pakistan ki sab se bari bandargah Karachi yahin hai aur Port Qasim bhi isi sobay me hai. Darya-e-Indus apne safar ka aakhri hissa Sindh me tay karke Arabian Sea me girta hai aur Indus Delta yahin banta hai. Mohenjo-daro ki purani tehzeeb Larkana ke qareeb hai. Aur Karachi mulk ka wo sheher hai jo Pakistan ka pehla darul hukumat tha.

Pin: 24.86, 67.00

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa ka darul hukumat Peshawar hai, jo Pakistan ke sab se purane shehron me se hai. Sobay ka raqba 101,741 murabba kilometre hai. 2018 me FATA ke ilaqay is sobay me zam hue, jis ke baad is ka raqba aur abadi dono barh gaye. 2023 ki mardum shumari ke mutabiq abadi takhreeban 4 crore 9 lakh hai.

Is sobay me 7 divisions hain aur 40 zilay hain. Divisions ke naam ye hain. Peshawar, Mardan, Hazara, Malakand, Kohat, Bannu aur Dera Ismail Khan. Zilon ki tadaad haal hi ke barson me naye zilon ke banne se barhi hai, is liye purani kitabon me kam tadaad milti hai, lekin mojooda tadaad 40 hai.

Paper wali baatein ye hain. Khyber Darra isi sobay me hai aur Afghanistan ki sarhad bhi isi sobay se milti hai. Zam shuda qabaeli ilaqay, jinhen pehle FATA kaha jata tha, ab isi sobay ka hissa hain, ye baat paper me bohat ati hai. Warsak Dam Peshawar ke qareeb darya-e-Kabul par hai.

Pin: 34.01, 71.58

Balochistan

Balochistan raqbe me Pakistan ka sab se bara soba hai aur is ka darul hukumat Quetta hai. Is ka raqba 347,190 murabba kilometre hai, jo pure Pakistan ke raqbe ka takhreeban 44 feesad banta hai. Lekin abadi me ye sab se chhota soba hai, 2023 ki mardum shumari ke mutabiq takhreeban 1 crore 49 lakh.

2026 me Balochistan ne apne intizami dhanchay ki bari tarteeb-e-nau ki, jis ke baad sobay me 11 divisions aur 41 zilay ho gaye hain. Is se pehle 8 divisions aur 36 zilay ginne jate the, is liye purani kitabon aur purane notes me wahi tadaad milegi. Mojooda tadaad 11 divisions aur 41 zilay hai. Divisions ke naam ye hain. Quetta, Pishin, Zhob, Loralai, Koh-e-Sulaiman, Sibi, Naseerabad, Khuzdar, Lasbela, Makran aur Rakhshan.

Paper wali baatein ye hain. Raqbe me sab se bara aur abadi me sab se chhota soba, ye jora Balochistan ki pehchan hai. Pakistan ki sab se lambi sahel patti isi sobay me hai aur Gwadar ki bandargah bhi yahin hai. Bolan Darra, Hanna Jheel aur Quetta ke seb aur khushk meve mashhoor hain. Aur zilon ki tadaad me Balochistan ab Punjab ke barabar 41 par hai, ye taaza baat paper me a sakti hai.

Pin: 30.18, 66.99

Gilgit-Baltistan

Gilgit-Baltistan ka darul hukumat Gilgit hai. Ye ilaqa Pakistan ke intehai shumal me hai jahan duniya ke teen bare pahari silsile, Karakoram, Himalaya aur Hindu Kush, aapas me milte hain. Is ka raqba 72,971 murabba kilometre hai aur abadi takhreeban 15 lakh ke qareeb hai.

Gilgit-Baltistan me 3 divisions hain aur 14 zilay hain. Divisions ke naam ye hain. Gilgit, Baltistan aur Diamer.

Paper wali baatein ye hain. Duniya ki dusri sab se unchi choti K2 isi ilaqay me hai aur duniya ke bare glaciers bhi yahin hain. China ki sarhad aur Khunjerab Darra bhi isi ilaqay se jure hain aur CPEC ka shumali darwaza yehi hai. Gilgit-Baltistan ko sobay ka baqaida darja hasil nahi, is ka apna alag intizami nizam hai, ye farq bhi paper me ata hai.

Pin: 35.92, 74.31

Azad Jammu-o-Kashmir

Azad Jammu-o-Kashmir ka darul hukumat Muzaffarabad hai. Ye ilaqa Pakistan ke shumal mashriq me hai aur is ka raqba 13,297 murabba kilometre hai. Abadi takhreeban 40 lakh se zyada hai.

Azad Kashmir me 3 divisions hain aur 10 zilay hain. Divisions ke naam ye hain. Muzaffarabad, Poonch aur Mirpur.

Paper wali baatein ye hain. Mangla Dam Mirpur ke qareeb hai aur us ka zakhira Azad Kashmir ki zameen par hai, is liye Mangla ko aksar Azad Kashmir ke sath jor kar poochha jata hai. Neelum Valley aur darya-e-Neelum bhi mashhoor hain. Line of Control isi ilaqay ko Bharat ke zir-e-intizam Kashmir se alag karti hai. Azad Kashmir ki apni assembly aur apna alag nizam hai.

Pin: 34.37, 73.47

Islamabad Capital Territory

Islamabad Pakistan ka wafaqi darul hukumat hai aur ye kisi sobay ka hissa nahi. Is ka raqba 906 murabba kilometre hai, jo sab se chhota intizami ilaqa hai, aur 2023 ki mardum shumari ke mutabiq abadi takhreeban 23 lakh 64 hazar hai.

Islamabad me koi division nahi hai aur ye aik hi zila ginna jata hai. Is ka intizam wafaqi hukumat ke zeir-e-asar chalta hai aur sheher sectors me banta hua hai.

Paper wali baatein ye hain. Islamabad 1960 ki dahai me Karachi ki jagah naya darul hukumat banaya gaya tha. Margalla pahariyan, Faisal Masjid, Pakistan Monument aur Rawal Jheel is ki pehchan hain. Ye bhi yaad rakho ke Rawalpindi Punjab me hai aur Islamabad alag ilaqa hai, dono judwaan sheher kehlate hain lekin intizami tor par alag hain.

Pin: 33.73, 73.09

Kul tadaad wali baat

Ab zara saari tadaad jama karke dekh lo. Punjab 41, Sindh 30, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa 40, Balochistan 41, Gilgit-Baltistan 14, Azad Kashmir 10 aur Islamabad 1. In sab ko jama karen to pure Pakistan me kul 177 zilay bante hain. Agar Islamabad ko alag rakhen to baqi ilaaqon ke 176 zilay hain.

Divisions ki kul tadaad 37 hai. Punjab 10, Sindh 6, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa 7, Balochistan 11, Gilgit-Baltistan 3 aur Azad Kashmir 3, aur Islamabad me koi division nahi.

Aakhri mashwara ye hai ke zilon ki tadaad ko hamesha taaza rakhna. Jab bhi kisi sobay me naya zila banne ki khabar aye, apni tadaad usi din durust kar lo, kyunke paper hamesha wohi tadaad durust manta hai jo paper wale saal me mojood ho. Aur ye teen jore kabhi mat bhoolna. Sab se bara soba raqbe me Balochistan, sab se bara abadi me Punjab, aur sab se zyada zilay Punjab aur Balochistan dono me 41, 41.
