Pakistan ke Darya
Daryaaon ka safar, pahar se samandar tak

Ye hissa kyun zaroori hai

Dekho, PPSC ke one paper me GK aur Pak Studies ke hisse me daryaaon par sawal har saal ata hai. Kabhi poochte hain ke falan darya kahan se nikalta hai, kabhi poochte hain ke falan bandh kis darya par hai, kabhi poochte hain ke do darya kahan milte hain, aur kabhi seedha pooch lete hain ke Lahore kis darya ke kinare hai. Ye sawal mushkil nahi hote, bas masla ye hai ke aksar students daryaaon ko alag alag tukron me yaad karte hain. Aaj hum har darya ka pura safar aik kahani ki tarah parhenge. Jahan se darya nikla, jahan jahan se guzra, raste me kon us ke sath mila, us par kya bana, aur aakhir me wo kahan ja kar khatm hua. Kahani zehen me beth jaye to naqsha khud ban jata hai, aur naqsha ban jaye to ye sawal kabhi ghalat nahi hote.

Aik aam baat pehle samajh lo. Pakistan ke bare darya taqreeban sab ke sab shimal ke unche paharon se nikalte hain, kyunke wahan glaciers aur barf ka pani hota hai. Pahar se utar kar ye medaan me ate hain, medaan me in ki raftaar dheemi ho jati hai, aur aakhir me ya to kisi bare darya me mil jate hain ya seedha Arabian Sea me ja girte hain. Punjab ke paanch mashhoor darya, Jhelum, Chenab, Ravi, Beas aur Sutlej, aakhir me Panjnad ke maqam par aik ho jate hain, aur phir ye mila hua pani Mithankot ke qareeb Indus me mil jata hai. Ye aik line pakki yaad kar lo, aadhe sawal yahin se bante hain.

Darya-e-Sindh, yani Indus River

Sab se pehle wo darya jis ke naam par pura mulk chalta hai. Indus ko Pakistan ki lifeline kehte hain, aur ye paper ka sab se bara aur sab se pasandeeda sawal hai. Is ki lambai taqreeban 3,180 kilometre hai. Kuch kitabon me is se kuch kam ya zyada bhi likha milta hai, lekin aam taur par textbook ka figure 3,180 kilometre hi chalta hai, tum bhi yehi yaad rakho.

Indus nikalta kahan se hai? Ye Tibet ke ilaaqe me Lake Mansarovar ke qareeb, Mount Kailash ke paas aik chashme se nikalta hai. Us maqam ko Singi Khamban kehte hain, jis ke matlab hain sher ke munh wala chashma. Wahan se ye pehle shimal maghrib ki taraf behta hai, Ladakh se guzarta hai, phir Gilgit Baltistan me dakhil hota hai. Skardu ke qareeb se guzar kar ye Bunji aur Jaglot ke paas aik bohat mashhoor jagah se guzarta hai jahan teen bare pahari silsile milte hain, Himalaya, Karakoram aur Hindu Kush. Isi ilaaqe me Gilgit River aur Hunza River is ke sath milte hain.

Phir Indus Khyber Pakhtunkhwa me ata hai. Besham aur Thakot ke paas se guzarta hai, aur yahin is par Pakistan ka sab se bara bandh Tarbela bana hai. Attock ke qareeb is ke sath Kabul River milta hai, aur mashhoor baat ye hai ke Attock tak Indus ka pani saaf neelay rang ka hota hai aur Kabul River ka gadla, dono rang sangam par alag alag nazar ate hain. Kalabagh ke paas Jinnah Barrage hai. Phir ye Dera Ismail Khan ke kinare se guzarta hai, jahan Gomal River is se milta hai. Punjab me Taunsa Barrage, phir Sindh me Guddu Barrage, Sukkur Barrage aur Kotri Barrage is par bane hain. Sukkur aur Hyderabad jaise sheher is ke kinare abad hain.

Indus ke madadgar darya bohat se hain. Shimal me Gilgit, Hunza aur Shyok. Maghrib ki taraf se Kabul, Swat, Kurram, Gomal aur Zhob. Aur mashriq ki taraf se Punjab ke paanch darya Panjnad ki surat me. Aakhir me Indus Thatta ke aage aik bare delta ki surat me Arabian Sea me ja girta hai. Delta wo jagah hoti hai jahan darya samandar se milne se pehle kai chhoti shaakhon me bat jata hai.

Pin: 27.70, 68.86

Darya-e-Jhelum, yani Jhelum River

Jhelum Kashmir se ane wala darya hai. Ye Kashmir ki wadi me Verinag ke chashme se nikalta hai, jo Pir Panjal ke paharon ke daman me hai. Wahan se ye Srinagar se guzarta hai aur mashhoor Wular Lake me se hota hua aage barhta hai. Baramulla ke baad ye pahari ilaaqe me dakhil ho jata hai, Uri ke paas se guzarta hai, aur phir Muzaffarabad ke qareeb is ke sath Neelum River milta hai, jisay Kishanganga bhi kehte hain. Neelum is ka sab se bara madadgar hai, aur dusra aham madadgar Kunhar River hai jo Naran aur Kaghan ki wadi se ata hai. Jhelum ki kul lambai taqreeban 725 kilometre hai. Kuch kitabon me ye figure thora kam ya zyada bhi milta hai, lekin aam textbook figure yehi hai. Is lambai ka matlab ye hai ke Jhelum Indus aur Chenab ke muqable me chhota darya hai, lekin is ka safar do bilkul mukhtalif ilaaqon se guzarta hai, pehle Kashmir ki sarsabz wadi, phir Pakistan ka medaan.

Pakistan me Jhelum par sab se mashhoor cheez Mangla Dam hai, jo Mirpur ke qareeb bana hai. Ye Pakistan ke bare tareen dams me se hai. Mangla ke baad darya Jhelum sheher ke kinare se guzarta hai, aur Rasul Barrage is par bana hai. Aakhir me Jhelum Trimmu ke maqam par, Jhang ke qareeb, Chenab me mil jata hai. Ye wala sangam paper me bohat ata hai, pakka yaad rakho ke Jhelum khatm kahan hota hai. Trimmu par.

Pin: 33.14, 73.64

Darya-e-Chenab, yani Chenab River

Chenab do pahari naalon ke milne se banta hai, Chandra aur Bhaga. Ye dono Bharat ke ilaaqe Himachal Pradesh me Baralacha Pass ke qareeb se nikalte hain aur Lahaul ki wadi me mil kar Chenab ban jate hain. Isi liye purani kitabon me Chenab ko Chandrabhaga bhi likha milta hai. Chandra aur Bhaga ke sangam wali baat paper me bar bar ati hai.

Pakistan me Chenab par pehla bara maqam Marala hai, Sialkot ke qareeb, jahan Marala Barrage bana hai. Phir Khanki Barrage aur Qadirabad Barrage is par hain. Chenab Gujranwala aur Gujrat ke ilaaqon ke qareeb se guzarta hai, Chiniot ke paas se hota hua aage barhta hai. Trimmu ke maqam par is ke sath Jhelum milta hai, aur us ke kuch aage Ravi bhi is me mil jata hai. Tawi River, jo Jammu ki taraf se ata hai, is ka aham madadgar hai. Chenab ki kul lambai taqreeban 974 kilometre hai, aur aakhir me ye Panjnad ke safar ka hissa ban kar Indus me mil jata hai. Asal me Chenab hi wo darya hai jo Trimmu ke baad Ravi ko bhi sath le kar Panjnad ki taraf jata hai, aur Panjnad Mithankot ke qareeb Indus me girta hai.

Pin: 31.72, 72.98

Darya-e-Ravi, yani Ravi River

Ravi Punjab ke paanch daryaaon me sab se chhota mashhoor darya hai, lekin paper me is ka naam is liye bara hai kyunke Lahore is ke kinare abad hai. Ravi Bharat ke Himachal Pradesh me, Kangra ke ilaaqe Bara Bangahal se nikalta hai, Rohtang Pass ke qareeb ke paharon se. Is ki lambai taqreeban 720 kilometre hai.

Ravi Madhopur ke qareeb se hota hua Pakistan me dakhil hota hai. Shahdara ke paas se guzar kar Lahore ke kinare behta hai. Lahore aur Ravi ka rishta itna mashhoor hai ke paper me ye sawal aam hai ke Lahore kis darya ke kinare hai, jawab Ravi. Ravi par Balloki Barrage bana hai jo Kasur aur Nankana ke ilaaqe me hai, aur Sidhnai Barrage bhi is par hai. Aakhir me Ravi Khanewal ke ilaaqe me, Sarai Sidhu ke qareeb, Chenab me mil jata hai. To kahani ye bani ke Ravi akela Indus tak nahi jata, raste me Chenab ke sath mil jata hai.

Pin: 31.55, 74.34

Darya-e-Beas, yani Beas River

Beas Punjab ke paanch daryaaon me woh darya hai jo taqreeban pura ka pura Bharat me behta hai. Ye Himachal Pradesh me Rohtang Pass ke qareeb Beas Kund se nikalta hai. Is ki lambai taqreeban 470 kilometre hai. Kullu aur Manali ki wadiyon se guzarta hai, Punjab ke medaan me ata hai, aur Harike ke maqam par Sutlej me mil jata hai. Harike wahi jagah hai jahan mashhoor Harike Wetland aur barrage hai.

Tum socho ge ke jab Beas Pakistan me behta hi nahi to paper me kyun ata hai. Is ki do wajah hain. Aik to ye ke paanch daryaaon ki ginti is ke baghair puri nahi hoti, aur dusri ye ke Indus Water Treaty, yani 1960 ke Sindh Taas muaahide me teen mashriqi darya, Ravi, Beas aur Sutlej, Bharat ke hisse me diye gaye the, jabke teen maghribi darya, Indus, Jhelum aur Chenab, Pakistan ke hisse me aye. Ye treaty wala sawal paper ka bohat hi mashhoor sawal hai, aur Beas usi me ata hai.

Pin: 31.17, 75.20

Darya-e-Sutlej, yani Sutlej River

Sutlej Punjab ke paanch daryaaon me sab se lamba hai, taqreeban 1,450 kilometre. Lekin aik baat yahan samajh lo ke is puri lambai ka zyada tar hissa Tibet aur Bharat me hai. Pakistan me Sutlej apne safar ke aakhri hisse me behta hai, is liye lambai sun kar ye mat samajhna ke ye pura ka pura Pakistan me hai. Ye Tibet me Lake Rakshastal se nikalta hai, jo Lake Mansarovar ke bilkul qareeb hai. Dekho, kitni dilchasp baat hai ke Indus aur Sutlej dono aik hi ilaaqe se nikalte hain, lekin aik maghrib ko jata hai aur dusra mashriq ko. Sutlej Shipki La ke daray se hota hua Himachal Pradesh me dakhil hota hai, jahan is par Bhakra Dam bana hai, jo Bharat ke bare dams me se hai.

Pakistan me Sutlej Bahawalpur ke ilaaqe se guzarta hai. Is par Sulemanki Barrage, Islam Barrage aur aakhir me Panjnad ke qareeb ke maqamat hain. Beas Harike par is me pehle hi mil chuka hota hai. Aakhir me Sutlej Panjnad ki surat me, yani baqi daryaaon ke sath mil kar, Mithankot ke qareeb Indus me mil jata hai. Sutlej ke kinare ke mashhoor ilaaqe Kasur, Pakpattan, Bahawalnagar aur Bahawalpur hain. Pakpattan is ke qareeb hone ki wajah se paper me ata hai, kyunke Baba Farid ka sheher hai.

Pin: 29.40, 71.68

Darya-e-Kabul, yani Kabul River

Ab shimal maghrib ke daryaaon ki taraf ao. Kabul River Afghanistan se ane wala darya hai aur Indus ka sab se aham maghribi madadgar hai. Ye Afghanistan me Hindu Kush ke Sanglakh silsile se, Unai Pass ke qareeb se nikalta hai. Is ki kul lambai taqreeban 700 kilometre hai. Ye Kabul sheher ke kinare se guzarta hai, Jalalabad se hota hai, aur phir Khyber ke ilaaqe se Pakistan me dakhil hota hai.

Pakistan me is par Warsak Dam bana hai, jo Peshawar ke qareeb hai aur azaadi ke baad banne wale pehle bare dams me se hai. Kabul River Peshawar ki wadi ko sairaab karta hai, Nowshera ke kinare se guzarta hai, aur Attock ke qareeb, Kund ke maqam par, Indus me mil jata hai. Is ke madadgar daryaaon me Swat River, Kunar River, Panjkora aur Bara River shamil hain. Kunar wahi darya hai jo Pakistan me Chitral River kehlata hai aur Afghanistan me ja kar Kunar ban jata hai. Ye wali baat aage Chitral River me phir ayegi, dono ko jor kar yaad rakho.

Pin: 34.01, 71.98

Darya-e-Swat, yani Swat River

Swat River Khyber Pakhtunkhwa ke sab se khubsurat ilaaqe ka darya hai. Ye Swat ke shimali paharon me, Kalam ke ilaaqe ke glaciers se banta hai, jahan Ushu aur Utrot ke naale milte hain. Hindu Kush ke barf posh pahar is ka asal zariya hain. Swat River ki lambai taqreeban 240 kilometre hai. Ye koi bohat lamba darya nahi, lekin is ki khaasiyat ye hai ke is ka taqreeban pura safar aik hi mashhoor wadi ke andar tay hota hai, Kalam se shuru ho kar Charsadda tak.

Kalam, Bahrain aur Madyan ke kinare se guzarta hua ye Mingora aur Saidu Sharif ke ilaaqe tak ata hai. Mingora Swat ka sab se bara sheher hai aur darya is ke qareeb se guzarta hai. Panjkora River, jo Dir ki taraf se ata hai, is ka aham madadgar hai. Aakhir me Swat River Charsadda ke qareeb Kabul River me mil jata hai. To is ka safar Indus tak seedha nahi, pehle Kabul River, phir Kabul ke sath Indus. Paper me aksar yehi phansaya jata hai ke Swat seedha Indus me milta hai, aisa nahi hai.

Pin: 34.77, 72.36

Darya-e-Gilgit, yani Gilgit River

Gilgit River Gilgit Baltistan ka aham darya hai. Ye Ghizer ke ilaaqe me Shandur ke qareeb ke pahari naalon aur jheelon se banta hai. Shandur wahi maqam hai jahan har saal mashhoor polo festival hota hai, aur Shandur Lake is ilaaqe ki pehchan hai. Gilgit River ki lambai taqreeban 240 kilometre hai, aur is chhote se safar me hi ye Ghizer aur Gilgit ki wadiyon ko sairaab karta hua Indus tak pohanch jata hai.

Gilgit River Gahkuch aur Gilgit sheher ke kinare se guzarta hai. Gilgit sheher is ke kinare abad hai. Is ka sab se aham madadgar Hunza River hai, jo Gilgit sheher ke bilkul qareeb is me milta hai. Phir ye mila hua darya Bunji aur Jaglot ke ilaaqe me Indus me ja milta hai. Jaglot wahi mashhoor jagah hai jahan se teen bare pahari silsile, Himalaya, Karakoram aur Hindu Kush, aik sath nazar ate hain. Darya aur paharon ka ye sangam paper me aksar ata hai.

Pin: 35.92, 74.31

Darya-e-Hunza, yani Hunza River

Hunza River shimal ka mashhoor darya hai. Ye Khunjerab Pass ke qareeb ke glaciers se banta hai, jahan se Pakistan aur China ki sarhad hai aur Karakoram Highway guzarti hai. Khunjerab duniya ki unchi tareen paved sarhadi guzargah me se hai, ye baat paper me alag se bhi ati hai. Hunza River ki lambai taqreeban 190 kilometre hai, yani ye aik chhota pahari darya hai, lekin apni wadi ki khubsurti aur Karakoram Highway ke sath sath chalne ki wajah se is ka naam har jagah ata hai.

Hunza River puri Hunza wadi se guzarta hai. Karimabad, Aliabad aur Ganish jaise mashhoor maqamat is ke kinare ya is ke qareeb hain. Rakaposhi ki barf posh choti is wadi se nazar ati hai. Nagar ki taraf se ane wala Hispar ka pani bhi isi ilaaqe me is ke sath milta hai. Aakhir me Hunza River Gilgit sheher ke qareeb Gilgit River me mil jata hai, aur phir Gilgit River ke sath Indus tak pohanchta hai. To tartib ye bani, Hunza pehle Gilgit me, Gilgit phir Indus me.

Pin: 36.33, 74.67

Darya-e-Chitral, yani Chitral River

Chitral River Hindu Kush ke unche glaciers se nikalta hai. Is ka asal sar chashma Chiantar Glacier hai, jo Broghil aur Darkot ke ilaaqe me hai. Ye Chitral ki wadi se guzarta hai, Chitral sheher aur Drosh is ke kinare ke mashhoor maqamat hain.

Ab is darya ki sab se khaas baat suno, jo paper me bohat ati hai. Chitral River Pakistan se nikal kar Afghanistan me dakhil ho jata hai, aur wahan is ka naam badal kar Kunar River ho jata hai. Kunar phir Jalalabad ke qareeb Kabul River me milta hai, aur Kabul River Attock par Indus me. Yani Chitral ka pani aik lambe safar ke baad aakhir Indus hi me pohanchta hai, lekin teen naam badal kar. Chitral, phir Kunar, phir Kabul, phir Indus. Ye silsila aik line me yaad kar lo. Is poore safar ki kul lambai, Chitral aur Kunar dono hisse mila kar, taqreeban 480 kilometre banti hai. Lambai hamesha dono naamon ko mila kar gini jati hai, kyunke darya aik hi hai, bas sarhad ke us paar naam badal jata hai.

Pin: 35.85, 71.79

Zhob aur Gomal, yani Zhob River aur Gomal River

Ye do darya aksar aik sath parhaye jate hain kyunke Zhob, Gomal ka madadgar hai. Pehle Gomal ko samjho. Gomal River Afghanistan ke ilaaqe Ghazni aur Katawaz se nikalta hai, South Waziristan se hota hua Pakistan me behta hai. Is ki kul lambai taqreeban 400 kilometre hai, jis ka aik hissa Afghanistan me aur baqi Pakistan me hai. Wana is ke ilaaqe ka mashhoor maqam hai. Is par Gomal Zam Dam bana hai, jo South Waziristan me hai. Phir ye Tank ke qareeb se guzarta hai aur Dera Ismail Khan ke ilaaqe me Indus me mil jata hai.

Zhob River Balochistan ke shimal mashriqi ilaaqe se nikalta hai, Toba Kakar ke pahari silsile ke qareeb se. Ye Zhob sheher ke kinare se guzarta hai aur aakhir me Gomal River me mil jata hai. Zhob ki apni lambai bhi taqreeban 410 kilometre hai, yani ye sirf aik chhota madadgar naala nahi, apni jagah aik lamba darya hai jo Balochistan ke shimaali ilaaqon se pani la kar Gomal ko deta hai. Zhob ka purana naam Fort Sandeman tha, ye baat bhi Pak Studies me ati hai. To tartib ye hai ke Zhob pehle Gomal me, aur Gomal phir Indus me. Dono darya mil kar Dera Ismail Khan aur Zhob ke khushk ilaaqon ke liye pani ka bara zariya hain.

Pin: 31.86, 70.90

Darya-e-Hub, yani Hub River

Ab Balochistan aur Sindh ke saahili daryaaon ki taraf ao. Hub River Kirthar ke pahari silsile se nikalta hai aur Balochistan aur Sindh ke darmiyan aik qisam ki sarhad ka kaam bhi karta hai. Is ki lambai taqreeban 350 kilometre hai, yani ye koi chhota naala nahi, Balochistan ke bare daryaaon me is ka shumar hota hai. Lekin is ki asal pehchan is ki lambai nahi, Karachi se is ka rishta hai.

Hub par Hub Dam bana hai, jo Karachi ke liye peene ke pani ka bara zariya hai. Karachi jaise bare sheher ke qareeb hona hi is darya ki sab se bari pehchan hai. Aakhir me Hub River Karachi ke maghrib me, Cape Monze ke qareeb, Arabian Sea me ja girta hai. Paper me sawal yun ata hai ke Hub Dam kis darya par hai, ya Karachi ko pani dene wala dam kon sa hai. Dono ka jawab isi darya se jura hai.

Pin: 25.25, 67.18

Darya-e-Porali, yani Porali River

Porali River Balochistan ke Lasbela ke ilaaqe ka darya hai. Ye Kirthar ke pahari silsile ki mashriqi dhalanon se nikalta hai aur Lasbela ke medaan se guzarta hai. Winder is ke kinare ka mashhoor qasba hai.

Porali ki khaas baat ye hai ke ye saal bhar behne wala darya nahi, aksar mosami hota hai, yani barishon ke mosam me pani ata hai aur khushk mosam me ye taqreeban sookh jata hai. Lasbela ke ilaaqe ki zarayi zindagi ka inhisaar bohat had tak isi par hai. Aakhir me ye Sonmiani ke qareeb Arabian Sea me mil jata hai. Balochistan ke aksar darya isi qisam ke hain, chhote aur mosami, kyunke wahan barish kam hoti hai. Ye wajah bhi samajh lo, paper me Balochistan ke daryaaon ki khushki par sawal banta hai.

Pin: 25.32, 66.65

Darya-e-Hingol, yani Hingol River

Hingol Balochistan ka sab se lamba darya hai, taqreeban 560 kilometre. Ye baat hi paper ka sawal hai, kyunke aam taur par students samajhte hain ke Balochistan me koi bara darya hai hi nahi. Hingol Balochistan ke pahari ilaaqon se nikalta hai aur Makran ke saahili silsilon se guzarta hua behta hai.

Hingol ka sab se mashhoor ilaaqa Hingol National Park hai, jo Pakistan ke sab se bare national parks me se hai. Darya park ke darmiyan se guzarta hai aur is ke kinare ki ajeeb pahari shaklein door door tak mashhoor hain. Hinglaj ka mashhoor mandir bhi isi darya ke kinare hai, jahan har saal yatra hoti hai. Aakhir me Hingol Arabian Sea me ja girta hai. Yaad rakhne ka tareeqa ye hai ke Balochistan ke teen saahili darya, Hub, Porali aur Hingol, teeno paharon se nikal kar seedha Arabian Sea me girte hain, kisi bare darya me nahi milte. Ye teeno ki mushtarka pehchan hai.

Pin: 25.60, 65.50

Aakhir me pura naqsha aik nazar me

Ab puri kahani ko aik dafa jor kar dekho. Shimal ke paharon se Indus, Jhelum, Chenab, Ravi, Beas aur Sutlej nikalte hain. Punjab ke paanch darya Panjnad par aik hote hain aur Mithankot par Indus me milte hain. Maghrib se Kabul, Swat, Chitral urf Kunar, Gomal aur Zhob ate hain aur Indus ke madadgar bante hain. Shimal me Gilgit aur Hunza bhi Indus hi me milte hain. Aur Balochistan ke Hub, Porali aur Hingol seedha Arabian Sea ka rukh karte hain. Agar ye chaar linein tumhe zehen me saaf nazar ane lag jayen to samajh lo Pakistan ke daryaaon ka pura naqsha tumhe a gaya, aur is topic ke number pakke ho gaye.
