Pehle Matlab Samjho — Geography ki Istilahaat
Har lafz ka asaan matlab, Pakistan ki asal misaal, aur paper wali baat

Ye topic kyun zaroori hai

Dekho, PPSC ke paper me bohat se sawal asal me mushkil nahi hote, bas lafz mushkil hota hai. Sawal poochta hai ke falan cheez delta hai ya plateau, doab kise kehte hain, barrage aur dam me kya farq hai. Student ko jagah yaad hoti hai lekin lafz ka matlab pakka nahi hota, aur wahi number kat jata hai. Is liye hum geography ki asal shuruaat yahan se karte hain. Pehle har lafz ka matlab itna asaan kar ke samjho ke tum apne lafzon me kisi ko samjha sako. Matlab pakka ho jaye to naqsha khud bolne lagta hai.

Har lafz ko teen hisso me parho ge. Pehle aik line me asaan matlab, jese ustad class me samjhata hai. Phir Pakistan ki aik asal misaal, taake lafz hawa me na rahe. Aur aakhir me wo baat jo paper me sawal banti hai. Bas yehi tartib zehen me bitha lo.

Doab

Matlab ye hai. Do daryaaon ke darmiyan phasi hui zameen ko doab kehte hain. Lafz hi apna matlab bata raha hai, do matlab do aur aab matlab pani yani darya. Do darya aur un ke darmiyan ki zameen, bas yehi doab hai.

Pakistan ki misaal ye hai. Punjab me paanch mashhoor doab hain. Indus aur Jhelum ke darmiyan Sindh Sagar Doab hai aur Chenab aur Ravi ke darmiyan Rachna Doab hai, jahan Faisalabad aur Gujranwala jese sheher hain.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Har doab ka naam unhi do daryaaon ke naamon ke pehle harfon se bana hai. Chaj matlab Chenab aur Jhelum, Bari matlab Beas aur Ravi. Naam toro, darya khud samne ajayenge, aur sawal hal ho jayega.

Darra (Pass, Mountain Pass)

Matlab ye hai. Pahari silsile ke andar wo tang aur nicha rasta jahan se guzar kar dusri taraf jaya ja sake, use darra kehte hain. Pahar to har jagah uncha hota hai, lekin darra wo kamzor nuqta hai jahan pahar insaan ko rasta de deta hai.

Pakistan ki misaal ye hai. Khyber Darra Pakistan ko Afghanistan se milata hai aur Peshawar se Kabul ka purana rasta yehi hai. Khunjerab Darra Pakistan ko China se milata hai aur duniya ki sab se unchi paved sarhad wali guzargah hai.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Sawal hamesha jora bana kar ata hai, konsa darra kin do ilaaqon ko milata hai. Khyber Afghanistan ke sath, Khunjerab China ke sath, Bolan Quetta ko Sibi aur Sindh ki taraf jane wale raste ke sath. Ye jore pakke kar lo.

Dam

Matlab ye hai. Dam darya ke raste me banayi gayi wo mazboot deewar hai jo pani ko peeche rok kar aik bara zakhira bana leti hai. Is zakhire se bijli bhi banti hai aur khushk mausam me faslon ko pani bhi milta hai.

Pakistan ki misaal ye hai. Tarbela Dam darya-e-Indus par hai aur mitti se bana duniya ka sab se bara dam kaha jata hai. Mangla Dam darya-e-Jhelum par hai.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Dam hamesha apne darya ke sath yaad rakho. Tarbela Indus par, Mangla Jhelum par, Warsak Kabul par. Paper dam ka naam de kar darya poochta hai ya darya de kar dam.

Barrage

Matlab ye hai. Barrage bhi darya par bani deewar hoti hai lekin dam se chhoti aur us ka kaam mukhtalif hai. Barrage pani ka bara zakhira nahi banata, bas darya ki satah zara unchi karke pani ko nehron ki taraf mor deta hai, taake aabpashi ho sake.

Pakistan ki misaal ye hai. Sukkur Barrage darya-e-Indus par 1932 me bana tha aur is ka purana naam Lloyd Barrage hai. Is se nikalne wali nehron se Sindh ki bohat bari zameen serab hoti hai.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Dam aur barrage ka farq paper ka pasandeeda sawal hai. Dam zakhira aur bijli ke liye, barrage pani nehron me morne ke liye. Aur Indus par aakhri barrage Kotri hai, use Ghulam Muhammad Barrage bhi kehte hain.

Delta

Matlab ye hai. Jahan darya samandar me girne se pehle sust ho jata hai, apne sath layi hui mitti aur ret wahan bicha deta hai aur kai chhoti shakho me bant kar samandar me milta hai, us pankhe jaisi zameen ko delta kehte hain.

Pakistan ki misaal ye hai. Darya-e-Indus Thatta aur Keti Bandar ke qareeb Arabian Sea me girta hai aur wahan Indus Delta banta hai. Ye duniya ke bare delton me se aik hai.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Pakistan ka sirf aik hi mashhoor delta hai aur wo Indus ka hai. Sawal seedha ata hai ke Indus Delta kis ilaqay me hai, jawab Thatta ke aas paas ka sahil hai.

Plateau (Satah Murtafa)

Matlab ye hai. Wo unchi zameen jo pahar ki tarah nukili na ho, balke upar se hamvar aur chat jaisi ho, use plateau ya satah murtafa kehte hain. Asaan alfaaz me, unchi magar seedhi zameen.

Pakistan ki misaal ye hai. Pothohar Plateau Indus aur Jhelum ke darmiyan hai, jahan Rawalpindi, Chakwal aur Jhelum ke ilaqay ate hain. Balochistan ka aksar hissa bhi aik uncha plateau hi hai.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Pothohar ko hamesha Salt Range aur soan ki tehzeeb ke sath jor kar poochha jata hai. Aur Balochistan raqbe me sab se bara soba hone ki aik wajah yehi wasee plateau hai.

Maidan (Plain)

Matlab ye hai. Wo neechi aur hamvar zameen jahan darya behte hain aur khetyan asaan hoti hain, use maidan kehte hain. Maidan daryaaon ki layi hui mitti se banta hai, is liye ye sab se zarkhez zameen hoti hai.

Pakistan ki misaal ye hai. Punjab aur Sindh ka aksar hissa Indus aur us ke madadgar daryaaon ka banaya hua maidan hai. Isi liye Pakistan ki zyada tar abadi aur zyada tar faslein inhi maidanon me hain.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Sawal ata hai ke Pakistan ki zyada tar abadi maidanon me kyun hai. Jawab yehi mitti aur pani hai. Maidan suno to Indus, Punjab aur Sindh zehen me lao.

Registran (Desert)

Matlab ye hai. Wo ilaaqa jahan barish bohat kam ho, pani na mile aur ret ya khushk zameen door tak phaili ho, use registran kehte hain. Registran me din garam aur raat thandi hoti hai.

Pakistan ki misaal ye hai. Sindh me Thar ka registran hai jahan Nagarparkar aur Umerkot hain. Punjab me Cholistan hai jahan Derawar Fort khara hai, aur Balochistan me Kharan ka registran hai.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Har registran ko us ke sobay aur aik pehchan wali jagah ke sath yaad rakho. Thar ke sath Sindh, Cholistan ke sath Bahawalpur aur Derawar Fort, Kharan ke sath Balochistan.

Glacier

Matlab ye hai. Paharon par jama hua barf ka wo wasee aur mota teh jo saalon me jamta hai aur bohat ahista ahista dhalan ki taraf sarkta hai, use glacier kehte hain. Glacier asal me barf ka darya hai, bas us ki raftaar bohat sust hoti hai.

Pakistan ki misaal ye hai. Siachen Glacier Karakoram me hai aur qatbi ilaaqon ke bahar duniya ke sab se lambe glachieron me se hai. Baltoro Glacier se K2 ki taraf jaya jata hai.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Siachen ko duniya ka sab se uncha medaan-e-jang bhi kaha jata hai aur yehi line paper me ati hai. Glacier suno to Karakoram aur barf zehen me lao, maidan nahi.

Madadgar Darya (Tributary)

Matlab ye hai. Wo chhota darya jo kisi bare darya me ja milta hai aur apna pani us ke hawale kar deta hai, use madadgar darya ya tributary kehte hain. Milne ke baad us ka apna naam khatam ho jata hai aur bara darya hi aage barhta hai.

Pakistan ki misaal ye hai. Darya-e-Kabul Attock ke qareeb Indus me milta hai, yani Kabul Indus ka madadgar darya hai. Jhelum Trimmu ke maqam par Chenab me milta hai.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Paper jore ulta karke phansata hai. Ravi Indus me nahi milta, Chenab me milta hai. Swat seedha Indus me nahi milta, pehle Kabul me milta hai. Ye milne wale maqamat pakke kar lo.

Sarhad (Border)

Matlab ye hai. Do mulkon ke darmiyan wo tay shuda lakeer jo aik mulk ki zameen ko dusre se alag karti hai, use sarhad kehte hain. Sarhad zameen par bhi hoti hai aur naqshay par bhi.

Pakistan ki misaal ye hai. Pakistan ki sarhadein chaar mulkon se milti hain. Mashriq me India, maghrib me Afghanistan aur Iran, aur shumal me China.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Sab se lambi sarhad Afghanistan ke sath hai aur sab se chhoti China ke sath. Sarhadon ki lambai wala sawal har dusre paper me ata hai, is liye har sarhad ko us ke mulk aur us ki khaas lakeer ke sath yaad rakho.

Line of Control

Matlab ye hai. Line of Control, jisay mukhtasaran LoC kehte hain, Pakistan aur India ke darmiyan Kashmir me wo aarzi lakeer hai jahan dono mulkon ki faujein aamne saamne hain. Ye tay shuda sarhad nahi, jang bandi se bani hui lakeer hai.

Pakistan ki misaal ye hai. Ye lakeer Azad Kashmir ko Bharat ke zir-e-intizam Kashmir se alag karti hai aur takhreeban 740 kilometre lambi hai. Neelum Valley is ke bilkul qareeb hai.

Paper wali baat ye hai. LoC 1972 ke Shimla Muahide ke baad is naam se pehchani gayi. Paper me ye sarhad se alag ginni jati hai, is farq ko samajh lo ke LoC aarzi hai, sarhad tay shuda hoti hai.

Durand Line

Matlab ye hai. Durand Line Pakistan aur Afghanistan ke darmiyan wo sarhad hai jo 1893 me aik muahide se tay hui thi. Is ka naam us angrez afsar Sir Mortimer Durand ke naam par hai jis ne ye muahida karwaya tha.

Pakistan ki misaal ye hai. Ye lakeer 2,430 kilometre lambi hai (kuch kitaabon me 2,640 bhi likha milta hai, lekin one paper ki mashhoor textbook rakam 2,430 hi hai) aur Khyber se Chaman tak ke ilaaqon ko Afghanistan se alag karti hai. Torkham aur Chaman is ki mashhoor guzargahen hain.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Teen baatein aik sath yaad rakho. Saal 1893, mulk Afghanistan, aur ye Pakistan ki sab se lambi sarhad hai. Ye teeno aik hi sawal ke mukhtalif roop hain.

Division

Matlab ye hai. Division sobay ki wo bari intizami ikai hai jo kai zilon ko mila kar banti hai. Soba sab se bara hota hai, us ke neeche division ata hai, aur division ke neeche zila. Division ka sarbarah Commissioner hota hai.

Pakistan ki misaal ye hai. Punjab me Lahore Division hai jis ke neeche Lahore, Kasur, Sheikhupura aur Nankana Sahib ke zilay ate hain. Sindh me Karachi Division hai.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Har sobay ki divisions ki tadaad aur naam pooche jate hain. Punjab ki 10 divisions sab se zyada dohrayi jati hain, aur Gujrat Division nisbatan nayi hai, ye baat bhi note kar lo.

District (Zila)

Matlab ye hai. Zila intizam ki wo ikai hai jahan se asal me awam ka roz ka kaam chalta hai. Har zilay ka sarbarah Deputy Commissioner hota hai, jisay aam zubaan me DC kehte hain. Zameen ke record, aman-o-aman aur sarkari kaam isi daftar se chalte hain.

Pakistan ki misaal ye hai. Lahore aik zila hai, Multan aik zila hai, Quetta aik zila hai. Har sobay me darjanon zilay hain aur waqt ke sath naye zilay bante rehte hain.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Har sobay ke zilon ki tadaad Pakistan Studies ka sab se mashhoor sawal hai. Tadaad badal sakti hai kyunke naye zilay bante rehte hain, is liye hamesha taaza tadaad yaad rakho. Is ki mukammal tafseel provinces wali file me di gayi hai.

Tehsil

Matlab ye hai. Tehsil zilay ke andar ki chhoti intizami ikai hai. Aik zilay me kai tehsilein hoti hain aur har tehsil ka sarbarah Assistant Commissioner hota hai. Zameen ki fard, intiqal aur registry ke kaam tehsil ki satah se jure hote hain.

Pakistan ki misaal ye hai. Zila Lahore me Lahore City, Model Town, Raiwind, Shalimar aur Cantt jaisi tehsilein hain. Har tehsil apne ilaqay ke dehat aur qasbon ko sambhalti hai.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Intizami seedhi yaad rakho. Soba, phir division, phir zila, phir tehsil, aur sab se neeche union council. Sawal aksar is tartib ko ulta seedha karke poochta hai.

Union Council

Matlab ye hai. Union council sab se chhoti intizami aur baldiyati ikai hai. Ye chand dehat ya sheher ke chand muhallon ko mila kar banti hai aur awam ke sab se qareeb hoti hai. Paidaish aur wafat ke certificate jese kaam yahin se hote hain.

Pakistan ki misaal ye hai. Har gaon aur har shehri muhalla kisi na kisi union council me ata hai. Baldiyati intikhabat me chairman aur councilor isi satah par chune jate hain.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Union council ko sab se chhoti ikai ke tor par yaad rakho. Paper me sawal ata hai ke sab se chhoti intizami ikai konsi hai, jawab union council hai.

Sobai Darul Hukumat (Provincial Capital)

Matlab ye hai. Har sobay ka wo sheher jahan sobay ki hukumat, assembly aur wazir-e-aala ka daftar ho, use sobai darul hukumat kehte hain. Soba apne sare bare faisle usi sheher se chalata hai.

Pakistan ki misaal ye hai. Punjab ka darul hukumat Lahore hai, Sindh ka Karachi, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa ka Peshawar aur Balochistan ka Quetta.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Gilgit-Baltistan ka darul hukumat Gilgit aur Azad Kashmir ka Muzaffarabad hai. Ye dono aksar bhool jate hain, is liye in par khaas tawajjo do.

Wafaqi Darul Hukumat (Federal Capital)

Matlab ye hai. Pure mulk ki hukumat jis sheher se chalti hai, jahan parliament, sadar aur wazir-e-azam ke daftar hain, use wafaqi darul hukumat kehte hain. Ye kisi sobay ka hissa nahi hota, is ka apna alag intizami darja hota hai.

Pakistan ki misaal ye hai. Islamabad Pakistan ka wafaqi darul hukumat hai. Ye 1960 ki dahai me Karachi ki jagah naya darul hukumat banaya gaya tha aur Margalla ki pahariyon ke daman me basta hai.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Pakistan ka pehla darul hukumat Karachi tha, phir kuch arsa Rawalpindi raha, aur aakhir me Islamabad bana. Ye tartib paper me bohat ati hai.

Bandargah (Port)

Matlab ye hai. Samandar ke kinare wo jagah jahan bare jahaz rukte hain, samaan charhaya aur utara jata hai aur mulk ki tijarat chalti hai, use bandargah kehte hain. Bandargah mulk ka samandar ki taraf khulne wala darwaza hoti hai.

Pakistan ki misaal ye hai. Karachi Port Pakistan ki sab se purani aur bari bandargah hai. Port Qasim bhi Karachi ke qareeb hai aur Gwadar Balochistan ke sahel par gehray pani ki jadeed bandargah hai.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Gwadar ko CPEC aur gehray samandar ke sath jor kar poochha jata hai. Aur ye bhi yaad rakho ke Pakistan ki tamam bandargahen Arabian Sea par hain.

Jheel (Lake)

Matlab ye hai. Chaaron taraf zameen se ghiri hui pani ki wo bari ikai jo darya se alag apni jagah bani ho, use jheel kehte hain. Kuch jheelein meethe pani ki hoti hain aur kuch khare pani ki.

Pakistan ki misaal ye hai. Manchar Jheel Sindh me hai aur Pakistan ki sab se bari meethe pani ki jheel kahi jati hai. Shumali ilaaqon me Saiful Muluk aur Attabad jaisi khoobsurat jheelein hain.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Manchar ko sab se bari, Keenjhar ko Karachi ko pani dene wali, aur Saiful Muluk ko Naran ke sath jor kar yaad rakho. Attabad 2010 me pahar girne se bani thi, ye baat bhi ati hai.

Aabshar (Waterfall)

Matlab ye hai. Jahan darya ya nala unchai se achanak neeche girta hai aur pani ki safed chaadar si ban jati hai, use aabshar kehte hain. Aabshar hamesha pahari ilaqay me hoti hai kyunke unchai wahin milti hai.

Pakistan ki misaal ye hai. Haripur ke qareeb Noori Waterfall hai aur Swat me Jarogo Waterfall Pakistan ki unchi aabsharon me ginni jati hai. Abbottabad ke qareeb Sajikot bhi mashhoor hai.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Pakistan me aabsharen kam hain, is liye jab sawal aye to shumali pahari ilaaqay zehen me lao. Aabshar ko jheel se ulta samjho, jheel thehra pani hai aur aabshar girta hua pani.

Canal (Nehr)

Matlab ye hai. Nehr insaan ki banayi hui wo pani ki naali hai jo darya ya barrage se pani nikaal kar door door ki zameenon tak le jati hai. Nehr ka pani gharon ke liye nahi, zyada tar faslon ki aabpashi ke liye hota hai.

Pakistan ki misaal ye hai. Lower Chenab Canal Faisalabad aur us ke gird ke ilaaqon ko serab karti hai. Pakistan ka nehri nizam duniya ke sab se bare aabpashi nizamon me ginna jata hai.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Har bari nehr ko us ke darya ke sath yaad rakho. Upper Jhelum Canal Jhelum se, Upper Chenab Canal Chenab se. Sawal aksar nehr ka naam de kar us ka darya poochta hai.

Link Canal

Matlab ye hai. Link canal wo khaas nehr hai jo aik darya ka pani dusre darya ki nehron tak pohanchati hai. Ye aam nehr se is liye alag hai ke ye zameen serab karne ke bajaye daryaaon ko aapas me jorti hai.

Pakistan ki misaal ye hai. Chashma Jhelum Link Canal Indus ka pani Jhelum ki taraf le jati hai. Rasul Qadirabad aur Qadirabad Balloki bhi mashhoor link canals hain.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Link canals Indus Waters Treaty ke baad aham hui thin, jab mashriqi daryaaon ka pani Bharat ko mila to maghribi daryaaon ka pani link canals se mashriqi ilaaqon tak pohanchaya gaya. Ye wajah paper me ati hai.

Headworks

Matlab ye hai. Headworks darya par wo maqam aur dhancha hai jahan se nehrein nikaali jati hain aur pani ka bahao control kiya jata hai. Asaan alfaaz me, nehron ka shuru hone wala darwaza headworks hota hai.

Pakistan ki misaal ye hai. Balloki Headworks darya-e-Ravi par hai aur wahan se Lower Bari Doab Canal nikalti hai. Sulemanki Headworks darya-e-Sutlej par hai.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Headworks aur barrage qareeb qareeb ki cheezein hain, farq bas ye hai ke headworks nehron ke nikalne ke maqam par zyada zor deta hai. Balloki ko Ravi aur Sulemanki ko Sutlej ke sath pakka kar lo.

Silsila (Mountain Range)

Matlab ye hai. Paharon ki wo lambi qataar jo aik hi simt me door tak phaili ho, use pahari silsila kehte hain. Aik pahar akela hota hai, lekin jab bohat se pahar aik line me jur jayen to silsila ban jata hai.

Pakistan ki misaal ye hai. Karakoram, Himalaya aur Hindu Kush Pakistan ke teen mashhoor silsile hain aur teeno shumal me milte hain. Maghrib me Sulaiman aur Kirthar silsile hain.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Har silsile ko us ki sab se unchi choti ke sath yaad rakho. Karakoram ke sath K2, Himalaya ke sath Nanga Parbat, Hindu Kush ke sath Tirich Mir. Ye jore paper ki jaan hain.

Choti (Peak)

Matlab ye hai. Kisi pahar ya silsile ka sab se uncha nuqta choti kehlata hai. Choti ki unchai samandar ki satah se naapi jati hai aur feet ya metre me batayi jati hai.

Pakistan ki misaal ye hai. K2 Pakistan ki sab se unchi aur duniya ki dusri sab se unchi choti hai, is ki unchai 8,611 metre hai. Nanga Parbat 8,126 metre ke sath Pakistan ki dusri bari choti hai.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Chotiyon ki tartib wala sawal bohat ata hai. K2, phir Nanga Parbat, phir Gasherbrum One, phir Broad Peak aur phir Gasherbrum Two. Ye unchai wali seedhi pakki kar lo.

Wadi (Valley)

Matlab ye hai. Do pahari silsilon ke darmiyan wo neechi aur lambi zameen jahan aksar darya behta hai aur log rehte hain, use wadi kehte hain. Wadi paharon ke darmiyan zindagi ki jagah hoti hai.

Pakistan ki misaal ye hai. Hunza Valley, Swat Valley aur Skardu Valley Pakistan ki mashhoor wadiyan hain. Azad Kashmir ki Neelum Valley bhi bohat mashhoor hai.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Har wadi ko us ke darya ke sath yaad rakho. Hunza ke sath Hunza darya, Swat ke sath darya-e-Swat, Neelum ke sath darya-e-Neelum. Sawal aksar wadi aur darya ka jora poochta hai.

Jazira (Island)

Matlab ye hai. Pani ke darmiyan wo zameen jo chaaron taraf se pani se ghiri ho aur mainland se kati hui ho, use jazira kehte hain. Jazira samandar me bhi hota hai aur bari jheel ya darya me bhi.

Pakistan ki misaal ye hai. Astola Jazira Balochistan ke sahel ke qareeb Arabian Sea me hai aur Pakistan ka sab se bara jazira hai. Karachi ke qareeb Bundal aur Manora jazeere hain.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Astola ko Haft Talar bhi kaha jata hai aur ye Pakistan ka sab se bara jazira hai. Jazira suno to Astola aur Gwadar ke aas paas ka sahel zehen me lao.

Sahil (Coast)

Matlab ye hai. Wo kinara jahan zameen khatam aur samandar shuru hota hai, use sahil kehte hain. Sahil par bandargahen, machhli ke ilaqay aur samandari hawa ka asar hota hai.

Pakistan ki misaal ye hai. Pakistan ka sahil takhreeban 1,046 kilometre lamba hai. Is ka aik hissa Sindh me Karachi se Thatta tak hai aur bara hissa Balochistan me Makran ka sahil hai jahan Gwadar hai.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Sahil ki lambai 1,046 kilometre pakki kar lo aur ye bhi ke Pakistan ka sahil sirf do sobon, Sindh aur Balochistan, ke sath lagta hai. Punjab aur Khyber Pakhtunkhwa ka koi sahil nahi.

Aakhri baat

Ab tumhare paas har lafz ki pehchan hai. Aage jab naqshay par koi nishan dekho ge to pehle khud se poochho ge ke ye hai kya, doab hai ya plateau, dam hai ya barrage. Yehi aik sawal aadhe number pakke kar deta hai. Agli files me yehi alfaaz asal jagahon ke sath milenge, aur har jagah ko tum in lafzon se pehchan sako ge.
