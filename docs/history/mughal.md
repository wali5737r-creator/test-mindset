Mughal Saltanat (1526 se 1857)
Teen sau saal se zyada chalne wali wo saltanat jis ke badshahon ki zindagi khud aik dastaan hai

Pehle poori tasveer dekh lo

Panipat ke maidan me jab Babur ki topein garjin to Hindustan me aik nayi saltanat ne janam liya, Mughal Saltanat. 1526 se 1857 tak, teen sau iktees saal. Is me chhe bare badshah aye jinhein tareekh Mughal e Azeem kehti hai: Babur, Humayun, Akbar, Jahangir, Shah Jahan aur Aurangzeb. Phir zawal ka lamba dor aya, aur aakhir me aik budha shair badshah reh gaya jis ke paas Delhi ke Lal Qilay se bahar koi hukumat nahi thi. Har badshah ka kirdar dusre se bilkul mukhtalif hai. Babur jangju aur likhne wala, Humayun khush qismat naam rakh kar bhi badqismat, Akbar nizam banane wala, Jahangir insaaf aur fun ka shaukeen, Shah Jahan imaaraton ka aashiq, aur Aurangzeb sab se taqatwar aur sab se tanha. In chhe kirdaron ko kahani ki tarah samajh lo, Mughal tareekh khud yaad ho jayegi.

Babur (1526 se 1530): Wo badshah jo qalam aur talwaar dono chalata tha

Zahiruddin Muhammad Babur Farghana ki chhoti si riyasat ka shahzada tha. Baap ki taraf se wo Amir Timur ki nasl se tha aur maa ki taraf se Changez Khan ki, is liye us ki saltanat ko Mughal kaha gaya. Us ki zindagi ka pehla hissa thokron ki kahani hai. Samarkand, jo us ke dada ki saltanat ka dil tha, us ne teen dafa fatah kiya aur teen dafa khoya. Aakhir us ne Kabul ko apna markaz banaya aur nazar Hindustan ki daulat par rakhi. Phir wo daawat ayi jo Lodhi sardaron ne di thi, aur Babur Hindustan aa gaya.

21 April 1526 ko Panipat ki pehli jang hui. Ibrahim Lodhi ke paas lakh ke qareeb lashkar tha, Babur ke paas mushkil se pachees tees hazar. Lekin Babur ke paas wo cheez thi jo Hindustan ne pehle kabhi maidan e jang me nahi dekhi thi: topein aur bandoqain. Us ne gaariyon ko zanjeeron se bandh kar un ke peeche topain aur bandoqchi khare kiye, aur tulughma hikmat se ghore sawaar dono taraf se ghoom kar dushman ko ghair lete the. Ustad Ali Quli ki topon ne Lodhi lashkar ke haathiyon aur bheerr ko bikher diya. Ibrahim Lodhi maidan me maara gaya aur Delhi Babur ka ho gaya. Paper ka fix sawal yahin se banta hai: Hindustan me maidan e jang me topon ka pehla istemal Panipat 1526 me Babur ne kiya. Us ke baad Babur ne 1527 me Khanwa ke maqam par Rana Sanga ko hara kar Rajput taqat ko roka, Chanderi aur Ghaghra ki jangon se apni hukumat mazboot ki. Babur ki do pehchan aur yaad rakho. Aik, us ne apni aap beeti Tuzuk e Baburi, jisay Baburnama kehte hain, khud Turki zubaan me likhi, aur ye kitaab apni saaf goi ki wajah se duniya ki mashhoor aap beetiyon me gini jati hai. Dusri, usay Kabul se aisi mohabbat thi ke marne ke baad us ki laash Kabul le ja kar us ke pasandeeda bagh me dafnayi gayi. Babur 1530 me Agra me chal basa, yani us ne Hindustan me sirf chaar saal hukumat ki, lekin un chaar saalon ne teen sau saal ki saltanat ki buniyad rakh di.

Humayun (1530 se 1540, phir 1555 se 1556): Badshah jis ka naam khush qismat, qismat na thi

Babur ka beta Humayun takht par baitha. Naam ka matlab hai khush qismat, lekin tareekh me is se zyada thokarein shayad hi kisi badshah ne khai hon. Wo parha likha, naram dil aur sitara shanaasi ka shaukeen tha, lekin us me baap jaisi sakhti aur fauji samajh nahi thi. Us ka asal dushman Bihar aur Bengal ka Afghan sardar Sher Khan tha, jo aage chal kar Sher Shah Suri bana. 1539 me Chausa aur 1540 me Kannauj ki jangon me Sher Shah ne Humayun ko shikast di, aur badshah apni jaan bacha kar bhaaga. Yahan se us ki pandrah saal ki jalawatni shuru hui. Sindh ke sehra me bhatakte hue Amarkot ke maqam par 1542 me us ke ghar wo beta paida hua jis ka naam Akbar rakha gaya. Humayun Iran gaya, wahan ke Shah Tahmasp se madad li, aur pandrah saal baad 1555 me wo Hindustan wapas aya aur Sirhind ki jang jeet kar Delhi dobara hasil kar liya.

Lekin qismat ne yahan bhi saath na diya. Takht wapas mile hue abhi chhe maah hi hue the ke January 1556 me aik sham wo Delhi me apne kitab khane, Sher Mandal, ki seerhiyon se utar raha tha. Wo sitaaron ka mushahida karke lota tha, azaan ki awaaz ayi to adab se jhukne laga, paon apne choghe me phansa, aur wo seerhiyon se gir gaya. Sar ki chot jaan lewa saabit hui. Us ki behen Gulbadan Begum ne baad me us ki zindagi par Humayun Nama likhi, jo is dor ki sab se qeemti kitaab hai. Paper ke liye Humayun ka khulasa aik jumle me band kar lo: takht Sher Shah se haara, pandrah saal jalawatni, wapas aya, aur kitab khane ki seerhiyon se gir kar wafat pai.

Darmiyan ka mehmaan: Sher Shah Suri (1540 se 1545)

Ab ye baat zehen me saaf kar lo ke Babur se Akbar tak ki kahani me darmiyan me paanch saal aise hain jab Delhi par Mughal nahi, aik Afghan badshah tha, Sher Shah Suri. Aur ye paanch saal itne aham hain ke Akbar ke nizam ki aadhi buniyad Sher Shah ne rakhi thi. Asal naam Farid Khan tha. Kehte hain Bihar me us ne akele nehar, yani sher, ko maar giraya tha, tabhi se wo Sher Khan kahlaya. Us ne pehle Chausa aur Kannauj me Humayun ko haraya aur phir takht par baith kar wo intezami nizam chalaya jo aaj bhi kitaabon me parhaya jata hai.

Us ke karnaame gin lo. Us ne Peshawar se Bengal tak Sadak e Azam banayi, jisay aaj GT Road yani Grand Trunk Road kehte hain. Ye sirf sarak nahi thi, is ke kinare sarayen thein jahan musafir thehar sake, dak ke ghore badle jate the, aur yun poori saltanat me khabron aur dak ka jaal bich gaya. Aaj ki dak ki tarah samjho, bas ghore us ke harakaray the. Us ne chaandi ka rupiya sikka chalaya, aur ye lafz rupiya aaj tak hamari currency ki pehchan hai. Zameen ki paimaish karwa kar kisan ko patta diya aur maal guzari ka wazeh nizam banaya, jo baad me Akbar ne apna liya. Us ne Rohtas ka qila banwaya, Delhi me Purana Qila mukammal kiya, aur Bihar ke Sasaram me aik jheel ke darmiyan apna maqbara banwaya jo apni khubsurti me Taj Mahal ka pesh ro kaha jata hai. Sher Shah 1545 me Kalinjar ke muhasre ke dauraan barood ke dhamake me chal basa. Sirf paanch saal ki hukumat, lekin nizam aisa ke aane wale badshah usi par chalte rahe. Paper me Sher Shah par sawal hamesha us ke nizam par banta hai: GT Road, dak ka nizam, rupiya, aur Sasaram ka maqbara.

Akbar (1556 se 1605): Wo badshah jis ne saltanat ko nizam bana diya

Humayun ki wafat ke waqt us ka beta Akbar sirf terah saal ka tha aur Punjab me apne ataleeq Bairam Khan ke saath tha. Kalanaur ke maqam par us ki tajposhi hui. Us saal Delhi par Hemu ne qabza kar liya tha, jo aik Hindu sardar tha aur apne aap ko badshah samajhta tha. 5 November 1556 ko Panipat ki dusri jang hui. Bairam Khan ki qayadat me Mughal fauj ne Hemu ko hara diya, Hemu ki aankh me teer laga aur jang ka naqsha palat gaya. Yahan se Akbar ka asal dor shuru hua, jo unanchaas saal chala aur Mughal Saltanat ko us ki asal shakal di.

Akbar ki sab se bari kamyabi us ki Rajput policy thi. Us ne samajh liya tha ke Hindustan par hukumat Rajputon ko dushman bana kar nahi, dost bana kar ho sakti hai. Us ne Amber ke Raja Bharmal ki beti se shadi ki, Rajput sardaron ko fauj aur darbar me sab se bare ohde diye, Raja Man Singh us ka sab se bharose ka sipah salar bana. Mazhabi rawadari me wo apne zamaane se boht aage tha. Us ne ghair Muslimon par laga jizya tax khatam kar diya, teerath yatra ka tax maaf kiya, aur us ki policy ko sulh e kul kehte hain, yani sab ke saath aman. Fatehpur Sikri ka sheher us ne basaya, jahan Gujarat ki fatah ki yaad me Buland Darwaza banaya gaya, jo aaj bhi Hindustan ke sab se buland darwazon me hai. Wahi Ibadat Khana bana jahan pehle musalman ulama behes karte the, phir Akbar ne Hindu, Jain, Parsi aur Esai mazahib ke alimon ko bhi bula liya. Isi mahaul me 1582 me Din e Ilahi ka elaan hua, jis par kuch tareekh danon ne sakht tanqeed ki ke ye badshah ki siyasi hikmat thi ya mazhab se doori, aur ye tanqeedi zawiya paper me bhi ata hai.

Intezaam me Akbar ne mansabdari nizam chalaya, jis me har ameer ka darja zaat aur sawaar ki tadaad se tay hota tha. Maal guzari me us ke wazeer Raja Todar Mal ne zabt ka nizam banaya, jis me zameen naapi jati, das saal ki ausat paidawaar par tax tay hota, is liye use dahsaala nizam bhi kehte hain. Us ke darbar ke no mashhoor ratan, jinhein navaratna kehte hain, paper ka fix sawal hain. Birbal apni hazir jawabi ke liye, Todar Mal maal guzari ke liye, Abul Fazl apni kitaab Ain e Akbari aur Akbarnama ke liye, aur Tansen sangeet ke liye sab se zyada poochay jate hain. Un ke ilawa Faizi, Abdur Rahim Khan e Khana, Raja Man Singh, Mulla Do Piyaza aur Faqir Aziao Din bhi is fehrist me ginay jate hain. Akbar ke dor me saltanat Kabul se Bengal aur Kashmir se Deccan tak phaili. 1605 me wo chal basa aur Agra ke paas Sikandra me dafan hua.

Jahangir (1605 se 1627): Insaaf ki zanjeer aur Noor Jahan ka dor

Akbar ka beta Salim, Jahangir ke naam se takht par baitha. Us ne apne dor ke aaghaz me Agra ke qilay se Jamna ke kinare tak insaaf ki zanjeer lagwayi, jis me ghantiyan bandhi thein. Koi bhi mazloom wo zanjeer hila kar badshah tak apni faryaad pohncha sakta tha. Ye zanjeer us ke insaaf pasandi ki alamat ban gayi aur paper me aksar poochi jati hai. Lekin Jahangir ke dor ki asal kahani us ki begum Noor Jahan hai. Us ka asal naam Mehrunnisa tha, aur 1611 me shadi ke baad wo itni taqatwar hui ke sarkari farmano par us ke dastakhat hone lage, sikkon par us ka naam aya, aur shikar aur siyaset dono me us ka koi saani nahi tha. Tareekh dan is dor ko Noor Jahan ke asar ka dor kehte hain.

Jahangir hi ke dor me angrez Hindustan ke darwaze par dastak dene lage. Pehle Captain Hawkins aya, phir 1615 me England ke badshah James Awwal ka safeer Sir Thomas Roe Jahangir ke darbar me pohncha aur tijarat ki ijazat hasil karne me kamyab hua. Yahi wo chhoti si shuruaat thi jo aage chal kar East India Company aur phir angrez hukumat ki surat me nikli, is liye ye saal paper me khaas ahamiyat rakhta hai. Jahangir ne apni aap beeti Tuzuk e Jahangiri khud likhi, aur usay tasveer kashi yani painting se gehra lagao tha, us ke dor me Mughal musawwari apne urooj par thi. 1627 me wo chal basa aur Lahore ke paas Shahdara me dafan hua.

Shah Jahan (1628 se 1658): Imaraton ka badshah aur takht ki jang

Jahangir ka beta Khurram, Shah Jahan ke naam se takht par baitha, aur us ka dor Mughal Saltanat ka sunehra dor kahlata hai, khaas taur par tameeron ki wajah se. Us ki mehboob begum Mumtaz Mahal 1631 me chal basi, aur us ki yaad me Shah Jahan ne Agra me Jamna ke kinare wo imarat banwayi jisay duniya Taj Mahal ke naam se janti hai. Safed sang e marmar ki ye imarat bees hazar karigaron ne baais saal me mukammal ki, aur is ke naqshay ke liye Ustad Ahmad Lahori ka naam sab se mashhoor hai. Isi dor me Delhi ko dobara darul hukumat banaya gaya, wahan Lal Qila aur Hindustan ki sab se bari masjidon me se aik Jama Masjid bani. Aur badshah ke baithne ke liye Takht e Taus, yani mor wala takht, tayyar hua jo apne jawahiraat ki wajah se duniya bhar me mashhoor tha. Ye sab imaaratein paper me Shah Jahan ke naam ke sath bandhi hui hain.

Lekin is chamakte dor ka anjaam khoon se bhara tha. 1657 me Shah Jahan bemar para to us ke chaar beton me takht ki jang chhir gayi. Sab se bara Dara Shikoh baap ka pasandeeda aur waliahd tha, rawadaar mizaaj ka tha aur us ne mazahib ke taqabul par kaam kiya tha. Dusri taraf Aurangzeb tha, tajurba kaar sipahi aur sakht mizaaj. 1658 me Samugarh ki jang me Aurangzeb ne Dara ko shikast di, Dara giraftar hua aur 1659 me use qatal kar diya gaya. Shah Jahan ko Agra ke qilay me nazarband kar diya gaya, jahan se wo Jamna ke paar Taj Mahal ko dekhta rehta tha. Aath saal ki qaid ke baad 1666 me wo chal basa, aur usay usi Taj Mahal me Mumtaz ke pehlu me dafan kiya gaya jisay us ne mohabbat ki nishani banaya tha. Kahani ka ye mod zaroor yaad rakho: takht ki hawas ne baap ko qaid aur bhai ko qatal tak pohncha diya.

Aurangzeb Alamgir (1658 se 1707): Sab se bari saltanat, sab se lamba dor, sab se tanha badshah

Aurangzeb ka dor unanchaas saal chala, jo tamam Mughal badshahon me sab se lamba hai. Us ke dor me Mughal Saltanat apni sab se bari wusat ko pohnchi. Kabul se Bengal aur Kashmir se Deccan ke aakhri hison tak, takreeban poora Barray Sagheer us ke qabze me tha. Lekin ye wusat usay boht mehngi pari. Apni zindagi ke aakhri pachees saal us ne Deccan me guzare. Wahan Bijapur aur Golconda ki saltanatein us ne fatah kar lein, lekin Marathaon ke khilaf jang khatam hone ka naam nahi leti thi. Shivaji ke baad us ke bete Sambhaji ko Aurangzeb ne qatal karwaya, magar Maratha chhapamar jang paharon aur qilon me jaari rahi aur Mughal khazana aur fauj is lambi jang me ghis'te rahe.

Aurangzeb apne dada Akbar se mazhabi policy me bilkul mukhtalif tha. Us ne 1679 me jizya dobara nafiz kiya, jo paper ka boht mashhoor sawal hai. Zaati zindagi me wo apne khandan ka sab se saada aur parhezgar badshah tha. Riwayat hai ke wo apne kharche ke liye khud topiyan seeta aur Quran e Pak ke nuskhe likhta tha, aur sarkari khazane se apne liye kuch nahi leta tha. Us ki wasiyat thi ke usay khuli qabar me dafnaya jaye, aur 1707 me Ahmednagar me wafat ke baad usay Aurangabad ke paas Khuldabad me aik saadi si qabar me dafan kiya gaya. Socho zara, jis badshah ke paas Hindustan ki sab se bari saltanat thi, us ki qabar sab se saadi hai. Lekin us ki sakhtiyon aur lambi Deccan muham ne saltanat ko andar se khokhla kar diya tha. Rajput, Sikh, Jat aur Maratha quwwatein ubhar rahi thein, aur jaise hi us ki aankh band hui, imarat girne lagi.

Zawal aur aakhri badshah: Jab saltanat sirf naam ki reh gayi

Aurangzeb ke baad jo badshah aye, wo saltanat ke wazan ko sambhal hi nahi sakte the. Takht par naam ke badshah baithte rahe aur asal taqat darbar ke ameeron ke haath me chali gayi, aik dor to aisa aya ke do bhai, jinhein Sayyid bandhu kehte hain, badshah banane aur girane wale ban gaye. Soobe aik aik karke azaad hote gaye, Bengal, Awadh aur Hyderabad ke nawab apni apni riyasaton ke malik ban gaye. Maratha taqat Deccan se nikal kar Delhi ke darwaze tak aan pohnchi.

Phir 1739 me Iran ka faateh Nadir Shah Durrani Hindustan par hamlaawar hua. Karnal ki jang me Mughal fauj hari, Delhi me qatal e aam hua, aur Nadir Shah jate hue Takht e Taus aur duniya ka mashhoor heera Koh e Noor apne sath le gaya. Us ke baad Ahmad Shah Abdali ke hamle baar baar hue, aur 1761 me Panipat ki teesri jang me us ne Marathaon ko shikast di, lekin is sab ka faida uthaya teesri taqat ne, angrezon ne. 1757 me Plassey aur 1764 me Buxar ki jangon ke baad asal taqat East India Company ke haath me chali gayi aur Mughal badshah un ki tankhwah par guzara karne wala naam ka badshah reh gaya.

Aakhri badshah Bahadur Shah Zafar tha, jo 1837 me takht par baitha. Wo badshah kam aur shair zyada tha, Zafar us ka takhallus tha. Us ki hukumat Lal Qilay ki chaar deewari tak mehdood thi. 1857 me jab angrezon ke khilaf Jang e Azaadi bhadki to sipahiyon ne usay apna rehnuma bana liya. Jang nakam hui, angrezon ne Delhi dobara fatah kiya, Zafar ke bete aur pote sar e aam qatal kiye gaye, aur budhe badshah ko muqadma chala kar Burma ke sheher Rangoon jalawatan kar diya gaya. 1862 me wahi us ki wafat hui aur wahi wo dafan hai. Us ka mashhoor sher is poori kahani ka khulasa hai: lagta nahi hai dil mera ujre dayar me. Teen sau saal pehle jo saltanat Babur ki topon se shuru hui thi, wo aik shair badshah ke aansuon par khatam hui.

Aakhir me aik saans me poori Mughal tartib dohra lo. Babur ne Panipat me topon se saltanat ki buniyad rakhi. Humayun takht kho kar pandrah saal baad wapas laya, aur darmiyan me Sher Shah Suri ne wo nizam banaya jis par baad ki saltanatein chalin. Akbar ne Rajput dosti, mansabdari aur sulh e kul se saltanat ko imarat banaya. Jahangir ke dor me Noor Jahan ka asar raha aur angrez darwaze par aye. Shah Jahan ne Taj Mahal se dor ko chamkaya lekin takht ki jang me qaid hua. Aurangzeb ne saltanat sab se bari ki lekin Deccan ki lambi jang ne usay khokhla kar diya. Phir kamzor badshah, Nadir Shah aur Abdali ke hamle, angrezon ki taqat, aur aakhir me Bahadur Shah Zafar Rangoon ki qabar. Ye tartib aur har badshah ki aik pehchan yaad kar lo, Mughal tareekh ka paper tum se kabhi nahi uljhega.
