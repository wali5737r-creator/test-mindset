Delhi Sultanate: Paanch Khandan
Teen sau bees saal, paanch khandan, battis sultan, aur har takht ke peeche aik kahani

Pehle poori tasveer dekh lo

Dekho, jab bhi Delhi Sultanate ka naam aye to dimagh me aik lambi film samjho. Saal 1206 se saal 1526 tak, pure teen sau bees saal, Delhi ke takht par paanch khandan baithay aur kul battis sultan aye aur gaye. Koi ghulam se uth kar badshah bana, koi aurat takht par baithi aur poora darbar us ke khilaf ho gaya, kisi ne bazaar ke daam bandh diye, kisi ne poora darul hukumat hi utha kar saikron meel door le ja kar phaink diya. Ye sirf tareekhon ki list nahi hai, ye kirdaron ki kahani hai, aur paper me sawal hamesha inhi kirdaron par banta hai.

Tartib yaad rakhne ki trick pehle zehen me bitha lo. Apni paanch ungliyan dekho. Angutha Ghulaman, us ke baad Khilji, phir Tughlaq, phir Sayyid, aur sab se chhoti ungli par Lodhi. Ghulam, Khilji, Tughlaq, Sayyid, Lodhi. Bas yehi paanch lafz tartib se yaad kar lo, kyunke paper ka pehla phanda yahi hota hai ke konsa khandan pehle aya aur konsa baad me. Aur aik baat aur. Har khandan ke sath us ka dor zehen me jor lo. Ghulaman 1206 se 1290, Khilji 1290 se 1320, Tughlaq 1320 se 1414, Sayyid 1414 se 1451, aur Lodhi 1451 se 1526. Ab aik aik karke andar chalte hain.

Pehla khandan: Khandan e Ghulaman (1206 se 1290)

Is khandan ko Mamluk khandan bhi kehte hain, aur naam hi is ki pehchan hai. Is ke pehle teen sultan kabhi ghulam reh chuke the. Yani takht par wo log baithay jo kabhi bazaar me bikte the. Tareekh me aisa kam hota hai, is liye ye khandan paper ka pasandeeda hai.

Qutbuddin Aibak (1206 se 1210). Ye Muhammad Ghauri ka ghulam tha, lekin ghulam aisa ke maalik ne use apna sab se bharose ka sipah salar bana diya tha. Ghauri ki wafat ke baad 1206 me Aibak Delhi aur Lahore ke ilaqe ka hukmaran bana, aur yahin se Sultanate ki buniyad pari. Us ka dor sirf chaar saal ka tha, lekin usi chaar saal me us ne wo kaam shuru kiya jo aaj tak khara hai. Delhi me Qutb Minar ki buniyad usi ne rakhi, pehli manzil usi ke dor me bani, baqi kaam Iltutmish ne pura kiya. Aibak apni sakhaawat ki wajah se Lakh Bakhsh kahlaya, yani lakhon dene wala. Kehte hain us ke dar se koi khaali haath nahi lota. Aur us ki maut bhi ajeeb thi. 1210 me Lahore me chaugan, yani polo, khelte hue wo ghore se gira aur chal basa. Lahore hi me us ki qabar hai. Paper me Aibak par teen sawal ghoomte hain: pehla sultan kon tha, Qutb Minar ki buniyad kis ne rakhi, aur Lakh Bakhsh kis ka laqab tha. Teeno ke jawab me Aibak hai.

Shamsuddin Iltutmish (1211 se 1236). Aibak ke baad us ka ghulam aur damad Iltutmish takht par baitha, aur tareekh dan isay Delhi Sultanate ka asal baani mante hain. Aibak ne buniyad rakhi thi, Iltutmish ne imarat khari ki. Samjho kyun. Us ne baghi sardaron ko kuchla, Mongolon ke toofaan se saltanat ko bachaya, jab Changez Khan aik bhagore shahzade ka peecha karta hua Sindh tak aa gaya to Iltutmish ne baree aqalmandi se us bhagore ko panah dene se inkaar kar diya, aur toofaan tal gaya. Us ne iqta ka nizam chalaya, yani bare bare ilaqe apne afsaron ko is shart par diye ke wo wahan se maal guzari jama karein aur fauj rakhein. Us ne chaandi ka tanka aur taanbe ka jital sikka chalaya, aur ye sikke sadiyon tak Hindustan ki pehchan rahe. Baghdad ke Khalifa se us ne apni hukumat ki sanad, yani manzoori, hasil ki, jis se us ki takht ko mazhabi aur siyasi taqat mili. Qutb Minar ko usi ne mukammal kiya. Aur sab se bari baat, marne se pehle us ne apne beton ko na-ahal samajh kar apni beti Razia ko janashin naamzad kiya. Ye faisla us zamaane ke lihaz se inqilabi tha.

Razia Sultana (1236 se 1240). Hindustan ki tareekh me wo wahid musalman aurat hai jo Delhi ke takht par baithi. Aur ye koi naam ki hukmaran nahi thi. Razia mardon ke libaas me darbar karti, fauj ki qayadat khud karti, ghore par sawaar ho kar jang me jati. Us ki qabiliyat par kisi ko shak nahi tha. Phir zawal kyun aya? Wajah us ki qabiliyat nahi, darbar ki siyaset thi. Turk sardaron ka garoh, jisay Chahalgani yani chaalis ka garoh kehte the, ye bardasht hi nahi kar saka ke aik aurat un par hukmaran ho aur wo ghulam zaade khud ko asl malik samajhte hon. Phir Razia ne aik Habshi ghulam Jamaluddin Yaqut ko bara ohda diya to aag aur bharak uthi. Bhatinda ke governor Altunia ne baghawat ki, Razia haraai gayi, phir us ne Altunia se shadi karke takht wapas lene ki koshish ki, lekin 1240 me Kaithal ke paas wo mari gayi. Paper me Razia par sawal fix hai: Hindustan ki pehli aur akeli musalman aurat hukmaran kon thi, aur us ke zawal ki wajah kya thi. Jawab yaad rakho, wajah na-ahliyat nahi, ameeron ki baghawat thi.

Ghiyasuddin Balban (1266 se 1287). Razia ke baad kamzor sultanon ka lamba silsila aya, phir takht par wo shakhs baitha jis ka naam sun kar darbar kaanpta tha. Balban khud kabhi ghulam tha, Chahalgani ka rukun tha, aur phir usi garoh ko us ne aik aik karke khatam kar diya taake koi takht ko challenge na kar sake. Us ki policy ko tareekh khoon aur lohe ki policy kehti hai, yani baghawat ho to koi riayat nahi. Us ne badshah ke rutbe ko aam insan se boht buland kar diya. Darbar me sijda, yani badshah ke aage sajda karna, aur paibos, yani badshah ke paon chumna, usi ne lazmi kiya. Us ka nazariya tha ke badshah zameen par Khuda ka saaya hai. Jasoosi ka itna mazboot jaal bichhaya ke har soobe ki khabar us tak pohnchti thi. Us ke dor ka sab se bara khatra Mongol the, jo baar baar sarhad par hamla karte the. Balban ka sab se pyara beta, Shahzada Muhammad, 1285 me Mongolon se larte hue shaheed ho gaya. Kehte hain is gham ne budhe badshah ko tod diya, aur do saal baad 1287 me wo chal basa. Paper me Balban ke teen nukaat sab se zyada dohraye jate hain: sijda o paibos, khoon aur lohe ki policy, aur Mongolon ka muqabla.

Dusra khandan: Khilji (1290 se 1320)

Ye khandan sirf tees saal raha, lekin in tees saalon me Delhi Sultanate apni taqat ke urooj par pohnch gayi. Wajah aik hi shakhs tha, Alauddin Khilji.

Jalaluddin Khilji (1290 se 1296). Khandan ka baani aik sattar saal ka budha tha, naram mizaaj, sab ko maaf kar dene wala. Usi neeche us ka bhatija aur damad Alauddin Kara ka governor tha, jo khamoshi se takht ki taraf barh raha tha. Devagiri ki fatah se Alauddin ne itni daulat jama ki ke fauj us ke peeche chal pari. 1296 me Jalaluddin apne bhatije se milne Kara gaya, aur wahan jis garam joshi se wo aage barha, wahin usay qatal kar diya gaya. Ye Sultanate ki tareekh ka wo moor hai jahan mohabbat aur takht ki hawas aamne saamne aati hai, aur hawas jeet jati hai.

Alauddin Khilji (1296 se 1316). Ab suno is badshah ko gaur se, kyunke one paper ka sab se bara hissa isi par hai. Alauddin woh pehla sultan tha jis ne poora Hindustan fatah karne ka khwab dekha aur takreeban pura bhi kiya. Pehle Mongolon ka masla samjho. Us ke dor me Mongol baar baar hamlaawar hue, aik dafa to Delhi ke darwazon tak aa gaye. Alauddin ne sarhad par qilay mazboot kiye, bari musta'id fauj khari ki, aur Mongolon ko aisi shikastein dein ke unhon ne Hindustan ka rukh karna chor diya. Phir futoohat ka silsila chala. Gujarat fatah hua, Ranthambore ka qila toota, 1303 me Chittor fatah hua jis ke sath Rani Padmini ki kahani juri hui hai. Aur phir us ka sipah salar Malik Kafur janub ki taraf gaya aur Devagiri, Warangal, Dwarasamudra aur Madurai tak Muslim faujon ki ye pehli bari pesh qadmi thi. Yani Alauddin ke dor me saltanat Kashmir ki sarhad se lekar Hindustan ke aakhri janubi kinare tak phail gayi.

Lekin Alauddin sirf jangju nahi tha, wo Sultanate ka sab se bara muntazim tha. Us ne bazaar control ka nizam chalaya. Ghalla, kapra, ghore, har cheez ke daam sarkar ne bandh diye. Is ke liye alag mehakma bana, Diwan e Riyasat, aur bazaar ka nigehbaan Shahna e Mandi muqarrar hua. Is nizam ki asal wajah tareekh dan ye batate hain ke kam tankhwah par bari fauj rakhni thi, aur fauj tabhi kam tankhwah par guzara kar sakti thi jab bazaar sasta ho. Us ne fauj me daagh ka nizam chalaya, yani sarkari ghoron par nishan lagaya jata taake sipahi apna kamzor ghora pesh karke dhoka na de, aur huliya nizam me har sipahi ki shakal o surat register me likhi jati. Zameen ki paimaish karwa kar us ne maal guzari ka nizam sakhti se nafiz kiya. Ye teen nukaat, bazaar control, daagh o huliya, aur zameen ki paimaish, paper me Alauddin ke naam ke sath bandhe hue hain, inhein kabhi na bhoolna. Alauddin 1316 me chal basa, aur us ke baad Khilji khandan sirf chaar saal me bikhar gaya.

Teesra khandan: Tughlaq (1320 se 1414)

Tughlaq khandan sab se lamba chala, takreeban chauranwe saal, aur is me Sultanate ke sab se ajeeb kirdar aye. Aik wo sultan jis ne nehrein khudwayein aur sheher basaye, aur aik wo jisay tareekh sab se zyada parha likha aur sab se zyada nakam tajurba kar badshah kehti hai.

Ghiyasuddin Tughlaq (1320 se 1325). Khandan ka baani Ghazi Malik ke naam se mashhoor tha, aik sipahi se uth kar sultan bana. Us ne Delhi ke paas Tughlaqabad ka mazboot qila sheher basaya. 1325 me Bengal ki muham se wapsi par Afghanpur ke maqam par us ke isteqbaal ke liye banaya gaya lakri ka mehal gir gaya aur sultan us ke neeche dab kar chal basa. Kuch tareekh danon ne ye shaq bhi zahir kiya ke ye haadsa nahi, saazish thi, aur ungli us ke bete Muhammad bin Tughlaq ki taraf uthi. Paper ke liye bas itna yaad rakho ke ye waqia aaj tak sawal bana hua hai.

Muhammad bin Tughlaq (1325 se 1351). Ab is kirdar ko khaas dhyaan se parho. Ye sultan mantiq, falsafa, tib, falakiyat aur khush khati me apne zamaane ke ulama se aage tha. Lekin us ke tajurbe aik aik karke ultay pare, aur har tajurbe ki kahani paper me alag sawal banti hai. Pehla tajurba, darul hukumat ki muntaqili. Us ne faisla kiya ke Delhi ke bajaye Devagiri ko, jis ka naam us ne Daulatabad rakha, darul hukumat banaya jaye, kyunke wo saltanat ke darmiyan me tha aur Mongolon ki zad se door tha. Soch apni jagah durust thi, lekin amal ne awaam ko rulaya. Darbar, daftar aur Delhi ke log saikron meel door janub bheje gaye, raaste me takleefon ka samna hua, phir jab tajurba nakam hua to sab ko wapas Delhi ana para. Dusra tajurba, taanbe ke sikke. Us ne taanbe aur peetal ke sikke ko chaandi ke tanke ke barabar qeemat par chalaya, jisay aaj ki zabaan me token currency kehte hain. Khayal bura nahi tha, lekin sarkar ne sikka banane par apni ijara daari qaim na rakhi. Nateeja ye hua ke har ghar taksaal ban gaya, jaali sikke bazaar me bhar gaye, tijarat thup ho gayi, aur sultan ko sikke wapas lekar khazane se asal chaandi deni pari. Teesra tajurba, Doab me tax. Ganga aur Jamna ke darmiyani zarkhaiz ilaqe me us ne maal guzari barhai, lekin badqismati se unhi saalon me qehat para. Kisan tax na de sake, baghawatein phoot parin, aur sakhti ne aag ko aur hawa di. In tajurbon ke baad tareekh dan Barani ne wo mashhoor jumla likha ke sultan ko awaam se aur awaam ko sultan se nijaat mil gayi. Muhammad bin Tughlaq 1351 me Thatta ke paas baghiyon ka peecha karte hue chal basa.

Firoz Shah Tughlaq (1351 se 1388). Muhammad bin Tughlaq ka chacha zaad bhai takht par baitha to lehja hi badal gaya. Ye jangon ka nahi, falahi kaamon ka badshah tha. Us ne bari nehrein khudwayein jin se khushk ilaqon me kheti shuru hui, Jamna se Hisar tak ki neher us ki pehchan hai. Us ne naye sheher basaye, Delhi me Firozabad, phir Hisar, Fatehabad aur Jaunpur, jo us ne apne marhoom sultan Jauna Khan yani Muhammad bin Tughlaq ki yaad me basaya tha. Gharibon ki madad ke liye Diwan e Khairat banaya, bemaaron ke muft ilaaj ke liye shafa khane khole, berozgaron ko kaam dilane ka daftar banaya. Ashoka ke do pathar ke satoon wo boht ehtiyat se Delhi mangwa kar le gaya, jinhein dekh kar wo hairan hota tha ke ye banaye kis ne honge. Lekin us ke naram nizam ne fauj aur intezaam ko dheela bhi kiya, aur us ki wafat ke baad saltanat tezi se kamzor hoti gayi.

Timur ka hamla (1398). Firoz Shah ke baad takht par kamzor naamen beth'te rahe, aur tabhi 1398 me Samarkand ka faateh Amir Timur Hindustan par toofaan ban kar toota. Us waqt naam ke sultan Nasiruddin Mahmud Tughlaq the. Delhi fatah hua, sheher me qatal e aam hua, aur Timur daulat, karigar aur fankaar samait kar wapas chala gaya. Is hamle ne Delhi Sultanate ki kamar tod di. Khandan ka naam 1414 tak chalta raha, lekin taqat kahin pehle khatam ho chuki thi.

Chautha khandan: Sayyid (1414 se 1451)

Ye sab se chhota aur sab se kamzor dor hai, is liye isay mukhtasar yaad rakho. Khandan ka baani Khizr Khan tha, jo Timur ka muqarrar karda tha. Us ne apne aap ko badshah kahlane ki bajaye Rayat e Aala ka laqab ikhtiyar kiya aur naam ke tor par Timur ke khandan ki hukumat ka numainda bana raha. Is dor me saltanat Delhi aur us ke aas paas ke ilaqon tak simat gayi, soobe aik aik karke azaad ho gaye. Aakhri Sayyid hukmaran Alam Shah itna be-bas tha ke 1451 me wo takht chor kar Badaun chala gaya aur apni baqi zindagi wahi guzari. Paper me Sayyid khandan se bas yahi do sawal bante hain, baani kon tha aur dor itna kamzor kyun tha.

Paanchwa khandan: Lodhi (1451 se 1526)

Lodhi Delhi Sultanate ka aakhri khandan tha, aur ye pehla khandan tha jo naslan Afghan yani Pathan tha. Is farq ko samajhna zaroori hai, kyunke Lodhi sultanon ka andaz pehle sultanon se alag tha. Afghan qabaili rawaiye me sardar khud ko badshah ka barabar samajhta tha, aur yahi rawaiya aage chal kar is khandan ke zawal ki wajah bana.

Bahlol Lodhi (1451 se 1489). Khandan ka baani aik tajir zaade se sultan bana. Us ne Jaunpur ko fatah karke saltanat ko dobara wusat di. Lekin us ki asal pehchan us ka rawaiya tha. Wo apne Afghan sardaron ko bhaiyon ki tarah rakhta, un ke sath aik hi qaleen par baithta, aur takht par akela uncha baithne ko apni shaan nahi samajhta tha. Isi rawaiye ne Afghanon ko us ke gird jama rakha.

Sikandar Lodhi (1489 se 1517). Bahlol ka beta is khandan ka sab se qabil sultan tha. 1504 me us ne Agra sheher ki buniyad rakhi aur phir use apna darul hukumat banaya, ye wahi Agra hai jo aage chal kar Mughalon ka garh bana. Zameen ki paimaish ke liye us ne gaz e Sikandari ka paimana chalaya jo sadiyon tak chalta raha. Wo sakht muntazim tha, bazaar ke daam par nazar rakhta, jasusi ka jaal rakhta, aur khud Gulrukhi ke takhallus se shairi bhi karta tha. Lekin aik baat me wo apne baap se mukhtalif tha, wo Afghan sardaron ko barabar ka darja dene ke bajaye unhein darbar ke aadab ka paaband banane laga, aur yahin se takrao ki buniyad pari.

Ibrahim Lodhi (1517 se 1526) aur Panipat. Sikandar ka beta takht par baitha to us ne wo jumla kaha jo us ki tabahi ka sabab bana, ke badshahat me koi rishtedari nahi hoti. Us ne purane Afghan sardaron ko zaleel kiya, unhein darbar me khara rakha, kuch ko qaid kiya. Naraz sardaron ne wo qadam uthaya jis ne tareekh badal di. Punjab ke governor Daulat Khan Lodhi aur Ibrahim ke chacha Alam Khan ne Kabul ke hukmaran Babur ko Hindustan par hamle ki daawat de di. 21 April 1526 ko Panipat ke maidan me pehli jang hui. Babur ki fauj taadad me kam thi, lekin us ke paas topein aur bandoqain thein aur us ki jangi hikmat, tulughma, Ibrahim ke bare lashkar par bhari pari. Ibrahim Lodhi maidan e jang me maara gaya. Kehte hain wo Delhi ka wahid sultan tha jo takht bachate hue maidan me kaam aya. Usi din Delhi Sultanate ke teen sau bees saal khatam hue, aur Hindustan me aik nayi saltanat ka pehla din shuru hua, Mughal Saltanat.

Aakhir me aik saans me poora khulasa zehen me dohra lo. Ghulaman ne buniyad rakhi, Aibak ne takht sambhala, Iltutmish ne saltanat banayi, Razia ne tareekh rachi, Balban ne khauf se nizam chalaya. Khiljiyon me Alauddin ne saltanat ko urooj diya, futoohat bhi, bazaar control bhi. Tughlaqon me Muhammad bin Tughlaq ke tajurbe ultay pare, Firoz Shah ne nahrein aur sheher diye, aur Timur ne aakar kamar tod di. Sayyid naam ke hukmaran rahe, aur Lodhi Afghanon ke baad Ibrahim ki ana ne Panipat me sab kuch khatam kar diya. Ye tartib aur ye kirdar yaad kar lo, Sultanate ka paper tumhare liye khul jayega.
