History: Pakistan ki Jangain aur Do Aalmai Jangain (1947 se 2026 tak)
Azadi ke baad ki jangain, un ki wajahain, aur duniya ko badal dene wale do aalmai takrao

Ye hisa kyun zaroori hai

One paper me jahan purani jangain poochi jati hain, wahan Pakistan ki apni jangain us se bhi zyada poochi jati hain. 1965 ki jang kitne din chali, Tashkent muahida kab hua, 1971 me Simla muahida kin ke darmiyan hua, Kargil me Nishan-e-Haider kis kis ko mila, ye sawalat takreeban har paper me ghum phir kar ate hain. Aur sath me do aalmai jangain bhi, kyunke UNO aur League of Nations jaisi tanzimon ki buniyad inhi jangon se juri hai. Is hisse me hum pehle Pakistan ki jangain kahani ki tarah parhenge, phir do aalmai jangain. Har jang me wahi tartib hogi jo tumhe aadat ho gayi hai: wajah, maidan, salar, faisla kun lamha, nateeja, aur paper wali khaas baat.

Pehli jang, 1947 se 1948. Kashmir se shuru hone wali kahani

Pakistan bane hue abhi do maah hi hue the ke wo masla khara ho gaya jo aj tak hal nahi hua, Kashmir ka masla. Pehle ye samajh lo ke masla bana kesay. Taqseem ke waqt riyasaton ko ikhtiyar diya gaya tha ke wo Pakistan ya Bharat me se kisi aik ke sath ilhaq kar lain. Jammu-o-Kashmir aik musalman aksariyat wali riyasat thi, lekin wahan ka hukmaran Maharaja Hari Singh hindu tha. Wo dono se alag rehne ki koshish me waqt guzaar raha tha, aur riyasat ke musalman apne haq ke liye uth khare hue the.

October 1947 me sarhad par qabaili logon ne Kashmir par hamla kiya aur wo teezi se Srinagar ki taraf barhne lage. Is par Maharaja ne Bharat se madad mangi, aur Bharat ne shart rakhi ke pehle ilhaq par dastakhat karo. Maharaja ne Bharat ke sath ilhaq ki dastaveez par dastakhat kar diye, aur is ke sath hi Bharti fouj hawai jahazon ke zariye Srinagar pohnch gayi. Yahan se Pakistan aur Bharat ki foujain pehli dafa amne saamne aa gayin. Larai pahari ilaqon me phail gayi, kabhi aik taraf aage barhti, kabhi dusri.

Ye jang kisi faisla kun jeet ke baghair ruki. Bharat masla UNO le gaya, UNO ne qarardadain pass ki ke jung bandi ho aur phir rai shumari, yani plebiscite ke zariye Kashmir ke logon se un ki marzi poochi jaye. 1 January 1949 ko jung bandi ho gayi aur aik ceasefire line kheench di gayi, jis ke aik taraf Pakistan ke zair-e-intezam Azad Kashmir aur Gilgit-Baltistan aye aur dusri taraf Bharat ke zair-e-intezam ilaqa. Yaad rakho, baad me jo line LoC kehlayi, us ki buniyad yehi ceasefire line hai.

Paper wali khaas baat: pehli jang 1947 se 1948, wajah Kashmir ka ilhaq, jung bandi 1 January 1949, aur UNO ki qarardadain rai shumari ke baare me. Ye chaar nukte aik sath yaad kar lo.

Tasveer ka khayal: Kashmir ke pahari naqshe par ceasefire line laal lakeer me wazeh dikhayi jaye, Srinagar aur Muzaffarabad nishan zad hon, aur dono taraf ke ilaqe alag rangon me hon taake student ko line ki jagah zehen me beth jaye.

1965 ki jang. 6 September ka din

Ab wo jang jo har Pakistani bache ko school se yaad hoti hai. 1965 ki jang ka pas-e-manzar bhi Kashmir hi tha. April 1965 me Rann of Kutch ke ilaqe me aik chhoti jhadap ho chuki thi. Phir August 1965 me Operation Gibraltar ke tehat Kashmir ke andar karwai ki koshish hui, aur us ke baad Operation Grand Slam ke tehat Pakistani fouj Chamb aur Jaurian ke ilaqe me aage barhi aur Akhnoor ki taraf rukh kiya, jo Bharat ke liye aik aham raasta tha.

Phir 6 September 1965 ki subah wo hua jis ne is jang ko tareekh bana diya. Bharat ne been-ul-aqwami sarhad paar karke Lahore ki taraf teen taraf se hamla kar diya, aur ye hamla baghair kisi baqaida elaan-e-jang ke tha. Isi liye 6 September ko Pakistan me Youm-e-Difa, Defence Day, manaya jata hai, ke ye wo din hai jab dushman ne achanak hamla kiya aur poori qoum fouj ke sath khari ho gayi. Lahore ko bachane me BRB nehar, yani Bambawali-Ravi-Bedian canal, aik mazboot difai lakeer sabit hui. Wahga aur Burki ke mahaz par sakht muqablay hue.

Is jang ke do maidan hamesha yaad rakho. Pehla, Chawinda ka mahaz Sialkot ke qareeb, jahan tankon ki wo azeem larai hui jo dusri aalmai jang ke baad tankon ki sab se bari jangon me gini jati hai. Sainkron tank amne saamne the aur Pakistani fouj ne dushman ki paish qadmi wahi rok di. Dusra, Lahore ka mahaz, jahan Major Raja Aziz Bhatti ne Burki ke qareeb BRB nehar ke kinare paanch din tak apni company ke sath muqabla kiya aur 12 September ko shaheed hue. Unhe Nishan-e-Haider diya gaya, jo Pakistan ka sab se bara fouji aizaz hai. Isi jang me fazaon me M. M. Alam ka wo karnama bhi mashhoor hai jis me unhon ne aik minute se kam waqt me paanch Bharti jahaaz giraye, jo aik aalami record mana jata hai.

Jang kul sattara din chali. UNO ki mudakhlat aur aalami dabao par 23 September 1965 ko jung bandi ho gayi. Phir January 1966 me Soviet Union ki mezbani me Tashkent ka muahida hua, jahan Pakistan ke Sadr Ayub Khan aur Bharat ke Wazir-e-Azam Lal Bahadur Shastri ne dastakhat kiye aur dono foujain jang se pehle wali positionon par wapas chali gayin. Aik talkh baat ye bhi tareekh ka hissa hai ke Shastri ka inteqal Tashkent me hi muahide ke agle din dil ke dauray se ho gaya.

Paper wali khaas baatain jama kar lo: hamla 6 September ko Lahore par hua, jang 17 din chali, tankon ki azeem jang Chawinda me hui, Aziz Bhatti ko Nishan-e-Haider mila, aur Tashkent muahida 1966 me Ayub Khan aur Shastri ke darmiyan hua.

Tasveer ka khayal: Naqshe par Lahore aur Sialkot ke mahaz teeron se dikhaye jayen, BRB nehar neeli lakeer me wazeh ho, Chawinda par tankon ke nishan hon, aur 6 September ki tareekh sab se numaya likhi ho.

1971 ki jang. Mashriqi Pakistan ka alag hona

Ye Pakistan ki tareekh ka sab se talkh bab hai, aur paper me ye sawal bhi utni hi sanjeedgi se poocha jata hai. Pakistan ke do hisay the, Mashriqi Pakistan aur Maghribi Pakistan, jin ke darmiyan aik hazaar meel se zyada Bharti ilaqa tha. 1970 ke aam intekhabat me Mashriqi Pakistan ki jamat Awami League ko aksariyat mili, lekin iqtidar ki muntakli par ikhtilaf is had tak barha ke mamla talkhi aur phir fouji karwai tak pohnch gaya. Lakhon log ghar chor kar sarhad paar chale gaye, aur Bharat ne isi surat-e-haal me mudakhlat ka mauqa dekha.

December 1971 me baqaida jang chir gayi. Bharat ne mashriqi mahaz par har taraf se paish qadmi ki aur us ki fouj Dhaka ki taraf barhne lagi, jab ke maghribi mahaz par bhi sakht jhadapen hui. Mashriq me ghera tang hota gaya, aur aakhir me 16 December 1971 ko Dhaka me Pakistani fouj ke mashriqi kamaan ke sarbarah ne hathyar daalne ki dastaveez par dastakhat kar diye. Is ke sath Mashriqi Pakistan alag ho kar Bangladesh ban gaya. Is jang me takreeban tiranway hazaar, yani 93,000 Pakistani fouji aur shehri Bharti qaid me chale gaye, jo dusri aalmai jang ke baad qaidioun ki sab se bari tadaad mani jati hai. Ye tadaad paper me bohat poochi jati hai.

Is ke baad July 1972 me Simla ka muahida hua, jahan Pakistan ke Sadr Zulfikar Ali Bhutto aur Bharat ki Wazir-e-Azam Indira Gandhi ne dastakhat kiye. Isi muahide me tay paya ke dono mulk apne masail do tarafah guftagu se hal karenge, aur Kashmir me jung bandi wali lakeer ko naya naam diya gaya, Line of Control, yani LoC. Gaur karo, 1949 wali ceasefire line hi 1972 me LoC kehlayi. Qaidion ki wapsi bhi batadreej isi muahide ke baad hui.

Aur aik wazahat jo owner ki hidayat par yahan pakki kar lo: Pakistan aur Bharat ke darmiyan aakhri bari aur mukammal jang 1971 ki thi. Us ke baad jo hua, wo Kargil 1999 ki mahdood jang thi, jo sirf LoC ke aik makhsoos ilaqe tak mehdood rahi. Ye farq paper me aksar trap ban kar ata hai, is liye isay alag se zehen me rakho.

Paper wali khaas baatain: 16 December 1971 ko Dhaka me hathyar daale gaye, 93,000 qaidi, Simla muahida 1972 me Bhutto aur Indira Gandhi ke darmiyan, aur LoC ka naam isi muahide se bana.

Tasveer ka khayal: 1971 se pehle ke Pakistan ka naqsha dikhaya jaye jis me dono hisay alag rang me hon aur darmiyan me Bharat wazeh ho, Dhaka par 16 December ka nishan ho, taake faasle wali buniyadi kamzori aik nazar me samajh aa jaye.

Siachen, 1984. Duniya ka sab se uncha maidan-e-jang

Ab aik aisi jang jo kisi sheher ya maidan me nahi, barf ke paharon me lari jati hai. Siachen aik glacier hai jo Karakoram ke pahari silsile me waqay hai aur us ki bulandi athara hazaar feet se zyada hai, jahan sardion me darja hararat manfi pachaas tak gir jata hai. 1984 me Bharat ne is ilaqe par qabza karne ke liye fouj utaari, aur jawab me Pakistan ne bhi apni positionain sambhal lein. Tab se dono mulkon ki foujain duniya ke sab se unche maidan-e-jang me amne saamne hain, jahan dushman se zyada khatra mausam aur bulandi se hota hai.

Paper me Siachen par sawal aye to ye do baatain pakki rakho: ye 1984 me shuru hua, aur ye duniya ka sab se uncha maidan-e-jang hai. Ye koi mukammal jang nahi, aik barfani mahaz hai jo aaj tak qayam hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Barf se dhake unche paharon ki tasveer ho, glacier ki safed wadi wazeh ho, aur dono taraf ki unchi chotion par chhote chhote fouji chowki wale nishan hon, sath me bulandi ka paimana dikhaya jaye.

Kargil ki jang, 1999. LoC ke paar mahdood takrao

1999 ki kahani aik ajab pas-e-manzar se shuru hoti hai. February 1999 me Bharat ke Wazir-e-Azam Atal Bihari Vajpayee bus par sawaar ho kar Lahore aye the aur Nawaz Sharif se dostana mulaqat hui thi, Lahore elaan jari hua tha. Kuch hi maheenon baad, May 1999 me, khabar ayi ke Kargil aur Dras ke ilaqe me LoC ke paar unchi chotion par kuch log positionain liye hue hain. Barf pighalne ke baad jab Bharti fouj apni sardion me khali ki hui chowkion par wapas gayi to muqabla shuru ho gaya.

Ye muqabla LoC ke un unche paharon me hua jahan saans lena bhi mushkil tha. Pakistani moqif ye raha ke ye Kashmir ke mujahedeen ki karwai hai, jab ke Bharat ne isay baqaida darandazi qarar diya. Larai ki sab se mashhoor kahani Tiger Hill ki hai, wo unchi choti jis par qabza is poore muqable ki pehchan ban gaya. Isi jang me do Pakistani sipahiyon ne wo bahaduri dikhayi jis par unhe Nishan-e-Haider mila. Captain Karnal Sher Khan ne dushman ke muqable me akhri saans tak position sambhali, aur un ki bahaduri ki tareef khud mukhalif taraf se bhi hui. Havildar Lalak Jan ne bhi apni chowki ka difa karte hue shahadat pai. Dono ke naam Nishan-e-Haider ke sath paper me lazmi pooche jate hain.

Ye jang aalami dabao ke baad khatam hui. July 1999 me Wazir-e-Azam Nawaz Sharif America gaye, President Clinton se mulaqat hui, aur Washington elaan ke baad wapsi ka amal shuru ho gaya. Kuch hi arse me mahaz khamosh ho gaya.

Paper wali khaas baatain: Kargil 1999 me LoC par hua, ye aik mahdood jang thi, Tiger Hill mashhoor choti hai, Karnal Sher Khan aur Lalak Jan ko Nishan-e-Haider mila, aur ye jang Lahore bus yatra aur Lahore elaan ke kuch maah baad hui. Aur dobara sun lo, aakhri mukammal jang 1971 thi, Kargil us ke baad ki mahdood jang hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Kargil ke unche khushk paharon ki tasveer ho, LoC lakeer ki surat me dikhayi jaye, Tiger Hill sab se numaya choti ke tor par nishan zad ho, aur dono Nishan-e-Haider pane walon ke naam aik alag khane me likhe hon.

Pehli Aalmai Jang, 1914 se 1918

Ab Pakistan se nikal kar duniya ki taraf chalo, kyunke one paper me aalmai jangain bhi poochi jati hain. Pehli Aalmai Jang ki wajahain barson se jama ho rahi thi. Europe ki bari taqatain do hison me banti hui thi, hathiyaron ki daur lagi thi, aur har mulk doosre se aage nikalne ki koshish me tha. Phir aakhri wajah wo goli bani jo 28 June 1914 ko Sarajevo ke sheher me chali, jahan Austria-Hungary ke wali ahad Archduke Franz Ferdinand ko qatal kar diya gaya. Aik maah ke andar andar ittehadon ki zanjeer harkat me aa gayi aur Europe jang me doob gaya.

Aik taraf Allies the, jin me France, Britain aur Russia shamil the, aur baad me America bhi 1917 me in ke sath aa mila. Dusri taraf Central Powers the, jin me Germany, Austria-Hungary aur Usmani saltanat shamil thi. Jang chaar saal chali, aur is ki pehchan khandaqon wali larai hai, jahan sipahi maheenon khemi hui khandaqon me beth kar chand gaz zameen ke liye larte rahe. Zehreeli gas aur tank isi jang me pehli dafa bare pemane par istemal hue. Crore ke qareeb log mare gaye.

Jang 11 November 1918 ko Germany ki shikast aur hathyar dalne par khatam hui. 1919 me Versailles ka muahida hua, jis me Germany par sakht shartein aur bhari tawan lagaya gaya. Isi jang ke baad duniya me aman qayam rakhne ke liye League of Nations naam ki tanzim banayi gayi. Aur hamare ilaqe ke liye sab se aham nateeja ye hua ke Usmani saltanat khatam ho gayi aur khilafat ka nizam bhi chand saalon me khatam ho gaya, jis ki lehar barre-sagheer ke musalmanon tak Khilafat tehreek ki surat me pohnchi thi, jo tum Pak Studies me parh chuke ho.

Paper wali khaas baatain: aakhri wajah Sarajevo me Ferdinand ka qatal, jang 1914 se 1918, Versailles muahida 1919, League of Nations isi ke baad bani, aur Usmani saltanat ka khatma.

Tasveer ka khayal: Europe ke naqshe par dono ittehad alag rangon me dikhaye jayen, maghribi mahaz par khandaqon ki lakeer bane, aur Sarajevo par wo nishan ho jahan se kahani shuru hui thi.

Dusri Aalmai Jang, 1939 se 1945

League of Nations duniya ko dusri jang se na bacha saki. Versailles ki sakht shartein Germany me narazgi paida karti rahin, aur isi maahol me Adolf Hitler iqtidar me aya. Us ne muahide ki shartein torna shuru ki, ilaqe par ilaqa liya, aur aakhir me 1 September 1939 ko Germany ne Poland par hamla kar diya. Britain aur France ne Germany ke khilaf elaan-e-jang kar diya, aur duniya dusri dafa aalmai jang me chali gayi.

Aik taraf Axis taqatain thi, Germany, Italy aur Japan. Dusri taraf Allies the, Britain, Soviet Union, aur baad me America. America is jang me tab aya jab December 1941 me Japan ne us ke bahri adday Pearl Harbor par achanak hamla kar diya. Ye hamla paper me bohat poocha jata hai. Jang Europe se Africa aur Asia tak phaili, aur ye tareekh ki sab se bari aur sab se tabah kun jang sabit hui, jis me croreon log mare gaye, sheher ke sheher malba ban gaye.

1945 me Germany ki shikast se Europe ki jang khatam ho gayi, lekin Japan larta raha. Phir August 1945 me America ne Japan ke do shehron, Hiroshima aur Nagasaki par atom bomb giraye. Insani tareekh me ye pehla aur ab tak ka aakhri istemal hai jo jang me hua. Chand dinon me Japan ne hathyar daal diye aur 2 September 1945 ko dusri aalmai jang khatam ho gayi. Kul muddat chhe saal banti hai.

Is jang ka sab se bara nateeja ye hua ke duniya ne aik nayi aalami tanzim banayi. October 1945 me United Nations, yani UNO, qayam hua, taake aindah aisi jang na ho. League of Nations nakam ho chuka tha, UNO usi ki jagah aya. Ye karri yaad rakho: pehli jang ke baad League of Nations, dusri jang ke baad UNO. Paper me ye jora aksar sath poocha jata hai.

Paper wali khaas baatain: jang 1939 me Hitler ke Poland par hamle se shuru hui, Pearl Harbor 1941 par hamle ke baad America aya, Hiroshima aur Nagasaki par August 1945 me atom bomb giraye gaye, jang chhe saal chali, aur UNO 1945 me isi ke baad bana.

Tasveer ka khayal: Duniya ke naqshe par Axis aur Allies alag rangon me hon, Pearl Harbor aur Hiroshima ke maqamat nishan zad hon, aur aakhir me UNO ke jhande wali aik chhoti jhalak ho ke jang ka nateeja sirf tabahi nahi, aik nayi tanzim bhi thi.

Aakhir me saal-war tartib aik nazar me

Ab poori kahani ko tartib se dohra lo. 1947 se 1948 me pehli Pak-Bharat jang hui, 1 January 1949 ko jung bandi aur ceasefire line bani. 1965 me sattara din ki jang hui, 6 September ko Lahore par hamla Defence Day ki wajah bana, aur 1966 me Tashkent muahida hua. 1971 me 16 December ko Dhaka me hathyar daale gaye, Bangladesh bana, aur 1972 me Simla muahide se LoC ka naam para. 1984 me Siachen ka barfani mahaz khula. 1999 me Kargil ki mahdood jang hui. Aur aalmai jangon ki tartib yun hai: pehli 1914 se 1918, jis ke baad League of Nations bana. Dusri 1939 se 1945, jis ke baad UNO bana.

Aik aakhri wazahat phir se sun lo, kyunke yehi is hisse ka sab se aham jumla hai. Pakistan aur Bharat ke darmiyan mukammal jangain teen hui, 1947 se 48, 1965 aur 1971. Us ke baad Kargil 1999 mahdood jang thi. Jab paper me poocha jaye ke aakhri bari jang kab hui to jawab 1971 hai. Jangon ko saalon ki jora bandi se yaad karo: 65 ke sath Tashkent 66, 71 ke sath Simla 72. Muahida hamesha jang ke agle saal ata hai, ye chhoti si trick tumhe kabhi saal nahi bhoolne degi.
