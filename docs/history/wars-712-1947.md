History: Barre-Sagheer ki Mashhoor Jangain (712 se 1947 tak)
Har jang aik kahani hai. Wajah, maidan, salar, aur wo aik lamha jis ne sab badal diya

Ye hisa kyun zaroori hai

One paper aur PPSC ke har test me tareekh ke sawalat ka aik bara hissa jangon se ata hai. Kabhi poocha jata hai ke Panipat ki pehli jang kab hui, kabhi poocha jata hai ke Plassey ki jang me ghadari kis ne ki, aur kabhi sirf ye ke 1857 ki jang ke baad hukumat kis ke paas chali gayi. Masla ye hai ke aksar students sirf saal rat lete hain, aur paper me jab do jangon ke naam mila kar options diye jate hain to wo confuse ho jate hain. Is liye hum har jang ko aik kahani ki tarah parhenge. Pehle wajah, ke jang hui kyun. Phir maidan, ke larai kahan hui. Phir salar, ke dono taraf fouj ki qiyadat kis ke haath me thi. Aur phir wo aik lamha, jis ne poori jang ka rukh badal diya. Jab kahani zehen me beth jayegi to saal khud ba khud yaad ho jayega, kyunke kahani bhoolti nahi, ratta bhool jata hai.

Aik usool pehle samajh lo. Barre-sagheer ki tareekh me kuch jangain itni aham hain ke unhon ne poori saltanat ka naqsha badal diya. 712 me Sindh ka darwaza khula, 1192 me Delhi ka takht badla, 1526 me Mughal aye, 1757 me Angrez ki buniyad pari, aur 1857 me pura nizam palat gaya. In paanch ko to tum neend me bhi pehchan lo, baqi in ke darmiyan ki karriyan hain.

Muhammad bin Qasim aur Sindh ki fatah, 712

Kahani yun shuru hoti hai. Arab taajiron ka aik jahaaz Debal ke paas samandari daakuon ne loot liya, aur us me kuch musalman khawateen bhi qaidi bana li gayin. Ye khabar Baghdad ke governor Hajjaj bin Yousuf tak pohnchi. Hajjaj ne Sindh ke raja, Raja Dahir, se mutalba kiya ke lutere pakar kar do aur maal wapas karo. Dahir ne jawab diya ke wo ilaqa us ke ikhtiyar me nahi. Yehi inkaar jang ki wajah ban gaya. Hajjaj ne apne bhanje aur damad, Muhammad bin Qasim ko, fouj de kar rawana kiya. Aur suno, us waqt Muhammad bin Qasim ki umar sirf satrah saal thi. Paper me ye umar wala sawal bohat ata hai, isay khaas yaad rakho.

Muhammad bin Qasim pehle Debal pohncha, jo aj ke Karachi ke qareeb aik bandargah thi. Wahan qila fatah hua, phir wo aage barhta gaya. Nirunkot, phir Sehwan, aur aakhir me Darya-e-Sindh ke kinare wo muqabla hua jis ka sab ko intezaar tha. Raja Dahir apne haathi par sawaar ho kar lara, lekin teer lagne se haathi badhak gaya aur raja maidaan me maara gaya. Is ke baad Aror, jo us waqt Sindh ka dar-ul-hukumat tha, fatah ho gaya, aur phir Multan tak ilaqa musalmanon ke paas aa gaya.

Nateeja ye nikla ke Islam barre-sagheer me daakhil ho gaya, aur isi liye Sindh ko Bab-ul-Islam, yani Islam ka darwaza, kaha jata hai. Ye lafz paper ka pasandida hai. Muhammad bin Qasim ne maqami logon ke sath narmi ka sulook kiya, mandiron ko aman diya, aur intezam aisa qayam kiya ke logon ne use qabool kar liya. Aik satrah saala salar ne aik poori tehzeeb ka darwaza khol diya tha, yehi is kahani ka asal nukta hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Naqshe par Sindh ka ilaqa dikhaya jaye, Debal se teer ka nishan Aror aur phir Multan tak jata hua, Darya-e-Sindh neeli lakeer me behta hua, aur Muhammad bin Qasim ki fouj ka raasta teeron se wazeh ho.

Tarain ki dusri jang, 1192

Is jang ko samajhne ke liye pehle aik saal peeche jana hoga. 1191 me Tarain ke maidan me hi Shahabuddin Muhammad Ghauri aur Delhi aur Ajmer ke raja Prithviraj Chauhan ka pehla muqabla hua tha, aur us me Ghauri ko shikast hui thi. Wo zakhmi ho kar wapas gaya. Paper me aksar pehli aur dusri Tarain ko mila kar confuse kiya jata hai, is liye ye jumla zehen me bitha lo: 1191 me Ghauri hara, 1192 me Ghauri jeeta.

Ghauri ne shikast ko bhoolne ke bajaye us se seekha. Aik saal tayyari ki, aik bari fouj banayi, aur 1192 me dobara Tarain ke maidan me aa khara hua. Tarain ka maidan aj ke Haryana me Thanesar ke qareeb hai. Is dafa Ghauri ne chaal badli. Us ne apni fouj ko hison me taqseem kiya, teer andaz dastay aage peeche hote rahe, aur din bhar ki larai ke baad aakhir me taza dam dastay ne wo hamla kiya jis ke saamne Rajput fouj thehar na saki. Prithviraj pakra gaya aur maara gaya.

Nateeja tareekh badal dene wala tha. Is jeet ke baad Delhi aur Ajmer musalmanon ke paas aa gaye, aur barre-sagheer me musalman hukumat ki wo buniyad pari jo phir sadion chali. Ghauri ne apne ghulam aur sab se bharosay wale salar, Qutbuddin Aibak ko, hindustan ke fatah shuda ilaqon ka nigehbaan banaya. Aur yaad rakho, yehi Aibak baad me 1206 me Delhi Sultanate ka pehla badshah bana. To Tarain ki dusri jang sirf aik jang nahi thi, ye aik nayi saltanat ka pehla din tha.

Tasveer ka khayal: Tarain ke khule maidan ki tasveer ho, aik taraf Ghauri ke teer andaz dastay hison me bante hue, dusri taraf Rajput fouj aur haathi, aur darmiyan me wo aakhri hamla teer ke nishan se dikhaya jaye.

Panipat ki pehli jang, 1526

Panipat ka naam yaad kar lo, kyunke is aik maidan me barre-sagheer ki teen sab se bari jangain hui hain. Pehli jang 21 April 1526 ko hui. Aik taraf Kabul ka hukmaran Zaheeruddin Muhammad Babur tha, aur dusri taraf Delhi ka sultan Ibrahim Lodhi.

Wajah ye thi ke Babur ki nazar hamesha Hindustan par thi. Wo Timur aur Changez Khan ki nasl se tha aur Delhi ke takht ko apna haq samajhta tha. Idhar Punjab ke governor Daulat Khan Lodhi aur doosre ameer Ibrahim Lodhi se naraaz the aur unhon ne Babur ko dawat di ke aa kar takht sambhalo. Babur aisi dawat ka intezaar hi kar raha tha.

Ab tadaad suno, kyunke yehi is jang ka sab se hairan kun hissa hai. Babur ke paas sirf bara hazaar ke qareeb fouj thi, aur Ibrahim Lodhi ke paas aik lakh ke qareeb sipahi aur hazaar ke qareeb haathi the. Lekin Babur ke paas do cheezain aisi thi jo Lodhi ke paas nahi thi. Pehli, top aur bandooq, jo hindustan ke maidan me pehli dafa is tarah istemal hui. Dusri, tulughma chaal, jis me ghud sawaar dastay dono taraf se ghoom kar dushman ko peeche se gher lete hain. Babur ne apni topain gaarion ki qatar ke peeche chupai, aur jab Lodhi ki dev-haikal fouj aage barhi to topain garjin, haathi badhak kar apni hi fouj ko kuchalne lage, aur ghud sawaar dono taraf se gherao kar chuke the. Aadhe din ki larai me Ibrahim Lodhi maidan me maara gaya aur us ki fouj bikhar gayi.

Nateeja ye hua ke Delhi aur Agra Babur ke paas aa gaye aur Mughal saltanat ki buniyad par gayi. Paper wali khaas baat ye hai ke top ka faisla kun istemal aur tulughma chaal, ye dono alfaaz isi jang ke sath jure hain. Jab bhi sawal aye ke kam fouj ne bari fouj ko kesay haraya, jawab Babur ki top aur us ki chaal hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Panipat ke maidan me aik taraf gaarion ki qatar ke peeche lagi topain aur un se nikalta dhuwan, dusri taraf Lodhi ke badhakte hue haathi, aur dono taraf se ghoomte hue ghud sawaar teeron ke nishan se dikhaye jayen.

Panipat ki dusri jang, 1556

Tees saal baad Panipat ke usi maidan me dusri jang hui, 5 November 1556 ko. Lekin is dafa kahani thori si mukhtalif hai. Babur ka pota Akbar us waqt sirf terah saal ka tha aur takht par naya naya betha tha. Asal taqat us ke ataleeq aur salar, Bairam Khan ke haath me thi. Dusri taraf Hemu tha, jo aik hindu wazir aur tajir se uth kar Delhi ka takht jeet chuka tha aur khud ko raja elaan kar chuka tha. Us ke paas bari fouj aur pandrah sau ke qareeb haathi the.

Larai zor ki hui aur aik lamhe ko laga ke jeet Hemu ki hogi, kyunke us ki fouj Mughalon ko daba rahi thi. Phir wo aik lamha aya jis ne tareekh badal di. Aik teer seedha Hemu ki ankh me laga. Wo behosh ho kar haathi se girne laga, aur jaise hi fouj ne apne salar ko girta dekha, us ke hosle toot gaye. Fouj salar ke baghair maidan me theharti nahi, ye jang ka purana usool hai. Hemu pakra gaya aur qatal kar diya gaya.

Nateeja ye hua ke Delhi aur Agra dobara Mughalon ke paas aa gaye, aur Akbar ki wo lambi hukumat shuru hui jo phir adhi sadi chali aur Mughal saltanat ko urooj par pohncha gayi. Paper me is jang ke sath hamesha do naam ate hain, Akbar aur Bairam Khan, aur jeet ki wajah ke tor par Hemu ki ankh me teer lagna. Teenon baatain aik sath yaad rakho.

Tasveer ka khayal: Maidan me Hemu ka haathi sab se uncha aur wazeh dikhaya jaye, us ke gird ghumasan ki larai, aur aik teer ki lakeer us ki taraf jati hui, taake student ko wo faisla kun lamha aik nazar me samajh aa jaye.

Sher Shah Suri, wo karri jo do jangon ko jorti hai

Panipat ki dusri jang se pehle aik chhota sa waqfa samajh lo, kyunke paper me ye sawal bhi ata hai. Babur ke bete Humayun ko aik afghan salar, Sher Khan ne, jo baad me Sher Shah Suri kehlaya, Chausa 1539 aur phir Kannauj 1540 ki jangon me hara kar Hindustan se nikal diya tha. Sher Shah ne sirf paanch saal hukumat ki, lekin is arse me us ne wo intezami nizam banaya jis par baad me Akbar ne apni saltanat khari ki. GT Road, jo us waqt Peshawar se Calcutta tak bani aur aj bhi maujood hai, daak ka nizam, aur rupiya naam ka sikka, ye sab Sher Shah ki den hain. Humayun pandrah saal baad wapas aya aur takht dobara jeeta, lekin us ke baad jald hi us ki wafat ho gayi aur terah saala Akbar takht par betha. Yehi wo pas-e-manzar hai jis se Panipat ki dusri jang samajh ati hai. To yaad rakho, Babur ne saltanat banayi, Sher Shah ne nizam banaya, aur Akbar ne dono ko mila kar saltanat ko urooj diya.

Plassey ki jang, 1757

Ab kahani us mor par ati hai jahan talwaar se zyada sazish kaam karti hai. 23 June 1757 ko Bengal ke aik aam ke bagh wale maidan, Plassey me, East India Company ke salar Robert Clive aur Bengal ke nawab Siraj-ud-Daula ka muqabla hua.

Wajah ye thi ke naujawan nawab Siraj-ud-Daula company ki badhti hui man mani se tang tha. Company Calcutta me us ki ijazat ke baghair qila bandi kar rahi thi aur tijarati riayaton ka galat istemal kar rahi thi. Nawab ne Calcutta par hamla kar ke qila fatah kar liya, aur jawab me Clive Madras se fouj le kar aa pohncha.

Lekin asal kahani maidan se pehle likhi ja chuki thi. Nawab ke sab se bare salar, Mir Jafar ne, Clive se khufiya muahida kar liya tha ke wo jang me hissa nahi lega aur badle me use Bengal ki nawabi milegi. Nateeja ye hua ke nawab ki takreeban pachaas hazaar fouj ke saamne Clive ke sirf teen hazaar ke qareeb sipahi the, lekin jab larai shuru hui to Mir Jafar ka bara hissa khara dekhta raha. Kuch hi golon ki bochar aur nawab ke wafadar salar Mir Madan ki shahadat ke baad nawab maidan chor gaya. Plassey is liye kehte hain ke ye jang kam aur sazish zyada thi.

Nateeja ye nikla ke Bengal, jo us waqt hindustan ka sab se ameer suba tha, amalan company ke qabze me chala gaya, aur Mir Jafar kath putli nawab ban gaya. Paper wali khaas baat zehen me rakho: Plassey 1757 me hui, Clive jeeta, aur jeet ki wajah fouji taqat nahi, Mir Jafar ki ghadari thi. Yehi wo buniyad hai jis par phir do sau saal ki angrez hukumat khari hui.

Tasveer ka khayal: Aam ke bagh ke darmiyan chhoti si company fouj aur us ke saamne nawab ki bari fouj, lekin fouj ka bara hissa alag khara dikhaya jaye jo lar nahi raha, aur Mir Jafar ke khaime par khufiya mulaqat ka ishara ho.

Panipat ki teesri jang, 1761

Ab teesri dafa Panipat. 14 January 1761. Ye barre-sagheer ki sab se khoon-raiz jangon me gini jati hai. Aik taraf Afghanistan ka badshah Ahmad Shah Abdali tha, jise Durrani bhi kehte hain, aur dusri taraf Maratha, jo us waqt hindustan ki sab se bari taqat ban chuke the aur un ki fouj Sadashivrao Bhau ki qiyadat me Delhi tak aa pohnchi thi.

Wajah ye thi ke Maratha shimal ki taraf barhte barhte Punjab tak aa gaye the, jo Abdali apna ilaqa samajhta tha. Abdali nauvi dafa hindustan aya aur dono foujain Panipat ke maidan me khaimay daal kar beth gayin. Maheenon muhasra raha, Maratha fouj ke sath aye lakhon ghair fouji logon ki wajah se raashan khatam hone laga, aur bhook ne Marathon ko maidan me nikalne par majboor kar diya. 14 January ko wo azeem takrao hua jo subah se dopahar tak jari raha. Pehle Maratha dabao me rahe, lekin phir Abdali ke mehfooz taza dastay maidan me utre aur Maratha fouj toot gayi. Bhau aur peshwa ka beta Vishwasrao dono mare gaye, aur hazaron sipahi aik din me kaam aye.

Nateeja bara talkh tha. Maratha taqat ka wo zor toot gaya jo kabhi wapas na aya, aur peshwa Balaji Baji Rao is sadme se kuch hi maheenon me chal basa. Lekin mazay ki baat ye hai ke Abdali bhi is jeet ka faida utha kar hindustan par hukumat na kar saka, wo wapas chala gaya. Khali hone wali jagah ko bharne ke liye ab sirf aik taqat bachi thi, aur wo thi East India Company. To teesri Panipat ne jeetne wale ko bhi kuch na diya aur harne wale ko sab kuch se mehroom kar diya, aur asal faida teesri taqat utha le gayi. Paper me is jang ki pehchan yehi hai, Maratha taqat ka ikhtitam.

Tasveer ka khayal: Panipat ke maidan me dono taraf ke bare khaimay aur darmiyan me ghumasan, aur naqshe par aik taraf Kabul se ata hua Abdali ka raasta aur dusri taraf Dakkan se ata hua Maratha raasta, dono teer Panipat par milte hue.

Buxar ki jang, 1764

Plassey ke saat saal baad, 22 October 1764 ko Buxar ke maidan me wo jang hui jis ne Plassey ki jeet ko pakka kar diya. Is dafa company ke saamne aik nahi, teen taqatoun ka ittehad tha. Bengal ka sabiq nawab Mir Qasim, jo company ki kath putli banne se inkar kar chuka tha. Awadh ka nawab Shuja-ud-Daula. Aur khud Mughal badshah Shah Alam Doum. Company ki fouj ki qiyadat Major Hector Munro ke paas thi.

Muqabla sakht hua, lekin company ki tarbiyat yafta fouj aur behtar top khana ittehad ki bari magar ghair munazzam fouj par bhaari para. Teenon hukumraan shikast kha gaye. Phir 1765 me Allahabad ka muahida hua, jis ke tehat Shah Alam Doum ne company ko Bengal, Bihar aur Orissa ki diwani de di. Diwani ka matlab hai maal guzari, yani tax wasool karne ka haq. Samjho ke is aik dastakhat se company taajir se hukmaran ban gayi, kyunke jang jeetna aik baat hai aur tax wasoolne ka qanuni haq milna bilkul dusri baat hai.

Paper me Plassey aur Buxar ko aksar mila kar poocha jata hai, to farq is tarah yaad rakho. Plassey 1757, sazish se Bengal mila. Buxar 1764, maidan ki jeet se diwani mili. Pehli ne darwaza khola, dusri ne ghar par qabza pakka kiya.

Tasveer ka khayal: Buxar ke maidan me teen alag jhande dikhaye jayen, Mir Qasim, Shuja-ud-Daula aur Shah Alam Doum ke, aur un ke saamne company ki munazzam qatar, aur kone me Allahabad muahide par dastakhat hoti hui chhoti si jhalak.

Mysore ki jangain aur Tipu Sultan, 1767 se 1799

Shimal se ab dakkan ki taraf chalo. Wahan company ko apne sab se sakht dushman ka saamna tha, Mysore ki saltanat. Pehle Haider Ali aur phir un ke bete Tipu Sultan ne company se chaar jangain larrien, jinhe Anglo-Mysore jangain kehte hain. Pehli do jangon me Haider Ali ne company ko sakht takkar di aur aik dafa to us ki fouj Madras ki deewaron tak pohnch gayi thi. 1782 me dusri jang ke darmiyan Haider Ali ka inteqal ho gaya aur kamaan Tipu Sultan ke haath ayi.

Tipu Sultan woh hukmaran tha jis ne angrez ko apna sab se bara dushman samjha aur zindagi bhar us se samjhota karne se inkar kiya. Us ne France se madad lene ki koshish ki, apni fouj ko jadeed tarbiyat di, aur raaketon ka istemal kia jo us daur ki jadeed technology thi. Teesri jang me use apni adhi saltanat aur bhari tawan se haath dhona para. Chauthi jang 1799 me hui. Company ne Nizam aur Marathaon ko sath mila liya tha, aur fouj ne Mysore ke dar-ul-hukumat Seringapatam ka muhasra kar liya. 4 May 1799 ko qile ki deewar tooti aur aakhri hamla hua. Tipu Sultan qile ke darwaze par larte hue shaheed hue. Riwayat hai ke un ka mashhoor jumla tha ke geedar ki sau saala zindagi se sher ki aik din ki zindagi behtar hai. Paper me ye jumla isi tarah riwayat ke tor par poocha jata hai, isay yaad rakho lekin ye bhi yaad rakho ke ye riwayat hai.

Nateeja ye hua ke Mysore ki saltanat khatam ho gayi aur dakkan me company ki taqat ko rokne wala koi na raha. Paper wali khaas baat: Tipu Sultan 1799 me Seringapatam me shaheed hue, aur unhe Mysore ka sher kaha jata hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Seringapatam ke qile ki mazboot deewarain, darya ke darmiyan wala qila, deewar me para hua shagaaf, aur qile ke darwaze par akhri muqable ki jhalak, sath me Tipu ke raaket ki chhoti si tasveer.

Anglo-Sikh jangain aur Punjab ka qabza, 1845 se 1849

Ab aakhri bari taqat ki baat karte hain jo company ke raaste me khari thi, Punjab ki Sikh saltanat. Maharaja Ranjit Singh ne apni zindagi me company se dostana taluqat rakhe aur Sutlej darya ko dono ke darmiyan sarhad mana gaya tha. Lekin 1839 me Ranjit Singh ki wafat ke baad Lahore darbar sazishon ka adda ban gaya, badshah badalte rahe, aur fouj beqabu hone lagi.

Isi bechaini me pehli Anglo-Sikh jang 1845 me chiri. Mudki, Ferozeshah aur Sobraon ki sakht jangain hui. Sikh fouj ne zabardast muqabla kiya, lekin darbar ke andar ke ghadaron, khaas tor par Lal Singh aur Tej Singh ki badniyati ne unhe harwaya. Lahore muahide ke tehat company ne bhari tawan aur ilaqe liye. Lekin ye sakoon zyada dair na raha. 1848 me Multan me baghawat hui aur dusri Anglo-Sikh jang chir gayi. Chillianwala ki jang me company ko sakht nuqsan pohncha, lekin aakhir me Gujarat ki jang 1849 me Sikh fouj ne hathyar daal diye. Lord Dalhousie ne Punjab ko company ke ilaqon me shamil kar liya, aur naujawan Maharaja Duleep Singh ko takht se hata kar England bhej diya gaya. Koh-e-Noor heera bhi isi daur me Malika Victoria ke paas pohncha.

Paper wali khaas baat ye hai ke Punjab sab se aakhir me, 1849 me, angrezon ke qabze me aya. Yani Bengal 1757 me gaya aur Punjab 1849 me, takreeban sau saal ka ye safar hai jis me company ne poora barre-sagheer le liya.

Tasveer ka khayal: Naqshe par Punjab ke paanch darya neeli lakeeron me dikhaye jayen, Sutlej par sarhad ki lakeer, aur Lahore par 1849 ka nishan, sath me Chillianwala aur Gujarat ke maidanon ke chhote nishan.

1857 ki Jang-e-Azadi

Ab us jang ki baat jo is poore hisse ki sab se aham kahani hai. 1857 ki jang ko angrez baghawat kehte hain aur hum Jang-e-Azadi kehte hain, aur paper me ye sawal dono naamon ke sath aa sakta hai.

Pehle wajah samjho, kyunke ye jang kisi aik din ka ghussa nahi thi. Sau saal ki company hukumat me zulm jama hota raha tha. Lord Dalhousie ki woh policy jis ke tehat jis riyasat ke badshah ka koi waaris na ho, wo riyasat company me mila li jati thi, is se Jhansi aur doosri riyasatain cheen li gayi thi. Nawab Wajid Ali Shah ko hata kar Awadh par qabza kar liya gaya tha, aur Awadh hi se sab se zyada sipahi company ki fouj me the. Maashi loot, mazhabi bechaini, aur sipahiyon ki kam tankhwah aur badsulooki ne aag tayyar kar di thi. Phir aakhri wajah wo naye kartoos bane jin ke baare me mashhoor hua ke un par gay aur khinzeer ki charbi lagi hai aur unhe daanton se kaatna parta hai. Hindu ke liye gay aur musalman ke liye khinzeer, dono ke mazhab par seedha hamla tha. Yehi wo chingari thi jo barood par ja giri.

Kahani ka aaghaz Barrackpore se hua jahan 29 March 1857 ko aik sipahi Mangal Pandey ne kartoos istemal karne se inkar karke angrez afsaron par hamla kar diya. Use phansi de di gayi, lekin aag phail chuki thi. 10 May ko Meerut ke sipahiyon ne baghawat ki, apne qaidi sathiyon ko churaya, aur Delhi ki taraf kuch kiya. Delhi me unhon ne barrey Mughal badshah, Bahadur Shah Zafar ko, jo us waqt laal qile me aik pension yafta badshah ki zindagi guzaar raha tha, apna rehnuma elaan kar diya. Ab ye baghawat aik jang-e-azadi ki shakal le chuki thi, kyunke us ke peeche aik badshah ka naam aa gaya tha.

Phir ye aag sheher sheher phaili. Delhi me maheenon muqabla raha. Lucknow me Begum Hazrat Mahal ne apne bete Birjis Qadr ko takht par bitha kar qiyadat ki aur residency ka muhasra tareekh me mashhoor hai. Kanpur me Nana Sahib peshwa ke waaris hone ke daaway ke sath uthe aur un ke sath Tantia Tope jaisa maahir salar tha. Jhansi me Rani Lakshmibai ne wo bahaduri dikhayi ke khud angrez afsaron ne un ki tareef ki, wo mardana libaas me ghore par sawaar ho kar larti thi aur 1858 me Gwalior ke qareeb larte hue shaheed hui. Bareilly, Jagdishpur aur doosre ilaqon me bhi muqabla hua, jahan Kunwar Singh jaise salar asy saal ki umar me larte rahe.

Phir jang kyun nakam hui, ye bhi samajhna zaroori hai kyunke paper me ye sawal aksar ata hai. Koi markazi qiyadat aur mushtarka mansuba nahi tha, har sheher apni jang lar raha tha. Punjab aur dakkan ki bari taqatoun ne angrez ka sath diya. Angrez ke paas taar, railway aur behtar top khana tha jo us ne fouj ko teezi se harkat dene me istemal kiya. Aur maqami sardaron ki ghadariyan bhi wohi purani kahani dohra gayin. September 1857 me Delhi dobara angrez ke paas chala gaya, Bahadur Shah Zafar ko girftar karke Rangoon jalawatan kar diya gaya jahan 1862 me un ka inteqal hua aur wohi dafan hue. 1858 ke aakhir tak muqabla khatam ho gaya.

Nateeja tareekhi tha. East India Company khatam kar di gayi. 1858 ke Act ke tehat Hindustan ki hukumat seedha Bartanvi takht, yani Crown ke paas chali gayi, aur Malika Victoria ka elaan jari hua jis me maqami logon ke mazhab me mudakhlat na karne ka wada kiya gaya. Mughal saltanat hamesha ke liye khatam ho gayi. Aur aik gehra nateeja ye bhi hua ke is jang ki yadein baad me azadi ki tehreekon ki buniyad bani. Paper wali khaas baatain aik sath jama kar lo: aakhri wajah charbi wale kartoos, pehli chingari Mangal Pandey Barrackpore, rehnuma Bahadur Shah Zafar, aur nateeja company ka khatma aur Crown ki hukumat 1858.

Tasveer ka khayal: Naqshe par wo sheher nishan zad hon jahan muqabla hua, Delhi, Meerut, Lucknow, Kanpur aur Jhansi, har sheher ke sath us ke rehnuma ka naam, aur Laal Qile ki tasveer sab se numaya ho kyunke wahi is jang ka markaz tha.

Aakhir me saal-war tartib aik nazar me

Ab poori kahani ko tartib se dohra lo, kyunke paper me aksar jangon ko tartib me lagane ya saal pehchanne ka sawal ata hai. 712 me Muhammad bin Qasim ne Sindh fatah kiya aur Sindh Bab-ul-Islam kehlaya. 1192 me Tarain ki dusri jang me Ghauri ne Prithviraj ko haraya aur Delhi ka raasta khula. 1526 me Panipat ki pehli jang me Babur ne top aur tulughma se Mughal saltanat ki buniyad rakhi. 1556 me Panipat ki dusri jang me Hemu ki ankh ke teer ne Akbar ka takht bacha liya. 1761 me Panipat ki teesri jang me Abdali jeeta lekin Maratha taqat hamesha ke liye toot gayi. 1757 me Plassey me Mir Jafar ki ghadari se Bengal gaya, aur gaur karo, Plassey saal ke hisab se teesri Panipat se pehle ati hai, 1757 pehle aur 1761 baad me, yehi tartib wala trap paper me lagta hai. 1764 me Buxar ki jeet se company ko diwani mili. 1799 me Tipu Sultan Seringapatam me shaheed hue. 1849 me Punjab company ke paas gaya. Aur 1857 me Jang-e-Azadi hui jis ke baad 1858 me company khatam aur Crown ki hukumat shuru ho gayi.

Aik aakhri mashwara ustad ki taraf se. Jangon ko alag alag ratne ke bajaye unhe aik zanjeer ki tarah yaad karo. Har jang pichli jang ke nateeje se nikalti hai. Plassey ne Buxar ko bulaya, Buxar ne diwani di, diwani ne taqat di, aur taqat ne 1857 ki wajahain paida ki. Jab zanjeer samajh aa jayegi to koi saal nahi bhoolega.
