Arkan-e-Islam aur Iman ki Buniyadein
Paanch satoon jin par Islam khara hai, aur chhe baatein jin par iman mukammal hota hai

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me Islamiat ke sab se pehle aur sab se zyada dohraye jane wale sawal yahin se bante hain. Islam ke arkan kitne hain, namaz kab farz hui, zakat ka nisab kitna hai, Hajj kab farz hua, iman ke arkan kaun se hain, Taurat kis par nazil hui, soor phoonkne wala farishta kaun hai. Ye sawal har paper me ghum phir kar ate hain aur ye number sab se asaan hote hain, bas sharte ye hai ke har baat apni jagah par pakki ho. Aik mashhoor hadees me Nabi Kareem Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ne farmaya ke Islam ki buniyad paanch cheezon par hai. Unhi paanch ko arkan-e-Islam kehte hain. Aur aik dusri mashhoor hadees, jo Hadees-e-Jibrail ke naam se mashhoor hai, us me iman ki chhe buniyadein bayan hui hain. Is lesson me pehle paanch arkan tafseel se parhen ge, phir iman ke chhe arkan, phir asmani kitabein aur mashhoor farishte. Tartib hamesha yahi rahe gi: Shahadat, Namaz, Roza, Zakat, Hajj. Is tartib ko aik saans me dohrane ki aadat daal lo, paper me arkan ki tartib hi aksar sawal ban jati hai.

Pehla rukn, Shahadat

Islam ka pehla rukn Shahadat hai, yani gawahi dena. Is ka matlab hai dil se manna aur zubaan se iqrar karna ke Allah ke siwa koi maabood nahi aur Hazrat Muhammad Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam Allah ke aakhri rasool hain. Isi gawahi ko Kalma-e-Tayyaba kaha jata hai, La ilaha illallah Muhammad-ur-Rasoolullah. Ye Islam ka darwaza hai, jo shakhs ye kalma sachhe dil se parh le wo Islam me dakhil ho jata hai. Baqi chaaron arkan isi buniyad par khare hain, is liye Shahadat hamesha pehle number par ati hai. Paper me sawal yun banta hai ke Islam ka pehla rukn kaun sa hai, ya Kalma-e-Tayyaba ko kya kehte hain. Dono ka jawab tumhe ab maloom hai.

Dusra rukn, Namaz

Islam ka dusra rukn Namaz hai. Namaz Allah se seedha rabta hai aur ye wo wahid rukn hai jo Nabi Kareem Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ko Miraj ki raat ata hua. Waaqia yun hai ke Miraj ke mauqe par pehle pachas namazain farz huin, phir Hazrat Musa Alaihis Salam ke mashware par Nabi Kareem Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam baar baar arz karte rahe aur aakhir me paanch namazain reh gayen, lekin sawab pachas hi ka rakha gaya. Isi liye kaha jata hai ke namaz momineen ke liye Miraj ka tohfa hai. Namaz Hijrat se pehle, Miraj ke mauqe par farz hui, ye point paper me tareekh ke sath poocha jata hai.

Din me paanch waqt ki namaz farz hai aur un ke naam tartib se yun hain: pehli Fajar, jo subah sadiq se sooraj nikalne se pehle tak parhi jati hai. Dusri Zohar, jo dopahar ko zawaal ke baad parhi jati hai. Teesri Asar, jo soraj dhalne ke baad maghrib se pehle hai. Chauthi Maghrib, jo sooraj ghuroob hote hi parhi jati hai. Panchwin Isha, jo raat ko shafaq ghayab hone ke baad parhi jati hai. In paanchon ke naam aur tartib dono paper me ate hain. Chand aur baatein bhi jor kar yaad kar lo. Wuzu namaz ki kunji hai, baghair wuzu namaz nahi hoti. Azan ka nizam Madina Munawwara me Hijrat ke baad pehle saal qayam hua, aur pehli azan dene wale Hazrat Bilal Habshi Radi Allahu Anhu hain. Jummah ki namaz Zohar ki jagah jamaat ke sath parhi jati hai aur ye har aqil baligh mard par farz hai.

Teesra rukn, Roza

Islam ka teesra rukn Roza hai. Roza Ramzan ke maheene me rakha jata hai, subah sadiq se ghuroob-e-aftab tak khane peene aur nafsani khwahishat se ruke rehne ka naam roza hai. Roza 2 Hijri me farz hua, ye tareekh paper ka fix sawal hai, isay Ramzan ki farziyat ke sath jor kar yaad karo. Roze ka asal maqsad Quran ne taqwa bayan kiya hai, yani Allah ka khauf aur parhezgari paida karna. Bhooka rehna asal maqsad nahi, asal maqsad nafs ko qaabu me karna hai. Ramzan hi wo maheena hai jis me Quran Majid ka nuzool shuru hua, aur isi maheene me Laylat-ul-Qadr hai jo hazar maheenon se behtar raat hai aur Ramzan ke aakhri ashray ki taaq raaton me talash ki jati hai. Ramzan ke baad pehli Shawwal ko Eid-ul-Fitr manayi jati hai, jo roza daron ke liye Allah ki taraf se inaam ka din hai. Eid se pehle Sadqa-e-Fitr ada karna wajib hota hai taake ghareeb bhi Eid ki khushi me shamil ho saken.

Chautha rukn, Zakat

Islam ka chautha rukn Zakat hai. Zakat ka matlab hai paak karna aur barhna. Shariat ki istilah me is ka matlab hai ke jo musalman sahib-e-nisab ho wo apne maal ka aik makhsoos hissa, jo aam tor par dhai feesad hota hai, saal guzarne par mustahiq logon ko de. Zakat maal ko paak karti hai aur muashre me ghareeb ki madad ka sab se bara nizam hai. Ab nisab ko samajh lo, kyunke paper me nisab ke number hi pooche jate hain. Nisab us kam az kam maal ko kehte hain jis par zakat farz hoti hai. Sone ka nisab saarhe saat tola hai, yani saatsath gram se kuch zyada, standard hisaab me 87.48 gram mashhoor hai. Chandi ka nisab saarhe baawan tola hai, yani 612.36 gram. Naqad raqam, tijarti samaan aur sone chandi par zakat ki sharah dhai feesad hai, yani chaaleeswan hissa. Ye teen number, saat saat saat tola nahi, saarhe saat tola sona, saarhe baawan tola chandi, aur dhai feesad sharah, inhe alag alag gin kar yaad rakho kyunke options me aksar yehi number idhar udhar karke phansaya jata hai. Kheti ki paidawar par zakat ko Ushr kehte hain, barish se sairab hone wali zameen par daswan hissa aur mehnat se sairab hone wali par beeswan hissa, ye point bhi kabhi kabhi a jata hai.

Ab ye bhi jaan lo ke zakat kin logon ko di ja sakti hai. Quran Majid ki Surah Tauba me zakat ke aath masarif, yani kharch ki jaghein, bayan hui hain. Pehle fuqara, yani ghareeb log. Dusre masakeen, yani intehai zarurat mand. Teesre wo log jo zakat wasool karne ke kaam par muqarrar hon, unhe amileen kehte hain. Chauthe wo log jin ke dilon ko Islam ki taraf mael karna maqsood ho. Panchwen gardanon ki azadi me, yani ghulamon ko azad karane me. Chhatay qarzdaaron ki madad me. Saatwen Allah ki raah me. Aathwen musafir ki madad me, chahe wo apne sheher me ameer hi kyun na ho, safar me zarurat mand ho to zakat le sakta hai. In aathon ko tartib se nahi to naam se zaroor pehchano, kyunke paper me aik option ghalat daal kar poocha jata hai ke in me se kaun zakat ka mustahiq nahi hai.

Panchwan rukn, Hajj

Islam ka panchwan aur aakhri rukn Hajj hai. Hajj zindagi me aik martaba har us musalman par farz hai jo sahib-e-istitaat ho, yani jis ke paas Makkah jaane ka kharcha aur sehat ho. Hajj 9 Hijri me farz hua, ye tareekh paper me poochi jati hai. Pehla Hajj Nabi Kareem Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ki sarbarahi me nahi hua tha, 9 Hijri me Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique Radi Allahu Anhu ameer-e-Hajj ban kar gaye, aur 10 Hijri me Nabi Kareem Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ne apna wahid Hajj ada farmaya jo Hajjat-ul-Wida ke naam se mashhoor hai.

Hajj ke tamam arkan me sab se bara rukn Wuqoof-e-Arafat hai. 9 Zil-Hajj ko Arafat ke maidan me theherna Hajj ki rooh hai. Nabi Kareem Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ka farmaan hai ke Hajj to Arafat hi hai, yani jis ne Arafat ka wuqoof pa liya us ne Hajj pa liya. Isi liye 9 Zil-Hajj ki tareekh aur Arafat ka naam jor kar yaad karo. Hajj ki poori ibadat Hazrat Ibrahim Alaihis Salam ke khandan se nisbatain rakhti hai. Kaaba ki tameer Hazrat Ibrahim aur Hazrat Ismail Alaihimus Salam ne ki. Safa aur Marwa ke darmiyan sai Hazrat Hajra ki yaad hai jo apne bache Hazrat Ismail ke liye pani ki talash me daudi theen. Aur 10 Zil-Hajj ki qurbani Hazrat Ibrahim ki us azeem qurbani ki yaad hai jab wo Allah ke hukam par apne bete ki qurbani ke liye tayyar ho gaye the aur Allah ne us ki jagah dumba bhej diya. Eid-ul-Azha isi yaad me manayi jati hai. Hajj ke dinon me haji ihram bandhta hai, Mina jata hai, Arafat me wuqoof karta hai, Muzdalifa me raat guzarta hai aur Jamarat par kankariyan marta hai. Ye tartib mukhtasar si zehen me rakh lo, tafseel nahi to naam zaroor pehchano.

Iman ke chhe arkan

Ab dusri taraf aao. Jis tarah Islam ke paanch arkan hain, usi tarah iman ki chhe buniyadein hain, jinhe arkan-e-iman kaha jata hai. Ye Hadees-e-Jibrail me bayan hui hain, jis me Hazrat Jibrail Alaihis Salam insani surat me aye aur Nabi Kareem Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam se Islam, Iman aur Ihsan ke sawal kiye. Iman ka matlab hai dil se tasdeeq aur yaqeen. Pehla rukn Allah par iman hai, yani Allah aik hai, wo har cheez ka khaliq aur malik hai, us ka koi shareek nahi. Dusra rukn farishton par iman hai, yani Allah ki aik noorani makhlooq farishte hain jo us ke ahkaam bajalate hain aur kabhi nafarmani nahi karte. Teesra rukn Allah ki kitabon par iman hai, yani jo kitabein aur saheefe Allah ne apne rasoolon par nazil farmaye, un sab ko sach manna, aur ye ke Quran un sab me aakhri aur mehfooz kitaab hai. Chautha rukn rasoolon par iman hai, yani Allah ne har dor me hidayat ke liye nabi aur rasool bheje, pehle nabi Hazrat Adam Alaihis Salam hain aur aakhri nabi Hazrat Muhammad Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam hain, aap ke baad koi nabi nahi aye ga. Panchwan rukn taqdeer par iman hai, yani duniya me jo khair aur sharr hota hai wo Allah ke ilm aur us ki mashiyyat se hai, aur ye yaqeen insaan ko sabr aur shukr dono sikhata hai. Chhata rukn roz-e-aakhirat par iman hai, yani maut ke baad aik din tamam insaan dobara zinda kiye jayenge, un ke aamaal ka hisaab hoga aur jannat aur jahannam ka faisla hoga. Ye chhe baatein dil me pakki ho jayen to iman mukammal hota hai.

Chaar asmani kitabein

Iman ke teesre rukn ki tafseel bhi paper me poochi jati hai, is liye chaar mashhoor asmani kitabon ko un ke rasoolon ke sath jor kar yaad kar lo. Pehli kitaab Taurat hai, jo Hazrat Musa Alaihis Salam par nazil hui. Dusri kitaab Zabur hai, jo Hazrat Dawood Alaihis Salam ko ata hui. Teesri kitaab Injeel hai, jo Hazrat Isa Alaihis Salam par nazil hui. Chauthi aur aakhri kitaab Quran Majid hai, jo Hazrat Muhammad Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam par nazil hui, aur ye qiyamat tak mehfooz hai kyunke is ki hifazat ki zimmedari khud Allah ne li hai. In chaaron ke ilawa Allah ne chand rasoolon ko chhote saheefe bhi ata farmaye. Hazrat Ibrahim Alaihis Salam ko diye gaye saheefe Suhuf-e-Ibrahim ke naam se mashhoor hain aur Quran me un ka zikr aya hai. Kitabon ki kul mashhoor tadaad chaar hai, aur saheefe un ke ilawa hain. Sawal yun banta hai ke Zabur kis nabi ko mili, ya sab se pehli asmani kitaab kaun si hai. Pehli ka jawab Taurat hai, kyunke chaar barri kitabon me tartib ke hisaab se wo sab se pehle nazil hui.

Chaar mashhoor farishte aur un ke kaam

Farishton par iman to parh liya, ab un chaar farishton ko pehchan lo jin ke naam aur kaam paper me bar bar ate hain. Pehle Hazrat Jibrail Alaihis Salam, ye sab se muazzaz farishte hain aur in ka kaam Allah ki wahi nabiyon tak pahunchana tha. Quran me inhe Rooh-ul-Ameen aur Rooh-ul-Qudus bhi kaha gaya hai. Quran Majid ka nuzool Nabi Kareem Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam par inhi ke zariye hua. Dusre Hazrat Mikail Alaihis Salam, in ke zimme barish aur makhlooq ke rizq ka intezam hai. Teesre Hazrat Israfil Alaihis Salam, in ke haath me soor hai. Jab Allah ka hukam hoga to ye soor phoonken ge jis se qiyamat barpa hogi, aur dusri dafa phoonkne par tamam log dobara zinda ho uthenge. Chauthe Hazrat Izrail Alaihis Salam, inhe Malak-ul-Maut bhi kehte hain aur in ka kaam jaandaron ki rooh qabz karna hai. Farq yun yaad rakho: wahi Jibrail ki, rizq aur barish Mikail ki, soor Israfil ka, aur rooh qabz karna Izrail ka kaam hai. Options me aksar Mikail aur Jibrail ke kaam ulta karke diye jate hain, wahi par kabhi mat phisalna, wahi sirf Jibrail laate the.

Paper ke fix facts, aik saans me dohra lo

Ab poore lesson ko gin kar dohra lo. Islam ke arkan paanch hain: Shahadat, Namaz, Roza, Zakat, Hajj. Pehla rukn Shahadat hai aur Kalma-e-Tayyaba usi ka naam hai. Namaz Miraj ki raat farz hui aur ye tohfa hai, din me paanch namazain Fajar, Zohar, Asar, Maghrib aur Isha hain, pehli azan Madina me Hazrat Bilal ne di, aur wuzu namaz ki kunji hai. Roza 2 Hijri me farz hua aur us ka maqsad taqwa hai. Zakat me sone ka nisab saarhe saat tola, chandi ka saarhe baawan tola aur sharah dhai feesad hai, masarif aath hain jo Surah Tauba me aye. Hajj 9 Hijri me farz hua, sab se bara rukn Wuqoof-e-Arafat hai jo 9 Zil-Hajj ko hota hai, aur Nabi Kareem Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ka Hajj Hajjat-ul-Wida 10 Hijri me hua. Iman ke arkan chhe hain: Allah, farishte, kitabein, rasool, taqdeer aur roz-e-aakhirat. Asmani kitabein chaar hain: Taurat Hazrat Musa ko, Zabur Hazrat Dawood ko, Injeel Hazrat Isa ko aur Quran Hazrat Muhammad Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ko mili, aur Suhuf-e-Ibrahim Hazrat Ibrahim ke saheefe hain. Farishte chaar pehchano: Jibrail wahi ke liye, Mikail rizq aur barish ke liye, Israfil soor ke liye, aur Izrail rooh qabz karne ke liye. Ye gin kar yaad ki hui baatein paper me kabhi ghalat nahi hoten, bas hall me jaldi me tartib mat badalna.
