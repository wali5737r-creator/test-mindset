Ghazwaat
Kitne hue, kahan hue, aur har maidan me asal me hua kya tha

Ye topic kyun zaroori hai

Ghazwaat se one paper me har saal sawal ata hai. Kabhi pooch lete hain ke kul ghazwaat kitne hue, kabhi Badr me tadaad kya thi, kabhi Mu'ta ke teen salar kaun the. Ye sawal un students ke liye mushkil hote hain jo sirf naam aur saal ratte hain, kyunke naam aur saal bhool jate hain. Lekin jo student har jang ko aik kahani ki tarah parh le, ke jang hui kyun, maidan me hua kya, aur khatm kis par hui, us ke liye ye sawal sab se mazay ke ban jate hain. Kahani kabhi nahi bhoolti. Is lesson me hum har bari jang ko bilkul wese hi parhenge, kahani ke andaaz me.

Pehle ghazwa aur sariya ka farq samajh lo

Sab se pehle ye farq saaf kar lo, kyunke paper yahi se phansata hai. Jis muhim me Nabi Kareem Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam khud shareek hue, usay ghazwa kehte hain. Aur jo muhim Aap ne kisi sahabi ko salar bana kar bheji aur khud Madina me rahe, usay sariya kehte hain. Tadaad ki baat ye hai ke mashhoor standard jawab 27 ghazwaat hai. Kuch kitabon me 25 aur kuch me 29 bhi milte hain, kyunke kuch muhaqqaqeen chhoti muhimmon ko ginne ya na ginne me ikhtilaf karte hain, lekin paper ke liye fix jawab 27 hi yaad rakho. Sab se pehla sariya Hazrat Hamza ki sarbarahi me 1 Hijri me bheja gaya tha, aur sab se pehla ghazwa Ghazwa-e-Waddan hai, jisay Abwa bhi kehte hain. Pehli bari aur faisla-kun jang Badr thi, jis ki kahani ab shuru hoti hai.

Ghazwa-e-Badr, jang jo sab kuch badal gayi

Wajah kya thi. Hijrat ke dusre saal, 2 Hijri me, khabar ayi ke Abu Sufyan ki sarbarahi me Quraish ka bara tijarati qafila Sham se Makkah ja raha hai, aur is qafile ki kamai musalmano ke khilaf jang par lagni thi. Dusri taraf Quraish bhi Madina par hamlay ki tayyari me the. Aap 313 sahaba ko lekar nikle. Is tadaad ko khaas yaad rakho: 313. In me se aksar ke pas na zira thi na hathiyar, kul do ghoray aur sattar oont the, jin par bari bari sawari hoti thi. Abu Sufyan qafila bacha kar nikal gaya, lekin Makkah se Abu Jahl ki sarbarahi me aik hazar ka lashkar poori tayyari ke sath Madina ki taraf barh aya. Aap ne Badr ke maqam par parhao dala, jahan pani ke kunwein the. Aik sahabi Hazrat Hubab bin Munzir ke mashware par musalmano ne kunwon ke qareeb jagah li taake pani un ke qabze me rahe. Jang se pehle Aap ne poori raat dua me guzari.

Munaasib tareekh 17 Ramzan 2 Hijri hai, aur is din ko Quran ne Yaum-ul-Furqan kaha hai, yani haq aur batil me farq karne wala din. Maidan me pehle mubarizat hui, yani aik aik karke muqabla, phir aam jang shuru ho gayi. Allah ne farishton ki madad bheji. Nateeja ye nikla ke musalmanon ki faisla-kun fatah hui. Musalmano ke 14 sahaba shaheed hue. Quraish ke 70 admi mare gaye aur 70 qaidi bane. Marne walon me Abu Jahl bhi tha, jo is ummat ka Firaun kahlata tha, aur Umayya bin Khalaf bhi. Qaidion ke sath Aap ne aisa sulook kiya ke jo parhe likhe the, un ka fidya ye rakha ke wo das das musalman bachon ko parhna likhna sikha dein. Is jang ne saabit kar diya ke fatah tadaad se nahi, iman aur nazm se milti hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Badr ke maidan ki tasveer me aik taraf khula maidan, darmiyan me pani ke kunwein jin par musalmano ka parhao dikhaya jaye, peeche pahari silsila, aur do chhoti safain, aik taraf 313 aur dusri taraf 1000 likha ho taake tadaad ka farq aankhon se dikhai de.

Ghazwa-e-Uhud, jeet-ti hui jang ka rukh kesay badla

Wajah Badr ki haar ka badla thi. 3 Hijri me Quraish teen hazar ka lashkar lekar aye, jis ki qayadat Abu Sufyan kar raha tha. Is dafa un ki auratain bhi sath thin taake koi maidan se na bhage. Musalmano ki tadaad 700 thi. Aap ne Uhud pahar ko apni pusht par rakha aur aik chhoti pahari par Hazrat Abdullah bin Jubair ki sarbarahi me pachaas teer-andaz bitha diye, aur sakht hukm diya ke chahe kuch ho jaye, ye jagah mat chorna. Jang shuru hui to musalman ghalib aa gaye, dushman bhagne laga. Teer-andazon ne samjha jang khatm ho gayi, aksar ne pahari chor di. Khalid bin Waleed, jo us waqt tak musalman nahi hue the, dushman ke ghudsawar dastay ko lekar usi khali pahari ke peeche se aa gaye aur peeche se hamla kar diya. Jeet-ti hui jang ka rukh palat gaya.

Is jang me 70 sahaba shaheed hue. Hazrat Hamza, Aap ke chacha aur Sher-e-Khuda kahlane wale, Wahshi nami ghulam ke neze se shaheed hue. Hazrat Mus'ab bin Umair bhi shaheed hue, ye wohi sahabi hain jo hijrat se pehle Madina bheje gaye the aur Islam ke pehle safeer kahlate hain. Aap Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam khud zakhmi hue, dandaan-e-mubarak shaheed hua. Is jang ka sab se bara sabaq ye hai ke salar ka hukm manna hi fatah hai, aur aik chhoti si nafarmani poori jeet ko badal sakti hai. Paper me Uhud se ye sawal fix ate hain: teer-andazon ki tadaad pachaas, un ke salar Abdullah bin Jubair, shuhada sattar, aur Hazrat Hamza ke qatil ka naam Wahshi.

Tasveer ka khayal: Uhud ki tasveer me bara pahaar peeche dikhaya jaye, us ke samne maidan, aur aik taraf chhoti si pahari jis par teer-andaz kharay hon, aur peeche se ghudsawaron ka ghoom kar ata hua raasta aik lakeer se dikhaya jaye taake student ko samajh aye ke jang ka rukh kesay badla.

Ghazwa-e-Khandaq, jab khandaq ne das hazar ko rok diya

5 Hijri me wo hua jo Arab me pehle kabhi nahi hua tha. Quraish, Ghatafan aur dusre qabail ne mil kar das hazar ka ittehadi lashkar tayyar kiya, is liye isay Ghazwa-e-Ahzab bhi kehte hain, ahzab yani giroh aur ittehad. Musalmano ki tadaad teen hazar ke qareeb thi. Aamne samne ki jang me jeetna mushkil tha. Tab Hazrat Salman Farsi ne aik mashwara diya jo Arab ke liye bilkul naya tha. Unho ne kaha ke Fars me jab dushman zyada ho to sheher ke gird khandaq, yani gehri khaai, khod dete hain. Aap ko mashwara pasand aya. Madina ke teen taraf pahaar aur baghaat the, sirf aik taraf khula maidan tha, wahi khandaq khodi gayi. Aap khud kodali chala kar mitti uthate rahe. Thand, bhook aur musalsal mehnat ke din the.

Dushman aya to samne gehri khaai dekh kar heran reh gaya, ghore aage barh hi nahi sakte the. Takreeban aik maah muhasra raha. Is doran dushman ke mashhoor pehelwan Amr bin Abd Wudd ne khandaq par karke muqabla talab kiya, to Hazrat Ali samne aye aur usay qatal kar diya. Aakhir aik raat sakht aandhi ayi, dushman ke khaimay ukhar gaye, bartanon tak ko ulta kar diya, aur ittehad toot kar wapas chala gaya. Bari jang hue baghair Madina bach gaya. Paper wali khaas baatein: khandaq ka mashwara Salman Farsi ka tha, dushman ki tadaad das hazar, aur ye 5 Hijri ki baat hai.

Tasveer ka khayal: Khandaq ki tasveer me Madina sheher aik taraf dikhaya jaye, us ke gird pahaar aur baghaat, aur khule maidan wali taraf aik gehri khaai ki lakeer bani ho jis ke dusri taraf dushman ke khaimay hon. Upar likha ho ke ye tadbeer Salman Farsi ki thi.

Banu Qurayza ka muhasra

Khandaq ke foran baad ka waqia hai, is liye mukhtasar samajh lo. Madina ke andar rehne wale Yahudi qabile Banu Qurayza ne muahida tora aur muhasre ke naazuk waqt me dushman se raabta kiya. Khandaq khatm hote hi Aap ne un ke qile ka muhasra kiya, jo takreeban pachis din raha. Aakhir unho ne khud ye shart manzoor ki ke Hazrat Saad bin Muaz jo faisla karenge wo manzoor hoga, aur unho ne Taurat ke qanoon ke mutabiq faisla diya. Ye waqia is liye aham hai ke paper me aksar poocha jata hai ke Khandaq ke baad konsi muhim hui aur faisla kis sahabi ne kiya tha.

Sulah Hudaybiyyah, jo sulah asal me fatah thi

6 Hijri me Aap 1400 sahaba ke sath Umrah ke irade se Makkah rawana hue. Hathiyar sath nahi the, sirf musafir ki talwar thi. Quraish ne raasta rok diya. Hudaybiyyah ke maqam par parhao hua. Wahan aik darakht ke neeche Aap ne sahaba se ahed liya jisay Bait-e-Rizwan kehte hain. Phir muzakrat hue aur aik sulah-nama likha gaya. Us ki shartein dekhne me musalmano ke khilaf lagti thin: is saal Umrah kiye baghair wapas jana hoga, jang das saal ke liye band rahegi, aur jo shakhs Makkah se bhag kar Madina aye ga usay wapas karna hoga. Kuch sahaba ko ye shartein sakht mehsoos hui. Lekin Allah ne is sulah ko Surah Al-Fath me Fatah-e-Mubeen, yani khuli fatah, qarar diya. Aur waqai aisa hi hua. Jang band hone se aman mila, dawat ka kaam puray Arab me khul kar hone laga, aur agle do saalon me musalmano ki tadaad is se pehle ke poore arse se zyada barh gayi. Paper ka trap yahan ye hai ke Hudaybiyyah koi jang nahi thi, sulah thi, aur tadaad 1400 thi. Aur khaas baat ye ke yehi sulah do saal baad Fatah Makkah ki buniyad bani, kyunke Quraish ne khud isay tora tha.

Ghazwa-e-Khaybar, qile jo aik aik karke gire

7 Hijri me Aap Khaybar ki taraf gaye. Khaybar Madina ke shimal me Yahoodion ke mazboot qilon ka ilaqa tha, jahan se Madina ke khilaf sazishain hoti rehti thin. Musalmano ki tadaad 1400 ke qareeb thi. Qile aik aik karke fatah hue. Aik mashhoor waqia ye hai ke Aap ne farmaya, kal main alam us shakhs ko dunga jis se Allah aur us ka Rasool mohabbat karte hain. Agle din alam Hazrat Ali ko diya gaya, halanke un ki ankhain dukh rahi thin. Hazrat Ali ne Khaybar ke mashhoor pehelwan Marhab ko muqable me qatal kiya aur qila fatah ho gaya. Nateeje me Khaybar ki paidawar ka aadha hissa musalmano ko milne ka muahida hua. Paper me Khaybar se do sawal fix hain: alam kis ko mila, Hazrat Ali ko, aur Marhab ko kis ne qatal kiya, Hazrat Ali ne.

Fatah Makkah, fatah jis me khoon nahi baha

8 Hijri me Quraish aur un ke ittehadiyon ne Sulah Hudaybiyyah tod di. Aap das hazar ka lashkar lekar Makkah rawana hue. Tadaad phir yaad rakho: das hazar. Itna bara lashkar dekh kar Makkah walo ki himmat jawab de gayi. Abu Sufyan Aap ki khidmat me hazir hua aur musalman ho gaya. Aap ne elaan farmaya ke jo Abu Sufyan ke ghar chala jaye wo mehfooz, jo apna darwaza band kar le wo mehfooz, jo Masjid-e-Haram me chala jaye wo mehfooz. Sheher takreeban baghair jang ke fatah ho gaya. Phir wo lamha aya jis ka duniya intezar karti thi. Wahi Quraish samne the jinho ne Aap ko aur sahaba ko barson sataya tha, aur badla lena Aap ke ikhtiyar me tha. Lekin Aap ne aam maafi ka elaan kar diya aur farmaya, aaj tum par koi ilzam nahi. Kaaba ko buton se paak kiya gaya. Ye duniya ki tareekh ki wo fatah hai jis me fateh ne badle ki bajaye maafi di. Isi liye Fatah Makkah ka sawal har paper me ata hai: 8 Hijri, Ramzan ka mahina, tadaad das hazar, aur aam maafi.

Ghazwa-e-Hunayn, jab tadaad par fakhar mehnga para

Fatah Makkah ke foran baad, 8 Hijri me hi, Hawazin aur Saqeef qabail bara lashkar lekar Wadi-e-Hunayn me jama hue. Musalmano ki tadaad is dafa bara hazar thi, sab se zyada. Kuch logon ke munh se nikal gaya ke aaj hamein kaun hara sakta hai. Subah ke dhundalke me dushman ne pahari darron se achanak teeron ki barish kar di. Musalman muntashir ho gaye, safain toot gayin. Us naazuk waqt me Aap apni jagah sabit qadam rahe aur buland awaz se pukara ke main Allah ka Rasool hun, ye jhoot nahi. Hazrat Abbas ki awaz par sahaba lotne lage, safain dobara bandhin, aur phir poori taqat se hamla hua to dushman ko shikast hui. Is jang ka sabaq Quran ne bhi bayan kiya ke Hunayn ke din tumhari kasrat ne tumhe maghroor kar diya tha, lekin wo kaam na ayi. Paper me Hunayn se yehi sabaq aur Fatah Makkah ke baad hone wali jang ka naam poocha jata hai.

Taif ka muhasra

Hunayn ke baad Aap Taif gaye, jahan Banu Saqeef qila-band ho gaye the. Ye wohi Taif hai jahan barson pehle Aap ko pathar mare gaye the. Muhasra takreeban bees din raha, lekin qila mazboot tha aur andar khana peena mojood tha. Aap ne muhasra utha liya aur Taif walo ke liye hidayat ki dua ki. Nateeja ye hua ke kuch arse baad Taif ka wafd khud Madina aya aur musalman ho gaya. Ye waqia sikhata hai ke har muamla talwar se nahi hota, kuch dil waqt se jeete jate hain. Paper me ye mukhtasar sawal banta hai ke Hunayn ke baad konsi muhim hui: Taif.

Ghazwa-e-Tabuk, aakhri ghazwa jahan jang hui hi nahi

9 Hijri me khabar ayi ke Room ki saltanat, yani Byzantine Empire, Sham ki sarhad par bara lashkar jama kar rahi hai. Mausam sakht garmi ka tha, faslain pak chuki thin, safar lamba tha. Aap ne tees hazar ka lashkar tayyar kiya, jo us waqt tak ka sab se bara Islami lashkar tha. Isay Jaysh-ul-Usra, yani tangi ka lashkar, kaha jata hai, kyunke sawari aur kharch ki sakht kami thi. Sahaba ne dil khol kar maal diya: Hazrat Usman ne bohat bara hissa diya, Hazrat Abu Bakr apna saara maal le aye, Hazrat Umar aadha le aye. Lashkar Tabuk tak gaya, wahan takreeban bees din qayam raha, lekin Roomi lashkar muqable par aya hi nahi. Baghair jang ke wapas aye, lekin is safar ne munafiqeen ko benaqab kar diya aur Arab ke shimali ilaqon me Islam ka roab qaim ho gaya. Paper ki khaas baat: Tabuk Aap ka aakhri ghazwa hai, tadaad tees hazar, aur yahan koi jang nahi hui thi. Ye teenon baatein aik saath yaad kar lo.

Jang-e-Mu'ta, sariya jo ghazwa se zyada mashhoor hai

Ab wo waqia jo paper ka sab se pyara trap hai. 8 Hijri me Aap ne apne safeer ko Busra ke hukmaran ki taraf bheja, jise raaste me qatal kar diya gaya. Safeer ka qatal us dor me jang ka elaan samjha jata tha. Aap ne teen hazar ka lashkar tayyar kiya aur use Mu'ta ki taraf bheja, jo aaj ke Jordan me hai. Lekin khaas baat suno: is muhim me Aap khud shareek nahi hue, is liye ye ghazwa nahi, sariya hai. Wahan dushman ki tadaad musalmano se kahin zyada thi, riwayat me aik lakh ke qareeb bayan hoti hai. Aap ne pehle se teen salar tartib se muqarrar kar diye the: pehle Hazrat Zaid bin Harisa, wo shaheed hon to Hazrat Jafar bin Abi Talib, wo shaheed hon to Hazrat Abdullah bin Rawaha. Maidan me teeno isi tartib se shaheed hue. Hazrat Jafar ke dono bazu kat gaye, is liye unhain Jafar-e-Tayyar aur Zul-Janahain, yani do paron wala, kaha jata hai, kyunke Allah ne unhain jannat me do par ata farmaye. Teeno salar shaheed hue to lashkar ne alam Hazrat Khalid bin Waleed ko diya. Unho ne aisi hikmat se lashkar ko ladhaya aur mehfooz wapas nikala ke Aap ne unhain Saifullah, yani Allah ki talwar, ka laqab diya. Ye tartib, teeno salar ke naam aur un ki shahadat ka silsila, paper me lazmi poocha jata hai.

Saal-war tartib, aakhir me aik nazar

Ab poori kahani ko saalon ki tartib me dohra lo. 2 Hijri me Badr hua, pehli faisla-kun fatah. 3 Hijri me Uhud hua, jahan nafarmani ka sabaq mila. 4 Hijri me Banu Nazeer ki muhim hui. 5 Hijri me Khandaq aur us ke baad Banu Qurayza hue. 6 Hijri me Sulah Hudaybiyyah hui, jo jang nahi sulah thi. 7 Hijri me Khaybar fatah hua. 8 Hijri sab se masroof saal tha: Mu'ta ka sariya, phir Fatah Makkah, phir Hunayn aur Taif. 9 Hijri me Tabuk hua, jo aakhri ghazwa tha. 10 Hijri ke baad koi ghazwa nahi hua, kyunke Arab ka aksar hissa Islam ke saaye me aa chuka tha. Kul mashhoor tadaad 27 ghazwaat hai, jin me se in baron ki kahani tum ne parh li. Agar paper me sirf ye tartib aur har jang ki aik khaas baat yaad ho, to ghazwaat ke taqreeban saare sawal hal ho jate hain.

Tasveer ka khayal: Poore lesson ke liye aik naqsha-numa tasveer sab se kaam ki hogi jis me Madina darmiyan me ho aur us ke gird Badr, Uhud, Khandaq, Khaybar, Makkah, Hunayn, Taif aur Tabuk ki simtain aur faaslay dikhaye gaye hon, har jagah ke sath us ka Hijri saal likha ho. Student naqsha dekh kar hi samajh jaye ke konsi jang kidhar hui aur kis tartib me hui.
