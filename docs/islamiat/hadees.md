Hadees — Tareef, Aqsaam aur Mashhoor Kitabein
حدیث کی pehchan, us ki qismein, aur woh chhe kitabein jin se paper banta hai

Ye topic kyun zaroori hai

Quran ke baad one paper me sab se zyada sawal Hadees se ate hain. Kabhi poocha jata hai ke hadees ki tareef kya hai, kabhi ye ke Sihah Sitta me konsi kitabein shamil hain, aur kabhi ye ke sab se mustanad kitab hadees konsi hai. Ye sawal un students ko phansate hain jo hadees ko bas aik lafz samajh kar agay barh jate hain. Asal baat ye hai ke hadees apne andar aik poora nizam rakhti hai. Us ke hisse hain, us ki qismein hain, use parakhne ka aik ilm hai, aur phir woh mashhoor kitabein hain jin me use mehfooz kiya gaya. Is lesson me hum ye saara nizam aik tartib se bithaenge, taake paper me hadees ka sawal jis pehlu se bhi aye, tum use pehchan lo.

Hadees ki tareef pehle samajh lo

Hadees kise kehte hain, ye baat teen lafzon me band hai. Huzoor صلی اللہ علیہ وسلم ka qawl, aap ka fail, aur aap ki taqreer. Qawl ka matlab hai aap ki farmayi hui baat. Fail ka matlab hai aap ka kiya hua koi amal. Aur taqreer wo cheez hai jo aksar students se reh jati hai. Taqreer ka matlab hai ke koi kaam aap ke samne kiya gaya aur aap ne use dekh kar khamoshi ikhtiyar ki ya use barqarar rakha, yani aap ki khamoshi hi us kaam ki manzoori ban gayi. To jab bhi hadees ki tareef poochi jaye to ye teenon hisse lazmi bayan karo. Qawl, fail aur taqreer. Teesra hissa bhoolna hi paper me number katwane ke barabar hai.

Har hadees ke do hisse hote hain

Ab hadees ki banawat samjho. Har hadees do hison par mushtamil hoti hai. Pehla hissa hai سند, sanad. Sanad woh zanjeer hai un logon ki jin ke zariye ye hadees hum tak pohchi. Falan ne falan se suna, unhon ne falan se suna, is tarah ye silsila chalte chalte Huzoor صلی اللہ علیہ وسلم tak pohchta hai. Dusra hissa hai متن, matan. Matan woh asal ibaraat hai jo hadees ka mazmoon hai, yani woh baat jo bayan ki gayi. Aik asaan misaal se samjho. Sanad woh rasta hai jis se khabar ayi, aur matan woh khabar khud hai. Paper me sanad aur matan ki tareef alag alag bhi ati hai aur dono ka farq bhi poocha jata hai. Jo shakhs hadees bayan karta hai use راوی, ravi kehte hain, aur us ke bayan karne ke amal ko riwayat kehte hain. Ravi aur riwayat, ye jora bhi yahin yaad kar lo.

Hadees-e-Qudsi aur Hadees-e-Nabawi ka farq

Ab woh farq ata hai jo paper ka sab se fix trap hai, aur jis par har saal sawal banta hai. Hadees ki aik qism hai حدیثِ قدسی, Hadees-e-Qudsi. Qudsi woh hadees hai jis ka maana aur mazmoon Allah Taala ki taraf se hota hai, lekin alfaaz Huzoor صلی اللہ علیہ وسلم ke hote hain. Yani baat Allah ki, bayan Nabi ka. Is me Huzoor صلی اللہ علیہ وسلم baat ko Allah ki taraf mansoob kar ke bayan farmate hain, ke Allah Taala farmata hai. Dusri taraf حدیثِ نبوی hai. Nabawi hadees woh hai jo Huzoor صلی اللہ علیہ وسلم ka apna qawl, fail ya taqreer ho, aur use aap apni taraf se bayan farmayein. To farq aik line me ye hai. Qudsi me maana Allah ka aur alfaaz Nabi ke, Nabawi me qawl o amal Nabi hi ka. Aur aik baat yahan wazeh kar dun ke Hadees-e-Qudsi Quran nahi hai. Quran woh wahi hai jis ke alfaaz bhi Allah ke hain aur maana bhi, jabke Qudsi me alfaaz Nabi ke hain. Ye bareek farq hi paper ka asal imtihan hai.

Sanad ke hisab se hadees ki aqsaam

Hadees ko is buniyad par bhi taqseem kiya jata hai ke woh hum tak kitne ravioun ke zariye pohchi. Pehli qism hai Mutawatir. Mutawatir woh hadees hai jise har zamane me itni barri tadaad me ravioun ne bayan kiya ho ke un sab ka jhoot par ikatha hona mumkin hi na ho. Aisi hadees ka ilm yaqeeni hota hai. Dusri qism hai Ahad. Ahad woh hadees hai jis ke bayan karne wale ravioun ki tadaad Mutawatir ki had tak na pohchi ho, chahe woh aik ravi ho ya chand ravi. Aam kitabein hadees ki aksar Ahad hi hoti hain. Paper ke liye itna pakka kar lo ke tadaad ki kasrat aur yaqeen wali hadees Mutawatir hai, aur is se kam darje wali Ahad.

Sihah Sitta kya hai

Ab hum un kitabon ki taraf ate hain jin par Islamiat ka paper khara hota hai. Hadees ki chhe sab se mustanad aur mashhoor kitabon ko ملا kar صحاحِ ستہ, Sihah Sitta kaha jata hai. Sitta ka matlab hai chhe. Ye chhe kitabein hain, Sahih Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, Sunan Abu Dawood, Jami Tirmizi, Sunan Nasa'i aur Sunan Ibn Majah. In chhe ke naam aur in ke musannifeen ke naam aik sath ratne ki bajaaye samajh kar yaad karo, kyunke paper kabhi kitab de kar musannif poochta hai aur kabhi musannif de kar kitab. Ab hum har kitab ko bari bari dekhte hain.

Sahih Bukhari, sab se mustanad kitab

Pehli aur sab se buland kitab hai صحیح بخاری, Sahih Bukhari. Is ke musannif hain Imam Muhammad bin Ismail Bukhari رحمۃ اللہ علیہ. Imam Bukhari ne hadees ki talash me door door ke safar kiye aur aik riwayat ke mutabiq unhon ne takreeban chhe lakh, yani 600,000 ahadees se intikhab kar ke apni kitab tayyar ki. Kitab me takrar ke sath takreeban 7,275 ahadees hain, aur takrar nikalo to ye tadaad takreeban 2,602 reh jati hai. Imam Bukhari ne har hadees ke liye sanad ki sakht tareen shartein rakhin, isiliye un ki kitab ko Quran ke baad sab se mustanad aur sab se sahi kitab mana jata hai, aur ise اصح الکتب بعد کتاب اللہ bhi kaha jata hai. Paper ka sab se seedha sawal yahin se ata hai. Sab se mustanad kitab hadees konsi hai, jawab Sahih Bukhari.

Sahih Muslim, dusri mustanad kitab

Dusri kitab hai صحیح مسلم, Sahih Muslim. Is ke musannif hain Imam Muslim bin Hajjaj رحمۃ اللہ علیہ, jo Nishapur ke rehne wale thay. Imam Muslim ne bhi apni kitab me sirf wohi ahadees rakhin jin ki sanad un ki sakht sharton par poori utri. Tartib aur tasnif ke aitbar se Sahih Muslim ko khaas maqam hasil hai. Bukhari aur Muslim dono ko mila kar صحیحین, Sahihain kaha jata hai, yani woh do sahi kitabein. Sahihain ka lafz paper me ata hai, aur is se murad hamesha yehi do kitabein hoti hain, Bukhari aur Muslim.

Baqi chaar kitabein

Teesri kitab hai سنن ابی داؤد, Sunan Abu Dawood, jis ke musannif Imam Abu Dawood Sulaiman bin Ash'as hain. Is kitab me khaas tor par woh ahadees jama ki gayin jin se roz marra ke fiqhi masail aur ahkam nikalte hain. Chauthi kitab hai جامع ترمذی, Jami Tirmizi, jis ke musannif Imam Muhammad bin Isa Tirmizi hain. Imam Tirmizi ki kitab ki pehchan ye hai ke woh har hadees ke sath us ka darja bhi bayan kar dete hain, ke ye hadees sahih hai, hasan hai ya zaeef hai. Panchwin kitab hai سنن نسائی, Sunan Nasa'i, jis ke musannif Imam Ahmad bin Shu'aib Nasa'i hain. In ki kitab sanad ki jaanch me apni sakhti ki wajah se mashhoor hai. Chhati kitab hai سنن ابن ماجہ, Sunan Ibn Majah, jis ke musannif Imam Muhammad bin Yazid Ibn Majah hain. In chhe kitabon ko mila kar Sihah Sitta banta hai. Tartib yaad rakhne ka asaan tareeqa ye hai ke pehle do Sahih hain, Bukhari aur Muslim, aur baqi chaar Sunan aur Jami hain, Abu Dawood, Tirmizi, Nasa'i aur Ibn Majah.

Muwatta Imam Malik, pehli tadween shuda kitab

Ab aik woh kitab jo Sihah Sitta me shamil nahi, lekin jis ke baghair hadees ki tareekh adhoori hai. وہ ہے موطا امام مالک, Muwatta Imam Malik. Imam Malik bin Anas رحمۃ اللہ علیہ Madina Munawwara ke Imam thay. Un ki kitab Muwatta ko hadees ki sab se pehli baqaida tadween shuda mashhoor kitab mana jata hai, yani hadees ko kitab ki surat me jama karne ka woh silsila jo baad ki tamam kitabon ki buniyad bana, us ki mashhoor tareen ibtida yahin se hoti hai. Sihah Sitta wali kitabein is ke baad ke zamane ki hain. Paper me jab poocha jaye ke hadees ki pehli tadween shuda kitab konsi hai to jawab Muwatta Imam Malik hai, aur jab sab se mustanad poochi jaye to jawab Sahih Bukhari hai. In dono sawalon ko uljhana hi sab se barri ghalti hai, dono ke jawab alag alag bitha lo.

Hadees parakhi kesay jati hai

Aakhir me ye samajh lo ke kisi hadees ko sahi ya zaeef kesay kaha jata hai. Is kaam ka buniyadi ilm hai علم الرجال, Ilm-e-Rijal, yani ravioun ke halaat ka ilm. Is ilm me har ravi ki zindagi ko parkha jata hai. Woh sachha tha ya nahi, us ki yaaddasht kesi thi, us ne hadees kis se suni aur us ke ustad se us ki mulaqat sabit hai ya nahi. Sanad ki har kari ko is tarah jaanch kar muhaddiseen hadees ka darja tay karte thay. Yahi wajah hai ke Imam Bukhari jaise muhaddiseen ne lakhon ahadees me se sirf chand hazar ka intikhab kiya. To hadees ki jaanch ka ilm Ilm-e-Rijal hai, aur jaanch ka markaz sanad ke ravi hote hain.

Paper ke fix facts ki recap

Ab saari baatain aik jagah dohra lo. Hadees ki tareef me teen hisse hain, qawl, fail aur taqreer, aur taqreer ka matlab hai samne kiye gaye kaam par khamoshi ikhtiyar kar lena. Har hadees ke do hisse hote hain, sanad jo ravioun ki zanjeer hai, aur matan jo asal mazmoon hai. Hadees bayan karne wale ko ravi kehte hain. Hadees-e-Qudsi me maana Allah ki taraf se aur alfaaz Nabi ke hote hain, jabke Hadees-e-Nabawi Nabi ka apna qawl o amal hai, aur Qudsi Quran nahi hai. Sanad ke hisab se kasrat wali yaqeeni hadees Mutawatir hai aur is se kam Ahad. Sihah Sitta chhe kitabein hain, Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawood, Tirmizi, Nasa'i aur Ibn Majah. Sahih Bukhari sab se mustanad kitab hai, is ke musannif Imam Muhammad bin Ismail Bukhari hain, aur unhon ne takreeban chhe lakh ahadees se intikhab kiya. Sahih Muslim ke musannif Imam Muslim bin Hajjaj hain, aur Bukhari o Muslim ko mila kar Sahihain kehte hain. Hadees ki pehli tadween shuda mashhoor kitab Muwatta Imam Malik hai. Aur hadees ki jaanch ka ilm Ilm-e-Rijal hai. Ye baatain pakki kar lo to hadees ka har sawal tumhare liye asaan ho jayega.
