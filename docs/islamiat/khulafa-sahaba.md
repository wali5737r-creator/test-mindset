Khulafa-e-Rashideen aur Mashhoor Sahaba
Nabi Kareem Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ke baad ke sab se bare naam, aur paper ke fix sawal

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me Islamiat ka hissa kabhi khali nahi jata, aur us hisse me Khulafa-e-Rashideen aur Sahaba par sawal lagbhag pakka hota hai. Kabhi poocha jata hai ke pehle khalifa kaun the, kabhi ye ke Zun-Noorain kis ka laqab hai, kabhi ye ke sab se zyada hadees kis sahabi se riwayat hain. Ye sawal mushkil nahi hote, bas naam, laqab aur karnaama teeno ko jor kar yaad karna hota hai. Is lesson me hum chaaron Khulafa ko tartib se parhen ge, phir un das sahaba ko jinhe duniya me jannat ki basharat mili, aur phir un mashhoor sahaba ko jin par paper baar baar sawal banata hai. Aik baat pehle din se zehen me bitha lo. Khilafat ki tartib wahi hai jo ummat ki tareekh me rahi: pehle Hazrat Abu Bakr, phir Hazrat Umar, phir Hazrat Usman, phir Hazrat Ali, Radi Allahu Anhum ajmaeen. Tartib ulta karna paper ki sab se purani ghalti hai.

Pehle ye samajh lo, khalifa kehte kis ko hain

Nabi Kareem Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ke wisaal ke baad ummat ki rehnumai ke liye jo shakhs muntakhab hua use khalifa kehte hain. Khalifa ka kaam deen ki hifazat, ummat ki rehnumai aur riyasat ka intezam tha. Pehle chaar khulafa ko Khulafa-e-Rashideen kaha jata hai, kyunke unhon ne Nabi Kareem Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ke tareeqe par khilafat chalai. Rashideen ka matlab hai hidayat yafta. In chaaron ka majmooi dor takreeban tees saal banta hai, aur ye tees saal ka number bhi paper me poocha jata hai.

Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique Radi Allahu Anhu

Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique ummat ke pehle khalifa hain. Aap ka asal naam Abdullah tha, Abu Bakr kunniyat thi, aur Siddique aur Ateeq aap ke mashhoor laqab hain. Siddique ka matlab hai bohat zyada sach bolne wala aur har baat ki tasdeeq karne wala. Jab Nabi Kareem Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ne waaqia-e-Miraj bayan farmaya to kafiron ne mazaaq uraya, lekin Hazrat Abu Bakr ne bila jhijhak kaha ke agar Aap ne farmaya hai to sach farmaya hai. Isi tasdeeq par aap ko Siddique ka laqab mila. Ateeq ka matlab hai azad karda, aur ye laqab bhi aap ko Nabi Kareem Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ki zubaani ata hua. Hijrat ke safar me aap hi Nabi Kareem Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ke rafeeq the, Ghar-e-Saur me teeno din aap sath rahe, aur Quran ne aap ko saaniya isnain, yani do me se dusra, keh kar zikr kiya. Aap Nabi Kareem Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ke sasur bhi the, Hazrat Ayesha Radi Allahu Anha aap ki beti hain.

Aap ki muddat-e-khilafat sirf do saal teen maah hai, ye Khulafa-e-Rashideen me sab se kam muddat hai, lekin kaam ke hisaab se ye dor bohat bhaari hai. Nabi Kareem Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ke wisaal ke baad Arab me bohat se qabail murtad ho gaye, kuch ne jhoote nabiyon ka sath diya aur kuch ne zakat dene se inkar kar diya. Hazrat Abu Bakr ne poori sakhti aur hikmat se in fitnon ka muqabla kiya. Aap ka mashhoor jumla hai ke agar log aik rassi ke barabar bhi zakat roken ge to main un se jang karunga. Ye jangain tareekh me Hurub-e-Ridda ke naam se mashhoor hain. Dusra bara faisla Hazrat Usama ke lashkar ki rawanagi tha. Ye lashkar Nabi Kareem Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ne apni zindagi me tayyar kiya tha lekin rawana na ho saka tha. Haalaat bohat nazuk the, sahaba ne mashwara diya ke lashkar rok liya jaye, lekin Hazrat Abu Bakr ne farmaya ke jo lashkar Nabi Kareem Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ne bandha hai use Abu Bakr nahi khole ga, aur lashkar rawana kar diya.

Aap ka sab se bara karnaama Quran Majid ki pehli jama hai. Jang-e-Yamama me bohat se huffaz sahaba shaheed ho gaye to Hazrat Umar ke mashware par aap ne Hazrat Zaid bin Sabit ki sarbarahi me Quran ko aik jagah jama karwaya. Us waqt Quran mukammal taur par mukhtalif sahaba ke paas mehfooz tha, lekin aik musalsal nuskhe ki surat me pehli dafa isi dor me murattab hua, aur ye nuskha Hazrat Abu Bakr ke paas raha, phir Hazrat Umar aur phir Hazrat Hafsa Radi Allahu Anha ke paas mehfooz raha. Isi liye Hazrat Abu Bakr ko Jami-e-Quran bhi kaha jata hai. Aap ka wisaal 13 Hijri me hua aur aap Nabi Kareem Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ke pehlu me, Hujra-e-Ayesha me madfoon hain. Paper wale fix facts ye hain: pehle khalifa, muddat do saal teen maah, laqab Siddique aur Ateeq, Usama ke lashkar ki rawanagi, murtadeen aur zakat se inkar karne walon ke khilaf jang, aur Quran ki pehli jama.

Hazrat Umar Farooq Radi Allahu Anhu

Hazrat Umar Farooq dusre khalifa hain aur aap ki muddat-e-khilafat dus saal hai. Farooq aap ka laqab hai, jis ka matlab hai haq aur baatil me farq karne wala. Ye laqab aap ko Nabi Kareem Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ne ata farmaya tha. Aap ke Islam laane se musalmanon ko khule aam ibadat ki himmat mili, is liye aap ke Islam ko tareekh me aik mod mana jata hai. Ameer-ul-Momineen ka laqab sab se pehle Hazrat Umar ke liye istemal hua. Aap se pehle khalifa ko Khalifat-ur-Rasool kaha jata tha, lekin aap ke dor me ye laqab riwaj ban gaya aur baad ke tamam khulafa ke liye yahi istemal hota raha. Ye point paper me aksar poocha jata hai, isay khaas yaad rakho.

Aap ka dor futoohat aur intezam ka dor hai. Aap ke zamane me Sham, Iraq, Faras aur Misr fatah hue. Bait-ul-Maqdis yani Yaroshalem bhi aap ke dor me fatah hua aur aap khud wahan tashreef le gaye. Jang-e-Yarmook me Roomi taaqat ka zor toota aur Jang-e-Qadisiyya me Faras ki hukumat khatam hui. Lekin Hazrat Umar ki asal pehchan sirf futoohat nahi, intezam hai. Aap ne adalati nizam qayam kiya aur shehron me qaazi muqarrar kiye. Bait-ul-Maal ko baqaida mehfooz khazane ki surat di aur us ka hisaab kitab rakha gaya. Fauj ke liye daftar aur tankhwahon ka nizam banaya, nayi aabadiyon me Basra aur Kufa jaise sheher abad kiye, nahrain khudwayen, police ka shoba banaya aur logon ki shikayat sunne ka baqaida intezam kiya. Ramzan me taraweeh ko baqaida jamaat ke sath parhne ka riwaj bhi aap ke dor me qayam hua.

Hijri calendar ka point paper ka pasandeeda hai aur yahi wo jagah hai jahan aksar students phisalte hain. Baat yun hai ke Hijri saal ki buniyad Hazrat Umar ke dor me rakhi gayi, taake sarkari kagazaat aur tareekhon ka hisaab theek rahe. Saal ka aaghaz kis waaqiye se ho, is par sahaba se mashwara hua, aur Hazrat Ali Radi Allahu Anhu ke mashware par ye tay paya ke saal ka aaghaz Nabi Kareem Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ki Hijrat se ho, aur pehla maheena Muharram rakha gaya. To jawab yun yaad karo: calendar Hazrat Umar ke dor ki yadgaar hai, aur us ke aaghaz ka mashwara Hazrat Ali ka tha. Aap 23 Hijri me aik ghulam Abu Lulu ke hamle se zakhmi hue aur shaheed ho gaye. Aap bhi Hujra-e-Ayesha me Nabi Kareem Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam aur Hazrat Abu Bakr ke pehlu me madfoon hain. Paper wale fix facts: dusre khalifa, muddat dus saal, laqab Farooq, Ameer-ul-Momineen ka laqab sab se pehle aap ke liye, Sham Faras aur Misr ki futoohat, Hijri calendar ki buniyad, adalati nizam aur Bait-ul-Maal ki tarteeb.

Hazrat Usman Ghani Radi Allahu Anhu

Hazrat Usman Ghani teesre khalifa hain aur aap ki muddat-e-khilafat baarah saal hai. Aap ko Zun-Noorain kaha jata hai, jis ka matlab hai do noor wala. Is laqab ki wajah bohat khoobsurat hai. Aap ke nikah me pehle Nabi Kareem Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ki sahibzadi Hazrat Ruqayya Radi Allahu Anha aayen, aur un ke wisaal ke baad dusri sahibzadi Hazrat Umm-e-Kulsoom Radi Allahu Anha aap ke nikah me aayen. Yani aik ke baad dusri, Nabi Kareem Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ki do betiyan aap ke nikah me rahen, isi nisbat se aap do noor wale kahlaye. Ye wajah paper me sawal ban kar ati hai, sirf laqab ratna kafi nahi, wajah bhi yaad rakho. Ghani ka laqab aap ki sakhawat ki wajah se hai. Jang-e-Tabook ke liye aap ne itna maal diya ke Nabi Kareem Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam khush ho gaye. Madina me jab meethe pani ki qillat thi to aap ne Beer-e-Ruma ka kuan khareed kar musalmanon ke liye waqf kar diya.

Aap ka sab se bara karnaama Quran Majid ka standard nuskha hai. Baat ye thi ke jab Islam door daraz ke ilaaqon tak phaila to mukhtalif ilaaqon me Quran ki qiraat me mamuli farq mehsoos hone laga. Hazrat Huzaifa bin Yaman ne is khatre ki taraf tawajjo dilai. Hazrat Usman ne Hazrat Hafsa ke paas mehfooz asal nuskhe ko mangwa kar Hazrat Zaid bin Sabit ki sarbarahi me aik committee se us ki baqaida naqlein tayyar karwayen, aur ye standard nuskhe tamam bare shehron me bhej diye gaye taake poori ummat aik hi nuskhe par muttafiq rahe. Ye nuskha Nuskha-e-Usmani ke naam se mashhoor hai, aur aaj duniya bhar me jo Quran parha jata hai wahi nuskha hai. Isi khidmat ki wajah se Hazrat Usman ko bhi Jami-e-Quran kaha jata hai. Farq yun yaad karo: Hazrat Abu Bakr ke dor me Quran pehli dafa aik jagah jama hua, Hazrat Usman ke dor me us ka aik standard nuskha poori ummat ke liye tayyar hua. Aap ke dor me Islam ki pehli baqaida bahri fouj yani samandari bera tayyar hua, jis ne Qubrus (Cyprus) ko fatah kiya. Ye Islami tareekh ki pehli navy hai, ye point bhi paper me ata hai. Aap 35 Hijri me shaheed hue, aur aap ki shahadat ke waqt aap Quran ki tilawat farma rahe the. Paper wale fix facts: teesre khalifa, muddat baarah saal, laqab Zun-Noorain aur Ghani, Quran ka standard nuskha, pehli bahri fouj, Beer-e-Ruma ki waqf.

Hazrat Ali Radi Allahu Anhu

Hazrat Ali chauthe khalifa hain. Aap Nabi Kareem Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ke chacha zaad bhai bhi hain aur damad bhi, kyunke aap ka nikah Khatoon-e-Jannat Hazrat Fatima Radi Allahu Anha se hua. Bacchon me sab se pehle Islam aap hi ne qabool kiya. Hijrat ki raat aap Nabi Kareem Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ke bistar par lete taake dushman dhoka khaye, ye waaqia aap ki jaan nisari ki sab se bari misaal hai. Aap apni bahaduri ki wajah se Asadullah, yani Allah ke sher, aur Haider ke laqab se mashhoor hain. Jang-e-Khaibar me aap ke haathon qila fatah hua aur Marhab ka muqabla tareekh ka mashhoor waaqia hai.

Ilm me aap ka darja bohat buland hai. Nabi Kareem Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ka mashhoor farmaan hai ke main ilm ka sheher hun aur Ali us ka darwaza hai. Isi liye aap ko Bab-ul-Ilm kaha jata hai. Aap ke khutbaat aur aqwaal ka majmua Nahj-ul-Balagha ke naam se mashhoor hai. Aap ke dor me dar-ul-hukumat Madina se Kufa muntaqil hua, ye point paper me poocha jata hai. Aap ke dor me do bari jangain huin, Jang-e-Jamal aur Jang-e-Siffin, jo musalmanon ke darmiyan pesh aayen aur tareekh ka hissa hain. Aap 40 Hijri me Ramzan ke maheene me Kufa ki masjid me namaz ke liye jate hue hamle me zakhmi hue aur shaheed ho gaye. Paper wale fix facts: chauthe khalifa, dar-ul-hukumat Kufa, Bab-ul-Ilm ka darja, Khatoon-e-Jannat ke shohar, Khaibar ki fatah me kardaar, shahadat 40 Hijri.

Ashra Mubashara, jannat ki basharat wale das sahaba

Ab wo das naam yaad kar lo jin par paper me seedha sawal ata hai ke Ashra Mubashara kin ko kehte hain. Ashra ka matlab hai das aur Mubashara ka matlab hai basharat diye gaye. Ye wo das sahaba hain jin ko Nabi Kareem Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ne duniya hi me jannat ki basharat di. In ke naam tartib se yun yaad karo, pehle chaar to wahi Khulafa-e-Rashideen hain. Pehle Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique, jo ummat ke pehle khalifa hain. Dusre Hazrat Umar Farooq, jo dusre khalifa hain. Teesre Hazrat Usman Ghani, jo Zun-Noorain hain. Chauthe Hazrat Ali, jo Bab-ul-Ilm hain. Panchwen Hazrat Talha bin Ubaidullah, jo Jang-e-Uhud me Nabi Kareem Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ki hifazat me shadeed zakhmi hue the. Chhatay Hazrat Zubair bin Awwam, jo Nabi Kareem Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ke phuphi zaad bhai aur Hawari-e-Rasool ke laqab se mashhoor hain. Saatwen Hazrat Abdur Rahman bin Auf, jo Madina ke bare taajiron me se the aur apni sakhawat me mashhoor hain. Aathwen Hazrat Saad bin Abi Waqqas, jo Uhud ke mashhoor teer andaz aur Qadisiyya ke salar hain. Nawen Hazrat Saeed bin Zaid, jo ibtedai musalmanon me se hain. Daswen Hazrat Abu Ubaidah bin Jarrah, jin ko Nabi Kareem Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ne is ummat ka ameen farmaya tha. Aik aasan trick ye hai ke pehle chaar khulafa yaad kar lo, baqi chhe naam Talha, Zubair, Abdur Rahman, Saad, Saeed aur Abu Ubaidah ki surat me aik saans me dohraya karo, das pure ho jayenge.

Mashhoor sahaba jin par paper sawal banata hai

Ab un sahaba ko aik aik karke parh lo jin ki koi na koi khaas pehchan paper ka sawal banti hai. Har sahabi ke sath us ki pehchan wali baat jor kar yaad karna, sirf naam ratne se number nahi milta.

Hazrat Hamza Radi Allahu Anhu Nabi Kareem Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ke chacha hain aur aap ko Syed-ush-Shuhada, yani shaheedon ke sardar, ka laqab mila. Aap apni taaqat aur bahaduri me poore Makkah me mashhoor the. Jang-e-Badar me aap ne numaya kardaar ada kiya, aur Jang-e-Uhud me aap shaheed hue. Paper me aap ka laqab Syed-ush-Shuhada sab se zyada poocha jata hai.

Hazrat Khalid bin Waleed Radi Allahu Anhu ko Saifullah, yani Allah ki talwaar, ka laqab khud Nabi Kareem Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ne ata farmaya tha. Ye waaqia Jang-e-Mauta ka hai, jahan teeno muqarrar salar yakay baad deegre shaheed ho gaye to Hazrat Khalid ne kamaan sambhali aur fouj ko mehfooz nikala. Aap tareekh ke un salaron me se hain jin hon ne apni zindagi me kisi jang me shikast nahi khayi. Aap ne Khulafa ke dor me Sham aur Iraq ki futoohat me markazi kardaar ada kiya, aur Jang-e-Yarmook aap ki sab se mashhoor fatah hai.

Hazrat Saad bin Abi Waqqas Radi Allahu Anhu Ashra Mubashara me bhi shamil hain. Jang-e-Uhud me aap Nabi Kareem Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ke qareeb khare ho kar teer chalate rahe aur Nabi Kareem Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam aap ko teer dete jate aur farmate jate ke chalao, mere maa baap tum par qurbaan. Ye jumla aap ki sab se bari pehchan hai. Baad me aap Jang-e-Qadisiyya me musalmanon ke sipah salar the, jis me Faras ki taaqat ka khatma hua.

Hazrat Bilal Habshi Radi Allahu Anhu Islam ke pehle muezzin hain. Aap Habash se talluq rakhne wale ghulam the, Islam laane par aap ko shadeed takleefain di gayen lekin aap Ahad Ahad kehte rahe. Hazrat Abu Bakr ne aap ko khareed kar azad karwaya. Madina me jab azan ka nizam bana to pehli azan aap hi ne di, aur Fatah-e-Makkah ke din Kaaba ki chhat par azan dene ka sharaf bhi aap hi ko mila.

Hazrat Salman Farsi Radi Allahu Anhu ka talluq Faras yani Iran se tha. Aap haq ki talash me lamba safar karke Madina pahunche aur Islam qabool kiya. Jang-e-Khandaq ke mauqe par Madina ke gird khandaq khodne ka mashwara aap hi ne diya tha, kyunke Faras me dafai jang ka ye tareeqa mashhoor tha. Isi mashware par jang ka naam hi Khandaq par gaya. Nabi Kareem Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ne aap ko apne ahl-e-bait me se qarar diya.

Hazrat Abu Huraira Radi Allahu Anhu sab se zyada hadees riwayat karne wale sahabi hain. Aap se riwayat karda ahadees ki mashhoor tadaad 5374 hai, ye number paper me jyon ka tyon poocha jata hai is liye gin kar yaad rakho, panch hazar teen so chauhatar. Aap ka asal naam Abdur Rahman bin Sakhr hai. Abu Huraira kunniyat ki wajah ye mashhoor hai ke aap aik billi ke bacche se bohat pyaar karte the aur use sath rakhte the, Huraira ka matlab hai chhoti billi. Aap Suffa ke ashab me se the aur apna tamam waqt Nabi Kareem Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ki sohbat aur hadees ki hifazat ke liye waqf kar diya tha.

Hazrat Zaid bin Sabit Radi Allahu Anhu katib-e-wahy the, yani jab Quran nazil hota to use likhne walon me aap sar-e-fahrist the. Hazrat Abu Bakr ke dor me jab Quran ko aik jagah jama karne ka faisla hua to is azeem kaam ki zimmedari aap hi ko di gayi, aur Hazrat Usman ke dor me standard nuskha tayyar karne wali committee ki sarbarahi bhi aap hi ne ki. To jab bhi sawal aye ke Quran ki jama ki zimmedari kis ko mili, jawab Hazrat Zaid bin Sabit hai.

Hazrat Jafar Tayyar Radi Allahu Anhu Hazrat Ali ke bare bhai hain. Aap ne Habsha ki taraf hijrat ki aur wahan ke badshah Najashi ke darbar me musalmanon ki taraf se wo mashhoor taqreer ki jo tareekh ka hissa ban gayi. Jang-e-Mauta me aap shaheed hue. Aap ne pehle aik haath se alam pakra, wo kata to dusre se, wo bhi kata to baazuon se alam ko girne na diya. Nabi Kareem Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ne farmaya ke Allah ne aap ko un haathon ke badle jannat me do par ata farmaye hain jin se aap farishton ke sath urte hain. Isi liye aap Tayyar aur Zul-Janahain, yani do paron wala, ke laqab se mashhoor hain.

Hazrat Ayesha Radi Allahu Anha Umm-ul-Momineen hain, yani momineen ki maa, aur ye darja tamam Azwaj-e-Mutahharat ko hasil hai. Aap Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique ki sahibzadi hain. Aap se bohat badi tadaad me ahadees riwayat hain aur bare bare sahaba deeni masail me aap se rujoo karte the. Ilm-e-deen ki ashayaat me aap ka maqam bohat buland hai, is liye paper me aap hadees aur fiqh ki riwayat ke hawale se poochi jati hain.

Hazrat Fatima Radi Allahu Anha Nabi Kareem Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ki sab se chhoti aur sab se pyari sahibzadi hain, Khatoon-e-Jannat aap ka mashhoor khitaab hai. Aap ka nikah Hazrat Ali se hua aur Hazrat Hasan aur Hazrat Husain Radi Allahu Anhuma aap hi ke sahibzade hain. Aap ki zindagi saadgi, sabr aur ibadat ki misaal hai.

Aakhir me dohra lo

Ab poore lesson ko aik saans me dohra lo taake paper wale din kuch na bhoole. Khulafa ki tartib Abu Bakr, Umar, Usman, Ali hai. Muddatain do saal teen maah, dus saal, baarah saal, aur Hazrat Ali ka dor un ke baad. Laqab yun joro: Siddique aur Ateeq Abu Bakr ke, Farooq aur Ameer-ul-Momineen Umar ke, Zun-Noorain aur Ghani Usman ke, Asadullah aur Bab-ul-Ilm Ali ke. Karnaame yun joro: Quran ki pehli jama aur Usama ka lashkar Abu Bakr se, Hijri calendar aur adalati nizam Umar se, standard nuskha aur pehli bahri fouj Usman se, Kufa dar-ul-hukumat Ali se. Ashra Mubashara me chaar khulafa ke ilawa Talha, Zubair, Abdur Rahman bin Auf, Saad bin Abi Waqqas, Saeed bin Zaid aur Abu Ubaidah hain. Aur pehchan wale sahaba: Syed-ush-Shuhada Hamza, Saifullah Khalid, Qadisiyya ke salar Saad, pehle muezzin Bilal, Khandaq ka mashwara Salman ka, sab se zyada hadees 5374 Abu Huraira se, jama-e-Quran ki zimmedari Zaid bin Sabit ki, aur do paron wala laqab Jafar Tayyar ka. Ye jor agar pakke ho jayen to Islamiat ke is hisse ka koi sawal tum se nahi nikle ga.
