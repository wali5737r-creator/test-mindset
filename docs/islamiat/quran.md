Quran Majeed — Mukammal Details
قرآن مجید کی woh saari details jo one paper me baar baar poochi jati hain

Ye topic kyun zaroori hai

PPSC aur FPSC ke one paper me Islamiat ka hissa har saal ata hai, aur us hisse me sab se zyada sawal Quran Majeed se bante hain. Kabhi poocha jata hai ke suraton ki tadaad kitni hai, kabhi ye ke sab se lambi surah konsi hai, kabhi ye ke Quran kis ke dor me jama hua. Ye sawal mushkil nahi hote, bas in ke jawab pakke hone chahiyein. Masla ye hai ke bazar me ghumne wale aam numbers me se kuch ghalat hain, aur paper bilkul unhi ghalat numbers ko option me rakh kar phansata hai. Is lesson me hum har number ko us ki sahi surat me bithaenge, aur jahan koi mashhoor ghalat fehmi hai, wahan use pehle se nishan laga kar rakhenge taake paper me tum use dekhte hi pehchan lo.

Pehle Quran ko samajh lo

Quran Majeed Allah Taala ki woh kitab hai jo Hazrat Muhammad صلی اللہ علیہ وسلم par wahi ke zariye nazil hui. Wahi ka matlab hai Allah ka woh paigham jo farishte Hazrat Jibrail علیہ السلام ke zariye Nabi tak pohchaya gaya. Ye kitab aik din me ya aik saal me nahi utri. Is ke nazil hone ka silsila takreeban 23 saal tak chala. Pehle 13 saal Makkah Mukarrama me guzre aur baqi 10 saal Madina Munawwara me. Isi liye hum kehte hain ke Quran ki mukammal wahi 23 saal me nazil hui, aur ye 23 wala number paper ka sab se pyara sawal hai.

Pehli wahi kab aur kahan nazil hui

Ab suno woh baat jahan se saari kahani shuru hoti hai. 610 Eiswi me, Ramzan ke mahine me, Huzoor صلی اللہ علیہ وسلم Makkah ke qareeb Jabal-e-Noor ki aik ghaar me ibadat me mashghool thay. Us ghaar ka naam hai غارِ حرا, Ghar-e-Hira. Wahin Hazrat Jibrail علیہ السلام tashreef laye aur pehli wahi nazil hui. Pehli wahi me سورۃ العلق ki pehli paanch ayaat nazil huin, jin ka pehla lafz hai اِقْرَأ, yani parho. Dekho, Islam ki pehli wahi ka pehla lafz hi ilm ke bare me hai, ye baat paper me bhi ati hai aur zehen me bithane ke liye bhi khoobsurat hai. To teen cheezain aik sath yaad kar lo. Pehli wahi 610 Eiswi, jagah Ghar-e-Hira, aur nazil hone wali ayaat Surah Alaq ki pehli paanch.

Ab numbers ka poora hisaab

Ab hum us hisse me ate hain jahan paper sab se zyada khelta hai. Quran Majeed ki kul suratain 114 hain. Har surah ayaat par mushtamil hoti hai, aur ayat ka matlab hai nishani. Kul ayaat ki tadaad 6236 hai. Yahan ruk kar aik zaroori baat sun lo. Tum ne bazar me aur aam kitabon me 6666 ka number bohat suna hoga. Ye number mashhoor zaroor hai lekin durust nahi hai. Mustanad tadaad 6236 hai. Paper hamesha 6666 ko option me rakhta hai taake woh student phanse jo suna sunaya number likhta hai. Aaj ke baad tumhare zehen me sirf 6236 rahega.

Quran ko 30 barabar hison me taqseem kiya gaya hai, har hisse ko para kehte hain aur Arabic me isay juz kehte hain. Kul paray 30 hain. Tilawat ki asani ke liye Quran ko 7 manzilon me bhi taqseem kiya gaya hai, taake aik haftay me poora Quran khatam ho sake. Manzil ki tadaad 7 hai. Is ke ilawa har surah ke andar rukne ki jaghon ko ruku kehte hain, aur kul ruku 540 hain. Aur jin ayaat par sajda wajib hota hai unhe sajda-e-tilawat kehte hain, aur woh ayaat 14 hain. Ab in numbers ko aik saans me dohra lo. Suratain 114, paray 30, ayaat 6236, ruku 540, sajday 14, manzilain 7. Ye cheh numbers paper ki jaan hain.

Makki aur Madani suratain

Quran ki suraton ko un ke nazil hone ke zamane ke hisab se do hison me taqseem kiya jata hai. Jo suratain Hijrat se pehle nazil huin, chahe woh Makkah ke ilawa kahin aur nazil hui hon, woh Makki kahlati hain. Aur jo suratain Hijrat ke baad nazil huin woh Madani kahlati hain. Yahan aik bareek nukta hai jo paper bohat pasand karta hai. Taqseem jagah ki nahi, zamane ki hai. Hijrat se pehle wali Makki, Hijrat ke baad wali Madani, jagah chahe jo bhi ho. Makki suraton me aam tor par tauheed, risalat, akhiraat aur pichli qaumon ke waqiat bayan hote hain, aur in ki ayaat aam tor par chhoti hoti hain. Madani suraton me ibadat, muamilaat, qawaneen aur muasharti ahkam tafseel se ate hain, aur in ki ayaat aam tor par lambi hoti hain.

Tadaad ki baat karo to standard jawab ye hai ke 86 suratain Makki hain aur 28 suratain Madani. Kuch ulama ke nazdeek chand suraton ki taqseem me ikhtilaf hai, is liye baaz kitabon me ye tadaad thori mukhtalif milti hai, lekin paper ke liye standard jawab 86 aur 28 hi hai. Jab bhi sawal aye to yehi do numbers likhne hain.

Sab se lambi aur sab se chhoti surah

Ab do suratain aisi hain jin ke baghair Islamiat ka paper adhoora rehta hai. Quran ki sab se lambi surah hai سورۃ البقرہ, Surah Al-Baqarah. Ye dusra para se shuru hoti hai aur is ki ayaat 286 hain. Baqarah ka matlab hai gaye, aur is surah me Bani Israel ki us gaye ka waqia bayan hua hai jis ki wajah se is ka ye naam para. Aur Quran ki sab se chhoti surah hai سورۃ الکوثر, Surah Al-Kausar, jis ki sirf 3 ayaat hain. Lambi wali ke 286 aur chhoti wali ke 3, ye jora hamesha sath yaad rakho.

Pehli surah aur aakhri wahi

Quran ki tartib me sab se pehli surah hai سورۃ الفاتحہ, Surah Al-Fateha. Ise Umm-ul-Quran yani Quran ki maa bhi kaha jata hai, aur ye har namaz ki har rakat me parhi jati hai. Ab dusri taraf dekho. Aakhri wahi ke bare me standard riwayat ye hai ke sab se aakhir me nazil hone wali ayat Surah Al-Baqarah ki ayat number 281 hai, jis me Allah ki taraf lotne ka zikr hai. Aur aakhri mukammal surah jo nazil hui woh سورۃ النصر, Surah An-Nasr hai. Paper kabhi aakhri ayat poochta hai aur kabhi aakhri surah, dono ka jawab alag alag zehen me rakho. Aakhri ayat Baqarah ki 281, aakhri mukammal surah An-Nasr.

Do suratain jin ke laqab paper me ate hain

Surah Yaseen ko Quran ka dil kaha jata hai. Ye laqab itna mashhoor hai ke paper me seedha poocha jata hai ke Quran ka dil konsi surah hai, jawab سورۃ یٰسین. Dusri taraf آیت الکرسی hai. Ayat-ul-Kursi Surah Al-Baqarah ki ayat number 255 hai, aur ise Quran ki sab se azeem ayat kaha jata hai. Is me Allah Taala ki qudrat aur us ki kursi ki wusat bayan hui hai. To do baatain pakki kar lo. Quran ka dil Surah Yaseen, aur sab se azeem ayat Ayat-ul-Kursi jo Baqarah ki 255 number ayat hai.

Bismillah wala woh sawal jo sab ko phansata hai

Har surah ke shuru me بِسْمِ اللّٰهِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِيْمِ ati hai, lekin aik surah aisi hai jis ke shuru me Bismillah nahi hai. Woh hai سورۃ التوبہ, Surah At-Tauba, Quran ki nawein surah. Is ke bilkul ult, aik surah aisi hai jis me Bismillah do martaba ati hai. Woh hai سورۃ النمل, Surah An-Naml. Aik martaba surah ke shuru me, aur dusri martaba us ayat me jahan Hazrat Sulaiman علیہ السلام ka khat Malika Bilqees ki taraf bheja gaya aur khat ki ibtida Bismillah se hui. To jora yaad rakho. Bina Bismillah wali Tauba, do Bismillah wali Naml.

Huroof-e-Muqattaat kya hain

Baz suraton ke shuru me kuch munqata huroof ate hain, jese الم ya حم. Inhe حروفِ مقطعات kehte hain, yani alag alag parhe jane wale huroof. Jin suraton ke shuru me ye huroof ate hain un ki tadaad 29 hai. In huroof ke asal maani ke bare me ulama ka khayal hai ke in ka ilm Allah Taala hi ke paas hai, lekin paper ke liye jo cheez zaroori hai woh sirf ye hai ke in ka naam Huroof-e-Muqattaat hai aur aisi suratain 29 hain.

Katib-e-Wahy kon thay

Jab koi wahi nazil hoti to Huzoor صلی اللہ علیہ وسلم use foran likhwa dete thay. Jin Sahaba ko ye saadat hasil hui unhe کاتبِ وحی kehte hain, yani wahi likhne wale. Katibeen-e-Wahy me Hazrat Zaid bin Sabit رضی اللہ عنہ ka naam sab se numayan hai, is ke ilawa Khulafa-e-Rashideen aur degar Sahaba bhi is farize ko anjam dete rahe. Us zamane me kagaz aam nahi tha, is liye wahi khajoor ki tehniyon, safed patharon, chamre ke tukron aur hadiyon par likhi jati thi, aur Sahaba use seene me mehfooz bhi karte thay. Katib-e-Wahy ka sawal paper me ata hai, aur is me Hazrat Zaid bin Sabit ka naam sab se aham is liye hai ke aage chal kar Quran ko jama karne ki zimmadari bhi inhi ko mili.

Quran jama kesay hua

Ab woh kahani suno jo paper me do sawalon ki surat me ati hai. Huzoor صلی اللہ علیہ وسلم ke is duniya se tashreef le jane ke baad Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique رضی اللہ عنہ ke dor me Jang-e-Yamama lari gayi. Us jang me bohat se Sahaba shaheed hue jin ke seenon me Quran mehfooz tha. Hazrat Umar رضی اللہ عنہ ko fikr hui ke agar Huffaz is tarah shaheed hote rahe to Quran ke kisi hisse ke zaya hone ka khadsha hai. Unhon ne Hazrat Abu Bakr ko mashwara diya ke Quran ko aik jagah jama kar liya jaye. Is kaam ki zimmadari Hazrat Zaid bin Sabit رضی اللہ عنہ ko di gayi. Unhon ne sakht ehtiyat ke sath, har ayat ko likhe hue nuskhe aur Huffaz ki gawahi se mila kar, poore Quran ko aik mus'haf ki surat me jama kar diya. Ye pehli jam'a thi, aur ye Hazrat Abu Bakr ke dor me hui.

Phir Hazrat Usman غنی رضی اللہ عنہ ke dor me Islam bohat door door tak phail gaya. Mukhtalif ilaqon me qiraat ke farq par ikhtilaf hone laga. Hazrat Usman ne wohi mus'haf jo Hazrat Abu Bakr ke dor me tayyar hua tha, us ki buniyad par Quran ke mustanad nuskhe tayyar karwa kar bare bare shehron me bhijwa diye, taake poori ummat aik hi tartib aur aik hi qiraat par muttahid ho jaye. Isi azeem khidmat ki wajah se Hazrat Usman ko جامع القرآن, Jami-ul-Quran kaha jata hai, yani Quran ko jama karne wale. Ab yahin ruk kar aik aur laqab bhi jor lo. Hazrat Usman ko ذوالنورین, Zun-Noorain bhi kaha jata hai, jis ka matlab hai do nooron wale. Wajah ye hai ke un ke nikah me yakke baad deegre Huzoor صلی اللہ علیہ وسلم ki do sahibzadian, pehle Hazrat Ruqayya رضی اللہ عنہا aur un ke intiqal ke baad Hazrat Umm-e-Kulsoom رضی اللہ عنہا, ayin. To do laqab aur un ke dor alag alag yaad rakho. Pehli jam'a Hazrat Abu Bakr ke dor me, mustanad nuskha Hazrat Usman ke dor me, aur Jami-ul-Quran ka laqab bhi Hazrat Usman hi ka hai.

Quran ke dusre naam

Quran Majeed ko khud Quran ke andar mukhtalif namon se pukara gaya hai, aur paper in namon ko bhi poochta hai. الفرقان, Al-Furqan, yani haq aur batil me farq karne wala. الکتاب, Al-Kitab, yani woh kitab. الذکر, Az-Zikr, yani nasihat aur yaad dehani. الھدیٰ, Al-Huda, yani hidayat. Ye chaar naam Quran hi ke diye hue hain, is liye jab sawal aye ke in me se konsa naam Quran ka hai to ghabrane ki zaroorat nahi, chaaron hi Quran ke naam hain.

Quran me Ambiya ka zikr

Quran Majeed me 25 Ambiya کرام ke naam ke sath zikr aya hai. In me Hazrat Adam, Hazrat Nooh, Hazrat Ibrahim, Hazrat Musa, Hazrat Isa علیہم السلام aur degar Ambiya shamil hain. 25 wala number is liye yaad rakho ke paper me aksar poocha jata hai ke Quran me kul kitne Ambiya ke naam aye hain. Jawab 25 hai.

Paron ke naam kesay bane

Aakhir me aik dilchasp baat jo tumhe paron ke naam yaad karne me madad degi. Har paray ka naam us paray ke pehle lafz ya pehle huroof se liya gaya hai. Pehle paray ka naam الم hai kyunke woh inhi huroof se shuru hota hai. Dusre paray ka naam سیقول hai kyunke us ki ibtida is lafz se hoti hai. To para ka naam koi alag se ratne wali cheez nahi, bas us paray ki pehli ibaraat hi us ka naam hai.

Paper ke fix facts ki recap

Ab saari baatain aik jagah dohra lo, kyunke paper inhi se banta hai. Wahi ki kul muddat 23 saal. Pehli wahi 610 Eiswi me Ghar-e-Hira me nazil hui, aur woh Surah Alaq ki pehli paanch ayaat thin. Suratain 114, paray 30, ayaat 6236, aur 6666 wala number ghalat mashhoor hai, use option me dekh kar phansna nahi. Ruku 540, sajda-e-tilawat 14, manzilain 7. Makki suratain 86 aur Madani 28, aur taqseem jagah ki nahi zamane ki hai. Sab se lambi surah Baqarah, ayaat 286. Sab se chhoti surah Kausar, ayaat 3. Pehli surah Fateha, jis ka laqab Umm-ul-Quran hai. Aakhri ayat Baqarah ki 281, aakhri mukammal surah An-Nasr. Quran ka dil Surah Yaseen. Sab se azeem ayat Ayat-ul-Kursi, Baqarah ki 255. Bina Bismillah wali surah Tauba, do Bismillah wali Naml. Huroof-e-Muqattaat wali suratain 29. Quran me naam ke sath 25 Ambiya ka zikr hai. Pehli jam'a Hazrat Abu Bakr ke dor me Hazrat Zaid bin Sabit ke zariye hui, aur mustanad nuskha Hazrat Usman ke dor me tayyar hua, isiliye un ka laqab Jami-ul-Quran hai, aur dusra laqab Zun-Noorain hai. Ye saari baatain pakki kar lo, to Quran wala koi sawal tumhe nahi phansa sakta.
