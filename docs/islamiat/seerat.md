Seerat-e-Tayyaba
Hazoor Pak Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ki zindagi, paidaish se wisal tak

Ye topic kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me Islamiat ke hisse me sab se zyada sawal seerat se hi bante hain. Kabhi tareekh pooch li jati hai, kabhi kisi waqie ka saal, kabhi pehle iman lane walon ki tartib. Ye sawal mushkil nahi hote, bas tartib yaad honi chahiye. Jo student seerat ko aik kahani ki tarah parh le, ke pehle kya hua aur us ke baad kya hua, us ke liye ye sawal sab se asaan number ban jate hain. Is liye hum zindagi ko shuru se aakhir tak aik silsile me parhenge. Tareekhain apne aap zehen me beth jayengi, ratne ki zaroorat hi nahi paregi.

Paidaish aur khandan

Aap Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ki paidaish Makkah Mukarrama me hui. Saal 571 AD tha, aur isi saal ko Aam-ul-Feel kaha jata hai, kyunke isi saal Abrahah hathi wala lashkar lekar Kaaba par hamla karne aya tha aur Allah ne usay nakam kar diya tha. Mashhoor tareekh 12 Rabi-ul-Awwal hai, peer ka din tha. Aap ke walid Hazrat Abdullah Aap ki paidaish se pehle hi wafat pa chuke the. Wo tijarati safar se wapas ate hue Madina me bimar hue aur wahi inteqal kar gaye. Yani Aap duniya me yateem tashreef laye. Walida ka naam Hazrat Amina bint Wahb hai, dada Hazrat Abdul Muttalib Quraish ke sardar the, aur khandan Banu Hashim tha, jo Quraish ka sab se moazzaz khandan mana jata tha.

Dai Halima aur bachpan ke pehle saal

Arab ke dastoor ke mutabiq bachon ko dehat bhej diya jata tha taake wo khuli hawa me palen aur saaf zaban seekhen. Aap ko Hazrat Halima Saadia ne raza'at ke liye liya, yani Aap un ke pas doodh peene aur parwarish ke liye rahe. Hazrat Halima bayan karti hain ke is bachay ke ane se un ke ghar me barkat aa gayi. Jab Aap ki umar chhay saal hui to walida Hazrat Amina Aap ko lekar Madina gayin, aur wapsi par Abwa ke maqam par un ka inteqal ho gaya. Ab Aap maa se bhi mehroom ho gaye. Phir dada Hazrat Abdul Muttalib ne kafalat ki, lekin jab Aap aath saal ke hue to dada bhi wafat pa gaye. Us ke baad chacha Abu Talib ne Aap ko apni kafalat me liya, aur ye kafalat zindagi bhar ke rishtay me badal gayi. Abu Talib ne hamesha Aap ki hifazat ki aur Makkah ke mushrikeen ke muqable me Aap ka sath diya.

Bachpan aur jawani ke mashhoor waqiat

Jab Aap takreeban bara saal ke hue to chacha Abu Talib ke sath Sham ke tijarati safar par gaye. Busra ke maqam par Bahira nami rahib ne Aap ko dekha, nubuwwat ki nishaniyan pehchanein aur Abu Talib ko mashwara diya ke is bachay ki hifazat karna. Ye waqia har Islamiat ki kitab me ata hai aur paper me bhi poocha jata hai. Jawani me Aap ne Harb-e-Fijar me hissa liya, jahan Aap apne chachaon ko teer uthakar dete the. Isi dor me Aap Hilf-ul-Fuzool me shareek hue, jo mazloom ki madad ka aik muahida tha. Ye muahida Abdullah bin Jud'an ke ghar hua tha. Aap nubuwwat ke baad bhi farmaya karte the ke agar mujhe aaj bhi is muahiday ki taraf bulaya jaye to main zaroor jaon ga. Is se pata chalta hai ke insaf aur mazloom ki madad Aap ki fitrat me shuru se thi. Makkah wale Aap ki sachai aur amanatdari ki wajah se Aap ko Al-Sadiq aur Al-Amin ke laqab se pukarte the.

Tijarat aur Hazrat Khadija se shadi

Aap ne jawani me tijarat ko pesha banaya. Hazrat Khadija bint Khuwaylid Makkah ki moazzaz aur maaldar khatoon thin, jo apna maal doosron ke zariye tijarat par lagati thin. Unho ne Aap ki amanatdari ki shohrat suni to apna maal dekar Sham bheja. Safar bohat kamiyab raha, munafa bhi zyada hua aur un ke ghulam Maysara ne Aap ki sachai aur akhlaq ki itni tareef ki ke Hazrat Khadija ne khud shadi ka paigham bheja. Us waqt Aap ki umar 25 saal thi aur mashhoor riwayat ke mutabiq Hazrat Khadija ki umar 40 saal thi. Ye shadi 25 saal tak qaim rahi. Jab tak Hazrat Khadija zinda rahin, Aap ne doosri shadi nahi ki. Mushkil tareen dinon me Hazrat Khadija Aap ka sab se bara sahara thin. Unho ne apna saara maal deen ki khidmat me laga diya.

Nabuwwat aur pehli wahi

Aap aksar Makkah se bahar Ghar-e-Hira me ja kar ibadat aur ghor o fikr farmate the. 610 AD me, jab Aap ki umar 40 saal thi, Ramzan ke mahine me Ghar-e-Hira me Hazrat Jibrail tashreef laye aur pehli wahi nazil hui. Ye Surah Al-Alaq ki pehli paanch ayat thin, jin ka pehla lafz Iqra hai, yani parho. Ghabraye hue Aap ghar tashreef laye to Hazrat Khadija ne tasalli di aur apne chacha-zad bhai Warqa bin Nawfal ke pas le gayin, jinho ne kaha ke ye wohi farishta hai jo Hazrat Musa par nazil hota tha. Paper me ye sawal bar bar ata hai ke pehli wahi kahan nazil hui, kis surah ki ayat thin aur us waqt Aap ki umar kya thi. Teeno jawab ab tumhare zehen me hain: Ghar-e-Hira, Surah Al-Alaq, 40 saal.

Pehle iman lane wale

Ye tartib paper ka fix sawal hai, isay kahani ki tarah yaad kar lo. Sab se pehle iman Hazrat Khadija layin, yani auraton me sab se pehle. Azad mardon me sab se pehle Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddiq iman laye. Bachon me sab se pehle Hazrat Ali iman laye, jin ki umar us waqt das saal ke qareeb thi. Aur ghulamon me sab se pehle Hazrat Zaid bin Harisa iman laye, jo Aap ke azad karda ghulam aur munh bole betay ki haisiyat rakhte the. Is ke baad Hazrat Usman, Hazrat Zubair, Hazrat Talha aur doosre azeem sahaba is karwan me shamil hote gaye. Is pehle giroh ko Assabiqoon-al-Awwaloon kaha jata hai, aur in ka darja Quran me bhi bayan hua hai.

Khufiya dawat aur Makkah ke mushkil saal

Pehle teen saal dawat khufiya rahi. Aap chhup kar logon ko Allah ki taraf bulate aur namaz parhte the. Phir jab elaaniya dawat ka hukm aya to Aap ne Koh-e-Safa par kharay ho kar poore Makkah ko awaz di. Yahi se mukhalfat shuru ho gayi. Quraish ne pehle lalach diya, sardari aur daulat ki peshkash ki, phir dhamkiyan dein, phir mazalim shuru kar diye. Ghareeb sahaba par sakht tareen zulm hue. Kuch sahaba ko Habsha ki taraf hijrat karna pari. Aik dor wo aya ke Banu Hashim ka teen saal tak social boycott raha, aur ye saal Sheb-e-Abi Talib me guzre jahan khane peene ki sakht takleef thi. Phir aik hi saal me pehle Abu Talib aur phir Hazrat Khadija ka inteqal ho gaya. Ye saal Aam-ul-Huzn, yani gham ka saal, kahlata hai.

Taif ka safar

Aam-ul-Huzn ke baad Aap Taif tashreef le gaye taake wahan ke logon ko dawat dein. Taif walo ne na sirf inkar kiya balke sheher ke awara ladkon ko peeche laga diya, jinho ne pathar mar mar kar Aap ko zakhmi kar diya. Hazrat Ayesha ne aik dafa poocha ke kya Aap par Uhud se bhi sakht din aya? Aap ne farmaya, haan, Taif wala din. Lekin itni takleef ke bawajood Aap ne Taif walo ke liye bad-dua nahi ki, balke un ki hidayat ki dua ki. Ye waqia Aap ke sabr aur rehmat ki sab se bari misaal hai, aur paper me aksar poocha jata hai ke Aap ki zindagi ka sab se sakht din konsa tha.

Isra aur Miraj

Isi mushkil dor me wo azeem waqia pesh aya jise Miraj kehte hain. Mashhoor tareekh 27 Rajab hai. Aik raat Aap ko Masjid-e-Haram se Masjid-e-Aqsa tak le jaya gaya, is safar ko Isra kehte hain, aur phir wahan se asmanon ki sair karai gayi, jisay Miraj kehte hain. Isi raat ummat ko paanch namazon ka tohfa mila. Pehle pachaas namazain farz hui thin, phir Hazrat Musa ke mashware par takhfeef hoti gayi aur aakhir paanch reh gayin, lekin sawab pachaas ka hi rakha gaya. Paper me teen sawal yahan se bante hain: Miraj kis mahine me hua, tohfa kya mila, aur Masjid-e-Aqsa kahan hai. Rajab, paanch namazain, aur Bait-ul-Muqaddas yani Jerusalem.

Hijrat-e-Madina

Jab Makkah me rehna mumkin na raha to Allah ke hukm se hijrat ka faisla hua. 622 AD me Aap Madina ki taraf rawana hue. Is safar me Hazrat Abu Bakr Aap ke rafeeq the, yani sathi. Quraish ke log peecha kar rahe the, is liye Aap ne teen din Ghar-e-Saur me qayam farmaya, jo Makkah ke qareeb Jabal-e-Saur ki ghaar hai. Hazrat Abu Bakr ke betay Abdullah khabrain late, beti Asma khana pohnchati, aur aakhir teen din ke baad karwan Madina ki taraf chal para. Pehle Quba me chand din qayam hua, jahan Masjid-e-Quba ki buniyad pari, phir Aap Madina munawwara me dakhil hue. Isi hijrat se Islami calendar, yani Hijri saal, ki ibtida hoti hai. Ye baat paper me lazmi poochi jati hai ke Hijri calendar kis waqie se shuru hua.

Madina me pehle kaam

Madina pohanch kar Aap ne jo kaam sab se pehle kiye, wo aik riyasat ki buniyad the. Pehla kaam Masjid-e-Nabawi ki tameer thi, jo sirf ibadat ki jagah nahi thi balke markaz-e-hukumat aur school bhi thi. Doosra kaam azan ki shuruaat thi, jis ke pehle muezzin Hazrat Bilal Habshi hue. Teesra azeem kaam muwakhat tha, yani Aap ne Makkah se ane wale muhajireen aur Madina ke ansar ke darmiyan bhai chara qaim kar diya. Ansar ne apni jaidad aur gharon me muhajireen ko hissa diya. Ye duniya ki tareekh ka anokha waqia hai. Chautha kaam Meesaq-e-Madina tha, Madina ke tamaam qabail, bashamool Yahoodion ke sath aik tehreeri muahida. Isay duniya ka pehla tehreeri dastoor kaha jata hai. In chaar kaamo ke naam aur tartib paper ke liye bohat aham hai.

Aakhri saal, Hajjat-ul-Wida aur wisal

Madina ke das saalon me Islam poore Arab me phail gaya. 8 Hijri me Makkah baghair bari jang ke fatah ho gaya, jis ki tafseel ghazwaat wale lesson me ayegi. 10 Hijri me Aap ne zindagi ka aakhri aur akela mukammal Hajj ada farmaya, jisay Hajjat-ul-Wida kehte hain. Arafat ke maidan me Aap ne wo tareekhi khutba diya jis me jan, maal aur izzat ki hurmat, auraton ke huqooq, aur tamaam insanon ki barabari ka elaan tha. Isi Hajj ke doran Surah Al-Maida ki wo ayat nazil hui jis me deen ke mukammal hone ki khabar di gayi. Wapsi ke kuch arsay baad Aap bimar hue aur 12 Rabi-ul-Awwal 11 Hijri, yani 632 AD, ko Madina Munawwara me wisal farmaya. Aap ki tadfeen usi hujre me hui jahan wisal hua tha, yani Hazrat Ayesha ke hujre me, aur yahi jagah aaj Masjid-e-Nabawi me Roza-e-Athar hai. Paper ka mashhoor sawal hai ke Aap ki qabr-e-mubarak kahan hai: Madina me, Hazrat Ayesha ke hujre me.

Azwaj-e-Mutahharat aur aulad

Aap ki azwaj ko Ummahat-ul-Momineen, yani momino ki maaen, kaha jata hai. In ke naam tartib se yaad kar lo: Hazrat Khadija, Hazrat Sawda, Hazrat Ayesha, Hazrat Hafsa, Hazrat Zaynab bint Khuzayma, Hazrat Umm-e-Salma, Hazrat Zaynab bint Jahsh, Hazrat Juwayria, Hazrat Umm-e-Habiba, Hazrat Safiyya aur Hazrat Maymuna. Kul tadaad gyara hai. Aap ki aulad me teen betay hue: Hazrat Qasim, jin ki nisbat se Aap ki kunniyat Abul-Qasim hai, Hazrat Abdullah, jinhain Tayyib aur Tahir bhi kaha jata hai, aur Hazrat Ibrahim, jo Hazrat Mariya Qibtiyya se hue. Teeno betay bachpan me hi wafat pa gaye. Betiyan chaar thin: Hazrat Zaynab, Hazrat Ruqayya, Hazrat Umm-e-Kulthum aur Hazrat Fatima. Hazrat Fatima se hi Aap ki nasal aage barhi, aur Hazrat Hasan aur Hazrat Hussain Aap ke nawase hain. Ye naam aur tadaadain paper me bar bar poochi jati hain.

Paper ke fix nuktaat

Ab wo baatein aik saath dohra lo jo paper me sab se zyada ati hain. Paidaish 571 AD, Aam-ul-Feel, 12 Rabi-ul-Awwal. Dai ka naam Halima Saadia. Shadi 25 saal ki umar me Hazrat Khadija se. Pehli wahi 610 AD me, Ghar-e-Hira me, Surah Al-Alaq ki ayat, umar 40 saal. Pehle musalman: auraton me Khadija, mardon me Abu Bakr, bachon me Ali, ghulamon me Zaid. Khufiya dawat teen saal. Gham ka saal Aam-ul-Huzn. Sab se sakht din Taif wala. Miraj 27 Rajab, tohfa paanch namazain. Hijrat 622 AD, rafeeq Abu Bakr, Ghar-e-Saur me teen din, Hijri calendar ki ibtida yahi se. Madina ke pehle kaam: Masjid-e-Nabawi, azan, muwakhat, Meesaq-e-Madina. Hajjat-ul-Wida 10 Hijri. Wisal 12 Rabi-ul-Awwal 11 Hijri, 632 AD, Madina me.

Tasveer ka khayal: Is lesson ke liye aik lambi timeline wali tasveer sab se kaam ki hogi. Aik seedhi lakeer par saal likhe hon, 571 paidaish, 595 shadi, 610 pehli wahi, 622 hijrat, 630 Fatah Makkah, 632 wisal, aur har saal ke neeche chhoti si tasveer ya nishan ho, jese paidaish par Makkah ka nishan, hijrat par raasta aur Ghar-e-Saur, aur aakhir me Madina aur Masjid-e-Nabawi. Student aik nazar me poori zindagi ki tartib dekh le, tareekhain khud ba khud kahani ban kar yaad ho jayen.
