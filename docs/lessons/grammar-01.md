Lesson 1: A, An and Nouns
A, An aur Nouns. Har cheez ka naam, aur "aik" ka sahi istemal

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me English ke hisse me article par, yani a, an aur the par, har saal sawal ata hai. Kabhi jumla theek karna hota hai aur kabhi chaar options me se sahi wala chunna hota hai. Aur mazay ki baat suno, ye sawal paper ke sab se asaan number hain. Bas aik chhota sa qanoon samajhna hai. Jo student ye lesson pakka kar le, wo paper me an honest aur a university jaisi ghalti kabhi nahi karega. Aur yaad rakho, yahi wo ghaltiyan hain jin par aadhe se zyada students apne number kaatte hain.

Pehle noun ko samajh lo

Dekho, sab se pehle noun ko samajh lo, baqi sab khud asaan ho jayega. Tum apne aas paas dekho. Ye kursi, ye meiz, ye kitaab jo tumhare haath me hai, in sab ka koi na koi naam hai. Ali tumhara dost hai, us ka naam Ali hai. Ustad ko ustad kehte hain aur doctor ko doctor. Lahore aik sheher ka naam hai, school aur bazaar jagah ke naam hain. Aur mohabbat? Wo nazar to nahi aati, lekin us ka bhi aik naam hai. Imaandari aur khof bhi naam hi hain, bas ye dil aur dimagh me hote hain.

English me har naam wali cheez ko noun kehte hain. Bas, baat khatam. Noun ki tareef ratne ki zaroorat nahi. Bas ye tasveer zehen me bitha lo ke jahan naam hai, wahan noun hai. Shakhs ka naam ho, jagah ka naam ho, cheez ka naam ho ya kisi khayal ka naam ho, sab noun hain.

Ab a aur an ki baat

Ab a aur an ko samjho. Urdu me hum kehte hain aik kitaab, aik admi. English me yehi "aik" kabhi a ban jata hai aur kabhi an. Asal masla sirf itna hai ke kab a lagega aur kab an.

Aik misaal se samjho. Farz karo tum kisi ko chai pila rahe ho. Tum Urdu me kaho ge, aik cup chai. Theek hai. Ab agar cup ki jagah apple aa jaye to? Aik apple. Lekin English me isay an apple kehte hain, a apple nahi. Kyun? Bol kar dekho. Apple bolte hue munh se pehli awaaz "a" ki nikalti hai, jo vowel ki awaaz hai. Aur jab pehli awaaz vowel ki ho to zabaan par "a" atakta hai aur "an" asani se nikalta hai. Angrezon ne apni asani ke liye ye qanoon bana liya.

Qanoon ye hai. Kaan se suno, aankh se mat dekho. Lafz ki pehli awaaz vowel ki ho, yani a, e, i, o, u jaisi awaaz ho, to an lagega. Pehli awaaz consonant ki ho to a lagega.

Yahi is lesson ka asal raaz hai. Letter nahi, awaaz. Kuch alfaaz dekhne me vowel se shuru hote hain lekin awaaz consonant ki dete hain. University ko bol kar dekho, "yu-niversity". Aur kuch alfaaz dekhne me consonant se shuru hote hain lekin un ka pehla letter khamosh hota hai. Honest ko bolo to "onest" banta hai, h ghayab. Paper hamesha inhi do cheezon par phansata hai, aur is lesson ke baad tum nahi phanso ge.

Aik aur zaroori baat zehen me rakho. A aur an sirf us noun ke sath ata hai jo aik ho aur gina ja sakta ho. Hum Urdu me "aik pani" nahi kehte, kyunke pani gina nahi jata. Isi liye English me a water bhi ghalat hai. Aur a books bhi ghalat hai, kyunke books to pehle hi aik se zyada hain.

Board par qanoon Angrezi me

Ustad jab board par qanoon likhta hai to kuch yun likhta hai. A noun is the name of a person, place, thing or idea. A and an mean "one", and they are used only with a singular countable noun. Use an before a word that begins with a vowel sound. Use a before a word that begins with a consonant sound. Aur sab se aham line ye hai: decide by sound, not by spelling. A university, an hour. Aur aakhir me ye bhi yaad rakho ke plural nouns aur uncountable nouns ke sath a ya an kabhi nahi ata.

Misaalein board se

Ab board par chand misaalein likhta hun. Har misaal ke sath us ki wajah bhi samjho, sirf jumla mat dekho.

1. She is an honest officer.
Honest me h khamosh hai. Pehli awaaz "o" hai, is liye an aya.

2. He studies at a university.
University ki awaaz "yu" se shuru hoti hai. Letter vowel ka hai, awaaz vowel ki nahi, is liye a aya.

3. I waited for an hour at the station.
Hour ka h bhi khamosh hai aur awaaz "au" ki hai, jo vowel ki awaaz hai.

4. Ali is a European.
European ki awaaz bhi "yu" hai, is liye an nahi, a.

5. He bought a book from the bazaar.
Book "b" ki awaaz se shuru hota hai, jo consonant hai. Seedha a.

6. She is an M.A. in English.
M ko bolte hue "em" awaaz ati hai. Pehli awaaz "e" hai, jo vowel hai, is liye an. Ye wala paper me bohat ata hai.

7. I saw a one-eyed man near the gate.
One ki awaaz "wun" hai. "W" consonant hai, is liye a.

8. He is an heir to a large property.
Heir ka h khamosh hai, awaaz "e" se shuru hoti hai.

9. It is a useful book for PPSC.
Useful bhi "yu" awaaz deta hai, is liye a lagega.

10. Lahore is a big city, and honesty is the best policy.
Lahore jagah ka naam hai aur honesty khayal ka naam hai. Dono noun hain, chahe aik dikhta hai aur doosra nahi.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Rozmarra ki bol chal me jab kisi cheez ka pehli dafa zikr karte hain to a ya an ata hai. I need a pen. She is a teacher. He bought an umbrella. Aur kisi ka pesha batana ho to a ya an lazmi ata hai. He is a doctor. She is an engineer. Ye peshe wala jumla paper ka pasandida jumla hai, isay khaas yaad rakho.

Ab paper wale andaaz ke do jumle dekho.

1. He is an honest and hardworking officer of the department.
2. She took admission in a university in Lahore and completed her degree in a year and a half.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab wo trick jo tumhe zindagi bhar kaam ayegi. Isay hum Kaan wala qanoon kehte hain. Jab bhi shak ho, lafz ko bol kar suno. Munh se pehli awaaz a, e, i, o, u jaisi nikli to an, warna a. Aankhein dhoka deti hain. University dekh kar an lagane ko dil karta hai, lekin kaan kabhi dhoka nahi dete. Bas ye aik jumla zehen me bitha lo: letter mat dekho, awaaz suno. An wahan, jahan vowel ki awaaz sunai de.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ab wo ghaltiyan dekho jo Pakistani students paper me karte hain. Main board par ghalat aur sahi sath sath likh raha hun, taake farq hamesha yaad rahe.

Ghalat: He is a honest man.
Sahi: He is an honest man.
Wajah: honest ka h khamosh hai aur pehli awaaz vowel ki hai.

Ghalat: She studies in an university.
Sahi: She studies in a university.
Wajah: university ki awaaz "yu" hai, vowel ki awaaz nahi.

Ghalat: He is an European.
Sahi: He is a European.
Wajah: European bhi "yu" se bola jata hai.

Ghalat: Give me an one-way ticket.
Sahi: Give me a one-way ticket.
Wajah: one ki awaaz "w" se shuru hoti hai, jo consonant hai.

Ghalat: He is a heir of this house.
Sahi: He is an heir of this house.
Wajah: heir ka h khamosh hai.

Ghalat: She gave me an advice.
Sahi: She gave me some advice. Ya phir: She gave me a piece of advice.
Wajah: advice gina nahi jata, ye uncountable hai, is liye is ke sath a ya an nahi lagta.

Ghalat: This is a news of great joy.
Sahi: This is a piece of news of great joy.
Wajah: news bhi uncountable hai.

Ghalat: He is doctor.
Sahi: He is a doctor.
Wajah: peshe se pehle a ya an lazmi ata hai. Ye ghalti Urdu ke asar se hoti hai, kyunke Urdu me "wo doctor hai" kehte hue koi "aik" nahi ata, lekin English me ata hai.

Jawab aur un ki wajah

1. Jawab B hai, an. Honest ka h khamosh hai aur pehli awaaz vowel ki hai. Kaan wala qanoon yaad hai na?
2. Jawab B hai, a. University ki awaaz "yu" hai, vowel ki awaaz nahi.
3. Jawab C hai, an. Hour ka h khamosh hai aur awaaz "au" se shuru hoti hai.
4. Jawab B hai, A. European "yu" awaaz deta hai, is liye a.
5. Jawab B hai. Advice, information aur news, teeno uncountable hain. A piece of advice hi sahi tareeqa hai.
6. Jawab C hai, a. One ki awaaz "w" hai, jo consonant hai.
7. Jawab B hai, an. M ki awaaz "em" hai aur pehli awaaz "e" hai, jo vowel hai.
8. Jawab D hai, koi article nahi. Honesty yahan aik aam khayal hai, abstract noun hai, aur aam baat me is par article nahi ata.

10. Jawab D hai, an. Owl vowel ki awaaz "au" se shuru hota hai.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye do tasveerein bohat kaam ki hongi. Pehli tasveer Kaan wale qanoon ki ho. Aik bare kaan ki tasveer ke sath likha ho ke awaaz suno. Neeche do raaste bane hon. Aik raaste par vowel ki awaaz wale alfaaz hon, apple, honest aur hour, aur un ke aage bara sa AN likha ho. Doosre raaste par consonant awaaz wale alfaaz hon, book, university aur one-way, aur un ke aage bara sa A likha ho. University aur honest ko khaas rang se dikhaya jaye, kyunke yehi do dhoka dene wale alfaaz hain jo hamesha yaad rehne chahiye.

Doosri tasveer noun ki chaar qismo ki chart ho. Shakhs, jagah, cheez aur khayal. Har qism ka apna rang ho aur us ke neeche teen misaalein hon, jaise shakhs ke neeche ustad, jagah ke neeche school, cheez ke neeche kitaab aur khayal ke neeche mohabbat. Student aik nazar me noun pehchan le, yehi is chart ka maqsad hai.
