Lesson 2: Plural Nouns
Plural Nouns. Aik se zyada, lekin har noun ka apna andaaz.

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me plural par do tarah ke sawal ate hain. Pehli tarah ke seedhe sawal hote hain, jaise koi pooche ke plural of criterion kya hai. Doosri tarah jumla theek karne wale sawal hote hain, jahan informations, sheeps ya brother-in-laws jaisi ghalti jumle ke andar chhupi hoti hai. Ye sawal un students ke number kaatte hain jo sirf aik usool jante hain ke s laga do, kaam khatam. Sach ye hai ke plural ke chand hi usool hain. Is lesson ke baad tum har noun ka plural khud bana sako ge, rata lagaye baghair.

Plural matlab kya hai

Pehle baat samajh lo. Urdu me bhi yehi hota hai. Kitaab aik hai, kitaabein zyada. Larka aik hai, larkay zyada. English me bhi jab noun aik se zyada ho jaye to us ki shakal badal jati hai. Isi badli hui shakal ko plural kehte hain.

Lekin aik baat pehle din se zehen me bitha lo. English me har noun aik hi tareeqe se plural nahi banta. Kuch noun seedha s le lete hain, jaise book se books. Kuch es mangte hain, jaise box se boxes. Kuch apni shakal hi badal dete hain, jaise man se men aur child se children. Aur kuch itne shareef hain ke aik hon ya hazaar, shakal waisi ki waisi rehti hai, jaise sheep se sheep.

Asal raaz: noun ka aakhir dekho

Ghabrane ki koi baat nahi. Ye sab usoolon me bandha hua hai. Tumhe bas ye dekhna hai ke noun khatam kis par ho raha hai. Aakhri letter hi faisla karta hai ke plural kese banega. Jaise darzi kapra dekh kar samajh jata hai ke silai kahan lagegi, waisay hi tum noun ka aakhir dekh kar us ka plural bana lo ge.

Chalo, usool aik aik karke samjhte hain.

Pehla usool sab se aam hai. Sirf s laga do. Book se books, pen se pens, boy se boys. Zyada tar nouns isi usool par chalte hain.

Doosra usool un nouns ke liye hai jo s, x, z, ch ya sh par khatam hon. Un ke aage es lagta hai. Bus se buses, box se boxes, watch se watches, dish se dishes. Kyun? Bol kar dekho. Boxs bolna mushkil hai, boxes zabaan par asaan parta hai. English hamesha asani dekhta hai. Ye baat is lesson me bhi yaad ayegi aur aage bhi.

Teesra usool y walon ka hai. Agar noun consonant ke baad y par khatam ho, jaise city ya baby, to y nikal kar ies lag jata hai. City se cities, baby se babies. Lekin agar y se pehle vowel ho, jaise boy ya day, to sirf s lagta hai. Boy se boys, day se days. Dekha? Y ke peeche wala letter faisla karta hai.

Chautha usool f aur fe walon ka hai. Kuch nouns jo f ya fe par khatam hon, un ka f ves ban jata hai. Knife se knives, wife se wives, leaf se leaves, half se halves. Lekin khabardar, sab aise nahi hote. Roof se roofs banta hai aur chief se chiefs. Ye seedha s lete hain. Ye chand alfaaz yaad karne wale hain, aur in ki trick aage bata raha hun.

Paanchva usool purane alfaaz ka hai. Ye apni andar ki awaaz badal dete hain. Man se men, woman se women, foot se feet, tooth se teeth, goose se geese, mouse se mice. Aur kuch bilkul khaas hain. Child se children, ox se oxen, person se people. In ka koi shortcut nahi. Ye istemal se yaad hote hain, aur paper inhi ko poochta hai.

Chhata usool shareef alfaaz ka hai. Sheep, deer, fish, series aur species. Ye aik hon ya zyada, in ki shakal nahi badalti.

Saatva usool mile jule alfaaz ka hai, jinhe compound nouns kehte hain. In me asal lafz plural hota hai, aakhir wala nahi. Brother-in-law ka plural brothers-in-law hai, brother-in-laws nahi. Asal lafz brother hai, law to peeche laga hua hai.

Aur kuch purane Greek aur Latin alfaaz bhi apna purana tareeqa rakhte hain. Criterion se criteria banta hai aur phenomenon se phenomena. Ye dono paper ke bohat pasandida hain.

Aakhir me aik khaas dhoka samajh lo. Kuch alfaaz dikhne me plural lagte hain lekin hote singular hain. News, mathematics, physics. The news is good hota hai, are nahi. Aur kuch alfaaz hamesha plural rehte hain. Scissors, trousers, glasses. In ka aik wala istemal karna ho to a pair of scissors kehna parta hai.

Board par qanoon Angrezi me

Board par ye usool kuch yun likhe jate hain. Most nouns add s, as book becomes books. Nouns ending in s, x, z, ch or sh add es, as box becomes boxes and watch becomes watches. When a noun ends in a consonant plus y, the y changes to ies, as city becomes cities, but after a vowel only s is added, as boy becomes boys. Many nouns ending in f or fe change to ves, as knife becomes knives and leaf becomes leaves, but remember, roof becomes roofs and chief becomes chiefs. Some plurals are irregular: man becomes men, woman becomes women, child becomes children, foot becomes feet, tooth becomes teeth, mouse becomes mice, ox becomes oxen and person becomes people. Some nouns never change: sheep, deer, fish, series and species. In compound nouns the main word becomes plural: brother-in-law becomes brothers-in-law. Aur aakhir me ye line kabhi mat bhulna: news, mathematics and physics look plural but are singular, while scissors, trousers and glasses are always plural.

Misaalein board se

1. The children are playing in the park.
Child ka plural children hai. Childs kabhi nahi hota.

2. She bought two loaves of bread.
Loaf ka f ves ban gaya, is liye loaves.

3. The sheep are grazing in the field.
Sheep aik ho ya zyada, shakal wahi rehti hai. Verb are se pata chala ke yahan zyada hain.

4. Mathematics is my favourite subject.
Mathematics dikhne me plural hai lekin hai singular, is liye is aya.

5. His new trousers are black.
Trousers hamesha plural hai, is liye are ayega.

6. Both his brothers-in-law are teachers.
Compound noun me asal lafz, yani brother, plural hota hai. Aakhir wala law nahi hota.

7. The criteria for this job are very strict.
Criterion se criteria banta hai. Ye Greek asal wala plural hai aur paper ka pasandida hai.

8. He took many photos on the trip.
Photo par sirf s lagega. Photoes ghalat hai.

9. The women of this village work very hard.
Woman se women banta hai, aur bolne me awaaz bhi badal jati hai, "wimin" jaisi.

10. A pair of scissors is lying on the table.
Scissors plural hai, lekin a pair of ke sath verb singular ata hai, kyunke asal subject pair hai, scissors nahi.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Rozmarra zindagi me jahan ginna ho, kharidari ho ya koi fehrist banana ho, har jagah plural chalta hai. Two books, three days, many students. Daftari aur sarkari angrezi me bhi yehi haal hai. Reports aur applications me likha hota hai ke the documents are attached, ya all candidates must bring their certificates.

Ab paper wale andaaz ke do jumle dekho.

1. The phenomena of unemployment and inflation are the main issues of our economy.
2. His advice was useful, but the information given in the news was wrong.

Doosre jumle par khaas tawajjo do. Advice, information aur news, teeno bina plural ke aye hain, kyunke ye gine nahi jate.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab wo kahani suno jo f wale saat alfaaz zindagi bhar yaad karwa degi. Wife ne knife se loaf ka half kata, leaf gira, wolf bhaga, thief pakra gaya, aur sab ke sab ves ho gaye. Gino to sahi. Wife se wives, knife se knives, loaf se loaves, half se halves, leaf se leaves, wolf se wolves, thief se thieves. Aik kahani, saat alfaaz, aur masla hal.

Aur same form walon ke liye aik chhoti si line yaad kar lo. Kheet ki teen dost, Sheep, Deer aur Fish, kapre nahi badaltay. Ye teenon aik hon ya hazaar, shakal nahi badaltay.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ghalat: He gave me many informations.
Sahi: He gave me much information.
Wajah: information gina nahi jata, ye uncountable hai, is liye is ka plural nahi banta.

Ghalat: The furnitures are new.
Sahi: The furniture is new.
Wajah: furniture bhi uncountable hai.

Ghalat: Two childs are crying.
Sahi: Two children are crying.
Wajah: child irregular hai, is ka plural children hai.

Ghalat: She has three brother-in-laws.
Sahi: She has three brothers-in-law.
Wajah: compound noun me pehla aur asal lafz plural hota hai.

Ghalat: The news are good.
Sahi: The news is good.
Wajah: news singular hai, chahe us ke aakhir me s laga ho.

Ghalat: Mathematics are difficult.
Sahi: Mathematics is difficult.
Wajah: subjects ke naam, jaise mathematics aur physics, singular hote hain.

Ghalat: He bought two sheeps.
Sahi: He bought two sheep.
Wajah: sheep ki shakal badalti hi nahi.

Ghalat: The criterions are strict.
Sahi: The criteria are strict.
Wajah: criterion se criteria banta hai.

Ghalat: Many peoples live here.
Sahi: Many people live here.
Wajah: people pehle hi plural hai. Peoples sirf qaumein ke matlab me ata hai.

Ghalat: She cut the paper with a scissor.
Sahi: She cut the paper with scissors. Ya phir: She cut the paper with a pair of scissors.
Wajah: scissors hamesha plural rehta hai.

Jawab aur un ki wajah

1. Jawab B hai, oxen. Ox un chand alfaaz me se hai jin ka plural en se banta hai, bilkul child se children ki tarah.
2. Jawab B hai. News dikhne me plural hai lekin hai singular, is liye is ayega.
3. Jawab C hai, brothers-in-law. Compound noun me asal lafz plural hota hai, aur yahan asal lafz brother hai.
4. Jawab B hai, sheep. Sheep ki shakal singular aur plural dono me aik hi rehti hai.
5. Jawab B hai. Mathematics subject ka naam hai aur singular hai, is liye verb is ayega.
6. Jawab C hai, criteria. Criterion Greek asal ka lafz hai aur is ka plural criteria hai.
7. Jawab C hai, pieces of advice. Advice uncountable hai. Ginna ho to pieces of advice kehte hain.
8. Jawab C hai, loaves. Loaf ka f ves me badal jata hai. Kahani yaad hai na, wife ne knife se loaf ka half kata?
9. Jawab B hai, are. Scissors hamesha plural hai, is liye plural verb ayega.
10. Jawab D hai, deer. Deer same form hai. Baqi teeno irregular hain, goose se geese, mouse se mice aur foot se feet.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Pehli tasveer plural banane wali machine ki ho. Aik machine bani ho jis me noun aik taraf se andar jata hai. Machine pehle noun ka aakhri letter dekhti hai, phir faisla karti hai ke s lagega, es lagega, ies lagega ya ves lagega, aur noun doosri taraf se plural ban kar nikalta hai. Har raaste ke neeche aik misaal bhi likhi ho. Is tasveer se student ke zehen me ye baat baith jayegi ke pehle aakhir dekho, phir raasta chuno.

Doosri tasveer dhoka dene wale alfaaz ki chart ho, teen khano me. Pehle khane me wo alfaaz hon jin ki shakal nahi badalti, sheep, deer aur fish. Doosre khane me wo jo dikhte plural hain lekin hain singular, news aur mathematics. Teesre khane me wo jo hamesha plural rehte hain, scissors, trousers aur glasses. Sach ye hai ke paper ke zyada tar plural traps inhi teen khano ka khulasa hain.
