Lesson 3: Pronouns and am, is, are
Pronouns aur am, is, are. Kaam karne wala kaun, aur kaam kis par hua

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me pronoun par har saal sawal ata hai, aur ye sawal aksar jumla theek karne wale hisse me chhupa hota hai. Kabhi Ali and me went likha hota hai aur kabhi between you and I. Dono jumle dekhne me theek lagte hain, lekin aik me ghalti hai. Aur be verb, yani am, is aur are, ka sawal to har paper me lazmi ata hai, kyunke har jumle me yehi verb chalta hai. Jo student ye lesson pakka kar le, wo pronoun wali ghalti kabhi nahi karega, aur am, is, are ka sawal us ke liye muft ke number ban jayega.

Pehle pronoun ko samajh lo

Dekho, pronoun ka matlab hai naam ki jagah ane wala lafz. Baar baar naam lena ajeeb lagta hai. Ali acha larka hai, Ali roz school jata hai, Ali ki kitaab kho gayi. Sunne me bhi bura lagta hai. Isi liye naam ki jagah he aa jata hai. Ali acha larka hai, he roz school jata hai, us ki kitaab kho gayi. Bas yehi pronoun hai, naam ka badal.

Pronoun do qism ke hote hain, aur ye farq hi is lesson ki jaan hai. Aik qism wo hai jo kaam karta hai. I, he, she, we, they. In ko subject pronoun kehte hain, kyunke ye jumle ke shuru me, kaam karne wale ki jagah par ate hain. Doosri qism wo hai jis par kaam hota hai. Me, him, her, us, them. In ko object pronoun kehte hain, kyunke ye kaam sehne wale ki jagah par ate hain.

Trick ye hai. Kaam karne wala hamesha I, he, she, we, they. Jis par kaam ho, wo hamesha me, him, her, us, them. Bas ye aik jumla zehen me bitha lo. I kaam karta hai, me kaam sehta hai. He khana khata hai, usay khana khilaya jata hai to him. She kitaab parhti hai, usay kitaab di jati hai to her. Ye farq samajh lo to aadhi ghaltiyan yahin khatam.

Ab am, is, are ki baat

Am, is, are, teeno aik hi verb hain, bas shakal badal jati hai, jaise kapre badal jate hain. Ye be verb kehlate hain, aur ye batate hain ke koi kya hai ya kahan hai. I am a student. She is a teacher. They are ready.

Qanoon bilkul seedha hai. I ke sath hamesha am ata hai. He, she, it aur kisi aik shakhs ya aik cheez ke sath is ata hai. You, we, they aur aik se zyada cheezon ke sath are ata hai. I am, he is, she is, it is, you are, we are, they are. Ye saat jode ratne ki zaroorat nahi. Sirf ye yaad rakho ke I akela hai to am, aik banda ya aik cheez ho to is, aur zyada hon ya tum ho to are.

Aik baat khaas yaad rakho. You ke sath hamesha are ata hai, chahe tum aik ho ya zyada. You is acha lagta hai bolne me, lekin English me you are hi sahi hai. Ye ghalti bohat se students karte hain, aur paper me yehi poochta hai.

Board par qanoon Angrezi me

Board par ye qanoon kuch yun likha jata hai. A pronoun takes the place of a noun. Subject pronouns are I, he, she, we and they. They do the action. Object pronouns are me, him, her, us and them. The action happens to them. Use am with I. Use is with he, she, it and any singular noun. Use are with you, we, they and any plural noun. Aur aakhir me ye line: after a preposition, always use an object pronoun, as between you and me.

Misaalein board se

1. I am a student of PPSC preparation.
I ke sath am ata hai. Ye joda kabhi nahi badalta.

2. She is an honest officer.
She aik hai, is liye is aya.

3. They are ready for the test.
They zyada hain, is liye are aya.

4. It is I who knocked at the door.
Formal English me be verb ke baad bhi subject pronoun ata hai. Bol chaal me It is me bhi suna jata hai, lekin paper ke formal rule ke mutabiq It is I sahi hai.

5. Between you and me, this paper was easy.
Between aik preposition hai, aur preposition ke baad hamesha object pronoun ata hai, is liye me, I nahi.

6. Ali and I went to Lahore yesterday.
Went ka kaam Ali aur I ne kiya. Kaam karne wala I hai, me nahi.

7. The teacher called him and me to the office.
Called ka kaam teacher ne kiya, aur kaam jin par hua wo him aur me hain. Dono object hain.

8. This is she speaking on the phone.
Phone par apna taaruf formal tareeqe se yun hota hai. She subject pronoun hai.

9. He is taller than I am.
Than ke baad asal me aik chhupa hua verb hota hai, than I am. Is liye I ata hai.

10. We are Pakistanis and we love our country.
We ke sath are ata hai, aur doosri dafa bhi we hi aya, kyunke love ka kaam bhi we hi kar rahe hain.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Rozmarra ki bol chaal me har jumle me pronoun chalta hai. I am going, she is coming, they are waiting. Daftari khat me likha hota hai ke I am writing to inform you, ya we are pleased to tell you. Aur paper me to ye har jagah hai, error detection me, fill in the blank me aur sentence correction me. Aik chhota sa I ya me poora number badal deta hai.

Ab paper wale andaaz ke do jumle dekho.

1. The principal said that he and I would check the papers tomorrow.
2. It was they who solved the problem first.

Doosre jumle par ghaur karo. It was they, kyunke formal English me be verb ke baad subject pronoun ata hai. Bol chaal me It was them bhi suna jata hai, lekin paper me they hi sahi mana jata hai.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab wo trick jo ye farq hamesha yaad rakhwayegi. Apne aap se poochho, kaam kis ne kiya. Jawab mile to wahan I, he, she, we, they me se aik ayega. Aur poochho, kaam kis par hua. Jawab mile to wahan me, him, her, us, them me se aik ayega. Kaam karne wala pehle, kaam sehne wala baad me. Ali and I went, kyunke jane ka kaam Ali aur I ne kiya. Teacher ne him ko bulaya, kyunke bulane ka kaam teacher ne kiya aur him par hua. Bas ye aik sawal har ghalti se bacha lega.

Aur am, is, are ke liye aik chhoti si line yaad kar lo. I ke sath am, aik ke sath is, zyada ke sath are. I am, he is, they are. Ye teeno jode aik saans me bol lo, aur jab bhi shak ho, inhi ko dohra lo.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ghalat: Ali and me went to Lahore.
Sahi: Ali and I went to Lahore.
Wajah: went ka kaam karne wala subject hai, is liye I ayega, me nahi.

Ghalat: Between you and I, this is a secret.
Sahi: Between you and me, this is a secret.
Wajah: between preposition hai, us ke baad object pronoun ata hai.

Ghalat: It is me who is responsible.
Sahi: It is I who am responsible.
Wajah: formal English me be verb ke baad subject pronoun ata hai, aur who ka verb bhi I ke hisaab se am hota hai.

Ghalat: The teacher gave the books to he and she.
Sahi: The teacher gave the books to him and her.
Wajah: to preposition hai, us ke baad object pronoun ate hain.

Ghalat: Let I go with you.
Sahi: Let me go with you.
Wajah: let ke baad object pronoun ata hai.

Ghalat: This is her speaking.
Sahi: This is she speaking.
Wajah: phone par formal taaruf me subject pronoun ata hai.

Ghalat: Him and Ali are brothers.
Sahi: He and Ali are brothers.
Wajah: are ka subject He hai, him nahi.

Ghalat: The prize was given to Ali and I.
Sahi: The prize was given to Ali and me.
Wajah: to ke baad object pronoun ata hai.

Ghalat: It is us who did it.
Sahi: It is we who did it.
Wajah: formal English me be verb ke baad subject pronoun ata hai.

Ghalat: You is my best friend.
Sahi: You are my best friend.
Wajah: you ke sath hamesha are ata hai, is kabhi nahi, chahe tum aik hi kyun na ho.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Pehli tasveer do khano wali chart ho. Aik khane ka unwaan ho, Kaam karne wale, aur us me I, he, she, we, they likhe hon. Doosre khane ka unwaan ho, Kaam sehne wale, aur us me me, him, her, us, them likhe hon. Har pronoun ke neeche aik chhoti misaal ho. Student aik nazar me samajh jaye ke konsa pronoun kahan ayega.

Doosri tasveer am, is, are ki table ho. Teen khane hon. Pehle me I am, doosre me He is, She is, It is, teesre me You are, We are, They are. Har khane ka apna rang ho. Ye table deewar par lagane wali ho, taake be verb ka sawal kabhi ghalat na ho.
