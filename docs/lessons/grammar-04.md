Lesson 4: What, This and That
What, This aur That. Paas wala ye, door wala wo, aur kya wala sawal

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me this, that, these, those aur what par sawal har saal ata hai. Kabhi jumla theek karna hota hai ke this books sahi hai ya these books, aur kabhi khali jagah me what ya which bharna hota hai. Ye sawal chhote lagte hain lekin yehi number kaatte hain, kyunke Urdu me ye aur wo to hai lekin this aur that ke chaar roop, this, that, these aur those, dimagh me ulajh jate hain. Is lesson me hum pehle ye farq itna saaf karenge ke ungli ke ishare se yaad rahe, phir what ka kaam samjhenge. Jo student ye lesson pakka kar le, wo in sawalo me kabhi nahi phansega.

Pehle ye aur wo ki baat

Dekho, baat bilkul seedhi hai. Tum apne haath ki taraf dekho. Jo cheez tumhare paas hai, haath me hai ya bilkul qareeb hai, Urdu me tum usay ye kehte ho. Aur jo cheez door hai, kamre ke doosre kone me hai ya gali ke paar hai, usay tum wo kehte ho. English me bas yehi farq hai. Paas wali cheez ke liye this, door wali cheez ke liye that.

Lekin English me aik aur baat hai jo Urdu me nahi. Aik cheez ho to aur lafz, zyada cheezein hon to aur lafz. Paas wali aik cheez this hai, paas wali zyada cheezein these hain. Door wali aik cheez that hai, door wali zyada cheezein those hain. Chaar alfaaz hain aur do sawal hain. Pehla sawal, paas hai ya door. Dusra sawal, aik hai ya zyada. In do sawalo ke jawab mila do, lafz khud nikal ayega.

This is my pen. Kalam haath me hai, paas hai, aik hai. These are my pens. Kalamein paas hain lekin zyada hain. That is your house. Ghar door hai aur aik hai. Those are your books. Kitaabein door hain aur zyada hain. Dekha, koi mushkil nahi.

Aik aur istemal bhi yaad kar lo, kyunke ye paper me ata hai. This aur that waqt ke liye bhi ate hain. Jo waqt chal raha hai ya qareeb hai, wo this leta hai. This morning, this week, this year. Aur jo waqt guzar gaya, door chala gaya, wo that leta hai. That day, that night, in those days. Jaise purani baat karte hue kehte hain, those were the days, wo bhi kya din the. Waqt me bhi wohi qanoon hai, jo qareeb wo this, jo door wo that.

Ab what ki baat

Ab what ko samjho. What sawal puchne wala lafz hai aur is ka matlab hai kya. What is your name? What is this? What are you doing? Jab kisi cheez ke baare me maloomat chahiye ho aur jawab khula ho, koi bhi ho sakta ho, to what ata hai.

Lekin yahan aik bareek farq hai jo paper bohat pasand karta hai. What aur which ka farq. Jab choices mehdood hon, samne rakhi hon, aur un me se aik chunna ho, to which ata hai. Which colour do you like, red or blue? Samne do rang hain, un me se chunna hai. Aur jab koi choice samne na ho, sawal bilkul khula ho, to what ata hai. What colour is your car? Yahan koi fehrist nahi, jawab kuch bhi ho sakta hai. Yaad rakho, chunna ho to which, khula poochna ho to what.

What ke sath verb ka mel bhi noun ke hisaab se hota hai. What is this? Aik cheez hai. What are these? Zyada cheezein hain. Ye chhoti si baat bhi paper me ghalti ban jati hai.

Aakhir me it aur this ka farq

Aik dhoka aur samajh lo. Kisi shakhs ka taaruf karwana ho ya kisi cheez ki taraf pehli dafa ishara karna ho to this ata hai. This is my friend Ali. Phone par bhi yehi hota hai. Hello, who is this? Aur jawab me kehte hain, this is Ahmed speaking. Lekin jis cheez ka zikr pehle ho chuka ho, us ke liye it ata hai. I bought a book yesterday. It is very interesting. Yahan it usi kitaab ke liye hai jis ka zikr ho chuka. Pehli dafa this, dobara it. Ye farq paper me chhupa hota hai.

Board par qanoon Angrezi me

Board par ye baat kuch yun likhi jati hai. This and these point to things that are near. This is for one thing and these is for more than one. That and those point to things that are far. That is for one thing and those is for more than one. This is also used for present time, as this morning, and that for past time, as that day. What asks a question with an open answer, but which asks us to choose from a small number of things. Aur aakhri line: we use this to introduce a person or a thing for the first time, and it for a thing already mentioned.

Misaalein board se

Ab board par chand misaalein likhta hun. Har misaal ke sath us ki wajah bhi samjho.

1. This is my book.
Kitaab paas hai aur aik hai, is liye this.

2. These shoes are new.
Jootay paas hain lekin zyada hain, is liye these. This shoes kabhi nahi hota.

3. That building is very old.
Imarat door hai aur aik hai, is liye that.

4. Those days were golden days.
Din guzar gaye, waqt me door chale gaye, aur zyada hain, is liye those.

5. What is your father?
Sawal khula hai, jawab pesha hoga. What aya, which nahi.

6. Which pen is yours, the black one or the blue one?
Do kalam samne hain, un me se chunna hai, is liye which.

7. This morning I met an old friend.
Subah chal rahi hai, waqt qareeb hai, is liye this morning.

8. Hello, who is this? This is Usman speaking.
Phone par taaruf this se hota hai, it se nahi.

9. I have bought a new car. It is very fast.
Car ka zikr pehle ho chuka hai, dusri dafa it aya.

10. What are those things on the roof?
Cheezein door bhi hain aur zyada bhi, is liye those aur sath are.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Rozmarra zindagi me kisi cheez ki taraf ishara karna ho to yehi alfaaz chalte hain. Dukan par kehte ho, this one, please. Kisi ko apne dost se milwana ho to kehte ho, this is my friend. Daftar me files ki taraf ishara karke kehte hain, these files are complete, those files are pending. Bol chaal ka bohat bara hissa inhi chaar alfaaz par khara hai.

Ab paper wale andaaz ke do jumle dekho.

1. These documents are ready, but those files are still incomplete.
2. Which of these two books is better for the one paper?

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab wo trick jo chaaro alfaaz kabhi bhoolne nahi degi. Apni ungli ko dekho. Jo cheez tumhari ungli ke bilkul neeche hai, haath ke paas hai, wo this hai. Aur jis cheez ki taraf ungli door ishara karti hai, wo that hai. Paas wala this, door wala that. Bas ye tasveer zehen me bitha lo. Ab dusra sawal. Cheez aik hai to lafz waisa hi rahega, this ya that. Aur cheez zyada hai to lafz ke aakhir me s ki awaaz ayegi, these ya those. To jab bhi shak ho, do sawal poochho. Paas hai ya door? Aik hai ya zyada? Do jawab, aik lafz. Aur what ke liye aik line aur. Chunna ho to which, khula poochna ho to what.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ab wo ghaltiyan dekho jo Pakistani students paper me karte hain. Ghalat aur sahi sath sath likh raha hun.

Ghalat: This books are mine.
Sahi: These books are mine.
Wajah: books zyada hain aur paas hain, is liye these ayega.

Ghalat: That pens are costly.
Sahi: Those pens are costly.
Wajah: pens zyada hain, is liye that ki jagah those ayega.

Ghalat: What is these?
Sahi: What are these?
Wajah: these zyada ke liye hai, is ke sath are ata hai.

Ghalat: This are my keys.
Sahi: These are my keys.
Wajah: keys zyada hain. This sirf aik cheez ke sath ata hai.

Ghalat: Those house is big.
Sahi: That house is big.
Wajah: house aik hai, is liye those nahi, that ayega.

Ghalat: What book do you want, this one or that one?
Sahi: Which book do you want, this one or that one?
Wajah: do kitaabon me se chunna hai, choices mehdood hain, is liye which.

Ghalat: It is my brother standing at the door. (Pehli dafa taaruf karwana ho to)
Sahi: This is my brother standing at the door.
Wajah: pehli dafa kisi ka taaruf this se hota hai.

Ghalat: That morning I am going to Lahore today.
Sahi: This morning I am going to Lahore today.
Wajah: aaj ki subah chal rahi hai, waqt qareeb hai, is liye this.

Ghalat: These is a gift for you.
Sahi: This is a gift for you.
Wajah: gift aik hai, is liye this aur is.

Ghalat: Who is it speaking on the phone?
Sahi: Who is this speaking on the phone?
Wajah: phone par sawal this se hota hai.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Pehli tasveer ungli wali ho. Aik haath bana ho, us ke bilkul paas aik kitaab ho jis par bara sa THIS likha ho, aur usi ke sath chand aur kitaabein hon jin par THESE likha ho. Phir ungli door ishara kar rahi ho jahan aik ghar ho jis par THAT likha ho, aur us ke sath chand ghar hon jin par THOSE likha ho. Neeche aik line likhi ho, paas wala this, door wala that, zyada wala s ke sath. Student aik nazar me chaaro alfaaz pehchan lega.

Dusri tasveer what aur which ke farq ki ho. Aik taraf khula aasmaan bana ho jis ke neeche WHAT likha ho aur likha ho ke jawab kuch bhi ho sakta hai. Dusri taraf aik chhoti si tokri me sirf do seb rakhe hon, aik surkh aik hara, aur neeche WHICH likha ho ke in me se aik chuno. Ye tasveer chunne wale aur khule sawal ka farq kabhi bhoolne nahi degi.
