Lesson 5: Prepositions of Place
Prepositions of Place. Cheez kahan hai, ye batane wale alfaaz

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me preposition ka sawal sab se zyada ata hai, aur un me bhi jagah wali prepositions, in, on, at, under, between, paper ki jaan hain. Kabhi jumle me khali jagah hoti hai aur kabhi pura jumla theek karna hota hai. Pakistani students ka sab se bara masla ye hai ke Urdu me par aik hi lafz hai. Meiz par, Lahore par, deewar par, sab par. Lekin English me har jagah ka apna lafz hai, in bhi hai, on bhi hai aur at bhi hai. Jo student in teeno ka farq samajh jaye aur baqi jagah wali prepositions ke Urdu matlab pakke kar le, us ke liye ye sawal asaan number ban jate hain. Is lesson me hum har preposition ko us ke Urdu matlab ke sath zehen me bithayenge.

Preposition kya hota hai

Pehle baat samajh lo. Preposition wo lafz hai jo kisi cheez ki jagah, waqt ya talluq batata hai. Is lesson me hum sirf jagah ki baat kar rahe hain. Billi meiz ke neeche hai. Kitaab meiz ke upar hai. Larka darwaze ke peeche hai. Ye neeche, upar aur peeche hi jagah wali prepositions hain. English me ye alfaaz hain: in, on, at, under, over, behind, between, near, beside aur in front of. Aik aik karke sab ko samajhte hain.

Pehle teen baray: in, on, at

In ka matlab hai ke andar. Jo cheez kisi band jagah ke andar ho, us ke liye in ata hai. The milk is in the fridge. The students are in the classroom. She lives in Lahore. Sheher, kamra, dabba, jeb, sab ke andar ke liye in. Yaad rakho, in hamesha kisi ghere hue hisse ke andar ki baat karta hai.

On ka matlab hai ke upar, lekin satah ko chhoo kar. The book is on the table. The picture is on the wall. The keys are on the desk. Meiz ki satah, deewar ki satah, farsh ki satah. Jo cheez kisi satah par rakhi ho ya lagi ho, wo on hai.

At ka matlab hai kisi nuqte par, kisi khaas jagah par mojood hona. He is at home. She is waiting at the gate. The teacher is at the door. At jagah ko aik nuqta samajhta hai, us ke andar bahar ki baat nahi karta. Isi liye ghar ke liye at home kehte hain, daftar ke liye at the office, aur station ke liye at the station.

Ab teeno ka farq aik hi misaal se samjho. Aik admi Lahore me hai, ye bara sheher hai, to in Lahore. Wo aik kamre me hai, to in the room. Lekin wo darwaze par khara hai, nuqte par, to at the door. Aur us ki topi meiz par hai, satah par, to on the table. Dabba ho to in, satah ho to on, nuqta ho to at. Bas yehi teen alfaaz ka poora raaz hai.

Baqi jagah wali prepositions

Under ka matlab hai ke neeche. The cat is under the table. The shoes are under the bed. Aur over ka matlab hai ke upar, lekin baghair chhuye, hawa me ya dhak kar. The plane is flying over the city. He put a blanket over the child. On aur over ka yehi farq hai. On satah ko chhoota hai, over upar se guzarta hai ya dhakta hai.

Behind ka matlab hai ke peeche. The garden is behind the house. Koi cheez chhupi ho ya peeche ho to behind. Between ka matlab hai ke darmiyan, aur ye hamesha do cheezon ke beech ke liye ata hai. The school is between the bank and the hospital. Lahore is between Gujranwala and Kasur. Aur near ka matlab hai ke qareeb. Our house is near the school. Beside ka matlab hai ke baghal me, bilkul sath. He sat beside me. Aur in front of ka matlab hai ke samne. The car is parked in front of the gate.

Do bareek baatein paper ke liye

Pehli baat kone wali hai. Kamre ke andar wale kone ke liye in the corner kehte hain. The chair is in the corner of the room. Lekin gali ya sarak ke kone ke liye at the corner kehte hain. We met at the corner of the street. Andar wala kona in, bahar wala kona at.

Dusri baat beside aur besides ki hai. Beside ka matlab hai baghal me. Besides ka matlab hai ke ilawa. He sat beside me, yani wo mere baghal me baitha. Besides English, he knows Urdu, yani English ke ilawa wo Urdu bhi janta hai. Aik s ka farq hai aur matlab zameen aasmaan ka badal jata hai. Paper isay bohat pasand karta hai.

Board par qanoon Angrezi me

Board par ye qanoon kuch yun likha jata hai. In is used for something inside an enclosed space, as in the room and in Lahore. On is used for a surface, as on the table and on the wall. At is used for a point or a particular place, as at home and at the door. Under means below something, over means above something without touching it, behind means at the back of something, between means in the middle of two things, near means close to something, and beside means by the side of something. Aur aakhri line: beside with s, besides, means in addition to.

Misaalein board se

Ab board par chand misaalein likhta hun. Har misaal ke sath us ki wajah bhi samjho.

1. The keys are in my pocket.
Jeb aik band jagah hai, keys us ke andar hain, is liye in.

2. The book is lying on the table.
Kitaab meiz ki satah par hai, usay chhoo rahi hai, is liye on.

3. She is at home today.
Ghar ko yahan aik jagah, aik nuqta samjha gaya hai, is liye at home. In home nahi kehte.

4. The cat is hiding under the bed.
Billi bistar ke neeche chhupi hai, is liye under.

5. A bridge was built over the river.
Pul darya ke upar hai, pani ko chhoo nahi raha, is liye over.

6. There is a small garden behind our house.
Bagh ghar ke peeche hai, is liye behind.

7. Multan is between Bahawalpur and Muzaffargarh.
Do shehro ke darmiyan hai, is liye between. Between hamesha do ke liye ata hai.

8. Our college is near the railway station.
College station ke qareeb hai, is liye near.

9. He came and sat beside me in the class.
Wo meri baghal me baitha, is liye beside. Besides hota to matlab ilawa ho jata.

10. The chair is in the corner of the room.
Kamre ke andar wala kona hai, is liye in the corner. Gali ka kona hota to at the corner kehte.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Rozmarra zindagi me har waqt jagah batani parti hai. Kisi ko rasta batana ho, ghar ka pata likhna ho, daftar me file ki jagah batani ho, har jagah yehi alfaaz chalte hain. The office is on the first floor. Your form is in the second drawer. Sarkari angrezi me to har dusri line me jagah wali preposition hoti hai.

Ab paper wale andaaz ke do jumle dekho.

1. The candidates were waiting at the gate of the examination hall.
2. He placed the file on the table and stood beside the officer.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab wo trick jo in, on aur at ka farq kabhi bhoolne nahi degi. Teen tasveerein zehen me banao. At aik nuqta hai. Map par ungli rakho, wo aik nuqta hai, bas wahi at hai. Darwaza, station, ghar, sab ko nuqta samjho. In aik dabba hai. Kamra, sheher, jeb, sab dabbe hain, jo andar hai wo in hai. Aur on aik satah hai. Meiz ki satah, deewar ki satah, farsh ki satah, jo us par tika hai wo on hai. Nuqta, dabba, satah. Bas jab bhi shak ho, poochho ke ye jagah nuqta hai, dabba hai ya satah hai. Jawab khud preposition bata dega.

Aur between ke liye aik chhoti si line. Between me khud tween chhupa hai jo two se milta hai. Do cheezein hon to between, do se zyada hon to among. Ye line bhi sath yaad kar lo, aage kaam ayegi.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ab wo ghaltiyan dekho jo Pakistani students paper me karte hain. Ghalat aur sahi sath sath likh raha hun.

Ghalat: The book is in the table.
Sahi: The book is on the table.
Wajah: kitaab meiz ke andar nahi, us ki satah par hai.

Ghalat: He is in home.
Sahi: He is at home.
Wajah: home ke sath at ka istemal pakka hai.

Ghalat: The picture is in the wall.
Sahi: The picture is on the wall.
Wajah: tasveer deewar ki satah par lagi hai, deewar ke andar nahi.

Ghalat: She lives at Lahore.
Sahi: She lives in Lahore.
Wajah: sheher bari jagah hai, dabba hai, is liye in.

Ghalat: The cat is on the table.
Sahi: The cat is under the table.
Wajah: billi meiz ke upar nahi, neeche hai. Urdu ke par par mat jao, manzar dekho. Neeche ho to under.

Ghalat: He is standing between the crowd.
Sahi: He is standing among the crowd.
Wajah: between sirf do ke darmiyan ke liye hai. Bheer zyada hai, is liye among.

Ghalat: Beside English, he knows Urdu.
Sahi: Besides English, he knows Urdu.
Wajah: yahan matlab ilawa hai, baghal me nahi. S wala besides ilawa ke liye hai.

Ghalat: The shop is on the corner of the room.
Sahi: The chair is in the corner of the room.
Wajah: kamre ke andar wala kona in leta hai.

Ghalat: The bridge is on the river.
Sahi: The bridge is over the river.
Wajah: pul darya ko chhoota nahi, us ke upar se guzarta hai, is liye over ayega, on nahi.

Ghalat: There is a garden in front the house.
Sahi: There is a garden in front of the house.
Wajah: in front of teen alfaaz ka jod hai. Of lazmi hai, ye aksar gir jata hai.

Ghalat: Our house is between the park.
Sahi: Our house is near the park.
Wajah: between ke liye do cheezein chahiye. Aik hi park hai, aur matlab qareeb ka hai, is liye near.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Pehli tasveer nuqta, dabba, satah wali ho. Teen khane bane hon. Pehle khane me aik map par nuqta bana ho aur neeche AT likha ho, sath at home aur at the door ki misaalein hon. Dusre khane me aik dabba bana ho jis ke andar aik seb rakha ho, neeche IN likha ho, sath in the room aur in Lahore ki misaalein hon. Teesre khane me aik meiz ki satah par kitaab rakhi ho, neeche ON likha ho, sath on the table aur on the wall ki misaalein hon. Ye aik tasveer teeno bare farq zehen me bitha degi.

Dusri tasveer billi aur meiz wali ho, jo is mazmoon ki sab se mashhoor tasveer hai. Aik meiz bani ho. Billi kabhi meiz ke upar ho to ON, kabhi neeche ho to UNDER, kabhi peeche ho to BEHIND, kabhi do kursiyo ke darmiyan ho to BETWEEN, aur kabhi meiz ke qareeb ho to NEAR. Har billi ke sath us ki preposition bare harfo me likhi ho. Student tasveer dekh kar har jagah wali preposition pehchan lega aur ye tasveer us ke zehen me hamesha rahegi.
