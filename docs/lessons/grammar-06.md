Lesson 6: There Is and There Are
There Is aur There Are. Kisi cheez ke hone ka bayan

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me there is aur there are par sawal bar bar ata hai. Kabhi khali jagah me is ya are bharna hota hai aur kabhi jumla theek karna hota hai, jahan there are a book jaisi ghalti chhupi hoti hai. Pakistani students ki sab se bari uljhan ye hai ke wo there ka tarjuma wahan karte rehte hain, halanke is jumle me there ka matlab wahan bilkul nahi hota. Dusri uljhan ye hai ke verb kis ko dekhega, kyunke noun to verb ke baad ata hai. Is lesson me hum ye dono uljhanein hamesha ke liye khatam karenge. Jo student ye samajh jaye ke faisla peeche wale pehle noun ka hota hai, us ke liye ye sawal asaan ho jayega.

There ka asal matlab kya hai

Pehle ye baat zehen se nikaal do ke there ka matlab hamesha wahan hota hai. There do kaam karta hai. Aik jagah batata hai, jaise put the book there, kitaab wahan rakho. Lekin dusra kaam bilkul alag hai. Jab there jumle ke shuru me ata hai, to ye sirf jumla shuru karne ka zariya hota hai. Is ka kaam ye batana hai ke koi cheez hai, mojood hai, wahan hai.

Urdu me hum kehte hain, meiz par aik kitaab hai. English me ye jumla ulta chalta hai. Pehle there ata hai, phir is ya are, phir asal cheez. There is a book on the table. There are two books on the table. Dekho, asal baat kitaab ki hai, lekin jumla there se shuru hua. Aise samjho ke there darwaza kholne wala hai. Darwaza khulta hai, phir mehmaan andar ata hai. Mehmaan asal noun hai jo peeche khara hai.

Ab is aur are ka faisla

Qanoon bilkul seedha hai. Agar peeche wala noun aik hai ya gina nahi jata, to is ayega. There is a pen in my pocket. There is some water in the glass. Aur agar noun zyada hai to are ayega. There are three pens in my pocket. There are many students in the hall.

Lekin yahan wo trap hai jo paper bohat pasand karta hai. Jab aik se zyada cheezein aik sath ayen aur pehli cheez aik ho, to verb pehli cheez ko dekhta hai. There is a pen and two books on the table. Yahan kul cheezein teen hain, lekin pehla noun pen hai jo aik hai, is liye is ayega. There are a pen kehna ghalat hai. Isko zehen me yun bithao ke pehla mehmaan pehla faisla karta hai. Verb hamesha sab se qareeb wale noun ki baat manta hai.

Sawal aur nafi kese banti hai

Sawal banana bohat asaan hai. Is ya are ko there se pehle le ao. Is there a hospital in your town? Are there any colleges in this area? Jawab bhi chhota hota hai. Yes, there is. No, there is not.

Nafi me not lagta hai. There is no water in the bottle. There are no students in the class. Aik baat yaad rakho, nafi me aksar any ata hai. There is not any milk in the jug. Aur sawal me bhi any ata hai. Is there any sugar in the tea? Some ka kaam misbat jumle me hai, any ka kaam sawal aur nafi me hai. Ye chhota sa farq aage wale lesson me tafseel se ayega, abhi sirf pehchan lo.

There aur it ka farq

Ab wo farq jo is lesson ka sab se bareek nuqta hai. There aur it dono jumla shuru karte hain, lekin kaam alag hai. Jab kisi cheez ka zikr pehli dafa karna ho, us ka wajood batana ho, to there ata hai. There is a letter for you. Aur jab us cheez ka zikr pehle ho chuka ho aur ab usi ki baat aage barh rahi ho, to it ata hai. There is a letter for you. It is from your brother. Pehle jumle me letter nayi baat thi, there aya. Dusre jumle me wahi letter hai, it aya.

It ka aik aur khaas kaam hai. Mausam, waqt aur faasle ke liye hamesha it ata hai, there kabhi nahi. It is raining. It is very hot today. It is five o clock. It is far from here. In jumlo me koi cheez mojood hone ki baat nahi ho rahi, is liye there ki jagah hi nahi banti.

Board par qanoon Angrezi me

Board par ye qanoon kuch yun likha jata hai. There is used with a singular or uncountable noun, and there are is used with a plural noun. There itself has no meaning here, it only begins the sentence. When two nouns are joined by and, the verb agrees with the first noun, as there is a pen and two books on the table. To make a question, put is or are before there. Aur aakhri do lines: use there to tell of a thing for the first time, and it for a thing already mentioned. For weather, time and distance, always use it.

Misaalein board se

Ab board par chand misaalein likhta hun. Har misaal ke sath us ki wajah bhi samjho.

1. There is a book on the table.
Kitaab aik hai, is liye is. There ne sirf darwaza khola hai.

2. There are fifty students in the hall.
Students zyada hain, is liye are.

3. There is a pen and two pencils in the box.
Pehla noun pen hai jo aik hai. Pehla mehmaan pehla faisla karta hai, is liye is.

4. There is no water in the jug.
Pani gina nahi jata aur jumla nafi ka hai, is liye is aur no.

5. Is there any hospital in this town?
Sawal banane ke liye is aage aa gaya, aur sawal me any aya.

6. There is a letter for you. It is from Lahore.
Pehle jumle me letter nayi baat thi, there aya. Dusre me wahi letter hai, it aya.

7. It is very cold today.
Mausam ki baat hai, yahan it hi ayega, there kabhi nahi.

8. There are many candidates waiting outside.
Candidates zyada hain, is liye are.

9. Are there any seats left in the hall?
Sawal me are aage aya aur any ka istemal hua.

10. There is some rice in the pot.
Rice uncountable hai, is liye is. There are some rice kehna ghalat hai.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Rozmarra zindagi me kisi jagah ki tafseel batani ho to yehi jumla chalta hai. There is a bank near our house. There are three rooms in this flat. Daftar me kehte hain, there is a meeting today, there are two files pending. Kisi sheher ya gaon ka taaruf hi there se hota hai. There is a big mosque in our village.

Ab paper wale andaaz ke do jumle dekho.

1. There is a library and two laboratories in our college.
2. There are no shortcuts to success in the one paper.

Pehle jumle ko dobara dekho. Library aik hai is liye is aya, chahe peeche laboratories zyada hain.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab wo trick jo ye lesson kabhi bhoolne nahi degi. There ko darwaza samjho aur noun ko mehmaan. Darwaza khud kuch nahi hota, asal ahmiyat mehmaan ki hoti hai. Aur qanoon ye hai ke pehla mehmaan pehla faisla karta hai. Verb darwaze ko nahi dekhta, wo peeche khare pehle mehmaan ko dekhta hai. Mehmaan aik hai to is, mehmaan zyada hain to are. Bas jab bhi jumla there se shuru ho, aik qadam peeche dekho. Pehla noun kya hai? Wahi tumhara jawab hai. Aur it ke liye aik line aur yaad kar lo. Mausam, waqt aur faasla, teeno it ke purane dost hain, wahan there ki daal nahi galti.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ab wo ghaltiyan dekho jo Pakistani students paper me karte hain. Ghalat aur sahi sath sath likh raha hun.

Ghalat: There are a book on the table.
Sahi: There is a book on the table.
Wajah: book aik hai, is liye is.

Ghalat: There are a pen and two books in the bag.
Sahi: There is a pen and two books in the bag.
Wajah: pehla noun pen hai jo aik hai. Verb pehle noun ko dekhta hai.

Ghalat: There is raining today.
Sahi: It is raining today.
Wajah: mausam ke liye it ata hai, there nahi.

Ghalat: There are some water in the bottle.
Sahi: There is some water in the bottle.
Wajah: water gina nahi jata, is liye is.

Ghalat: Is there are any students in the class?
Sahi: Are there any students in the class?
Wajah: students zyada hain, sawal are se banega.

Ghalat: There is three men at the door.
Sahi: There are three men at the door.
Wajah: men zyada hain, is liye are.

Ghalat: There is no any book in the bag.
Sahi: There is no book in the bag. Ya phir: There is not any book in the bag.
Wajah: no ke sath any nahi ata. Ya no akele, ya not ke sath any.

Ghalat: A book is there on the table. (Wajood batana maqsad ho to)
Sahi: There is a book on the table.
Wajah: kisi cheez ke hone ka bayan there wale jumle se hota hai. Ye English ka pakka andaaz hai.

Ghalat: There is a new hospital in our town. There is very big.
Sahi: There is a new hospital in our town. It is very big.
Wajah: dusri dafa usi hospital ki baat hai, is liye it ayega.

Ghalat: There are much milk in the jug.
Sahi: There is much milk in the jug.
Wajah: milk uncountable hai, is liye is.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Pehli tasveer darwaze wali ho. Aik bara darwaza bana ho jis par THERE likha ho. Darwaze ke peeche mehmaan khare hon. Pehle number par aik aadmi khara ho aur us ke upar IS likha ho. Us ke peeche teen aadmi khare hon lekin un ki taraf teer ka nishan baad me ja raha ho. Neeche line likhi ho ke pehla mehmaan pehla faisla karta hai. Ye tasveer qareeb wale noun wale qanoon ko zehen me pakka kar degi.

Dusri tasveer there aur it ke farq ki ho. Aik taraf aik nayi chamakti hui cheez bani ho jis par THERE likha ho ke pehli dafa zikr. Dusri taraf wahi cheez dobara dikhai de lekin halki rangat me, aur us par IT likha ho ke dobara zikr. Neeche teen chhoti tasveerein aur hon, badal aur barish, ghari, aur do shehro ke darmiyan faasla, aur un sab par IT likha ho. Student samajh jayega ke mausam, waqt aur faasla hamesha it ke hain.
