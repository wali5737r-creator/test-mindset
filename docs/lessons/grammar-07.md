Lesson 7: Possessive Adjectives and Whose
Possessive Adjectives aur Whose. Mera, tumhara, us ka, aur kis ka wala sawal

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me possessive par har saal sawal ata hai, aur is mazmoon ki aik ghalti to itni mashhoor hai ke paper banane wale usay lazmi shamil karte hain. Wo ghalti hai its aur it is wali, yani its aur apostrophe wale its ka farq. Is ke sath whose aur who is wale whose ka farq bhi bar bar poocha jata hai. Ye sawal is liye number kaatte hain kyunke sunne me dono aik jaise lagte hain, farq sirf likhne me hai. Is lesson me hum saat possessive adjectives ko pakka karenge, phir in do mashhoor dhoko ko itna saaf karenge ke paper me ankh band karke sahi jawab lage. Jo student apostrophe wala test seekh jaye, wo ye number kabhi nahi harega.

Pehle possessive adjective ko samajh lo

Dekho, possessive ka matlab hai malkiat wala. Meri kitaab, tumhara kalam, us ki gaari. English me ye alfaaz hain: my, your, his, her, its, our, their. My matlab mera ya meri, your matlab tumhara ya aap ka, his matlab us ka (larke ke liye), her matlab us ki (larki ke liye), its matlab is ka ya us ka (cheez ke liye), our matlab hamara, their matlab un ka.

In sab ki aik pehchan yaad kar lo jo is lesson ki jaan hai. Ye sab adjective hain, aur adjective hamesha kisi noun ke sath khara hota hai. My book, your pen, her bag, their house. Ye alfaaz akele kabhi nahi khare hote, in ke peeche hamesha aik noun ata hai. My ke baad kitaab, your ke baad kalam. Agar noun na ho to ye alfaaz bhi nahi ate. Isi pehchan se paper ke aadhe sawal hal hote hain.

Har pronoun ka apna possessive hai, joda bana lo. I ka my, you ka your, he ka his, she ka her, it ka its, we ka our, they ka their. Aur aik baat khaas yaad rakho. His aur her me aik farq hai. Her do kaam karta hai. Aik, us ki, jaise her book. Dusra, us ko, jaise I saw her. Jumle me her ke baad noun aye to ye malkiat hai, na aye to ye object hai. Paper is bareek farq ko bhi parkhta hai.

Ab sab se mashhoor trap: its aur it is wala its

Ab wo baat suno jis ke liye ye lesson likha gaya hai. English me do its hain. Aik its baghair apostrophe ke, is ka matlab hai is ka ya us ka, kisi cheez ki malkiat. The dog is wagging its tail. Yahan its kutte ki dum ki malkiat bata raha hai. Dusra its apostrophe ke sath hai, aur ye it is ka chhota roop hai. It is hot ka chhota roop its hot wali shakal me likha jata hai, apostrophe ke sath. Yaad rakho, apostrophe yahan malkiat nahi dikhata, ye ghayab letters ki jagah khara hai. It is ke i aur s ke darmiyan se jo hissa ghayab hua, us ki nishani apostrophe hai.

Parkhne ka tareeqa bohat asaan hai, aur yehi asal trick hai. Jumle me its ki jagah it is rakh kar dekho. Agar jumla ban jaye to apostrophe wala its sahi hai. Agar jumla bigar jaye to baghair apostrophe wala its ayega. The dog is wagging it is tail, sunne me hi ghalat hai, to yahan its ayega baghair apostrophe ke. Aur its raining today ko it is raining today kar ke dekho, jumla ban gaya, to apostrophe wala sahi hai. Bas, ye aik test har shak khatam kar dega.

Ab whose ki baat

Whose sawal wala lafz hai aur is ka matlab hai kis ka ya kis ki. Whose book is this? Ye kitaab kis ki hai? Whose car is parked outside? Whose ke baad bhi hamesha noun ata hai, bilkul possessive adjectives ki tarah. Whose pen, whose house, whose result. Sawal ka jawab malkiat me hota hai. This is Ali is book, ye Ali ki hai.

Lekin yahan dusra mashhoor dhoka hai. Whose aur who is wale whos ki awaaz bilkul aik hai, lekin matlab zameen aasmaan ka alag hai. Whose malkiat poochta hai, kis ka. Aur apostrophe wala whos, who is ka chhota roop hai, ye ye poochta hai ke kon hai. Whose is coming to the party, kon aa raha hai? Aur whose book is this, kitaab kis ki hai? Yahan bhi wohi test chalega. Whose ki jagah who is rakh kar dekho. Jumla bane to apostrophe wala, na bane to whose.

Aik chhoti si pehchan aur

Possessive adjectives ke sath aik aur khandan hai, possessive pronouns ka. Mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs. In ka farq sirf itna hai ke in ke baad noun nahi ata, ye akele khare hote hain. This book is mine. Adjective wale ke peeche noun lazmi hai, my book. Pronoun wala akele khara hai, mine. Is ki tafseel aage aik alag lesson me ayegi, abhi sirf pehchan lo ke noun aye to my, na aye to mine.

Board par qanoon Angrezi me

Board par ye qanoon kuch yun likha jata hai. Possessive adjectives are my, your, his, her, its, our and their. They always come before a noun. Its without an apostrophe shows possession, as the dog wagged its tail. It is with an apostrophe is the short form of it is. Whose asks about possession and means kis ka. Who is with an apostrophe is the short form of who is and means kon hai. Aur aakhri line: to test the apostrophe, put it is or who is in the sentence and see if the sentence still makes sense.

Misaalein board se

Ab board par chand misaalein likhta hun. Har misaal ke sath us ki wajah bhi samjho.

1. This is my book.
My ke baad noun book aya hai. Adjective hamesha noun ke aage khara hota hai.

2. The cat is drinking its milk.
Doodh billi ka apna hai, malkiat hai, is liye its baghair apostrophe ke.

3. It is a hot day today. (Apostrophe wali shakal me: its a hot day)
Yahan it is ka matlab hai, is liye apostrophe wala its ayega. It is rakh kar dekho, jumla banta hai.

4. Whose pen is this?
Kis ka kalam hai, malkiat ka sawal hai, is liye whose.

5. Who is at the door? (Apostrophe wali shakal: whos at the door)
Kon hai, ye sawal hai, is liye who is wala roop.

6. Her brother is a doctor.
Her ke baad noun brother aya hai, ye malkiat hai, us ka bhai.

7. I met her in the market.
Yahan her ke baad noun nahi aya. Ye object hai, us ko mili. Wohi her, do kaam.

8. Our team won its first match.
Team hamari hai, our aya. Match team ka apna hai, its aya. Aik jumle me dono possessive aa gaye.

9. Their house is bigger than ours.
Their ke baad house hai, adjective hai. Ours akele khara hai, pronoun hai.

10. The bird built its nest in the tree.
Ghosla parinde ka apna hai. Its ayega. It is nest kar ke dekho, jumla bigar jayega.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Rozmarra zindagi me malkiat har dusre jumle me ati hai. This is my seat. Where is your office? His father is a teacher. Daftar ki angrezi me bhi yehi hai. Apni darkhwast me likhte ho, my name is, aur letter ke aakhir me yours faithfully ata hai, jo isi khandan ka hai. Aur ghalti bhi yahin hoti hai, khaas taur par its wali, jo likhne wale ki samajh ka pata deti hai.

Ab paper wale andaaz ke do jumle dekho.

1. The dog hurt its leg while running after the ball.
2. Whose documents are these? These are mine.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab wo trick jo apostrophe wale masle ko hamesha ke liye khatam kar degi. Apostrophe ko ghayab letter ki nishani samjho. Jahan apostrophe hai, wahan kuch ghayab hai. It is se kuch ghayab hua to its bana, who is se kuch ghayab hua to whos bana. To jab bhi shak ho, ghayab hissa wapas rakh kar dekho. Jumle me it is ya who is pura likh kar parho. Jumla theek lage to apostrophe wala sahi hai. Jumla ajeeb lage to samjho yahan malkiat hai, baghair apostrophe wala ayega. Bas ye aik test hai, aur ye kabhi dhoka nahi deta.

Aur possessive adjectives ke liye aik line aur. Ye saat alfaaz naukar hain, malik ke baghair kahin nahi jate. Malik yani noun. Jahan ye nazar ayen, peeche noun lazmi khara hoga. Naukar akele dikhe to samjho wo is khandan ka hai hi nahi, wo pronoun hai.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ab wo ghaltiyan dekho jo Pakistani students paper me karte hain. Ghalat aur sahi sath sath likh raha hun.

Ghalat: The dog is wagging it is tail. (Apostrophe wale its ke sath likha jata hai)
Sahi: The dog is wagging its tail.
Wajah: yahan malkiat hai, dum kutte ki apni hai. It is rakh kar dekho, jumla bigarta hai.

Ghalat: Its raining heavily today. (Baghair apostrophe ke)
Sahi: It is raining heavily today. (Apostrophe wale its ke sath)
Wajah: yahan matlab it is hai. Ghayab hissa wapas rakho, jumla banta hai.

Ghalat: Who is book is this? (Whose ki jagah who is likhna)
Sahi: Whose book is this?
Wajah: sawal malkiat ka hai, kis ki kitaab. Kon hai wala sawal nahi.

Ghalat: Whose coming with us? (Who is ki jagah whose likhna)
Sahi: Who is coming with us? (Apostrophe wale whos ke sath)
Wajah: sawal kon ka hai, kis ka ka nahi.

Ghalat: This is me book.
Sahi: This is my book.
Wajah: noun se pehle possessive adjective ata hai, object pronoun nahi.

Ghalat: Their is a house near the river.
Sahi: There is a house near the river.
Wajah: their malkiat hai, un ka. Wajood batane ke liye there ata hai. Awaaz aik hai, kaam alag hai.

Ghalat: Your welcome to our city.
Sahi: You are welcome to our city. (Apostrophe wale youre ke sath bhi likha jata hai)
Wajah: yahan you are ka matlab hai. Your malkiat hai, tumhara. Ye bhi awaaz wala dhoka hai.

Ghalat: I saw she bag in the room.
Sahi: I saw her bag in the room.
Wajah: bag se pehle her ayega. She jumla shuru karta hai, noun se pehle nahi khara hota.

Ghalat: The school is famous for it is discipline.
Sahi: The school is famous for its discipline.
Wajah: discipline school ka apna hai, malkiat hai, its ayega.

Ghalat: Ours house is small.
Sahi: Our house is small.
Wajah: house noun mojood hai, is liye adjective wala our ayega. Ours akele khara hota hai.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Pehli tasveer naukar aur malik wali ho. Saat naukar bane hon jin par my, your, his, her, its, our aur their likha ho, aur har naukar ke peeche us ka malik noun khara ho, kitaab, kalam, ghar, gaari. Neeche line likhi ho ke ye naukar malik ke baghair kahin nahi jate. Us ke sath aik kone me mine aur yours akele khare hon aur un ke peeche koi na ho, taake farq saaf nazar aye.

Dusri tasveer apostrophe wale test ki ho. Aik jumla bara bara likha ho, the dog wagged its tail, aur neeche aik teer ke sath wohi jumla it is rakh kar likha ho, the dog wagged it is tail, aur us par bara sa ghalat ka nishan ho. Dusri taraf its raining wala jumla ho jahan it is rakhne se jumla ban jata ho aur sahi ka nishan ho. Ye aik tasveer ghayab letter wala test zehen me hamesha ke liye bitha degi.
