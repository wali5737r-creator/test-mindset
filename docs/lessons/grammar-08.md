Lesson 8: Adjectives and Adverbs
Adjectives aur Adverbs. Cheez ki khoobi aur kaam ki khoobi

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me adjective aur adverb ka sawal har saal ata hai, aur ye un sawalo me se hai jahan student ko lagta hai ke jawab theek hai lekin hota ghalat hai. He speaks good, she sings beautiful, ye jumle sunne me theek lagte hain lekin grammar me ghalat hain. Paper me kabhi sahi jumla chunna hota hai aur kabhi good aur well me se aik lagana hota hai. Is lesson ki khaas baat ye hai ke is ka qanoon bohat chhota hai. Adjective cheez ki khoobi batata hai, adverb kaam ki khoobi batata hai. Bas ye aik line samajh lo aur ly wale chand dhoke pehchan lo, to ye sawal tumhare liye asaan ho jayega. Ye buniyadi lesson hai, lekin paper me is ki ghalti bare bare tayyar students bhi kar jate hain.

Pehle adjective ko samajh lo

Dekho, jab tum kisi cheez ki khoobi ya burai batao, koi cheez kesi hai ye batao, to jo lafz ye kaam kare wo adjective hai. A tall boy. A beautiful garden. An honest officer. Cold water. Yahan tall larke ki khoobi hai, beautiful bagh ki, honest officer ki aur cold pani ki. Har adjective kisi noun ke sath jura hai, kyunke khoobi hamesha kisi cheez ki hoti hai.

Adjective do jagah ata hai. Aik noun se pehle, jaise a rich man. Dusra verb ke baad, jaise the man is rich. Dono jagah kaam wohi hai, noun ki khoobi batana. The sky is blue. She looks happy. Yahan blue aasmaan ki aur happy us ki khoobi hai.

Ab adverb ki baat

Adverb ka kaam kaam ki khoobi batana hai. Kaam kese hua, kab hua, kahan hua, kitna hua. He runs fast. Kaam bhagna hai aur fast bata raha hai ke bhaga kese. She speaks clearly. Bolna kaam hai, clearly bata raha hai ke boli kese. They came yesterday. Ana kaam hai, yesterday bata raha hai ke aye kab.

Farq zehen me yun bithao. Agar lafz kisi cheez ko describe kare to adjective. Agar lafz kisi kaam ko describe kare to adverb. Sawal pooch kar dekho. Larka kesa hai? Tall. Jawab adjective. Larka kese bhaga? Fast. Jawab adverb. Kesa wala sawal cheez ke liye, kese wala sawal kaam ke liye.

Adverb banane ka qanoon

Zyada tar adverbs adjective me ly lagane se bante hain. Slow se slowly, careful se carefully, quick se quickly, beautiful se beautifully. Ye ly hi adverb ki sab se bari pehchan hai. Jahan ly dekho, samjho kaam ki khoobi batanay wala khara hai.

Lekin ly lagane ke do chhote usool paper ke liye yaad kar lo. Pehla, agar adjective y par khatam ho to y badal kar ily ho jata hai. Happy se happily, easy se easily, busy se busily. Dusra, agar adjective le par khatam ho to sirf y lagta hai. Gentle se gently, simple se simply. Ye spelling wale farq paper me pooche jate hain.

Ab teen khaas dhoke

Pehla dhoka fast, hard aur late ka hai. Ye teen alfaaz adjective bhi hain aur adverb bhi, aur in ke sath ly nahi lagta. A fast car, adjective. He runs fast, adverb. Fastly kehna ghalat hai. Hard ka bhi yehi haal hai. A hard question, adjective. He works hard, adverb. Aur khabardar, hardly aik alag hi lafz hai jis ka matlab hai mushkil se. He hardly works ka matlab hai wo mushkil se hi kaam karta hai, yani karib karib nahi karta. Hard aur hardly ke matlab me zameen aasmaan ka farq hai. Ye is lesson ka sab se khatarnaak trap hai.

Dusra dhoka good aur well ka hai. Good adjective hai, cheez ki khoobi. A good player. Well adverb hai, kaam ki khoobi. He plays well. He is a good singer aur he sings well. Dono jumle sahi hain, farq sirf ye hai ke pehle me khoobi singer ki hai, dusre me khoobi gaane ke kaam ki hai. Aik aur baat, well sehat ke liye adjective bhi hota hai. I am well, yani meri sehat theek hai.

Teesra dhoka friendly jaisi alfaaz ka hai. Kuch alfaaz ke aakhir me ly hota hai lekin wo adjective hote hain, adverb nahi. Friendly, lovely, lonely, costly. A friendly man sahi hai. He behaved friendly ghalat hai, sahi hai he behaved in a friendly way. To ly dekh kar foran adverb mat samajh lena, pehle ye dekho ke lafz kis ki khoobi bata raha hai.

Adjectives ki tartib ki aik jhalak

Aakhir me aik bareek baat ki sirf pehchan kar lo. Jab aik noun ke sath kai adjectives ayen to un ki aik tartib hoti hai. Pehle apni raaye, phir size, phir umar, phir rang, phir asal jagah. A beautiful big old red Pakistani carpet. Raaye beautiful, size big, umar old, rang red, asal Pakistani. Is tartib ki tafseel aage ke lessons me ayegi, abhi sirf itna yaad rakho ke rang hamesha noun ke qareeb hota hai aur raaye sab se pehle ati hai.

Board par qanoon Angrezi me

Board par ye qanoon kuch yun likha jata hai. An adjective tells us more about a noun, as a tall boy and an honest man. An adverb tells us more about a verb, as he runs fast and she speaks clearly. Most adverbs are made by adding ly to an adjective, as slow becomes slowly. If the adjective ends in y, the y changes to ily, as happy becomes happily. Fast, hard and late are both adjectives and adverbs, and they never take ly. Aur aakhri do lines: good is an adjective and well is an adverb, and hardly means almost not, which is quite different from hard.

Misaalein board se

Ab board par chand misaalein likhta hun. Har misaal ke sath us ki wajah bhi samjho.

1. He is a careful driver.
Careful driver ki khoobi hai. Driver noun hai, is liye adjective.

2. He drives carefully.
Yahan khoobi drive karne ke kaam ki hai, is liye adverb carefully.

3. She is a good singer and she sings well.
Good singer ki khoobi hai, well gaane ke kaam ki. Aik jumle me dono farq saaf hain.

4. Usman works hard to pass the test.
Works kaam hai aur hard usi ki khoobi hai. Hardly yahan hota to matlab ulta ho jata.

5. The train is fast and it arrived fast.
Pehla fast train ki khoobi, dusra fast ane ke kaam ki. Lafz aik, kaam do, aur ly kahin nahi.

6. She speaks English fluently.
Bolne ke kaam ki khoobi hai, is liye ly wala adverb fluently.

7. He is a friendly teacher.
Ly ke bawajood friendly adjective hai, kyunke khoobi teacher ki hai.

8. The baby is sleeping soundly.
Sone ke kaam ki khoobi hai, soundly adverb hai.

9. She did the work easily.
Happy wale usool par easy se easily bana, y ghayab aur ily aya.

10. Ali is a brave soldier. He fought bravely.
Brave sipahi ki khoobi, bravely larne ke kaam ki. Pehle adjective, phir adverb.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Rozmarra zindagi me har khoobi aur har tareef inhi alfaaz se hoti hai. She is intelligent. He speaks politely. Daftar ki angrezi me likhte hain, your application has been carefully examined, jahan carefully adverb hai. Interview me bhi yehi parkha jata hai ke tum apni khoobi adjective me bata sakte ho ya nahi. I am hardworking, I work hard. Dono jumle sahi hain, bas pehle me khoobi shakhs ki hai aur dusre me kaam ki.

Ab paper wale andaaz ke do jumle dekho.

1. The officer listened carefully and gave a fair decision.
2. He is a fast bowler, but today he bowled badly.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab wo trick jo ye farq kabhi bhoolne nahi degi. Do doston ki tasveer zehen me banao. Adjective noun ka dost hai, hamesha usi ke sath ghumta hai aur usi ki tareef karta hai. Adverb verb ka dost hai, hamesha kaam ke peeche khara hota hai aur batata hai ke kaam hua kese. Jab bhi shak ho, bas ye poochho ke ye lafz kis ki khoobi bata raha hai. Cheez ki, to adjective. Kaam ki, to adverb. Aur good well ke liye aik chhoti si line. Cheez achhi ho to good, kaam acha ho to well. A good cook cooks well. Ye aik jumla dono ka farq zehen me pakka kar dega. Aur hardly ke liye zehen me bara sa khatra likh lo. Hard matlab mehnat se, hardly matlab mushkil se. Sath wale kabhi mat ghulna.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ab wo ghaltiyan dekho jo Pakistani students paper me karte hain. Ghalat aur sahi sath sath likh raha hun.

Ghalat: He speaks English good.
Sahi: He speaks English well.
Wajah: khoobi bolne ke kaam ki hai, is liye well ayega, good nahi.

Ghalat: She sings beautiful.
Sahi: She sings beautifully.
Wajah: gaane ke kaam ki khoobi chahiye, ly wala adverb.

Ghalat: He runs very fastly.
Sahi: He runs very fast.
Wajah: fast ke sath ly kabhi nahi lagta. Fast khud hi adverb hai.

Ghalat: He works hardly to pass.
Sahi: He works hard to pass.
Wajah: hardly ka matlab mushkil se hai. Mehnat ke liye hard ayega.

Ghalat: The food smells deliciously.
Sahi: The food smells delicious.
Wajah: smell jaisi verbs ke baad khoobi khane ki hoti hai, kaam ki nahi, is liye adjective.

Ghalat: She is a really well teacher.
Sahi: She is a really good teacher.
Wajah: teacher noun hai, us ki khoobi good se hogi. Well kaam ke liye hai.

Ghalat: He did the work very good.
Sahi: He did the work very well.
Wajah: karne ke kaam ki khoobi hai, is liye well.

Ghalat: The baby slept peaceful.
Sahi: The baby slept peacefully.
Wajah: sone ke kaam ki khoobi hai, ly wala adverb chahiye.

Ghalat: He behaved friendly with the guests.
Sahi: He behaved in a friendly way with the guests.
Wajah: friendly adjective hai, adverb nahi. Aise alfaaz ke sath in a friendly way kehna parta hai.

Ghalat: She writes very careful.
Sahi: She writes very carefully.
Wajah: likhne ke kaam ki khoobi hai, carefully ayega.

Jawab aur un ki wajah

1. Jawab B hai, well. Bolne ke kaam ki khoobi hai, is liye well, good nahi.
2. Jawab B hai. Singer ki khoobi beautiful, gaane ke kaam ki khoobi beautifully.
3. Jawab B hai, hard. Mehnat ke liye hard ata hai. Hardly ka matlab mushkil se hai, yahan ulta ho jata.
4. Jawab B hai, fast. Fast ke sath ly kabhi nahi lagta.
5. Jawab B hai, good. Student noun hai, us ki khoobi good se hogi.
6. Jawab B hai, studies. Hardly pehle hi nafi ka matlab rakhta hai, mushkil se. Us ke sath seedha verb studies ayega.
7. Jawab B hai, nice. Taste ke baad khoobi soup ki hai, kaam ki nahi, is liye adjective.
8. Jawab B hai, carefully. Chalane ke kaam ki khoobi hai.
9. Jawab C hai, friendly. Ly ke bawajood ye adjective hai. Baqi teeno asal adverbs hain.
10. Jawab B hai, easily. Easy ka y badal kar ily ho gaya, aur kaam ki khoobi chahiye thi.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Pehli tasveer do doston wali ho. Aik taraf aik larka khara ho jis ke upar NOUN likha ho aur us ke sath us ka dost khara ho jis ki shirt par ADJECTIVE likha ho, aur darmiyan me aik kitaab ho jis par tall, honest, good jaisi misaalein hon. Dusri taraf aik larka daur raha ho jis ke upar VERB likha ho aur us ke peeche us ka dost khara ho jis ki shirt par ADVERB likha ho, aur neeche fast, carefully, well jaisi misaalein hon. Neeche line likhi ho ke adjective cheez ka dost, adverb kaam ka dost.

Dusri tasveer dhoke wale alfaaz ki ho, teen khano me. Pehle khane me fast, hard aur late hon aur un ke upar likha ho ke ly nahi lagta. Dusre khane me hard aur hardly amne samne hon, hard ke neeche mehnat se aur hardly ke neeche mushkil se likha ho, aur darmiyan me bare harfo me likha ho ke matlab ulta hai. Teesre khane me good aur well hon, good ke neeche a good cook aur well ke neeche cooks well likha ho. Ye tasveer is lesson ke teeno bare dhoke aik nazar me saaf kar degi.
