Lesson 9: Verbs
Verbs. Kaam wale alfaaz, jumle ka engine

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me English ka aadha khail hi verb ka hai. Tense ka sawal ho, active passive ho, jumla theek karna ho ya khali jagah bharna ho, har jagah pehle verb ko pehchanna parta hai. Aur sach ye hai ke zyada tar students verb ko pehchan hi nahi sakte, is liye tense un ke liye hamesha andhere me teer chalana banta hai. Jo student verb, us ki shaklein aur madadgar verb ka farq pakka kar le, us ke liye aane wale saare tense wale lessons asaan ho jate hain. Ye lesson poori English grammar ki neev hai, isay halka mat lena.

Verb matlab kya hai

Dekho, verb ko samajhne ke liye bas aik baat zehen me rakho. Verb wo lafz hai jo batata hai ke kaam ho raha hai, ho gaya hai, ya kisi ki haalat kya hai. Ali kitaab parhta hai. Is jumle me parhta hai hi asal khabar hai. Ali aur kitaab to bas naam hain, asal kaam parhne wala hai. Agar parhta hai nikal do to jumla murda ho jata hai. Ali kitaab. Bas? Khabar khatam hi nahi hui.

Isi liye main hamesha kehta hun, jumla aik gaari hai aur verb us ka engine hai. Engine nikaal do to gaari khari to rahegi, lekin chalegi nahi. Noun gaari ka dhacha hai, lekin harkat verb se ati hai. Tum khud aazma lo. Koi bhi jumla lo aur us me se verb nikaal do, jumla wahin ruk jayega. Verb hai to jumla zinda hai, verb nahi to jumla sirf alfaaz ka dher hai.

Verb ki teen baree qismein

Ab verb ki qismein samajh lo, lekin ratta nahi, pehchan ke sath.

Pehli qism action verb ki hai. Ye wo verb hai jo koi kaam dikhata hai. Run, write, eat, teach, build. Ali tez daurta hai. Is me daurna aik kaam hai jo nazar bhi ata hai. Kuch action verbs ka kaam nazar nahi ata lekin hota zaroor hai, jaise think, know, believe. Main sochta hun bhi aik kaam hai, bas dimagh ke andar hota hai. Inhe state verbs bhi kehte hain, kyunke ye haalat batate hain. Paper me ye is liye ahem hain ke in par continuous tense nahi lagta, lekin wo baat tense wale lesson me ayegi. Abhi bas itna yaad rakho ke sochna, janna, chahna bhi verb hi hain, chahe haath paon na hilein.

Doosri qism linking verb ki hai. Ye verb kaam nahi dikhata, bas subject ko us ki haalat ya pehchan se jorta hai. Is, am, are, was, were, seem, look, become. She is a teacher. Is jumle me is koi kaam nahi kar raha, bas She ko teacher se jod raha hai. She looks tired. Looks yahan dekhne ka kaam nahi kar raha, thakawat wali haalat bata raha hai. Linking verb ko pehchanna asaan hai. Is ki jagah is ya was rakh kar dekho. Jumla phir bhi theek lage to ye linking verb hai.

Teesri qism sab se ahem hai, helping verb. Aur yahi is lesson ka dil hai.

Main verb aur helping verb ka farq

Har bare jumle me aksar do verb sath khade hote hain. Aik asal kaam karta hai, doosra us ki madad karta hai. Asal kaam karne wala main verb hai aur madad karne wala helping verb. Misaal dekho. She is reading a book. Is me reading asal kaam hai, ye main verb hai. Is sirf ye batane aya hai ke kaam abhi chal raha hai, ye helping verb hai. He has finished his work. Finished main verb hai, has helping hai jo ye bata raha hai ke kaam mukammal ho chuka hai.

Ab farq ko aik misaal se hamesha ke liye zehen me bitha lo. Daftar me aik officer hota hai jo asal file par sign karta hai, aur aik peon hota hai jo file utha kar lata hai, waqt batata hai, daftar khula hai ya band, ye khabar deta hai. Main verb officer hai, asal kaam usi ka hai. Helping verb peon hai, wo khud koi faisla nahi karta, lekin us ke baghair file aage nahi barhti. He will come tomorrow. Is me come officer hai aur will peon hai jo kal wali khabar laya hai.

Helping verbs ki fehrist chhoti si hai aur ye ratne ke bajaye pehchan se yaad hoti hai. Is, am, are, was, were. Has, have, had. Do, does, did. Aur phir will, shall, can, could, may, might, must, should, would. Ye jab bhi kisi main verb ke sath khade hon to helping hain. Lekin khabardar, yehi alfaaz kabhi kabhi akelay main verb bhi ban jate hain. She is happy. Yahan is akela hai, koi aur verb nahi, to yehi main verb ka kaam kar raha hai, linking verb ban kar. Pehchan ka usool ye hai ke dekho, kya is ke sath koi aur asal verb khara hai? Hai to ye helping hai, nahi hai to ye khud main hai.

Aik aur pehchan bhi seekh lo jo paper me bohat kaam ati hai. Transitive aur intransitive ka farq. Kuch verbs ko apna kaam poora karne ke liye kisi cheez ki zaroorat parti hai, jisay object kehte hain. He wrote a letter. Wrote ke baad a letter aya, kaam us par hua. Ye transitive hai. Kuch verbs akelay hi poore hote hain. The baby cried. Cried ke baad kuch nahi chahiye, jumla poora hai. Ye intransitive hai. Abhi bas pehchan seekho, passive voice wale lesson me yehi farq tumhare bohat kaam ayega, kyunke passive sirf transitive verb ka banta hai.

Verb ki teen shaklein, V1 V2 V3

Ab is lesson ka doosra bara hissa. Har verb ki teen shaklein hoti hain jinhe duniya bhar me V1, V2 aur V3 kaha jata hai. V1 asal shakal hai, jo dictionary me milti hai, jaise go, write, eat. V2 guzre hue waqt ki shakal hai, jaise went, wrote, ate. V3 wo shakal hai jo has, have ya be ke sath mil kar kaam poora karti hai, jaise gone, written, eaten.

In teenon ko aik waqt wali misaal se samjho. Tum kal bazaar gaye thay. Jana aik hi kaam hai. Aaj ki baat karo to go hai. Kal ki baat karo to went hai. Aur jab kehte ho ke main ja chuka hun, to gone hai. Aik hi kaam, teen libaas, aur libaas waqt dekh kar badalta hai. Paper ka aadha tense wala hissa inhi teen shaklon ki pehchan par khara hai. Jis student ko pachaas ahem verbs ki teenon shaklein yaad hain, wo tense ke sawal me kabhi nahi phansta.

Kuch verbs shareef hote hain. Un ki V2 aur V3 me sirf ed lag jata hai. Play, played, played. Work, worked, worked. Inhe regular verbs kehte hain. Lekin kuch verbs purane aur ziddi hote hain. Un ki shaklein andar se badal jati hain. Go, went, gone. Write, wrote, written. Eat, ate, eaten. See, saw, seen. Take, took, taken. Break, broke, broken. Speak, spoke, spoken. Drive, drove, driven. Inhe irregular verbs kehte hain aur yehi paper ke pasandida hain. In ka koi usool nahi hota, ye istemal aur dohrane se yaad hote hain. Aik mashwara dun? Roz paanch verbs ki teenon shaklein aik copy par likho aur bol kar parho. Aik mahine me tumhari fehrist paper wali fehrist ban jayegi.

Board par qanoon Angrezi me

Ustad board par kuch yun likhta hai. A verb is a word that shows an action, an occurrence or a state of being. Every verb has three main forms. The first form, V1, is the base form: go, write, eat. The second form, V2, is used for the past: went, wrote, ate. The third form, V3, is the past participle, used with has, have, had and be: gone, written, eaten. A helping verb helps the main verb to show tense or mood: is, am, are, was, were, has, have, had, do, does, did, will, can, must. In "She is reading", reading is the main verb and is is the helping verb. Regular verbs add ed in V2 and V3, but irregular verbs change their form, as go becomes went and gone.

Misaalein board se

Ab board par chand misaalein likhta hun. Har misaal me verb ko pehchanna seekho, sirf jumla mat dekho.

1. The boys are playing football in the ground.
Playing main verb hai aur are us ka helping hai. Kaam chal raha hai.

2. She has written three letters today.
Written main verb hai, ye write ki V3 shakal hai. Has helping hai.

3. He went to Lahore yesterday.
Went go ki V2 shakal hai. Guzra hua waqt hai, is liye helping ki zaroorat hi nahi pari.

4. Ali is a hardworking student.
Is yahan akela khara hai. Koi aur verb nahi, is liye ye linking verb hai jo Ali ko us ki pehchan se jod raha hai.

5. They will build a new school here.
Build main verb hai aur will helping hai jo aane wale waqt ki khabar de raha hai.

6. The baby was crying all night.
Crying ke baad koi object nahi aya. Ye intransitive verb hai, akela hi poora hai.

7. She teaches English in a school.
Teaches action verb hai. Kaam saaf nazar araha hai.

8. He took my pen and broke it.
Took take ki V2 hai aur broke break ki V2 hai. Dono irregular hain, dono paper ke pasandida hain.

9. I know the answer.
Know koi zahiri kaam nahi dikhata, ye dimagh ki haalat hai. Phir bhi ye verb hai, state verb.

10. The window was broken by the wind.
Broken break ki V3 hai aur was us ka helping hai. Ye passive ki shakal hai, jis ki tafseel apne lesson me ayegi.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Jahan jumla hai wahan verb hai. Rozmarra ki har baat, daftar ki har application, akhbar ki har khabar, sab verb par khari hai. Tum apni zindagi ki koi bhi baat verb ke baghair keh kar dikha do, mumkin hi nahi. Aur imtehan me to haal ye hai ke tense, narration aur passive, teeno bare hisse verb ki pehchan se shuru hote hain. Verb pehchan liya to aadha paper pehchan liya.

Ab paper wale andaaz ke do jumle dekho.

1. The committee has approved the budget, and the work will start next week.
2. He spoke the truth and was praised by everyone.

Pehle jumle me approved main verb hai aur has helping. Doosre me spoke speak ki V2 hai aur praised ke sath was helping hai. Kaun sa lafz main hai aur kaun sa helping, ye pehchan hi asal khail hai.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab wo trick jo verb ko zindagi bhar yaad karwa degi. Jumle ko gaari samjho aur verb ko us ka engine. Jab bhi kisi jumle me verb dhundna ho to khud se aik hi sawal poochho: is jumle me ho kya raha hai? Jo lafz us ka jawab de, wahi verb hai. Bachay khel rahe hain. Ho kya raha hai? Khelna ho raha hai. Khelna verb hai. Wo mera dost hai. Ho kya raha hai? Hona ho raha hai. Hai verb hai.

Aur teen shaklon ke liye ye chhota sa jumla zehen me bitha lo. V1 aaj wala, V2 kal wala, V3 chuka wala. Go aaj, went kal, gone ja chuka. Has ya have dikha to samjho V3 ki bari hai, kyunke chuka wala kaam hamesha V3 me ata hai. Paper me jab bhi has, have ya had dikhe, us ke baad V3 dhundo, jawab wahi chhupa hota hai.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ab wo ghaltiyan dekho jo Pakistani students paper me karte hain. Main board par ghalat aur sahi sath sath likh raha hun.

Ghalat: He has went to school.
Sahi: He has gone to school.
Wajah: has ke baad hamesha V3 ati hai, V2 nahi. Went V2 hai, gone V3 hai.

Ghalat: She did not wrote the letter.
Sahi: She did not write the letter.
Wajah: did ke baad hamesha V1 ati hai. Guzra waqt did ne bata diya, main verb ko badalne ki zaroorat nahi.

Ghalat: He is play football.
Sahi: He is playing football.
Wajah: is ke baad chal raha kaam ing wali shakal me ata hai. Is akela main verb ka sahara nahi ban sakta.

Ghalat: They has completed the work.
Sahi: They have completed the work.
Wajah: has sirf he, she, it aur aik naam ke sath ata hai. They ke sath have ata hai.

Ghalat: She has break the glass.
Sahi: She has broken the glass.
Wajah: break ki V3 broken hai. Has ke baad V1 kabhi nahi ati.

Ghalat: He do his homework daily.
Sahi: He does his homework daily.
Wajah: he ke sath do nahi, does ata hai. Helping verb subject ko dekh kar chalta hai.

Ghalat: I am agree with you.
Sahi: I agree with you.
Wajah: agree khud aik poora verb hai, usay am ki zaroorat nahi. Ye ghalti Urdu ke "main mutafiq hun" ka seedha tarjuma karne se hoti hai.

Ghalat: She has ate her lunch.
Sahi: She has eaten her lunch.
Wajah: eat ki V2 ate hai aur V3 eaten hai. Has ke sath eaten ayega.

Jawab aur un ki wajah

1. Jawab B hai, written. Write ki teen shaklein hain, write, wrote, written.
2. Jawab C hai, is. Singing asal kaam hai, is sirf us ki madad kar raha hai.
3. Jawab D hai, went. Go ki V2 went aur V3 gone hai. Dono ko alag alag yaad rakho, paper yahi phansata hai.
4. Jawab B hai. Has ke baad V3 ati hai, aur go ki V3 gone hai.
5. Jawab C hai, finished. Has helping hai, asal kaam finished hai.
6. Jawab D hai, take. Take, took, taken andar se badalta hai. Baqi teeno ed wale regular hain.
7. Jawab B hai. Did ke baad hamesha V1, yani write, ati hai.
8. Jawab C hai, ate aur eaten. Eat irregular hai, is ka ed wala koi scene nahi.
9. Jawab B hai, intransitive. Cried ke baad koi object nahi, jumla akelay hi poora hai.
10. Jawab B hai, know. Know dimagh ki haalat hai, zahiri kaam nahi. Ye state verb hai.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye do tasveerein bohat kaam ki hongi. Pehli tasveer gaari aur engine wali ho. Aik gaari bani ho jis ka engine bahar nikaal kar alag dikhaya gaya ho. Gaari ke dhachay par noun likha ho aur engine par bara sa VERB likha ho. Neeche aik line ho ke engine nahi to gaari nahi, verb nahi to jumla nahi. Student aik nazar me samajh jaye ke verb jumle ki jaan kyun hai.

Doosri tasveer verb ki teen shaklon wali seedhi ho. Teen seedhiyan bani hon. Pehli seedhi par V1 aur go likha ho, doosri par V2 aur went, teesri par V3 aur gone. Har seedhi ke sath aik chhota sa jumla bhi ho, taake student ko dikhe ke aik hi verb waqt ke sath kese libaas badalta hai. Ye seedhi wali tasveer tense wale har lesson me kaam ayegi.
