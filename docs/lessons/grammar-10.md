Lesson 10: Question Sentences
Question Sentences. Sawal banana bhi aik fun hai

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me sawal wale jumle do tarah se ate hain. Aik to seedha sawal theek karna hota hai, jahan lafzon ki tartib ulta pulta di hoti hai. Doosra narration aur indirect speech me ata hai, jahan sawal jumle ke andar chhupa hota hai aur aksar students wahan ghalti karte hain. Is ke ilawa interview aur rozmarra zindagi me sawal poochna hi sab se zyada kaam hai. Khushi ki baat ye hai ke English me sawal banane ka aik hi bandha hua tareeqa hai. Wo seekh lo to har sawal khud ban jayega, chahe wo yes no wala ho ya wh wala.

Sawal ki pehchan

Dekho, sawal wala jumla asal me aam jumle ka ulta roop hai. Aam jumle me pehle banda ata hai, phir madadgar verb, phir asal kaam. He is coming. Lekin jab yehi baat sawal ban jati hai to madadgar verb bande se aage nikal ata hai. Is he coming? Bas, yehi aik harkat hai jo aam jumle ko sawal bana deti hai. Madadgar aage, banda peeche.

Main apne students ko kehta hun ke sawal me jumla apna coat ulta pehen leta hai. Coat wahi hai, alfaaz wohi hain, bas tartib palat gayi hai. Subject aur helping verb ne apni jagah badal li hai. Ye palatna hi sawal ki jaan hai. Aur yaad rakho, aakhir me sawal ka nishan, yani question mark, lazmi ata hai. Us ke baghair jumla sawal nahi banta, chahe tartib palat hi kyun na gayi ho.

Yes no wale sawal

Pehle wo sawal samjho jin ka jawab haan ya na me milta hai. Inhe yes no questions kehte hain. In ka tareeqa bilkul saaf hai. Helping verb sab se pehle, phir subject, phir main verb. Is he ready? Has she finished? Can you swim? Do they live here?

Lekin yahan aik masla ata hai jis par aadhe students phanste hain. Har jumle me helping verb pehle se mojood nahi hota. He plays cricket. Is me koi is, has ya will nahi hai. To sawal kese bane? Yahan aik naya madadgar bulana parta hai, aur us ka naam hai do. Does he play cricket? Dekha? Do apni jagah does ban kar aya kyunke subject he hai, aur sath hi plays ka s utar gaya aur play reh gaya. Ye baat sonay se likhne wali hai. Jab does ya did ajaye to main verb hamesha apni asal shakal, yani V1, me wapas chala jata hai. Guzra waqt ho to did ata hai. Did he play cricket? Play hi rahega, played nahi hoga. Kyunke guzra waqt batane ka kaam did akela kar chuka hai, verb ko dobara past banane ki koi zaroorat nahi.

Isi tarah jin jumlo me is, am, are, was, were, has, have pehle se hon, unhe bahar se madadgar ki zaroorat nahi parti. Unhi ko aage kar do, sawal tayyar hai. Are you a teacher? Were they present? Has he come?

Wh wale sawal

Ab doosri qism. Kuch sawal aise hote hain jin ka jawab haan ya na me nahi mil sakta, poori maloomat chahiye hoti hai. Ye wh alfaaz se bante hain. What kya, where kahan, when kab, why kyun, who kaun, whom kis ko, whose kis ka, which konsa, how kesay. In sawalon ka tareeqa bhi wahi hai, bas aik izafa hai. Wh lafz sab se pehle ata hai, us ke baad helping verb, phir subject, phir main verb. Where is he going? What does she want? When did they come? Why are you late?

Gaur karo, wh ke baad bhi wahi ulta coat wala qanoon chalta hai. Wh pehle ajata hai, lekin us ke peeche helping verb aur subject ki tartib sawal wali hi rehti hai. Aur does ya did aye to main verb phir V1 me hoga. What did he say? Say ayega, said nahi.

Aik khaas baat who wale sawal ki bhi samajh lo. Jab who ya what khud jumle ka subject ho to tartib nahi palat ti. Who broke the glass? Is me who khud kaam karne wala hai, is liye us ke baad seedha verb aya, koi do nahi aya. Who came yesterday? bhi aise hi hai. Lekin jab who se kisi aur ke bare me poochha jaye to tartib paltegi. Who did you meet? Yahan milne wala you hai, who sirf poochhne aya hai. Ye bareek farq paper ko bohat pasand hai.

Jumle ke andar chhupa sawal

Ab wo hissa jahan sab se zyada number katte hain. Kabhi sawal kisi bare jumle ke andar chhupa hota hai. I know where he lives. Dekho, is poore jumle ke aakhir me full stop hai, question mark nahi. Aur andar where ke baad he lives aya hai, lives he nahi. Kyun? Kyunke ye asal me sawal hai hi nahi, ye aik khabar hai ke main janta hun wo kahan rehta hai. Jab sawal kisi jumle ke andar chala jaye to wo apna ulta coat utaar deta hai aur seedha aam jumla ban jata hai. Subject pehle, verb baad me.

Yehi qanoon tell me, ask him aur do you know ke baad bhi chalta hai. Tell me where he is. Can you tell me what time it is? Yahan poora jumla sawal lagta hai lekin andar wala hissa seedha hai, what time it is, it is what time nahi. Paper is par har saal phansata hai, aur ab tum nahi phanso ge.

Aakhir me question tag ki pehchan bhi kar lo. Kabhi jumle ke aakhir me chhota sa sawal jura hota hai. He is late, isn't he? She can swim, can't she? Ye tag hai. Usool ye hai ke jumla haan me ho to tag nafi me hota hai, aur jumla nafi me ho to tag haan me. Abhi bas pehchan seekho, tafseel aage ayegi.

Board par qanoon Angrezi me

Board par qanoon kuch yun likha jata hai. To make a question, put the helping verb before the subject: He is coming becomes Is he coming? If there is no helping verb, use do, does or did, and the main verb returns to its base form: Does he play? Did he play? Wh questions begin with a question word, then the helping verb, then the subject: Where does he live? But when a question is hidden inside another sentence, it loses its question form: I know where he lives. There is no question mark and no inversion in the hidden question.

Misaalein board se

Ab board par chand misaalein likhta hun. Har misaal me tartib ko gaur se dekho.

1. Is she a doctor?
Is sab se aage aya, ye yes no wala sawal hai. Jawab haan ya na me milega.

2. Does he play football?
He ke sath does aya, aur plays ka s utar kar play reh gaya. Ye wala qanoon paper ka pasandida hai.

3. Did you sleep well last night?
Did aya to sleep apni asal shakal me raha. Slept likhna ghalti hoti.

4. Where has he gone?
Wh lafz pehle, phir has, phir he, phir gone. Tartib bilkul bandhi hui hai.

5. What do you want?
Do yahan helping hai aur want main verb hai. What ne sawal ko maloomat wala bana diya.

6. Who broke the window?
Who khud subject hai, is liye tartib nahi palti aur koi do nahi aya.

7. I know where he is.
Ye chhupa sawal hai. Where ke baad he is aya, is he nahi, aur aakhir me full stop hai.

8. Can you tell me what time it is?
Bahir wala sawal can se bana hai, lekin andar wala hissa seedha hai, it is.

9. He is your brother, isn't he?
Jumla haan me hai, is liye tag nafi me aya, isn't he.

10. Why did she cry?
Did ke sath cry V1 me hai. Cried hota to sawal ghalat ho jata.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Sawal poochna zindagi ka roz ka kaam hai. Interview me, daftar me, bazaar me, har jagah. Aur jo banda sahi sawal banana janta hai, us ki English foran parakhi jati hai. Paper ke andar bhi yehi haal hai. Direct sawal bhi ata hai, aur narration ke andar chhupa sawal bhi.

Ab paper wale andaaz ke do jumle dekho.

1. The teacher asked the students if they had completed their homework.
2. Do you know where the office of the deputy commissioner is?

Pehle jumle me sawal reported hai, is liye if ke baad seedhi tartib ayi hai. Doosre me andar wala sawal the office is ki seedhi shakal me hai, is the office nahi. Dono me wahi chhupe sawal wala qanoon chala hai.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab wo trick jo sawal banana kabhi bhulne nahi degi. Sawal ka jumla aik bandhi hui train hai, aur us ke teen dabbe hamesha isi tartib me lagte hain. Pehle madadgar, phir banda, phir kaam. Helping verb, subject, main verb. Ye teen alfaaz apne zehen me train ke dabbo ki tarah jod lo. Is he coming? Pehla dabba is, doosra he, teesra coming. Sawal banta hi is train se hai.

Aur jab jumla train ke baghair khara ho, yani us me helping verb hi na ho, to station se aik dabba bulana parta hai, aur wo hai do, does ya did. Lekin yaad rakho, ye bula hua dabba lagte hi asal kaam wala dabba apni asal shakal me wapas ajata hai. Does aya to plays se play, did aya to went se go. Aur chhupe sawal me train seedhi ho jati hai, ulta coat utar jata hai. Bas ye teen baatein, aur sawal ka poora chapter tumhari jeb me hai.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ab wo ghaltiyan dekho jo Pakistani students paper me karte hain.

Ghalat: Does he plays cricket?
Sahi: Does he play cricket?
Wajah: does ajane ke baad main verb V1 me wapas chala jata hai. S does ne le liya hai.

Ghalat: Where you are going?
Sahi: Where are you going?
Wajah: sawal me helping verb subject se pehle ata hai. Are ko you se aage ana tha.

Ghalat: Did he went to school?
Sahi: Did he go to school?
Wajah: did guzra waqt bata chuka hai, is liye go apni asal shakal me ayega.

Ghalat: I know where is he.
Sahi: I know where he is.
Wajah: jumle ke andar chhupa sawal seedha hota hai. Subject pehle, verb baad me.

Ghalat: What she wants?
Sahi: What does she want?
Wajah: sawal ko helping verb chahiye. She ke sath does ayega aur want V1 me rahega.

Ghalat: Tell me where is the station?
Sahi: Tell me where the station is.
Wajah: ye darkhwast hai, seedha sawal nahi. Andar wala hissa khabar hai, is liye tartib seedhi hai aur aakhir me full stop hai.

Ghalat: Who did break the glass?
Sahi: Who broke the glass?
Wajah: jab who khud subject ho to do ya did nahi ata, verb seedha V2 me ata hai.

Ghalat: Is he has a car?
Sahi: Does he have a car? Ya phir: Has he a car?
Wajah: aik jumle me do helping verb aise sath nahi khade hote. Has ho to is nahi ayega.

2. Jawab B hai, Have. You ke sath have ata hai aur finished V3 hai, is liye has ya is nahi chalega.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye do tasveerein bohat kaam ki hongi. Pehli tasveer train wali ho. Teen dabbo wali train bani ho. Pehle dabbe par helping verb likha ho, doosre par subject, teesre par main verb. Neeche wahi train ulta coat wali misaal ke sath dikhayi jaye ke aam jumle me dabbo ki tartib kya thi aur sawal me kese badli. Student aik nazar me sawal ki poori kahani samajh jayega.

Doosri tasveer chhupe sawal wali ho. Aik bara sa jumla aik khirki ki tarah bana ho jis ke andar aik chhota seedha jumla betha ho. Bahir wale hisse par likha ho ke ye khabar hai, aur andar wale hisse par nishan laga ho ke sawal yahan seedha ho gaya hai. I know where he is wali misaal is tasveer me sab se neeche bare alfaaz me likhi ho, kyunke paper ka sab se mehnga number isi aik line me chhupa hai.
