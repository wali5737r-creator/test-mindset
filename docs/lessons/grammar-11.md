Lesson 11: Negative Sentences
Negative Sentences. Nafi ka aik hi darwaza hai

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me negative jumle har jaga ghuse hue hain. Jumla theek karne wale sawal me doesn't went jaisi ghalti chhupi hoti hai, narration me not ki jagah badli hoti hai, aur double negative wala trap to examiner ka khaas pasandida hai. Pakistani students Urdu ke asar me sab se zyada yahi ghaltiyan karte hain, kyunke Urdu me do nafi aik sath chal jati hain aur English me nahi chalti. Ye lesson un saari ghaltiyon ka aik hi ilaj hai. Nafi ka qanoon aik hai, chhota hai, aur hamesha chalta hai.

Nafi matlab kya hai

Dekho, nafi matlab hai inkaar. Jumle me kisi baat ko na kehna, kaam ke na hone ko batana. He is coming ka matlab hai wo araha hai. He is not coming ka matlab hai wo nahi araha. Bas aik chhota sa lafz not beech me aya aur poori baat palat gayi. Itna taqatwar hai ye lafz. Lekin is ki taqat ke sath is ka aik sakht usool bhi hai. Not hamesha helping verb ke peeche baithta hai, kabhi aage nahi, kabhi akela nahi.

Ye baat zehen me bitha lo. Not aik mehmaan hai jo hamesha madadgar ki godi me baithta hai. Is hai to is not. Has hai to has not. Will hai to will not. Can hai to cannot, aur ye cannot hamesha aik hi lafz likha jata hai, ye chhoti si baat paper me ati hai. Aur jahan helping verb ho hi na to? Wahan wahi purana dost bulana parta hai, do, does ya did, aur not us ke peeche baith jata hai. He does not play. They did not come. Dekha, jahan madadgar nahi tha, wahan pehle madadgar bulaya gaya, phir not ko bithaya gaya. Not kabhi nangay sar nahi ghumta, usay hamesha kisi madadgar ka kandha chahiye.

Do not, does not, did not ka qanoon

Ab wo hissa jahan sab se zyada number katte hain. Jab do, does ya did aye to main verb hamesha apni asal shakal, yani V1, me wapas chala jata hai. He does not play cricket. Plays nahi, play. Kyun? Kyunke does ne pehle hi bata diya hai ke baat he ki ho rahi hai. S does ke paas chala gaya, verb ke paas kuch nahi bacha. She did not write the letter. Wrote nahi, write. Kyunke guzra waqt did ne bata diya. Verb ko dobara past banane ki zaroorat khatam ho gayi.

Isko yun samjho. Does aur did mehmaan nawaz hain. Wo ghar ate hi saara bojh apne kandho par utha lete hain. Waqt ka bojh bhi un ka, subject ka bojh bhi un ka. Main verb halka phulka apni asal shakal me reh jata hai. Jab bhi paper me does not ya did not dikhe, us ke baad V1 dhundo. Agar wahan V2 ya s wala verb khara hai to jumla ghalat hai, aankhein band karke nishan laga do.

Roz ki bol chal me in ki chhoti shaklein chalti hain. Do not ka don't, does not ka doesn't, did not ka didn't, is not ka isn't, has not ka hasn't, will not ka won't. Ye won't wali shakal khaas yaad rakho, will not se won't banta hai, willn't kabhi nahi hota. Paper formal English mangta hai, lekin pehchan ke liye ye shaklein janna zaroori hai.

Double negative, sab se bara trap

Ab is lesson ka sab se mehnga hissa suno. English me aik jumle me aik hi nafi chalti hai. Do nafi aik sath ajayein to wo aik doosri ko kaat deti hain aur jumla haan ban jata hai. Ye bilkul hisaab wala qanoon hai. Minus aur minus mil kar plus ban jata hai. I don't have nothing ka asal matlab ban jata hai ke mere paas kuch hai, kyunke don't ne nafi ki aur nothing ne dobara nafi ki, dono kat gayein. Sahi jumla hai, I don't have anything. Ya phir, I have nothing. Dono me se aik raasta chuno, dono aik sath nahi.

Urdu me hum kehte hain, mere paas kuch nahi hai. Yahan kuch aur nahi sath sath chal rahe hain, koi masla nahi. Lekin English me yehi jumla banane jao to phans jao ge. I have nothing theek hai. I don't have anything theek hai. I don't have nothing ghalat hai. Paper is trap ko bar bar lata hai, kabhi not ke sath never mila kar, kabhi no ke sath not mila kar. He does not need no help ghalat hai. Sahi hai, He does not need any help.

Isi qanoon ki aik aur shakal bhi yaad rakho. Kuch alfaaz apne andar hi nafi liye hue hain. Never, nothing, nobody, nowhere, no one. Aur phir hardly, scarcely, seldom, rarely. Ye alfaaz pehle se negative hain, in ke sath not lagana doosri nafi lagana hai. I hardly know him ka matlab hai main usay mushkil se janta hun, taqreeban nahi janta. Is me not nahi lagega. He never comes here theek hai. He does not never come ghalat hai.

No aur not ka chhota sa farq

Aakhir me aik bareek farq bhi seekh lo. Not verb ke sath chalta hai, no noun ke peeche nahi, noun ke aage ata hai. There is no water in the jug. Yahan no water ke sath aya hai. There is not water ghalat hoga. Aur aik mashhoor jumla yaad kar lo jo paper me ata hai. He has no money. Aur He does not have any money. Dono theek hain, dono ka matlab aik hai. Bas tareeqa alag hai. No noun ko pakarta hai, not verb ko.

Board par qanoon Angrezi me

Board par qanoon kuch yun likha jata hai. Not always comes after a helping verb: is not, has not, will not, cannot. If there is no helping verb, use do, does or did before not, and the main verb returns to its base form: He does not play. She did not write. Never use two negatives in one sentence: say I have nothing, or I do not have anything, but never I do not have nothing. Words like never, hardly, seldom and nobody are already negative, so do not add not with them.

Misaalein board se

Ab board par chand misaalein likhta hun. Har misaal me not ki jagah gaur se dekho.

1. He is not a teacher.
Not is ke peeche baitha hai. Yahi us ki asal jagah hai.

2. She does not like tea.
Does ke baad like V1 me hai. Likes hota to ghalti hoti.

3. They did not attend the meeting.
Did ne guzra waqt bata diya, is liye attend apni asal shakal me raha.

4. I have not finished my work yet.
Not has aur have wale khandan me bhi helping ke foran baad ata hai.

5. He cannot swim.
Cannot aik hi lafz hai. Can not do alag alfaaz me likhna paper me ghalti gina jata hai.

6. She will not attend the class tomorrow.
Will not ki chhoti shakal won't hai. Willn't koi lafz nahi hota.

7. He has no money in his pocket.
Yahan not nahi aya, no aya hai, kyunke nafi noun, yani money, par lagani thi.

8. I don't know anything about this matter.
Aik hi nafi hai, don't. Us ke baad anything aya hai, nothing nahi.

9. He hardly knows me.
Hardly apne andar nafi rakhta hai, is liye not ki zaroorat hi nahi pari.

10. Nobody was present in the hall.
Nobody pehle se negative hai. Was not nobody jaisa jumla kabhi nahi banta.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Inkaar zindagi ka roz ka hissa hai. Mana karna, mana hona, ijazat na milna, har jagah nafi chalti hai. Daftari angrezi me to aadhe jumle hi nafi wale hote hain. The application has not been approved. Candidates are not allowed to bring mobile phones. Aur paper me haal ye hai ke narration ho ya jumla theek karna ho, not ki jagah aur main verb ki shakal, yehi do cheezein parkhi jati hain.

Ab paper wale andaaz ke do jumle dekho.

1. The students did not submit their assignments on time.
2. He does not have any objection to this proposal.

Pehle jumle me did ke baad submit V1 me hai. Doosre me does not ke baad have V1 me hai aur any aya hai, no nahi. Dono jumle is lesson ke do bare qanoono ki zinda misaalein hain.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab wo trick jo nafi ko zindagi bhar yaad karwa degi. Not ko aik chhota sa bacha samjho jo hamesha apne bare bhai, yani helping verb, ki ungli pakar kar chalta hai. Bhai hai to bacha us ke peeche hai. Bhai nahi hai to pehle bhai ko bulana parta hai, do, does ya did ko, phir bacha us ki ungli pakarta hai. Bacha kabhi akela sadak par nahi ata. Jab bhi not ko kisi jumle me akela khada dekho, samajh lo jumla ghalat hai.

Aur double negative ke liye hisaab wala qanoon yaad rakho. Minus aur minus brabar plus. Do nafi kat jati hain. Jab bhi jumle me do negative alfaaz dikhein, don't ke sath nothing, ya not ke sath never, foran samajh jao ke ye trap hai. Aik nafi rakho, doosri ko any wale khandan se badal do. Nothing ki jagah anything, never ki jagah ever. Bas, jumla theek ho jayega.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ab wo ghaltiyan dekho jo Pakistani students paper me karte hain.

Ghalat: She does not likes music.
Sahi: She does not like music.
Wajah: does ne s apne paas rakh liya hai. Main verb V1 me wapas jayega.

Ghalat: He did not went to school.
Sahi: He did not go to school.
Wajah: did guzra waqt bata chuka hai, is liye go apni asal shakal me ayega.

Ghalat: I don't have nothing to say.
Sahi: I don't have anything to say.
Wajah: don't aur nothing do nafi hain, dono kat jayengi. Aik nafi ke baad anything ata hai.

Ghalat: He is not can swim.
Sahi: He cannot swim.
Wajah: can khud helping verb hai. Us ke sath is nahi ata, not seedha can ke peeche lagta hai aur cannot aik lafz banta hai.

Ghalat: She has not money.
Sahi: She has no money. Ya phir: She does not have any money.
Wajah: money noun hai. Us ke liye no ayega, ya tareeqa badal kar not ke sath any ayega.

Ghalat: He does not never helps anyone.
Sahi: He never helps anyone.
Wajah: never pehle se negative hai. Us ke sath does not lagana doosri nafi hai.

Ghalat: They wasn't present yesterday.
Sahi: They were not present yesterday.
Wajah: they ke sath was nahi, were ata hai. Nafi lagane se pehle helping verb ka subject se mel bhi dekhna hota hai.

Ghalat: I am not agree with this decision.
Sahi: I do not agree with this decision. Ya phir: I disagree with this decision.
Wajah: agree poora verb hai, am not us ka helping nahi ban sakta. Nafi ke liye do not ayega.

2. Jawab C hai, attend. Did not ke baad hamesha V1 ata hai.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye do tasveerein bohat kaam ki hongi. Pehli tasveer ungli pakarne wale bache ki ho. Aik bara sa helping verb aik bare bhai ki shakal me khara ho aur us ki ungli pakre aik chhota sa not khara ho. Neeche teen bare bhai aur dikhaye jayen, do, does aur did, taake student ko yaad rahe ke bhai na ho to in me se aik ko bulana parta hai. Tasveer ka matlab saaf ho ke not kabhi akela nahi chalta.

Doosri tasveer hisaab wali ho. Aik bade se board par minus aur minus ke brabar plus likha ho, aur us ke neeche English ki misaal ho ke don't aur nothing mil kar haan bana dete hain. Sath me sahi jumla bhi likha ho. Ye tasveer double negative wale trap ko hamesha ke liye zehen me bitha degi, kyunke hisaab ka qanoon koi student nahi bhulta.
