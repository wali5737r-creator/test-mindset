Lesson 12: Imperative Sentences
Imperative Sentences. Hukam, darkhwast aur mashwara

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me imperative jumle teen jagah ate hain. Pehle to seedhi pehchan ke sawal me, jahan chaar jumlo me se hukam wala jumla chunna hota hai. Doosra narration me, jahan hukam wale jumle ko reported speech me badalna parta hai aur yahi wo jagah hai jahan imperative ka asal imtehan hota hai. Teesra jumla theek karne me, jahan let ya do not ki ghalti chhupi hoti hai. Khushi ki baat ye hai ke imperative sab se chhota aur sab se seedha jumla hai. Is me na tense ka jhanjhat hai, na subject verb ke mel ka. Bas aik chhupa hua raaz hai, wo samajh lo to poora lesson tumhara hai.

Imperative matlab kya hai

Dekho, imperative wo jumla hai jis me hukam diya jaye, darkhwast ki jaye ya mashwara diya jaye. Close the door. Please sit down. Take this medicine twice a day. Teeno jumle imperative hain. Pehla hukam hai, doosra darkhwast, teesra mashwara. Lehjay alag alag hain lekin banaoat aik hi hai.

Ab wo raaz suno jis par poora lesson khara hai. Imperative jumle me subject dikhai nahi deta. Close the door me kaun darwaza band karega? Tum. Lekin you jumle me likha hi nahi. Subject chhupa hota hai aur wo hamesha you hota hai. Kyun? Kyunke hukam hamesha saamne khare bande ko diya jata hai, aur saamne khara banda you hi hota hai. Ustad class me kehta hai, Open your books. Kisi ko shak nahi hota ke kitabain kis ne kholni hain. Sunne wala khud samajh jata hai ke baat usi se ho rahi hai. Yehi haal har imperative ka hai. You andhere me khara hai, dikhta nahi, lekin hota wahi hai.

Aur doosri pehchan ye hai ke imperative hamesha verb ki asal shakal, yani V1, se shuru hota hai. Go, come, sit, read, listen. Koi is, koi has, koi s, kuch nahi. Verb bilkul nangay sar khara hota hai, kyunke na waqt batana hai, na banda batana hai, bas kaam batana hai. Aakhir me kabhi full stop ata hai aur zor wala hukam ho to exclamation mark bhi ajata hai. Stand up! lekin asal pehchan wahi hai, V1 se shuru aur subject ghayab.

Please wali narmi

Ab aik baat jo zindagi me bhi kaam ayegi aur paper me bhi. Hukam aur darkhwast me farq sirf aik lafz ka hai, please. Close the door hukam hai. Please close the door darkhwast hai. Please aage bhi askta hai aur peeche bhi. Close the door, please. Dono theek hain. Please lagte hi wohi jumla naram ho jata hai, lekin us ki banaoat nahi badalti. Wo phir bhi imperative hi rehta hai.

Daftar aur imtehan hall me tumhe ye jumle har jagah milenge. Fill in the form in block letters. Do not write your name on the answer sheet. Candidates are requested to sit in silence. Ye sab imperative khandan ke jumle hain. Aur ghar me maa ke jumle bhi yehi hain. Khana kha lo. Jaldi karo. Zindagi ka aadha bol chal imperative par chalta hai, bas hum gaur nahi karte.

Let us wale jumle

Ab imperative ki aik khaas shakal samajh lo. Jab bolne wala khud ko bhi hukam me shamil kar le to let us ata hai, jis ki chhoti shakal let's hai. Let us go for a walk. Is me hukam sirf sunne wale ko nahi, bolne wala khud bhi sath hai. Chalo hum sab chalte hain, ye matlab hai. Let us start the lesson. Let us pray for him.

Lekin khabardar, let ka aik doosra istemal bhi hai jo is se alag hai. Let me go ka matlab hai mujhe jane do, ijazat do. Yahan let ijazat wala hai. Aur let him sit here me ijazat teesre bande ke liye hai. Paper me ye farq ata hai. Let us me hum sab shamil hain, let me aur let him me ijazat mangi ya di ja rahi hai. Aik aur mashhoor shakal bhi yaad rakho. Let there be light. Aur imtehan wali, Let the door be closed. Ye imperative ki passive shakal hai, jis ki pehchan tumhe honi chahiye ke ye bhi hukam hi hai, bas andaaz daftar wala hai.

Negative imperative, mana karna

Jis tarah hukam diya jata hai, usi tarah mana bhi kiya jata hai. Mana karne ka tareeqa bilkul saaf hai. Jumle ke shuru me Do not laga do, baad me V1. Do not make a noise. Do not touch the wire. Do not be late. Gaur karo, be wala jumla bhi isi tarah banta hai. Do not be silly. Be yahan V1 hai.

Chhoti shakal don't hai. Don't shout. Don't waste your time. Aur aik sakht wali shakal bhi hai jo khaas paper me ati hai. Never ke sath. Never tell a lie. Never give up. Ye mana karne ka sab se zor daar tareeqa hai aur ye bhi imperative hi hai. Aik ghalti jo students karte hain wo ye hai ke do not ke baad verb par s ya ing laga dete hain. Do not goes ghalat hai. Do not go sahi hai. Do not aya ho ya na aya ho, imperative ka verb hamesha V1 hi rehta hai. Ye line rat lo, paper me kaam ayegi.

Reported hukam ki pehchan

Aakhir me wo hissa jo narration se jura hai. Jab kisi ka hukam kisi aur ko sunaya jata hai to jumla badal jata hai. The teacher said to the students, "Open your books." Ye asal hukam hai. Jab yehi baat aage sunayi jaye to banti hai, The teacher ordered the students to open their books. Dekha, hukam reported hote hi to ke sath V1 me chala gaya aur said ki jagah ordered, requested ya advised ajata hai. Hukam ho to ordered, darkhwast ho to requested, mashwara ho to advised. Aur mana kiya gaya ho to not to ata hai. He told me not to go there. Abhi bas pehchan seekho ke imperative reported hote hi to wala jumla ban jata hai. Poori tafseel narration wale lesson me ayegi, lekin ye pehchan abhi se pakki kar lo, kyunke paper me aadha sawal yahi hota hai ke konsa lafz ayega, ordered ya requested.

Board par qanoon Angrezi me

Board par qanoon kuch yun likha jata hai. An imperative sentence gives a command, a request or advice. Its subject is always "you", but it is hidden. It begins with the base form of the verb: Sit down. Open the book. Please makes it soft: Please sit down. For "we", use let us: Let us go. To forbid, use do not before the base verb: Do not make a noise. When reported, an imperative changes into "to" plus the base verb: He ordered me to go, and the negative becomes "not to": He told me not to go.

Misaalein board se

Ab board par chand misaalein likhta hun. Har misaal me chhupe hue you ko dhundo.

1. Close the door.
Seedha hukam hai. Subject you chhupa hai aur verb close V1 me hai.

2. Please pass me the salt.
Please ne hukam ko darkhwast bana diya. Banaoat wahi imperative wali hai.

3. Take two tablets after meals.
Ye mashwara hai, doctor wala andaaz. Phir bhi imperative hai.

4. Let us start our work now.
Let us aya hai, yani bolne wala khud bhi kaam me shamil hai.

5. Do not park your car here.
Mana kiya gaya hai. Do not ke baad park V1 me hai.

6. Never tell a lie.
Never wala mana sab se sakht hota hai. Ye bhi imperative hai.

7. Let me finish my work first.
Yahan let ijazat wala hai, mujhe poora karne do. Let us se farq gaur se dekho.

8. Do not be late for the exam.
Be wala jumla bhi do not ke sath V1 me rehta hai. Be late, be silly, be careful, sab aise hi bante hain.

9. Fill in the form with a blue pen.
Imtehan aur daftar ke jumle yehi hote hain. Hukam saaf hai, subject ghayab hai.

10. The officer ordered the guard to open the gate.
Ye reported hukam hai. Ordered ke baad to open aya hai. Asal jumla tha, Open the gate.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Imperative tumhari zindagi ke har kone me hai. Kitaabon ke mashq wale sawal, dawa ki botal par likhi hidayat, sadak ke board, imtehan hall ki hidayat, maa ki awaz, ustad ka hukam. Aur daftari angrezi to imperative se bhari hui hai. Submit the form by Monday. Attach your documents. Report to the office at nine. Jo banda imperative ko pehchan leta hai, wo hidayat ko kabhi ghalat nahi parhta.

Ab paper wale andaaz ke do jumle dekho.

1. Candidates are requested not to write anything on the question paper.
2. The doctor advised him to take rest for a week.

Pehla jumla darkhwast wali reported shakal hai, not to wali. Doosra mashware ki reported shakal hai, advised ke sath to take. Dono me imperative apna reported roop le chuka hai.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab wo trick jo imperative ko kabhi bhulne nahi degi. Imperative ka subject aik aisa sipahi hai jo andhere me khara hai. Dikhai nahi deta, lekin hukam usi ko mil raha hai. Jab bhi koi jumla V1 se shuru ho aur samajh na aye ke ye kis se keh raha hai, to zehen me aik you la kar jumle ke aage khara kar do. You close the door. Baat foran saaf ho jayegi. Ye chhupa sipahi har imperative me mojood hai, bas parade par nahi ata.

Aur reported wali pehchan ke liye ye chhota sa jumla yaad rakho. Hukam safar kare to to pakar leta hai. Jahan ordered, requested ya advised dikha aur us ke baad to ke sath V1 dikha, samajh lo ye kisi imperative ka reported roop hai. Aur nafi dikhe to not to hoga. Bas, ye do nishaniyan tumhe narration ke aadhe sawal hal karwa dengi.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ab wo ghaltiyan dekho jo Pakistani students paper me karte hain.

Ghalat: You please close the door. (imperative ke tor par)
Sahi: Please close the door.
Wajah: imperative me you likha nahi jata, wo chhupa rehta hai. You likh do to ye aam jumla ban jata hai, hukam wala andaaz khatam ho jata hai.

Ghalat: Do not goes there.
Sahi: Do not go there.
Wajah: do not ke baad bhi verb V1 me rehta hai. Goes ka s yahan nahi chalega.

Ghalat: Let us to play now.
Sahi: Let us play now.
Wajah: let us ke baad seedha V1 ata hai, to nahi lagta. To sirf reported wale jumle me ata hai.

Ghalat: Please to help me.
Sahi: Please help me.
Wajah: imperative seedha V1 se shuru hota hai. Please ke baad to lagana Urdu ke "meharbani karke meri madad karne" wale tarjume ki ghalti hai.

Ghalat: He ordered me that I should open the gate. (seedha reported andaaz)
Sahi: He ordered me to open the gate.
Wajah: hukam reported hote hi to ke sath V1 me badal jata hai. Yehi is ki pehchan hai.

Ghalat: Don't to make a noise.
Sahi: Don't make a noise.
Wajah: don't ke baad bhi to nahi ata, seedha V1 ata hai.

Ghalat: Never to give up.
Sahi: Never give up.
Wajah: never ke baad bhi verb apni asal shakal me ata hai.

Ghalat: Let me to go home.
Sahi: Let me go home.
Wajah: let ke baad har surat me V1 ata hai, chahe let us ho, let me ho ya let him.

Practice sawal

Ab apni samajh ko parkho. Pehle khud hal karo, jawab baad me dekho.

1. Which of the following is an imperative sentence?

B. Close the door.
C. He has closed the door.
D. The door is closed.

C. Do not going there.

A. Let me to finish this work.

10. "Take this medicine twice a day" is:
A. an order
B. a question

Jawab aur un ki wajah

1. Jawab B hai. Close the door V1 se shuru hai aur is ka subject you chhupa hai. Baqi teeno aam jumle hain.
2. Jawab C hai, you. Hukam hamesha saamne wale ko milta hai, is liye chhupa subject hamesha you hota hai.
3. Jawab B hai. Do not ke baad go V1 me ayega.
4. Jawab C hai. Let us ke baad seedha V1 ata hai, to nahi lagta.
5. Jawab B hai, request. Please ne hukam ko darkhwast bana diya hai.
6. Jawab A hai. Hukam reported hote hi ordered ke sath to stand up ban jata hai.
7. Jawab C hai. Never ke baad tell V1 me ayega.
8. Jawab B hai. Do not smoke mana karne wala imperative hai. Baqi jumle khabar wale hain.
9. Jawab B hai. Let me ke baad finish V1 me ayega, to nahi lagega.
10. Jawab C hai, advice. Dawa khane ki baat mashwara hai, doctor wala andaaz.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye do tasveerein bohat kaam ki hongi. Pehli tasveer andhere me khare sipahi wali ho. Aik darwaza bana ho, us ke saamne aik dhundla sa khara banda dikhaya jaye jis par halka sa you likha ho, aur upar bara sa jumla ho, Close the door. Tasveer ka matlab ye ho ke banda dikhe ya na dikhe, hukam usi ke liye hai. Ye tasveer chhupe subject ko hamesha ke liye zehen me bitha degi.

Doosri tasveer teen darwazo wali ho. Pehle darwaze par hukam likha ho aur aik sakht chehra, doosre par darkhwast aur please ke sath naram lehja, teesre par mashwara aur doctor ki shakal. Teeno darwazo ke neeche wahi aik hi banaoat dikhayi jaye, V1 se shuru jumla. Student samajh jaye ke lehja badal sakta hai, lekin imperative ki pehchan nahi badalti.
