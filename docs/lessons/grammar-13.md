Lesson 13: Uncountable Nouns
Uncountable Nouns. Jo gine nahi jate, un ki apni duniya hai

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me uncountable nouns examiner ka sab se pasandida topic hai, aur is ki wajah saaf hai. Pakistani students Urdu me har cheez ko gin lete hain. Aik mashwara, do mashware. English me ye nahi chalta. Advices, informations, furnitures, ye alfaaz paper me is liye rakhe jate hain taake Urdu soch wale student ko phansaya ja sake. Aur phanste bhi hain, har saal, har paper me. Ye lesson usi jaal ko kaatne ke liye hai. Aik dafa ye samajh ajaye ke konsi cheezein gini hi nahi ja saktin, to article wale sawal, much many wale sawal aur jumla theek karne wale sawal, teeno me number pakke ho jate hain.

Ginna aur naapna, asal farq

Dekho, duniya ki cheezein do tarah ki hain. Kuch cheezein alag alag utha kar gini ja sakti hain. Aik kitaab, do kitaabein, teen kursi, chaar seb. Ungliyon par gin lo, hisaab khatam. Inhe countable nouns kehte hain. Lekin kuch cheezein aisi hain jinhe alag alag utha hi nahi sakte. Pani ko gin kar dikhao. Aik pani, do pani? Nahi. Pani naapa jata hai, litre me, glass me. Chawal tola jata hai, kilo me. Khabar aur mashwara to haath hi nahi lagta, ginna to door ki baat hai. Inhe uncountable nouns kehte hain.

Main apne students ko ret wali misaal deta hun. Sahil par pari ret ko gin kar dikhao. Aik ret, do ret, teen ret? Hansi ayegi. Ret ke daane ginna mumkin hi nahi, is liye ret ko muthi bhar, bori bhar, kilo bhar kehte hain. Bilkul yehi haal uncountable nouns ka hai. Wo ginne ke liye bane hi nahi hain. Wo naape jate hain, tole jate hain, ya bas mehsoos kiye jate hain.

To pehchan ka usool ye bana. Jis cheez ko aik, do, teen keh kar alag alag na utha sako, wo uncountable hai. Aur jo uncountable hai, us ke teen qanoon hain. Pehla, us ke sath a ya an nahi lagta. Doosra, us ka plural nahi banta, aakhir me s nahi lagta. Teesra, us ke sath hamesha singular verb ata hai, is aur was wala. Ye teen qanoon is poore lesson ki jaan hain.

Pasandida fehrist

Ab wo fehrist suno jo paper me ghum phir kar ati hai. In alfaaz ko apne zehen me laal nishan laga kar rakho.

Advice, mashwara. Information, maloomat. Furniture, ghar ka samaan. Luggage aur baggage, safar ka samaan. Knowledge, ilm. Homework, ghar ka kaam. Scenery, manzar. Machinery, machinein majmooi tor par. Poetry, shayari. News, khabar. Progress, taraqqi. Traffic, sadak ka rush. Aur phir khane peene aur madden wali cheezein. Water, milk, rice, bread, sugar, salt, money, hair.

Money par khaas gaur karo. Money uncountable hai, lekin rupee countable hai. Two rupees theek hai, two moneys ghalat hai. Isi tarah hair uncountable hai jab poore sar ke baal murad hon, lekin aik safed baal ginna ho to a hair keh sakte hain. Ye bareek baatein abhi pehchan ki had tak yaad rakho, asal phansa yahi hota hai ke student har jagah aik hi usool laga deta hai.

Aur aik baat. Kuch alfaaz dikhne me ajeeb lagte hain. News ke aakhir me s hai, lekin wo singular hai aur uncountable bhi. The news is good. Furniture aur luggage bhi majmooi naam hain. Poora samaan aik lafz me agaya, is liye alag alag ginne ka sawal hi nahi uthta. Chairs gine ja sakte hain, lekin furniture nahi gina ja sakta. Table countable hai, furniture uncountable. Ye joda paper ko bohat pasand hai.

Much aur many ka farq

Ab miqdar batane wale do alfaaz ka farq samajh lo, kyunke ye sawal har paper me ata hai. Many sirf countable ke sath ata hai. Many books, many students, many rupees. Much sirf uncountable ke sath ata hai. Much water, much money, much information. Bas ye aik line hai. Gin sakte ho to many, nahi gin sakte to much.

Sawal me bhi yehi chalta hai. How many pens do you have? How much water is in the tank? Aur nafi wale jumlo me bhi yehi joda kaam karta hai. He does not have much time. There are not many seats left. Haan wale jumlo me roz ki bol chal me a lot of zyada chalta hai, dono ke sath. A lot of books, a lot of water. Lekin paper jab much aur many me se chunne ko kahe to wahi purana usool hai. Pehle ye dekho ke noun gina ja sakta hai ya nahi, jawab khud nikal ayega.

A piece of wala hal

Ab wo sawal jo har student poochta hai. Agar advice ginna hi hai to phir aik mashwara kese kahein? English ne is ka hal nikala hua hai. A piece of. A piece of advice. A piece of information. A piece of news. A piece of furniture. Piece ginna jata hai, aur us ke sath lagi cheez apni uncountable shakal me rehti hai. Do mashware kehna ho to two pieces of advice. Dekha, s pieces par laga, advice par nahi.

Isi khandan ke aur tareeqe bhi hain. A glass of water, a cup of tea, a loaf of bread, a kilo of rice, a bottle of milk. Har cheez ka apna bartan hai jis se wo naapi jati hai. Ye bartan countable hota hai, is liye us ka plural bhi ban jata hai. Two glasses of water. Lekin water waisa ka waisa rehta hai. Paper me jab bhi uncountable ko ginna ho to yehi bartan wala tareeqa sahi jawab hota hai.

Board par qanoon Angrezi me

Board par qanoon kuch yun likha jata hai. Uncountable nouns cannot be counted with numbers. They have no plural form and they never take a or an. They take a singular verb: The information is correct. Use much with uncountable nouns and many with countable nouns: much water, many books. To count an uncountable thing, use a piece of: a piece of advice, a piece of news, a piece of furniture. Remember these common uncountable nouns: advice, information, furniture, luggage, knowledge, homework, scenery, news, money and progress.

Misaalein board se

Ab board par chand misaalein likhta hun. Har misaal me pehle ye dekho ke noun gina ja sakta hai ya nahi.

1. He gave me a piece of advice.
Advice gina nahi jata, is liye piece ka sahara liya gaya hai.

2. The information given in the report is correct.
Information uncountable hai, is liye is aya, are nahi.

3. How much money do you need?
Money naapa jata hai, is liye much aya, many nahi.

4. There are many students in the hall.
Students gine ja sakte hain, is liye many aya.

5. She bought new furniture for her house.
Furniture majmooi naam hai. Furnitures kehna ghalti hoti.

6. The news is not good today.
News ke aakhir me s hai, lekin verb is hai. Dikhawa dhoka hai.

7. He has a lot of knowledge about this subject.
Knowledge gina nahi jata. Knowledges koi lafz nahi.

8. Please bring two glasses of water.
Glasses gine gaye hain, water nahi. Bartan countable hota hai.

9. His luggage was very heavy.
Luggage bhi uncountable hai, is liye was aya.

10. She made great progress in her studies.
Progress par na a laga, na s. Ye hamesha aise hi ata hai.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Daftar, bazaar aur rozmarra, har jagah miqdar batani parti hai. How much is this? I need some information. The furniture of the office. Aur paper ke andar haal ye hai ke article, much many, verb ka mel aur jumla theek karna, chaar tarah ke sawal is aik topic se bante hain. Isi liye examiner isay itna pasand karta hai. Aik topic seekho, chaar tarah ke number pakke karo.

Ab paper wale andaaz ke do jumle dekho.

1. The committee was given much information about the case, but little progress was made.
2. He bought a new piece of furniture and two pieces of luggage for his journey.

Pehle jumle me much information aur little progress, dono uncountable ke sath aye hain. Doosre me piece wala hal istemal hua hai. Dono jumle paper ke dil ke qareeb hain.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab wo trick jo uncountable ko zindagi bhar yaad karwa degi. Apne zehen me aik tarazu aur aik ungliyon wali ginti sath sath rakho. Koi noun samne aye to pehle khud se poochho: isay ungliyon par gin sakta hun ya tarazu par rakhna parega? Ungliyon par gina jaye to countable hai, many lagega, plural banega, a bhi lagega. Tarazu, litre ya muthi se naapa jaye to uncountable hai. Na s lagega, na a lagega, verb bhi singular ayega.

Aur ret wali baat kabhi mat bhulna. Ret ko koi nahi ginta, ret ko naapte hain. Jis din tumhe kisi lafz par shak ho, usay ret ke sath rakh kar dekho. Pani ret jaisa hai, information ret jaisi hai, advice ret jaisa hai. Jo ret ke khandan ka hai, wo uncountable hai. Bas, ye aik tasveer tumhe paper ke har trap se bacha legi.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ab wo ghaltiyan dekho jo Pakistani students paper me karte hain.

Ghalat: She gave me many advices.
Sahi: She gave me much advice. Ya phir: She gave me many pieces of advice.
Wajah: advice uncountable hai. Advices koi lafz nahi. Ginna ho to pieces of advice.

Ghalat: The furnitures of this office are old.
Sahi: The furniture of this office is old.
Wajah: furniture majmooi naam hai. Na plural banega, na are ayega.

Ghalat: He has many informations about the case.
Sahi: He has much information about the case.
Wajah: information gina nahi jata, is liye much ayega aur s bhi nahi lagega.

Ghalat: How many money do you have?
Sahi: How much money do you have?
Wajah: money naapa jata hai, gina nahi jata. Is liye much.

Ghalat: The news are shocking.
Sahi: The news is shocking.
Wajah: news dikhne me plural hai lekin hai singular aur uncountable.

Ghalat: She bought two luggages.
Sahi: She bought two pieces of luggage.
Wajah: luggage uncountable hai. Ginna ho to pieces ka sahara lo.

Ghalat: He gave me an advice.
Sahi: He gave me a piece of advice.
Wajah: uncountable ke sath a ya an nahi lagta, chahe matlab aik hi kyun na ho.

Ghalat: There is much students in the class.
Sahi: There are many students in the class.
Wajah: students gine ja sakte hain. Much sirf uncountable ka hai.

Jawab aur un ki wajah

1. Jawab B hai. Advice uncountable hai, ginna ho to a piece of advice.
2. Jawab B hai, much. Water naapa jata hai, gina nahi jata.
3. Jawab B hai. Information ka na plural banta hai, na are ata hai.
4. Jawab C hai, pieces. Piece countable hai, is liye two ke sath pieces, aur furniture apni shakal me rahega.
5. Jawab B hai. News singular hai, is ayega.
6. Jawab B hai, many. Books gine ja sakte hain.
7. Jawab C hai. Luggage uncountable hai, is liye is aur koi s nahi.
8. Jawab C hai, much. Time yahan miqdar ke matlab me hai aur uncountable ke sath much ata hai.
9. Jawab B hai. Knowledge uncountable hai, a lot of dono ke sath chal jata hai.
10. Jawab C hai, scenery. Manzar majmooi cheez hai, gina nahi jata. Chair, rupee aur student teeno gine ja sakte hain.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye do tasveerein bohat kaam ki hongi. Pehli tasveer tarazu aur ungliyon wali ho. Aik taraf aik tarazu bana ho jis par pani ka jug, chawal ki bori aur ret ka dher rakha ho, aur upar uncountable likha ho. Doosri taraf ungliyon par ginti hoti dikhayi jaye, kitaabein, kursiyan aur seb ginne jate hue, aur upar countable likha ho. Student aik nazar me faisla karna seekh jayega ke konsi cheez kidhar jayegi.

Doosri tasveer laal nishan wali fehrist ki ho. Aik bare board par wo alfaaz likhe hon jin par Pakistani students har saal phanste hain. Advice, information, furniture, luggage, knowledge, news. Har lafz ke sath us ki ghalat shakal par laal cross laga ho, advices par cross, furnitures par cross. Neeche piece wala hal bare alfaaz me likha ho. Ye fehrist wali tasveer paper se aik raat pehle dekhne wali cheez hai.
