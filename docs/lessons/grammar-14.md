Lesson 14: Possessive and Objective Pronouns
Possessive aur Objective Pronouns. Mine wale aur me wale alfaaz

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me pronoun ke sawal har saal ate hain, aur un me sab se mashhoor aik hi trap hai jo pachas saal se chal raha hai. Between you and me ayega ya between you and I? Aadhe se zyada students I laga kar number katwa lete hain. Is ke ilawa my aur mine ka farq, aur me, him, her, us, them ki sahi jagah, ye sab paper ke pakke sawal hain. Ye lesson in teeno ko aik sath saaf kar dega. Farq bohat chhota hai, lekin number is chhote se farq par hi milta hai.

Do khandan, pehle unhe pehchano

Dekho, is lesson me hum do khandano se mil rahe hain. Pehla khandan malikiyat wala hai. Mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs. Ye batate hain ke cheez kis ki hai. Doosra khandan wo hai jo kaam ka nishaana banta hai. Me, him, her, us, them. Ye batate hain ke kaam kis par hua. Dono khandan pehle wale pronoun wale lesson se age ki baat hain, is liye ghabrana nahi, tartib se samjhte hain.

Pehle objective khandan ko samajh lo, kyunke ye asaan hai. Har bande ki do shaklein hoti hain. Aik wo jo kaam karti hai, I, you, he, she, we, they. Doosri wo jis par kaam hota hai, me, you, him, her, us, them. He helped me. Is jumle me he kaam karne wala hai aur me wo hai jis par madad wala kaam hua. She called him. Him par call wala kaam hua. The teacher praised them. Them par tareef wala kaam hua. Usool bilkul saaf hai. Verb ke baad ane wala pronoun hamesha objective shakal me hoga. Aur preposition ke baad bhi. With me, for her, between us. Ye doosri baat zehen me laal nishan se likh lo, aage trap yahi se ayega.

Ab possessive khandan ki taraf ao. Yahan do tarah ke alfaaz hain aur in ka farq hi poore lesson ki jaan hai. Aik hain my, your, his, her, its, our, their. Inhe possessive adjectives kehte hain. Ye hamesha kisi noun ke sath chipke rehte hain. My book, your pen, her bag. Doosre hain mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs. Inhe possessive pronouns kehte hain. Ye akelay khade hote hain, in ke sath noun nahi ata. This book is mine. The pen is yours. Farq bas itna hai ke adjective ko sahara chahiye, pronoun akela raja hai.

My aur mine ka farq, dil se samjho

Is farq ko aik misaal se hamesha ke liye zehen me bitha lo. My aik naukar hai jo apne malik, yani noun, ke baghair aik qadam nahi chal sakta. My book, my car, my house. Jahan my hai wahan peeche noun khara hoga. Aur mine aik malik hai jo akela darbar lagata hai. The book is mine. Yahan mine ne my book dono ka kaam akela kar liya hai. Mine ke andar noun chhupa hota hai. Isi liye mine ke baad noun lagana doosri ghalti hai. The book is mine book ghalat hai. Ya to my book kaho, ya mine kaho. Dono aik sath nahi.

His is mamle me sab se shareef hai. His adjective bhi hai aur pronoun bhi. His book bhi theek hai aur the book is his bhi theek hai. Shakal aik hi hai, kaam dono karti hai. Aur hers, ours, yours, theirs par aik chhoti si pehchan yaad rakho. In sab ke aakhir me s hai. Lekin its par khabardar. Its bina apostrophe ke malikiyat wala hai, the dog wagged its tail. Aur it's apostrophe ke sath it is ka chhota roop hai. Ye trap possessive wale lesson me bhi aya tha aur yahan bhi ata hai, kyunke paper isay chhorta hi nahi.

Aik khoobsurat shakal bhi seekh lo jo paper me ati hai. A friend of mine. Aik dost mera. Yahan of ke baad mine aya hai, my nahi. A brother of hers. A student of ours. Ye andaaz angrezon ka pasandida hai. Of ke baad hamesha possessive pronoun ata hai, adjective nahi lag sakta, kyunke wahan noun maujood hi nahi jise adjective sahara de.

Between you and me, mashhoor trap

Ab wo hissa jis ke liye ye lesson likha gaya hai. Between you and I ghalat hai. Between you and me sahi hai. Wajah tum ab jante ho. Between aik preposition hai, aur preposition ke baad hamesha objective shakal ati hai. Me objective hai, I subjective hai. I sirf wahan ata hai jahan wo kaam karne wala ho. I am going. Lekin between ke peeche I kabhi nahi askta.

Ye ghalti hoti kyun hai? Kyunke students ko lagta hai ke I zyada izzat wala aur zyada sahi lafz hai, me kehna jaise badtamizi hai. Sach is ke ulta hai. Sahi jagah par me hi izzat hai. Aur ghaur karo, between you and me me you dono jagah aik hi rehta hai, is liye shak sirf me aur I par hota hai. Paper bilkul yahi deta hai. This secret is between you and me. Let me and him go bhi isi usool par hai. Let ke baad bhi objective ata hai, me aur him, I aur he nahi. Aur aik aur mashhoor jumla. He is taller than me bhi theek hai aur than I bhi suna jata hai, lekin paper ki formal English me comparison me aksar I am wala andaaz pasand kiya jata hai. Asal pakka trap between wala hai, us par koi behes nahi. Between ke baad me, him, her, us, them. Bas.

Aik aur chhoti si ghalti bhi saaf kar lo. Jab do pronoun sath ayen to izzat wala usool hai ke doosre ko pehle rakho. You and I are friends. You and me nahi, jab kaam karne wale hon. Aur jab nishaana hon to, He invited you and me. Dono me you pehle aya. Ye adab bhi hai aur paper ka sawal bhi.

Board par qanoon Angrezi me

Board par qanoon kuch yun likha jata hai. Objective pronouns come after a verb or a preposition: me, you, him, her, us, them. He helped me. She sat beside him. Possessive adjectives stand with a noun: my book, her bag. Possessive pronouns stand alone and never take a noun after them: mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs. The book is mine. After "of" we use a possessive pronoun: a friend of mine. And remember the famous rule: after "between" always use the objective form, between you and me, never between you and I.

Misaalein board se

Ab board par chand misaalein likhta hun. Har misaal me pronoun ki jagah ko gaur se dekho.

1. This house is mine, not yours.
Mine aur yours dono akelay khade hain. Kisi ke sath noun nahi aya.

2. She gave me her notebook.
Me objective hai, verb ke baad aya hai. Her adjective hai, notebook ke sath aya hai. Aik hi jumle me dono khandan hain.

3. The secret is between you and me.
Between preposition hai, is liye me aya. Yahi is lesson ka sab se mehnga jumla hai.

4. He is a friend of mine.
Of ke baad mine ata hai, my nahi.

5. The dog was wagging its tail.
Its malikiyat wala hai, bina apostrophe ke. It's hota to matlab it is ho jata.

6. They invited us to the dinner.
Us objective hai. Invited wala kaam hum par hua hai.

7. This pen is hers, and that one is his.
Hers aur his dono possessive pronoun hain. His ki shakal adjective wali bhi yahi hoti hai.

8. Let him complete his work.
Let ke baad him aya, he nahi. Aur his work me his adjective hai.

9. Our school is bigger than theirs.
Theirs akela khara hai. Us ke peeche school dobara nahi likha gaya, mine wale usool ki tarah theirs ne noun ka kaam bhi kar liya.

10. You and I will go to the market.
Yahan dono kaam karne wale hain, is liye subjective shaklein ayi hain, aur izzat ke usool par you pehle aya hai.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Rozmarra ki baat cheet me pronoun har doosre jumle me ata hai. Galti ho to sunne wale ko foran pata chal jata hai ke banda English me kacha hai. Daftari khat o kitabat me bhi yehi haal hai. Interviews me between you and me wala jumla bolne wale ko alag hi pehchan milti hai. Aur paper me to ye seedha number hai, kabhi objective ki jagah par, kabhi mine aur my ke farq par.

Ab paper wale andaaz ke do jumle dekho.

1. The principal asked Ali and me to attend the meeting.
2. This land is ours, and the house built on it is theirs.

Pehle jumle me asked ke baad me aya hai, I nahi, kyunke me yahan nishaana hai. Doosre me ours aur theirs akelay khade hain, bina kisi noun ke. Dono jumle is lesson ke do bare qanoono par khade hain.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab wo trick jo dono khandan zindagi bhar yaad karwa degi. My ko naukar samjho aur mine ko malik. Naukar malik ke baghair nahi chal sakta, is liye my hamesha noun ke sath ayega. Malik akela darbar lagata hai, is liye mine hamesha akela ayega. Jab bhi shak ho, bas ye poochho: peeche noun khara hai ya nahi? Khara hai to my wala khandan, nahi hai to mine wala khandan. Masla hal.

Aur between wale trap ke liye aik chhota sa imtehan zehen me rakho. You ko jumle se nikaal kar dekho. Between I, ajeeb lagta hai na? Between me, bilkul theek lagta hai. Jab do alfaaz sath hon to unhe alag alag karke parkho, ghalti khud bol parti hai. Ye parkhne wala tareeqa har us jagah kaam ayega jahan and ya between ke sath do pronoun khade hon.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ab wo ghaltiyan dekho jo Pakistani students paper me karte hain.

Ghalat: This matter is between you and I.
Sahi: This matter is between you and me.
Wajah: between preposition hai aur us ke baad objective shakal ati hai. I kaam karne walon ki shakal hai.

Ghalat: The book is mine book.
Sahi: The book is mine. Ya phir: This is my book.
Wajah: mine ke andar noun chhupa hota hai. Mine ke baad noun dobara nahi lagta.

Ghalat: He is a friend of my.
Sahi: He is a friend of mine.
Wajah: of ke baad possessive pronoun ata hai, adjective nahi, kyunke wahan noun maujood nahi.

Ghalat: She gave the pen to he.
Sahi: She gave the pen to him.
Wajah: to preposition hai. Us ke baad him ayega, he nahi.

Ghalat: Let I and he do this work.
Sahi: Let me and him do this work.
Wajah: let ke baad bhi objective shaklein ati hain.

Ghalat: The cat hurt it's leg.
Sahi: The cat hurt its leg.
Wajah: malikiyat wale its me apostrophe nahi hota. It's ka matlab it is hai.

Ghalat: Me and Ali went to the bazaar.
Sahi: Ali and I went to the bazaar.
Wajah: yahan dono kaam karne wale hain, is liye subjective shaklein ayengi, aur izzat ka usool hai ke doosre ka naam pehle aye.

Ghalat: This house is her's.
Sahi: This house is hers.
Wajah: hers me apostrophe kabhi nahi ata. Mine, yours, hers, ours, theirs, sab bina apostrophe ke hain.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye do tasveerein bohat kaam ki hongi. Pehli tasveer naukar aur malik wali ho. Aik taraf aik naukar khara ho jis ke peeche bara sa noun, yani kitaab, khara ho aur us par my likha ho. Doosri taraf aik malik akela takht par betha ho aur us par mine likha ho. Neeche aik line ho ke naukar ko sahara chahiye, malik akela kaafi hai. Ye tasveer my aur mine ka farq aik nazar me zehen me bitha degi.

Doosri tasveer between wale trap ki ho. Aik bare se darwaze par between likha ho. Darwaze ke andar jane walon ki line lagi ho. Me, him, her, us aur them andar ja rahe hon aur I aur he ko darwaze par roka gaya ho, un ke aage laal cross laga ho. Neeche bara sa jumla likha ho, between you and me. Paper ka sab se mashhoor trap agar aik tasveer me yaad ho jaye to yehi wo tasveer hai.
