Lesson 15: Helping Verbs Can, Must and Have To
Can, Must aur Have To. Sakta hai, lazmi hai, aur majboori hai.

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me can, must aur have to par har saal sawal ata hai. Kabhi khali jagah bharni hoti hai aur kabhi jumla theek karna hota hai. Lekin is lesson ka sab se qeemti hissa aik farq hai jo aadhe se zyada students ko nahi pata hota. Must not aur do not have to ka farq. In dono ka matlab aik doosre ke bilkul ulta hai, aur paper hamesha yahi par parkhta hai. Jo student ye farq samajh gaya, wo in sawalo me kabhi number nahi katayega.

Pehle ye samajh lo ke ye alfaaz hain kya

Dekho, can, must aur have to ko helping verbs kehte hain. Naam se hi pata chal raha hai, ye madad karne wale hain. Ye akelay khare nahi hote, hamesha kisi asal verb ke sath ate hain aur us verb ke matlab me aik rang bhar dete hain. Can qabiliyat ka rang bharta hai, must lazmi hone ka, aur have to majboori ka.

In teeno ka aik pakka usool pehle din zehen me bitha lo. In ke baad hamesha verb ki pehli form ati hai, aur beech me to kabhi nahi ata. He can swim sahi hai, he can to swim ghalat hai. You must go sahi hai, you must to go ghalat hai. Aur doosri baat, ye alfaaz apni shakal nahi badalte. He ke sath bhi can hi rahega, must hi rahega. Sirf have to badalta hai, wo he aur she ke sath has to ban jata hai. Baqi dono har subject ke sath aik jaise rehte hain.

Can: sakta hai

Can ka matlab hai sakta hai. Ye do kaam karta hai. Pehla kaam qabiliyat batana hai. I can swim ka matlab hai mujhe tairna ata hai, me tair sakta hun. She can speak English ka matlab hai wo English bol sakti hai. Doosra kaam ijazat ka hai. Can I come in matlab kya me andar aa sakta hun. Aur kisi ko ijazat deni ho to bhi yehi ata hai. You can go now.

Ab can ke do rishtedaaron se bhi mil lo, kyunke paper unhe bhi poochta hai. Pehla hai could. Ye can ka guzra hua roop hai. When I was young, I could run fast ka matlab hai me jawani me tez daur sakta tha. Lekin could ka aik doosra kaam bhi hai jo bohat kaam ka hai. Narmi se kuch kehna ho to could ata hai. Could you help me, please? Ye can you help me se zyada narm aur tameez wala jumla hai. Darkhwast me could lagana paper bhi pasand karta hai aur log bhi.

Doosra rishtedaar hai might. Ye imkan batata hai, lekin kamzor imkan. It might rain ka matlab hai mumkin hai barish ho jaye, lekin pakka nahi. Bas ye pehchan kaafi hai ke might matlab shayad, lekin dil pura mutmaen nahi.

Must: andar ki majboori

Must ka matlab hai lazmi, zaroor. Ye wo majboori hai jo bolne wale ke andar se ati hai. Ustad kahe ke you must work hard, to ye us ka apna hukum hai, us ki apni zimme dari wali baat hai. Doctor kahe ke you must take rest, to ye us ka sakht mashwara hai.

Must ka aik aur istemal bhi hai jo paper me bohat ata hai aur students ko uljhan me dalta hai. Pakka andaza. He must be tired ka matlab ye nahi ke us par lazim hai ke wo thake. Matlab hai wo lazmi thaka hua hoga, halaat yehi bata rahe hain. He has not eaten since morning, he must be hungry. Yahan must andaze ki pakki baat kar raha hai. Jab jumle me koi duty wali baat na ho aur halaat se natija nikala ja raha ho, to must ka yehi matlab hota hai.

Have to: bahar ki majboori

Have to bhi majboori batata hai, lekin ye majboori bahar se ati hai. Qanoon se, daftar ke usool se, ya halaat se. A soldier has to wear a uniform. Yahan sipahi ki apni marzi nahi, fouj ka qanoon hai. I have to submit the form by Friday. Yahan daftar ki tareekh majboor kar rahi hai.

Farq zehen me yun bithao. Must bolne wale ke dil se nikalta hai, have to bahar ke qanoon se ata hai. Aam bol chal me dono ka matlab qareeb qareeb aik hi samjha jata hai, lekin paper me ye bareek farq kabhi kabhi pooch liya jata hai.

Aur guzre waqt ki majboori ke liye had to ata hai. Yesterday I had to go to the office. Must ka apna koi past nahi hota. Guzri majboori ho to had to hi ayega.

Sab se bara farq: must not aur do not have to

Ab is lesson ki jaan par ao, aur is hisse ko do dafa parho. Must not ka matlab hai mana hai. Ye kaam karna hi nahi. You must not smoke in the hospital. Yahan ye nahi kaha ja raha ke tumhe cigarette peene ki zaroorat nahi. Kaha ja raha hai ke sakhti se mana hai.

Do not have to ka matlab hai zaroorat nahi. Kaam mana nahi hai, bas lazmi nahi hai. Karna chaho to kar lo, na karo to koi pabandi nahi. Tomorrow is a holiday, so we do not have to get up early. Yahan jaldi uthna mana nahi hai, bas lazmi nahi raha.

Dekha tum ne? Aik ka matlab hai hargiz na karo, doosre ka matlab hai karo ya na karo, tumhari marzi. Jo student in dono ko aik samjhega, wo paper me jumle ka matlab hi ulta kar dega. Ye farq is lesson ki sab se bari kamai hai.

Board par qanoon Angrezi me

Board par ye baatein kuch yun likhi jati hain. Can shows ability or permission: I can swim, Can I come in? Must shows a strong duty or a sure guess: You must stop here. He must be ill. Have to shows a duty that comes from a rule or a situation: I have to wear a uniform. After can, must and have to, we always use the first form of the verb, without to. These words do not change with he or she, except have to, which becomes has to. Must not means it is forbidden. Do not have to means it is not necessary. The past of must and have to is had to. Could is the past of can and a polite way of asking. Might shows a weak possibility: it might rain.

Misaalein board se

Ab board par chand misaalein likhta hun. Har misaal ke sath us ki wajah bhi samjho.

1. She can speak three languages.
Can yahan qabiliyat bata raha hai. Wo teen zabaanein bol sakti hai.

2. Can I use your pen, please?
Yahan can ijazat mang raha hai.

3. When he was young, he could run ten miles.
Could guzre waqt ki qabiliyat hai. Jawani me wo das meel daur sakta tha.

4. Could you open the window, please?
Darkhwast narmi se karni ho to could ata hai. Ye jumla bohat tameez wala hai.

5. It might rain in the evening.
Might kamzor imkan hai. Shayad barish ho, pakka nahi.

6. Students must reach the hall before time.
Must yahan lazmi hone ki baat kar raha hai. Ye pabandi hai.

7. He must be hungry; he has not eaten since morning.
Yahan must pakka andaza hai. Halaat bata rahe hain ke wo lazmi bhooka hoga.

8. A soldier has to wear a uniform.
Ye majboori fouj ke qanoon se ai hai, is liye has to aya. He ke sath has to ata hai, ye bhi dekh lo.

9. You must not smoke in the hospital.
Yahan sakhti se mana hai. Must not ka yehi matlab hai.

10. Tomorrow is a holiday, so we do not have to get up early.
Yahan jaldi uthna mana nahi, bas zaroori nahi raha. Farq saaf nazar aya na?

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Rozmarra zindagi me ijazat mangna, darkhwast karna, apni duty batana, ye sab inhi alfaaz se hota hai. Daftar ke notice board par likha hota hai ke visitors must not enter without permission. School me ustad kehta hai ke you do not have to bring the book tomorrow.

Ab paper wale andaaz ke do jumle dekho.

1. All candidates must bring their original documents on the day of the test.
2. You do not have to submit the fee today; the last date is Friday.

Doosre jumle ko khaas dekho. Fee jama karana mana nahi hai, bas aaj lazmi nahi hai. Yahi bareek farq paper me parkha jata hai.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab wo trick jo must not aur do not have to ka farq zindagi bhar yaad karwa degi. Do darwaze zehen me banao. Pehle darwaze par tala laga hai aur upar board hai, dakhla mana hai. Ye darwaza must not hai. Is ke andar jana hi nahi, baat khatam. Doosra darwaza khula para hai. Na tala hai, na board hai. Jana chaho to jao, na jao to koi pochne wala nahi. Ye darwaza do not have to hai. Jab bhi paper me ye dono options ayein, pehle zehen me darwaza dekho. Tala dikha to must not, khula darwaza dikha to do not have to.

Aur aik chhoti si line must aur have to ke liye bhi yaad kar lo. Must dil se ata hai, have to qanoon se ata hai.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ab wo ghaltiyan dekho jo Pakistani students paper me karte hain. Main board par ghalat aur sahi sath sath likh raha hun.

Ghalat: He can to swim.
Sahi: He can swim.
Wajah: can ke baad to nahi ata, seedhi pehli form ati hai.

Ghalat: You must to stop here.
Sahi: You must stop here.
Wajah: must ke baad bhi to nahi ata. Ye ghalti Urdu ke "jana hai" wale andaaz se ati hai.

Ghalat: She musts try again.
Sahi: She must try again.
Wajah: must kabhi s nahi leta. He ho ya she, must waisa hi rehta hai.

Ghalat: You do not have to smoke in the hospital.
Sahi: You must not smoke in the hospital.
Wajah: yahan baat mana hone ki hai, zaroorat na hone ki nahi. Yahi is lesson ka sab se mehnga trap hai.

Ghalat: He don't have to wait.
Sahi: He does not have to wait.
Wajah: he ke sath does not ata hai, do not nahi.

Ghalat: Yesterday I must go to the office.
Sahi: Yesterday I had to go to the office.
Wajah: must ka past nahi hota. Guzri majboori ke liye had to ata hai.

Ghalat: He has to goes early.
Sahi: He has to go early.
Wajah: has to ke baad bhi pehli form ati hai. Has to ka s has to me hi aa gaya, verb ko doosra s nahi chahiye.

Ghalat: Can you to pass the salt?
Sahi: Can you pass the salt?
Wajah: sawal me bhi can ke baad to nahi ata.

Jawab aur un ki wajah

1. Jawab A hai, can. Qabiliyat ke liye can ata hai, aur can kabhi s nahi leta.
2. Jawab B hai, must not. Forbidden ka matlab hai mana hai, aur mana ke liye must not ata hai. Do not have to yahan hota to matlab hota ke zaroorat nahi, jo jumle ko ulta kar deta.
3. Jawab B hai, do not have to. Chhutti ke din jaldi uthna mana nahi hai, bas zaroori nahi. Khula darwaza yaad karo.
4. Jawab B hai, could. Guzre waqt ki qabiliyat ke liye could ata hai.
5. Jawab B hai. Has to ke baad pehli form ati hai, go. Goes ghalat hai.
6. Jawab A hai, must. Yahan must duty nahi, pakka andaza hai. Saari raat na soya ho to lazmi thaka hoga.
7. Jawab B hai, Could. Narmi aur tameez se darkhwast karni ho to could sab se behtar hai.
8. Jawab B hai, had to. Guzri majboori ke liye had to ata hai, must ka past nahi hota.
9. Jawab A hai, must. Qanoon ki pabandi ke liye must bilkul sahi hai, aur must ke sath s nahi lagta.
10. Jawab B hai, might. Pakka yaqeen nahi, kamzor imkan hai, is liye might.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye pehli tasveer do darwazon wali ho. Aik darwaze par bara sa tala laga ho aur upar board par likha ho ke dakhla mana hai, aur us ke neeche must not ki misaal ho. Doosra darwaza khula ho, us ke paas koi board na ho, aur neeche do not have to ki misaal ho. Student aik nazar me farq pehchan le, yehi is tasveer ka kaam hai, kyunke ye farq lafzon se zyada tasveer se zehen me baithta hai.

Doosri tasveer teen khano wali chart ho. Pehle khane me can ho, us ka Urdu matlab sakta hai, aur neeche do misaalein hon, qabiliyat ki aur ijazat ki. Doosre khane me must ho, matlab lazmi, aur neeche duty wali aur pakke andaze wali misaal ho. Teesre khane me have to ho, matlab bahar ki majboori, aur us ke neeche uniform wali misaal ho. Chart ke sab se neeche aik laal patti par ye line ho ke modal ke baad hamesha pehli form ati hai, to kabhi nahi ata.
