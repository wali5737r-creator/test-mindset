Lesson 16: Some, Any and How Many, How Much
Some, Any aur Miqdar. Kuch ka sahi istemal.

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me some aur any wale sawal har saal ate hain, aur ye wo sawal hain jin me sab se zyada students phanste hain. Wajah saaf hai. Urdu me kuch aur koi har jagah chal jate hain, lekin English me some ki jagah alag hai aur any ki alag. Is ke sath how many aur how much ka farq bhi paper ka pasandida hai, aur aakhir me few aur little ke beech wo chhota sa farq jo jumle ka matlab hi badal deta hai. Ye lesson in teeno cheezon ko aik sath pakka kar dega. Ye sawal mushkil nahi hote, bas bareek hote hain, aur bareek cheez hi paper me number dilati hai.

Pehle do khane bana lo

Sab se pehle apne zehen me nouns ke do khane bana lo. Aik khana ginne walon ka hai. Kitaabein, students, seb, sawal. Inhe aik aik karke gina ja sakta hai. Doosra khana un cheezon ka hai jo gini nahi jati, sirf naapi ya toli jati hain. Paani, cheeni, paisa, waqt, information. Inhe hum aik paani, do paani nahi kehte.

Ye do khane is liye zaroori hain kyunke miqdar batane wale alfaaz inhi khano ke hisaab se chalte hain. Many ginne walon ke sath ata hai. Much na ginne walon ke sath. How many ginne walon ke liye sawal hai, how much na ginne walon ke liye. Aur how much ka aik aur kaam bhi hai, qeemat pochna. How much is this book ka matlab hai is kitaab ki qeemat kitni hai.

Some kahan ata hai

Some ka aam matlab hai kuch. Ye zyada tar positive jumlo me ata hai, jahan baat haan me ho rahi ho. I have some money. She bought some books. Yahan koi sawal nahi, koi nafi nahi, seedhi baat hai, to some aya.

Lekin rukho, asal trap yahan nahi hai. Asal baat ye hai ke some sawal wale jumlo me bhi ata hai, lekin sirf do surat me. Pehli surat darkhwast ki hai. Can I have some water, please? Yahan sawal to hai, lekin asal me tum kuch mang rahe ho. Doosri surat peshkash ki hai. Would you like some tea? Yahan tum kisi ko kuch pesh kar rahe ho. Darkhwast aur peshkash me some hi ata hai, any nahi. Ye paper ka sab se mashhoor trap hai, isay taare se nishan laga lo zehen me.

Any kahan ata hai

Any ka ghar sawal aur nafi hai. Sawal ho to any. Do you have any money? Is there any sugar in the jar? Nafi ho to bhi any. I do not have any money. He did not ask any question.

Aur any ka aik teesra roop bhi hai jo paper kabhi kabhi poochta hai. Positive jumle me any ka matlab ban jata hai koi sa bhi. Any student can solve this question ka matlab hai koi sa bhi student, jo marzi aa jaye, hal kar sakta hai. Come on any day ka matlab hai jis din marzi aa jao. Yahan any kuch ke matlab me nahi, har aik ke matlab me hai. Jumle ka andaaz dekh kar pehchanna hota hai.

Much aur many ki aik aur pehchan

Aik aur bareek baat bata raha hun jo paper me kaam ati hai. Much aur many sawal aur nafi me bohat natural lagte hain. How much money do you have? I do not have much money. Lekin seedhe positive jumle me much lagana ajeeb lagta hai. He has much friends kehna ghalat bhi hai kyunke friends ginne wale hain, lekin he has much money bhi aam bol chal me ajeeb hai. Wahan a lot of ata hai. He has a lot of money. He has a lot of friends. A lot of dono khano ke sath chal jata hai. Positive jumle me a lot of, sawal aur nafi me many aur much, ye tartib yaad rakho.

Few aur little ka chhota sa lafz, bara farq

Ab is lesson ke sab se bareek hisse par ao. Few ginne walon ke liye hai aur little na ginne walon ke liye. Ye to seedha hai. Asal farq us chhote se a me hai jo in se pehle ata hai.

A few ka matlab hai kuch hain. Baat umeed wali hai. I have a few friends ka matlab hai mere kuch dost hain, kaam chal jata hai, me akela nahi hun. Few ka matlab hai na hone ke barabar. I have few friends ka matlab hai dost hain hi nahi ke barabar, me akela hun. Dekha? Aik chhota sa a gira aur jumle ki khushi mayusi me badal gayi.

Bilkul yehi haal a little aur little ka hai. A little ka matlab hai kuch hai, kaafi hai. There is a little milk, chai ban jayegi. Little ka matlab hai na hone ke barabar. There is little milk, chai nahi ban sakti. Paper me jumle ke doosre hisse se pehchanna hota hai ke baat umeed ki hai ya mayusi ki. Agar natija acha nikal raha hai to a wala chuno, natija bura hai to baghair a wala.

Board par qanoon Angrezi me

Board par ye usool kuch yun likhe jate hain. Some is used in positive sentences, and also in requests and offers: Can I have some water? Would you like some tea? Any is used in questions and negative sentences: Do you have any money? I do not have any money. In a positive sentence, any can mean koi sa bhi: Any student can do it. Many is used with countable nouns and much with uncountable nouns. How many asks about number, and how much asks about quantity and about price. A few means a small number, but enough. Few means almost none. A little means a small amount, but enough. Little means almost nothing. In positive sentences, a lot of is used with both kinds of nouns.

Misaalein board se

1. I have some friends in this city.
Positive jumla hai, is liye some aya.

2. Do you have any question?
Sawal hai, is liye any aya.

3. She does not have any money today.
Nafi wala jumla hai. Nafi me hamesha any ata hai.

4. Would you like some tea?
Ye peshkash hai. Mehmaan ko chai pesh karte hue some ata hai, any nahi. Yahi paper ka trap hai.

5. Can I have some water, please?
Ye darkhwast hai. Mangne me bhi some ata hai.

6. How many students are present today?
Students ginne wale hain, is liye how many.

7. How much sugar do you take in tea?
Cheeni gini nahi jati, is liye how much.

8. Any candidate can apply for this post.
Yahan any ka matlab hai koi sa bhi. Ye positive jumle me any ka khaas istemal hai.

9. He has a few good books on PPSC.
A few matlab kuch hain aur kaam ke hain. Baat umeed wali hai.

10. There is little hope of his success.
Little matlab umeed na hone ke barabar hai. A little hota to matlab hota ke kuch umeed baqi hai.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Bazaar me qeemat pochna how much se hota hai. Mehmaan nawazi me peshkash some se hoti hai. Daftar ke form me sawal any se bante hain. Aur kisi ki tayyari ka andaza lagana ho to few aur a few ka farq hi asal baat batata hai. Jis candidate ke paas a few achhi kitaabein hain aur a little waqt bhi hai, wo paper de sakta hai. Jis ke paas few kitaabein hain, usay pehle kharidari karni hogi.

Ab paper wale andaaz ke do jumle dekho.

1. Would you like some more rice?
2. How many marks are required to pass this test?

Pehla jumla peshkash hai, is liye sawal hote hue bhi some aya. Yehi wo pehchan hai jo paper parkhta hai.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Is lesson ki trick mehmaan wali hai. Jab bhi some aur any me shak ho, zehen me mehmaan ko bithao. Mehmaan ghar aye to tum us se ye nahi poochte ke kuch hai ya nahi. Tum pesh karte ho. Chai pesh karni hai to some, paani mangna hai to some. Peshkash aur darkhwast mehmaan nawazi hai, aur mehmaan nawazi me hamesha some ata hai. Baqi har sawal aur har nafi me any. Bas aik line yaad kar lo. Peshkash me some, sawal me any, nafi me any.

Aur few wale farq ke liye ye chhoti si baat yaad rakho. Chhota sa a, bari umeed. A lag gaya to matlab kuch hai. A gir gaya to matlab jholi khali hai.

Ghalat: I do not have some money.
Sahi: I do not have any money.
Wajah: nafi wale jumle me any ata hai, some nahi.

Ghalat: Would you like any tea?
Sahi: Would you like some tea?
Wajah: peshkash me some ata hai. Ye paper ka sab se mashhoor trap hai.

Ghalat: How much books did you buy?
Sahi: How many books did you buy?
Wajah: books ginne wale hain, is liye how many ayega.

Ghalat: How many money do you need?
Sahi: How much money do you need?
Wajah: money ginna nahi jata, is liye how much ayega.

Ghalat: There are much students in the hall.
Sahi: There are many students in the hall.
Wajah: students ginne wale hain, much un ke sath nahi ata.

Ghalat: I have few friends, so I never feel lonely.
Sahi: I have a few friends, so I never feel lonely.
Wajah: few ka matlab hai na hone ke barabar, us se akelapan hona chahiye tha. Jumle ka doosra hissa umeed wala hai, is liye a few ayega.

Ghalat: She has a little money, so she cannot buy the book.
Sahi: She has little money, so she cannot buy the book.
Wajah: a little ka matlab hai kaafi kuch hai, phir kitaab kyun nahi khareed sakti? Yahan na hone ke barabar wala little chahiye.

Ghalat: Do you have some brothers or sisters?
Sahi: Do you have any brothers or sisters?
Wajah: ye aam sawal hai, na darkhwast hai na peshkash, is liye any ayega.

Jawab aur un ki wajah

1. Jawab B hai, some. Positive jumla hai aur information ginni nahi jati, is liye many aur few to pehle hi bahar ho gaye.
2. Jawab B hai, any. Aam sawal me any ata hai.
3. Jawab C hai, some. Ye peshkash hai, aur peshkash me hamesha some ata hai. Mehmaan wali trick yaad hai na?
4. Jawab B hai, many. Chapters ginne wale hain.
5. Jawab B hai, much. Qeemat pochne ke liye how much ata hai.
6. Jawab B hai, a few. Wo kabhi akela nahi hota, matlab kuch dost hain. Few hota to matlab hota ke dost hain hi nahi.
7. Jawab B hai, little. Doodh chai ke liye kaafi nahi, matlab na hone ke barabar hai. A little hota to chai ban jati.
8. Jawab B hai, any. Did not wali nafi me any ata hai.
9. Jawab B hai, Any. Yahan any ka matlab hai koi sa bhi, kyunke library sab ke liye khuli hai.
10. Jawab B hai, few. Kursiyan taqreeban saari khali hain, matlab log na hone ke barabar hain.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Pehli tasveer do tokriyon wali ho. Aik tokri me seb rakhe hon aur har seb par number likha ho, taake saaf dikhe ke ye ginne wale hain, aur tokri ke neeche many aur how many likha ho. Doosri taraf paani ka jag ho jis me paani naapa to ja sakta hai lekin gina nahi ja sakta, aur us ke neeche much aur how much likha ho. Student aik nazar me dono khane alag kar le, yehi is tasveer ka maqsad hai.

Doosri tasveer few aur a few ke farq ki ho. Aik taraf aik shakhs ki jeb me chand sikke hon aur wo chai ke dhabe par khara muskurah raha ho, neeche a few likha ho. Doosri taraf aik shakhs apni khali jeb ulta kar dikha raha ho aur chehra mayus ho, neeche few likha ho. Bilkul waisi hi aik jodi little aur a little ke liye bhi ho, doodh ke glass ke sath. Ye tasveer is liye zaroori hai kyunke ye farq lafzon ka nahi, ehsas ka hai, aur ehsas tasveer se hi zehen me baithta hai.
