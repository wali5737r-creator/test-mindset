Lesson 17: Comparative and Superlative
Comparative aur Superlative. Muqabla aur sab se barha hua.

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me degrees of comparison har saal ati hain. Than wale jumle, sab se wala superlative, aur as as wali barabari, ye teeno paper ke pakke sawal hain. Aur sach ye hai ke ye sawal sab se asaan number dete hain, bas chand usool pakke karne hote hain. Is lesson ki sab se qeemti cheezein teen hain. Pehli, more better jaisi dohri ghalti jo aadhe students karte hain. Doosri, good aur bad ke irregular roop jo rata mangte hain. Teesri, do cheezon me se aik chunne wala the taller of the two wala usool jo bohat kam students ko ata hai. Ye teeno is lesson me pakke ho jayenge.

Teen manzilein samajh lo

Dekho, English me kisi cheez ki khoobi ya burai teen darjo me bayan hoti hai. Pehla darja positive hai, seedhi baat. Ali is tall. Yahan koi muqabla nahi, bas aik baat kahi gayi hai. Doosra darja comparative hai. Yahan do cheezon ka muqabla hota hai. Ali is taller than Ahmed. Aik ko doosre se barha hua dikhaya gaya. Teesra darja superlative hai. Yahan aik ko sab se barha hua dikhate hain. Ali is the tallest boy in the class. Class ke saare larko me sab se upar.

Bas ye tasveer zehen me bitha lo. Aik ho to positive, do ka muqabla ho to comparative, sab ka muqabla ho to superlative. Ab sawal sirf ye hai ke har darje ki shakal kese banti hai.

Chhote alfaaz: er aur est lagta hai

Jo adjectives chhote hote hain, aik hisse wale, un ke aakhir me er lagane se comparative banta hai aur est lagane se superlative. Tall se taller aur tallest. Small se smaller aur smallest. Fast se faster aur fastest.

In ke teen chhote chhote spelling usool bhi sath yaad kar lo. Pehla, agar lafz e par khatam ho to sirf r aur st lagta hai. Large se larger aur largest. Doosra, agar lafz consonant ke baad y par khatam ho to y i ban jata hai. Happy se happier aur happiest. Easy se easier aur easiest. Lekin y se pehle vowel ho to y nahi badalta. Teesra usool wahi purana hai jo tum peeche parh aye ho. Chhota lafz ho, beech me aik vowel ho aur aakhir me aik consonant ho to wo consonant dohra ho jata hai. Big se bigger aur biggest. Hot se hotter aur hottest. Thin se thinner aur thinnest. Ye dohre wala usool har jagah chalta hai, isay apna dost bana lo.

Lambe alfaaz: more aur most ata hai

Jo adjectives lambe hain, do ya zyada hisso wale, un par er est nahi lagta. Un se pehle more ata hai comparative ke liye aur most ata hai superlative ke liye. Beautiful se more beautiful aur the most beautiful. Difficult se more difficult aur the most difficult. Useful, important, careful, ye sab isi khane me hain.

Ab wo ghalti suno jo paper har saal parkhta hai. More aur er aik sath kabhi nahi ate. More better ghalat hai, more taller ghalat hai. Aik darja, aik hi nishan. Ya er lagao ya more lagao, dono aik sath lagaye to jumla ghalat ho jayega. Bilkul waisi hi most aur est bhi aik sath nahi ate. The most tallest ghalat hai, the tallest sahi hai.

Irregular: jo usool nahi mante

Kuch mashhoor adjectives apna alag rasta rakhte hain. In ka koi usool nahi, ye rata mangte hain, aur paper inhi ko sab se zyada poochta hai. Good ka comparative better hai aur superlative best. Gooder aur goodest naam ki koi cheez nahi hoti. Bad ka worse aur worst hai. Far ka farther aur farthest hai. Little ka less aur least hai. Much aur many ka more aur most hai.

In me good aur bad ko khaas yaad karna. Paper me better, best, worse aur worst har saal kisi na kisi surat me ate hain. Ye chaar alfaaz is lesson ki sab se sasti kamai hain.

Than, the aur as as

Ab teen chhote alfaaz ka kaam samajh lo jo in darjo ki pehchan hain. Comparative ke sath than ata hai. Taller than, better than, more useful than. Than dikha to samajh jao ke muqabla do ke beech hai aur comparative ayega.

Superlative se pehle the ata hai. The tallest, the best, the most difficult. The superlative ki shaan hai, wo kabhi akela nahi ata. Aur superlative ke baad group batana ho to in ya of ata hai. Jagah ke liye in, the tallest building in Lahore. Group ke liye of, the best of all students.

Aur barabari dikhani ho to as as ka joda ata hai. She is as tall as her sister. Beech me hamesha positive darja ata hai, tall. As taller as kehna ghalat hai. Nafi me not as as ka matlab hota hai kam. He is not as strong as his brother, matlab wo apne bhai se kamzor hai.

Do me se aik chunna aur any other wala trap

Ab do bareek baatein jo is lesson ko paper ki satah par le jati hain. Pehli ye ke jab do me se aik ko chuna jaye to superlative nahi, comparative ata hai, lekin us se pehle the lagta hai. Of the two brothers, Aslam is the taller. Do hain, is liye comparative. Chunav hai, is liye the. The tallest of the two kehna ghalat hai, kyunke tallest to teen ya zyada ke liye hota hai.

Doosri baat any other wali hai. Jab koi sheher apne hi mulk ke shehron se bara ho to jumle me other lazmi lagana parta hai. Karachi is larger than any other city in Pakistan. Other is liye lagaya ke Karachi khud bhi Pakistan ka sheher hai. Other na lagao to jumle ka matlab banta hai ke Karachi Karachi se bhi bara hai, jo bewaqoofi hai. Paper is bareek nuqte ko bohat pasand karta hai.

Aik lafz aur bata dun. Elder ghar ke baron ke liye ata hai, jaise my elder brother. Lekin elder ke sath than nahi ata. He is elder than me ghalat hai. Ya to he is older than me kaho, ya phir purane andaaz me he is elder to me. Aam paper me older than wala jumla hi sahi mana jata hai.

Board par qanoon Angrezi me

Board par ye usool kuch yun likhe jate hain. The comparative compares two things and is followed by than. The superlative compares more than two and takes the before it. Short adjectives add er and est: tall, taller, the tallest. If the adjective ends in y after a consonant, y changes to i: happy, happier, the happiest. Long adjectives use more and most: more beautiful, the most beautiful. Never use more with er, and never use most with est. Good becomes better and best. Bad becomes worse and worst. As and as shows equality: as tall as. When we choose one of two, we use the with the comparative: the taller of the two.

Misaalein board se

1. Ali is taller than Ahmed.
Do ka muqabla hai, is liye comparative aur sath than aya.

2. Ali is the tallest boy in the class.
Sab ka muqabla hai, is liye superlative, aur pehle the, baad me in the class.

3. This book is more useful than that one.
Useful lamba lafz hai, is liye er nahi, more aya.

4. English is easier than mathematics for me.
Happy wale usool par y i ban gaya, is liye easier.

5. Today is hotter than yesterday.
Hot chhota lafz hai, aakhri letter dohra hua, is liye hotter.

6. He is a better player than his brother.
Good ka comparative better hai. Gooder naam ki koi cheez nahi.

7. This is the worst result of my life.
Bad ka superlative worst hai, aur superlative ke sath the aya.

8. She is as intelligent as her sister.
Barabari ke liye as as aya, aur beech me positive darja raha.

9. Of the two brothers, Aslam is the taller.
Do me se chunav hai, is liye the ke sath comparative. Yahi is lesson ka bareek nuqta hai.

10. Karachi is larger than any other city in Pakistan.
Karachi khud Pakistan ka sheher hai, is liye any other lagana lazmi tha.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Rozmarra zindagi muqablo se bhari hai. Ye wala mehnga hai, wo wala sasta hai. Aaj kal se zyada garmi hai. Daftar me reports me likha hota hai ke is saal ki performance pichle saal se behtar hai. Aur kehawatein bhi inhi darjo par bani hain. Prevention is better than cure. Health is better than wealth. Ye jumle paper me bhi ate hain aur zindagi me bhi.

Ab paper wale andaaz ke do jumle dekho.

1. K2 is the second highest peak in the world.
2. Prevention is better than cure.

Pehle jumle me dekh lo, superlative se pehle the aya hai aur number ke sath highest chala hai. Ye andaaz paper me bohat ata hai.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab wo trick jo teeno darje hamesha yaad karwa degi. Teen bhaiyon ki daur zehen me lao. Chhota bhai tez hai, ye positive hai, seedhi baat. Darmiyana bhai us se zyada tez hai, ye comparative hai, aur is ke peeche than bhaga aa raha hai. Sab se bara bhai sab se tez hai, ye superlative hai, aur us ke sar par jeet ka taaj hai, jo the hai. Bas aik line zehen me bitha lo. Do hon to er aur than, sab hon to est aur the.

Aur good aur bad ke liye ye baat yaad rakho. Ye dono badtameez hain, line me nahi lagte, apna alag rasta lete hain. Good better best, bad worse worst. Inhe kahani ki tarah dohra lo, rata khud lag jayega.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ghalat: He is more better than me.
Sahi: He is better than me.
Wajah: better pehle hi comparative hai. More doosri dafa lagana dohri ghalti hai.

Ghalat: She is the most tallest girl in the class.
Sahi: She is the tallest girl in the class.
Wajah: est ke sath most nahi ata. Aik darja, aik nishan.

Ghalat: Ali is taller of the two brothers.
Sahi: Ali is the taller of the two brothers.
Wajah: do me se chunav ho to comparative se pehle the ata hai.

Ghalat: He is elder than me.
Sahi: He is older than me.
Wajah: elder ke sath than nahi ata. Muqable ke liye older than sahi hai.

Ghalat: This is the best of the two books.
Sahi: This is the better of the two books.
Wajah: do ke muqable me comparative ata hai, superlative nahi.

Ghalat: She is as taller as her sister.
Sahi: She is as tall as her sister.
Wajah: as as ke beech hamesha positive darja ata hai.

Ghalat: Lahore is bigger than any city in Pakistan.
Sahi: Lahore is bigger than any other city in Pakistan.
Wajah: Lahore khud bhi Pakistan ka sheher hai. Other laga kar usay muqable se bahar rakho.

Ghalat: He is the goodest student.
Sahi: He is the best student.
Wajah: good irregular hai. Us ka superlative best hai, goodest koi lafz nahi.

Ghalat: My house is larger than your.
Sahi: My house is larger than yours.
Wajah: your ke baad noun chahiye. Bina noun ke yours ata hai.

Jawab aur un ki wajah

1. Jawab B hai, taller. Than saaf bata raha hai ke comparative chahiye.
2. Jawab C hai, best. The ke sath superlative ata hai aur good ka superlative best hai.
3. Jawab A hai, more beautiful. Beautiful lamba lafz hai, us par er est nahi lagta.
4. Jawab A hai, the taller. Do me se chunav hai, is liye the ke sath comparative. Tallest yahan sab se mashhoor trap hai.
5. Jawab B hai, colder. Do dino ka muqabla hai aur than mojood hai.
6. Jawab B hai. More wiser dohri ghalti hai, aik darje par aik hi nishan lagta hai.
7. Jawab A hai, fast. As as ke beech positive darja ata hai.
8. Jawab B hai, worse. Bad ka comparative worse hai aur than bhi yehi mang raha hai.
9. Jawab C hai, largest. The ke sath superlative ayega, aur large ka superlative largest hai.
10. Jawab B hai, yours. Your ke baad noun lazmi ata hai. Bina noun ke yours sahi hai.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Pehli tasveer teen seerhiyon wali ho. Pehli seerhi par aik larka khara ho aur neeche tall likha ho. Doosri seerhi us se unchi ho, us par doosra larka ho aur neeche taller than likha ho. Teesri seerhi sab se unchi ho, us par teesra larka jeet ka taaj pehne khara ho aur neeche the tallest likha ho. Taaj is liye ke student ko yaad rahe ke superlative ke sar par hamesha the ka taaj hota hai.

Doosri tasveer teen pahaaron wali ho. Aik chhota pahaar, aik darmiyana aur aik sab se bara, aur teeno ke neeche un ke darje likhe hon. Us ke sath aik chhoti si alag patti par irregular wale alfaaz hon, good better best aur bad worse worst, aur un ke upar likha ho ke ye usool nahi mante. Ye patti is liye zaroori hai kyunke paper ke zyada tar number inhi do alfaazon se ate hain.
