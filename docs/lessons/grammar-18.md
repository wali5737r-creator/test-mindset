Lesson 18: Past Tense
Past Tense. Guzra hua waqt, mukammal kaam.

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me past tense har jagah chhupa hota hai. Tareekh ke sawal is me bante hain, jumla theek karne wale sawal is me phansate hain, aur irregular verbs to seedhe seedhe pooche hi jate hain ke go ka past kya hai, teach ka past kya hai. Is lesson ka sab se bara trap did wala hai. Did ate hi verb apni pehli form me wapas chala jata hai, lekin aadhe students did you went likh dete hain. Doosra bara trap yesterday ke sath have gone lagana hai. Ye dono traps is lesson me hamesha ke liye khatam ho jayenge, aur sath me irregular verbs ki wo fehrist bhi mil jayegi jo paper me bar bar ati hai.

Past tense matlab kya hai

Pehle baat samajh lo. Past tense us kaam ke liye hai jo ho chuka aur khatam ho chuka. File band ho gayi hai. I went to Lahore yesterday. Kaam bhi khatam, waqt bhi guzar gaya.

Is tense ki pehchan us ke isharay hain. Jab jumle me yesterday dikhe, last night ya last week dikhe, ago dikhe, ya koi guzri tareekh dikhe jaise in 1947, to samajh jao ke baat past tense ki ho rahi hai. Ye isharay paper me tumhari sab se bari madad karte hain. Sawal parhne se pehle inhe dhundo, aadha jawab wahi mil jayega.

Regular verbs: ed lagta hai

Jo verbs usool ke paband hain, un ke aakhir me ed lagane se past ban jata hai. Work se worked, visit se visited, end se ended. Inhe regular kehte hain.

In ke teen chhote spelling usool hain, bilkul waisay hi jaise tum peeche parh aye ho. Pehla, lafz e par khatam ho to sirf d lagta hai. Live se lived, die se died. Doosra, consonant ke baad y ho to y i ban jata hai aur phir ed lagta hai. Carry se carried, study se studied. Lekin y se pehle vowel ho to y nahi badalta. Play se played. Teesra, chhota lafz ho, beech me aik vowel aur aakhir me aik consonant ho to consonant dohra ho jata hai. Stop se stopped, plan se planned.

Irregular verbs: shaklein badal jati hain

Ab asal maidan par ao. English ke sab se zyada istemal hone wale verbs irregular hain. Ye ed nahi lete, apni shakal badal dete hain. Inhe groups me bandh kar yaad karo to kaam asaan ho jata hai.

Pehla group un ka hai jin ki andar ki awaaz badal jati hai. Sing se sang, drink se drank, run se ran, sit se sat, swim se swam, begin se began, ring se rang. In sab me beech wala vowel badal jata hai, ye in ki pehchan hai.

Doosra group bilkul nayi shakal le leta hai. Go se went, see se saw, come se came, take se took, write se wrote, speak se spoke, break se broke, choose se chose, drive se drove, give se gave, eat se ate. Aur kharidari wale ye teen khaas yaad rakho, buy se bought, teach se taught, think se thought. Sath me bring se brought aur catch se caught bhi isi khandan ke hain. Bought, taught, thought aur brought ko aik sath dohra lo, ye chaar paper ke bohat pasandida hain.

Teesra group wo hai jin ki doosri aur teesri shakal aik hi hoti hai. Say se said, tell se told, sell se sold, leave se left, feel se felt, keep se kept, sleep se slept, meet se met, win se won, lose se lost, pay se paid, send se sent. Aur chautha group sab se shareef hai, in ki teeno shaklein aik hi rehti hain. Cut, put, set aur read. Read par khaas tawajjo do. Likha teeno jagah read hi jata hai, lekin past me bola red jata hai. Paper is awaaz wale farq ko bhi parkhta hai.

Did ka qanoon: is lesson ka sab se bara trap

Ab wo baat jo is lesson ki jaan hai. Past tense me sawal banana ho ya nafi karni ho to did ka sahara lena parta hai. Aur did ke ate hi aik kamaal hota hai. Asal verb apni doosri shakal chor kar pehli shakal me wapas chala jata hai.

Seedha jumla dekho. He went to school. Ab is ka sawal banao. Did he go to school? Dekha? Went wapas go ban gaya. Nafi banao. He did not go to school. Phir go. Wajah samajh lo taake kabhi na bhulo. Past ki saari taqat did ne apne andar le li hai. Went ka past hona did pehle hi bata raha hai, is liye verb ko dobara past banne ki zaroorat hi nahi. Aik kaam, aik hi nishan. Did you went kehna aise hai jaise aik hi darwaze par do taalay laga diye hon.

Yehi qanoon nafi me bhi chalta hai. Did not ke baad hamesha pehli form. She did not come. I did not see him. Paper me did you went, did not came aur didn't saw jaise jumle de kar tumhe phansaya jata hai. Ab tum nahi phanso ge.

Was aur were

Har jumle me kaam nahi hota, kabhi halat aur pehchan ki baat hoti hai. Wahan was aur were ate hain. He was ill. They were happy. She was a teacher. Was I, he, she aur it ke sath ata hai. Were you, we aur they ke sath ata hai. Ye wohi be verb hai jo tum shuru me parh aye thay, bas ye us ka guzra hua roop hai. Am aur is ka was, are ka were.

Aur aakhir me wo trap jo pehle btaya tha. Guzre waqt ke pakke isharay, yesterday, ago, last year, ke sath have ya has kabhi nahi ata. I have gone yesterday kehna ghalat hai. Yesterday dikha to seedha went. Have wala jumla us waqt ki baat karta hai jis ka taaluq ab se ho, aur yesterday us taaluq ko kaat deta hai. Paper me ye trap bohat chalta hai.

Board par qanoon Angrezi me

Board par ye usool kuch yun likhe jate hain. The simple past is used for actions that finished at a known time in the past. Regular verbs add ed: worked, lived, carried, stopped. Many common verbs are irregular: go went, see saw, buy bought, teach taught, think thought. In questions and negatives we use did, and the main verb comes back to its first form: Did you go? I did not go. Was is used with I, he, she and it. Were is used with you, we and they. Yesterday, last night, ago and past dates are the signs of this tense, and has or have never comes with them.

Misaalein board se

1. We visited Lahore last year.
Regular verb hai, ed lag gaya, aur last year past ka ishara hai.

2. She bought a new book yesterday.
Buy irregular hai, is liye bought aya. Buyed koi lafz nahi.

3. Did you see the match last night?
Did ke ate hi see apni pehli form me raha. Saw yahan hota to jumla ghalat ho jata.

4. He did not come to the office yesterday.
Nafi me bhi did not ke baad pehli form ati hai, is liye come aya, came nahi.

5. The teacher taught us a new lesson.
Teach ka past taught hai. Teached kehna paper ki mashhoor ghalti hai.

6. They were very happy at the result.
Halat ki baat hai aur they ke sath were ata hai.

7. I met him two days ago.
Ago matlab aaj se peeche ginna. Do din peeche wali mulaqat.

8. Pakistan came into being in 1947.
Tareekhi sach bhi past tense me bayan hota hai, aur come ka past came hai.

9. She wrote a letter and posted it.
Aik jumle me irregular bhi hai, wrote, aur regular bhi, posted. Dono ko pehchano.

10. He stopped the car suddenly.
Stop chhota lafz hai, aakhri letter dohra hua, phir ed laga.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Guzri hui baat cheet, tareekh, safar ki kahani, application me apni taleem ka bayan, ye sab past tense me hota hai. I passed my examination in 2022. We shifted to this city five years ago. Daftar ki report me likha hota hai ke the committee visited the site last week. Aur roz ki guftagu ka zyada tar hissa bhi yahi hai, kyunke hum din bhar guzri baatein hi sunate hain.

Ab paper wale andaaz ke do jumle dekho.

1. The meeting ended two hours ago.
2. Did the committee approve the plan last week?

Doosre jumle me did ke sath approve apni pehli form me hai. Yahi pehchan paper mangta hai.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Is lesson ki trick sakht ustad wali hai. Did ko aik sakht ustad samjho jo class me dakhil hua hai. Us ke ate hi saari class seedhi ho jati hai. Went apni akad chor kar seedha go ban jata hai, came apna roop chor kar come ban jata hai, saw bhi see ho jata hai. Ustad ke samne koi apni doosri shakal nahi dikha sakta. Bas aik line zehen me bitha lo. Jahan did, wahan pehli form.

Aur ago ke liye ye chhoti si tasveer yaad rakho. Ago matlab ghari ki suiyan peeche ghumana. Two years ago sunte hi zehen do saal peeche chala jaye. Jis din ye tasveer pakki ho gayi, ago wala koi jumla tumhe dhoka nahi dega.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ghalat: Did you went to school yesterday?
Sahi: Did you go to school yesterday?
Wajah: did ke baad pehli form ati hai. Past ki taqat did me pehle hi mojood hai.

Ghalat: She did not came this morning.
Sahi: She did not come this morning.
Wajah: did not ke baad bhi pehli form. Came yahan dohri ghalti hai.

Ghalat: I have gone to Karachi yesterday.
Sahi: I went to Karachi yesterday.
Wajah: yesterday guzre waqt ka pakka ishara hai. Us ke sath have nahi, simple past ata hai.

Ghalat: He buyed a car last month.
Sahi: He bought a car last month.
Wajah: buy irregular hai. Us ka past bought hai.

Ghalat: They was present in the class.
Sahi: They were present in the class.
Wajah: they ke sath were ata hai, was nahi.

Ghalat: Our teacher teached us grammar.
Sahi: Our teacher taught us grammar.
Wajah: teach ka past taught hai. Bought wale khandan ka lafz hai.

Ghalat: I didn't saw him at the fair.
Sahi: I didn't see him at the fair.
Wajah: didn't ke baad pehli form ati hai, saw nahi. Did ki taqat pehle hi mojood hai, verb ko dobara past nahi banna.

Ghalat: The train leaved at nine.
Sahi: The train left at nine.
Wajah: leave ka past left hai.

Ghalat: He stoped the bus.
Sahi: He stopped the bus.
Wajah: chhote lafz me aakhri letter dohra hota hai, phir ed lagta hai.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Pehli tasveer waqt ki seedhi lakeer wali ho. Aik lambi lakeer par teen nishan hon, guzra waqt, aaj aur ane wala waqt. Guzre waqt wale hisse ko gehray rang me dikhaya jaye aur us ke neeche us ke isharay likhe hon, yesterday, last night, ago aur in 1947. Student aik nazar me samajh jaye ke ye isharay dikhe to past tense pakka hai.

Doosri tasveer did wale qanoon ki ho. Aik sakht ustad darwaze se andar ata hua dikhaya jaye aur us ke samne teen verbs apni pehli shakal me seedhe khare hon, go, come aur see, jabke un ki doosri shaklein, went, came aur saw, peeche deewar par dhundli si bani hon. Tasveer ke neeche aik line ho ke did aya to pehli form wapas. Ye tasveer is liye zaroori hai kyunke did wala trap lafzon se zyada is manzar se zehen me baithta hai.
