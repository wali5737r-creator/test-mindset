Lesson 19: Present and Past Continuous
Present aur Past Continuous. Jo ho raha hai, jo ho raha tha.

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me continuous tense ke sawal har saal ate hain. Kabhi ing ki spelling poochi jati hai ke running me n dohra kyun hai, kabhi while aur when wale jumle me sahi tense chunna hota hai, aur kabhi wo bareek trap ata hai jis me know aur want jaise verbs ko continuous bana kar tumhe phansaya jata hai. Ye lesson teeno cheezein pakki kar dega. Continuous tense mushkil nahi hai. Ye bas aik chalti hui tasveer hai. Jo student ye tasveer dekhna seekh gaya, wo is tense me kabhi ghalti nahi karega.

Chalti hui tasveer samajh lo

Pehle farq zehen me bithao. Simple tense me kaam aik nuqta hota hai, ho gaya. Continuous me kaam aik chalti hui film hai. Jab tum kehte ho I read, to baat khatam. Jab tum kehte ho I am reading, to parhai is lamhe me chal rahi hai, band nahi hui.

Present continuous un kaamo ke liye hai jo is waqt ho rahe hain. I am teaching now. The baby is crying. Is ki shakal hai am, is ya are ke sath verb par ing. I ke sath am, he she it ke sath is, you we they ke sath are. Ye wohi be verb hai jo tum pehle din se parh rahe ho, bas ab us ke sath ing wala saathi bhi aa gaya hai.

Past continuous un kaamo ke liye hai jo guzre waqt ke kisi lamhe me chal rahe thay. At nine last night, we were watching the news. Raat no baje film chal rahi thi. Is ki shakal hai was ya were ke sath ing. I, he, she, it ke sath was. You, we, they ke sath were.

Is tense ki pehchan ke isharay bhi yaad kar lo. Now, at the moment, look aur listen wale jumle present continuous mangte hain. At that time, at nine last night aur while wale jumle past continuous ki taraf ishara karte hain.

Ing lagane ke spelling qanoon

Ab wo hissa jahan paper number katata hai. Ing lagana asaan hai, lekin chand alfaaz apni shakal thori badal lete hain.

Aam usool seedha hai. Sirf ing laga do. Play se playing, read se reading, watch se watching.

Doosra usool khamosh e ka hai. Agar verb e par khatam ho aur wo e bola na jata ho to e gir jata hai aur ing lag jata hai. Make se making, write se writing, take se taking. Lekin khabardar, jahan e bola jata ho ya lafz chhota ho wahan e nahi girta. Be se being, see se seeing, agree se agreeing.

Teesra usool wahi purana dohre wala hai jo ab tumhara dost ban chuka hai. Chhota lafz ho, beech me aik vowel aur aakhir me aik consonant ho to consonant dohra ho jata hai. Run se running, sit se sitting, swim se swimming, get se getting, begin se beginning. Aur jahan lafz do hisso wala ho aur aakhir me w ya x ho, wahan dohra nahi hota. Open se opening, fix se fixing.

Chautha usool sab se chhota hai lekin paper me ata hai. Jo verb ie par khatam ho, us ka ie y ban jata hai. Die se dying, lie se lying, tie se tying. Dieing likhna ghalat hai.

Aadat aur abhi ka farq

Aik bareek baat aur samajh lo jo paper bohat parkhta hai. Roz ki aadat simple present me ati hai aur is waqt ka kaam continuous me. He smokes ka matlab hai ye us ki aadat hai. He is smoking ka matlab hai is lamhe me wo pee raha hai. Jumle me everyday ya usually dikhe to aadat hai, now ya at the moment dikhe to continuous hai. Aur present continuous ka aik aur kaam bhi pehchan lo. Pakka mustaqbil ka plan bhi is me bayan hota hai. I am leaving for Islamabad tomorrow. Plan pakka hai, ticket le liya hai, is liye continuous aya hai.

Jo verbs continuous nahi hote

Ab is lesson ka sab se bareek trap. Kuch verbs aise hain jo continuous me ate hi nahi. Inhe stative verbs kehte hain. Ye wo verbs hain jo koi chalta hua kaam nahi batate, balke dil ki halat, dimagh ki baat ya malikiyat batate hain. Know, believe, understand, like, love, hate, want, need, seem aur own, ye is khandan ke mashhoor naam hain.

I know the answer sahi hai. I am knowing the answer ghalat hai. Wajah zehen me yun bithao. Ilm koi kaam nahi jo chal raha ho, ye aik halat hai jo thehri hui hai. Jo cheez mehsoos hoti hai, wo chalti nahi, thehri rehti hai. Is liye in verbs ko ing wala saathi nahi milta. Paper me I am wanting, she is liking jaise jumle de kar parkha jata hai. Ab tum pehchan lo ge.

While aur when: lamba kaam, chhota jhatka

Ab past continuous ka sab se khubsurat istemal samajh lo. Jab guzre waqt me aik lamba kaam chal raha ho aur us ke beech me aik chhota sa kaam aa jaye, to lamba kaam past continuous me hota hai aur chhota kaam simple past me. I was sleeping when the phone rang. Neend lambi thi, film chal rahi thi. Ghanti aik jhatka thi, baj kar ruk gayi.

While ke sath aam tor par continuous ata hai, kyunke while ka matlab hi hai ke us doran. While she was cooking, the bell rang. When dono ke sath aa sakta hai, lekin paper me ye joda sab se zyada ata hai ke lamba kaam was ya were ke sath ing le, aur darmiyan me ane wala chhota kaam apni doosri shakal le. Ye tartib zehen me bitha lo, aadhe sawal yahi se hal honge.

Board par qanoon Angrezi me

Board par ye usool kuch yun likhe jate hain. The present continuous is am, is or are plus verb-ing: I am reading now. The past continuous is was or were plus verb-ing: It was raining last night. Drop the silent e before ing: make, making. Double the last letter in short words: run, running; sit, sitting. Change ie to y before ing: die, dying. Verbs of knowing, feeling and owning do not come in the continuous: I know the answer, not I am knowing. A long action in the past continuous is cut by a short action in the simple past: I was sleeping when the phone rang. The present continuous can also show a fixed plan for the future: I am leaving tomorrow.

Misaalein board se

1. Look! The children are playing in the rain.
Look wala ishara bata raha hai ke kaam is waqt chal raha hai, is liye are playing.

2. She is making tea for the guests.
Make ka e gir gaya aur ing lag gaya. Makeing ghalat hota.

3. He is running after the bus.
Run chhota lafz hai, n dohra hua, phir ing laga.

4. The plant is dying without water.
Die ka ie y ban gaya. Ye spelling paper me ati hai.

5. At nine last night, we were watching the news.
Guzre waqt ka aik lamha hai aur film chal rahi thi, is liye were watching.

6. They were sleeping when the earthquake came.
Neend lamba kaam hai, continuous me ai. Zalzala chhota jhatka hai, simple past me aya.

7. I am leaving for Islamabad tomorrow morning.
Ye mustaqbil ka pakka plan hai, is liye present continuous aya. Ye pehchan bhi paper me ati hai.

8. Listen! Someone is crying outside.
Listen ke sath hamesha wo kaam ata hai jo is lamhe me ho raha ho.

9. While she was cooking, the bell rang.
While ke sath lamba kaam continuous me hai aur ghanti simple past me.

10. The sun is shining brightly today.
Is waqt ki halat bayan ho rahi hai, is liye is shining.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Live commentary me yehi tense chalta hai. The batsman is hitting the ball hard. Mausam ki report me, phone par kisi ko batana ho ke is waqt kya ho raha hai, kisi manzar ki tasveer kheenchna ho, har jagah continuous hai. Daftar me bhi likha hota hai ke the work is going on. Aur kisi guzre manzar ko bayan karna ho to past continuous hi wo rang bharta hai jo kahani ko zinda karta hai.

Ab paper wale andaaz ke do jumle dekho.

1. It was raining heavily when we reached home.
2. The students are preparing for the test these days.

Doosre jumle me these days par tawajjo do. Ye bhi present continuous ka ishara hai, kaam in dino chal raha hai.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Is lesson ki trick ghanti wali hai, aur ye tumhe kabhi nahi bhulegi. Raat ka manzar zehen me lao. Tum gehri neend so rahe ho. Neend lambi hai, film chal rahi hai, ye past continuous hai. Achanak ghanti bajti hai. Aik jhatka, aik lamha, khatam. Ye simple past hai. Bas aik line zehen me bitha lo. Lamba kaam ing wala, darmiyan me ghanti simple past. Jab bhi paper me while ya when wala jumla aye, pehle ghanti dhundo. Jo ghanti hai wo simple past, jo neend hai wo continuous.

Aur ing ki spelling ke liye ye do chhoti baatein bhi sath bandh lo. E bechara khamosh tha, ing ate hi nikal gaya. Chhota lafz akela tha, ing ne us ka aakhri letter pakar kar dohra kar diya.

Sahi: I know the answer.
Wajah: know stative verb hai. Ilm chalti hui cheez nahi, thehri hui halat hai.

Wajah: khamosh e ing se pehle gir jata hai.

Wajah: run chhota lafz hai, us ka n dohra hoga.

Wajah: they ke sath were ata hai, was nahi.

Ghalat: I was sleep when he called.
Sahi: I was sleeping when he called.
Wajah: was ke baad ing lazmi hai. Sirf was se continuous nahi banta.

Ghalat: He is wanting a new phone.
Sahi: He wants a new phone.
Wajah: want bhi stative hai, chahna koi chalta hua kaam nahi.

Ghalat: I am liking this book very much.

Wajah: like dil ki halat hai, is liye simple me rahega.

Wajah: sawal me bhi be ke sath ing lazmi hai. Sirf be se kaam nahi chalta.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Pehli tasveer ghanti wale manzar ki ho. Aik lambi patti par raat ka manzar bana ho, aik shakhs so raha ho aur patti ke neeche likha ho ke lamba kaam, past continuous. Us patti ko beech me aik chhota sa teer kaat raha ho jis par ghanti bani ho aur neeche likha ho ke chhota jhatka, simple past. Student aik nazar me while aur when wale jumle ki tartib samajh jaye, yehi is tasveer ka kaam hai.

Doosri tasveer ing ke spelling qanoon ki teen khano wali chart ho. Pehle khane me seedhe alfaaz hon, play playing aur read reading. Doosre khane me e girne wale hon, make making aur write writing, aur gire hue e ko halke rang me dikhaya jaye. Teesre khane me dohre hone wale hon, run running aur sit sitting, aur dohre letter ko gehray rang me dikhaya jaye. Aik kone me chhoti si jhalak ie wale khandan ki bhi ho, die dying. Ye chart board par bhi waisi hi lagegi jaisi paper ke sawal me ati hai.
