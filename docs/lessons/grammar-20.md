Lesson 20: Future. Will and Going To
Future. Ane wala waqt, aur faisla abhi ka hai ya plan pehle ka

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me ane wale waqt par sawal har saal ata hai. Kabhi will aur going to me se sahi wala chunna hota hai, kabhi jumla theek karna hota hai, aur kabhi wo mashhoor trap ata hai jis me when ke baad will laga kar aadhe students phans jate hain. Mazay ki baat ye hai ke ye sawal mushkil nahi hote. Bas tumhe ye samajhna hai ke bolne wala faisla is waqt kar raha hai ya plan pehle se bana hua hai. Ye aik farq samajh lo, is lesson ke saare number tumhare hain.

Pehle baat samajh lo

Dekho, ane wala waqt to aik hi hai, lekin us ki taraf jane ke raaste do hain. Farz karo tum bazaar khade ho aur achanak barish shuru ho jati hai. Tum usi waqt kehte ho, I will buy an umbrella. Ye faisla tum ne is lamhe me kiya, pehle se koi plan nahi tha. Ab doosri surat socho. Tum ne subah se socha hua hai ke sham ko dost se milna hai, ticket bhi le rakhi hai. Tum kaho ge, I am going to meet my friend this evening. Plan pehle se bana hua hai. English me yehi do raaste hain. Fori faisla will se, pehle se bana plan going to se.

Will kahan ata hai

Will teen jagah par sab se zyada ata hai. Pehli jagah wo faisla hai jo bolne ke lamhe me ho. The phone is ringing. I will answer it. Tum ne pehle se nahi socha tha, ghanti baji aur faisla ho gaya. Doosri jagah waada hai. I will help you with your preparation. I will not tell anyone your secret. Waade me will ka rang saaf nazar ata hai. Teesri jagah andeisha ya rai hai, jab hum apne khayal se ane wale waqt ki baat kehte hain. I think Pakistan will win the match. In my opinion, prices will rise next year. Yahan koi pakki nishani samne nahi, bas rai hai.

Aik aur jagah bhi yaad rakho. Madad ki peshkash aur darkhwast me bhi will ata hai. I will carry your bag. Will you please open the window? Ye jumle paper me bohat ate hain.

Going to kahan ata hai

Going to ki bhi do bari jagah hain. Pehli, pehle se socha hua plan ya irada. We are going to start a new batch in January. She is going to apply for the lecturer post. Faisla pehle ho chuka hai, ab bas elan ho raha hai. Doosri jagah wo andaza hai jo samne ki nishani dekh kar lagaya jaye. Look at those black clouds. It is going to rain. Badal samne hain, nishani maujood hai, is liye going to aya. Agar sirf rai hoti, baghair nishani ke, to will ata.

Dono ka farq aik jumle me yun samajh lo. Will bolne wale ke dil ka usi waqt ka faisla hai, going to us ke zehen me pehle se bani hui tasveer hai.

Shall ki baat bhi kar lo

Purani English me I aur we ke sath shall ata tha. Aaj ki English me will sab ke sath chal jata hai, lekin paper me shall do jagah ab bhi zinda hai. Pehli, peshkash me. Shall I open the window? Yani kya me khidki khol dun, tumhari ijazat aur marzi poochhi ja rahi hai. Doosri, mashware me. Shall we start the class? Chalo class shuru karen? He, she ya they ke sath shall aam tor par nahi ata, aur agar aye to us ka matlab sakht hukum ya qanoon hota hai, jo sarkari zuban me milta hai. Candidates shall bring their original documents. Aam bol chal ke liye bas ye yaad rakho ke shall ke sawal wale jumle I aur we ke sath ate hain.

Kal wale waqt ke isharay

Ane wale waqt ki pehchan aksar jumle me chupe isharo se hoti hai. Tomorrow, tomorrow morning, next week, next year, soon, in a few days, tonight. Ye isharay dekh kar tumhe foran pata chal jana chahiye ke jumla ane wale waqt ka hai. Paper me ye isharay tumhari madad ke liye diye jate hain, inhe nazar andaz mat karo.

Sab se bara trap: when wala jumla

Ab is lesson ki sab se aham baat suno, kyunke yehi paper ka sab se pasandeeda trap hai. Jab jumle ke do hisse hon aur aik hissa when, as soon as, before, after, until ya if se shuru ho, to us wale hisse me will nahi ata. Wahan present simple ata hai, chahe baat ane wale waqt ki hi kyun na ho. I will call you when I reach home. Dekho, reach home ane wale waqt ki baat hai, lekin when ke baad will nahi aya. When he comes, we will start the test. Doosre hisse me will hai, pehle hisse me nahi. Is qanoon ko zehen me bandh lo. Paper har saal is par number kat ta hai.

Board par qanoon Angrezi me

Board par ye baatein kuch yun likhi jati hain. Will is used for a decision made at the moment of speaking, for a promise, and for an opinion about the future. Going to is used for a plan made earlier and for a prediction based on something we can see now. Shall is used with I and we in questions that offer or suggest something, as in Shall I help you? Aur sab se aham line ye hai: after when, as soon as, before, after, until and if, use the present simple for the future, not will. When he comes, we will leave.

Misaalein board se

1. Watch out! You are going to fall.
Samne nishani hai, admi ladkhada raha hai. Is liye going to aya.

2. I will answer the door.
Ghanti is waqt baji aur faisla is waqt hua. Is liye will aya.

3. We are going to visit Murree next month.
Safar ka plan pehle se bana hua hai, is liye going to.

4. I promise I will work hard for the test.
Ye waada hai, aur waade me will ata hai.

5. Look at the sky. It is going to rain soon.
Kale badal samne dikhai de rahe hain. Nishani par andaza going to se hota hai.

6. I think he will pass the PPSC test this time.
Ye sirf rai hai, koi nishani samne nahi, is liye will.

7. Shall we revise the grammar lesson once more?
Shall we se mashwara manga ja raha hai. Ye shall ki asal jagah hai.

8. I will send you the notes when I reach home.
When ke baad will nahi aya, present simple aya. Yehi is lesson ka bara trap hai.

9. She is going to become a lecturer.
Us ne tayyari aur plan pehle se kar rakha hai, is liye going to.

10. The train will arrive at six tomorrow morning.
Waqt ka ishara tomorrow morning saaf keh raha hai ke baat ane wale waqt ki hai.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Rozmarra zindagi me har waqt ane wale waqt ki baatein hoti hain. Waade, plan, elan, darkhwastein. Daftar ki angrezi me likha hota hai ke the meeting will be held on Monday, ya the department is going to announce new posts. Akhbar ki surkhiyan bhi yahi zuban bolti hain.

Ab paper wale andaaz ke do jumle dekho.

1. The commission will announce the result as soon as the interviews are completed.
2. Many candidates are going to appear in the test next month.

Pehle jumle me as soon as ke baad dekho, will nahi aya, are completed aya hai. Yehi wo trap hai jo ab tum se nahi chhupega.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab wo trick jo will aur going to ka farq zindagi bhar yaad karwa degi. Do doston ki tasveer zehen me banao. Pehla dost Will hai. Wo darwaze par khara hota hai. Ghanti bajti hai to kehta hai, I will open it. Us ka har faisla usi lamhe ka hota hai, jeb me koi diary nahi. Doosra dost Going To hai. Us ki jeb me diary hoti hai jis me kal ka saara plan pehle se likha hota hai. Wo kabhi achanak faisla nahi karta, wo apni diary parh kar batata hai. Bas ye jumla yaad kar lo: darwaze par faisla Will, jeb ki diary Going To. Aur when wale trap ke liye aik line aur bandh lo: when aye to will ko bahar roko.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ghalat: I will call you when I will reach Lahore.
Sahi: I will call you when I reach Lahore.
Wajah: when wale hisse me will nahi ata, present simple ata hai.

Ghalat: Watch out, you will fall down from the stairs.
Sahi: Watch out, you are going to fall down from the stairs.
Wajah: girne ki nishani samne hai, is liye going to sahi hai. Paper is farq ko bohat poochta hai.

Ghalat: I am going to help you with this heavy bag, wait a moment.
Sahi: I will help you with this heavy bag, wait a moment.
Wajah: madad ki peshkash usi lamhe ka faisla hai, pehle se plan nahi, is liye will.

Ghalat: He will to arrive tomorrow.
Sahi: He will arrive tomorrow.
Wajah: will ke baad verb ki pehli shakal seedhi ati hai, to nahi lagta.

Ghalat: Shall he help us in this matter?
Sahi: Will he help us in this matter?
Wajah: aam sawal me shall sirf I aur we ke sath ata hai.

Ghalat: If it will rain tomorrow, the match will be cancelled.
Sahi: If it rains tomorrow, the match will be cancelled.
Wajah: if wale hisse me bhi will nahi ata, bilkul when ki tarah.

3. Jawab C hai, will answer. Ghanti is waqt baji aur faisla is waqt hua. Darwaze wala dost Will yaad hai na?

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Pehli tasveer do doston ki ho, Will aur Going To. Will darwaze par khara ho, ghanti baj rahi ho aur us ke zehen me usi lamhe ka faisla chamak raha ho. Going To ke hath me diary ho jis me kal ka plan likha ho, aur us ke peeche train ka ticket aur calendar dikhai de. Neeche aik line likhi ho: darwaze par faisla Will, jeb ki diary Going To. Ye tasveer dekh kar student dono ka farq kabhi nahi bhulega.

Doosri tasveer when wale trap ki ho. Aik darwaza bana ho jis par when likha ho. Will us darwaze ke bahar ruka hua ho aur andar present simple khara ho. Jumla is tarah dikhaya jaye ke when he comes pehle hisse me ho aur we will start doosre hisse me, aur will sirf doosre hisse me chamak raha ho. Paper ka sab se mehnga trap aik nazar me zehen me baith jayega.
