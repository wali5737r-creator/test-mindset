Lesson 21: Frequency Adverbs and Prepositions of Time
Frequency Adverbs aur Time ke Prepositions. Kitni dafa, aur waqt ke sath at, on, in

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me is lesson ke do hisse hain aur dono hi har saal number dete hain. Pehla hissa frequency adverbs ka hai, yani always, usually, often, sometimes aur never. Paper in ki jagah poochta hai, jumle me ye kahan bethte hain. Doosra hissa waqt ke prepositions ka hai, at, on aur in, aur ye to paper ka sab se purana dost hai. At five o clock, on Monday, in June. Teen chhote se alfaaz, lekin galti karne par poora jumla ghalat ho jata hai. Achi baat ye hai ke dono hisso ke qanoon bohat saaf hain. Aik dafa jagah aur size wala qanoon samajh lo, ye sawal tumhare liye muft ke number ban jayenge.

Pehle frequency wali baat samajh lo

Dekho, kuch alfaaz batate hain ke koi kaam kitni dafa hota hai. Roz hota hai, kabhi kabhi hota hai, ya kabhi bhi nahi hota. In alfaaz ko frequency adverbs kehte hain. In ki aik seedhi si sarhi zehen me bana lo. Always sab se upar hai, is ka matlab hai har dafa, so feesad. Us ke neeche usually ata hai, aam tor par, takreeban assi feesad. Phir often hai, aksar. Darmiyan me sometimes hai, kabhi kabhi, adha adha mamla. Us ke neeche rarely aur seldom hain, bohat kam. Aur sab se neeche never hai, kabhi nahi, zero feesad.

Asal masla in ki jagah ka hai

Paper asal me ye poochta hai ke ye alfaaz jumle me bethte kahan hain. Qanoon yaad kar lo. Agar jumle me aam verb ho, jaise go, read ya play, to frequency adverb us verb se pehle ata hai. He always reads the newspaper. We usually go to the village on Fridays. They never tell a lie. Verb se pehle, ye pehli jagah hai.

Lekin agar jumle me is, am, are, was ya were ho, yani be wala verb ho, to qanoon ulta ho jata hai. Frequency adverb be verb ke baad ata hai. She is always late. They are often busy. I am never free on Sunday. Be pehle, adverb baad me. Yehi farq paper ka trap hai.

Aik teesri surat bhi hai. Agar helping verb sath ho, jaise has, have, can ya will, to frequency adverb helping verb ke baad aur asal verb se pehle ata hai. He has never visited Lahore. You can always ask me. Do verb hon to adverb un ke darmiyan beth jata hai.

Never ke sath aik khaas khabardari

Never khud hi nafi hai, is ka matlab hi kabhi nahi hai. Is liye is ke sath doosri nafi nahi lagti. He never comes late sahi hai. He does not never come kehna ghalat hai, kyunke do nafi mil kar matlab ulta kar deti hain. Rarely aur seldom ka bhi yehi haal hai, ye bhi apne andar nafi rakhe hue hain. Paper double negative wala trap bohat shauq se lagata hai.

Ab waqt wale at, on aur in ki baat

Ab doosre hisse ki taraf ao. Waqt ke sath at, on aur in ka faisla size se hota hai. Ghari ka waqt sab se chhota hota hai, is liye us ke sath at ata hai. At five o clock, at half past seven, at noon, at midnight. Din aur tareekh darmiyan ke size ke hain, un ke sath on ata hai. On Monday, on Friday, on the tenth of June, on my birthday. Aur mahina, saal, mausam aur sadi bare dabbe hain, un ke sath in ata hai. In June, in winter, in 2026, in the twenty first century.

Din ke hisso me bhi yehi chalta hai. In the morning, in the afternoon, in the evening. Lekin khabardar, night ke sath at ata hai, at night. Ye chhota sa exception paper ka bohat pasandeeda hai. Aur agar kisi khaas din ki subah ya sham ho to on ata hai. On Monday morning, on Friday evening. Din ka naam aa jaye to on aa jata hai, chahe hissa subah ka hi kyun na ho.

Do khaas jagah aur yaad rakho. Aik, at the weekend British andaaz hai aur paper isi ko sahi manta hai. Doosri, tomorrow, yesterday, tonight, next aur last ke sath koi preposition nahi ata. He came yesterday, sahi hai. He will come tomorrow, sahi hai. In ke aage in, on ya at lagana seedhi ghalti hai.

Board par qanoon Angrezi me

Board par ye qanoon kuch yun likha jata hai. Frequency adverbs come before the main verb, as in He always works hard, but after the verb be, as in She is always late. With a helping verb they stand between the two verbs, as in He has never lied. Never is already negative, so do not add another not with it. For time: use at for clock times and for night, use on for days and dates, and use in for months, years, seasons and parts of the day. At 5 o clock, on Monday, in June. Aur aakhri line: no preposition is used before tomorrow, yesterday, tonight, next or last.

Misaalein board se

1. He is always punctual.
Be verb is aya hai, is liye always us ke baad betha hai.

2. She often helps the poor.
Aam verb helps hai, is liye often us se pehle aya hai.

3. They have never seen snow.
Helping verb have aur asal verb seen ke darmiyan never betha hai.

4. The class begins at nine o clock.
Ghari ka waqt hai, sab se chhota, is liye at.

5. The test will be held on Monday.
Din ka naam hai, is liye on.

6. He was born in June 1998.
June mahina hai aur 1998 saal, dono bare dabbe hain, is liye in.

7. Birds sing in the morning.
Din ka hissa hai, kisi khaas din ka naam nahi, is liye in.

8. He reached home on Friday night.
Din ka naam Friday aa gaya, is liye night ke bawajud on aya.

9. She studies at night.
Night ke sath hamesha at ata hai. Ye exception wala jumla hai, isay khaas yaad rakho.

10. We will submit the form next week.
Next ke sath koi preposition nahi ata. In next week kehna ghalat hai.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Rozmarra ki zindagi inhi do cheezon se bhari hui hai. Waqt batana, din banana, aadat bayan karna. Daftar ki angrezi me notifications me likha hota hai ke the interview will be held on Monday at ten o clock in the main hall. Dekho, aik hi jumle me teeno aa gaye, on din ke liye, at ghari ke liye, in jagah ke bare dabbe ke liye. Jo student ye farq samajh jaye, us ke liye aisi lines parhna khail ban jati hain.

Ab paper wale andaaz ke do jumle dekho.

1. Candidates are always advised to reach the centre before nine o clock on the test day.
2. He has never used unfair means in any examination.

Pehle jumle me are ke baad always betha hai aur doosre me has aur used ke darmiyan never. Dono jagah wahi qanoon chal raha hai jo upar parha.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Frequency ke liye aik chhoti si line yaad kar lo. Be ke baad, verb se pehle. Be verb aye to adverb us ke peeche chipak jata hai, aam verb aye to adverb us ke aage khara ho jata hai. Bas.

Aur at, on, in ke liye size wali trick hai, jo kabhi nahi bhulti. Teen cheezein samne rakho. Ghari, calendar aur bara dabba. Ghari sab se chhoti hai, us ke sath at. Calendar ka aik din darmiyan hai, us ke sath on. Mahina aur saal bara dabba hai, us ke andar bohat se din hote hain, us ke sath in. Jab bhi shak ho, bas ye poochho ke ye waqt ghari jitna chhota hai, din jitna hai ya dabbe jitna bara hai. Jawab khud preposition bata dega.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ghalat: He always is late for class.
Sahi: He is always late for class.
Wajah: be verb is hai, is liye always us ke baad ayega.

Ghalat: She does not never miss her prayers.
Sahi: She never misses her prayers.
Wajah: never khud nafi hai, doosri nafi sath nahi lagti.

Ghalat: The meeting is in Monday.
Sahi: The meeting is on Monday.
Wajah: din ke sath on ata hai, in nahi.

Ghalat: He was born on June.
Sahi: He was born in June.
Wajah: mahina bara dabba hai, is liye in.

Ghalat: The train arrives in five o clock.
Sahi: The train arrives at five o clock.
Wajah: ghari ke waqt ke sath at ata hai.

Ghalat: I will meet you in tomorrow.
Sahi: I will meet you tomorrow.
Wajah: tomorrow ke sath koi preposition nahi ata.

Ghalat: He reads a book in the night.
Sahi: He reads a book at night.
Wajah: waqt ke tor par night ke sath at ata hai, in nahi.

Ghalat: On the morning, he left early.
Sahi: In the morning, he left early. Aur agar din bhi ho to: On Monday morning, he left early.
Wajah: sirf morning ho to in, din ka naam sath ho to on.

Jawab aur un ki wajah

1. Jawab B hai, is always. Be verb ke baad frequency adverb ata hai.
2. Jawab A hai, never. Has aur cheated ke darmiyan never betha hai aur nafi khud usi me hai.
3. Jawab C hai, at. Ghari ka waqt sab se chhota hota hai, is liye at.
4. Jawab C hai, on. Din ke naam ke sath on ata hai.
5. Jawab C hai, in. Saal bara dabba hai, is liye in.
6. Jawab B hai. Never ke sath doosri nafi nahi lagti, yehi double negative wala trap tha.
7. Jawab C hai, at. Midnight ghari ki duniya ka waqt hai, is liye at.
8. Jawab B hai, on. Friday ka naam aa gaya to evening ke bawajud on lagega.
9. Jawab C hai. Tomorrow ke sath koi preposition nahi ata.
10. Jawab B hai, usually play. Aam verb se pehle frequency adverb ata hai.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Pehli tasveer size wale qanoon ki ho. Teen cheezein aik sarhi ki shakal me rakhi hon. Sab se chhoti seedhi par ghari ho aur us ke sath bara sa AT likha ho. Darmiyan wali seedhi par calendar ka aik panna ho jis par Monday ghera laga ho aur sath ON likha ho. Sab se upar bare dabbe ka khana ho jis me June, winter aur 2026 likhe hon aur sath IN likha ho. Student aik nazar me samajh jayega ke waqt jitna chhota, preposition bhi utna hi makhsoos hai.

Doosri tasveer frequency wali sarhi ki ho. Aik thermometer jaisi sarhi bani ho jis ke sab se upar always so feesad par ho, phir usually, often, sometimes, rarely aur sab se neeche never zero par. Har lafz ke samne aik chhota sa jumla bhi ho jis me us ki sahi jagah dikhai de, be wale jumle me baad me aur aam verb wale jumle me pehle. Ye sarhi zehen me baith gayi to paper ke dono hisse pakke ho jayenge.
