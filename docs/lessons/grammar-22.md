Lesson 22: Infinitive and Gerund
Infinitive aur Gerund. To wala verb, aur ing wala verb jo noun ban jata hai

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me aik sawal aisa zaroor ata hai jahan do verbs sath sath aa jate hain, aur paper poochta hai ke doosre verb ke sath to lagega ya ing. I want to go sahi hai ya I want going? I enjoy swimming sahi hai ya I enjoy to swim? Zyada tar students ye sawal andaze se karte hain aur andaza paper me number nahi deta. Sach ye hai ke ye verbs ki chhoti chhoti families hain. Har family ka apna mizaj hai. Jo student families pehchan le, us ke liye ye sawal zindagi bhar asaan ho jata hai. Isi liye ye lesson paper ke hisaab se bohat aham hai.

Pehle dono ko pehchan lo

Dekho, pehle ye samajh lo ke hum kis cheez ki baat kar rahe hain. Jab verb se pehle to lag jaye, jaise to go, to read, to learn, to usay infinitive kehte hain. Aur jab verb ke sath ing lag kar wo noun ka kaam karne lage, jaise swimming, reading, walking, to usay gerund kehte hain. Gerund ki pehchan ye hai ke wo jumle me noun ki jagah beth jata hai. Smoking is bad for health. Dekho, smoking jumle ka subject hai, bilkul kisi noun ki tarah. Reading improves your mind. Yahan bhi reading noun ka kaam kar raha hai.

Urdu me socho to baat aur saaf ho jayegi. Swimming ka matlab hai tairna, aur tairna khud aik kaam ka naam hai. Jab kaam naam ban jaye to wo gerund hai.

To wali family

Ab pehli family se milo. Kuch verbs aisi hain jin ke baad hamesha to wala verb ata hai. Want, hope, decide, plan, promise, agree, learn, need, expect, offer, refuse, manage aur fail. In ki misalein dekho. I want to become a teacher. We decided to start early. She promised to help me. He refused to answer. Tum aik baat mehsoos karo ge ke in sab verbs me aane wale waqt ki, irade ki aur aage barhne ki khushbu hai. Want aage ki khwahish hai, decide aage ka faisla hai, promise aage ka waada hai. Isi liye in ke peeche to ata hai, kyunke to ka matlab hi aage ki taraf hai.

Ing wali family

Doosri family ka mizaj is se ulta hai. Kuch verbs ke baad hamesha ing wala verb ata hai. Enjoy, avoid, mind, finish, keep, practice, suggest, admit, deny, miss, risk aur imagine. Misalein dekho. I enjoy reading poetry. Avoid making noise. She finished writing the letter. He admitted taking the money. In verbs me jo kaam ata hai wo pehle se chal raha hota hai ya aik aadat aur tajurbe ki surat me samne ata hai. Enjoy usi cheez ka hota hai jo ho rahi ho. Finish usi kaam ka hota hai jo chal raha tha.

Gerund noun ke tor par

Ing wali shakal ki aik aur bari jagah samajh lo. Jab ing wala verb jumle ka subject ho ya kisi preposition ke baad aye to wo lazmi ing hi hoga. Walking is the best exercise. She is fond of singing. Thank you for coming. Preposition ke baad to kabhi nahi ata, hamesha ing ata hai. Ye qanoon bohat pakka hai. I am interested in learning English. In ke baad learning aya, to learn nahi. Paper is qanoon ko bohat azmata hai.

Aik khaas trap: to jab preposition ho

Ab wo trap suno jo bare bare students ko phansata hai. Kuch jumlo me to verb wala to nahi hota, wo preposition hota hai, aur us ke baad ing ata hai. I look forward to meeting you. I am used to getting up early. She objected to working late. In jumlo me to ke baad meeting, getting aur working aya hai. Pehchan ye hai ke agar to ke baad seedha koi noun rakh kar jumla ban jaye, jaise I look forward to your reply, to ye to preposition hai aur us ke baad verb aye to ing lagega.

Stop wala mashhoor farq

Aik joda aisa hai jis ka matlab to aur ing se badal jata hai, aur paper isay bohat pasand karta hai. Stop smoking ka matlab hai cigrette peena chor do. Stop to smoke ka matlab hai kisi kaam ko rok kar cigrette peene jao. Dekha? Ing aye to kaam chorne ki baat hai, to aye to kaam rok kar doosra kaam shuru karne ki baat hai. Remember aur forget ke sath bhi aisa hi halka sa farq ata hai. I remember meeting him ka matlab hai mujhe us se milna yaad hai, kaam ho chuka. Remember to lock the door ka matlab hai darwaza band karna yaad rakhna, kaam aage karna hai. Peeche ki yaad par ing, aage ki zimmedari par to.

Board par qanoon Angrezi me

Board par ye baatein kuch yun likhi jati hain. The infinitive is to plus the base verb: to go, to learn. The gerund is verb plus ing used as a noun: swimming, reading. Some verbs are followed by the infinitive: want, hope, decide, plan, promise, agree, learn, need, expect, offer and refuse. Some verbs are followed by the gerund: enjoy, avoid, mind, finish, keep, practice, suggest, admit, deny and miss. After a preposition, always use the gerund: fond of singing, interested in learning. Aur aakhri line: stop smoking means give it up, stop to smoke means pause for it.

Misaalein board se

1. She wants to join the police service.
Want to wali family ka sardar hai, is liye to join aya.

2. We enjoy playing cricket in the evening.
Enjoy ke baad hamesha ing ata hai, is liye playing.

3. He decided to apply for the post.
Decide ke baad to ata hai. Faisla aage ka hai.

4. Avoid talking loudly in the library.
Avoid ke baad ing. Talking sahi hai, to talk ghalat hoga.

5. Smoking is injurious to health.
Smoking jumle ka subject hai. Gerund noun ban kar betha hai.

6. I am looking forward to hearing from you.
Yahan to preposition hai, is liye hearing aya. Ye paper ka pasandeeda trap hai.

7. She promised to return the book on time.
Promise ke baad to. Waada hamesha aage ka hota hai.

8. He kept asking questions during the lecture.
Keep ke baad ing ata hai, kaam jari rehne ki surat hai.

9. They stopped working to take some rest.
Kaam roka taake aram karein. Ruk kar doosra kaam karna ho to to ata hai.

10. The patient stopped smoking on the advice of the doctor.
Yahan smoking chorne ki baat hai, is liye ing. Pichli misaal se farq gaur se dekho.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Rozmarra ki bol chal aur khat o kitabat me ye jumle har jaga milte hain. I hope to see you soon. Thank you for inviting me. We are planning to visit Lahore. Daftari angrezi me bhi yahi chalta hai. The candidates are advised to bring their documents. You are requested to avoid using mobile phones. Jo student families pehchan le, wo ye jumle parhte hue kabhi rukta nahi.

Ab paper wale andaaz ke do jumle dekho.

1. He admitted copying in the test and promised never to do it again.
2. Students are advised to avoid wasting time and to keep practising daily.

Dekho, pehle jumle me admit ke baad copying aur promise ke baad to do, dono qanoon aik sath aa gaye hain. Paper bilkul aise hi jumle banata hai.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab wo trick jo dono families ko hamesha ke liye zehen me bitha degi. Do gharon ki tasveer banao. Pehle ghar ka naam hai Aage Wale. Is ghar me Want, Hope, Decide, Plan aur Promise rehte hain. Ye sab aage ki sochne wale log hain, in ke zehen me hamesha kal hota hai. Ye jab bhi bahar jate hain to apne dost To ko sath le jate hain, kyunke to ka matlab hi aage ki taraf hai. Doosre ghar ka naam hai Hote Kaam Wale. Is me Enjoy, Avoid, Finish, Admit aur Keep rehte hain. Ye un kaamo ki baat karte hain jo ho rahe hain ya ho chuke hain, is liye ye apne dost Ing ko sath rakhte hain. Bas ye jumla yaad kar lo: aage ki soch, sath To. Hota hua kaam, sath Ing. Shak ho to bas ye poochho, ye verb aage dekh raha hai ya hote kaam ki baat kar raha hai.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ghalat: I enjoy to read books.
Sahi: I enjoy reading books.
Wajah: enjoy ing wali family ka hai, is ke baad to nahi ata.

Ghalat: He wants going home.
Sahi: He wants to go home.
Wajah: want ke baad to ata hai.

Ghalat: She suggested to take a break.
Sahi: She suggested taking a break.
Wajah: suggest ke baad ing ata hai. Ye ghalti bohat aam hai.

Ghalat: I look forward to meet you.
Sahi: I look forward to meeting you.
Wajah: yahan to preposition hai, is liye us ke baad ing ayega.

Ghalat: He is fond of to sing.
Sahi: He is fond of singing.
Wajah: preposition of ke baad hamesha ing ata hai.

Ghalat: The doctor advised him to stopping smoking.
Sahi: The doctor advised him to stop smoking. Aur agar chorne ki baat ho to: He stopped smoking.
Wajah: to ke baad verb ki pehli shakal ati hai, ing nahi. Aur stop ke baad ing ka matlab chorna hota hai.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Pehli tasveer do gharon ki ho. Aik ghar ke darwaze par Aage Wale likha ho, andar Want, Hope, Decide, Plan aur Promise khade hon aur un ke sath un ka dost To ho, jis ke aage kal ka calendar dikhai de. Doosre ghar par Hote Kaam Wale likha ho, andar Enjoy, Avoid, Finish aur Admit hon aur un ke sath Ing ho, peeche chalti hui gharri aur hote hue kaam ki tasveer ho. Neeche aik line ho: aage ki soch, sath To. Hota hua kaam, sath Ing.

Doosri tasveer stop wale farq ki ho. Aik admi do raasto ke darmiyan khara ho. Aik raaste par cigrette bujhi hui dikhai de aur likha ho stop smoking, matlab chor do. Doosre raaste par admi kursi par betha cigrette ki taraf ja raha ho aur likha ho stop to smoke, matlab ruk kar peene gaya. Ye aik tasveer paper ke is pasandeeda farq ko hamesha ke liye saaf kar degi.
