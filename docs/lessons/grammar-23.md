Lesson 23: Present and Past Participle
Present aur Past Participle. Ing aur ed wali shaklein jab adjective ban jayen

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me aik trap aisa hai jo har saal, bina nagha, number kat ta hai. Interesting aur interested ka trap. The film was interesting sahi hai, I was interested sahi hai, lekin aadhe se zyada students in dono ko ulta kar dete hain. Is ke ilawa broken window, running water aur boiled egg jaise jumle bhi paper me ate hain, jahan verb ki shaklein adjective ban kar bethi hoti hain. Achi khabar ye hai ke is poori uljhan ka hal aik chhoti si line me hai jo is lesson ke aakhir tak tumhe rat jayegi. Cheez ing, insan ed. Bas ye samajh lo, is lesson ke saare sawal tumhare hain.

Pehle participle ko pehchan lo

Dekho, verb ki do shaklein aisi hain jo aksar adjective ka kaam karti hain. Aik wo jis ke aakhir me ing ata hai, jaise interesting, boring, running, sleeping. Isay present participle kehte hain. Doosri wo jis ke aakhir me ed ya teesri shakal ati hai, jaise interested, bored, broken, written. Isay past participle kehte hain. Tum ne ye shaklein tense wale lessons me dekhi hain. He is running. She has written a letter. Wahan ye verb ka hissa thi. Lekin jab yehi shaklein kisi cheez ya shakhs ki khoobi bayan karne lag jayen to ye adjective ban jati hain. A running train. A written test. Yahan running aur written train aur test ki sifft bayan kar rahe hain.

Asal raaz: feeling kis ki hai

Ab is lesson ka dil suno. Ing aur ed ka farq feeling ki direction se banta hai. Kuch cheezein feeling paida karti hain, aur kuch log us feeling ko mehsoos karte hain. Film feeling paida karti hai, is liye film interesting hoti hai. Tum feeling mehsoos karte ho, is liye tum interested hote ho. Lecturer ki lecture feeling paida karta hai, wo boring hota hai. Students us feeling ko jhelte hain, wo bored hote hain. Qanoon saaf hai. Jo cheez ya wajah feeling paida kare, us ke liye ing wali shakal. Jo shakhs feeling mehsoos kare, us ke liye ed wali shakal.

Urdu me socho to baat foran saaf ho jayegi. Dilchasp aur dilchaspi lene wala aik nahi hain. Kitaab dilchasp hoti hai, parhne wala dilchaspi rakhta hai. English me dilchasp ke liye interesting aur dilchaspi rakhne wale ke liye interested ata hai. Jab bhi shak ho, Urdu me ye sawal poochho: ye feeling de raha hai ya le raha hai? Dene wala ing, lene wala ed.

Jodon ki fehrist zehen me bitha lo

Ye jode paper me bar bar ate hain, inhe jodon ki surat me hi yaad karo. Interesting aur interested. Boring aur bored. Exciting aur excited. Surprising aur surprised. Confusing aur confused. Tiring aur tired. Satisfying aur satisfied. Amazing aur amazed. Disappointing aur disappointed. Har jode me pehla lafz wajah ke liye hai aur doosra mehsoos karne wale ke liye. The news was surprising. We were surprised at the news. The work is tiring. I am tired. Dekho, har misaal me wahi aik qanoon chal raha hai.

Noun se pehle wale participle

Participle ki doosri bari jagah noun se pehle hai, jahan ye seedha adjective ban kar bethta hai. Running water ka matlab hai behta hua pani. A broken window ka matlab hai tuti hui khidki. A sleeping baby sota hua bacha hai. Boiled water ubla hua pani hai aur a boiled egg ubla hua anda. Fallen leaves gire hue patte hain. In jumlo me ing wali shakal us kaam ke liye hai jo abhi chal raha hai, aur ed wali shakal us cheez ke liye hai jis par kaam ho chuka hai. Pani behta hai, is liye running. Khidki tooti ja chuki hai, is liye broken. Ye farq bhi paper poochta hai.

Aik teesri pehchan bhi rakh lo. Jab ye shaklein has, have ya is, was ke sath ain to ye verb ka hissa hoti hain, adjective nahi. He has broken the window me broken verb hai. The window is broken me broken halat bayan kar raha hai. Paper me in ki pehchan sawal ka hissa hoti hai, lekin filhal tum bas itna yaad rakho ke wahan bhi teesri shakal hi chalti hai, broke nahi.

Board par qanoon Angrezi me

Board par ye baat kuch yun likhi jati hai. The present participle ends in ing and the past participle ends in ed or takes the third form. Used as adjectives, the ing form describes the thing that causes a feeling, and the ed form describes the person who feels it. The book is interesting, I am interested in the book. The lesson is boring, the students are bored. Participles also stand before nouns: running water, a broken chair, a sleeping child, a written application. Aur aakhri line wahi hai jo is lesson ki jaan hai: the thing takes ing, the feeler takes ed.

Misaalein board se

1. The lecture was so boring that half the class slept.
Lecture feeling paida kar raha hai, is liye boring.

2. The students were bored by the long lecture.
Students feeling mehsoos kar rahe hain, is liye bored.

3. I am interested in this post.
Dilchaspi rakhne wala insan hai, is liye interested.

4. It is an interesting book on Pakistan history.
Kitaab dilchaspi paida karti hai, is liye interesting.

5. We were surprised at his success.
Mehsoos karne wale hum hain, is liye surprised.

6. Running water should not be wasted.
Behta hua pani. Kaam chal raha hai, is liye running adjective ban kar aya hai.

7. Someone has broken the window; it is a broken window now.
Pehle jumle me broken verb ka hissa hai, doosre me khidki ki halat bayan kar raha hai.

8. She was tired after the long journey.
Thakawat mehsoos karne wali wo hai, is liye tired. Tiring us safar ke liye hota.

9. The exciting match kept everyone awake.
Match feeling paida kar raha hai, is liye exciting.

10. Please submit a written application with your documents.
Written application yani likhi hui darkhwast. Ed wali shakal noun se pehle adjective hai.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Rozmarra ki angrezi me ye jumle har jagah hain. I am satisfied with my preparation. The results were disappointing. Daftari aur sarkari zuban me to ye aur bhi zyada hain. Interested candidates may apply. A written test will be held. Damaged documents will not be accepted. PPSC ki apni advertisement bhi inhi alfaaz se bhari hoti hai. Jo student ye farq samajh le, wo advertisement parhte hue bhi nahi rukta aur paper me bhi number nahi katwata.

Ab paper wale andaaz ke do jumle dekho.

1. The candidates were confused by the confusing instructions given at the centre.
2. Only interested and qualified candidates are invited to apply for the advertised posts.

Pehle jumle me dono shaklein aik sath aayi hain. Instructions confusing hain kyunke wo uljhan paida karti hain, aur candidates confused hain kyunke wo uljhan mehsoos karte hain. Aik jumla, poora qanoon.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab wo trick jo is farq ko zindagi bhar yaad karwa degi. Aik cinema ki tasveer zehen me banao. Parde par film chal rahi hai. Film ke neeche bara sa ING likha hai, kyunke film feeling dene wali hai. Samne kursiyon par bethe log hain, un ke upar bara sa ED likha hai, kyunke wo feeling lene wale hain. Film interesting hai to log interested hain. Film boring hai to log bored hain. Bas ye jumla zehen me bandh lo: film ING hai, main ED hun. Paper me jab bhi ye sawal aye, pehle ye poochho ke ye lafz film ki jagah hai ya kursiyon walon ki jagah. Jawab khud mil jayega.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ghalat: I am very interesting in this job.
Sahi: I am very interested in this job.
Wajah: dilchaspi rakhne wala insan hai, is liye ed wali shakal interested ayegi. Ye is lesson ki sab se mashhoor ghalti hai.

Ghalat: The story was very interested.
Sahi: The story was very interesting.
Wajah: kahani feeling dene wali hai, lene wali nahi, is liye ing.

Ghalat: He felt boring during the lecture.
Sahi: He felt bored during the lecture.
Wajah: mehsoos karne wala wo hai. Boring lecture ke liye hota.

Ghalat: She gave a surprised look to the news.
Sahi: The news was surprising, and she was surprised.
Wajah: news dene wali hai is liye surprising, wo lene wali hai is liye surprised. Dono ko apni jagah rakho.

Ghalat: The window is broke.
Sahi: The window is broken.
Wajah: is ke baad teesri shakal broken ati hai, broke past tense hai, yahan nahi chalega.

Ghalat: I am confuse by this rule.
Sahi: I am confused by this rule. Aur rule ke liye: This rule is confusing.
Wajah: confuse verb hai, adjective chahiye to ed wali shakal confused lagegi.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Pehli tasveer cinema wali ho. Bare parde par film chal rahi ho aur us ke neeche bara ING likha ho. Samne kursiyon par log bethe hon, koi muskurata hua, koi oonghta hua, aur un ke upar bara ED likha ho. Neeche aik line ho: film ING hai, main ED hun. Is aik tasveer se interesting aur interested ka farq kabhi ulta nahi hoga.

Doosri tasveer do khano wali chart ho. Pehle khane me noun se pehle wale ing participle hon, running water, a sleeping baby, boiling milk, aur har aik ke sath chhoti si tasveer ho. Doosre khane me ed wale hon, a broken window, boiled rice, a written application, gire hue patte. Upar aik line likhi ho ke chal raha kaam ing, ho chuka kaam ed. Paper ke participle wale sawal is chart se hal ho jayenge.
