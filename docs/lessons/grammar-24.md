Lesson 24: Passive Voice ki Buniyad
Passive Voice ki Buniyad. Jab kaam aham ho, karne wala nahi

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me passive voice par sawal na aye, aisa kam hi hota hai. Kabhi active ko passive me badalna hota hai, kabhi passive ko pehchanna hota hai, aur kabhi jumla theek karna hota hai jahan is wrote jaisi ghalti chhupi hoti hai. Akhbar ki surkhiyan, sarkari notifications aur PPSC ki apni advertisements, teeno passive se bhari hui hain. Applications are invited. The last date has been announced. Ye jumle parhne ke liye bhi passive ki samajh lazmi hai. Aik wazeh baat pehle se sun lo. Ye lesson sirf buniyad hai. Is me hum sirf present aur past ke saada passive seekhen ge. Poora passive, har tense ke sath, modal ke sath aur hukum wale jumlo ke sath, Level 2 me alag lesson me ayega. Buniyad pakki ho to wo lesson asaan lagega.

Active aur passive ka farq pehle samajh lo

Dekho, har jumle me aik kaam hota hai aur aik karne wala. Jab jumla karne wale se shuru ho to wo active hai. The teacher checked the papers. Yahan teacher aage hai, kaam peeche. Lekin kabhi kabhi karne wala aham nahi hota, kaam aur jis par kaam hua wo aham hota hai. Tab jumla ulta kar diya jata hai. The papers were checked. Ab papers aage hain, aur karne wala ya to peeche chala gaya ya ghayab ho gaya. Isi ulti surat ko passive voice kehte hain.

Urdu me bhi ye roz hota hai. Hum kehte hain ke darkhwastein talab ki gayi hain. Kis ne talab ki, ye nahi bataya. Khana pakaya gaya. Kis ne pakaya, ghayab. Urdu ki yehi gayi, gaya wali surat English me passive hai. Jab bhi Urdu me gaya ya gayi sunai de, samajh lo ke English me passive banega.

Passive banta kese hai

Banane ka tareeqa teen kadmo me hai aur hamesha yehi teen kadam chalte hain. Pehla kadam, jis par kaam hua hai usay jumle ke aage le ao. Doosra kadam, us ke sath be ki munasib shakal lagao, is, are, was ya were. Teesra kadam, verb ki teesri shakal lagao. Bas, passive tayyar hai.

Misaal se dekho. Active hai: He writes a letter. Letter ko aage lao. Letter aik hai, is liye is lagega. Phir writes ki teesri shakal written. Jumla bana: A letter is written by him. Ab past ki misaal lo. Active hai: She wrote a letter. Letter aage aya. Waqt past hai, is liye was lagega. Phir written. Jumla bana: A letter was written by her. Dekha? Be ka faisla waqt aur aage wale noun se hota hai, aur aakhri shakal hamesha teesri hoti hai. Written kabhi wrote nahi banta, aur is ke sath wrote kabhi nahi ata.

Present aur past ka saada passive

Is lesson me hum sirf do waqto par rahen ge. Present me is aur are ke sath teesri shakal ati hai. English is taught in our school. The rooms are cleaned daily. Past me was aur were ke sath teesri shakal ati hai. The fort was built in 1648. The windows were broken in the storm. Aik hai to is ya was, zyada hain to are ya were. Ye faisla aage wale noun se hota hai, purane karne wale se nahi. Yehi chhoti si baat aksar ghalti karwati hai, is liye isay zehen me bitha lo.

By wala hissa: kab aye, kab na aye

Passive ke aakhir me aksar by ke sath karne wala ata hai. The poem was written by Iqbal. Yahan karne wala aham hai, is liye by ke sath aya. Lekin har jagah by lagana zaroori nahi. Agar karne wala maloom hi nahi, to by ghayab rahega. My bicycle was stolen. Kis ne churayi, maloom nahi, by a thief likhna fazool hoga. Agar karne wala aam sa hai, log ya koi bhi, tab bhi by hat jata hai. He was arrested last night. Police ka zikr karna zaroori nahi, sab ko pata hai ye kaam police hi karti hai. Qanoon ye hai: karne wala aham aur khaas ho to by se batao, warna jumla us ke baghair zyada saaf hota hai. Aur aik chhoti si khabardari, by ke baad me, him, her, us, them ate hain, I, he, she, we, they nahi. By him sahi hai, by he ghalat hai.

Passive kab behtar hota hai

Ab ye samajh lo ke passive istemal hi kyun karte hain. Teen jagah par passive active se behtar hota hai. Pehli, jab karne wala maloom na ho. The bank was robbed at night. Doosri, jab kaam karne wale se zyada aham ho, jaise sarkari elan me. The date of the test has been fixed. Yahan kis ne fix ki, is se kisi ko gharz nahi, date aham hai. Teesri, jab karne wale ka naam lena munasib na ho ya baat narmi se kehni ho. Mistakes were made. Daftari zuban is liye passive se bhari hoti hai. Akhbar bhi yehi karta hai. Five people were injured in the accident.

Board par qanoon Angrezi me

Board par passive ka qanoon kuch yun likha jata hai. In the passive voice, the object of the active sentence becomes the subject. The verb becomes be plus the third form: is written, are written, was written, were written. The doer, if mentioned, comes at the end with by: by him, by the teacher. The doer is dropped when it is unknown or unimportant. Aur aakhri aham line ye hai: the form of be depends on the new subject and the tense, and the main verb is always in the third form, never the second.

Misaalein board se

1. Urdu is spoken in Pakistan.
Bolne wale aam log hain, zikr fazool hai. Is liye passive aya aur by ghayab hai.

2. This school was built in 1950.
Bananay wale maloom nahi aur aham bhi nahi. Past hai, is liye was built.

3. The letter was written by her.
Karne wali khaas hai, is liye by her aya. By she kehna ghalat hota.

4. The thieves were arrested last night.
Giraftari police ne ki, sab ko maloom hai. By police likhne ki zaroorat nahi.

5. The test is held every year in June.
Har saal hone wala mamla hai. Present passive hai, is held.

6. My watch was stolen from the hostel.
Chor maloom nahi. By lagana mumkin hi nahi.

7. English is taught in almost every school.
Present me is aur teesri shakal taught. Taught verb ki teesri shakal hai.

8. The new bridge was opened by the chief minister.
Yahan karne wala aham aur khaas hai, is liye by ke sath aya.

9. All the tickets were sold in one day.
Tickets zyada hain, is liye were aya. Be ka faisla aage wale noun se hua.

10. The room is cleaned every morning.
Roz ka mamla hai. Present passive, is cleaned.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Sach ye hai ke sarkari aur akhbari angrezi passive ke baghair adhuri hai. PPSC ki advertisement kholo to likha hoga: Applications are invited from suitable candidates. The candidates will be informed by post. Asal imtehan me bhi notices, reports aur surkhiyan isi zuban me ati hain. Rozmarra me bhi hum kehte hain ke bijli band kar di gayi hai, result ka elan kar diya gaya hai. Urdu ke gaya, gayi wale jumle samajh lo to English passive parhna asaan ho jata hai.

Ab paper wale andaaz ke do jumle dekho.

1. The merit list was displayed on the notice board by the clerk.
2. Free books are distributed among poor students every year.

Dono jumlo me kaam aage hai aur karne wala ya peeche hai ya ghayab. Yehi passive ki pehchan hai.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab wo trick jo passive ka dhacha zindagi bhar yaad karwa degi. Aik stage ki tasveer zehen me banao. Active jumle me hero karne wala hota hai, wo stage ke aage khara hota hai. Passive me spot light ghum jati hai. Ab roshni us cheez par parti hai jis par kaam hua, wo aage aa jati hai. Karne wala stage ke peeche chala jata hai, aur kabhi kabhi to stage se hi ghayab ho jata hai. Aur stage par khari cheez hamesha do saathi le kar ati hai, be aur teesri shakal. Bas ye jumla yaad kar lo: kaam wala aage, be sath khara, teesri shakal aakhir me. Aur Urdu wali pehchan bhi bandh lo: jahan gaya ya gayi, wahan passive pakka.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ghalat: The letter was wrote by him.
Sahi: The letter was written by him.
Wajah: be ke baad hamesha teesri shakal ati hai. Wroted aur wrote dono ghalat hain, written sahi hai.

Ghalat: English is teach in our school.
Sahi: English is taught in our school.
Wajah: teach ki teesri shakal taught hai. Pehli shakal passive me nahi chalti.

Ghalat: The poem was written by he.
Sahi: The poem was written by him.
Wajah: by ke baad him ata hai, he nahi.

Ghalat: The rooms is cleaned daily.
Sahi: The rooms are cleaned daily.
Wajah: rooms zyada hain, is liye are ayega. Be ka faisla aage wale noun se hota hai.

Ghalat: A new hospital was build last year.
Sahi: A new hospital was built last year.
Wajah: build ki teesri shakal built hai.

Ghalat: He was arrested by the police last night, aur yahan by the police fazool hai.
Sahi: He was arrested last night.
Wajah: jab karne wala sab ko maloom ho aur aham na ho, to by wala hissa hata dena behtar hota hai. Paper me aise jumle ki pehchan poochhi jati hai.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Pehli tasveer stage wali ho. Aik stage par spot light aik meiz par rakhi file par pari ho aur file ke sath do saathi khade hon, aik ke hath me be ka board ho aur doosre ke hath me teesri shakal ka board. Stage ke peeche andhere me karne wala khara ho, aadha dikhai deta hua, aur us ke sath by likha ho jo kabhi aage ata hai aur kabhi ghayab ho jata hai. Neeche aik line ho: kaam wala aage, be sath, teesri shakal aakhir me.

Doosri tasveer teen kadmo wali sarhi ki ho. Pehli seedhi par aage aya hua noun ho, jaise letter. Doosri seedhi par be ki shaklein hon, is, are, was, were, jin me se sahi wali rang se chamak rahi ho. Teesri seedhi par teesri shaklon ki fehrist ho, written, built, spoken, stolen. Student sarhi dekh kar hi passive banana seekh jayega, kyunke passive asal me inhi teen kadmo ka naam hai.
