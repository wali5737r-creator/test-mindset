Lesson 25: Parts of Speech — Complete
Parts of Speech mukammal. Aath hisse, aik jumla, aur paper wali pehchan

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

Level 1 me tum ne har hisse ko alag alag pehchanna seekha tha. Ab Level 2 me paper tum se wohi pehchan aik hi jumle ke andar karwata hai. One paper me is par seedha sawal ata hai ke is jumle me ye lafz kis part of speech hai. Sawal dekhne me chhota lagta hai, lekin phansata bohat hai, kyunke paper wale jan bujh kar wo lafz dete hain jo do hisso ka kaam karta hai. Water kabhi noun hai aur kabhi verb. Fast kabhi adjective hai aur kabhi adverb. Jo student lafz ko nahi, us ke kaam ko pehchanta hai, wo ye number kabhi nahi khota. Ye lesson tumhe wahi nazar dega. Aakhir tak tum kisi bhi jumle ke har lafz ka kaam bata sako ge, aur yehi skill aage narration aur sentence wale lessons ki buniyad hai.

Aath hisse, aik hi khandan

Dekho, English ka har jumla alfaaz se banta hai, aur har lafz jumle me koi na koi kaam kar raha hota hai. Ussi kaam ke hisaab se alfaaz ko aath hisso me baanta gaya hai. In aath hisso ko parts of speech kehte hain. Ye aath naam ratne wali cheez nahi hain. Ye aik khandan ke aath afrad hain, aur har aik ka apna kaam hai. Kaam pehchan lo, naam khud yaad ho jayega.

Pehla hissa noun hai. Ye naam wala hai. Shakhs, jagah, cheez ya khayal ka naam. Ali, Lahore, kitaab, mohabbat. Ye hum Lesson 1 me tafseel se parh chuke hain. Doosra hissa pronoun hai. Ye noun ka naib hai. Noun ki jagah ata hai taake naam bar bar na dohrana pare. I, you, he, she, it, we, they, aur in ki dusri shaklein me, him, her, us, them. Ye hum Lesson 3 aur Lesson 14 me parh chuke hain. Teesra hissa verb hai. Ye jumle ka dil hai. Kaam ya halat batata hai. Run, write, eat, aur is, am, are, was, were bhi verb hi hain, kyunke ye halat batate hain. Chautha hissa adjective hai. Ye noun ya pronoun ki khoobi ya khami batata hai, us ki miqdar ya ginti batata hai. Brave, honest, five, some, this. Ye hum Lesson 8 me parhna shuru kiya tha aur Lesson 27 me is ki saari qismein ayengi. Panchwan hissa adverb hai. Ye verb ki, adjective ki ya kisi dusre adverb ki khoobi batata hai. Quickly, very, yesterday, here. Chhata hissa preposition hai. Ye rabta wala hai. Noun ya pronoun ka rishta jumle ke baqi hisse se jorta hai. In, on, at, under, between. Ye hum Lesson 5 me parh chuke hain. Saatwan hissa conjunction hai. Ye jorne wala hai. Do alfaaz, do hisse ya do jumle jorta hai. And, but, or, because, although. Aathwan aur aakhri hissa interjection hai. Ye achanak jazbaat wala hai. Wah, alas, hurrah, oh. Ye jumle ke grammar se alag khara hota hai, bas dil ki awaaz hai.

Ab aik aham baat zehen me bitha lo. Lafz ka hissa us ki surat se nahi, jumle me us ke kaam se tay hota hai. Yehi is poore lesson ki jaan hai, aur yehi paper ka sab se bara jaal hai.

Aik lafz, kai kirdaar

Ab wo baat jo paper me sab se zyada phansati hai. Aik hi lafz mukhtalif jumlo me mukhtalif hisse ban sakta hai. Water ko dekho. The water of this well is cold. Yahan water cheez ka naam hai, noun hai. Ab doosra jumla. Please water the plants in the evening. Yahan water aik kaam hai, pani dena. Ye verb hai. Lafz wahi, kaam badal gaya, hissa badal gaya.

Love bhi aisa hi hai. Love is a noble feeling. Yahan love khayal ka naam hai, noun hai. I love my country. Yahan love kaam hai, verb hai. Book bhi. This book is very interesting. Yahan book noun hai. He books his ticket in advance. Yahan books ka matlab hai ticket karwana, ye verb hai. Fast ka jaal aur bhi mashhoor hai. He is a fast bowler. Yahan fast bowler ki khoobi bata raha hai, adjective hai. He ran fast. Yahan fast daurne ke tareeqe ko bata raha hai, adverb hai. Aur yaad rakho, fast ka adverb fast hi hota hai. Fastly naam ki koi cheez English me nahi hai.

Since bhi do kaam karta hai. He has been ill since Monday. Yahan since aik waqt se rabta jor raha hai, preposition hai. Since he was ill, he stayed at home. Yahan since do jumlo ko jor raha hai aur wajah bata raha hai, conjunction hai. Round bhi kai kirdaar nibhata hai, lekin paper ke liye ye teen misaalein, water, fast aur since, sab se zyada aham hain. Usool aik hi hai. Lafz ko mat dekho, ye dekho ke wo jumle me kar kya raha hai.

Board par qanoon Angrezi me

Ustad board par aath hisso ki tareef yun likhta hai. A noun is the name of a person, place, thing or idea. A pronoun is a word used in place of a noun. A verb is a word that shows action or state. An adjective is a word that describes a noun or a pronoun. An adverb is a word that describes a verb, an adjective or another adverb. A preposition is a word that shows the relation of a noun or a pronoun with another word in the sentence. A conjunction is a word that joins words, phrases or sentences. An interjection is a word that shows a sudden feeling. Aur sab ke aakhir me ustad ye line moti kar ke likhta hai: the part of speech of a word depends on the work it does in the sentence, not on its form. Isi aik line par is lesson ke saare number hain.

Misaalein board se

Ab board par misaalein likhta hun. Har jumle me wo lafz pehchano jis ki baat ho rahi hai, aur sath wajah bhi parho.

1. Honesty is the best policy.
Honesty khayal ka naam hai. Ye noun hai, aur aage is ki qismein Lesson 26 me ayengi.

2. They are waiting for us at the gate.
Us pronoun hai, jo noun ki jagah aya hai. Aur for rabta jor raha hai, preposition hai.

3. The brave soldier fought bravely.
Brave soldier ki khoobi hai, adjective hai. Bravely ladne ke tareeqe ko bata raha hai, adverb hai. Aik hi khandan ke do alfaaz, do alag kaam.

4. He drinks water in the morning, and his sister waters the plants.
Pehla water noun hai, doosra waters verb hai. Aur and do jumlo ko jor raha hai, conjunction hai.

5. She has not met him since Friday.
Since yahan waqt ke sath rabta jor raha hai. Ye preposition hai.

6. Since the road was closed, we took another way.
Yahan since wajah bata kar do jumlo ko jor raha hai. Ye conjunction hai.

7. Alas! Our team has lost the final match.
Alas achanak afsos ka izhar hai. Ye interjection hai, aur is ka jumle ke grammar se koi lena dena nahi.

8. The book on the table belongs to me.
On table aur book ka rishta jor raha hai. Ye preposition hai.

9. He is a fast runner, and he also runs fast.
Pehla fast adjective hai, runner ki khoobi. Doosra fast adverb hai, runs ke tareeqe ki khoobi.

10. Love is blind, yet lovers love truly.
Pehla love noun hai. Doosra love verb hai. Yet jorne wala hai, conjunction hai, aur truly adverb hai. Aik jumle me chaar hisse chhupe hain.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Rozmarra ki English me tum ye hisse bolte hue istemal to karte ho, lekin pehchanna paper ke kaam ata hai. Paper me is ki teen shaklein ati hain. Kabhi aik jumla de kar kisi lafz ka hissa poochha jata hai. Kabhi chaar jumle de kar poochha jata hai ke kis jumle me ye lafz verb hai. Aur kabhi aik hi lafz ke chaar jumle de kar har dafa ka hissa alag alag pehchanna hota hai. Teeno shaklo ka jawab aik hi hai: kaam dekho, surat nahi.

Ab paper wale andaaz ke do jumle dekho, in me chhupe hisse khud pehchano.

1. The old man walked slowly towards the mosque, and his grandson walked with him.
2. What a surprise! She has completed the whole work in one day.

Pehle jumle me old adjective hai, slowly adverb hai, towards preposition hai aur with bhi preposition hai. Doosre jumle me what a surprise interjection wala izhar hai, aur whole adjective hai jo work ki miqdar bata raha hai.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab wo trick jo aath hisso ko hamesha ke liye zehen me band kar degi. Aik hi jumla yaad kar lo, aath ke aath hisse us ke andar chhupe hain. Jumla ye hai. Wow! The brave Ali and Ahmed ran very quickly to the ground, and they cheered loudly.

Ab is ke tukre karo. Wow jazbaat hai, interjection. Brave khoobi hai, adjective. Ali, Ahmed aur ground naam hain, noun. Ran aur cheered kaam hain, verb. Very aur quickly aur loudly kaam ki khoobi hain, adverb. To rabta hai, preposition. And jorne wala hai, conjunction. They naam ki jagah aya hai, pronoun. Bas. Jab bhi koi hissa bhool jaye, ye jumla zehen me chalao aur hisse gin lo. Aath ke aath tumhare samne aa jayenge.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ab wo ghaltiyan dekho jo in hisso ki pehchan na hone ki wajah se hoti hain. Main board par ghalat aur sahi sath sath likh raha hun.

Ghalat: He ran fastly to catch the bus.
Sahi: He ran fast to catch the bus.
Wajah: fast ka adverb fast hi hota hai. Fastly English me hota hi nahi.

Ghalat: She is a very happily girl.
Sahi: She is a very happy girl.
Wajah: girl noun hai, aur noun ki khoobi adjective batata hai, adverb nahi. Happily adverb hai, wo yahan nahi chalega.

Ghalat: He works hardly day and night.
Sahi: He works hard day and night.
Wajah: hard ka matlab hai mehnat se, aur hardly ka matlab hai mushkil se. Dono ke matlab zameen asman ka farq hai. Ye paper ka pasandeeda jaal hai.

Ghalat: This secret is between you and I.
Sahi: This secret is between you and me.
Wajah: between preposition hai, aur preposition ke baad pronoun ki objective shakal ati hai, I nahi, me. Ye hum Lesson 14 me parh chuke hain.

Ghalat: I am agree with you.
Sahi: I agree with you.
Wajah: agree verb hai, adjective nahi. Is ke sath am lagana Urdu ke asar ki ghalti hai.

Ghalat: She is much happy today.
Sahi: She is very happy today.
Wajah: adjective ki shiddat batane ke liye very ata hai. Much ka istemal is jagah nahi hota.

Ghalat: He plays cricket good.
Sahi: He plays cricket well.
Wajah: plays verb hai, aur verb ki khoobi adverb batata hai. Good adjective hai, well adverb hai.

Jawab aur un ki wajah

1. Jawab B hai, adjective. Honest officer ki khoobi bata raha hai, aur khoobi batane wala hissa adjective hota hai.
2. Jawab C hai, conjunction. But do jumlo ko jor raha hai. Jorne wala kaam conjunction ka hai.
3. Jawab D hai, verb. Yahan water pani dene ka kaam hai. Kaam wala lafz verb hota hai, chahe wo aam tor par noun hi kyun na ho.
4. Jawab B hai, adverb. Fast yahan ran ki khoobi bata raha hai, ke wo kese daura. Verb ki khoobi adverb hoti hai.
5. Jawab C hai, interjection. Alas achanak afsos ka izhar hai. Yehi interjection ki pehchan hai.
6. Jawab B hai, conjunction. Because wajah bata kar do jumlo ko jor raha hai. Monday wale since se isay mat milana, wahan wo preposition tha.
7. Jawab D hai, adjective. Beloved country ki khoobi hai.
8. Jawab B hai, adverb. Very khud softly ki shiddat bata raha hai. Jo lafz adverb ki khoobi bataye, wo bhi adverb hota hai.
9. Jawab C hai, preposition. In garden aur playing ka rishta jor raha hai.
10. Jawab B hai, verb. Books yahan ticket karwane ka kaam hai. Lafz book hai, lekin kaam verb ka hai. Yehi is lesson ka asal usool hai.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye pehli tasveer usi trick wale jumle ki ho. Aik bara jumla board par likha ho, Wow! The brave Ali and Ahmed ran very quickly to the ground, and they cheered loudly. Har lafz ke neeche us ke hisse ka naam apne alag rang me likha ho. Noun neela, verb laal, adjective hara, adverb narangi. Student aik nazar me dekhe ke aik hi jumle me aath hisse kese baithe hain.

Doosri tasveer water wale jaal ki ho. Aik taraf glass me pani ki tasveer ho aur neeche likha ho water, noun. Doosri taraf aik bacha podon ko pani de raha ho aur neeche likha ho water, verb. Beech me bara sa sawal ho ke lafz wahi hai, to hissa kese badal gaya. Is tasveer ka maqsad ye zehen me bithana hai ke hissa surat se nahi, kaam se banta hai.
