Lesson 26: Noun and Its Types
Noun aur us ki qismein. Khaas, aam, jamaat, jazbaat aur jins

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

Lesson 1 me tum ne seekha tha ke noun har naam wali cheez ko kehte hain. Ab paper us se aik kadam aage jata hai. Paper poochhta hai ke ye noun kis qism ka hai. Proper hai ya common, collective hai ya abstract. Aur is se bhi aage, collective noun ke sath verb singular ayega ya plural, ye sawal one paper me bar bar ata hai. Committee has ya committee have, jury its ya jury their, yehi wo bareek jagah hai jahan tayyari wala student number leta hai aur rata lagane wala reh jata hai. Is lesson me hum paanch qismein aisi pakki karenge ke pehchan bhi ho jaye aur verb wala masla bhi hamesha ke liye hal ho jaye.

Paanch qismein, paanch pehchan

Pehli qism proper noun hai. Ye kisi khaas shakhs, khaas jagah ya khaas cheez ka apna naam hota hai. Ali, Lahore, Pakistan, Quaid-e-Azam, the Indus. Is ki pehchan asan hai. Ye hamesha bare harf, capital letter, se shuru hota hai, aur ye sirf aik ka hai, poori qism ka nahi. Boy aam hai, lekin Ali khaas hai. City aam hai, lekin Lahore khaas hai.

Doosri qism common noun hai. Ye aik poori qism ka aam naam hota hai. Boy, city, book, teacher, river. Ye har us fard par lagta hai jo us qism ka ho. Koi bhi larka boy hai, koi bhi sheher city hai. Proper aur common ka farq sirf itna hai ke aik naam aik ka hai aur doosra naam sab ka hai.

Teesri qism collective noun hai. Ye meri pasandeeda qism hai kyunke yehi paper ka jaal hai. Ye aik lafz hota hai, lekin us ke andar poora group chhupa hota hai. Team gyarah khilariyon ka group hai, lekin lafz aik hai. Army, committee, jury, crowd, family, class, government, audience, flock, bunch, public. Aik lafz, poori jamaat. Ye pehchan yaad rakho, kyunke verb wala qanoon isi par chalta hai.

Chauthi qism abstract noun hai. Ye un cheezon ke naam hain jo mehsoos hoti hain, lekin ankh se dikhti nahi aur hath se chhui nahi jatin. Honesty, bravery, happiness, childhood, poverty, beauty, knowledge, freedom. Tum mohabbat ko dekh nahi sakte, bas mehsoos kar sakte ho. Ye hum Lesson 1 me khayal wale naam ke tor par parh chuke hain, abstract usi ka proper naam hai.

Panchvi qism material noun hai. Ye us cheez ka naam hai jis se dusri cheezein banti hain. Gold, silver, iron, wood, cotton, milk, water, wheat. Anguthi cheez hai, lekin gold wo jins hai jis se anguthi bani. Is qism ka apna qanoon hai. Ye aam tor par na gini jati hai, na is par a ya an ata hai, aur na hi is ki plural banti hai. Golds kehna ghalat hai.

Collective noun aur verb ka masla

Ab is lesson ka sab se aham hissa. Collective noun ke sath verb kab singular aur kab plural? Qanoon ye hai. Jab jamaat aik jaan ho kar, aik unit ban kar kaam kare, to verb singular ata hai aur pronoun its ata hai. Jab jamaat ke log alag alag soch rahe hon ya alag alag kaam kar rahe hon, to verb plural ata hai aur pronoun their ata hai.

Misaal se samjho. The committee has submitted its report. Yahan committee ne aik ho kar report di hai. Aik unit hai, is liye has aur its. Ab doosri surat. The committee are divided in their opinion. Yahan members ki raye alag alag hai. Jamaat aik nahi rahi, log alag ho gaye, is liye are aur their. Jury par bhi yehi qanoon hai. The jury has given its verdict. Faisla aik hai, jamaat aik hai. Lekin the jury were divided in their opinion me har member apni jagah khara hai.

Paper is me is tarah phansata hai ke jumle ke do hisse mila deta hai. The committee has given their report. Ye ghalat hai, kyunke has singular hai aur their plural. Ya dono singular rakho, has aur its, ya dono plural, have aur their. Aadha idhar aadha udhar, yehi ghalti hai. Aur public par bhi nazar rakho. Public aam tor par logo ke majmue ke tor par plural verb leta hai, lekin jab wo aik idare ki tarah baat kare to singular bhi ata hai. Paper me jo hissa jumle me pehle se diya ho, verb usi ke hisaab se chunna hai.

Abstract aur material ke chhote qanoon

Abstract noun ke sath sab se aam ghalti a ya an lagana hai. A honesty, an advice jaisi ghaltiyan Urdu soch se ati hain. Yaad rakho, jo cheez gini nahi ja sakti, us ke sath a ya an nahi ata. Advice par ye ghalti hum Lesson 13 me bhi dekh chuke hain, kyunke advice uncountable bhi hai aur abstract bhi. Haan, aik bareek baat aur hai. Kuch abstract nouns ke sath jab koi khoobi jude to article kabhi kabhi aa sakta hai, lekin paper ki satah par tum bas ye yaad rakho ke akela abstract noun article nahi leta.

Material noun ka qanoon is se bhi seedha hai. Ye wazan aur miqdar se naapa jata hai, gina nahi jata. Two golds ghalat hai, two grams of gold sahi hai. Aur jab kisi khaas cheez ki taraf ishara ho to the aa sakta hai. The gold of this ring is pure. Yahan gold aam jins nahi, is anguthi ka gold hai, is liye the aya.

Board par qanoon Angrezi me

Ustad board par paanch qismo ki tareef yun likhta hai. A proper noun is the name of a particular person, place or thing, and it begins with a capital letter. A common noun is the name of a whole class or kind. A collective noun is the name of a group of persons or things taken as one whole. An abstract noun is the name of a quality, state or idea that can only be felt, not seen or touched. A material noun is the name of the substance from which things are made. Aur collective ke liye ustad alag se ye line likhta hai: a collective noun takes a singular verb when the group acts as one unit, and a plural verb when the members act separately. Ye line is lesson ki sab se qeemti line hai.

Misaalein board se

Ab board par misaalein likhta hun. Har jumle me noun ki qism pehchano.

1. Quaid-e-Azam was a great leader of the Muslims.
Quaid-e-Azam proper noun hai, khaas shakhs ka naam. Leader common noun hai.

2. The army marched into the city at night.
Army collective noun hai. Poori fouj aik lafz me aa gayi.

3. The committee has submitted its report to the minister.
Committee collective hai, aur yahan wo aik unit hai, is liye has aur its aya.

4. The committee are divided in their opinion about the new rules.
Wahi committee, lekin yahan members alag alag hain, is liye are aur their aya. Farq lafz ka nahi, matlab ka hai.

5. Honesty of the officer impressed everyone in the office.
Honesty abstract noun hai. Dikhti nahi, mehsoos hoti hai.

6. This ring is made of gold.
Gold material noun hai. Ye wo jins hai jis se anguthi bani hai.

7. A flock of sheep was grazing in the field.
Flock collective noun hai. Bhedon ka group aik lafz me.

8. Childhood is the happiest period of life.
Childhood abstract noun hai. Ye umar ki aik halat ka naam hai, cheez ka nahi.

9. Cotton is grown in Sindh and Punjab.
Cotton material noun hai. Sindh aur Punjab proper nouns hain.

10. She teaches in a school in Lahore.
School common noun hai aur Lahore proper noun hai. Aik hi jumle me aam aur khaas sath sath.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Paper me is lesson ke sawal teen rang ke hote hain. Pehla, noun ki qism pehchano. Doosra, collective noun ke sath sahi verb chuno. Teesra, jumle ki ghalti theek karo, jahan aksar a honesty ya golds jaisi ghalti chhupi hoti hai. Teeno ka hal tumhare pas ab hai. Qism pehchanne ke liye sawal poochho: kya ye khaas naam hai, aam naam hai, group hai, ehsaas hai ya jins hai. Verb ke liye sawal poochho: jamaat aik ho kar kaam kar rahi hai ya log alag alag hain.

Ab paper wale andaaz ke do jumle dekho.

1. The jury has announced its decision after a long discussion.
2. Poverty is a curse, but honesty and hard work can change a man's life.

Pehle jumle me jury collective hai aur unit ke tor par has aur its ke sath aya hai. Doosre jumle me poverty, honesty aur work abstract nouns hain, aur life bhi yahan aik aam khayal ke tor par aya hai.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab wo trick jo paanch qismo ko hamesha yaad rakhegi. Noun ke paanch bhai samajh lo. Pehla Khaas bhai. Ye hamesha topi pehen kar ata hai, matlab capital letter se. Ye proper noun hai. Doosra Aam bhai. Ye har gali me milta hai, sab par lagta hai. Ye common noun hai. Teesra Jamaat bhai. Ye akela khara dikhta hai, lekin is ke peeche poori team khari hoti hai. Ye collective noun hai. Chautha Jazbaat bhai. Ye nazar nahi ata, bas dil me mehsoos hota hai. Ye abstract noun hai. Panchwan Jins bhai. Ye wo hai jis se baqi cheezein banti hain, sona, loha, lakri. Ye material noun hai. Khaas, Aam, Jamaat, Jazbaat, Jins. Paanch bhai zehen me bitha lo, qismein kabhi nahi bhulo ge. Aur collective wale bhai ke liye aik chhoti si line aur yaad rakho: jamaat aik ho to verb aik, log alag hon to verb bhi alag.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ab wo ghaltiyan dekho jo is lesson se seedhi judi hain.

Ghalat: He showed a great bravery in the battle.
Sahi: He showed great bravery in the battle.
Wajah: bravery abstract noun hai. Akela abstract noun a ya an nahi leta.

Ghalat: lahore is the capital of Punjab.
Sahi: Lahore is the capital of Punjab.
Wajah: proper noun hamesha capital letter se shuru hota hai, jumle ke beech me ho ya shuru me.

Ghalat: The jury has given their verdict.
Sahi: The jury has given its verdict.
Wajah: has singular hai to pronoun bhi singular hoga, its. Has ke sath their milana aadhi singular aadhi plural wali ghalti hai.

Ghalat: She bought two golds from the jeweller.
Sahi: She bought gold from the jeweller.
Wajah: gold material noun hai. Ye naapa jata hai, gina nahi jata, is liye is ki plural nahi banti.

Ghalat: Poverty are a curse for any nation.
Sahi: Poverty is a curse for any nation.
Wajah: poverty abstract noun hai aur hamesha singular mana jata hai, is liye is ayega.

Ghalat: The committee meet every Monday in this hall.
Sahi: The committee meets every Monday in this hall.
Wajah: yahan committee aik unit ke tor par baithi hai jo baqaida meeting karti hai, is liye singular verb meets ayega.

Ghalat: He has a knowledge of many languages.
Sahi: He has knowledge of many languages.
Wajah: knowledge abstract aur uncountable hai. A knowledge paper me ghalat mana jata hai.

Jawab aur un ki wajah

1. Jawab C hai, abstract noun. Bravery mehsoos hoti hai, dikhti nahi. Jazbaat bhai yaad hai na?
2. Jawab B hai, collective noun. Fleet jahazon ke group ka naam hai, aik lafz me poori jamaat.
3. Jawab A hai, material noun. Wood wo jins hai jis se kursi bani hai.
4. Jawab C hai. Committee yahan aik unit hai, is liye has aur its dono singular. A me have ke sath its aur B me has ke sath their, dono aadhe idhar aadhe udhar hain.
5. Jawab D hai, proper noun. Imran khaas shakhs ka naam hai aur capital se shuru hua hai.
6. Jawab A hai, collective noun. Bunch chabiyon ke guchhe ko kehte hain, group aik lafz me.
7. Jawab D hai, abstract noun. Childhood aik halat ka naam hai, koi cheez nahi.
8. Jawab C hai. Advice gina nahi jata. A piece of advice hi sahi tareeqa hai. Ye hum Lesson 13 me bhi parh chuke hain.
9. Jawab C hai, silver. Team collective hai, beauty abstract hai aur Lahore proper hai.
10. Jawab B hai, collective noun. Aur ghaur karo, yahan jury ke sath were aur their aya hai, kyunke members apni apni raye par alag the. Qism wahi hai, verb matlab ke hisaab se badla hai.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye pehli tasveer paanch bhaiyon ki ho. Paanch kirdaar aik sath khare hon. Khaas bhai topi pehne hue, us ke neeche Ali aur Lahore likha ho. Aam bhai aam kapron me, neeche boy aur city. Jamaat bhai ke peeche poori team ki halki si jhalak ho, neeche team aur army. Jazbaat bhai dil par hath rakhe hue, neeche honesty aur happiness. Jins bhai ke hath me sone ka tukra aur lakri ho, neeche gold aur wood. Har bhai ka apna rang ho taake student aik nazar me paanch qismein alag kar le.

Doosri tasveer committee wali ho, do hisso me. Aik taraf aik gol meiz ke gird saare log aik taraf dekh rahe hon aur neeche likha ho, aik unit, has aur its. Doosri taraf wahi log alag alag hath utha kar behes kar rahe hon aur neeche likha ho, log alag, are aur their. Ye tasveer verb wale poore masle ko aik nazar me hal kar degi.
