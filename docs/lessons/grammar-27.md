Lesson 27: Adjective and Adverb — Types
Adjective aur Adverb ki qismein. Khoobi kis ki hai, yehi asal sawal hai

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

Lesson 8 me tum ne adjective aur adverb ki buniyad parhi thi. Adjective cheez ki khoobi batata hai aur adverb kaam ki khoobi. Level 2 me paper is se aage jata hai. Wo poochhta hai ke ye adjective kis qism ka hai, ye adverb kis qism ka hai. Aur us se bhi zyada, wo jumle ki ghalti me adjective ki jagah adverb aur adverb ki jagah adjective rakh kar tumhe phansata hai. He speaks English fluent. Ye jumla sunne me theek lagta hai, lekin paper me yehi ghalat hai. Is lesson me hum dono ki qismein bhi pakki karenge aur un ki jagah ke qanoon bhi, taake pehchan bhi ho aur istemal bhi.

Adjective ki saat qismein

Pehle adjective ki qismein samjho. Har qism noun ke bare me aik alag sawal ka jawab deti hai.

Pehli qism adjective of quality hai. Ye batata hai ke cheez kaisi hai. Honest, brave, beautiful, intelligent, old. Sawal poochho, kaisa ya kaisi, jawab mil jaye to quality hai. Doosri qism adjective of quantity hai. Ye uncountable cheezon ki miqdar batata hai. Some, much, little, enough, whole. Sawal hai, kitna, lekin us cheez ke liye jo gini nahi jati, jaise much water, little hope. Teesri qism adjective of number hai. Ye ginti batata hai. Five, many, first, second, all. Five books me ginti saaf hai, aur first me tartib hai, dono number ke hisse hain. Chauthi qism demonstrative adjective hai. Ye ishara karke batata hai. This, that, these, those. This book me ishaara hai ke ye wali kitaab. Panchvi qism possessive adjective hai. Ye milkiyat batata hai. My, your, his, her, its, our, their. Ye hum Lesson 7 me parh chuke hain. Yaad rakho, ye noun se pehle ate hain aur in ke baad noun lazmi ata hai. My book sahi hai, my akele nahi khara hota. Chhati qism interrogative adjective hai. Ye sawal poochhne ke liye noun se pehle ata hai. Which, what, whose. Which pen do you want? Yahan which pen ke bare me sawal kar raha hai. Saatvi qism proper adjective hai. Ye proper noun se banta hai. Pakistani, English, Asian. Pakistani food, English language. Ye bhi apne walid ki tarah capital letter se shuru hota hai.

Adverb ki paanch qismein

Ab adverb ki qismein. Adverb verb ko, adjective ko ya apne jaisi doosri adverb ko rang deta hai, aur har rang ka apna sawal hai.

Pehli qism adverb of manner hai. Ye batata hai kaam kese hua. Quickly, slowly, carefully, bravely. Aksar ye ly par khatam hote hain. Doosri qism adverb of time hai. Kab hua. Now, yesterday, today, tomorrow, soon. Teesri qism adverb of place hai. Kahan hua. Here, there, outside, everywhere. Chauthi qism adverb of frequency hai. Kitni dafa hua. Always, usually, often, sometimes, never. In ki jagah ka khaas qanoon hum Lesson 21 me parh chuke hain, ke ye aam verb se pehle ate hain aur be verb ke baad ate hain. He always comes late, lekin he is always late. Panchvi qism adverb of degree hai. Kitna, kis had tak. Very, too, quite, almost, enough. Very happy me very happy ki shiddat bata raha hai. Ye khud koi kaam nahi batata, bas rang gehra ya halka karta hai.

Jagah ke qanoon, jahan paper phansata hai

Qismein ratna kaafi nahi. Paper jagah par phansata hai, is liye ye qanoon pakke kar lo.

Adjective noun se pehle ata hai. A brave soldier. Aur linking verb ke baad bhi adjective hi ata hai. She is happy. The soup tastes good. Be, seem, look, feel, taste, smell, ye linking verbs hain. In ke baad cheez ki halat bayan hoti hai, is liye adjective ata hai, adverb nahi. The flower smells sweet sahi hai, sweetly ghalat hai.

Adverb of manner aam tor par verb ke baad ata hai, aur agar object ho to object ke baad. He speaks English fluently. Fluently ko English se pehle rakhna, he speaks fluently English, English me ghalat hai. Urdu me hum kehte hain wo rawani se English bolta hai, tareeqa pehle ata hai. English me tareeqa baad me ata hai. Yehi Urdu soch ki sab se aam ghalti hai.

Adverb of degree hamesha us lafz se pehle ata hai jis ki shiddat batata hai. Very honest, too late, quite sure. Lekin aik lafz is qanoon ka badshah badmash hai, enough. Ye adjective ke baad ata hai. Strong enough, old enough. Enough strong kehna ghalat hai.

Aur do jaal khaas yaad rakho. Hard aur hardly wala, jo hum Lesson 25 me bhi dekh chuke hain. Hard adverb hai, matlab mehnat se. Hardly ka matlab hai mushkil se, taqreeban nahi. Aur fast wala. Fast adjective bhi hai aur adverb bhi, aik hi surat me. Fastly koi lafz nahi.

Board par qanoon Angrezi me

Ustad board par yun likhta hai. An adjective qualifies a noun or a pronoun. Its kinds are quality, quantity, number, demonstrative, possessive, interrogative and proper. An adverb modifies a verb, an adjective or another adverb. Its kinds are manner, time, place, frequency and degree. Aur jagah ke liye ustad ye teen lines likhta hai: an adjective comes before a noun or after a linking verb; an adverb of manner comes after the verb or after its object; an adverb of degree comes before the word it modifies, except enough, which comes after an adjective. Ye aakhri teen lines is lesson ke aadhe number hain.

Misaalein board se

Ab board par misaalein likhta hun. Har jumle me qism pehchano.

1. She is an intelligent girl.
Intelligent girl ki khoobi hai. Ye adjective of quality hai.

2. He has little knowledge of this subject.
Little knowledge ki miqdar bata raha hai, aur knowledge gina nahi jata. Ye adjective of quantity hai.

3. The first boy in the row won the prize.
First tartib bata raha hai. Ye adjective of number hai.

4. This book is mine, and that one is yours.
This aur that ishaara kar rahe hain. Ye demonstrative adjectives hain.

5. He loves Pakistani food.
Pakistani proper noun Pakistan se bana hai. Ye proper adjective hai, is liye capital se likha gaya.

6. The child solved the sum quickly.
Quickly batata hai hal kese hua. Ye adverb of manner hai.

7. We shall leave for Karachi tomorrow.
Tomorrow waqt batata hai. Ye adverb of time hai.

8. The children are playing outside.
Outside jagah batata hai. Ye adverb of place hai.

9. He is always ready to help others.
Always bar bar hone ko batata hai. Ye adverb of frequency hai, aur dekho, be verb is ke baad aya hai, is liye always us ke baad betha hai.

10. The water of this well is very cold.
Very cold ki shiddat bata raha hai. Ye adverb of degree hai, aur adjective cold se pehle aya hai.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Paper me is lesson ke sawal do rang ke ate hain. Pehla rang pehchan ka hai. Jumla de kar poochha jata hai ke ye adjective ya adverb kis qism ka hai. Is ka hal sawal poochhna hai. Noun se poochho kaisa, kitna, konsa, kis ka. Verb se poochho kese, kab, kahan, kitni dafa, kis had tak. Jo sawal jawab de, wahi qism hai. Doosra rang ghalti theek karne ka hai, jahan jagah ya surat badli hoti hai. Fluently ki jagah fluent, good ki jagah well. Wahan qanoon yaad karo, linking verb ke baad adjective, aam verb ke baad adverb.

Ab paper wale andaaz ke do jumle dekho.

1. She sang the national anthem very beautifully before the chief guest.
2. He is quite confident that he will top the list this year.

Pehle jumle me beautifully adverb of manner hai aur very us ki shiddat batane wala adverb of degree hai. Doosre jumle me quite adverb of degree hai aur this demonstrative adjective hai.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab wo trick jo dono ki har qism ko aik sawal me band kar deti hai. Isay hum Sawal wala qanoon kehte hain. Jab bhi shak ho, jumle se sawal poochho. Agar jawab noun ke bare me hai, to adjective hai. Kaisa mila to quality, kitna mila to quantity, kitne mile to number, konsa mila to demonstrative, kis ka mila to possessive, sawal wala mila to interrogative, aur khaas naam se bana mila to proper. Agar jawab verb ke bare me hai, to adverb hai. Kese mila to manner, kab mila to time, kahan mila to place, kitni dafa mila to frequency, kis had tak mila to degree. Bas, sawal poochho, qism khud bol paregi. Rata khatam, pehchan pakki.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ab wo ghaltiyan dekho jo is lesson me bar bar hoti hain.

Ghalat: He speaks English fluent.
Sahi: He speaks English fluently.
Wajah: speaks verb hai, aur verb ki khoobi adverb batata hai. Fluent adjective hai, wo yahan nahi chalega.

Ghalat: She is a beautifully singer.
Sahi: She is a beautiful singer.
Wajah: singer noun hai. Noun ki khoobi adjective se hogi, beautifully adverb se nahi.

Ghalat: The soup tastes well.
Sahi: The soup tastes good.
Wajah: taste yahan linking verb hai. Linking verb ke baad halat adjective se bayan hoti hai. Well tab ata hai jab sehat ki baat ho, he is well.

Ghalat: He is working hardly these days.
Sahi: He is working hard these days.
Wajah: hardly ka matlab hai mushkil se. Mehnat ke liye hard ata hai.

Ghalat: The horse ran very fastly.
Sahi: The horse ran very fast.
Wajah: fast ka adverb fast hi hai. Fastly koi lafz nahi.

Ghalat: He is enough strong to lift this box.
Sahi: He is strong enough to lift this box.
Wajah: enough tamam degree walo se ulta hai. Ye adjective ke baad ata hai, pehle nahi.

Ghalat: She sings sweet.
Sahi: She sings sweetly.
Wajah: sings verb hai, is liye us ke tareeqe ke liye adverb sweetly chahiye.

Jawab aur un ki wajah

1. Jawab B hai, interrogative adjective. Which sawal poochh raha hai aur road noun se pehle aya hai.
2. Jawab A hai, possessive adjective. My milkiyat batata hai aur brother noun se pehle hai.
3. Jawab C hai, adverb of time. Today kab ka jawab hai. Sawal wala qanoon yahan kaam aya.
4. Jawab A hai, adverb of place. Outside kahan ka jawab hai.
5. Jawab B hai. Writes verb hai, us ki khoobi neatly se hogi. Neat adjective hai, wo verb ki khoobi nahi bata sakta.
6. Jawab D hai, adjective of quantity. Enough money ki miqdar bata raha hai, aur money uncountable hai.
7. Jawab A hai, adverb of degree. Quite happy ki shiddat bata raha hai. Ye adjective se pehle betha degree wala hai.
8. Jawab B hai. Smell yahan linking verb hai, is liye us ke baad adjective sweet ayega, sweetly nahi.
9. Jawab C hai, adjective of quality. Wooden table ki surat aur jins bata raha hai, kaisa table hai, is liye quality hai.
10. Jawab B hai, adverb of frequency. Often kitni dafa ka jawab hai.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye pehli tasveer sawal wale qanoon ki ho. Aik bare board par do darwaze bane hon. Aik darwaze par likha ho noun aur us ke neeche sawal hon, kaisa, kitna, konsa, kis ka. Doosre darwaze par likha ho verb aur us ke neeche sawal hon, kese, kab, kahan, kitni dafa. Student dekhe ke sawal hi qism tak le jata hai.

Doosri tasveer jagah wale qanoon ki ho. Aik jumla teen rang ke tukro me likha ho, He speaks English fluently. English par neela rang ho ke ye object hai, aur fluently par narangi rang ho ke ye us ke bilkul baad betha hai. Neeche aik ghalat jumla bhi ho jis me fluently English se pehle rakha ho aur us par kaata laga ho. Ye tasveer Urdu soch wali sab se aam ghalti ko hamesha ke liye theek kar degi.
