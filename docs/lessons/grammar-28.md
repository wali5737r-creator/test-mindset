Lesson 28: Sentences — Structure and Kinds
Jumle ki saakht aur qismein. Clause gino, jumla pehchan lo

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

Ab tak tum alfaaz aur un ke kaam parh rahe the. Ab paper tum se poora jumla pehchanne ko kehta hai. Ye jumla simple hai ya compound, complex hai ya sirf aik tukra jo jumla hi nahi. One paper me sentence ki saakht pehchanne ka sawal taqreeban har dafa ata hai, aur narration seekhne se pehle ye pehchan lazmi hai, kyunke narration me jumlo ko jorna aur torna yehi kaam hai. Achi baat ye hai ke is ki pehchan ka aik hi usool hai. Clause gino. Jo student clause ginna seekh jaye, us ke liye ye sawal sab se asaan number ban jata hai.

Pehle clause ko samajh lo

Clause jumle ka wo hissa hota hai jis me aik subject aur aik verb ho, aur wo koi matlab de raha ho. The sun rises. Ye aik clause hai, subject sun hai, verb rises hai. Clause ki do qismein hain, aur inhi do par poori saakht khari hai.

Pehli qism independent clause hai. Ye apne pao par khara ho sakta hai. Akela bhi poora jumla ban sakta hai. He was honest. Khatam, baat mukammal hai. Doosri qism dependent clause hai. Is me subject aur verb dono hote hain, lekin ye akela adhoora rehta hai. Because he was honest. Sunne wala intezaar karta hai ke aage kya. Ye kisi aur clause ka ghulam hota hai, apne malik ke baghair nahi chal sakta. Because, although, when, if, that, who, which, ye alfaaz aam tor par dependent clause ki pehchan hain. Jahan ye nazar aye, samajh lo ke aik ghulam clause aa raha hai.

Aik aur farq zehen me rakho. Phrase clause nahi hota. Phrase me verb nahi hota. In the morning, a phrase hai. The sun rises in the morning me clause aik hi hai, kyunke verb aik hi hai. Clause ginne ka matlab hai verb ginna, lekin har verb ke sath us ka subject bhi dekho.

Ab teen saakhtain

Pehli saakht simple sentence hai. Is me sirf aik independent clause hota hai. Aik subject, aik verb, baat khatam. The boy is playing cricket. Lekin khabardar, aik jaal hai. Subject do hon ya verb do hon, tab bhi jumla simple reh sakta hai. Ali and Ahmed play cricket in the evening. Subject do hain, lekin clause aik hai, kyunke jora aik hi kaam kar raha hai. He opened the door and entered the room. Verb do hain, lekin subject aik hi hai aur koi doosra clause nahi bana. Ye bhi simple hai. Clause gino, alfaaz nahi.

Doosri saakht compound sentence hai. Is me do ya zyada independent clauses hote hain, aur wo barabar ke hote hain. Koi kisi ka ghulam nahi. Ye coordinating conjunction se jude hote hain. For, and, nor, but, or, yet, so. In saat ko yaad rakhne ka mashhoor tareeqa in ke pehle haroof hain, F A N B O Y S. He was poor, but he was honest. Do clause, dono apne pao par khare, but ne hath milaya. Kabhi kabhi ye semicolon se bhi jude hote hain, lekin paper me zyada tar FANBOYS wala rang ata hai.

Teesri saakht complex sentence hai. Is me aik independent clause hota hai aur us ke sath aik ya zyada dependent clauses. Although he was poor, he was honest. Dekho, matlab wahi hai jo compound wale jumle ka tha, lekin saakht badal gayi. Although wale hisse ne aik clause ko ghulam bana diya. Independent clause he was honest hai, dependent although he was poor hai. Paper yehi farq par sab se zyada phansata hai. But aye to compound, although aye to complex, chahe matlab aik ho.

Ab jumle ki paanch qismein matlab ke hisaab se

Saakht ke ilawa jumle ko us ke matlab aur maqsad se bhi baanta jata hai. Ye paanch qismein hain.

Pehli assertive sentence hai, jisay declarative bhi kehte hain. Ye koi baat bayan karti hai. He is coming today. Aur is ki negative surat bhi assertive hi hoti hai. He is not coming today. Bayan hai, chahe han me ho ya na me. Doosri interrogative sentence hai. Ye sawal poochhti hai aur aakhir me sawal ka nishan ata hai. Ye hum Lesson 10 me tafseel se parh chuke hain. Teesri imperative sentence hai. Ye hukam, darkhwast ya mashwara deti hai. Ye hum Lesson 12 me parh chuke hain. Close the door. Is ka subject you chhupa hota hai. Chauthi exclamatory sentence hai. Ye achanak jazbaat ka zor se izhar karti hai. What a beautiful scene it is! How lucky you are! Is me aik bareek baat hai. Exclamatory jumle me lafzo ki tartib bayan wali rehti hai. What a lovely day it is sahi hai. What a lovely day is it ghalat hai, kyunke wo tartib sawal wali hai. Panchvi optative sentence hai. Ye dua ya khwahish bayan karti hai. May you succeed in life! May Allah bless you! Is ki pehchan may ka jumle ke shuru me ana aur dua wala matlab hai. Paper me isay aksar assertive ya exclamatory ke sath mila kar phansaya jata hai. Dua hai to optative hai, chahe aakhir me full stop ho ya exclamation ka nishan.

Board par qanoon Angrezi me

Ustad board par yun likhta hai. A clause is a group of words that has a subject and a verb. An independent clause can stand alone as a sentence. A dependent clause cannot stand alone. A simple sentence has one independent clause. A compound sentence has two or more independent clauses joined by a coordinating conjunction. A complex sentence has one independent clause and one or more dependent clauses. Aur qismo ke liye ustad ye likhta hai: an assertive sentence states something, an interrogative sentence asks a question, an imperative sentence gives a command or request, an exclamatory sentence shows strong feeling, and an optative sentence expresses a wish or prayer. Ye lines rat lo nahi, samajh lo, pehchan khud hogi.

Misaalein board se

Ab board par misaalein likhta hun. Har jumle ki saakht aur qism dono pehchano.

1. The sun rises in the east.
Aik clause hai. Ye simple hai, aur matlab ke hisaab se assertive hai.

2. Ali opened the book and started reading.
Subject aik hai, clause aik hai. And yahan do clause nahi jor raha, do kaam jor raha hai. Ye simple hai. Yehi wo jaal hai jo ginne me hota hai.

3. He was poor, but he was honest.
Do independent clause but se jude hain. Ye compound hai.

4. Although he was poor, he was honest.
Although wale hisse ne aik clause ko dependent bana diya. Ye complex hai. Pichhle jumle se mila kar dekho, matlab aik, saakht alag.

5. When the bell rang, the students left the classroom.
When wala hissa dependent hai. Ye complex hai.

6. Do you know where he lives?
Qism ke hisaab se ye interrogative hai. Saakht ke hisaab se complex hai, kyunke where he lives aik dependent clause hai jo know ka object bana hai. Aik jumla dono sawalo ka jawab de sakta hai, is liye sawal gaur se parho ke poochha kya hai.

7. Please close the door quietly.
Ye imperative hai. Hukam hai, subject you chhupa hai.

8. What a beautiful garden it is!
Ye exclamatory hai. Aur tartib dekho, it is aya hai, is it nahi.

9. May you live long and prosper!
Ye optative hai. Dua hai, is liye may shuru me aya hai.

10. The boy who is wearing a red cap is my cousin.
Who wala hissa boy ki pehchan bata raha hai aur dependent hai. Ye complex hai. Aise clause ko relative clause kehte hain.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Paper me is lesson ke sawal teen rang ke hote hain. Pehla, saakht pehchano, simple compound ya complex. Doosra, qism pehchano, khaas tor par optative aur exclamatory ko assertive se alag karna. Teesra, jumla mukammal hai ya fragment, yani adhoora tukra. Because he was ill, ye fragment hai, jumla nahi, kyunke dependent clause akela khara hai. Jab tum aage narration parho ge to yehi pehchan wahan kaam ayegi, kyunke wahan jumlo ko torna aur jorna yehi khel hai.

Ab paper wale andaaz ke do jumle dekho.

1. If you work hard, you will succeed, but if you waste time, you will repent.
2. He asked me whether I was ready for the test.

Pehla jumla dekho. Is me independent clause bhi hain aur if wale dependent bhi. Jab independent ke sath dependent bhi ho, to jumla complex kehlata hai, chahe beech me but bhi aye. Aise mile jule jumle ko kuch kitabein compound complex kehti hain, lekin paper me jab ye do hisso wala sawal aye to dependent ki mojoodgi complex ki taraf ishara karti hai. Doosra jumla complex hai aur assertive hai, chahe us ke andar sawal chhupa hai. Bahar wala jumla bayan hai, is liye assertive.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab wo trick jo teen saakhtain hamesha yaad rakhegi. Isay hum Barabar dost aur ghulam wala qanoon kehte hain. Simple jumla akela musafir hai. Aik clause, apna rasta khud. Compound jumla do barabar dost hain jo hath mila kar chal rahe hain. Dono barabar, koi chhota bara nahi, aur hath milane wala FANBOYS hai. Complex jumla aik malik aur aik ghulam hai. Malik independent clause hai jo akela bhi khara ho sakta hai, aur ghulam dependent clause hai jo because, although, when jaisi zanjeer se bandha hai. Jab bhi shak ho, bas ye poochho: clause kitne hain, aur koi ghulam hai ya sab barabar hain. Ghulam hai to complex, sab barabar hain aur aik se zyada hain to compound, aik hi hai to simple.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ab wo ghaltiyan dekho jo is lesson se seedhi judi hain.

Ghalat: Because he was ill, so he stayed at home.
Sahi: Because he was ill, he stayed at home. Ya phir: He was ill, so he stayed at home.
Wajah: because aur so aik sath nahi ate. Aik wajah batane ke liye kaafi hai. Ye ghalti Urdu ke kyunke aur is liye ko sath lane se hoti hai. Paper ki sab se mashhoor ghaltiyon me se aik hai.

Ghalat: May you are happy and healthy.
Sahi: May you be happy and healthy.
Wajah: optative jumle me may ke baad verb ki pehli shakal ati hai, be. Are nahi ayega.

Ghalat: What a lovely day is it!
Sahi: What a lovely day it is!
Wajah: exclamatory jumle me tartib bayan wali hoti hai, subject pehle, verb baad me. Is it wali tartib sawal ki hai.

Ghalat: I wish I was a king.
Sahi: I wish I were a king.
Wajah: jo khwahish asal haqiqat ke khilaf ho, wahan was ki jagah were ata hai, har subject ke sath. Ye bareek nuqta paper me ata hai.

Ghalat: Unless you do not work hard, you will fail.
Sahi: Unless you work hard, you will fail.
Wajah: unless ke andar khud nafi chhupi hai, matlab hai if not. Us ke sath do not lagana dohri nafi hai, jo ghalat hai.

Ghalat: He asked me that where I lived.
Sahi: He asked me where I lived.
Wajah: wh wale lafz ke sath that nahi ata. Ye ghalti agle lesson, narration, me bohat kaam ayegi.

Jawab aur un ki wajah

1. Jawab B hai, compound. Do independent clause hain aur but ne unhe jora hai. Barabar dost yaad hai na?
2. Jawab D hai, complex. Who called you yesterday aik dependent clause hai jo man ko pehchanwa raha hai.
3. Jawab C hai, optative. May se shuru hone wali dua optative hoti hai.
4. Jawab A hai, exclamatory. Achanak afsos ka zor se izhar hai.
5. Jawab C hai, simple. Subject do hain lekin clause aik hi hai. Clause gino, alfaaz nahi.
6. Jawab D hai, complex. If wala hissa dependent clause hai.
7. Jawab B hai, imperative. Hukam hai aur subject you chhupa hai. Ye hum Lesson 12 me parh chuke hain.
8. Jawab C hai, assertive. Nafi wala bayan bhi assertive hi hota hai. Not ane se qism nahi badalti.
9. Jawab C hai. Because he was late aik dependent clause akela khara hai. Ye fragment hai, mukammal jumla nahi.
10. Jawab D hai, interrogative. Bahar wala jumla sawal poochh raha hai, is liye qism interrogative hai, chahe andar who he is aik dependent clause hai.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye pehli tasveer barabar dost aur ghulam wali ho. Aik taraf do dost hath milaye khare hon aur un ke darmiyan FANBOYS ke saat harf likhe hon, neeche compound likha ho. Doosri taraf aik malik khara ho aur us ke sath zanjeer se bandha aik ghulam ho, zanjeer par because, although, when likha ho, neeche complex likha ho. Aur kone me aik akela musafir ho jis ke neeche simple likha ho. Aik nazar me teen saakhtain zehen me beth jayengi.

Doosri tasveer clause ginne wali ho. Aik lamba jumla rang ke tukro me likha ho. Independent clause hare rang me aur dependent clause narangi rang me. Neeche ginne ka tareeqa likha ho ke pehle verb gino, phir dekho kon apne pao par khara hai. Ye tasveer student ko har jumla khol kar dekhna sikha degi.
