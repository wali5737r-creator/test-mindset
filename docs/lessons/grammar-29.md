Lesson 29: Direct and Indirect Speech
Direct aur Indirect. Dusre ki baat apne alfaaz me, baghair matlab badle

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

One paper me narration ka sawal na aye, ye taqreeban na mumkin hai. Kabhi direct ko indirect karna hota hai, kabhi indirect ko direct me pehchanna hota hai, aur chaar options me se teen is qanoon ki kisi na kisi bareek ghalti par bane hote hain. Achi khabar ye hai ke narration koi jadoo nahi. Is ke chand pakke qanoon hain. Tense aik kadam peeche jata hai, pronoun bolne aur sunne wale ke hisaab se badalta hai, aur waqt ke alfaaz bhi peeche ho jate hain. Ye teen cheezein samajh lo to narration paper ka sab se pakka number ban jata hai. Is lesson me hum pura qanoon aik sath rakhenge, taake exam hall me koi surat tumhe uljha na sake.

Pehle do shaklein pehchano

Jab hum kisi ki baat bilkul usi ke alfaaz me sunate hain, to use direct speech kehte hain. He said, "I am busy." Yahan busy wala hissa bolne wale ke apne alfaaz hain, is liye wo commas ke andar band hai aur pehla harf capital hai. Jab hum usi baat ko apne alfaaz me, matlab wahi rakh kar sunate hain, to use indirect ya reported speech kehte hain. He said that he was busy. Ab commas khatam, aur jumla hamare jumle ke andar ghul gaya.

Har narration wale jumle ke do hisse hote hain. Pehla reporting clause hai, he said wala hissa, jis ka verb reporting verb kehlata hai. Doosra reported clause hai, jo asal baat hai. Qanoon reporting verb ke tense par chalta hai. Agar reporting verb past me hai, said, told, asked, to andar wali baat peeche jati hai. Agar reporting verb present ya future me hai, says, will say, to andar wali baat bilkul nahi badalti. Ye buniyad hai. Isay pakka kar lo, baqi sab is par khara hai.

Said aur told ka farq

Sab se pehle ye chhota sa farq hal kar lo, kyunke paper yahin se shuru karta hai. Said ke baad sunne wale ka naam aye to to lagta hai. He said to me. Aur said akele bhi aa sakta hai. He said that he was ill. Lekin told ke baad sunne wale ka naam lazmi ata hai aur to nahi ata. He told me that he was ill. Told akele nahi khara hota. He told that he was ill kehna ghalat hai. Yaad rakho: said akele chal sakta hai, told ko sahara chahiye.

Tense ka peeche jana, backshift

Ab asal qanoon. Jab reporting verb past me ho, to reported clause ka tense aik kadam peeche jata hai. Har tense ki surat gaur se samjho.

Present simple past simple ban jata hai. "I am busy" se he was busy. Present continuous past continuous ban jata hai. "I am waiting" se he was waiting. Present perfect past perfect ban jata hai. "I have finished my work" se he had finished his work. Past simple aksar past perfect ban jata hai. "I bought a book" se he had bought a book. Haan, jab waqt bilkul saaf diya ho to kabhi past simple bhi reh jata hai, lekin paper ke jawab me past perfect wali surat mehfooz mani jati hai. Past continuous past perfect continuous ban jata hai. "I was sleeping" se he had been sleeping. Lekin past perfect aur past perfect continuous sab se aakhri manzil hain. Ye aur peeche nahi jate, yehi rehte hain.

Helping verbs bhi peeche jate hain. Ye hum Lesson 15 me parh chuke hain. Will would ban jata hai. Can could ban jata hai. May might ban jata hai. Shall aam tor par would ya should ban jata hai. Must ya to had to ban jata hai ya must hi rehta hai, khaas tor par jab wo koi aam qanoon ya lazmi mashwara ho. Ye tense wale form tum Lesson 18 aur Lesson 19 me parh chuke ho, is liye peeche wali shaklein tumhe pehchanne me mushkil nahi hogi.

Lekin teen jagah ye qanoon ruk jata hai, aur yehi paper ke sab se qeemti number hain. Pehli jagah universal truth hai. Jo baat hamesha sach hai, wo peeche nahi jati. The teacher said that the earth revolves round the sun. Revolves wahi raha, revolved nahi hua, kyunke zameen ka ghoomna kal bhi sach tha, aaj bhi hai. Kahawatein aur aam sachai bhi aisi hi hai. The teacher said that honesty is the best policy. Doosri jagah wo baat hai jo is waqt bhi bilkul waisi hi hai, jaise koi aadat ya mustaqil halat. Lekin paper me is ka sab se saaf istemal universal truth par hota hai, is liye wahi pakka rakho. Teesri jagah wo hai jahan reporting verb khud present ya future me ho. He says that he is ready. Yahan is ready hi rahega, kyunke bolna abhi ho raha hai, peeche jane ki wajah hi nahi.

Pronoun ka badalna, SON wala qanoon

Ab doosra bara qanoon. Pronoun kis ke hisaab se badlega? Is ke liye ustaad aik lafz dete hain, SON. Isay yaad kar lo, pronoun ka masla khatam ho jayega.

S ka matlab hai subject. Reported speech ka first person pronoun, I, we, my, our, reporting clause ke subject ke hisaab se badalta hai. Bolne wala khud apni baat suna raha hai to I wahi rahega. Ali said, "I am ill" se Ali said that he was ill. Yahan I Ali ke hisaab se he ban gaya, kyunke Ali subject hai. O ka matlab hai object. Second person pronoun, you, your, reporting clause ke object ke hisaab se badalta hai. Ali said to me, "You are late" se Ali told me that I was late. You sunne wale, yani me, ke hisaab se I ban gaya. N ka matlab hai no change. Third person pronoun, he, she, it, they, bilkul nahi badalta. Wo jis ke liye tha, usi ke liye rehta hai. Bas, SON yaad hai to pronoun kabhi ghalat nahi hoga.

Waqt aur jagah ke alfaaz bhi peeche jate hain

Teesra qanoon ye hai ke jo alfaaz is waqt aur is jagah ki taraf ishara karte hain, wo bhi peeche ho jate hain, kyunke baat ab kisi aur waqt me sunai ja rahi hai. Now then ban jata hai. Today that day ban jata hai. Tonight that night ban jata hai. Yesterday the previous day ban jata hai. Last night the previous night ban jata hai. Tomorrow the next day ban jata hai. Next week the following week ban jata hai. Ago before ban jata hai. Here there ban jata hai. This that ban jata hai, aur these those ban jata hai. Inhe aik silsile me samjho. Jaise tense peeche gaya, waise ye alfaaz bhi peeche gaye. Aik hi harkat hai, bas do shaklein hain.

Sawal wale jumlo ki narration

Ab jumlo ki qismo ki narration alag alag samjho. Pehle sawal wale jumle, jo hum Lesson 10 me banana seekh chuke hain.

Sawal ho to reporting verb said ki jagah asked ya enquired ban jata hai. Aur do batein lazmi hoti hain. Aik, jumle ki tartib bayan wali ho jati hai. Doosra, sawal ka nishan khatam ho jata hai. Ab sawal do rang ke hote hain. Agar sawal yes ya no wala hai, yani helping verb se shuru hota hai, to jorne ke liye if ya whether ata hai. She said to him, "Are you coming tomorrow?" se she asked him if he was coming the next day. Dekho, are you wali ulti tartib seedhi ho gayi, he was aya. Aur agar sawal wh wale lafz se shuru hota hai, to wahi lafz jorne ka kaam karta hai, us ke sath that nahi ata. He said to me, "Where do you live?" se he asked me where I lived. Do wala sahara bhi khatam ho gaya, kyunke past me live, lived ban gaya. Ye do ghaltiyan paper me sab se zyada ati hain: that ke sath where lagana, aur tartib ulti rakhna. Dono se bachna hai.

Hukam aur darkhwast wale jumlo ki narration

Ab imperative jumle, jo hum Lesson 12 me parh chuke hain. Hukam, darkhwast, mashwara ya mana karna ho to reporting verb matlab ke hisaab se badalta hai. Hukam ho to ordered, darkhwast ho to requested, mashwara ho to advised, mana karna ho to forbade. Aur jumla to ke sath jorta hai. The officer said to the peon, "Bring the file at once" se the officer ordered the peon to bring the file at once. Verb ki pehli shakal to ke baad waisi hi rehti hai. Nafi wala hukam ho to not to ata hai. Mother said to me, "Do not waste your time" se mother advised me not to waste my time. Aur let wale jumle, jahan koi tajweez ho, wahan aam tor par proposed ya suggested ke sath that aur should ata hai. He said, "Let us go for a walk" se he proposed that they should go for a walk. Paper me let wala kam ata hai, lekin pehchan rakho.

Khushi aur afsos wale jumle bhi kabhi kabhi ate hain. Un me reporting verb exclaimed with joy ya exclaimed with sorrow ban jata hai aur baat bayan wale jumle ki tarah that se jur jati hai. She said, "Hurrah! We have won" se she exclaimed with joy that they had won. Bas itna yaad rakho, tafseel paper me kam hi poochhi jati hai.

Board par qanoon Angrezi me

Ustad board par narration ka khulasa yun likhta hai. When the reporting verb is in the past tense, the tense of the reported speech moves one step back. Present becomes past, past becomes past perfect, will becomes would, can becomes could and may becomes might. First person pronouns change according to the subject, second person according to the object, and third person does not change. Words of time and place also move back: now becomes then, today becomes that day and tomorrow becomes the next day. Universal truths and proverbs do not change. Aur ustad aakhir me ye line likhta hai: in reported questions, the word order becomes that of a statement, and in reported commands, the verb is joined with to. Ye khulasa zehen me ho to koi surat mushkil nahi.

Misaalein board se

Ab board par misaalein likhta hun. Har jore ko gaur se dekho, konsi cheez kahan gayi.

1. He said, "I am busy today."
Indirect: He said that he was busy that day.
Am was ban gaya, today that day ban gaya. Do harkatein aik sath hui hain.

2. She said to me, "I will help you."
Indirect: She told me that she would help me.
Said to told ban gaya. Will would ban gaya. Aur you, object me ke hisaab se, me ban gaya. Ye SON ka O hai.

3. Ali said, "I have finished my work."
Indirect: Ali said that he had finished his work.
Have finished had finished ban gaya. Aur my, subject Ali ke hisaab se, his ban gaya. Ye SON ka S hai.

4. The teacher said, "The earth revolves round the sun."
Indirect: The teacher said that the earth revolves round the sun.
Kuch nahi badla. Ye universal truth hai. Yehi wo jaal hai jahan jaldi karne wala revolves ko revolved karke number khota hai.

5. He said to me, "Where do you live?"
Indirect: He asked me where I lived.
Wh wala sawal hai, is liye where hi jorne wala bana, that nahi aya. Do live me ghul gaya aur lived ban gaya.

6. She said to him, "Are you coming tomorrow?"
Indirect: She asked him if he was coming the next day.
Yes no wala sawal hai, is liye if aya. Tartib seedhi ho gayi aur tomorrow the next day ban gaya.

7. The officer said to the peon, "Bring the file at once."
Indirect: The officer ordered the peon to bring the file at once.
Hukam hai, is liye ordered aya aur jumla to se juda.

8. Mother said to me, "Do not go out in the rain."
Indirect: Mother told me not to go out in the rain.
Mana karna hai, is liye not to aya. Yahan advised bhi aa sakta tha, matlab par hai.

9. They said, "We are playing cricket now."
Indirect: They said that they were playing cricket then.
Are playing were playing ban gaya aur now then ban gaya.

10. He says, "I am ready."
Indirect: He says that he is ready.
Reporting verb present me hai, is liye andar kuch nahi badla. Ye surat bhoolna mat, paper isay bhi deta hai.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Paper me narration teen rang me ata hai. Pehla rang, direct diya hai, sahi indirect chuno. Doosra rang, indirect diya hai, sahi direct chuno. Is me peeche wali harkat ko ulta chalana hota hai. Would dekho to will par jao, that day dekho to today par jao. Teesra rang, jumle ki ghalti theek karo, jahan aksar told ke baad object gayab hota hai ya that ke sath where laga hota hai. Teeno rang ka hal aik hi checklist hai. Pehle reporting verb dekho, phir tense aik kadam peeche, phir SON se pronoun, phir waqt ke alfaaz, aur aakhir me jumle ki qism ke hisaab se jorne wala lafz.

Ab paper wale andaaz ke do jumle dekho, in ki indirect khud banao.

1. The doctor said to the patient, "Take this medicine twice a day."
2. Sana said to her friend, "I cannot come to your house today."

Pehle jumle me doctor mashwara de raha hai, is liye advised the patient to take that medicine twice a day ayega. Dekho, this bhi that ban gaya. Doosre jumle me Sana said to her friend that she could not come to her house that day ayega. Cannot could not ban gaya aur today that day.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab wo trick jo poori narration ko aik tasveer me band kar degi. Isay hum Aik kadam peeche wala qanoon kehte hain. Samjho tum aik time machine me bethe ho jo sirf peeche jati hai. Narration bante hi sab kuch aik kadam peeche sark jata hai. Tense aik kadam peeche, am se was, will se would. Waqt aik kadam peeche, today se that day, now se then. Jagah aik kadam peeche, here se there. Aur pronoun ke liye tumhara saathi SON hai, jo khara dekh raha hai ke kon kis ke hisaab se badlega. Bas aik cheez is machine me bhi nahi hilti, aur wo hai hamesha wala sach. Zameen kal bhi ghoomti thi, aaj bhi ghoomti hai, is liye revolves wahi rehta hai. Aik kadam peeche, SON ka saath, aur sach apni jagah. Ye teen batein yaad hain to narration tumhara hai.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ab wo ghaltiyan dekho jo paper me sab se zyada number katwati hain.

Ghalat: He told that he was ill.
Sahi: He said that he was ill. Ya phir: He told me that he was ill.
Wajah: told ke baad sunne wala lazmi ata hai. Told akele nahi khara hota.

Ghalat: She asked me that where I was going.
Sahi: She asked me where I was going.
Wajah: wh wale lafz ke sath that nahi ata. Where khud jorne wala hai.

Ghalat: He asked me where was I going.
Sahi: He asked me where I was going.
Wajah: reported sawal me tartib bayan wali hoti hai. Was I wali ulti tartib sirf asal sawal me chalti hai.

Ghalat: The teacher said that the sun rose in the east.
Sahi: The teacher said that the sun rises in the east.
Wajah: suraj ka mashriq se nikalna universal truth hai. Ye peeche nahi jata.

Ghalat: He ordered to me to go at once.
Sahi: He ordered me to go at once.
Wajah: ordered ke baad object seedha ata hai, to nahi lagta. To sirf verb se pehle ata hai.

Ghalat: She said that she will come tomorrow.
Sahi: She said that she would come the next day.
Wajah: reporting verb past me hai, is liye will would banega aur tomorrow the next day. Dono harkatein lazmi hain.

Ghalat: Ali said to me that you are a good boy.
Sahi: Ali told me that I was a good boy.
Wajah: you sunne wale, yani me, ke hisaab se I banega. Ye SON ka O hai. Aur said to ki jagah told zyada durust hai.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye pehli tasveer time machine wali ho. Aik seedhi bani ho jis ke teen kadam hon. Upar wale kadam par present ke tense likhe hon, beech wale par past ke, aur neeche wale par past perfect ke. Aik teer peeche ki taraf ja raha ho aur us par likha ho, aik kadam peeche. Seedhi ke sath teen alfaaz khare hon, now, today aur tomorrow, aur un ke samne un ki peeche wali shaklein hon, then, that day aur the next day. Aur seedhi ke bilkul upar aik suraj bana ho jo hil nahi raha, us ke neeche likha ho, hamesha ka sach apni jagah rehta hai.

Doosri tasveer SON wali ho. Teen bare harf S, O aur N aik board par likhe hon. S ke neeche aik subject ki tasveer ho aur likha ho, first person subject ke hisaab se. O ke neeche sunne wale ki tasveer ho aur likha ho, second person object ke hisaab se. N ke neeche aik pathar bana ho aur likha ho, third person hilta nahi. Ye tasveer pronoun wale poore masle ko aik nazar me hal kar degi.
