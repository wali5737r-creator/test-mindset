Lesson 30: Active and Passive Voice — Complete
Active aur Passive Voice. Har tense ka passive, aik hi formula se

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me voice change ka sawal taqreeban har dafa ata hai. Kabhi active jumla de kar us ki passive chunni hoti hai aur kabhi passive de kar active. Aur ye sawal un sawalon me se hai jo pura number dete hain, kyunke in ka aik hi formula hai aur jo student wo formula pakka kar le, wo tense koi sa bhi ho, ghalti nahi karta. Lesson 24 me hum ne passive ki buniyad seekhi thi, ke jab kaam aham ho aur karne wala nahi to jumla passive banta hai. Ab is lesson me hum usi buniyad ko mukammal karte hain. Sab tenses ka passive, kis tense ka passive banta hi nahi, by kab lagta hai aur kab chhup jata hai, do object wale jumle aur hukum wale jumlo ka passive. Ye sab is aik lesson me hai. Isay pakka kar lo, voice wala hissa paper me tumhara bank ban jayega.

Pehle buniyad taza karo

Pehle wo baat taza kar lo jo Lesson 24 me seekhi thi. Active voice me karne wala aham hota hai. Ahmed ne khat likha. Yahan nazar Ahmed par hai. Passive voice me kaam aham hota hai. Khat likha gaya. Yahan nazar khat par hai, likhne wala peeche chala gaya. Sarkari daftaron ki angrezi, akhbar ki khabrein aur science ki kitabein passive se bhari hoti hain, is liye paper banane wale isay bohat pasand karte hain.

Ab formula samajh lo, kyunke poori game isi aik formula ki hai. Passive banane ke teen qadam hain. Pehla qadam, active jumle ke object ko utha kar jumle ke shuru me le ao, wo naya subject ban jayega. Dusra qadam, be ki wo shakal lagao jo jumle ke tense ke mutabiq ho. Teesra qadam, asal verb ko us ki teesri form, yani V3, me badal do. Aur aakhir me agar karne wale ka zikr zaroori ho to by laga kar purana subject likh do. Bas. Har tense ka passive isi teen qadam se banta hai. Tense badalne se sirf be ki shakal badalti hai, verb hamesha V3 hi rehta hai. Ye jumla zehen me bitha lo, aadha lesson yahin khatam ho gaya.

Ab tense ke hisaab se be ki shaklein dekho. Present simple me is, am ya are ata hai. The letter is written. Past simple me was ya were. The letter was written. Present continuous me is being, am being ya are being. The letter is being written. Present perfect me has been ya have been. The letter has been written. Past continuous me was being ya were being. Past perfect me had been. Future simple me will be. The letter will be written. Future perfect me will have been. Aur jab jumle me can, must, should jaisa modal ho to us ke baad seedha be ajata hai. The letter must be written. Modal ke maazi wali shakal me have been ata hai. The work should have been finished.

Ab wo baat jo paper sab se zyada parkhta hai. Chaar tense aisay hain jin ka passive nahi banta. Present perfect continuous, past perfect continuous, future continuous aur future perfect continuous. Agar jumle me has been reading, had been reading ya will be reading jaisi shakal nazar aye to samajh lo is ka passive nahi ban sakta, aur paper me jo option passive banane ki koshish kare wo ghalat hoga. Dusri rukawat ye hai ke jis verb ka object hi nahi hota, us ka passive bhi nahi banta. Go, come, sleep, die, arrive, ye sab aisay verb hain jin ke peeche koi object nahi ata. He died in 1947 ko passive me nahi badal sakte, kyunke agay le jane ke liye koi object hi nahi hai.

Ab by wali baat samajh lo, kyunke yahan bhi number bante aur katte hain. By kab lagta hai? Jab karne wala aham ho ya nayi khabar de raha ho. The thief was arrested by the police. Yahan police ka zikr khabar hai. By kab nahi lagta? Teen soraton me. Aik, jab karne wala maloom hi na ho. My watch has been stolen. Churane wala kaun hai, pata nahi, by kis ke sath lagayein. Do, jab karne wala zahiri baat ho. The road was repaired. Zahir hai mazdooron ne ki hogi, likhne ki zaroorat nahi. Teen, jab karne wala aam sa lafz ho jaise people ya someone. Aik aur khaas baat. Kuch passive jumlo me by ki jagah doosri preposition ati hai. I was surprised at his behaviour. She is known to everyone. The box is filled with sand. In jumlo me by lagana ghalti hogi.

Ab do object wale jumle. Kuch verb do cheezein lete hain, aik shakhs aur aik cheez. He gave me a book. Yahan me bhi object hai aur a book bhi. Aisay jumlo ke do passive bante hain. I was given a book by him. A book was given to me by him. Dono sahi hain, lekin pehla wala, jo shakhs se shuru hota hai, zyada aam hai aur paper me aksar wahi sahi jawab hota hai. Give ke ilawa offer, show, teach, send, promise aur tell bhi isi khandan ke verb hain.

Aakhir me hukum wale jumle, yani imperative ka passive. Open the door ka passive banane ka tareeqa Let wala hai. Let the door be opened. Tartib yaad rakho. Pehle let, phir object, phir be, phir V3. Nafi wala hukum ho to not beech me ata hai. Let the door not be opened. Aur aik narm andaaz bhi hai jo daftar ki angrezi me chalta hai. You are requested to open the door. Dono tareeqe paper me ate hain.

Board par qanoon Angrezi me

Board par ustad ye lines likhta hai. In the passive voice, the object of the active sentence becomes the subject. The verb be takes the form of the tense, and the main verb is always used in its third form. Four tenses have no passive: the present perfect continuous, the past perfect continuous, the future continuous and the future perfect continuous. A verb with no object has no passive. The word by is used only when the doer is important or new; it is dropped when the doer is unknown, obvious or unimportant. A sentence with two objects can make two passives, and the one beginning with the person is more common. An imperative sentence becomes passive with let: Let the door be opened.

Misaalein board se

Ab board par misaalein dekhte hain. Har misaal me pehle active jumla hai, phir us ka passive, phir wajah ki line hai.

1. Active: She writes a letter. Passive: A letter is written by her.
Present simple hai. Be ki shakal is ayi aur write ki teesri form written ban gayi.

2. Active: She is writing a letter. Passive: A letter is being written by her.
Present continuous hai, is liye is ke sath being bhi aya. Being bhoolna is tense ki sab se aam ghalti hai.

3. Active: She has written a letter. Passive: A letter has been written by her.
Present perfect hai, is liye has been aya. Been bhool jao to jumla ghalat ho jata hai.

4. Active: She wrote a letter. Passive: A letter was written by her.
Past simple hai, be ki maazi shakal was ayi.

5. Active: She was writing a letter. Passive: A letter was being written by her.
Past continuous me was being ata hai.

6. Active: She had written a letter. Passive: A letter had been written by her.
Past perfect me had been sab ke sath aik jaisa ata hai.

7. Active: She will write a letter. Passive: A letter will be written by her.
Future simple me will be ata hai.

8. Active: She can write a letter. Passive: A letter can be written by her.
Modal ke baad seedha be ata hai, koi being ya been nahi.

9. Active: Someone stole my watch. Passive: My watch was stolen.
Karne wala maloom nahi aur someone jaisa aam lafz hai, is liye by wala hissa gir gaya. Paper isay bohat pasand karta hai.

10. Active: The teacher gave the students a test. Passive: The students were given a test by the teacher.
Do object wala jumla hai, is liye shakhs wale object ko agay laya gaya hai, jo zyada aam tareeqa hai.

11. Active: Close the gate. Passive: Let the gate be closed.
Hukum wale jumle ka passive let se banta hai.

12. Active: Did he break the window? Passive: Was the window broken by him?
Sawal ka passive bhi sawal hi rehta hai, sirf be agay ajata hai aur verb teesri form le leta hai.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Akhbar ki surkhi parho to aadhi passive me hoti hain. Five persons were injured. The bill was passed. Daftar ki reporton me bhi yahi rang hai. The application was received on Monday. Aur rozmarra me jab karne wale ka naam lena na chahein to passive kaam ata hai. Mistakes were made. Paper me ye jumle kabhi sedhe diye jate hain aur kabhi voice change kar ke.

Ab paper wale andaaz ke do jumle dekho.

1. The new hospital was inaugurated by the chief minister yesterday.
2. All the applications must be submitted before the last date.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab wo trick jo har tense ke passive ko aik pal me hal kar degi. Isay Uniform wali trick kehte hain. Samjho ke passive jumle me do mulazim hain. Aik hai be, aur dusra hai asal verb. Be wo mulazim hai jo har roz apne daftar ke waqt ke mutabiq kapde badalta hai. Subah present ka libaas, kal past ka, kal future ka. Lekin asal verb wo sipahi hai jo hamesha aik hi uniform pehenta hai, aur us uniform ka naam hai teesri form, V3. Tense jo bhi ho, sipahi ki uniform nahi badalti. Tumhe sirf itna karna hai ke be ko sahi waqt ke kapde pehna do aur verb ko V3 ki uniform me khara kar do. Passive tayyar hai. Be tense ke kapde pehenta hai, verb hamesha V3 ki uniform me rehta hai. Bas ye aik line yaad rakh lo.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ab wo ghaltiyan dekho jo students voice change me karte hain. Main ghalat aur sahi sath sath likh raha hun.

Ghalat: The letter was wrote by him.
Sahi: The letter was written by him.
Wajah: passive me verb ki hamesha teesri form ati hai. Wrote dusri form hai, written teesri.

Ghalat: My pen has stolen.
Sahi: My pen has been stolen.
Wajah: present perfect ke passive me has ke sath been lazmi hai.

Ghalat: The work will finished tomorrow.
Sahi: The work will be finished tomorrow.
Wajah: future ke passive me will ke baad be ata hai.

Ghalat: The house is building.
Sahi: The house is being built.
Wajah: ghar khud nahi ban raha, banaya ja raha hai. Continuous ke passive me being aur built dono aayenge.

Ghalat: English is speaking in many countries.
Sahi: English is spoken in many countries.
Wajah: English bolti nahi, boli jati hai. Kaam lene wala nahi, kaam pane wala subject hai.

Ghalat: He was died in an accident.
Sahi: He died in an accident.
Wajah: die ka koi object nahi hota, is liye is ka passive banta hi nahi. Ye jumla active me hi rahega.

Ghalat: Let be opened the door.
Sahi: Let the door be opened.
Wajah: let ke baad pehle object ata hai, phir be, phir teesri form. Tartib bigre to jumla ghalat.

Ghalat: A book was gave to me.
Sahi: A book was given to me.
Wajah: was ke baad give ki teesri form given ayegi, gave nahi.

Jawab aur un ki wajah

1. Jawab B hai. Has finished present perfect hai, is liye passive me has been finished ayega. Been wala option hi sahi hota hai.
2. Jawab C hai. Arrested past simple hai, is liye was arrested. Tense passive me kabhi nahi badalta.
3. Jawab D hai. Present perfect continuous un chaar tenses me se hai jin ka passive nahi banta.
4. Jawab A hai. Hukum wale jumle ka passive let se banta hai. Let, phir object, phir be, phir teesri form.
5. Jawab B hai. Do object wale jumle ka sab se aam passive shakhs se shuru hota hai. I was given a pen.
6. Jawab C hai. Who jab passive me jata hai to by whom ban jata hai aur sawal ki tartib ke liye was agay ata hai.
7. Jawab B hai. Bridge banaya ja raha hai, kaam chal raha hai, is liye is being built. A wala option wohi mashhoor trap hai.
8. Jawab B hai. Will ke baad be aur phir teesri form. Will be completed.
9. Jawab A hai. Karne wala maloom nahi aur someone aam lafz hai, is liye by wala hissa gir jata hai. Sirf My watch ki tarah, My bicycle was stolen.
10. Jawab B hai. Is explaining present continuous hai, is liye passive me is being explained ayega. Being wala option hi is tense ki pehchan hai.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye pehli tasveer Uniform wali trick ki ho. Aik sipahi khara ho jis ki uniform par V3 likha ho aur wo har mausam me waisa ka waisa hi ho. Us ke sath aik dusra kirdar ho jo kabhi present ke kapdo me ho, kabhi past ke aur kabhi future ke, aur us par Be likha ho. Neeche aik line ho ke be waqt ke kapde badalta hai, verb hamesha V3 ki uniform me rehta hai. Student ye tasveer aik dafa dekh le to koi tense use dhoka nahi de sakta.

Doosri tasveer teen qadam wali seedhi ki ho. Pehli seedhi par object ko agay lata hua teer, dusri seedhi par be ki mukhtalif shaklein ghari ki soiyon ke sath, aur teesri seedhi par verb ki teesri form ka bara sa nishan. Seedhi ke aakhir me by wala chhota sa darwaza ho jo kabhi khula ho aur kabhi band, taake student ko yaad rahe ke by kab lagta hai aur kab nahi lagta.
