Lesson 31: All 12 Tenses — Pehchan aur Farq
Barah Tenses. Aik hi kahani, barah waqt, aur har tense ki pehchan

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me tense ka sawal sab se zyada ane wale sawalon me se hai. Kabhi jumla de kar poocha jata hai ke ye konsa tense hai, kabhi khali jagah me sahi verb lagana hota hai, aur kabhi chaar jumlo me se aik theek jumla chunna hota hai. In teeno soraton me jeet usi ki hoti hai jo tense ko pehchan sakta hai. Masla ye hai ke aksar students barah tenses ko barah alag cheezein samajh kar ratte hain aur paper me sab gadh madh ho jata hai. Is lesson me hum barah tenses ko aik hi kahani par chala kar dekhenge, har tense ki pehchan wali nishani seekhenge, aur Urdu tarjume se tense pehchanne ka wo tareeqa seekhenge jo kabhi dhoka nahi deta. Lesson 18 me hum ne Past Tense ko tafseel se parha tha aur Lesson 19 me continuous wale rang dekhe thay. Ab un sab ko aik jagah jama karke poori tasveer dekhte hain.

Aik kahani, teen waqt

Sab se pehle ye samajh lo ke tense asal me sirf teen hain. Maazi, yani past. Haal, yani present. Aur mustaqbil, yani future. Waqt bas ye teen hain. Phir har waqt ki chaar soratein hain. Kaam seedha sada bayan ho to simple. Kaam chal raha ho to continuous. Kaam mukammal ho chuka ho to perfect. Aur kaam aik muddat se chal raha ho to perfect continuous. Teen waqt gunah chaar soratein, kul barah tense. Bas, barah ka ye hisaab zehen me bitha lo, aadha bojh yahin utar gaya.

Ab Ali ki kahani suno. Ali kitaab parhta hai, aur hum usi aik kaam ko barah waqton me ghuma kar dekhte hain. Present simple me Ali roz kitaab parhta hai. Ali reads a book every day. Present continuous me Ali is waqt kitaab parh raha hai. Ali is reading a book now. Present perfect me Ali kitaab parh chuka hai. Ali has read the book. Present perfect continuous me Ali subah se kitaab parh raha hai. Ali has been reading the book since morning. Dekha tum ne? Waqt wohi haal hai, lekin kaam ki sorat har dafa badal gayi. Kabhi aadat, kabhi chal raha kaam, kabhi mukammal kaam, kabhi muddat se chal raha kaam.

Ab yahi kahani maazi me le jao. Past simple me Ali ne kal kitaab parhi. Ali read the book yesterday. Past continuous me jab main kamre me aya to Ali kitaab parh raha tha. Ali was reading a book when I entered the room. Past perfect me mere ane se pehle Ali kitaab parh chuka tha. Ali had read the book before I came. Past perfect continuous me Ali do ghante se kitaab parh raha tha. Ali had been reading the book for two hours. Aur ab mustaqbil. Future simple me Ali kal kitaab parhega. Ali will read the book tomorrow. Future continuous me Ali is waqt kal kitaab parh raha hoga. Ali will be reading a book at this time tomorrow. Future perfect me Ali itvaar tak kitaab parh chuka hoga. Ali will have read the book by Sunday. Future perfect continuous me Ali aath baje tak teen ghante se kitaab parh raha hoga. Ali will have been reading the book for three hours by eight o clock. Barah tense mukammal. Kahani aik, waqt barah.

Ab har tense ki pehchan wali nishaniyan samajh lo, kyunke paper me yehi nishaniyan tumhe jawab tak le jati hain. Present simple ki nishani hai roz wala waqt. Always, usually, every day, daily. Verb ki pehli form ati hai aur he, she, it ke sath s ya es lagta hai. Present continuous ki nishani hai now, at this moment, look aur listen wale jumle. Is, am, are ke sath ing wali form ati hai. Present perfect ki nishaniyan hain just, already, yet, ever, never, since aur for. Has ya have ke sath teesri form ati hai. Present perfect continuous me bhi since aur for ate hain, lekin sath waqt ki muddat lazmi hoti hai aur been ke sath ing wali form ati hai. Since subah, for do ghante.

Maazi ki nishaniyan aur saaf hain. Past simple ki nishani hai guzra hua waqt saaf likha hua. Yesterday, ago, last night, last year. Verb ki dusri form ati hai. Past continuous ki nishani hai while aur when wale jumle, jahan aik lamba kaam chal raha tha aur beech me chhota kaam aa gaya. Was ya were ke sath ing wali form. Past perfect ki pehchan ye hai ke maazi me do kaam hain aur jo pehle hua tha us par had ke sath teesri form hai. Before aur already is ki nishaniyan hain. Past perfect continuous me had been ke sath ing wali form aur muddat hoti hai. Mustaqbil ki nishaniyan sab se asaan hain. Tomorrow, next week, next year. Future simple me will ke sath pehli form, future continuous me will be ke sath ing, future perfect me by ke sath will have aur teesri form, aur future perfect continuous me will have been ke sath ing aur muddat. By wala lafz future perfect ki sab se pakki nishani hai. By Sunday, by 2030, by tomorrow.

Ab wo farq jo paper har saal parkhta hai. Present perfect aur past simple ka farq. Qanoon ye hai ke jab jumle me guzra hua waqt saaf likha ho, yesterday, ago ya last year, to hamesha past simple ayega, present perfect kabhi nahi ayega. I met him yesterday. Ye sahi hai. I have met him yesterday kehna ghalat hai, kyunke yesterday ate hi perfect ka darwaza band ho jata hai. Aur jab waqt na diya ho aur kaam ka asar ab bhi ho to present perfect. I have lost my key. Anaaj gum hai aur ab bhi gum hai, is liye perfect. Dusra mashhoor joda hai past continuous aur past perfect continuous ka. Dono me kaam chal raha tha, farq sirf muddat ka hai. Jab sirf ye batana ho ke us waqt kaam chal raha tha to past continuous. He was sleeping at nine. Aur jab ye batana ho ke kaam kitni muddat se chal raha tha to past perfect continuous. He had been sleeping for three hours. Muddat nazar aye to had been wala, muddat na ho to was wala.

Board par qanoon Angrezi me

Board par ustad tense ki pehchan yun likhta hai. There are three times and four states, and three times four makes twelve tenses. The simple tense tells a fact or a habit. The continuous tense shows an action going on. The perfect tense shows an action completed. The perfect continuous tense shows an action going on for a period of time. Aur sab se aham line ye hai: when a past time like yesterday, ago or last year is given in the sentence, use the past simple, never the present perfect. Learn the signal words and the tense will name itself.

Misaalein board se

Ab board par wahi barah misaalein aik sath dekho. Har misaal ke neeche us ki Urdu pehchan aur nishani likhi hai.

1. Ali reads a book every day.
Urdu: Ali roz kitaab parhta hai. Nishani every day hai aur verb ki pehli form par s laga hai. Ye present simple hai.

2. Ali is reading a book now.
Urdu: Ali is waqt kitaab parh raha hai. Nishani now hai aur raha hai wali awaz hai. Ye present continuous hai.

3. Ali has read the book.
Urdu: Ali kitaab parh chuka hai. Chuka hai wali awaz aur has ke sath teesri form. Ye present perfect hai.

4. Ali has been reading the book since morning.
Urdu: Ali subah se kitaab parh raha hai. Muddat ke sath raha hai, is liye ye present perfect continuous hai. Sirf raha hai hota to continuous tha, se wali muddat ate hi perfect continuous ban gaya.

5. Ali read the book yesterday.
Urdu: Ali ne kal kitaab parhi. Nishani yesterday hai aur verb ki dusri form read hai. Ye past simple hai.

6. Ali was reading a book when I entered the room.
Urdu: Jab main kamre me dakhil hua, Ali kitaab parh raha tha. Lamba kaam chal raha tha aur beech me chhota kaam aa gaya. Ye past continuous hai.

7. Ali had read the book before I came.
Urdu: Mere ane se pehle Ali kitaab parh chuka tha. Maazi ke do kaamo me pehle wale par had laga hai. Ye past perfect hai.

8. Ali had been reading the book for two hours.
Urdu: Ali do ghante se kitaab parh raha tha. Maazi me muddat ke sath chal raha kaam. Ye past perfect continuous hai.

9. Ali will read the book tomorrow.
Urdu: Ali kal kitaab parhega. Nishani tomorrow aur will ke sath pehli form. Ye future simple hai.

10. Ali will be reading a book at this time tomorrow.
Urdu: Ali kal is waqt kitaab parh raha hoga. Raha hoga wali awaz aur will be ke sath ing. Ye future continuous hai.

11. Ali will have read the book by Sunday.
Urdu: Ali itvaar tak kitaab parh chuka hoga. Nishani by hai aur will have ke sath teesri form. Ye future perfect hai.

12. Ali will have been reading the book for three hours by eight o clock.
Urdu: Ali aath baje tak teen ghante se kitaab parh raha hoga. Mustaqbil me muddat ke sath chal raha kaam. Ye future perfect continuous hai, barah me sab se lamba aur sab se kam ane wala tense.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Tense ki pehchan sirf tense wale sawal me kaam nahi ati. Voice change karna ho to pehle tense pehchanna parta hai, narration badalni ho to bhi pehle tense dekhna parta hai, aur jumla theek karne wale har sawal ki buniyad tense hi hai. Aik tarah se ye poori English grammar ki kunji hai. Jo student tense pehchan le, us ke liye agay ke sab darwaze khul jate hain.

Ab paper wale andaaz ke do jumle dekho.

1. He has been teaching in this school since 2015.
2. The train had left before we reached the station.

Pehla jumla present perfect continuous hai, since dekh kar pehchan lo. Dusra past perfect hai, station pohonchne se pehle train ja chuki thi.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab wo trick jo barah tenses ko aik hi nazar me pehchanwa degi. Isay Aakhir suno wali trick kehte hain. Jab bhi koi jumla samne aye, us ka Urdu tarjuma karo aur Urdu jumle ke aakhri alfaaz suno. Aakhir tumhe khud tense ka naam bata dega. Agar aakhir me ta hai, ti hai ya te hain aye to simple hai. Agar raha hai, rahi hai ya raha tha aye to continuous hai. Agar chuka hai, chuki thi ya chuka hoga aye to perfect hai. Aur agar raha hai ke sath se wali muddat bhi aye, do ghante se, subah se, to perfect continuous hai. Phir sirf waqt ka faisla baqi rehta hai ke baat haal ki hai, maazi ki ya mustaqbil ki, aur wo jumle ke isharay bata dete hain. Pehle aakhir suno, sorat pehchano. Phir waqt dekho, tense pehchano. Bas.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ab wo ghaltiyan dekho jo tense ke sawalo me Pakistani students karte hain.

Ghalat: I have met him yesterday.
Sahi: I met him yesterday.
Wajah: yesterday ate hi present perfect khatam ho jata hai. Guzra waqt saaf likha ho to hamesha past simple.

Ghalat: He is suffering from fever since Monday.
Sahi: He has been suffering from fever since Monday.
Wajah: since ke sath muddat se chal raha kaam present perfect continuous me ata hai, present continuous me nahi.

Ghalat: When I reached the station, the train left.
Sahi: When I reached the station, the train had left.
Wajah: train ka jana station pohonchne se pehle ka kaam hai. Maazi ke pehle wale kaam par had lagta hai.

Ghalat: I am knowing the answer.
Sahi: I know the answer.
Wajah: know, believe, like jaisay verb halat batate hain, chal raha kaam nahi. Ye continuous me nahi ate. Lesson 19 me hum ne ye baat seekhi thi.

Ghalat: She has wrote a letter.
Sahi: She has written a letter.
Wajah: has aur have ke baad hamesha teesri form ati hai. Wrote dusri hai, written teesri.

Ghalat: He did not went to school.
Sahi: He did not go to school.
Wajah: did ate hi verb apni pehli form me wapas chala jata hai. Did ne maazi ka bojh utha liya, verb ko dusri form ki zaroorat nahi.

Ghalat: I have been knowing him for years.
Sahi: I have known him for years.
Wajah: know ko muddat ke sath bhi continuous nahi banate. Has known hi sahi hai.

Jawab aur un ki wajah

1. Jawab C hai. Has been ke sath ing wali form aur for two hours ki muddat hai. Muddat ke sath chal raha kaam present perfect continuous hota hai.
2. Jawab B hai. Yesterday saaf likha hai, is liye sirf past simple ayega. Aakhir suno wali trick ke sath ye paper ka sab se dohraya jane wala qanoon hai.
3. Jawab C hai. Since 2019 muddat bata raha hai aur kaam ab bhi chal raha hai, is liye has been working.
4. Jawab B hai. Teacher ke ane ke waqt shor mach raha tha. Chal raha maazi ka kaam past continuous me ata hai, were making.
5. Jawab B hai. By next year future perfect ki pakki nishani hai. Will have built.
6. Jawab B hai. Mareez ka marna doctor ke ane se pehle ka kaam hai. Pehle wale kaam par had died ayega.
7. Jawab C hai. Just now ke sath abhi mukammal hua kaam present perfect me ata hai. Has finished.
8. Jawab B hai. Were ke sath ing wali form aur at that time ka ishara. Past continuous.
9. Jawab B hai. Daily aadat hai aur he, she, it wale khandan me verb par s lagta hai. Goes.
10. Jawab B hai. Does ate hi verb apni pehli form me ajata hai. Like sahi hai, likes ghalat.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye pehli tasveer aik bare ghari wali deewar ki ho jis par teen bare hisse hon. Maazi, haal aur mustaqbil. Har hisse ke andar chaar khane hon. Seedha sada kaam, chal raha kaam, mukammal kaam aur muddat se chal raha kaam. Har khane me Ali ki kahani ka aik jumla usi rang me likha ho. Student aik nazar dekh le to barah tenses ka poora naksha us ke zehen me beth jaye.

Doosri tasveer Aakhir suno wali trick ki ho. Aik student jumle ka Urdu tarjuma kar raha ho aur us ke kaan ke paas chaar awazein likhi hon. Ta hai to simple, raha hai to continuous, chuka hai to perfect, aur raha hai ke sath se aye to perfect continuous. Neeche aik line ho ke pehle aakhir suno, sorat pehchano, phir waqt dekho, tense pehchano.
