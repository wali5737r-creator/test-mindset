Lesson 32: Usage — Is Am Are, Has Have Had, Do Does Did
Teen Khandan. Is, has aur does kiske sath ate hain, Urdu matlab ke sath

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me agar kisi aik cheez par sab se zyada sawal bante hain to wo yehi chhote chhote helping verb hain. Is, am, are. Has, have, had. Do, does, did. Khali jagah me inhi me se aik lagana hota hai, jumla theek karna ho to inhi ki ghalti chhupi hoti hai, aur tense ka har sawal bhi inhi se shuru hota hai. Masla ye hai ke students inhe alag alag lafz samajh kar ratte hain, halanke ye sirf teen khandan hain. Jo student teen khandano ka hisaab samajh le, us ke liye English ka aadha darwaza khul jata hai. Lesson 3 me hum ne pronouns ke sath am, is, are ki pehli pehchan ki thi. Ab un teen khandano ko mukammal karte hain, Urdu matlab ke sath, taake paper me kabhi shak na ho.

Pehle teen khandan pehchano

Dekho, ye nau alfaaz asal me teen gharano ke log hain. Pehla gharana be wala hai, is, am, are, aur is ke maazi wale rishtedar was aur were. Is gharane ka Urdu matlab hai hai, hun, hain, tha, the. Ye halat aur pehchan batata hai. Wo doctor hai, main student hun. Dusra gharana have wala hai, has, have aur had. Is ka matlab hai ke paas hona, rakhna, aur perfect tense me chuka wala kaam bhi yehi gharana uthata hai. Teesra gharana do wala hai, do, does aur did. Ye gharana khud koi matlab nahi rakhta, ye sirf sawal aur nafi banane ke liye bulaya jata hai, aur as a main verb is ka matlab hota hai karna. Teen gharane zehen me bitha lo, ab har aik ka hisaab alag alag dekhte hain.

Ab pehla sawal jo har khandan me poocha jata hai. Kiske sath konsa? Is ka jawab aik hi hai aur teeno khandano par lagoo hota hai. He, she, it aur koi aik naam, in ke sath khaas form ata hai. Baqi sab ke sath, I, we, you, they aur zyada cheezon ke sath, aam form ata hai. Be wale gharane me khaas form is hai aur aam form are hai, bas I beech me apna alag am rakhta hai. Have wale gharane me khaas form has hai aur aam form have hai. Do wale gharane me khaas form does hai aur aam form do hai. Aur maazi me aakar ye farq ghat jata hai. Had sab ke sath aik hi ata hai, did bhi sab ke sath aik hi ata hai. Bas be wale gharane me was aik ke sath aur were zyada ke sath, ye chhota sa farq reh jata hai.

Ab has been aur have been wali baat, jo paper ki pasandida hai. Jab has ya have ke baad been aye to do matlab nikalte hain. Aik matlab hai muddat se chal rahi halat. He has been ill since Monday. Wo Peer se beemar hai. Been ke baad ing wali form ajaye to kaam muddat se chal raha hota hai, jaisa Lesson 31 me parha tha. He has been teaching for ten years. Dusra matlab hai ja kar ana ya ja chuka hona, aur yahan been aur gone ka farq paper bohat parkhta hai. He has been to Lahore ka matlab hai wo Lahore ja kar wapas aa chuka hai. He has gone to Lahore ka matlab hai wo Lahore gaya hai aur abhi wahin hai, wapas nahi aya. Been matlab ja aya, gone matlab gaya aur abhi gaya hua hai. Ye farq zehen me nikaal kar rakh lo.

Ab do wale gharane ki khaas baat samajh lo, kyunke ye do chehray rakhta hai. Kabhi ye main verb hota hai, khud kaam karta hai. He does his homework daily. Yahan does ka matlab hai karta hai. She did her work. Aur kabhi ye sirf madadgar hota hai, sawal aur nafi banane ke liye. Does he play cricket? He does not play cricket. Yahan does khud koi kaam nahi kar raha, sirf asal verb play ki madad kar raha hai. Aur jab do madadgar ban kar ata hai to aik qanoon lazmi yad rakho. Does aur did apne sath asal verb ko us ki pehli form me lete hain. Does he go, did he go. Go par na s lagega, na went banega. Maazi ka aur s wala sara bojh does aur did ne khud utha liya hota hai.

Has wale gharane ki bhi aik do-chehri sorat hai. Jab has aur have ke baad teesri form aye to ye perfect tense banate hain. She has finished. Aur jab in ke baad koi cheez aye to ye rakhne ka matlab dete hain. She has a car. She has finished me has madadgar hai, she has a car me has khud asal verb hai. Aur is dusri sorat me sawal do se banta hai. Does she have a car? Ye jumla paper me aksar ata hai aur aksar galat kiya jata hai.

Board par qanoon Angrezi me

Board par ustad ye lines likhta hai. There are three families: be, have and do. With he, she, it and a singular name, use is, has and does. With I, we, you and they, use have and do, and am or are for be. In the past, had and did are the same for every subject. After has and have, the main verb is always in its third form. After do, does and did, the main verb is always in its first form. Aur aakhri line: has been to a place means went and returned; has gone to a place means went and is still there.

Misaalein board se

Ab board par misaalein dekho. Har misaal ke sath us ki wajah bhi samjho.

1. She is a doctor.
Is aya kyunke she khaas khandan me hai. Urdu matlab hai wo doctor hai.

2. I am a student of this college.
I ke sath hamesha am ata hai. Ye be wale gharane ka woh wahid farq hai jo ratna parta hai.

3. They have two sons.
They ke sath have aya. Matlab hai un ke do bete hain, yani wo rakhte hain.

4. He has been ill since Monday.
Has ke baad been aur since ke sath muddat. Matlab hai wo Peer se beemar hai, halat chal rahi hai.

5. My father has gone to Karachi.
Gone aya hai, matlab hai wo Karachi gaye hain aur abhi wahin hain, wapas nahi aye.

6. My father has been to Karachi many times.
Been aya hai, matlab hai wo kayi dafa Karachi ja kar wapas aa chuke hain. Gone aur been ka yahi farq paper me ata hai.

7. Does your brother work in a bank?
Does madadgar ban kar aya hai, is liye asal verb work apni pehli form me hai. Works nahi likha.

8. He does his homework in the evening.
Yahan does khud main verb hai, matlab karta hai. Pichhli misaal se farq dekh lo, wahan does sirf madadgar tha.

9. Did you sleep well last night?
Did sab ke sath aik hi ata hai aur us ke baad pehli form sleep hai. Slept nahi aya.

10. We had a small house in our village last year.
Had have wale gharane ki maazi shakal hai, sab ke sath aik jaisi. Matlab hai hamare paas tha.

11. She has written three letters.
Has ke baad teesri form written hai. Ye perfect tense hai, has yahan rakhne wala matlab nahi de raha.

12. Does she have a car?
Has wali dusri sorat ka sawal do se banta hai aur have apni pehli form me rehta hai. Ye jumla paper ka pasandida hai.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Rozmarra ki har baat inhi teen khandano se banti hai. Tum kya karte ho, us ke paas kya hai, wo kahan tha, ye sawal poochna ho ya nafi karni ho, har jagah yehi alfaaz kaam ate hain. Daftar ki darkhwast ho ya dost se guftagu, is, has aur does ka sahi mel hi tumhari English ko sahi ya ghalat banata hai. Paper me to ye haal hai ke har dusra sawal inhi ke gird ghoomta hai.

Ab paper wale andaaz ke do jumle dekho.

1. He has been serving in this department for fifteen years.
2. Does the committee have any objection to this proposal?

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab wo trick jo teen khandano ko aik line me bandh degi. Isay S wale teen dost kehte hain. Gaur karo, he, she, it ke sath ane wale teeno khaas formon me s ata hai. Is, has, does. Teeno me s ki awaz hai. Aur ye teen dost sirf teen mehmaano ke ghar jate hain, he, she aur it ke ghar, ya kisi aik naam ke ghar. Baqi sab ke darwaze par aam form khare hain, are, have aur do, jin me s nahi hai. To jab bhi shak ho, pehle dekho subject he, she, it hai ya koi aik naam. Hai to s wala dost bulao, is, has ya does. Nahi hai to baghair s wala form lagao, are, have ya do. Aur maazi me to jhagra hi khatam, had aur did har ghar me aik jaise jate hain. Bas ye yaad rakho, s wale teen dost sirf he, she, it ke dost hain.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ab wo ghaltiyan dekho jo in teen khandano me Pakistani students karte hain.

Ghalat: Does he goes to school?
Sahi: Does he go to school?
Wajah: does ne s wala bojh khud utha liya hai. Us ke baad verb apni pehli form me ayega, goes nahi, go.

Ghalat: She has went to Lahore.
Sahi: She has gone to Lahore.
Wajah: has ke baad teesri form ati hai. Went dusri form hai, gone teesri.

Ghalat: Did you went to the bazaar?
Sahi: Did you go to the bazaar?
Wajah: did ke baad hamesha pehli form. Did khud maazi hai, verb ko maazi banane ki zaroorat nahi.

Ghalat: He do not have a pen.
Sahi: He does not have a pen.
Wajah: he ke sath do wala khaas form does ata hai, do nahi. S wale teen dost wali baat yaad hai na?

Ghalat: I am agree with you.
Sahi: I agree with you.
Wajah: agree khud aik verb hai, us ke aage am lagana ghalti hai. Ye ghalti Urdu ke "main raazi hun" wale asar se hoti hai.

Ghalat: She is having two brothers.
Sahi: She has two brothers.
Wajah: rakhne ke matlab me have ko continuous me nahi rakhte. Has two brothers hi sahi hai.

Ghalat: He has been to Karachi yesterday.
Sahi: He went to Karachi yesterday.
Wajah: yesterday ate hi perfect khatam, past simple ajata hai. Yehi qanoon Lesson 31 me parha tha.

Ghalat: Does she has a car?
Sahi: Does she have a car?
Wajah: does ke baad verb pehli form me ata hai, aur yahan asal verb have hai. Has nahi, have ayega.

Jawab aur un ki wajah

1. Jawab B hai, has. He ke sath have wale gharane ka khaas form has ata hai, aur matlab hai us ke paas nayi car hai.
2. Jawab C hai, Does. Sawal banane ke liye do wala gharana ata hai aur he ke sath does. Us ke baad work pehli form me hai.
3. Jawab B hai, has. Been aur since wali muddat present perfect ki pehchan hai, aur she ke sath has ata hai.
4. Jawab B hai. Does ke baad verb pehli form me ata hai, play. Plays wala option is lesson ka sab se mashhoor trap hai.
5. Jawab D hai, have. They aam khandan me hai, is ke sath has nahi, have ata hai.
6. Jawab C hai, Has. Finished teesri form hai, ye perfect tense ka sawal hai, is liye has. Does ke sath finished nahi ajata.
7. Jawab B hai. Did ke baad pehli form go ati hai. Went aur gone dono yahan ghalat hain.
8. Jawab C hai, does. Nafi banane ke liye do wala gharana ata hai aur he ke sath does not. Like apni pehli form me raha.
9. Jawab B hai, has been. Wo ja kar usi din wapas aa gaya, ye been wala matlab hai. Gone ka matlab hota hai gaya aur abhi wahin hai.
10. Jawab C hai. She ke sath has, aur has ke baad teesri form gone. Dono qanoon aik sath lagay hain.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye pehli tasveer teen gharon wali ho. Aik gali me teen ghar bane hon. Pehle ghar ke darwaze par Be likha ho aur andar is, am, are bethe hon. Dusre ghar par Have likha ho aur andar has, have, had hon. Teesre ghar par Do likha ho aur andar do, does, did hon. Gali me he, she, it teen mehmaan khare hon aur un ki taraf s wale teen dost, is, has aur does, barh rahe hon. Student aik nazar me samajh jaye ke konsa lafz kis ghar ka hai aur kis ke sath jata hai.

Doosri tasveer been aur gone ke farq ki ho. Aik aadmi Lahore jane wale raste par khara ho. Aik teer Lahore ki taraf ja raha ho jis par gone likha ho aur aadmi abhi wahin khara dikhai de. Dusra teer wapas aa raha ho jis par been likha ho aur aadmi ghar ke darwaze par khara ho. Neeche aik line ho ke been matlab ja aya, gone matlab gaya aur abhi gaya hua hai.
