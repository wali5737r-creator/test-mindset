Lesson 33: Usage — Should, Could, Would, May, Might, Must
Chhe Madadgar. Har lafz ka Urdu chehra, mashwara, ijazat, imkan aur lazmiat

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me modal verbs ke sawal har saal ate hain. Should, could, would, may, might, must. Khali jagah me in me se sahi wala lagana hota hai, aur ghalti is liye hoti hai ke in sab ke peechay Urdu ke mukhtalif matlab chhupe hote hain. Student angrezi jumla parhta hai lekin zehen me Urdu matlab nahi ban pata, to andaza lag jata hai. Is lesson ka kaam ye hai ke har modal ko us ka Urdu chehra de diya jaye. Jab tumhe ye pata chal jaye ke should matlab chahiye, might matlab shayad lekin ummeed kam, must matlab lazmi, to paper ka sawal andaze ka nahi rehta, pehchan ka ban jata hai. Lesson 15 me hum ne can, must aur have to ki buniyad rakhi thi. Ab usi khandan ke baqi afrad ko gehraai se dekhte hain.

Pehle khandan ka usool samajh lo

Ye chhe alfaaz modal kehlate hain aur in sab ka aik mushtarak qanoon hai jo kabhi nahi tootta. In ke baad hamesha verb ki pehli form ati hai, bilkul saadi, baghair to ke aur baghair s ke. He should go, he must go, she may come. Kabhi he should to go nahi hota aur kabhi he must goes nahi hota. Modal khud itna taqatwar hai ke verb ko apni asal shakal me rehne deta hai. Ye qanoon zehen me bitha lo, phir har modal ko alag alag pehchano.

Pehla hai should. Is ka Urdu chehra hai chahiye. Ye mashwara deta hai aur farz ki narmi wali sorat batata hai. You should respect your teachers. Tumhe apne ustadon ki izzat karni chahiye. We should keep our city clean. Should ki nafi hai should not, matlab nahi karna chahiye. You should not waste time. Aur jab should ke baad have ke sath teesri form ajaye to ye maazi par afsos ban jata hai. You should have told me. Tumhe mujhe bata dena chahiye tha, lekin bataya nahi. Ye sorat bhi paper me ati hai.

Dusra hai could. Is ka pehla chehra hai sakta tha, ye can ki maazi shakal hai. When he was young, he could run ten miles. Jawani me wo das meel daur sakta tha. Lekin khaas baat ye hai ke could sirf maazi nahi hai. Ye aaj ki narm darkhwast me bhi ata hai. Could you please help me? Kya aap meri madad kar sakte hain? Can se zyada narmi could me hai. Paper me jab darkhwast ka lehja narm ho to jawab could hota hai, aur jab guzri hui salahiyat ki baat ho tab bhi could hota hai.

Teesra hai would. Ye khandan ka sab se zyada chehron wala lafz hai. Pehla chehra narmi aur peshkash ka hai. Would you like a cup of tea? Kya aap aik cup chai piyenge? Would you please open the window? Dusra chehra khwahish ka hai. I would like to meet him. Main us se milna chahunga. Aur teesra chehra wo hai jo paper sab se zyada parkhta hai, maazi ki aadat. Jab would kisi guzre zamane ki kahani me aye to is ka matlab hota hai kiya karta tha, jaya karta tha. In those days, we would go to the river every evening. Un dino hum har sham darya par jaya karte thay. Yahan would ka mustaqbil se koi lena dena nahi, ye maazi ki aadat hai. Ye pehchan bohat number deti hai.

Chautha aur paanchwa hain may aur might, ye dono imkan wale hain lekin in ki taqat barabar nahi. May ka pehla kaam ijazat hai. May I come in, sir? Kya main andar aa sakta hun? Ye izzat wali ijazat hai, daftar aur class ka andaaz. May ka dusra kaam imkan hai, aur ye imkan kuch mazboot hota hai. It may rain today. Ho sakta hai aaj barish ho, ummeed achhi khasi hai. May dua me bhi ata hai. May you succeed in life. Allah kare tum kamyab ho. Ab might ko dekho. Might may ka chhota bhai hai, imkan waisa hi hai lekin ummeed kam hai. He is very ill; he might not recover. Wo sakht beemar hai, shayad theek na ho paye. Aur might kabhi kabhi may ki maazi sorat me bhi ata hai, jab kahani maazi me chal rahi ho. He said that he might come. Darja bandi yaad rakho. Might kamzor ummeed, may mazboot ummeed.

Chhetha hai must. Is ka Urdu chehra hai lazmi, zaroor, har haal me. Ye farz aur sakht qanoon batata hai. Students must wear the uniform. Talib ilmo ko uniform pehenna lazmi hai. You must stop here. Must ki nafi aik alag hi cheez hai. Must not ka matlab hai hargiz nahi, sakht mana hai. You must not smoke in the hospital. Ye Lesson 15 wali baat hai ke must not aur do not have to me zameen asman ka farq hai. Must not matlab karna mana hai, do not have to matlab karne ki zaroorat nahi. Must ka aik dusra chehra bhi hai jo paper parkhta hai, pakka yakeen. He has worked all day; he must be tired. Us ne saara din kaam kiya, wo zaroor thaka hua hoga. Yahan must farz nahi, andaze ka yakeen hai.

Aur aik aakhri baat. In sab modalon ki maazi sorat aik hi tareeqe se banti hai. Modal ke baad have aur teesri form. Should have gone, could have helped, must have reached. He must have reached home by now. Wo ab tak ghar pohonch chuka hoga. Ye sorat imkan aur yakeen ko maazi me le jati hai aur one paper me kabhi kabhi ati hai, is liye pehchan rakh lo.

Board par qanoon Angrezi me

Board par ustad har modal ki pehchan yun likhta hai. Should means duty and advice. Could is the past of can and also a polite request. Would is used for polite offers and requests, and for past habits. May is used for permission, for a strong possibility, and in prayers. Might shows a weaker possibility than may. Must shows a strong duty, and it also shows a strong guess about something. Aur sab se aham line: after every modal, the verb is always used in its first form, without to and without s.

Misaalein board se

Ab board par misaalein dekho. Har misaal ke sath us modal ka Urdu chehra bhi likha hai.

1. You should obey your parents.
Should hai, chehra chahiye. Tumhe apne walidain ka kehna manna chahiye. Ye mashwara aur farz hai.

2. He could swim very well in his childhood.
Could hai, chehra sakta tha. Wo bachpan me bohat achha tair sakta tha. Ye maazi ki salahiyat hai.

3. Could you please lend me your pen?
Could hai lekin yahan maazi nahi, narm darkhwast hai. Lehja bata raha hai ke ye aaj ki baat hai.

4. Would you like to visit our village?
Would hai, chehra narmi wali peshkash. Kya aap hamare gaon ana pasand karenge?

5. Every summer, we would bathe in the canal.
Would hai, chehra jaya karte thay wala. Ye maazi ki aadat hai, is lesson ka sab se khaas jumla. Would ko yahan mustaqbil samajhna ghalti hogi.

6. May I use your phone, please?
May hai, chehra izzat wali ijazat. Daftar aur class me ijazat is lafz se mangi jati hai.

7. It may rain in the evening.
May hai, imkan mazboot hai. Ho sakta hai sham ko barish ho, ummeed achhi hai.

8. He has missed the bus; he might be late.
Might hai, imkan kamzor hai. Shayad use dair ho jaye, ummeed kam hai is liye might aya.

9. All candidates must bring their identity cards.
Must hai, chehra lazmi. Har ummeedwar ko shanakhti card lana har haal me zaroori hai.

10. You must not park your car here.
Must not hai, chehra sakht mana hai. Yahan gari khari karna hargiz allowed nahi.

11. She has won the first prize; she must be very happy.
Must hai, chehra pakka yakeen. Wo zaroor bohat khush hogi. Yahan farz wala matlab nahi, andaze ka yakeen hai.

12. May you live long!
May hai, chehra dua. Allah kare tum lambi umar pao. Dua wale jumle me may jumle ke shuru me ata hai.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Daftar ki angrezi in modalon se bhari hoti hai. Darkhwast me narmi chahiye to could aur would, qanoon ki sakhti batani ho to must, mashwara dena ho to should. Rozmarra me bhi yehi haal hai. Ijazat mangni ho to may, kisi cheez ki ummeed batani ho to may ya might. Jo aadmi in ke Urdu chehre pehchanta hai, wo na sirf paper me sahi option chunta hai, wo asal zindagi me bhi lehje ke mutabiq lafz chunta hai.

Ab paper wale andaaz ke do jumle dekho.

1. Candidates must not bring mobile phones into the examination hall.
2. He said that he might visit us next week.

Pehle jumle me must not sakht manahi hai. Dusre me might kamzor imkan hai aur kahani maazi me hai, is liye may ki jagah might aya.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab wo trick jo is poore khandan ko zehen me bitha degi. Har modal ko us ke Urdu chehre se pehchano, English lafz se nahi. Jab jumla samne aye to pehle zehen me Urdu me socho ke kehne wala kya keh raha hai. Chahiye bol raha hai to should. Sakta tha bol raha hai to could. Narmi se peshkash kar raha hai ya kiya karta tha bol raha hai to would. Ijazat mang raha hai ya ummeed mazboot hai to may. Ummeed kamzor hai to might. Lazmi bol raha hai ya pakka yakeen dikha raha hai to must. Aur imkan wale do bhaiyon ko is tasveer se yaad rakho. May bara bhai hai, qad lamba, ummeed zyada. Might chhota bhai hai, qad chhota, ummeed kam. Dono ka kaam aik, taqat ka farq hai. Bas Urdu chehra pehchano, modal khud chun jayega.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ab wo ghaltiyan dekho jo modal wale sawalo me hoti hain.

Ghalat: He should to work hard.
Sahi: He should work hard.
Wajah: modal ke baad to kabhi nahi ata. Verb apni saadi pehli form me rehta hai.

Ghalat: May I coming in, sir?
Sahi: May I come in, sir?
Wajah: may ke baad pehli form come ayega, coming nahi. Modal ke baad ing wali form nahi ati.

Ghalat: He might comes tomorrow.
Sahi: He might come tomorrow.
Wajah: modal ke baad verb par s nahi lagta, chahe subject he hi kyun na ho. S wala qanoon modal ke agay khatam ho jata hai.

Ghalat: She musts follow the rules.
Sahi: She must follow the rules.
Wajah: must khud kabhi nahi badalta. Is par s lagana doहरी ghalti hai.

Ghalat: You must not have to bring the file. Jab matlab ho ke file lane ki zaroorat nahi hai.
Sahi: You do not have to bring the file.
Wajah: must not ka matlab hai lana mana hai, aur do not have to ka matlab hai lane ki zaroorat nahi. Matlab ulta ho jata hai, paper yehi parkhta hai.

Ghalat: In his youth, he will go to the fields every morning. Jab matlab ho ke wo jaya karta tha.
Sahi: In his youth, he would go to the fields every morning.
Wajah: maazi ki aadat ke liye would ata hai, will nahi. Will mustaqbil ka hai, kahani maazi ki chal rahi hai.

Ghalat: Could you please helps me?
Sahi: Could you please help me?
Wajah: could ke baad bhi verb pehli form me ayega. Darkhwast narm hai lekin qanoon wohi hai.

Jawab aur un ki wajah

1. Jawab B hai, should. Mashwara aur farz ki baat hai, Urdu chehra hai chahiye.
2. Jawab C hai, May. Izzat wali ijazat may se mangi jati hai. Ye class aur daftar ka andaaz hai.
3. Jawab B hai, could. Maazi ki salahiyat hai, sakta tha. Can aaj ki baat hai, kahani bachpan ki hai.
4. Jawab C hai, might. Kehne wala khud keh raha hai ke use bilkul yakeen nahi, ummeed kamzor hai. Kamzor ummeed par chhota bhai might ata hai.
5. Jawab B hai, must. Hazri lazmi hai, koi narmi nahi. Lazmiat par must ata hai.
6. Jawab D hai, Would. Peshkash narmi se hoti hai, would you like paper ka mashhoor jumla hai.
7. Jawab B hai. Modal ke baad verb saadi pehli form me ata hai. Na to, na s, na ing.
8. Jawab B hai, would. Kahani guzre dino ki hai aur ye maazi ki aadat hai, bataya karte thay. Will yahan bilkul ghalat hota.
9. Jawab B hai, must. Subah se kuch nahi khaya, to pakka yakeen hai ke wo bhooka hoga. Ye must ka yakeen wala chehra hai, farz wala nahi.
10. Jawab B hai. Lane ki zaroorat nahi, is ke liye do not have to ata hai. Must not ka matlab hota hai lana mana hai, jo yahan ulta matlab de deta.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye pehli tasveer do bhaiyon wali ho. Aik bara bhai khara ho jis par May likha ho aur us ke sath ummeed ka bara sa suraj ho. Us ke sath chhota bhai khara ho jis par Might likha ho aur us ke sath chhota sa badal ho. Neeche aik line ho ke may bara bhai, ummeed zyada, might chhota bhai, ummeed kam. Student ye tasveer dekh le to imkan wale sawal me kabhi uljhega nahi.

Doosri tasveer har modal ke Urdu chehre wali aik chart ho. Chhe khane hon aur har khane me modal ke neeche us ka Urdu chehra bara sa likha ho. Should ke neeche chahiye, could ke neeche sakta tha, would ke neeche narmi aur kiya karta tha, may ke neeche ijazat aur mazboot ummeed, might ke neeche kamzor ummeed, must ke neeche lazmi. Upar aik line ho ke pehle Urdu chehra pehchano, modal khud chun jayega.
