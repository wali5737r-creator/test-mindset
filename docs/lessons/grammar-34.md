Lesson 34: Usage — Prepositions At, By, For, Of, From, With, On, In
Aath Prepositions. Har lafz ke kayi chehre, Urdu matlab aur jumlo ke sath

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me preposition ka sawal hamesha hota hai, aur aksar aik nahi, kayi hote hain. Khali jagah se pehle koi mashhoor jumla de diya jata hai aur chaar prepositions me se aik chunna hota hai. Ye sawal ratne se hal nahi hote, kyunke har preposition ke kayi matlab hain aur jumla dekh kar chehra pehchanna parta hai. At, by, for, of, from, with, on, in. Ye aath lafz chhote hain lekin English ka aadha istemal inhi par khara hai. Lesson 5 me hum ne jagah wali prepositions seekhi theen aur Lesson 21 me waqt ke sath at, on, in ka qanoon parha tha. Ab in aath lafzon ke tamam aham chehre aik jagah jama karte hain, Urdu matlab ke sath, taake paper ka koi preposition tumhe dhoka na de sake.

Pehle at ko dekho

At ka pehla chehra waqt ka hai. Ghari ke waqt par at ata hai. At five o clock, at noon, at night. At night wala jumla khaas yaad rakho, kyunke raat ke sath in nahi, at ata hai. At ka dusra chehra jagah ka nuqta hai. Jab jagah ko aik nuqte ki tarah dekho to at. At home, at the door, at the station, at the bus stop. Sheher bara ho to us ke andar rehne par in ata hai, lekin kisi khaas jagah ko nuqta bana lo to at. He lives in Lahore, lekin he is waiting at the gate. Aur at ke sath kuch pakke jumle hain jo ratne hi parte hain. Good at, bad at, at last, at once, at the end. He is good at English.

Ab by ko dekho, is ke chehre sab se zyada hain

By ka pehla chehra paas wala hai. Come and sit by me. Mere paas betho. Dusra chehra zariye wala hai. Safar ka zariya ho to by ata hai. By bus, by train, by hand, by post. Teesra chehra wo hai jo Lesson 30 me parha tha, passive me karne wale ke sath by ata hai. The letter was written by Ahmed. Chautha chehra waqt ki had wala hai, aur ye paper bohat parkhta hai. By Friday ka matlab hai Jummay tak, Jummay se pehle pehle kaam ho jana chahiye. Submit the form by Monday. By aur until me ye farq hai ke by had batata hai, kaam us se pehle mukammal ho. Aur by the way aik alag muhawara hai, matlab hai wese aik baat aur.

Ab for ko dekho

For ka pehla chehra liye wala hai. This gift is for you. Ye tohfa tumhare liye hai. Dusra chehra maqsad wala hai. He went out for a walk. Sair ke liye gaya. Teesra chehra sab se aham hai, muddat wala. Jab batana ho ke koi halat kitni muddat se hai to for ata hai. He has been ill for three days. Teen din se. Aur yahin for aur since ka wo farq ata hai jo har paper me poocha jata hai. Since waqt ka wo nuqta batata hai jahan se kaam shuru hua. Since Monday, since 2020, since morning. For us puri muddat ko batata hai jo guzar gayi. For three days, for two years. Aik misal se bitha lo. Wo Peer se beemar hai, ye since Monday hai. Wo teen din se beemar hai, ye for three days hai. Urdu me dono me se ata hai, is liye Urdu wala student confuse hota hai, lekin English me nuqta ho to since aur muddat ho to for. Pakke jumle bhi yaad rakho. For sale, for example, wait for, ask for.

Ab of ko dekho

Of ka sab se bara chehra ka aur ki wala hai, milkiyat aur talluq batata hai. The capital of Pakistan is Islamabad. Pakistan ka darul hukumat. The leg of the table. Dusra cheez miqdar wala hai. A cup of tea, a piece of advice, a bottle of water. Teesra chehra made of wala hai, jo neeche from ke sath samjhaya jayega. Aur of ke pakke jumle bohat hain jo paper me ate hain. Afraid of, fond of, proud of, full of, because of, in front of. Wo darasal pura khandan hai jo of ke gird ghumta hai.

Ab from ko dekho

From ka asal chehra se wala hai, judai aur shuruaat batata hai. He comes from Multan. Wo Multan se hai. The train leaves from Lahore. From Monday, from today. Ab wo aham farq jo of aur from ko alag karta hai, made wala farq. Jab koi cheez kisi dusri cheez se bane aur banne ke baad asal cheez apni shakal me dikhai de to made of ata hai. The table is made of wood. Meiz lakri ki hai aur lakri ab bhi lakri dikhai de rahi hai. Lekin jab banne ke amal me asal cheez ki shakal hi badal jaye to made from ata hai. Butter is made from milk. Makkhan doodh se banta hai lekin bane hue makkhan me doodh kahin nazar nahi ata. Shakal wohi rahi to of, shakal badal gayi to from. Bas ye pehchan hai.

Ab with ko dekho

With ka pehla chehra sath wala hai. He came with his friends. Dusra chehra auzaar wala hai, jis cheez se kaam kiya jaye. He cut the apple with a knife. Chaku se kata. Teesra chehra wo khaas jumle hain jahan with ke sath wala farq paper parkhta hai. Shakhs se raazi hona ho to agree with ata hai. I agree with you. Main tum se mutafiq hun. Lekin kisi baat, tajweez ya plan par raazi hona ho to agree to ata hai. The committee agreed to the plan. Shakhs ke sath with, baat ke sath to. Aur with ke pakke jumle bhi hain. Angry with, pleased with, filled with, busy with.

Ab on aur in ko dekho

On ka pehla chehra upar lekin sath laga hua hai. The book is on the table. Kitaab meiz ke upar hai aur satah ko chhu rahi hai. Dusra chehra din wala hai. On Monday, on Sunday morning, on the first of January. Teesra chehra pakke jumlo wala hai. Depend on, matlab bharosa. Children depend on their parents. On foot, matlab paidal, by foot kehna ghalti hai. Aur on ka sab se mashhoor joda hai on time aur in time ka farq. On time ka matlab hai bilkul muqarrar waqt par, na pehle na baad. The train arrived on time. In time ka matlab hai waqt ke andar, dair hone se pehle pohonch jana. We reached the station in time to catch the train. Aik waqt par hai, dusra waqt rehte hai.

In ka pehla chehra andar wala hai. The books are in the bag. Dusra chehra bare waqt wala hai. Mahina ho to in, saal ho to in, din ka hissa ho to in. In June, in 2025, in the morning, in the evening. Lekin raat ke sath at night ata hai, ye us ka khaas istisna hai. Teesra chehra muddat ke baad wala hai, mustaqbil ki taraf. He will return in two days. Do din me, yani do din ke baad. Aur in ke pakke jumle hain. Interested in, in time, in the end, in Urdu, in ink. Interested ke sath hamesha in ata hai, ye paper me bohat ata hai.

Board par qanoon Angrezi me

Board par ustad waqt wali seedhi yun likhta hai. Use at with clock time, on with days and dates, and in with months, years and parts of the day. At five o clock, on Monday, in June. Phir baqi chehre. By shows the doer in the passive voice, the means of travel, and a deadline. For shows a purpose and a length of time; since shows the starting point. Made of is used when the material is still seen; made from is used when the material has changed. Agree with a person, but agree to a plan. On time means at the fixed time; in time means before it is too late.

Misaalein board se

Ab board par misaalein dekho. Har misaal ke sath wajah bhi samjho.

1. The meeting will start at 9 o clock.
At aya kyunke ghari ka waqt hai. Waqt wali seedhi ka pehla darja.

2. He was standing at the door.
Jagah ko nuqta bana kar dekha hai, is liye at. Darwaza sheher nahi, aik nuqta hai.

3. This poem was written by Mir Taqi Mir.
Passive jumla hai, karne wale ke sath by aya hai. Lesson 30 wala qanoon.

4. Please send your application by Saturday.
By had wala hai. Matlab hai Haftay tak, us se pehle pehle darkhwast pohonch jani chahiye.

5. She travelled to Karachi by train.
Safar ka zariya hai, is liye by. Lekin yaad rakho, paidal ke liye by nahi, on foot ata hai.

6. He has been absent for a week.
For aya kyunke muddat hai, pura aik hafta. Nuqta hota to since ata.

7. She has been absent since Monday.
Ab since aya kyunke shuruaat ka nuqta diya hai, Peer ka din. Pichhli misaal se joda bana kar yaad rakho.

8. Butter is made from milk.
From aya kyunke doodh makkhan ban kar apni shakal kho deta hai. Shakal badli to from.

9. This chair is made of wood.
Of aya kyunke lakri kursi me ab bhi lakri hi dikhai de rahi hai. Shakal wohi rahi to of.

10. I agree with your opinion, but the board agreed to the proposal.
Aik hi jumle me dono chehre hain. Shakhs aur us ki raye ke sath with, tajweez ke sath to.

11. Children depend on their parents.
Depend ke sath hamesha on ata hai. Ye pakka jumla hai, depend of ya depend with nahi hota.

12. The bus arrived on time, and we reached home in time for dinner.
On time matlab muqarrar waqt par, in time matlab waqt rehte. Dono ka farq aik hi jumle me dekh lo.

13. He is interested in history.
Interested ke sath in pakka hai. Ye paper ke pasandida jumlo me se hai.

14. She goes to her office on foot.
Paidal ke liye on foot ata hai. By foot likhna Pakistani students ki mashhoor ghalti hai.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Daftar ka koi khat uthao, naukri ki darkhwast dekho, akhbar ki khabar parho, har dusre jumle me inhi aath me se koi khara hoga. Waqt batana ho to at, on, in ki seedhi, had batani ho to by, muddat batani ho to for aur since, zariya batana ho to by, bharosa batana ho to depend on. Ye aath lafz seekh lo to tumhari English ka roz ka aadha bojh hal ho jayega, aur paper me preposition wale sawal tohfe ban jayenge.

Ab paper wale andaaz ke do jumle dekho.

1. He has been working in this office since 2018 and is very popular with his colleagues.
2. The last date for the submission of the form is fixed, and every candidate must submit it by that date.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab do tricks jo is lesson ko hamesha ke liye bitha dengi. Pehli trick waqt wali seedhi hai. Ghari ka waqt sab se chhota hai, us par at. Din us se bara hai, us par on. Mahina aur saal sab se bare hain, un par in. At ghari, on din, in mahina. Chhota waqt chhota lafz, bara waqt bara lafz. Dusri trick made wali hai. Koi cheez bane to apni ankhon se dekho ke asal material ab bhi dikhai de raha hai ya nahi. Kursi me lakri dikhai de rahi hai to made of. Makkhan me doodh kahin nahi dikhta to made from. Shakal wohi to of, shakal badli to from. Bas ye do pehchanein yaad rakho, aath me se paanch prepositions ke aadhe sawal yahin hal ho jayenge.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ab wo ghaltiyan dekho jo preposition wale sawalo me hoti hain.

Ghalat: He travels in the bus.
Sahi: He travels on the bus.
Wajah: bus, train aur jahaz par sawar hone ke liye on ata hai. In the car theek hai kyunke car chhoti aur band hai, lekin bus par on lagta hai.

Ghalat: We reached at Lahore at night.
Sahi: We reached Lahore at night.
Wajah: reach ke baad at nahi ata. Reach khud pohonchna hai, us ke baad seedha jagah ka naam ata hai.

Ghalat: He has been ill since three days.
Sahi: He has been ill for three days.
Wajah: three days muddat hai, nuqta nahi. Muddat par for, nuqte par since.

Ghalat: Wine is made of grapes.
Sahi: Wine is made from grapes.
Wajah: angoor apni shakal kho dete hain. Shakal badal jaye to from ata hai, of nahi.

Ghalat: I agree with this proposal.
Sahi: I agree to this proposal.
Wajah: tajweez aur plan ke sath to ata hai. With sirf shakhs aur us ki raye ke sath ata hai.

Ghalat: She is good in English.
Sahi: She is good at English.
Wajah: good aur bad ke sath at pakka hai. Good in kehna Urdu ke asar ki ghalti hai.

Ghalat: He depends of his father.
Sahi: He depends on his father.
Wajah: depend ke sath on hamesha ata hai. Ye pura jumla aik sath ratna parta hai.

Ghalat: They went to school by foot.
Sahi: They went to school on foot.
Wajah: paidal safar ke liye on foot hi sahi hai. By safar ke zariye ke liye hai, lekin foot is ka khaas istisna hai.

Jawab aur un ki wajah

1. Jawab C hai, at. Ghari ka waqt hai, waqt wali seedhi ka pehla darja. At ghari, on din, in mahina.
2. Jawab B hai, since. 2015 shuruaat ka nuqta hai. Nuqte par since, muddat par for.
3. Jawab C hai, for. Three days muddat hai. Pichhle sawal ka ulta chehra hai, dono ko joda bana kar yaad rakho.
4. Jawab B hai, of. Angoothi me sona ab bhi sona dikhai de raha hai. Shakal wohi rahi to made of.
5. Jawab C hai, from. Lakri kagaz ban kar apni shakal kho deti hai. Shakal badli to made from.
6. Jawab B hai, by. Had batayi gayi hai, das baje tak har haal me pohonchna hai. At 10 ka matlab hota theek das baje, by ka matlab das se pehle pehle.
7. Jawab B hai. Plan aur tajweez ke sath agree to ata hai. Shakhs hota to agree with tha.
8. Jawab B hai, at. Good aur weak jis mazmoon me hon, us ke sath at pakka hai. Weak at mathematics.
9. Jawab B hai. Train muqarrar waqt par ayi, is ke liye on time ata hai. In time ka matlab hota hai dair hone se pehle pohonchna.
10. Jawab C hai, on. Depend ke sath on hamesha ata hai. Ye pakka jumla hai.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye pehli tasveer waqt wali seedhi ki ho. Teen darjay bane hon. Sab se neeche chhote darje par ghari bani ho aur at likha ho. Beech ke darje par calendar ka aik din ho aur on likha ho. Sab se upar bare darje par poora mahina aur saal hon aur in likha ho. Sath aik line ho ke at ghari, on din, in mahina. Student seedhi ki tasveer dekh le to waqt wala preposition kabhi ghalat nahi hoga.

Doosri tasveer made wale farq ki ho. Aik taraf lakri ki bani kursi ho jis me lakri saaf dikhai de rahi ho aur neeche made of likha ho. Dusri taraf doodh ka glass aur makkhan ka tukra ho, teer doodh se makkhan ki taraf ja raha ho aur neeche made from likha ho. Beech me aik line ho ke shakal wohi to of, shakal badli to from.
