Lesson 35: Most Repeated Idioms
Sab se zyada dohraye jane wale idioms. Har kagaz me ane wale tees muhawaray

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

Dekho, one paper ki English me aik hissa aisa hai jo samajhne se nahi, pehchanne se hal hota hai, aur wo hai idioms. Idiom matlab wo jumla ya muhawara jis ka matlab us ke alfaaz ke matlab se bilkul alag hota hai. Paper banane wala ye baat janta hai ke student idiom ko lafz ba lafz tarjuma karke phanse ga. Isi liye har saal PPSC, FPSC aur NTS ke papers me wahi puraane tees chaalees idioms ghuma phira kar ajate hain. Aik saal "white elephant" ata hai, agle saal "eleventh hour", phir "bread and butter". List wohi hai, bas number badal jata hai. Jo student ye tees idioms pakkay kar le, wo har paper me do teen number bina mehnat ke jeb me daal leta hai.

Ab in ko seekhna kese hai, ye bhi sun lo. Idiom ko ratta laga kar kabhi yaad nahi hota. Har idiom ke peeche aik chhoti si tasveer ya kahani hoti hai. Jab tum "burn the midnight oil" parho to zehen me wo purana zamana lao jab raat ko tel ka diya jala kar parhte thay. Tasveer zehen me beth gayi to matlab khud yaad ajayega. Is lesson me har idiom ke sath aik jumla de raha hun, taake tum dekho ke asal English me ye kahan aur kese istemal hota hai. Jahan koi mashhoor ghalti hoti hai, wahan chhoti si hidayat bhi sath de di hai.

Board par tees idioms

1. A blessing in disguise. Urdu matlab hai chhupa hua faida, wo cheez jo pehle museebat lage lekin baad me faida de. Jumla dekho: Losing that job proved to be a blessing in disguise, because he started his own business. Naukri jana museebat lagta tha, lekin wahi faida ban gaya.

2. To burn the midnight oil. Urdu matlab hai raat dair tak jaag kar parhna ya kaam karna. Jumla: She burned the midnight oil for months and finally passed the PPSC exam. Diya jalane wali tasveer yaad rakho, raat ki mehnat khud zehen me ajayegi.

3. Once in a blue moon. Urdu matlab hai bohat kam, kabhi kabhar. Jumla: He visits his village once in a blue moon. Neela chaand roz nazar nahi ata, bas yehi matlab hai.

4. To cry over spilt milk. Urdu matlab hai guzri hui baat par rona, jo nuqsan ho gaya us par afsos karte rehna. Jumla: The match is lost, so there is no use crying over spilt milk. Gira hua doodh wapas glass me nahi ata, yehi is ki kahani hai.

5. A white elephant. Urdu matlab hai wo mehngi cheez jo rakhne me kharcha zyada aur faida kuch nahi. Jumla: The old government building has become a white elephant for the department. Khayal rakho, is ka matlab safed hathi nahi hai, paper isi par phansata hai.

6. To be in hot water. Urdu matlab hai mushkil ya museebat me hona. Jumla: He is in hot water because he missed the deadline again. Garam pani me gira hua insan jese bechain hota hai, waisa hi haal.

7. Bread and butter. Urdu matlab hai rozi roti, zariya e maash. Jumla: Teaching is his bread and butter. Ye alfaaz roz sunte ho, lekin paper me in ka matlab khana nahi, rozi hai. Ye farq yaad rakho.

8. To turn a deaf ear. Urdu matlab hai ansuna kar dena, baat par dhayan na dena. Jumla: The officer turned a deaf ear to all our requests. Behra aadmi sun nahi sakta, aur ye jan bhooj kar nahi sunta.

9. At the eleventh hour. Urdu matlab hai bilkul aakhri waqt par. Jumla: He submitted his application at the eleventh hour. Gyarah baje ka matlab ghari ka waqt nahi hai, aakhri lamha hai. Ye idiom har doosre paper me ata hai.

10. To bite off more than one can chew. Urdu matlab hai apni taqat se zyada kaam utha lena. Jumla: By joining three academies at once, he bit off more than he could chew. Munh me itna bara niwala daal lena ke chabaya na jaye, yehi tasveer hai.

11. To add fuel to the fire. Urdu matlab hai jalti par tel dalna, baat ko aur bigaar dena. Jumla: His angry words only added fuel to the fire. Urdu ki kahawat se ye bilkul milta hai, is liye ye foran yaad ho jayega.

12. To beat about the bush. Urdu matlab hai idhar udhar ki baatein karna, asal baat par na ana. Jumla: Stop beating about the bush and tell me the truth. Paper me is ka matlab jhaari ko peetna kabhi nahi hota, ye wala dhoka har saal chalta hai.

13. By hook or by crook. Urdu matlab hai kisi bhi tareeqe se, jayaz ho ya na ho. Jumla: He is determined to pass the exam by hook or by crook. Hook aur crook dono pakarne ke auzaar hain, matlab har haal me kaam nikalna.

14. To call a spade a spade. Urdu matlab hai saaf saaf sach kehna, bina jhijhak. Jumla: She is honest and always calls a spade a spade. Kodaaal ko kodaal hi kehna, ghoomana nahi.

15. A dark horse. Urdu matlab hai chhupa rustam, wo shakhs jis ki qabiliyat ka kisi ko andaza na ho aur wo achanak jeet jaye. Jumla: Nobody expected much from him, but he proved to be a dark horse in the election.

16. To face the music. Urdu matlab hai apne kiye ke nateejay ka samna karna, saza ya dant ke liye tayyar hona. Jumla: He broke the rules and now he must face the music. Is ka matlab gana sunna nahi hai, ye baat paper me khaas tor par parhi jati hai.

17. A fish out of water. Urdu matlab hai ajnabi mahol me bechain aur begaana mehsoos karna. Jumla: In the new city, she felt like a fish out of water. Machli pani se bahar tarphti hai, bilkul waisi hi halat.

18. To get cold feet. Urdu matlab hai aakhri waqt par darr jana, hosla jawab de jana. Jumla: He wanted to speak on stage but got cold feet at the last moment. Pairon ka thanda hona dar ki alamat hai, sard mausam nahi.

19. To hit the nail on the head. Urdu matlab hai bilkul sahi baat kehna, nishane par teer marna. Jumla: You hit the nail on the head when you said the plan needs more time. Keel par hathora bilkul sahi jagah lagna, yehi tasveer hai.

20. In black and white. Urdu matlab hai likha hua, tehreer ki surat me. Jumla: The agreement is in black and white, so he cannot deny it. Zabani baat urr jati hai, kaalay aur safed me likhi hui baat saboot ban jati hai.

21. To keep an eye on. Urdu matlab hai nazar rakhna, khayal rakhna. Jumla: Please keep an eye on my bag while I buy the tickets. Aankh ka matlab yahan nigrani hai.

22. A lion's share. Urdu matlab hai sab se bara hissa. Jumla: He took the lion's share of the profit. Sher jungle ka raja hai, hissa bhi sab se bara usi ka. Ye idiom FPSC ke papers me bohat aya hai.

23. To make both ends meet. Urdu matlab hai mushkil se guzara karna, aamdani me kharcha pura karna. Jumla: With his small salary, he finds it hard to make both ends meet. Mahina ke dono siray, aamdani aur kharcha, milana hi mushkil ho jaye to ye idiom bolte hain.

24. To nip in the bud. Urdu matlab hai burai ya maslay ko shuru me hi khatam kar dena. Jumla: The teacher nipped the mischief in the bud. Bud matlab kali hai. Kali ko wahi kuchal dena, phool banne hi na dena.

25. Once bitten, twice shy. Urdu matlab hai aik dafa dhoka kha kar dobara ehtiyaat karna. Jumla: After losing money in that scheme, he became careful, once bitten, twice shy. Urdu me is ka matlab wohi hai jo hum kehte hain, doodh ka jala chhaach bhi phoonk phoonk kar peeta hai.

26. A piece of cake. Urdu matlab hai bohat asaan kaam. Jumla: The test was a piece of cake for the well-prepared students. Cake khana sab ko asaan lagta hai, bas wahi baat hai.

27. To rain cats and dogs. Urdu matlab hai tez, moosla dhaar barish hona. Jumla: It rained cats and dogs last night. Is ka matlab billi kuttay girna nahi hai, paper ke options me ye wala matlab de kar phansaya jata hai.

28. To read between the lines. Urdu matlab hai chhupa hua matlab samajhna, ishara pakarna. Jumla: Reading between the lines, I understood that he was not happy with the offer. Satron ke darmiyan likha to kuch nahi hota, samajhna parta hai.

29. To smell a rat. Urdu matlab hai daal me kuch kala mehsoos hona, shak parna. Jumla: When he offered me a huge profit in one week, I smelled a rat. Choha chhupa ho to us ki boo ajati hai, dhoka bhi aise hi mehsoos hota hai.

30. To throw cold water on. Urdu matlab hai kisi ke josh ya mansoobay par pani pher dena, hosla todna. Jumla: Do not throw cold water on his plans before hearing him fully. Thanda pani josh thanda kar deta hai, yehi is ki asal hai.

Idioms seekhne ki trick

Ab wo tareeqa suno jis se ye tees idioms zehen me pakkay beth jayenge. Har idiom ko apni zindagi ki kisi asal soorat se jor do. Jab tum khud kabhi aakhri waqt par form jama karwao to zehen me kaho, at the eleventh hour. Jab koi mehngi bekaar cheez dekho to kaho, white elephant. Zehen apni kahani kabhi nahi bhoolta, kitab ki line bhool jata hai. Dusri baat ye hai ke idioms ko joron me yaad karo. Hot water aur cold feet dono jism ke ehsaas wale hain. Midnight oil aur eleventh hour dono waqt aur mehnat wale hain. Jora bana lo to aik yaad ate hi doosra khud ajata hai. Aur akhri baat, har idiom ko aik dafa apne jumle me bol kar dekho. Jo cheez tum ne apni zabaan se kahi, wo tumhari ho gayi.

Aik aam ghalti jo sab karte hain

Paper me sab se bara dhoka ye hota hai ke option me idiom ka lafzi matlab de diya jata hai. White elephant ke samne safed hathi, rain cats and dogs ke samne billi kuttay ki barish, bread and butter ke samne double roti aur makkhan. Ye options un students ke liye hain jo pehli dafa idiom dekh rahe hain. Tum ne ye lesson parh liya hai, ab tumhara kaam sirf itna hai ke option dekh kar pehle dil me kaho ke lafzi matlab hamesha ghalat hota hai, asal matlab wo hai jo ustad ne btaya. Is aik aadat se idioms wale saare number tumhare ho jayenge.

Jawab aur un ki wajah

1. Jawab A hai. Jo cheez pehle museebat lage aur baad me faida de, wahi blessing in disguise hai.
2. Jawab C hai. Eleventh hour ka matlab ghari ka waqt nahi, aakhri lamha hai. Option A wala dhoka khaas tor par rakha gaya tha.
3. Jawab B hai. Purane zamanay me raat ko diya jala kar parhte thay, isi se ye idiom bana.
4. Jawab C hai. White elephant mehngi lekin bekaar cheez ko kehte hain, rang se is ka koi lena dena nahi.
5. Jawab B hai. Neela chaand bohat kam nazar ata hai, is liye matlab hai kabhi kabhar.
6. Jawab C hai. Deaf ear karna matlab ansuna kar dena, baat par dhayan na dena.
7. Jawab A hai. Gira hua doodh wapas nahi ata, guzri baat par rona bekaar hai.
8. Jawab B hai. Bread and butter ka matlab rozi roti hai, khana nahi.
9. Jawab C hai. Dark horse wo chhupa rustam hai jis ki jeet ki kisi ko umeed na ho.
10. Jawab B hai. Smell a rat matlab daal me kala mehsoos hona, shak parna.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye pehli tasveer idioms ki tasveeri fehrist wali ho. Har idiom ke samne us ki chhoti si drawing ho, jaise midnight oil ke samne jalta hua diya, white elephant ke samne safed hathi jis par kharchay ke bojh ka nishan ho, aur blue moon ke samne neela chaand. Student tasveer dekh kar idiom bolay, ye khel is pure lesson ko aik nazar me dohra dega.

Doosri tasveer ghalat aur sahi wali ho. Aik taraf barray barray lafzi matlab likhe hon jin par kaata laga ho, safed hathi, billi kuttay ki barish, aur doosri taraf un ke asal matlab sabz rang me hon. Ye tasveer paper ke dhokay ko aankhon ke samne la khara karegi, aur student exam hall me beth kar bhi ye farq nahi bhulega.
