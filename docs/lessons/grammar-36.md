Lesson 36: Proverbs
Mashhoor English kahawatein. Panjis kahawatein aur un ki Urdu brabar wali baatein

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

Dekho, proverb matlab kahawat, wo chhota sa jumla jis me poori zindagi ka tajurba band hota hai. One paper me kahawatein teen tareeqon se ati hain. Kabhi adhoori kahawat de kar us ka agla hissa poocha jata hai, jese honesty is the best aur agay kya ayega. Kabhi kahawat de kar us ka matlab options me poocha jata hai. Aur kabhi aik soorat e haal de kar poocha jata hai ke is par konsi kahawat fit beth ti hai. Teeno sooraton me number usi ko milta hai jis ne kahawat ko us ke matlab ke sath yaad kiya ho. Khushi ki baat ye hai ke English ki zyada tar mashhoor kahawaton ki brabar ki kahawat Urdu me pehle se maujood hai. Tum aadhi kahawatein asal me pehle se jantay ho, bas un ka English roop pehchanna hai.

Seekhne ka tareeqa bhi sun lo. Kahawat ko lafzon se nahi, us ki hikmat se yaad karo. Jab me kahun "a stitch in time saves nine" to zehen me phati hui qameez lao. Aik tanka waqt par laga lo to baad ke no tankay bach jate hain. Ye tasveer zehen me aayi to kahawat kabhi nahi bhulegi. Is lesson me har kahawat ke sath us ka saada matlab de raha hun, aur jahan Urdu ki mashhoor kahawat us ki brabar ki hai, wo bhi de raha hun taake tumhara purana ilm naye ilm ka sahara ban jaye.

Board par panjis kahawatein

1. Honesty is the best policy. Matlab hai imaandari sab se behtreen hikmat e amli hai. Sach bolna sirf akhlaq nahi, sab se faida mand raasta bhi hai. Ye kahawat har paper ki fehrist me pehle numberon par hoti hai.

2. A stitch in time saves nine. Matlab hai waqt par laga aik tanka baad ke no tankon se bacha leta hai, yani maslay ko shuru me hal karna bare nuqsan se bachata hai. Jumla: The roof was repaired in time, for a stitch in time saves nine.

3. Actions speak louder than words. Matlab hai amal alfaaz se zyada bolta hai. Urdu me hum kehte hain, kehne se karna behtar hai. Jo insan sirf baatein kare aur amal na kare, us ki baat ka wazan nahi hota.

4. Practice makes a man perfect. Matlab hai mashq insan ko mahir aur kaamil banati hai. Urdu ki mashhoor kahawat is ke bohat qareeb hai, karat karat abhyaas ke jarhmati hot sujaan, yani lagatar mashq be waqoof ko bhi aqalmand bana deti hai.

5. Where there is a will, there is a way. Matlab hai jahan chah aur iraada ho, wahan raasta nikal hi ata hai. Jumla: He had no money for books, but where there is a will, there is a way, and he studied in the library.

6. A friend in need is a friend indeed. Matlab hai museebat me kaam ane wala hi asal dost hai. Khushi me sab sath hote hain, dost ki pehchan dukh me hoti hai. Indeed ka matlab hai asal me, ye lafz is kahawat ki jaan hai.

7. Time and tide wait for none. Matlab hai waqt aur samandar ki lehar kisi ka intezar nahi karti. Urdu me kehte hain, gaya waqt phir haath nahi ata. Tide matlab lehar hai, ye lafz paper me alag se bhi poocha jata hai.

8. Health is wealth. Matlab hai sehat hi asal daulat hai. Urdu ki mashhoor kahawat is ki brabar ki hai, jaan hai to jahan hai. Karoron ho aur sehat na ho to sab bekaar.

9. Knowledge is power. Matlab hai ilm hi taqat hai. Jis ke pas ilm hai, wo halaat ko apne haq me mor sakta hai. Ye kahawat essay aur interview me bhi bohat kaam ati hai.

10. Prevention is better than cure. Matlab hai ilaaj se parhez behtar hai. Urdu me bilkul yehi kahawat boli jati hai, parhez ilaaj se behtar hai. Pehle ehtiyaat, baad ka ilaaj.

11. A little knowledge is a dangerous thing. Matlab hai adhoora ilm khatarnak hota hai. Is ki Urdu brabar wali kahawat har Pakistani janta hai, neem hakeem khatra e jaan. Paper me ye kahawat is Urdu wali surat me bohat ati hai.

12. Birds of a feather flock together. Matlab hai aik jaisi tabiyat ke log aik sath rehte hain. Feather matlab par hai, flock matlab jhund bana kar rehna. Ache log achon ke sath aur buray buron ke sath nazar ate hain, yehi is ki pehchan hai.

13. Every cloud has a silver lining. Matlab hai har museebat ke peeche umeed ki kiran chhupi hoti hai. Silver lining matlab badal ke kinaray par chamakti hui roshni. Gham ke dinon me ye kahawat hosla deti hai.

14. Necessity is the mother of invention. Matlab hai zaroorat hi ijad ki maan hai. Jab insan majboor hota hai to dimagh naye raaste dhundta hai. Invention matlab ijad, ye lafz alag se bhi yaad rakho.

15. Barking dogs seldom bite. Matlab hai bhonknay wale kuttay kam hi kaat tay hain, yani zyada shor karne walay log aksar amal nahi karte. Urdu me kehte hain, jo garajte hain wo baraste nahi. Seldom ka matlab hai bohat kam, ye lafz bhi paper me ata hai.

16. Do not count your chickens before they hatch. Matlab hai anday se bachay nikalne se pehle unhe mat gin lo, yani nateeja ane se pehle hi us par khush hona aur bharosa karna ghalat hai. Hatch matlab anday se bacha nikalna.

17. All that glitters is not gold. Matlab hai har chamakne wali cheez sona nahi hoti, zahiri chamak par dhoka mat khao. Glitter matlab chamakna. Ye kahawat Shakespeare ki mashhoor line se ayi hai aur papers me bohat dohraayi jati hai.

18. United we stand, divided we fall. Matlab hai ittihad me khade hain to mazboot hain, bikhre to gir jayenge. Urdu me kehte hain, aik aur aik gyarah hote hain. Stand aur fall ka tazad is kahawat ki khoobsurti hai.

19. Look before you leap. Matlab hai chhalang lagane se pehle dekh lo, yani koi bara qadam uthane se pehle soch samajh lo. Leap matlab chhalang. Paper me ye kahawat mashwara dene wale sawalon me ati hai.

20. Slow and steady wins the race. Matlab hai ahista lekin musalsal chalne wala hi daur jeetta hai. Ye kahawat usi kachway aur khargosh wali kahani se ayi hai jo tum bachpan se jante ho. Steady matlab musalsal, bina ruke.

21. Too many cooks spoil the broth. Matlab hai zyada bawarchi shorba bigaar dete hain, yani aik kaam me zyada logon ki mudakhlat kaam kharab kar deti hai. Urdu ki mashhoor kahawat is par bilkul fit hai, do mullahon me murgi haram. Broth matlab shorba ya yakhni.

22. A bird in hand is worth two in the bush. Matlab hai haath me mojood aik cheez, umeed ki do cheezon se behtar hai. Jo pakka haath me hai usay mat chhoro, us cheez ke peeche jo sirf umeed hai. Worth matlab qeemat ya barabar.

23. Cut your coat according to your cloth. Matlab hai apne kapray ke hisaab se coat kato, yani aamdani dekh kar kharcha karo. Urdu me ye kahawat lafz ba lafz boli jati hai, jitni chaadar ho utne hi paon phailao. Ye is lesson ki sab se asaan kahawat hai kyunke tum ye pehle se jante ho.

24. Might is right. Matlab hai taqat hi haq hai, jis ke pas zor hai usi ki baat mani jati hai. Urdu ki kahawat is ki bilkul brabar hai, jis ki lathi us ki bhains. Ye kahawat duniya ke zulm wale nizam par tanz hai.

25. Example is better than precept. Matlab hai misaal naseehat se behtar hai, yani khud karke dikhana, sirf kehne se zyada asar rakhta hai. Precept matlab naseehat ya usool. Ustad khud amal kare to student lafzon se zyada us ke amal se seekhta hai.

Kahawaton ko yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab trick suno jis se ye panjis kahawatein zehen me pakki ho jayengi. Kahawaton ko un ki Urdu brabar wali kahawat se baandh do. Jab tum "might is right" parho to foran dil me kaho, jis ki lathi us ki bhains. Jab "a little knowledge" ayay to neem hakeem yaad karo. Zehen purani cheez ko nayi cheez ki rassi bana leta hai, aur rassi se bandhi cheez girti nahi. Dusri trick ye hai ke kahawaton ko joron me rakho. Kuch kahawatein waqt ki hain, time and tide aur stitch in time. Kuch dost aur ittihad ki hain, friend in need aur united we stand. Kuch ehtiyaat ki hain, look before you leap aur prevention is better than cure. Teen teen ke guchay bana lo, poori fehrist chhoti lagne lagegi. Aur akhri baat, adhoori kahawat wala sawal paper me sab se asaan hota hai, kyunke agla lafz aksar wo hota hai jo kahawat ki jaan hai, jese policy, nine, indeed, none. In lafzon par khaas nazar rakho.

Aik aam ghalti jo paper me hoti hai

Paper banane wala kahawat ke mashhoor lafz ko badal kar dhoka deta hai. Ghalat surat dekh lo taake pehchan sako. Ghalat: Honesty is the best policy. ki jagah option me honesty is the best quality de dena. Sahi lafz policy hi hai, quality nahi. Ghalat: A stitch in time saves ten. Sahi: A stitch in time saves nine. Number bhi badal diya jata hai, nine hi sahi hai. Ghalat: Time and tide wait for nobody ko time and waves bana dena. Sahi lafz tide hai. Wajah har jagah aik hi hai, kahawat apne asal alfaaz ke sath hi kahawat hoti hai. Aik lafz badla to wo kisi aur ki baat ban gayi. Is liye kahawat ko hamesha poori, asal alfaaz ke sath yaad karo.

Practice sawal

Ab apni samajh ko parkho. Ye das sawal bilkul paper wale andaaz ke hain. Pehle khud hal karo, jawab baad me dekho.

B. Policy

3. Complete the proverb: A friend in need is a friend ___.

B. In deed

D. In need

5. "Barking dogs seldom bite" means:

6. Complete the proverb: Time and tide wait for ___.

7. "Neem hakeem khatra e jaan" is best expressed in English by:

8. Complete the proverb: All that glitters is not ___.

Jawab aur un ki wajah

1. Jawab B hai, policy. Kahawat ka asal lafz policy hai, quality wala option dhoka tha.
2. Jawab B hai. Waqt par chhota sa amal bare nuqsan se bachata hai, yehi tankay wali kahawat ka matlab hai.
3. Jawab A hai, indeed. Indeed aik lafz hai aur matlab hai asal me. Museebat wala dost hi asal dost hai.
4. Jawab B hai. Kaam ko waqt par na karne se wo pahaar ban gaya, yehi stitch in time wali soorat hai.
5. Jawab B hai. Jo garajte hain wo baraste nahi. Shor zyada, amal kam.
6. Jawab C hai, none. Waqt aur lehar kisi ke liye nahi rukte, none hi kahawat ka asal lafz hai.
7. Jawab B hai. Neem hakeem matlab adhoora ilm wala, aur wohi a little knowledge is a dangerous thing hai.
8. Jawab C hai, gold. Har chamakti cheez sona nahi hoti.
9. Jawab B hai. Jitni chaadar utne paon, aamdani dekh kar kharcha karo.
10. Jawab C hai. Us ka amal us ki naseehat se zyada bol raha hai, yehi example is better than precept hai.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye pehli tasveer Urdu aur English kahawaton ke jore wali chart ho. Aik taraf jis ki lathi us ki bhains likha ho aur us ke brabar might is right, neem hakeem ke brabar a little knowledge, chaadar wali kahawat ke brabar cut your coat. Student apni pehchani hui Urdu kahawat dekh kar English wali khud bol de, yehi is chart ka kamaal hoga.

Doosri tasveer adhoori kahawaton wala khel ho. Har kahawat adhoori likhi ho aur us ka akhri lafz chhupa ho. Neeche chaar options hon jin me se aik sahi ho. Ye bilkul paper wala sawal hai, bas tasveer ki surat me. Jo student is khel me jeet gaya, wo exam hall me ye number kabhi nahi chorega.
