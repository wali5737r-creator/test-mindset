Lesson 37: Phrasal Verbs
Sab se zyada ane wale phrasal verbs. Tees aise jumlay jo har paper me ghumte hain

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

Dekho, phrasal verb matlab verb ke sath aik chhota sa lafz, jese up, off, out, into, lag jana, aur poora matlab hi badal jana. Break ka matlab torna hai, lekin break down ka matlab kharab ho jana hai, break into ka matlab zabardasti ghusna hai, aur break out ka matlab jang ya aag ka phoot parna hai. Aik hi verb, teen chhote lafz, teen alag matlab. Paper banane wala ye khel bohat pasand karta hai, kyunke kamzor student verb ka purana matlab pakar kar ghalat option chun leta hai. FPSC aur PPSC ke har doosre paper me paanch chhe phrasal verbs lazmi ate hain. Aur khoobi ye hai ke fehrist tay hai. Wahi tees chaalees phrasal verbs saalon se ghuma phira kar a rahe hain.

Inhe seekhne ka usool bhi samajh lo. Phrasal verb ko do alag lafzon ki tarah kabhi mat dekho. Ye aik poori ikai hai, aik hi lafz ki tarah seekho. Jab tum "call off" parho to zehen me "mansookh" ka aik hi khana kholo. Dusri baat, har phrasal verb ko jumle me seekho. Akela "put off" bhool jayega, lekin "the match was put off due to rain" zehen me beth jayega, kyunke barish aur match ki tasveer sath ajayegi. Is lesson me har aik ke sath jumla is liye de raha hun.

Board par tees phrasal verbs

1. Break down. Matlab hai kharab ho jana, ya jazbati tor par toot jana. Jumla: Our bus broke down on the way to Lahore. Gaari ke liye ye lafz har paper me ata hai, aur insan ke rone par tootne ko bhi yehi kehte hain.

2. Break into. Matlab hai zabardasti ghusna, naqab zani karna. Jumla: Thieves broke into the house at night. Into andar jane ka ehsaas deta hai, yehi is ki pehchan hai.

3. Break out. Matlab hai achanak shuru ho jana, khaas tor par jang, aag ya bimari ka. Jumla: The war broke out in 1939. Fire broke out bhi bilkul yehi surat hai.

4. Bring up. Matlab hai parwarish karna, aur dusra matlab hai kisi baat ka zikr cheirna. Jumla: She brought up five children alone. Meeting me "he brought up the question of salary" bhi yehi lafz hai. Dono matlab paper me ate hain, dono yaad rakho.

5. Call off. Matlab hai mansookh kar dena. Jumla: The match was called off because of rain. Off yahan band karne ke matlab me hai. Ye phrasal verb papers ka sab se purana mehman hai.

6. Carry on. Matlab hai jaari rakhna, rukna nahi. Jumla: Carry on with your work, do not stop. On aage chalne ka ishara hai, jese kaam aage barh raha ho.

7. Come across. Matlab hai ittefaqan mil jana, insan ho ya cheez. Jumla: I came across an old friend in the bazaar. Bina bulaye, bina dhoonde jo mil jaye, us ke liye ye lafz hai.

8. Get over. Matlab hai bimari ya sadme se ubhar ana, bhool pana. Jumla: It took him months to get over the loss of his father. Over yahan paar karne ke matlab me hai, dard ko paar karna.

9. Give up. Matlab hai haar maan lena, koshish chor dena. Jumla: Never give up, try once more. Ye jumla khud itna mashhoor hai ke paper me is surat me bhi ajata hai.

10. Look after. Matlab hai dekh bhaal karna, khayal rakhna. Jumla: She looks after her old parents. After yahan peeche reh kar nigrani ke matlab me hai.

11. Look into. Matlab hai tehqeeq karna, jaanch karna. Jumla: The police are looking into the matter. Into andar dekhna hai, baat ki teh tak jana.

12. Look up. Matlab hai dictionary ya fehrist me lafz dekhna, aur dusra matlab hai halaat ka behtar hona. Jumla: Look up the word in the dictionary. Aur business is looking up matlab kaam behtar ho raha hai. Dono matlab yaad rakho.

13. Make out. Matlab hai mushkil se samajh pana ya dekh pana. Jumla: I could not make out what he was saying. Jab awaaz ya likhai saaf na ho aur dimagh zor laga kar samjhe, ye lafz ata hai.

14. Put off. Matlab hai multavi karna, aage par daal dena. Jumla: Do not put off till tomorrow what you can do today. Ye jumla khud aik naseehat hai aur paper me isi surat me ata hai.

15. Put out. Matlab hai aag ya roshni bujha dena. Jumla: The firemen put out the fire after two hours. Put off aur put out ka farq paper ki mashhoor ghalti hai. Off matlab taalna, out matlab bujhana. Ye farq zehen me bitha lo.

16. Run into. Matlab hai ittefaqan takra jana ya mil jana, aur mushkil me phasne ko bhi yehi kehte hain. Jumla: I ran into my teacher at the station. Daurna nahi hai is me, achanak aamna saamna hai.

17. Set up. Matlab hai qayam karna, banana, shuru karna. Jumla: He set up a small school in his village. Committee set up hona aur business set up karna, dono yahi hain.

18. Take after. Matlab hai kisi par jana, shakl ya aadat me mushabihat hona. Jumla: She takes after her mother in kindness. After yahan peeche chalne ke matlab me hai, maa ke naqsh e qadam par.

19. Turn down. Matlab hai radd kar dena, aur dusra matlab hai awaaz ya roshni kam karna. Jumla: He turned down the job offer. Aur turn down the volume bhi yehi lafz hai. Paper dono matlab deta hai.

20. Turn up. Matlab hai achanak hazir ho jana, a jana. Jumla: He did not turn up for the interview. Up yahan zahir hone ke matlab me hai. Turn down aur turn up ka jora bana kar yaad karo, aik radd karna, dusra hazir hona.

21. Pass away. Matlab hai inteqal kar jana, wafat pana. Jumla: His grandfather passed away last year. Kisi ki maut ki khabar narmi se dene ka ye sab se mohtaram tareeqa hai, aur paper me die ka mutabadil ban kar ata hai.

22. Put on. Matlab hai pehenna. Jumla: He put on his coat and left. Kapray, chashma, topi, jo jism par charhaya jaye, us ke liye put on hai.

23. Take off. Matlab hai utaarna, aur jahaz ka urhna bhi yehi hai. Jumla: The plane took off on time. Put on ka ulta take off hai, ye jora hamesha sath yaad karo. Kapray utaarna bhi take off hai aur jahaz ki urhan bhi.

24. Find out. Matlab hai maloom karna, pata chalana. Jumla: I could not find out who took my book. Out yahan chhupi baat bahar ane ke matlab me hai.

25. Work out. Matlab hai hal nikal lena, aur dusra matlab hai warzish karna. Jumla: We worked out a plan to save money. Gym me work out karna bhi yehi lafz hai. Dono matlab mashhoor hain.

26. Get rid of. Matlab hai chutkara pana, peecha churana. Jumla: He sold his old car to get rid of its daily repairs. Rid ka matlab hai nijaat. Ye teen lafzon wala phrasal verb paper me bohat ata hai.

27. Hold on. Matlab hai zara intezar karna, aur dusra matlab hai mazbooti se pakray rehna. Jumla: Hold on, I am coming. Phone par hold on kehna bhi yehi hai.

28. Keep up. Matlab hai barqarar rakhna, silsila na tootne dena. Jumla: Keep up the good work. Up yahan satah oonchi rakhne ke matlab me hai.

29. Hand over. Matlab hai hawale kar dena, supurd karna. Jumla: He handed over the keys to the new officer. Daftar ki charge handover wali baat isi lafz se hoti hai.

30. Run out of. Matlab hai khatam ho jana, paas kuch na rehna. Jumla: We ran out of petrol on the highway. Petrol, waqt, paisa, sab par ye lafz chalta hai. Run into aur run out of alag hain, milna aur khatam hona, ye farq yaad rakho.

Phrasal verbs yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab trick suno. Chhote lafzon ko tasveer me badal do. Up aksar zahir hone ya poora karne ka ehsaas deta hai, turn up matlab zahir ho jana, set up matlab khara kar dena. Off aksar alag hone ya band hone ka hai, call off matlab band, take off matlab utaar kar alag. Out aksar bahar ane ya bujh jane ka hai, find out matlab baat bahar ana, put out matlab aag ka bujhna. Ye usool har jagah laagu nahi hota, lekin aadhi fehrist isi aik ishare se zehen me beth jati hai. Dusri trick jore wali hai. Put on aur take off, turn up aur turn down, break in aur break out. Ulta jora bana lo, aik yaad ayega to dusra khud ajayega, aur paper me aksar option me yehi jora de kar tumhe parkha jata hai.

Aik aam ghalti jo paper me hoti hai

Sab se mashhoor ghalti put off aur put out me hoti hai. Ghalat: The fire was put off by the firemen. Sahi: The fire was put out by the firemen. Wajah: aag bujhane ko put out kehte hain, put off ka matlab hai kaam ko taal dena. Dusri ghalti look after aur look for me hai. Ghalat: She is looking after a job in the newspaper. Sahi: She is looking for a job in the newspaper. Wajah: look after matlab dekh bhaal karna hai, aur dhundne ko look for kehte hain. Teesri ghalti pass away ki hai. Kisi ke inteqal ki khabar me die ki jagah pass away likhna narmi hai, lekin paper me sawal is ka matlab poochta hai, aur option me guzar jana ke sath sath guzar kar jana wala lafzi dhoka bhi hota hai. Matlab hamesha wafat hi hai.

Jawab aur un ki wajah

1. Jawab A hai. Meeting mansookh hui, call off matlab mansookh karna hai.
2. Jawab B hai. Break into matlab zabardasti ghusna hai. Shuru ho jana break out hota hai, option C wohi dhoka tha.
3. Jawab A hai. Samajh na pana make out hai. Awaaz saaf nahi thi aur wohi is ki pehchan hai.
4. Jawab C hai. Aag bujhana put out hai. Put off taalna hota hai, ye jora paper ki jaan hai.
5. Jawab A hai. Kisi par jana take after hai. Looks after dekh bhaal hai, wo yahan nahi beth ta.
6. Jawab B hai. Kaam taalna put off hai, aur ye jumla khud aik mashhoor naseehat hai.
7. Jawab C hai. Tehqeeq karna look into hai. Police case ki jaanch kar rahi hai, dekh bhaal nahi.
8. Jawab B hai. Radd kar dena turn down hai. Turn up hazir hona hota hai, us ka ulta.
9. Jawab C hai. Petrol khatam ho gaya, run out of yehi kehta hai. Run into milna hota hai.
10. Jawab A hai. Pass away matlab inteqal kar jana hai. Pass out ka matlab behosh hona hota hai, ye farq bhi yaad rakho.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye pehli tasveer break wale khandan ki ho. Aik bara lafz BREAK likha ho aur us se teen shakhein nikli hon, down, into aur out, har shakha ke samne us ke matlab ki chhoti si drawing ho, kharab gaari, tootta hua taala, aur phoot ti hui aag. Student aik nazar me samajh jayega ke chhota lafz kese poora matlab badal deta hai.

Doosri tasveer put wale jore ki ho. Aik taraf match par taale ki tasveer ke sath put off likha ho, dusri taraf bujhti hui aag ke sath put out. Yehi do lafz paper me sab se zyada ultaye jate hain. Ye tasveer dekhne ke baad student in dono ko kabhi apas me nahi milayega.
