Lesson 38: One-Word Substitution
Aik lafz poore jumle ki jagah. Tees alfaaz jo lambe jumlon ka kaam aik saans me karte hain

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

Dekho, one paper me aik sawal is surat me ata hai ke aik lamba sa jumla diya jata hai, jese "a person who speaks many languages", aur neeche chaar options hote hain jin me se aik lafz us poore jumle ki jagah leta hai. Is sawal ko one-word substitution kehte hain. Ye sawal samajh ka nahi, fehrist ka hai. Jis student ne mashhoor alfaaz ki fehrist parhi hui hai, wo dus second me jawab de deta hai. Jis ne nahi parhi, wo chaaro options ko dekh kar andaze lagata rehta hai. Har paper me is qism ke teen chaar sawal ate hain, aur in ki fehrist bhi wahi ghisi piti hai jo saalon se chal rahi hai. Aaj ki ye tees wali fehrist usi ghisi piti fehrist ka nichor hai.

In alfaaz ko seekhne ka tareeqa bhi pehle samajh lo. Har lafz ke peeche us ki buniyad chhupi hoti hai. Bio matlab zindagi, graphy matlab likhna. Biography yani zindagi ki likhai. Auto matlab khud. Autobiography yani khud apni zindagi ki likhai. Jab tum ye buniyadein pakar lo ge to na sirf ye tees alfaaz yaad rahen ge, kal ko paper me koi naya lafz ajaye to us ka matlab bhi andaze se nikal lo ge. Is lesson me jahan buniyad asaan hai, wahan sath de raha hun.

Board par tees alfaaz

1. Autobiography. Wo kitab jis me koi shakhs apni zindagi ki kahani khud likhe. Auto matlab khud. Jumla: He wrote his autobiography at the age of seventy. Ye lafz har fehrist ki pehchan hai.

2. Biography. Kisi ki zindagi ki kahani jo koi dusra shakhs likhe. Jumla: This biography of Quaid-e-Azam was written by a famous historian. Autobiography aur biography ka farq sirf auto ka hai, khud likhi ya dusre ne likhi. Ye jora paper me sath sath ata hai.

3. Audience. Sunne walon ka majma, wo log jo taqreer ya program sunne aye hon. Jumla: The audience listened to the speech in silence. Dekhne walon ko spectators kehte hain, sunne walon ko audience. Ye farq bhi paper me ata hai.

4. Century. Sau saal ka arsa, aur cricket me sau runs ko bhi century kehte hain. Jumla: Pakistan came into being in the twentieth century. Twenty first century matlab ikkiswi sadi.

5. Democracy. Wo nizam e hukumat jis me hukumat awam ki ho, awam ke liye ho. Jumla: Democracy gives every citizen the right to vote. Demo matlab awam, aur ye buniyad is lafz ko hamesha yaad rakhegi.

6. Illiterate. Wo shakhs jo parhna likhna na janta ho, anparh. Jumla: Special schools were opened for illiterate adults. Literate parha likha hota hai, il aage lagte hi ulta ho jata hai.

7. Invisible. Jo cheez dikhai na de, jo nazar na aye. Jumla: Germs are invisible to the naked eye. Visible dikhai dene wala hai, in lagte hi ghayab. Yehi in wala usool aage aur alfaaz me bhi ayega.

8. Linguist. Wo shakhs jo bohat si zabaanein janta ho ya zabanon ka mahir ho. Jumla: He is a linguist who speaks six languages. Paper me is sawal ka jumla aksar bilkul yehi hota hai, a person who knows many languages.

9. Manuscript. Haath se likhi hui kitab ya dastawaiz, chhapi hui nahi. Jumla: The old manuscript is kept safe in the museum. Manu matlab haath, script matlab likha hua. Buniyad yaad rakho, lafz khud yaad rahega.

10. Orphan. Wo bacha jis ke maa baap dono wafat pa chuke hon, yateem. Jumla: The school gives free education to orphans. Sirf baap guzra ho to bhi aam bol chal me yateem hi kehte hain, lekin paper is lafz ko isi matlab me leta hai.

11. Posthumous. Wo cheez jo kisi ke marne ke baad ho, jese marne ke baad peda hone wala bacha, ya marne ke baad chhapne wali kitab. Jumla: His posthumous book was published a year after his death. Ye lafz mushkil lagta hai lekin paper me bohat ata hai.

12. Synonym. Wo lafz jis ka matlab dusre lafz jaisa ho, ham maani lafz. Jumla: Happy is a synonym of glad. Ye lafz khud isi course me tum roz istemal kar rahe ho, is liye ye wala to bhoolna hi nahi chahiye.

13. Antonym. Wo lafz jis ka matlab dusre lafz ke bilkul ulta ho, mutazaad lafz. Jumla: Good is the antonym of bad. Synonym aur antonym ka jora hai, aik jaisa matlab, dusra ulta matlab. Dono ko sath yaad karo.

14. Teetotaller. Wo shakhs jo sharab bilkul na piye, kabhi haath na lagaye. Jumla: He is a teetotaller and never touches wine. Ye lafz dekhne me ajeeb lagta hai lekin fehrist ka pakka sawal hai, is liye isay khaas nishan laga lo.

15. Unanimous. Wo faisla jis par sab aik raye hon, muttafiq ul raye. Jumla: The decision was unanimous, no one opposed it. Uni matlab aik. Sab ki raye aik, yehi is ki buniyad hai.

16. Versatile. Wo shakhs jo har kaam kar sake, har fun me mahir ho, har fun maula. Jumla: He is a versatile player who can bat, bowl and field well. Paper me is ke options me aksar aise alfaaz hote hain jo student ne pehle kabhi dekhe nahi hote, lekin ye lafz tumhara pehchana hua hoga.

17. Widow. Wo aurat jis ka shohar wafat pa gaya ho, bewa. Jumla: The widow received a monthly grant from the fund.

18. Widower. Wo mard jis ki biwi wafat pa gayi ho. Jumla: The old widower lives alone in the village. Widow aur widower ka jora hai, aurat ke liye widow, mard ke liye widower. Akhri er hi farq hai, yehi paper parhta hai.

19. Optimist. Wo shakhs jo har baat me umeed aur achai dekhe, pur umeed insan. Jumla: An optimist always hopes for the best. Is ke ulta wala lafz foran baad araha hai, jora bana lo.

20. Pessimist. Wo shakhs jo har baat me burai aur mayoosi dekhe, bad gumaan insan. Jumla: A pessimist sees difficulty in every chance. Optimist aur pessimist ka jora paper ki jaan hai. Aik umeed wala, dusra mayoosi wala.

21. Ambidextrous. Wo shakhs jo dono hathon se barabar kaam kar sake. Jumla: He is ambidextrous and writes with both hands. Ye lafz lamba hai lekin is ka sawal bilkul tay hota hai, a person who can use both hands equally. Dekhte hi pehchan lo ge.

22. Bibliophile. Wo shakhs jo kitabon se mohabbat kare, kitabon ka shaukeen. Jumla: A true bibliophile spends his savings on books. Biblio matlab kitab, phile matlab mohabbat karne wala. Buniyad phir kaam ayi.

23. Edible. Wo cheez jo khane ke qabil ho, khayi ja sakti ho. Jumla: Not all mushrooms are edible. Khane ke qabil ke liye eatable nahi, edible hi sahi lafz hai. Ye chhota sa farq paper me ata hai.

24. Immigrant. Wo shakhs jo dusre mulk me aa kar bas jaye. Jumla: Many immigrants settled in Canada for a better life. Jo mulk chhore use emigrant kehte hain aur jo naye mulk me aye use immigrant. A aur E ka ye khel bhi paper me chalta hai.

25. Omnipotent. Wo zaat jo har cheez par qudrat rakhti ho, sab kuch kar sakne wali. Jumla: God is omnipotent. Omni matlab sab. Ye lafz Allah ki sifaat ke jumlon me ata hai aur Islamiat ke sath mil kar bhi poocha jata hai.

26. Omniscient. Wo zaat jo sab kuch janti ho, har baat ka ilm rakhne wali. Jumla: God is omniscient and knows what is in our hearts. Omnipotent taqat wala, omniscient ilm wala. Dono ka jora aur dono ka farq yaad rakho.

27. Fatal. Wo cheez jo jaan lewa ho, maut ka sabab bane. Jumla: He suffered a fatal injury in the accident. Fatal accident matlab wo hadsa jis me maut hui ho. Sirf khatarnak nahi, jaan lene wala, ye farq paper parhta hai.

28. Inaudible. Wo awaaz jo sunai na de, bohat ahista ho. Jumla: His voice was inaudible in the last row. Audible sunai dene wala hai, in lagte hi ghayab. Invisible wala usool yahan bhi chal raha hai, dekh lo.

29. Incredible. Wo baat jis par yaqeen na aye, na qabil e yaqeen. Jumla: He showed incredible courage during the rescue. Credible yaqeen ke qabil hota hai, incredible us se aage ki cheez hai jo yaqeen se bahar lage.

30. Patriot. Wo shakhs jo apne watan se mohabbat kare, muhibb e watan. Jumla: A patriot is ready to serve his country at any cost. Ye lafz Pak Studies ke jumlon me bhi ata hai aur English me bhi, dono jagah kaam ayega.

Alfaaz ko yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab trick suno, aur ye trick is pure lesson ki jaan hai. Alfaaz ko un ki buniyadon se tor kar seekho. In ka matlab hai nahi ya ghayab, jese invisible me dikhai nahi deta, inaudible me sunai nahi deta. Uni ka matlab hai aik, jese unanimous me sab ki raye aik. Omni ka matlab hai sab, jese omnipotent me sab taqat aur omniscient me sab ilm. Bio ka matlab hai zindagi, auto ka matlab hai khud. Jab tum ne paanch chhe buniyadein pakar leen to tees alfaaz tumhe rata nahi lagen ge, tum unhe parh kar bana lo ge. Dusri trick joron ki hai. Autobiography aur biography, optimist aur pessimist, widow aur widower, synonym aur antonym. Jora banao, farq wali aik line sath yaad karo. Paper aksar jore ke dono alfaaz options me rakh kar farq hi poochta hai.

Aik aam ghalti jo paper me hoti hai

Sab se mashhoor ghalti biography aur autobiography me hoti hai. Ghalat soch: His autobiography was written by a famous historian. Wajah: jab likhne wala koi aur ho to wo autobiography ho hi nahi sakti, wo biography hai. Auto matlab khud, ye lafz dekh kar hi jawab pakka kar lo. Dusri ghalti audience aur spectators me hai. Ghalat: The audience watched the football match. Sahi: The spectators watched the football match. Wajah: audience sunne walon ko kehte hain, dekhne walon ko spectators. Teesri ghalti widow aur widower me hai, jahan akhri er par nazar na jaye to aurat wala lafz mard par lag jata hai. In teen farqon par nishan laga lo, yehi paper ke pasandeeda jaal hain.

Jawab aur un ki wajah

1. Jawab A hai, linguist. Zabanon ka mahir aur bohat si zabaanein janne wala linguist hota hai.
2. Jawab C hai. Khud apni zindagi likhna autobiography hai. Dusra likhe to biography hoti hai.
3. Jawab B hai, orphan. Maa baap dono guzar jayein to bacha yateem, yani orphan, kehlata hai.
4. Jawab C hai, invisible. Dikhai na dena invisible hai. Sunai na dena inaudible hota hai, option A wohi dhoka tha.
5. Jawab B hai, optimist. Umeed aur achai dekhne wala optimist, mayoosi wala pessimist.
6. Jawab C hai, unanimous. Uni matlab aik, sab ki raye aik ho to faisla unanimous hai.
7. Jawab B hai, widower. Aurat ke liye widow aur mard ke liye widower, akhri er hi farq hai.
8. Jawab C hai, posthumous. Marne ke baad hone wali cheez posthumous kehlati hai.
9. Jawab A hai, ambidextrous. Dono hathon se barabar kaam karne wala yehi lafz hai.
10. Jawab B hai, democracy. Demo matlab awam, awam ki hukumat democracy hai.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye pehli tasveer buniyadon wali chart ho. In, uni, omni, bio aur auto, har buniyad ka apna rang ho aur us ke neeche us se bane hue alfaaz hon. Student chart dekh kar samajh jayega ke alfaaz bante kese hain, aur phir usay koi fehrist ratne ki zaroorat hi nahi rahegi.

Doosri tasveer joron wali ho. Har jora aamne saamne rakha ho, autobiography ke samne biography, optimist ke samne pessimist, widow ke samne widower, aur darmiyan me farq wali aik line ho. Paper ka dhoka hamesha jore ke andar chhupa hota hai, is tasveer se wo dhoka aankhon ke samne ajayega.
