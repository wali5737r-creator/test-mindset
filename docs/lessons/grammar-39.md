Lesson 39: Synonyms and Antonyms
Ham maani aur ultay alfaaz aik sath. Panjis jore jo har paper me wapas ate hain

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

Dekho, one paper me alfaaz do taraf se pooche jate hain. Aik taraf synonym hai, yani wo lafz jis ka matlab diye gaye lafz jaisa ho. Dusri taraf antonym hai, yani wo lafz jis ka matlab bilkul ulta ho. Har paper me in dono ke milay huay paanch chhe sawal lazmi hote hain. Aur yahan aik khoobsurat baat ye hai ke ye dono sawal aik hi tayyari se hal hote hain. Jo student lafz ko us ke matlab, us ke ham maani aur us ke ultay, teeno ke sath seekhta hai, wo aik parhai me do sawalon ka haq ada kar deta hai. Isi liye is lesson me har lafz ko jore ki surat me de raha hun. Aik taraf lafz aur us ka Urdu matlab, sath us ka synonym, aur sath hi us ka antonym. Teen cheezein, aik line, poori tayyari.

Aik hidayat pehle sun lo. Paper me sab se bara dhoka ye hota hai ke synonym wale sawal ke options me antonym chhupa diya jata hai, aur antonym wale sawal me synonym. Student jaldi me pehla milta julta lafz utha leta hai. Is ka hal aik hi hai. Option chunne se pehle dil me poochho, sawal ne mujh se jaisa manga hai ya ulta. Ye aik second ki ehtiyaat tumhare do number bacha legi. Dusri baat, jo alfaaz is lesson me hain ye wohi hain jo saalon se PPSC, FPSC aur NTS ke papers me ghum rahe hain. Fehrist lambi karne ka koi faida nahi, ye panjis jore pakkay kar lo, kaam ban jayega.

Board par panjis jore

1. Abundant. Urdu matlab hai bharpoor, bohat zyada miqdaar me. Is ka synonym hai plentiful, yani kasrat se mojood. Is ka antonym hai scarce, yani kamyaab, bohat kam. Jumla: Mangoes are abundant in summer but scarce in winter. Aik hi jumle me dono taraf aa gayi, yehi is jore ko yaad karne ka behtreen tareeqa hai.

2. Ancient. Urdu matlab hai qadeem, bohat purana. Synonym hai old. Antonym hai modern, yani jadeed. Jumla: Multan is an ancient city with a modern airport. Purani cheez ke liye ancient, aaj ki cheez ke liye modern.

3. Brave. Urdu matlab hai bahadur, nidar. Synonym hai courageous. Antonym hai cowardly, yani buzdil. Jumla: A brave soldier never runs away, but a cowardly man hides at the first sound of danger. Paper me brave ka antonym aksar coward ya cowardly me se ata hai, dono pehchan lo.

4. Cruel. Urdu matlab hai zaalim, sangdil. Synonym hai merciless, yani be reham. Antonym hai kind, yani meherban. Jumla: The cruel master punished the servant, but the kind neighbour gave him food. Cruel ka ulta kind hai, ye jora bohat dohraaya jata hai.

5. Expand. Urdu matlab hai phailana, bara karna. Synonym hai enlarge. Antonym hai contract, yani sikorna, chhota karna. Jumla: Metal expands in heat and contracts in cold. Ye jumla science ke paper me bhi ata hai aur English me bhi, dono jagah kaam ayega.

6. Freedom. Urdu matlab hai azadi. Synonym hai liberty. Antonym hai slavery, yani ghulami. Jumla: Our forefathers won freedom and ended slavery. Liberty aur freedom bilkul aik matlab ke hain, paper in dono ko apas me badal kar deta hai.

7. Genuine. Urdu matlab hai asli, khalis. Synonym hai authentic, aur aam bol chal me real bhi yehi hai. Antonym hai fake, yani jaali. Jumla: This is a genuine leather bag, not a fake one. Asli aur jaali ka ye jora roz ki zindagi se juda hai, foran yaad ho jayega.

8. Humble. Urdu matlab hai aajiz, inkisari wala. Synonym hai modest. Antonym hai proud, yani maghroor. Jumla: Despite his success, he remained humble, while his brother became proud and rude. Humble ka ulta proud hai, ye baat zehen me bitha lo.

9. Ignorant. Urdu matlab hai la ilm, na waqif. Synonym hai unaware. Antonym hai aware, yani ba khabar, waqif. Jumla: He was ignorant of the new rule, but now he is fully aware of it. Ignorant ka matlab bewaqoof nahi hai, lafz ka ye dhoka paper me chalta hai. Matlab hai ilm na hona.

10. Joy. Urdu matlab hai khushi, musarrat. Synonym hai delight. Antonym hai sorrow, yani gham. Jumla: The news of his success filled the family with joy, not sorrow. Khushi aur gham ka ye jora sab se seedha hai.

11. Keen. Is ke do matlab hain, tez bhi aur shaukeen bhi. Synonym dono ke hisaab se sharp aur eager hai. Antonym hai dull, yani kund aur be dilchaspi. Jumla: She has a keen interest in history and a keen eye for detail. Paper keen ko aksar shaukeen wale matlab me leta hai, dono matlab yaad rakho.

12. Loyal. Urdu matlab hai wafadar. Synonym hai faithful. Antonym hai disloyal, aur jo shakhs wafadari tore use noun me traitor kehte hain. Jumla: A loyal friend stands by you, but a disloyal man leaves you in trouble. Loyal ka ulta traitor kehna paper me chalta hai lekin asal antonym disloyal hai, farq samajh lo.

13. Mercy. Urdu matlab hai reham, daya. Synonym hai compassion. Antonym hai cruelty, yani zulm. Jumla: The judge showed mercy to the young man and freed him from cruelty of a long sentence. Mercy aur cruel ka rishta is lesson ke chauthe jore se bhi juda hai, dono ko mila kar yaad karo.

14. Narrow. Urdu matlab hai tang, kam chora. Synonym hai slim is surat me kam istemal hota hai, asal synonym hai tight ya thin, lekin paper me narrow ka brabar aksar limited bhi ata hai. Antonym hai wide, yani chora, wasee. Jumla: The narrow street of the old city opens into a wide road. Tang aur chora, ye jora roz dekhte ho.

15. Obey. Urdu matlab hai hukm manna, farmabardari karna. Synonym hai follow, ya comply. Antonym hai disobey, yani na farmani karna. Jumla: Good citizens obey the law and never disobey the police. Obey ke aage dis lagte hi ulta ban jata hai, ye usool aur alfaaz me bhi chalta hai jese loyal aur disloyal.

16. Praise. Urdu matlab hai tareef karna. Synonym hai admire, ya applaud. Antonym hai blame, yani ilzam dena ya bura kehna. Jumla: The teacher praised the honest boy and blamed the one who had cheated. Tareef aur ilzam ka ye jora school ki zindagi se juda hai.

17. Rapid. Urdu matlab hai tez, fori. Synonym hai swift, ya fast. Antonym hai slow, yani ahista. Jumla: The rapid flow of the river became slow near the plains. Rapid ka matlab tez raftaar hai, ye lafz akhbaron me bohat ata hai.

18. Scarce. Urdu matlab hai kamyaab, nayaab, bohat kam. Synonym hai rare. Antonym hai plentiful, ya abundant. Jumla: Water is scarce in the desert but plentiful near the river. Ghour karo, ye jora pehle wale abundant ka hi ulta chehra hai. Aik hi jora dono taraf se pakka ho gaya.

19. Transparent. Urdu matlab hai shaffaaf, jis ke aar paar nazar aye. Synonym hai clear. Antonym hai opaque, yani ghair shaffaaf, jis ke aar paar na dikhe. Jumla: The glass is transparent, but the wooden door is opaque. Opaque mushkil lafz hai lekin yehi is sawal ki jaan hai, isay nishan laga lo.

20. Victory. Urdu matlab hai fateh, jeet. Synonym hai triumph. Antonym hai defeat, yani shikast. Jumla: Victory and defeat are part of every game. Ye jumla khud itna mashhoor hai ke paper me isi surat me ajata hai.

21. Wisdom. Urdu matlab hai hikmat, danai. Synonym hai prudence, ya gehri samajh. Antonym hai folly, yani be waqoofi, nadaani. Jumla: Wisdom teaches us patience, but folly makes us hasty. Folly mushkil lafz lagta hai, lekin wisdom ke ultay ke tor par yehi paper ka jawab hota hai.

22. Yield. Is ke do matlab hain, haar manna ya jhukna, aur fasal ya nateeja dena. Synonym pehle matlab me surrender hai, dusre me produce. Antonym hai resist, yani muqabla karna, na jhukna. Jumla: The army refused to yield and resisted bravely. Yield ka sawal aksar haar manne wale matlab me ata hai.

23. Zeal. Urdu matlab hai josh, lagan, garam joshi. Synonym hai enthusiasm, ya passion. Antonym hai apathy, yani be dilchaspi, sard mehri. Jumla: He worked with zeal, while his partner showed complete apathy. Zeal chhota sa lafz hai lekin papers me bohat bar aya hai.

24. Barren. Urdu matlab hai banjar, be paidawar. Synonym hai sterile, ya unproductive. Antonym hai fertile, yani zarkhez. Jumla: Nothing grows in barren land, but fertile land feeds the whole village. Ye jora geography aur agriculture ke jumlon me ata hai.

25. Conceal. Urdu matlab hai chhupana, poshida rakhna. Synonym hai hide. Antonym hai reveal, yani zahir karna, khol dena. Jumla: He tried to conceal the truth, but the letter revealed everything. Chhupana aur kholna, ye akhri jora fehrist ko mukammal karta hai.

Jore yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab trick suno jis se ye panjis jore zehen se kabhi nahi niklen ge. Har jore ko aik jumle me bandh do jis me dono tarafen mojood hon. Jese metal expands in heat and contracts in cold. Aik jumla yaad, do alfaaz aur un ka rishta pakka. Apne jumle khud banao, apni zindagi se. Jo jumla tum ne khud banaya, wo tumhe paper wale din bhi yaad ayega. Dusri trick ye hai ke in joron ko ulta bhi parho. Abundant ka ulta scarce yaad hai to scarce ka ulta abundant bhi wahi se nikal ayega. Paper kabhi aik taraf se poochta hai, kabhi dusri taraf se. Jo student jora dono taraf se janta hai, wo dono sawalon ka malik hai. Aur akhri baat, har jore ke synonym ko bhi usi line me rakho. Teen ka gucha banao, lafz, jaisa, ulta. Teenon aik sath yaad hon ge ya aik sath hi paper me number den ge.

Sawal ki pehchan wali ghalti

Ab wo ghalti suno jo sab se zyada number khaati hai. Ghalat tareeqa: sawal me antonym manga ho aur student synonym wala option chun le. Soorat ye hoti hai ke option dekh kar dil kehta hai ye lafz to bilkul milta hai, aur wahi milta julta hona hi jaal hota hai, kyunke sawal ulta maang raha tha. Sahi tareeqa ye hai ke pehle sawal ka akhri lafz parho. Opposite ya antonym likha hai to zehen me ulta wala khana kholo, similar ya synonym likha hai to jaisa wala khana. Aik aur dhoka ye hai ke option me diye gaye lafz ka koi dusra matlab fit beth jata hai. Keen ka matlab tez bhi hai aur shaukeen bhi. Jumla dekho, phir matlab chuno. Ye do ehtiyaatein tumhare vocabulary wale saare number mehfooz kar den gi.

Jawab aur un ki wajah

1. Jawab B hai, plentiful. Scarce is ka ulta hai, option A wohi dhoka tha jo pehle btaya tha.
2. Jawab C hai, modern. Old is ka synonym hai, antonym wale sawal me synonym hi sab se bara jaal hota hai.
3. Jawab B hai, authentic. Fake is ka antonym hai, sawal synonym ka tha.
4. Jawab C hai, contract. Metal garmi me expand aur thand me contract karta hai, wahi jumla yaad karo.
5. Jawab B hai, hide. Reveal is ka ulta hai.
6. Jawab C hai, defeat. Triumph victory ka synonym hai, sawal ulta maang raha tha.
7. Jawab B hai, traitor. Wafadar kabhi ghaddar nahi hota. Antonym disloyal hai aur shakhs traitor kehlata hai.
8. Jawab B hai, swift. Rapid aur swift dono tez ke hain.
9. Jawab C hai, opaque. Shaffaaf ka ulta ghair shaffaaf, yani opaque.
10. Jawab B hai, blame. Admire aur applaud dono tareef wale hain, ulta sirf blame hai.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye pehli tasveer tarazu wali ho. Aik palre me lafz aur us ka synonym ho aur dusre palre me us ka antonym, aur tarazu bilkul darmiyan me khara ho. Student dekh kar samajh jayega ke har lafz ke do pehlu hain, aik jaisa aur aik ulta, aur dono ko sath tolna hai.

Doosri tasveer seedhi wali ho jis ke har khane me aik jora bandha ho. Pehle khane me abundant aur scarce, dusre me ancient aur modern, teesre me victory aur defeat. Har khane ke sath chhota sa jumla ho jis me dono alfaaz mojood hon. Ye seedhi aik nazar me poori fehrist dohra degi, aur paper se pehle wali raat yehi tasveer sab se zyada kaam ayegi.
