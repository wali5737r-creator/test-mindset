Lesson 40: Confusing Words and Spelling
Ulajhne wale alfaaz aur ghalat hone wale spelling. Bees jore aur spelling ke pakke qanoon

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

Dekho, English me kuch alfaaz aise hain jo sunne me aik jaise hain ya dekhne me aik doosre ke bhai lagte hain, lekin un ka matlab zameen asman ka farq rakhta hai. Advice aur advise me sirf c aur s ka farq hai, lekin aik cheez hai aur dusra kaam. Principal aur principle me bhi yehi haal hai. One paper me ye alfaaz do tareeqon se ate hain. Kabhi jumla de kar sahi lafz chunna hota hai, aur kabhi chaar spellings de kar sahi wala pehchanna hota hai. Ye dono sawal un students ko phansate hain jo alfaaz ko aankh se nahi, andaze se parhte hain. Aur khoobi ye hai ke ye wala hissa sab se zyada pakka hota hai, kyunke jore tay hain aur spelling ke qanoon tay hain. Jo aik dafa samajh liya, wo har paper me kaam ayega.

Seekhne ka usool bhi sun lo. Har jore ko farq wali aik line se yaad karo. Farq jumle me dikhao, akelay lafz me nahi. Advice cheez hai jo di jati hai, advise kaam hai jo kiya jata hai. Ye line zehen me beth gayi to jora kabhi nahi ulega. Is lesson ke pehle hisse me bees jore isi tareeqe se de raha hun. Dusre hisse me spelling ke wo qanoon hain jo paper ke spelling wale sawal ko hal karte hain, aur aakhir me wo das alfaaz jo duniya me sab se zyada ghalat likhe jate hain aur hamare papers me bar bar ate hain.

Board par bees ulajhne wale jore

1. Their, there aur they're. Their ka matlab hai un ka, malikiyat. There ka matlab hai wahan, jagah. They're ka matlab hai they are ka chhota roop. Jumla: Their house is there, and they're going to sell it. Teeno aik hi jumle me aa gaye. Their me malikiyat dekho, there me jagah, aur they're ko hamesha they are keh kar parh lo, farq khud saaf ho jayega.

2. Affect aur effect. Affect kaam hai, asar dalna. Effect cheez hai, asar ya nateeja. Jumla: The weather affected his health, and the effect was serious. Yaad rakhne ki line ye hai, affect action hai aur effect end result hai. Dono ke pehle lafz se hi ishara mil gaya.

3. Accept aur except. Accept ka matlab hai qabool karna. Except ka matlab hai siwa, ke ilawa. Jumla: He accepted all the gifts except the watch. Qabool karna aur chor dena, ye dono ultay kaam hain, is liye in ka ultna sab se khatarnak hai.

4. Advice aur advise. Advice cheez hai, mashwara jo diya jata hai. Advise kaam hai, mashwara dena. Jumla: He advised me to follow the doctor's advice. C wali cheez hai aur s wala kaam. C ko cheez se jor do, kabhi nahi bhoolo ge.

5. Lose aur loose. Lose ka matlab hai khona ya haar jana. Loose ka matlab hai dheela. Jumla: Do not lose your loose change in the bus. Aik o wala khona hai, do o wala dheela hai. Do o wali dheel khud spelling me nazar ajati hai.

6. Principal aur principle. Principal shakhs hai, school ka sarbarah, ya asal raqam. Principle usool hai, qanoon. Jumla: The principal of our college is a man of principle. Yaad rakhne ki line ye hai, principal tumhara pal yani dost jaisa shakhs hai, aur principle sirf usool. Akhri pal aur ple ka farq hi jaan hai.

7. Stationary aur stationery. Stationary ka matlab hai ruka hua, jo harkat na kare. Stationery ka matlab hai likhne ka samaan, kagaz aur qalam waghera. Jumla: The bus remained stationary near the stationery shop. E wala samaan hai, envelope wala e yaad rakho, kagaz bhi usi dukaan par milta hai.

8. Later aur latter. Later ka matlab hai baad me, waqt ke liye. Latter ka matlab hai dusra wala, do cheezon me se akhri zikr wala. Jumla: He came later, and of tea and coffee he preferred the latter. Later me waqt hai, latter me tartib. Coffee dusri cheez thi, is liye latter aya.

9. Beside aur besides. Beside ka matlab hai ke sath, baghal me. Besides ka matlab hai ke ilawa, is ke sath sath. Jumla: He sat beside me, and besides this book he bought two more. Aik s ka farq hai aur wohi matlab badal deta hai. Jagah ho to beside, ilawa ho to besides.

10. Elder aur older. Elder sirf ghar ke logo aur rishton ke liye hai aur is ke baad than nahi ata. Older har cheez ke liye hai aur than ke sath ata hai. Jumla: He is my elder brother, and he is older than me. Elder brother sahi hai, elder than me ghalat hai. Ye chhoti si baat paper me bar bar parhi jati hai.

11. Desert aur dessert. Desert registan hai, aur verb ke tor par chor jana bhi hai. Dessert meetha hai jo khane ke baad khaya jata hai. Jumla: Camels live in the desert, and we had ice cream for dessert. Dessert me do s hain, mithaas wale do hisse yaad rakho. Meetha zyada pyara hai is liye us ke s bhi do hain.

12. Quiet aur quite. Quiet ka matlab hai khamosh, pur sukoon. Quite ka matlab hai bilkul ya kafi. Jumla: The room was quiet, and I was quite tired. E ki jagah hi farq hai. Quite me e aakhir me hai, quiet me darmiyan me. Parhne me ahista parho, paper yahi parhta hai.

13. Than aur then. Than muqable ke liye hai, se zyada. Then waqt ke liye hai, phir, us waqt. Jumla: He is taller than me, and then he left. Muqabla ho to than, waqt ka silsila ho to then.

14. Weather aur whether. Weather mausam hai. Whether ka matlab hai ke aya, ya ya. Jumla: I do not know whether the weather will be fine tomorrow. Dono aik hi jumle me aa gaye. Mausam wale me ea ki awaaz saaf hai, aur whether faisle wale sawal me ata hai.

15. Brake aur break. Brake gaari rokne wala purza hai, aur rokna kaam bhi. Break torna hai, ya darmiyan ka waqfa. Jumla: He pressed the brake so hard that it did not break. Rokna brake, torna break. Akhbar me brake failure wali khabar yehi lafz istemal karti hai.

16. Dairy aur diary. Dairy doodh aur us se bani cheezon ki jagah ya karobar hai. Diary roz ki baatein likhne wali kitab hai, roz namcha. Jumla: He writes in his diary that he visited a dairy farm. Diary me i darmiyan me hai jese roz ki line, dairy me ai ka jora hai. Jumle se farq pakka kar lo.

17. Eligible aur illegible. Eligible ka matlab hai ahel, kisi cheez ke qabil hona. Illegible ka matlab hai jo parha na ja sake, ghaseet khut. Jumla: He is eligible for the post, but his handwriting is illegible. Aik insan ki ahliyat hai, dusra us ki likhai. Dono dekhne me bhai lagte hain, matlab me ajnabi.

18. Eminent aur imminent. Eminent ka matlab hai mashhoor, numaya shakhsiyat. Imminent ka matlab hai ane wala, jo bas hone hi wala ho. Jumla: An eminent scholar warned that a storm was imminent. E wala insan hai, i wala waqt aur khatra. Ye farq paper me khaas tor par rakha jata hai.

19. Farther aur further. Farther asal faasle ke liye hai jo naapa ja sake. Further mazeed ya agay ke matlab me hai, baat aur maloomat ke liye. Jumla: The village is farther than I thought, and we need further information. Naap tol ho to farther, baat aage barhe to further.

20. Human aur humane. Human ka matlab hai insani, insan se mutaliq. Humane ka matlab hai reham dil, insaniat wala bartao. Jumla: To err is human, but to forgive is humane. Akhri e reham lata hai. Ye jumla khud itna mashhoor hai ke paper me isi surat me ajata hai.

Ab spelling ke qanoon samajh lo

Ab dusra hissa suno, spelling. Spelling wala sawal mushkil is liye lagta hai ke student lafz ko awaaz se likhta hai, jabke English ke spelling awaaz ke ghulam nahi hote. Lekin kuch qanoon hain jo aadhe se zyada maslay hal kar dete hain. Pehla qanoon ie aur ei ka hai. Aam usool ye hai ke i pehle ayega aur e baad me, jese believe, piece, field. Lekin jab in se pehle c ho to usool ulta ho jata hai, pehle e phir i, jese receive, deceive, ceiling. Board par isay yun likha jata hai, i before e, except after c. Ye aik line hazaron alfaaz ki chabi hai. Dusra qanoon dohre hurf wala hai. Kuch alfaaz me aik lafz do dafa ata hai aur student aik gira deta hai. Is ka hal ye hai ke mashhoor dohre hurf wale alfaaz ko un ke jore ke sath yaad karo, jo aage das wali fehrist me de raha hun.

Aur ab wo das alfaaz jo sab se zyada ghalat hote hain

1. Accommodation. Is me c bhi do hain aur m bhi do. Ye fehrist ka sab se mashhoor ghalat hone wala lafz hai. Accommodation ko yun yaad karo, rehne ki jagah bari hai, hurf bhi bare bare, do do.

2. Occurrence. Is me c bhi do hain aur r bhi do. Accommodation ke sath is ka jora bana lo, dono me do jore hain.

3. Separate. Is me darmiyan wala a sab se zyada bhoolta hai, log seprate likh dete hain. Yaad karne ki line ye hai, separate ke darmiyan me rat chhupa hai, a rat. A wala hissa dekh kar lafz kabhi ghalat nahi hoga.

4. Definitely. Is me i ki jagah a likhna sab se aam ghalti hai, definately ghalat hai. Darmiyan me finite wala hissa hai, wahi se pakro.

5. Government. Is me pehla n aksar gir jata hai, goverment ghalat hai. Govern ke baad ment hai, govern pura likho phir ment lagao.

6. Environment. Is me bhi darmiyan ka n chhupa hota hai, enviroment ghalat hai. Environ ke baad ment hai, n lazmi hai.

7. Necessary. Is me c aik hai aur s do hain. Mashhoor line ye hai, aik collar aur do sleeves, qameez ka aik gala aur do aastein. Necessary ki tasveer qameez se bana lo, spelling pakki ho jayegi.

8. Embarrass. Is me r bhi do hain aur s bhi do. Sharmindagi wala lafz khud bhi bhari hai, dono hurf dohre hain.

9. Success. Is me c bhi do hain aur s bhi do, aur aakhir me bhi ss hai. Kamyabi wale lafz me dohre hurf hi hurf hain, yehi is ki pehchan hai.

10. Tomorrow. Is me m aik hai aur r do hain. Log tommorrow likh kar m bhi dohra dete hain. Yaad rakho, kal wale lafz me r ka jora hai, m akela hai.

Spelling aur jore yaad rakhne ki trick

Trick ye hai ke har mushkil lafz ko aik chhoti si tasveer ya line se bandh do. Necessary ki qameez, separate ka chhupa hua rat, dessert ki do mithaas wali s. Zehen kahani aur tasveer ko hamesha sambhal kar rakhta hai, akelay hurf ko nahi. Dusri trick ye hai ke spelling wale sawal me lafz ko ahista ahista, hissa hissa karke parho. Paper ke chaar options me se teen me aik na aik hurf gira ya badla hota hai. Jo student lafz ko poora, sabr se dekhta hai, wo gira hua hurf pakar leta hai. Jaldi yahan sab se bara dushman hai.

Aik aam ghalti jo paper me hoti hai

Ghalat: The doctor's advise was very useful. Sahi: The doctor's advice was very useful. Wajah: yahan mashwara cheez hai, is liye c wala advice ayega. Advise kaam hai, dena. Dusri ghalti dekho. Ghalat: He is elder than me. Sahi: He is older than me. Wajah: than ke sath elder kabhi nahi ata, older ata hai. Elder sirf elder brother jaisi surat me hai. Teesri ghalti spelling ki hai. Ghalat options me accomodation aur occurence de kar parkha jata hai ke tum ne dohre hurf ginne hain ya nahi. Sahi hamesha accommodation aur occurrence hai, dono me dono hurf dohre. In teen jaalon ko pehchan lo to is lesson ke sawal tumhare liye khulay number hain.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye pehli tasveer joron wali deewar ho. Har jora aamne saamne likha ho aur darmiyan me farq wali aik line ho, advice cheez, advise kaam, principal shakhs, principle usool. Har jore ka apna rang ho. Student deewar ko aik nazar dekhe aur saare farq us ke zehen me taaza ho jayen.

Doosri tasveer spelling wale das alfaaz ki seedhi ho. Har khane me aik lafz itna bara likha ho ke us ke dohre hurf alag rang me chamken. Accommodation ke do c aur do m, necessary ka aik c aur do s. Rang hi student ko btayega ke paper ki nazar kis hurf par hai, aur wahi hurf usay exam hall me bhi nazar ajayega.
