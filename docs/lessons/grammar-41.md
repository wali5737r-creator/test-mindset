Lesson 41: Common Mistakes 1. Subject-Verb Agreement ki Ghaltiyan
Faail aur fail ka mel. Paper ki sab se zyada dohrai jane wali ghalti

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

One paper ke English hisse me aik sawal aisa hota hai jisme aik jumla diya hota hai aur tumhe us me chhupi ghalti pakarni hoti hai. Is ko error detection kehte hain. Aur suno, is qism ke sawalon me sab se zyada ghaltiyan faail aur fail ke mel par banti hain. Angrezi me isay subject-verb agreement kehte hain, yani faail aik ho to fail bhi aik wala, faail zyada hon to fail bhi zyada wala.

Ab ye bhi samajh lo ke examiner tumhe phansata kese hai. Wo jaan boojh kar faail aur fail ke darmiyan lambi doori bana deta hai. Beech me alfaaz ka aik dher laga deta hai, taake tum fail ke bilkul sath wale lafz ko faail samajh kar ghalat fail chun lo. Asal pehchan ka tareeqa sirf aik hai. Pehle poore jumle ka asal faail dhundo, chahe wo kahin bhi chhupa ho, aur phir us se aik sawal poochho ke tum aik ho ya zyada. Jo jawab mile, fail usi hisaab se hoga. Is lesson ki har ghalti isi aik jaal ki koi na koi shakal hai.

Pehle wo khandan jo dekhne me zyada, asal me aik hai

Sab se pehla jaal ye hai ke kuch alfaaz dekhne me bohat zyada lagte hain, lekin grammar me wo aik aik karke ginay jate hain. Each, everyone, everybody, no one, nobody, one of — ye sab is khandan ke hain. In ke sath fail hamesha aik wala ayega.

1. Ghalat: Each of the boys have submitted his homework.
Sahi: Each of the boys has submitted his homework.
Wajah: each ka matlab hai har aik alag alag. Aik aik karke dekho ge to fail bhi aik wala hoga, has.

2. Ghalat: Everyone in the hall were silent.
Sahi: Everyone in the hall was silent.
Wajah: everyone sunne me poori jamaat lagta hai, lekin grammar me ye aik aik shakhs hai. Was ayega.

3. Ghalat: One of my friends live in Karachi.
Sahi: One of my friends lives in Karachi.
Wajah: asal faail one hai, jo aik hai. Friends sirf of ke peeche khara hai, faail nahi hai. Isi liye lives aya, s ke sath.

4. Ghalat: He is one of the best student in the college.
Sahi: He is one of the best students in the college.
Wajah: ye wali ghalti bilkul ulti hai aur paper ki bohat pasandeeda hai. One of the ke baad noun hamesha zyada wala hoga, kyunke matlab hai bohat so me se aik. Students hoga, student nahi.

Ab mehman alfaaz wala jaal

Dusra jaal mehmano ka hai. Kuch alfaaz jumle me aise ghus jate hain jese wo faail ka hissa hon, lekin grammar me wo mehmaan hain. Along with, as well as, together with, in addition to — in ke sath jo noun aye, wo faail nahi banta. Fail hamesha asal faail se milega, mehmaan se nahi.

5. Ghalat: The teacher, along with his students, were going on a trip.
Sahi: The teacher, along with his students, was going on a trip.
Wajah: asal faail teacher hai, jo aik hai. Students along with ke sath aya hua mehmaan hai. Fail teacher se milega, was.

6. Ghalat: He as well as his brothers are ready.
Sahi: He as well as his brothers is ready.
Wajah: as well as sunne me and jaisa lagta hai, lekin grammar me ye and nahi hai. Pehla faail He hai, fail is ayega.

Ab number wala jaal, jo sab ko dhoka deta hai

Teesra jaal number ka hai, aur ye sab se khatarnak hai kyunke yahan do jumle bilkul aik jaisay lagte hain lekin un ke fail ulti taraf chalte hain. A number of aur the number of me zameen asman ka farq hai. A number of ka matlab hai bohat se, yani asal faail peeche wale log ya cheezein hain. Aur the number of ka matlab hai kul tadaad, yani faail khud number hai, jo aik hi hota hai.

7. Ghalat: A number of students was absent today.
Sahi: A number of students were absent today.
Wajah: a number of matlab bohat se students. Faail students hai, jo zyada hain, is liye were.

8. Ghalat: The number of students in this college are increasing.
Sahi: The number of students in this college is increasing.
Wajah: yahan baat tadaad ki ho rahi hai, students ki nahi. Tadaad aik hai, is liye is.

Ab wo cheezein jo aik unit ban kar ati hain

Chautha jaal un cheezon ka hai jo dekhne me zyada hoti hain lekin matlab me aik poori ikai, aik unit, hoti hain. Raqam, waqt, faasla — jab ye aik kul miqdaar ke tor par ayen to fail aik wala hoga. Isi khandan me kuch alfaaz aise bhi hain jo hamesha aik wale rehte hain chahe un ke aakhir me s hi kyun na ho, jaise news aur subjects ke naam. Aur kuch aise hain jo hamesha zyada wale hote hain, jaise police aur cattle.

9. Ghalat: The police has arrested the thief.
Sahi: The police have arrested the thief.
Wajah: police aik idara lagta hai, lekin grammar me ye hamesha zyada wala hai. Have ayega.

10. Ghalat: The cattle is grazing in the field.
Sahi: The cattle are grazing in the field.
Wajah: cattle maweshion ki jamaat hai aur hamesha plural rehta hai. Is ke sath s bhi nahi lagta aur fail bhi zyada wala ata hai.

11. Ghalat: Ten thousand rupees are a big amount for him.
Sahi: Ten thousand rupees is a big amount for him.
Wajah: yahan dus hazar alag alag sikay nahi ginay ja rahe, aik kul raqam ki baat ho rahi hai. Raqam aik unit hai, is liye is.

12. Ghalat: Two hours are enough for this paper.
Sahi: Two hours is enough for this paper.
Wajah: waqt bhi jab aik muddat ke tor par aye to aik unit hai. Is ayega.

13. Ghalat: Bread and butter are his favourite breakfast.
Sahi: Bread and butter is his favourite breakfast.
Wajah: ye do alag cheezein nahi hain, aik hi nashta hai. Jab and se juday do lafz aik hi cheez ban jayen to fail aik wala ata hai.

14. Ghalat: Mathematics are an easy subject for him.
Sahi: Mathematics is an easy subject for him.
Wajah: subjects ke naam, Mathematics, Physics, Economics, chahe s par khatam hon, aik hi subject hain. Is ayega. Aur news bhi yunhi hai, this news is, are kabhi nahi.

Aakhir me qareeb wala qanoon

Aakhri jaal neither aur nor ka hai, aur yahan qanoon bilkul seedha hai. Neither ke sath nor aye, ya either ke sath or aye, to fail us faail se milega jo fail ke sab se qareeb hai. Door wala faail bhool jao, qareeb wala dekho.

15. Ghalat: Neither the teacher nor the students was present.
Sahi: Neither the teacher nor the students were present.
Wajah: fail ke qareeb students hai, jo zyada hain. Were ayega, chahe door teacher aik hi kyun na ho.

16. Ghalat: Neither Ali nor his friend were at home.
Sahi: Neither Ali nor his friend was at home.
Wajah: is dafa qareeb wala friend hai, jo aik hai. Was ayega. Dekha, qanoon aik hai, qareeb wala jeet-ta hai.

17. Ghalat: More than one student have failed in English.
Sahi: More than one student has failed in English.
Wajah: more than one sunne me zyada lagta hai, lekin is ke andar one ki rooh hai. Student bhi aik wala aya hai, fail bhi aik wala hoga, has.

Apna jumla parkhne ka tareeqa

Ab wo tareeqa jo tumhe paper ke hall me tees second ke andar har aise jumle ko parkhne ki taqat dega. Isay mehmaan bhagao wala tareeqa kehte hain. Pehla qadam, jumle me wo hissa zehen se kaat do jo commas ke darmiyan hai ya along with, as well as, together with se shuru hota hai. Wo mehmaan hai, usay bahar nikalo. Dusra qadam, jo asal faail bacha, us ke neeche zehen me lakeer lagao. Teesra qadam, us faail se aik sawal poochho, tum aik ho ya zyada. Aur chautha qadam, fail ko usi jawab se mila kar dekho.

Aik aur chhoti si nishani bhi zehen me bitha lo, jo bohat kaam ki hai. English me fail ke aakhir me s ana aam tor par is baat ki nishani hai ke faail aik hai. Has, is, was, plays, goes — ye sab aik wale faail ke sath ate hain. Ye ulta qanoon hai, lekin yahi paper me sab se zyada kaam ata hai. Fail par s dekho to faail aik dhundo, fail khaali dekho to aksar faail zyada hoga.

Jawab aur un ki wajah

1. Jawab B hai. Each aik aik karke dekhta hai, fail has ayega. Baaki teeno me fail zyada wala laga hai.
2. Jawab C hai. Baat tadaad ki hai, applications ki nahi. The number aik hai, is liye is.
3. Jawab B hai. Neither nor me fail qareeb wale faail se milta hai. Qareeb players hai, jo zyada hain, is liye were.
4. Jawab D hai. Along with wala hissa mehmaan hai. Asal faail headmaster aik hai, is liye has.
5. Jawab C hai. Pair aik hai, chahe shoes do hon. Are ki jagah is hona chahiye tha. Baaki teeno jumle bilkul theek hain, police ke sath have sahi hai, news ke sath is sahi hai, aur faasla aik unit hai.
6. Jawab B hai. One aik hai, was ayega.
7. Jawab C hai. A number of ka matlab bohat se hai. Books zyada hain, are ayega. Yahi number wale jaal ka ulti taraf wala hissa hai.
8. Jawab B hai. Bread and butter aik nashta hai, aik unit, is liye is.
9. Jawab D hai. More than one ke andar one ki rooh hai, fail aik wala hoga, was.
10. Jawab B hai. One of the ke baad noun hamesha zyada wala hota hai, men. Man ka plural men hota hai, mans kabhi nahi.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye pehli tasveer mehmaan bhagao wale tareeqe ki honi chahiye. Aik lamba jumla board par likha ho, us ka darmiyan wala hissa, along with his students, halke rang me kata hua dikhaya gaya ho aur us ke upar likha ho ye mehmaan hai. Asal faail aur fail mote rang me chamak rahe hon, taake student ki aankh hamesha pehle asal faail par jaye.

Doosri tasveer number wale jaal ki ho. Aik taraf a number of students khade hon aur un ke sath bara sa WERE likha ho. Doosri taraf aik bari si tadaad wali parchi ho jis par the number likha ho aur us ke sath bara sa IS ho. Ye dono shaklein dekh kar student zindagi bhar a number of aur the number of ko nahi milayega.
