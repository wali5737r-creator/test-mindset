Lesson 42: Common Mistakes 2. Tense ki Ghaltiyan
Waqt ka ishara dekho, fail khud sahi ho jayega

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

Error detection ke sawalon me dusri sab se bari qism tense ki ghaltiyon ki hai. Aur is qism ki khaas baat ye hai ke is me jumla dekhne me bilkul theek lagta hai. Alfaaz bhi sahi hote hain, matlab bhi banta hai, bas waqt ka mel bigra hota hai. Examiner janta hai ke Pakistani student verb ki formain to rat leta hai, lekin ye nahi seekhta ke kis waqt ke sath konsi form jayegi.

Pehchanne ka tareeqa yahan bhi aik hi hai. Har tense wale jumle me kahin na kahin waqt ka aik ishara chhupa hota hai. Yesterday, since, for, when, if, said — ye sab isharay hain. Pehle wo ishara dhundo, phir fail ko us isharay se milao. Ishara aur fail ka mel theek hai to jumla theek hai, mel bigra hai to wahi ghalti hai. Is lesson ki har ghalti kisi na kisi isharay ki nafarmani hai.

Pehle since aur for wala khandan

Sab se pehla aur sab se mashhoor ishara since hai. Qanoon zehen me bitha lo. Jab koi kaam guzre waqt se shuru hokar ab tak chal raha ho, to English me have ya has wali perfect shakal ati hai, seedhi present wali nahi. Aur since ke sath waqt ka nuqta-e-aghaz ata hai, jaise since 2015, since Monday, since morning. Muddat, yani kitni der, batani ho to for ata hai, jaise for three days, for two hours.

1. Ghalat: I am living in Lahore since 2015.
Sahi: I have been living in Lahore since 2015.
Wajah: since dekhte hi perfect wali shakal dhundo. Am living Urdu ke asar se ata hai, English me yahan have been living hoga.

2. Ghalat: He has been ill since three days.
Sahi: He has been ill for three days.
Wajah: three days muddat hai, aghaz ka nuqta nahi. Muddat par for, nuqte par since. Ye chhota sa farq har dusre paper me ata hai.

3. Ghalat: I have met him yesterday.
Sahi: I met him yesterday.
Wajah: yesterday guzra hua tay waqt hai. Jahan waqt tay hokar guzar gaya ho, wahan have wala perfect nahi chalta, seedha past chalta hai. Yesterday aur have dushman hain, aik jumle me nahi reh sakte.

Ab did wala qanoon, jo fail ko pehli shakal me wapas bhejta hai

Dusra ishara did hai. Did khud guzre waqt ki nishani hai. Jab jumle me did a gaya to waqt ka kaam ho gaya, ab asal fail apni pehli shakal me wapas ajayega. Did ke baad dusri shakal lagana aisi hi ghalti hai jese aik kaam do dafa karna.

4. Ghalat: Did you went to school yesterday?
Sahi: Did you go to school yesterday?
Wajah: went ki jagah go ayega. Did pehle hi bata raha hai ke baat guzre waqt ki hai.

5. Ghalat: He did not came with us.
Sahi: He did not come with us.
Wajah: did not ke baad bhi pehli shakal. Came nahi, come.

6. Ghalat: He has went to Karachi.
Sahi: He has gone to Karachi.
Wajah: has aur have ke baad hamesha teesri shakal ati hai. Go ki teesri shakal gone hai, went dusri hai. Ye ghalti bol chal me itni aam hai ke paper me lazmi ati hai.

7. Ghalat: I had went to see him, but he was not at home.
Sahi: I had gone to see him, but he was not at home.
Wajah: had ke baad bhi teesri shakal. Has, have, had — teeno ke baad gone, done, seen, written wali teesri shakal hi ayegi.

Ab do guzre kaam aur pehle wale ka khaas fail

Teesra ishara wo hai jahan aik hi jumle me do kaam guzre waqt ke hon. Wahan ye dekhna hota hai ke pehle konsa hua. Jo kaam pehle hua, us ke liye had wali shakal, yani past perfect, ati hai. Jo baad me hua, us ke liye seedha past. Student aksar dono par seedha past laga deta hai aur tartib bigar jati hai.

8. Ghalat: When I reached the station, the train left.
Sahi: When I reached the station, the train had left.
Wajah: train mere pohonchne se pehle nikal chuki thi. Pehle wala kaam had left, baad wala reached.

9. Ghalat: The patient died before the doctor arrived.
Sahi: The patient had died before the doctor arrived.
Wajah: marna pehle hua, doctor ka ana baad me. Before wale jumle me ye tartib parkhna paper ka pasandeeda khel hai.

Ab khayali jumle, jahan was ki jagah were ata hai

Chautha ishara if aur wish hain. Jab baat khayal ki ho, aisi surat jo asal me hai hi nahi, to English me was ki jagah were ata hai, chahe faail aik hi kyun na ho. If I were you, ye jumla tum ne suna hoga. Ye ghalti nahi, yehi qanoon hai.

10. Ghalat: If I was you, I would accept this job.
Sahi: If I were you, I would accept this job.
Wajah: tum main nahi ban sakte, ye khayal hai. Khayali surat me har faail ke sath were.

11. Ghalat: I wish I was a king.
Sahi: I wish I were a king.
Wajah: wish ke baad bhi wahi khayali duniya hai. Were ayega.

Ab reported speech, jahan fail aik qadam peeche jata hai

Paanchva ishara said aur told hain. Jab kisi ki baat doosre ko sunate hain to fail aik qadam peeche chala jata hai. Will ban jata hai would, is ban jata hai was, aur tomorrow ban jata hai the next day. Student ye peeche wala qadam bhool jata hai aur baat wese ki wese likh deta hai.

12. Ghalat: He said that he will come tomorrow.
Sahi: He said that he would come the next day.
Wajah: said guzra waqt hai, to andar wali baat bhi guzre me jayegi. Will se would, aur tomorrow se the next day.

13. Ghalat: She told me that she is very busy today.
Sahi: She told me that she was very busy that day.
Wajah: told ke baad is ka was banta hai aur today ka that day. Aik qadam peeche, har cheez peeche.

14. Ghalat: He asked me where do I live.
Sahi: He asked me where I lived.
Wajah: ye do ghaltiyon wali hai. Aik to sawal wala ulta order jumle ke andar seedha ho jata hai, do I live ki jagah I live. Dusri, asked ke peeche fail bhi peeche gaya, lived.

Aakhir me jode wale aur mashroot jumle

Ab kuch aise jumle jo jodo ki surat me yad rakhne parte hain. No sooner ke sath hamesha than ata hai, when nahi. Hardly aur scarcely ke sath hamesha when ata hai, than nahi. Ye jode ulta dena paper ki tay ghalti hai. Aur when wale waqt ke hisse me will kabhi nahi ata, chahe baat ane wale waqt ki hi kyun na ho.

15. Ghalat: No sooner did he see the police when he ran away.
Sahi: No sooner did he see the police than he ran away.
Wajah: no sooner ka joda than hai. No sooner dekho to than dhundo.

16. Ghalat: Hardly had I reached home than it began to rain.
Sahi: Hardly had I reached home when it began to rain.
Wajah: hardly ka joda when hai. Dono jode aik dusre se badal dena hi yahan ki ghalti hoti hai.

17. Ghalat: When he will come, I will tell him everything.
Sahi: When he comes, I will tell him everything.
Wajah: when ke baad wale hisse me will nahi ata, chahe matlab ane wale waqt ka ho. Will sirf dusre hisse me rahega.

18. Ghalat: If you had worked hard, you will pass.
Sahi: If you had worked hard, you would have passed.
Wajah: had worked guzri hui mashroot hai, to natija bhi guzra hua hoga, would have passed. If wale hisse ka waqt dusre hisse ko bhi apne sath kheench leta hai.

19. Ghalat: It is two years since he has died.
Sahi: It is two years since he died.
Wajah: since ke baad jab poora jumla aye to wo seedhe past me hota hai. Ye bareek ghalti hai aur ache students ko bhi lag jati hai.

Apna jumla parkhne ka tareeqa

Ab wo tareeqa jo tees second me har tense wale jumle ko parakh dega. Isay waqt ke isharay wala tareeqa kehte hain. Pehla qadam, jumla aik dafa parho aur sirf waqt ke isharay dhundo. Yesterday hai, since hai, for hai, did hai, when hai, if hai, said hai — jo bhi mile, us ke neeche zehen me lakeer lagao. Dusra qadam, har isharay ka apna fail khandan zehen me rakho. Since aur for perfect mangte hain, yesterday seedha past mangta hai, did pehli shakal mangta hai, do guzre kaamo me pehle wala had mangta hai, said har cheez ko aik qadam peeche bhejta hai. Teesra qadam, fail ko us isharay se mila kar dekho. Mel kha gaya to jumla saaf hai, na khaya to ghalti wahi hai.

Aik nishani aur yad rakho. Jumle me agar do fail hon to pehle ye tay karo ke kaam pehle konsa hua. Tartib tay ho jaye to aadhi tense ki ghaltiyan khud khatam ho jati hain, kyunke tense asal me waqt ki nahi, tartib ki zabaan hai.

Jawab aur un ki wajah

1. Jawab B hai. Yesterday tay guzra waqt hai, have wala perfect wahan nahi chalta. Seedha past, saw.
2. Jawab C hai. Four days muddat hai, muddat par for ata hai, since nuqte par ata hai.
3. Jawab B hai. Did ke baad fail pehli shakal me wapas ata hai, call.
4. Jawab C hai. Match hamare pohonchne se pehle shuru ho chuka tha. Pehle wala kaam had started.
5. Jawab C hai. Wish ke baad khayali duniya hai, was ki jagah were hona chahiye tha, I wish I were a bird. Baaki teeno jumle theek hain.
6. Jawab B hai. Jumle ke andar sawal seedha jumla ban jata hai aur asked ki wajah se fail aik qadam peeche jata hai, lived.
7. Jawab C hai. Train pehle nikal chuki thi, is liye had left. Has left is liye ghalat hai ke reached guzra waqt hai.
8. Jawab B hai. No sooner ka joda than hai, when nahi.
9. Jawab B hai. When wale hisse me will kabhi nahi ata. Returns aur will start ka mel sahi hai.
10. Jawab D hai. Had informed guzri mashroot hai to natija bhi guzra hoga, would have helped.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye pehli tasveer waqt ki line wali honi chahiye. Aik seedhi line par do station bane hon. Pehle station par train nikalti hui dikhai de aur us ke neeche had left likha ho, dusre station par musafir pohonchta hua ho aur us ke neeche reached likha ho. Student aik nazar me samajh jaye ke jo kaam line par pehle hai, us ka fail had wala hoga.

Doosri tasveer since aur for ki honi chahiye. Aik ghari bani ho jis par aik nuqta nishan laga ho aur us ke sath since Monday likha ho, aur usi ghari ke gird aik lamba faasla dikhaya gaya ho jis par for three days likha ho. Nuqta aur faasla, ye do shaklein hi is farq ko hamesha ke liye zehen me bitha dengi.
