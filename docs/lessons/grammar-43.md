Lesson 43: Common Mistakes 3. Preposition ki Ghaltiyan
Har lafz ka apna joda hota hai, aur paper usi jode ko parkhta hai

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

Error detection me teesri bari qism preposition ki ghaltiyon ki hai. Ye wo chhote chhote alfaaz hain, in, on, at, to, from, with, of, jo jumle me nazar bhi mushkil se ate hain lekin number inhi par kat-ta hai. In ghaltiyon ki sab se bari wajah hamari Urdu hai. Urdu me hum kehte hain mujh se bara, us ke sath shadi, is par gussa — aur student wohi ke wohi English me le ata hai. English ka har lafz apna joda khud chunta hai, Urdu ke tarjume se nahi chunta.

Pehchanne ka tareeqa yahan ye hai. Jumle me wo mashhoor alfaaz dhundo jin ke jode muqarrar hain, jaise married, senior, depend, insist, suffer. Un ke sath wale chhote lafz par ruk kar dekho ke joda sahi hai ya nahi. Aur is ke ulta bhi yad rakho, kuch alfaaz aise hain jo bina kisi jode ke seedha chalte hain, jaise discuss, reach, enter, resemble. Un ke peeche agar koi fazool lafz chipka ho to wahi ghalti hai. Is lesson me dono taraf ki ghaltiyan hain.

Pehle wo alfaaz jin ke peeche kuch nahi lagta

Sab se pehle wo khandan samajh lo jin ke peeche student bila wajah about, in, at, with jod deta hai. Ye alfaaz apna matlab khud poora karte hain. In ke peeche koi chhota lafz dikhe to samjho wahi paper ki ghalti hai.

1. Ghalat: We discussed about the matter for two hours.
Sahi: We discussed the matter for two hours.
Wajah: discuss ke andar hi baat cheet ka matlab poora hai. About lagana Urdu ke "ke baare me" ka asar hai. Discussion noun ke sath about ata hai, lekin discuss verb ke sath nahi.

2. Ghalat: He entered in the room without knocking.
Sahi: He entered the room without knocking.
Wajah: enter ke baad jagah seedhi ati hai, in nahi lagta. Han, aik jagah enter into ata hai, jab muahide ya bandobast ki baat ho, he entered into an agreement. Kamre me dakhil hone par into nahi.

3. Ghalat: We reached at Lahore late at night.
Sahi: We reached Lahore late at night.
Wajah: reach ke baad jagah seedhi ati hai, at nahi. Jumle ke aakhir wala at night bilkul theek hai, wo waqt ke liye hai. Ghalti sirf reach ke sath wale at me hai, aur paper bilkul yunhi phansata hai.

4. Ghalat: She resembles with her mother.
Sahi: She resembles her mother.
Wajah: resemble ke baad with nahi lagta. Shakhs seedha ata hai.

5. Ghalat: He ordered for a cup of tea.
Sahi: He ordered a cup of tea.
Wajah: order jab hukm ya farmayish ke matlab me ho to cheez seedhi ati hai, for nahi lagta.

6. Ghalat: The team comprises of eleven players.
Sahi: The team comprises eleven players.
Wajah: comprise ke baad of nahi ata. Of wala matlab dena ho to lafz badal kar kehte hain, the team consists of eleven players. Consist ke sath of hai, comprise ke sath nahi. Ye joda paper ka bohat purana pasandeeda hai.

Ab wo alfaaz jin ka joda to hai, than nahi

Dusra khandan un alfaazon ka hai jo muqable me ate hain lekin than se dushmani rakhte hain. Senior, junior, superior, inferior, prefer, prior — in sab ke sath to ata hai. Student in par bhi than laga deta hai kyunke Urdu me "se" ata hai aur dusre muqable wale alfaaz, taller, bigger, ke sath than chalta hai. Lekin ye khandan Latin se aya hai aur apna joda to hi rakhta hai.

7. Ghalat: He is senior than me in this office.
Sahi: He is senior to me in this office.
Wajah: senior ke sath to. Yehi qanoon junior, superior aur inferior par bhi hai.

8. Ghalat: She is junior than her sister.
Sahi: She is junior to her sister.
Wajah: junior ke sath bhi to. Than dekhte hi in alfaazon par shak karo.

9. Ghalat: I prefer tea than coffee.
Sahi: I prefer tea to coffee.
Wajah: prefer ke sath bhi to ata hai, than nahi. Pasand ke muqable me bhi yehi joda hai.

Ab wo jode jo hamesha sath chalte hain

Teesra khandan un alfaazon ka hai jin ka joda itna pakka hai ke use alag karna hi ghalti hai. In ko lafz ke sath ratna nahi, jode ki surat me pehchanna hai. Insist ke sath on, depend ke sath on, suffer ke sath from, afraid ke sath of, good ke sath at, married ke sath to. Joda badla to jumla ghalat.

10. Ghalat: She was married with a doctor.
Sahi: She was married to a doctor.
Wajah: married ka joda to hai. Urdu me "ke sath shadi" kehte hain, is liye with ajata hai, lekin English me to hi ayega.

11. Ghalat: He insisted to go home at once.
Sahi: He insisted on going home at once.
Wajah: insist ke sath on ata hai, aur on ke baad verb ki ing wali shakal ati hai, going. Do cheezein aik sath yaad rakho, joda bhi on aur us ke baad ing.

12. Ghalat: Your success depends of your hard work.
Sahi: Your success depends on your hard work.
Wajah: depend ka joda on hai. Dependent ke sath bhi on hi ata hai, he is dependent on his father.

13. Ghalat: She suffers with fever every winter.
Sahi: She suffers from fever every winter.
Wajah: suffer ka joda from hai. Takleef aur beemari ke liye suffer from hi jumla hai.

14. Ghalat: He is afraid from dogs.
Sahi: He is afraid of dogs.
Wajah: afraid ka joda of hai. Dar hamesha of se jodta hai.

15. Ghalat: He is good in English.
Sahi: He is good at English.
Wajah: maharat batani ho to at ata hai. Good at, bad at, clever at — teeno ka joda at hai. In ki jagah in lagana Urdu ke "English me acha hai" ka asar hai.

16. Ghalat: Please listen me carefully.
Sahi: Please listen to me carefully.
Wajah: listen ka joda to hai. Bina to ke listen adhoora rehta hai.

Aakhir me do bareek jode

Ab do aise jode jo aik hi lafz ke do matlabon par alag alag chalte hain. Die ke sath beemari aur bhook ke liye of ata hai, lekin zakham jaisi bahari wajah ke liye from ata hai. Aur angry ke sath shakhs ke liye with ata hai, lekin kisi baat ya natije ke liye at ata hai.

17. Ghalat: He died from cholera last year.
Sahi: He died of cholera last year.
Wajah: beemari se marne par of. Aur zakham se marne par from hota hai, he died from his wounds. Wajah andar ki ho to of, bahar ki ho to from.

18. Ghalat: The teacher was angry on the students.
Sahi: The teacher was angry with the students.
Wajah: shakhs par gussa ho to with. Baat par gussa ho to at, he was angry at the result. On to yahan ata hi nahi.

Aik chhoti si fehrist aur zehen me bitha lo

Jaane se pehle kuch aur jode bhi isi tarah bandh lo, kyunke paper inhen bhi parkhta hai. Wait ke sath for ata hai, we waited for the bus. Look ke sath at ata hai jab nazar kisi cheez par ho, look at the board. Belong ke sath to ata hai, this book belongs to me. Aur jab kisi cheez ki wajah ya sabab batana ho to due ke sath to ata hai, the delay was due to rain. In sab me bhi wahi usool hai, lafz akela nahi, joda yaad karo. Jis din tum alfaaz ko jodo ki surat me dekhna shuru kar do ge, us din se preposition ki ghalti tumhe jumla parhte hi nazar ane lagegi, dhundni nahi paregi.

Apna jumla parkhne ka tareeqa

Ab wo tareeqa jo tees second me preposition wale jumle ko parakh dega. Isay joda parkhne wala tareeqa kehte hain. Pehla qadam, jumle me wo mashhoor alfaaz dhundo jin ke jode muqarrar hain. Married, senior, junior, prefer, depend, insist, suffer, afraid, good, listen, angry — in me se jo bhi mile, wahi rukne ki jagah hai. Dusra qadam, us lafz ke bilkul sath wale chhote lafz ko dekho aur apne zehen ki jodo wali list se milao. Joda mila to theek, na mila to ghalti wahi hai. Teesra qadam, ulta bhi check karo. Discuss, reach, enter, resemble, order, comprise — in ke peeche agar koi chipka hua lafz hai to wahi fazool hai.

Asal baat zehen me bitha lo. Preposition Urdu ke tarjume se nahi banti, lafz ke jode se banti hai. Is liye in ko jumlo me jode ki surat me yad karo. Married to, senior to, depend on — ye poora joda aik lafz ki tarah zehen me rakho ge to paper me kabhi nahi phanso ge.

Jawab aur un ki wajah

1. Jawab B hai. Discuss ke peeche about nahi lagta. About Urdu ke tarjume se ata hai.
2. Jawab C hai. Married ka joda to hai.
3. Jawab D hai. Senior ke sath to ata hai, than kabhi nahi.
4. Jawab B hai. Enter ke baad kamra seedha ata hai. Into muahide ke liye hota hai.
5. Jawab C hai. Reach ke baad at nahi lagta, we reached the airport hona chahiye tha. Baaki teeno ke jode bilkul sahi hain.
6. Jawab B hai. Insist ke sath on, aur on ke baad going.
7. Jawab C hai. Depend ka joda on hai.
8. Jawab B hai. Beemari se marne par of ata hai. From zakham jaisi bahari wajah ke liye hai.
9. Jawab C hai. Shakhs par gussa ho to with. Baat par ho to at hota.
10. Jawab B hai. Comprise ke baad of nahi ata. Of chahiye to consists of kehna hoga.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye pehli tasveer jodo ki zanjeer wali honi chahiye. Har mashhoor lafz aik bare dabbe me likha ho aur us ke sath zanjeer se bandha hua us ka joda ho. Married bandha ho to se, depend bandha ho on se, suffer bandha ho from se. Student dekh kar samajh jaye ke ye jode alag nahi ho sakte.

Doosri tasveer khabardar karne wali ho. Aik taraf wo alfaaz hon jin ke peeche kuch nahi lagta, discuss, reach, enter, resemble, aur un ke upar likha ho seedha chalao, peeche kuch na jodo. Dusri taraf than par kaat ka nishan ho aur us ke neeche senior, junior, prefer khade hon. Ye aik tasveer paper ki aadhi preposition ghaltiyan khatam kar degi.
