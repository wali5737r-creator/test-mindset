Lesson 44: Common Mistakes 4. Article ki Ghaltiyan
A, an aur the. Teen chhote lafz, aur paper ke sab se saste number

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

A, an aur the ko article kehte hain, aur ye teen chhote se lafz one paper me har saal ghalti ban kar ate hain. Pehle lesson me tum ne a aur an ka kaan wala qanoon seekha tha, awaaz suno, letter mat dekho. Is lesson me baat us se aage ki hai. Yahan the ki ghaltiyan bhi hain, aur wo ghaltiyan bhi jahan article lagana hi ghalat hai. Examiner teen taraf se phansata hai. Jahan article chahiye wahan wo use ghayab kar deta hai, jahan nahi chahiye wahan laga deta hai, aur jahan the chahiye wahan a laga deta hai.

Pehchanne ka tareeqa ye hai ke har noun ke samne ruk kar teen sawal poochho. Pehla, kya ye aik hai aur pehli dafa a raha hai. Dusra, kya ye khaas hai, duniya me aik hi hai, ya sab se agay hai. Teesra, kya ye koi aam khayal, poori jins, zubaan ya roz ka kaam hai. Pehle sawal ka jawab han ho to a ya an, dusre ka han ho to the, teesre ka han ho to kuch bhi nahi. Is lesson ki har ghalti inhi teen sawalon me se kisi aik ke galat jawab se bani hai.

Pehle awaaz wala qanoon paper ki satah par

Kaan wala qanoon tum seekh chuke ho, lekin paper me ye aur bareek surat me ata hai. Khaas tor par ohdon aur degrees ke mukhtasar naamo par, jaise SDO, M.A., M.P.A. In ko bol kar dekho, pehli awaaz hi faisla karegi.

1. Ghalat: He is a SDO in the education department.
Sahi: He is an SDO in the education department.
Wajah: S ko bolte hue awaaz "es" ati hai, pehli awaaz vowel ki hai. Is liye an. Paper me ohdon wale jumle bohat ate hain aur yahi un ki ghalti hoti hai.

2. Ghalat: She is studying in an university in Lahore.
Sahi: She is studying in a university in Lahore.
Wajah: university ki awaaz "yu" hai. Letter vowel ka hai, awaaz nahi. A ayega.

Ab jahan the lazmi hai aur student use bhool jata hai

Dusra khandan un jaghon ka hai jahan the lazmi hai. Duniya me aik hi honay wali cheezein, suraj, chand, zameen. Simtein, east, west. Sab se wala darja, best, first, longest. Daryaon, samandaron aur akhbaaron ke naam. Aur same jaisa lafz, jis ke sath the chipka hota hai. In jaghon par the ghayab ho to jumla adhoora lagta hai aur wahi ghalti hai.

3. Ghalat: Sun sets in the west.
Sahi: The sun sets in the west.
Wajah: do the ghayab hain. Suraj duniya me aik hai, the sun. Aur simt ke sath bhi the ata hai, the west, the east.

4. Ghalat: He is best player of our team.
Sahi: He is the best player of our team.
Wajah: sab se wale darje, superlative, ke sath the lazmi hai. Best, tallest, first, last — in se pehle the dhundo, na mile to ghalti wahi hai.

5. Ghalat: Indus is the longest river of Pakistan.
Sahi: The Indus is the longest river of Pakistan.
Wajah: daryaon ke naam ke sath the ata hai, the Indus, the Chenab. Samandaron ke sath bhi, the Pacific, the Atlantic. Is jumle me dusra the to hai, pehla wala ghayab hai, aur paper bilkul yunhi aadha the kha jata hai.

6. Ghalat: I read Dawn every morning.
Sahi: I read the Dawn every morning.
Wajah: akhbaaron ke naam ke sath the ata hai. The Dawn, the News. Naam khaas hai lekin the phir bhi ayega, ye is khandan ka apna qanoon hai.

7. Ghalat: He plays flute very well.
Sahi: He plays the flute very well.
Wajah: saazon, yani bajane wale aalaat, ke sath the ata hai. The flute, the guitar, the tabla.

8. Ghalat: He was first boy to reach the school.
Sahi: He was the first boy to reach the school.
Wajah: tartib wale number, first, second, third, ke sath bhi the lazmi hai.

9. Ghalat: This is same pen that I lost yesterday.
Sahi: This is the same pen that I lost yesterday.
Wajah: same ke sath the chipka hua hai. Same dekho to us se pehle the dhundo.

10. Ghalat: Higher we go, cooler it becomes.
Sahi: The higher we go, the cooler it becomes.
Wajah: ye aik joda hai, the aur the. Muqabla jitna barhta jaye, dusri taraf bhi the ayega. Aik the ghayab ho to jumla ghalat hai.

Ab jahan the lagana hi ghalti hai

Teesra khandan is se ulta hai. Kuch jaghon par student bila wajah the laga deta hai, khaas tor par Urdu ke "wo wala" asar se. Aam matlab me ane wale khayal, jaise honesty, wisdom. Poori jins ke liye Man. Zubanon ke naam. Khaas shehron aur logo ke naam. Aur roz ke kaam, khane aur asal maqsad wali jagahen. In sab par the lagana ghalti hai.

11. Ghalat: The honesty is the best policy.
Sahi: Honesty is the best policy.
Wajah: honesty yahan aam khayal hai, kisi khaas shakhs ki imaandari ki baat nahi ho rahi. Aam khayal par the nahi ata. Ye jumla khud aik kahawat hai aur paper me kahawat hi ghalat karke di jati hai.

12. Ghalat: The man is mortal.
Sahi: Man is mortal.
Wajah: jab Man ka matlab poori insan jins ho to the nahi ata. Han, kisi khaas admi ki baat ho to the man bilkul sahi hai. Farq matlab ka hai.

13. Ghalat: The English is spoken all over the world.
Sahi: English is spoken all over the world.
Wajah: zubaan ke naam par the nahi ata. The English ka matlab hota hai angrez log, zubaan nahi. Ye bareek farq paper ko bohat pasand hai.

14. Ghalat: The Lahore is a historical city.
Sahi: Lahore is a historical city.
Wajah: shehron aur logo ke khaas naamo ke sath the nahi ata. The sirf un khaas naamo ke sath ata hai jo tum upar parh aye ho, darya, samandar, akhbaar.

15. Ghalat: He goes to the school to study.
Sahi: He goes to school to study.
Wajah: jab koi jagah apne asal maqsad ke liye istemal ho to the nahi ata. School parhne ke liye, hospital ilaj ke liye, jail saza ke liye. Han, agar maqsad dusra ho to the ayega, the father went to the school to meet the teacher, kyunke wo parhne nahi gaya tha.

16. Ghalat: The patient was taken to the hospital for treatment.
Sahi: The patient was taken to hospital for treatment.
Wajah: mareez ilaj ke liye gaya hai, yani hospital apne asal maqsad me hai. The nahi ayega. Yehi school wale qanoon ki dusri shakal hai.

17. Ghalat: I had the breakfast at eight o clock.
Sahi: I had breakfast at eight o clock.
Wajah: roz ke khano, breakfast, lunch, dinner, par aam tor par the nahi ata. Kisi khaas dawat ki baat ho to baat alag hai.

Aur aik jaga jahan the ghayab karna ghalti hai

Aakhir me aik aisi surat jo dono taraf se ulta hai. Jab the ke sath koi khoobi wala lafz, adjective, aye to wo poora tabqa ban jata hai. The poor ka matlab hai sare ghareeb log. Wahan the ghayab kar do to jumla toot jata hai.

18. Ghalat: We should help poor.
Sahi: We should help the poor.
Wajah: the poor aik poora tabqa hai. The rich, the blind, the young — in sab me the lazmi hai.

Aik aur qanoon jo aadhi article ghaltiyan khatam kar deta hai

Jaane se pehle aik aur baat zehen me bitha lo, jo in teeno khandon ke upar chalti hai. Jab koi cheez pehli dafa aye to a ya an ata hai, aur jab usi cheez ka zikr dusri dafa ho to wo khaas ho jati hai aur the ajata hai. I saw a dog in the street. The dog was very thin. Pehle jumle me dog naya tha, a aya. Dusre jumle me wahi dog hai, pehchana hua, is liye the. Paper me aksar dusri dafa wale the ko ghayab karke jumla diya jata hai. Is liye jab bhi the ghayab lage to ye bhi poochh lo ke kya is cheez ka zikr pehle ho chuka hai. Ho chuka hai to the lazmi tha, aur us ka ghayab hona hi ghalti hai.

Apna jumla parkhne ka tareeqa

Ab wo tareeqa jo tees second me article wale jumle ko parakh dega. Har noun ke samne ruk kar teen sawal poochho. Pehla sawal, kya ye aik hai aur pehli dafa a raha hai. Jawab han ho to a ya an, aur faisla kaan se karna hai, bol kar suno. Dusra sawal, kya ye khaas hai, duniya me aik hi hai, sab se agay hai, ya darya, samandar, akhbaar, saaz jaisi muqarrar fehrist me hai. Jawab han ho to the. Teesra sawal, kya ye aam khayal hai, poori jins hai, zubaan hai, khaas naam hai, ya asal maqsad wali jagah hai. Jawab han ho to khaali, koi article nahi.

Ye teen sawal tartib se poochho ge to article ki ghalti chhup nahi sakegi. Aksar ghalti dusre aur teesre sawal ke ulat jawab se banti hai, is liye in dono par khaas rukna.

Jawab aur un ki wajah

1. Jawab B hai. SDO ki pehli awaaz "es" hai, vowel ki awaaz, is liye an. Kaan wala qanoon ohdon par bhi wahi hai.
2. Jawab C hai. Honesty aam khayal hai, the nahi ayega.
3. Jawab B hai. Best sab se wala darja hai, us ke sath the lazmi hai.
4. Jawab C hai. Daryaon ke naam ke sath the ata hai, the Indus.
5. Jawab C hai. English zubaan hai, zubaan par the nahi ata. Baaki teeno theek hain, school asal maqsad me hai, sun ke sath the sahi hai, aur the poor poora tabqa hai.
6. Jawab B hai. Man poori jins ke liye bina article ke ata hai.
7. Jawab C hai. Saazon ke sath the ata hai, the guitar.
8. Jawab D hai. Same ke sath the chipka hota hai.
9. Jawab B hai. Akhbaar ke naam ke sath the ata hai, the Dawn.
10. Jawab C hai. Ye the aur the ka joda hai, dono taraf the ayega.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye pehli tasveer teen sawalon wali honi chahiye. Aik raasta teen darwazon se guzarta hua dikhaya jaye. Pehle darwaze par likha ho aik hai, pehli dafa hai, to a ya an. Dusre par likha ho khaas hai, aik hi hai, sab se hai, to the. Teesre par likha ho aam khayal, jins, zubaan, to khaali. Student har noun ko is raaste se guzar kar faisla kare.

Doosri tasveer the wali khaas fehrist ki ho. Suraj aur chand aik taraf, behta darya dusri taraf, khula akhbaar neeche, aur sab se uncha darja batata hua aik pehla number wala tamgha. Ye chaar shaklein dekh kar student ko hamesha yaad rahega ke the kahan lazmi hai.
