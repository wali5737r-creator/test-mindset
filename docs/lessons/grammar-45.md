Lesson 45: Common Mistakes 5. Pronoun aur Word Order ki Ghaltiyan
Me ki jagah I, aur jumle ke andar chhupa sawal. Chhoti ghaltiyan, pakke number

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

Pronoun wo lafz hai jo naam ki jagah ata hai, I, me, he, him, who, whom, myself. Aur error detection me pronoun ki ghaltiyan is liye bohat ati hain kyunke ye sunne me bilkul theek lagti hain. Between you and I, ye jumla tum roz sunte ho, ganaano aur filmo me bhi yahi ata hai, lekin paper me ye ghalat hai. Examiner janta hai ke jo cheez bol chal me sahi lagti hai, student use paper me bhi sahi samjhega. Isi liye ye ghaltiyan har paper me dohrai jati hain.

Is ke sath is lesson ka dusra hissa word order ka hai, yani alfaaz ki tartib. Jumle ke andar jab aik chhota sawal ghus jata hai to us ki tartib badal jati hai, aur only jaise lafz ki jagah badalne se poora matlab badal jata hai. Pehchanne ka tareeqa ye hai. Pronoun dekhte hi poochho ke ye faail ki jagah hai ya object ki jagah, aur sawal wala hissa dekhte hi use zehen me seedha jumla bana kar parho.

Pehle faail aur object wala farq

Har pronoun ke do chehre hote hain. Aik faail wala, jo kaam karta hai, I, he, she, we, they, who. Dusra object wala, jis par kaam hota hai ya jo kisi preposition ke baad ata hai, me, him, her, us, them, whom. Ghalti wahan hoti hai jahan student tameez ke zevar me faail wala chehra object ki jagah laga deta hai. Between you and I is ki sab se mashhoor misaal hai.

1. Ghalat: Between you and I, this matter is secret.
Sahi: Between you and me, this matter is secret.
Wajah: between aik preposition hai, aur preposition ke baad hamesha object wala chehra ata hai, me. I yahan tameez nahi, ghalti hai.

2. Ghalat: Let he and his brother go home.
Sahi: Let him and his brother go home.
Wajah: let ke baad bhi object wala chehra ata hai, him. Ye bhi between wale qanoon ki hi shakal hai.

3. Ghalat: He is taller than me.
Sahi: He is taller than I.
Wajah: paper ka qanoon ye hai ke than ke peeche aik chhupa hua jumla hai, than I am. Faail chhupa hai is liye faail wala chehra ayega, I. Bol chal me me bohat sunai deta hai, lekin error detection me I hi sahi mana jata hai, aur ye baat khaas yad rakho kyunke paper bol chal ko nahi, qanoon ko parkhta hai.

4. Ghalat: It is me who is responsible for this loss.
Sahi: It is I who am responsible for this loss.
Wajah: ye bhi paper ke qanoon ki ghalti hai. It is ke baad I ata hai, aur sath wale fail par bhi asar parta hai, is ki jagah am. Bol chal me me chal jata hai, paper me nahi.

Ab one, each aur everyone ke sath pronoun ka mel

Jis tarah fail faail se milta hai, usi tarah pronoun bhi apne peeche wale lafz se milta hai. One ke baad one ka hi pronoun ayega, each ke baad usi aik ki jins ke mutabiq ayega. Student yahan their laga deta hai jo dekhne me asan lagta hai lekin mel kharab kar deta hai.

5. Ghalat: One should do his duty honestly.
Sahi: One should do one's duty honestly.
Wajah: one ka pronoun one hi hai, one's. His lagana mel todna hai.

6. Ghalat: Each of the girls should bring their own books.
Sahi: Each of the girls should bring her own books.
Wajah: each aik aik karke dekhta hai, aur yahan baat larkion ki hai. Pronoun her ayega, their nahi.

Ab who, whom aur whose ki pehchan

Teeno sunne me aik jaise, kaam me bilkul alag. Who faail hai, kaam karne wala. Whom object hai, jis par kaam ho. Whose malik hai, kis ki cheez. Pehchanne ka asan tareeqa ye hai ke jawab me he ajaye to who, him ajaye to whom, aur his ajaye to whose.

7. Ghalat: The man whom came yesterday is my uncle.
Sahi: The man who came yesterday is my uncle.
Wajah: came ka faail chahiye, karne wala. Whom object hai, faail nahi ban sakta. Who ayega.

8. Ghalat: The boy who I met in the bazaar was very tall.
Sahi: The boy whom I met in the bazaar was very tall.
Wajah: yahan larka met ka object hai, main ne use dekha, us ne mujhe nahi. Object hai to whom. Paper me ye joda ulta karke bohat diya jata hai.

9. Ghalat: The student who's book was lost is crying.
Sahi: The student whose book was lost is crying.
Wajah: who's ka matlab hai who is. Yahan malik chahiye, kis ki kitab. Whose ayega. Ye dono itne milte julte hain ke paper inhen lazmi parkhta hai.

10. Ghalat: The teacher which taught us English has retired.
Sahi: The teacher who taught us English has retired.
Wajah: which cheezon ke liye hai, shakhs ke liye who. Teacher shakhs hai.

11. Ghalat: All which glitters is not gold.
Sahi: All that glitters is not gold.
Wajah: all, any aur sab se wale darje ke baad that ata hai, which nahi. Ye kahawat khud paper me ghalat karke di jati hai.

Ab myself wala jaal

Self wale alfaaz, myself, himself, yourself, bohat tameez wale lagte hain, is liye student inhen har jagah laga deta hai. Lekin qanoon ye hai ke ye alfaaz faail nahi ban sakte. Ye ya to faail ke peeche zor dene ate hain, ya object ban kar jab kaam ghoom kar faail par hi aye.

12. Ghalat: Myself Ali will complete this work.
Sahi: I, Ali, will complete this work.
Wajah: myself faail ki kursi par nahi beth sakta. Faail I hai. Zor dena ho to I myself will complete this work keh sakte hain, myself peeche ayega, aage nahi.

13. Ghalat: He introduced myself to the guests.
Sahi: He introduced me to the guests.
Wajah: yahan mehmaano se taruf mera ho raha hai lekin karne wala he hai. Kaam mujh par dusre ne kiya hai, is liye me ayega, myself nahi. Myself tab ata jab karne wala aur jis par ho raha hai aik hi ho, I hurt myself.

Ab tartib wali do ghaltiyan, each other aur alfaaz ki tameez

Do ke liye each other, do se zyada ke liye one another. Aur jab do log jumle me ayen to tameez ka qanoon hai ke dusra pehle, main aakhir me. Ye 231 wali tartib kehlati hai.

14. Ghalat: The five brothers love each other very much.
Sahi: The five brothers love one another very much.
Wajah: paanch bhai hain, do se zyada. One another ayega. Do dost hon to each other theek hai.

15. Ghalat: I and Ali went to the market.
Sahi: Ali and I went to the market.
Wajah: tameez ka qanoon hai ke I hamesha aakhir me ata hai. Pehle dusra, phir main. Ye ghalti number to aik hi kat-ti hai lekin bohat aam hai.

Ab word order, jumle ke andar chhupa sawal

Ab is lesson ka dusra hissa. Jab sawal akele khara ho to us ki tartib ulti hoti hai, where is he. Lekin jab wahi sawal kisi bare jumle ke andar ghus jaye to wo sawal nahi rehta, seedha jumla ban jata hai, where he is. Student andar bhi wahi ulti tartib rakhta hai aur wahi ghalti hai.

16. Ghalat: I asked him where does he live.
Sahi: I asked him where he lives.
Wajah: asked ke baad wala hissa ab sawal nahi hai, aik khabar hai. Does ghayab ho jayega aur tartib seedhi ho jayegi, he lives.

17. Ghalat: Do you know where is the post office?
Sahi: Do you know where the post office is?
Wajah: jumla Do you know se pehle hi sawal ban chuka hai. Andar wala hissa seedha hoga, the post office is. Do sawal aik jumle me nahi hote.

Aakhir me only ki jagah, jahan matlab badal jata hai

Only aik chhota sa lafz hai jo jis ke paas khara ho jaye, matlab usi par rok deta hai. Jagah badli to matlab badal gaya. Paper me ye jumla dekar poochha jata hai ke kehna kya tha.

18. Ghalat: The teacher only praised Ali for his work.
Sahi: The teacher praised only Ali for his work.
Wajah: farq samjho. Pehle jumle ka matlab hai ustaad ne sirf tareef ki, kuch aur nahi kiya, na daanta na inaam diya. Dusre jumle ka matlab hai tareef sirf Ali ki hui, kisi aur ki nahi. Kehna dusra tha, is liye only Ali ke paas gaya. Only ko hamesha usi lafz ke bilkul paas rakho jis par zor hai.

Apna jumla parkhne ka tareeqa

Ab wo tareeqa jo tees second me pronoun wale jumle ko parakh dega. Isko kasauti ke teen sawal kehte hain. Pehla sawal, ye pronoun faail ki jagah hai ya object ki jagah. Preposition ke baad hai, let ke baad hai, ya kisi fail ke baad hai to object wala chehra, me, him, whom. Fail se pehle khara hai to faail wala, I, he, who. Dusra sawal, ye kis ki taraf ishara kar raha hai. Aik ki taraf ya zyada ki taraf, larke ki taraf ya larki ki taraf. One ke baad one's, each ke baad usi ka pronoun. Teesra sawal, kya jumle ke andar koi chhupa sawal to nahi. Hai to use zehen me seedha jumla bana kar parho, aur only ko dekho ke wo sahi lafz ke paas khara hai ya nahi.

Ye teen sawal tartib se poochh lo to is poori qism ki ghaltiyan nikal ayengi. Khaas tor par pehla sawal, kyunke pronoun ki aadhi ghaltiyan sirf faail aur object ke ulat jawab se banti hain.

Jawab aur un ki wajah

1. Jawab B hai. Between preposition hai, us ke baad object wala chehra, me.
2. Jawab C hai. One ka pronoun one's hota hai. His aur their mel tod dete hain.
3. Jawab B hai. Came ka faail chahiye, who. Whom object hai.
4. Jawab D hai. Myself faail nahi ban sakta. Faail I hai.
5. Jawab C hai. Let ke baad him hona chahiye tha, let him go. Baaki teeno theek hain, andar wala sawal seedha hai aur do doston ke liye each other sahi hai.
6. Jawab B hai. Malik chahiye, whose. Who's ka matlab who is hota hai.
7. Jawab C hai. Tameez ki tartib me I aakhir me ata hai, aur faail wala chehra chahiye.
8. Jawab D hai. All ke baad that ata hai.
9. Jawab B hai. Than ke peeche chhupa hua jumla hai, than I am. Paper ka qanoon I hai.
10. Jawab B hai. Chaar behnein hain, do se zyada. One another ayega.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye pehli tasveer aaine wali honi chahiye. Har shakhs ke do chehre aaine ke dono taraf dikhaye jayen. Aik taraf faail wala chehra, I, he, who, aur dusri taraf object wala chehra, me, him, whom. Neeche likha ho ke jagah dekho, chehra khud chun lo. Student ye tasveer dekh kar hamesha pehle jagah parkhega.

Doosri tasveer only ki honi chahiye. Aik hi jumla teen dafa likha ho aur har dafa only alag jagah par ho, aur har jumle ke neeche us ka badla hua matlab saada alfaaz me likha ho. Student apni aankh se dekhe ke aik chhota sa lafz jagah badal kar poora matlab badal deta hai.
