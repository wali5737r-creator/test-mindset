Lesson 46: Common Mistakes 6. Ghalat Lafz aur Spelling ki Ghaltiyan
Sunne me aik jaise, likhne me alag. Aur wo lambe alfaaz jin ki spelling har dafa kat-ti hai

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

Common mistakes ki is aakhri qism me ghalti qanoon ki nahi, lafz ki hai. Kuch alfaaz sunne me bilkul aik jaise hain lekin likhne me aur matlab me zameen asman ka farq hai. Their aur there, its aur it's, loose aur lose. Student bol kar sochta hai, aur kaan in me farq bata hi nahi sakta. Dusri taraf wo lambe alfaaz hain jin ki spelling Pakistani students ko har dafa dhoka deti hai. Embarrass, government, separately. Paper in dono ko mila kar parkhta hai, kabhi sahi jumla chunne ko deta hai aur kabhi sahi spelling wala lafz.

Pehchanne ka tareeqa yahan qanoon wala nahi, pehchan wala hai. Har milte julte jode ke liye aik chhota sa pehchan jumla zehen me rakho, aur mushkil spelling ke liye lafz ko tukron me bol kar likho. Is lesson me pehle jode ayenge, phir spelling wale alfaaz, aur aakhir me aik aisi mashq jisme aik hi jumle me kai ghaltiyan chhupi hongi, bilkul paper ke aakhri sawal ki tarah.

Pehle wo jode jo kaan ko dhoka dete hain

In jodo ka aik hi ilaaj hai. Har jode ke dono lafzon ka matlab aik aik jumle ke sath bandh lo. Phir paper me jab wo lafz aye to pehchan jumla zehen me chalao, faisla khud ho jayega.

1. Ghalat: The students went to there classes.
Sahi: The students went to their classes.
Wajah: their ka matlab hai un ka, milkiyat. There ka matlab hai wahan, jagah. Classes un ki hain, wahan nahi hain. Pehchan ye rakho, their me heir chhupa hai, waris, yani milkiyat wala.

2. Ghalat: The dog wagged it's tail happily.
Sahi: The dog wagged its tail happily.
Wajah: it's ka matlab hai it is. Jumle me it is rakh kar dekho, the dog wagged it is tail, matlab hi nahi banta. Milkiyat ke liye its hai, bina nishan ke. Ye paper ki sab se mashhoor spelling ghalti hai.

3. Ghalat: Do not loose your temper.
Sahi: Do not lose your temper.
Wajah: lose verb hai, khona. Loose adjective hai, dheela. Pehchan ye hai ke lose me aik o hai aur kaam khone ka hai, loose me do o hain aur matlab dheela kapra. Gussa khota hai, dheela nahi hota.

4. Ghalat: The medicine did not effect him.
Sahi: The medicine did not affect him.
Wajah: affect verb hai, asar karna. Effect noun hai, asar ya natija. Yahan kaam ho raha hai, is liye affect. Aik asan pehchan, a se affect aur a se action, e se effect aur e se end result.

5. Ghalat: The affect of his speech was great.
Sahi: The effect of his speech was great.
Wajah: yahan natija chahiye, noun. Effect ayega. Dekha, yehi joda ulti taraf bhi ata hai, is liye pehchan kaam ki hai, rata nahi.

6. Ghalat: He gave me a good advise.
Sahi: He gave me a piece of good advice.
Wajah: advice noun hai, mashwara, aur c se likha jata hai. Advise verb hai, mashwara dena, aur s se likha jata hai. Aur ye bhi yad rakho ke advice gin nahi sakte, is liye a good advice ki jagah a piece of good advice.

7. Ghalat: Be quite in the class.
Sahi: Be quiet in the class.
Wajah: quiet ka matlab khamosh. Quite ka matlab bilkul ya kaafi. Bolne me dono aik jaise, likhne me e ki jagah hi farq hai. Khamoshi ke liye quiet, e aakhir me.

8. Ghalat: Beside English, he also speaks Urdu.
Sahi: Besides English, he also speaks Urdu.
Wajah: beside ka matlab hai ke paas, sath betha hua. Besides ka matlab hai ke ilawa. Yahan ilawa kehna hai, is liye s wala besides. Aik s poora matlab badal deta hai.

9. Ghalat: Our principle is a strict man.
Sahi: Our principal is a strict man.
Wajah: principal shakhs hai, school ka head, aur asal ya aham cheez ke liye bhi yehi hai. Principle usool hai, qanoon. Pehchan ye rakho, principal ke aakhir me pal hai, dost, yani shakhs. Principle ke aakhir me ple hai, usool wala.

Ab spelling wale alfaaz, jahan har dafa number kat-ta hai

Ab wo lambe alfaaz jin ki spelling ka koi qanoon nahi, bas khatarnaak jagah hai jahan har student phisalta hai. Har lafz ke sath me tumhe us ki khatarnaak jagah bata raha hun. Wahi jagah zehen me nishan laga lo, poora lafz khud sahi ho jayega.

10. Ghalat: The college provides free accomodation to poor students.
Sahi: The college provides free accommodation to poor students.
Wajah: is lafz me do do ka jaal hai. C bhi do, m bhi do. Accommodation matlab rehayish. Do c aur do m, yehi is ki pehchan hai.

11. Ghalat: She felt embarassed before the whole class.
Sahi: She felt embarrassed before the whole class.
Wajah: yahan bhi do do hain. R bhi do, s bhi do. Aik r aur aik s likhna is lafz ki tay ghalti hai.

12. Ghalat: Write your name seperately on the paper.
Sahi: Write your name separately on the paper.
Wajah: beech wala hissa sab ko dhoka deta hai. Pehchan ye hai ke separate ke beech me a rat chhupa hai, se-pa-rat-ely. Rat nazar ajaye to a yaad ajayega, e kabhi nahi ayega.

13. Ghalat: He will definately pass the test.
Sahi: He will definitely pass the test.
Wajah: beech me a nahi hai. Is lafz ke andar finite chhupa hai, de-finite-ly. A likhne wale asal me suna hua likhte hain, tukre karke dekho ge to a gayab ho jayega.

14. Ghalat: The goverment has announced a holiday.
Sahi: The government has announced a holiday.
Wajah: beech me aik n chhupa hai jo bolne me sunai nahi deta aur likhne me ghayab ho jata hai. Govern ke baad ment hai. N par ungli rakho.

15. Ghalat: We must protect our enviroment.
Sahi: We must protect our environment.
Wajah: yahan bhi wahi chhupa n hai, environ ke baad ment. Government aur environment dono me ment se pehle wala n hi khatarnaak jagah hai.

16. Ghalat: On this ocassion, prizes were distributed.
Sahi: On this occasion, prizes were distributed.
Wajah: yahan hisaab ulta hai. C do hain, s aik hai. Do c aur aik s, ye is ki pehchan hai.

17. Ghalat: He does not posess a car.
Sahi: He does not possess a car.
Wajah: ye lafz s ki bharmar hai. Aakhir me do s ke do jode hain, possess. Kam s likhna yahan ki ghalti hai.

18. Ghalat: He has a good knowlege of history.
Sahi: He has a good knowledge of history.
Wajah: beech me d chhupa hai, know ke baad ledge. Bolne me d sunai nahi deta, likhne me lazmi hai.

19. Ghalat: I reccomend this book to every student.
Sahi: I recommend this book to every student.
Wajah: yahan hisaab accommodation se ulta hai. C aik hai, m do hain. Aik c aur do m, ye joda ulta kar dena bohat aam ghalti hai.

Aakhir me paper wala drill, aik jumle me kai ghaltiyan

Ab wo mashq jo is poore hisse ka imtihan hai. Paper ke aakhri sawalon me aik jumla aisa ata hai jisme aik nahi, kai ghaltiyan chhupi hoti hain. Student aik pakar kar khush ho jata hai aur baaki choot jati hain. Qanoon ye hai ke jumle ko lafz lafz parkho, har mashhoor jaal ko aik aik karke check karo.

20. Ghalat: Their is no doubt that the goverment will definately help the effected people.
Sahi: There is no doubt that the government will definitely help the affected people.
Wajah: is aik jumle me chaar ghaltiyan theen. Their ki jagah there, jagah batani thi. Goverment me n ghayab tha. Definately me a ghus aya tha. Aur effected ki jagah affected, asar parne wale log. Chaar me se aik bhi choot jati to number kat jata. Yehi sabak hai, jumla aik dafa nahi, jaal dar jaal parkho.

Aik ustaad wala mashwara jo zindagi bhar kaam ayega

Jaane se pehle aik aadat bana lo. Apni aik alag copy rakho, ghaltiyon wali copy. Jab bhi kisi test ya paper me tumhari koi spelling ya jode wali ghalti nikle, wo lafz sahi surat me us copy me likh lo. Hafte me aik dafa sirf wo copy parho. Wajah ye hai ke har student ki ghaltiyan us ki apni hoti hain. Kisi ko government ka n kat-ta hai, kisi ko its aur it's. Tumhari copy tumhe tumhari hi khatarnaak jagahen dikhayegi, aur do teen maah me wo fehrist itni chhoti ho jayegi ke paper me katne wala kuch bachega hi nahi.

Apna jumla parkhne ka tareeqa

Ab wo tareeqa jo tees second me is qism ke jumle ko parakh dega. Is ke do hisse hain. Pehla hissa jodo wala hai. Jumle me milte julte alfaaz dhundo, their, there, its, it's, loose, lose, affect, effect, advice, advise. Jo bhi mile, us par ruk kar pehchan jumla chalao. It's ke samne it is rakh kar dekho, jumla bane to theek, na bane to its. Their ke samne milkiyat ka sawal poochho, kis ki cheez hai. Dusra hissa spelling wala hai. Jo lafz lamba lage, use zehen me tukron me bolo. Se-pa-rate-ly, gov-ern-ment, de-finite-ly. Har tukra alag sunai dega aur khatarnaak jagah khud nazar ajayegi.

Aur aakhir ki aik baat hamesha yad rakho. Aik jumle me aik ghalti mil jane par rukna nahi hai. Paper ka jumla aksar do ghaltiyon wala hota hai. Pehli ghalti milne ki khushi me dusri choot jati hai. Poora jumla aakhir tak parkho, phir jawab chuno.

Jawab aur un ki wajah

1. Jawab B hai. School un ka hai, milkiyat hai, their ayega. There jagah hai.
2. Jawab B hai. It is rakh kar dekho, matlab nahi banta. Milkiyat ke liye its.
3. Jawab C hai. Gussa khone ka kaam hai, lose. Loose dheela hota hai.
4. Jawab B hai. Natija chahiye, noun chahiye, effect. Affect kaam hai.
5. Jawab D hai. Paas bethne ke liye beside hai, s ke baghair. Besides ka matlab ke ilawa hota hai. Baaki teeno theek hain.
6. Jawab C hai. Embarrass me r bhi do, s bhi do.
7. Jawab D hai. Accommodation me c bhi do, m bhi do.
8. Jawab B hai. Separate ke beech me a rat chhupa hai, is liye a ayega.
9. Jawab D hai. Government me govern ke baad ment hai, beech wala n lazmi hai.
10. Jawab C hai. Baaki teeno me koi na koi purani ghalti chhupi hai, their, goverment, definately ya effected. Sirf C har jaal se saaf hai. Yehi aakhri drill wala sabak hai, poora jumla parkho.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye pehli tasveer jodo ki pehchan wali honi chahiye. Har joda aamne samne likha ho aur har lafz ke neeche us ki pehchan ka chhota jumla ho. Its ke neeche us ka matlab us ka, it's ke neeche it is. Their ke neeche un ka, there ke neeche wahan. Student joda nahi ratay ga, pehchan ratay ga.

Doosri tasveer spelling ke khatarnaak maqamat wali ho. Har mushkil lafz bara likha ho aur us ki khatarnaak jagah mote rang me dikhai gayi ho. Accommodation ke do c aur do m, government ka chhupa n, separate ke beech wala a rat. Ye rang barangi jagahen hi student ki aankh me hamesha ke liye beth jayengi.
