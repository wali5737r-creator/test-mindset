Lesson 47: Punctuation
Nishanaat ka istemal. Chhote chhote nishan, aur un par katne wale bare number

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me har saal aik do sawal aisay hote hain jin me chaar jumle diye jate hain aur poocha jata hai ke kis jumle me nishanaat sahi lage hain, aur kabhi khali jagah par sahi nishan chunna hota hai. Aik comma idhar se udhar ho jaye to sahi jumla ghalat ban jata hai. Khushkhabri ye hai ke nishanaat ke qanoon gine chune hain. Jo student is lesson ko pakka kar le, wo in sawalo me number nahi ganwayega.

Pehle ye samajh lo ke nishan hote kya hain

Dekho, jab hum bolte hain to hum sirf alfaaz nahi bolte. Hum kahin rukte hain, kahin saans lete hain, kahin awaaz unchi karte hain aur kahin sawal wali awaaz banate hain. Likhai me masla ye hai ke wahan awaaz nahi hoti. To likhne wale ne har rukawat aur har lehje ke liye aik nishan bana liya. Yehi nishan punctuation hain.

Aik misaal se baat saaf ho jayegi. Pehla jumla: Let us eat Grandma. Dusra: Let us eat, Grandma. Pehle jumle me daadi ko khane ki dawat di ja rahi hai. Dusre jumle me daadi ko khane par bulaya ja raha hai. Sirf aik comma ne daadi ki jaan bacha li. Yahi nishano ki taqat hai.

Full stop, jahan baat khatam

Full stop jumle ke khatam hone ka nishan hai, har bayan wala jumla isi par khatam hota hai. The train arrived at nine. Full stop mukhtasar alfaaz me bhi ata hai, jaise Mr. Khan aur Dr. Ahmad, lekin aj kal inhe baghair full stop ke likhna bhi aam hai. Paper me full stop ki sab se aam ghalti ye hai ke do mukammal jumlo ko baghair full stop jod diya jata hai. Yehi ghalti aage comma wali bari ghalti banti hai.

Comma, halki si rukawat

Comma sab se zyada istemal hone wala aur sab se zyada ghalat lagne wala nishan hai. Is ka matlab hai halki si rukawat, aik saans bhar ka waqfa. Iski aham jagahen bitha lo. Pehli: aik silsile ki cheezein ginna hon to har cheez ke baad comma. I bought apples, oranges, bananas and grapes. Dusri: kisi ko seedha mukhatib karna ho to naam ke gird comma. Ali, please open the door. Naam beech me aye to dono taraf. Listen, children, and learn. Teesri: jumle ki shuruaat wale taarufi hisse ke baad. After the rain, the weather became pleasant. However, he did not agree. Chauthi: do mukammal jumlo ko and, but, or ya so se jodna ho to jodne wale lafz se pehle comma. He worked hard, but he failed. Aur tareekh me bhi comma ata hai. He was born on 14 August, 1947, in Lahore.

Ab is lesson ka sab se aham hissa

Ab wo do nishan aate hain jin par paper sab se zyada phansata hai, semicolon aur colon. In ka farq pakka kar lo.

Semicolon, aadha full stop

Semicolon ko yun samjho ke ye full stop ka chhota bhai hai. Is ka kaam hai do mukammal jumlo ko jodna jo gehre jure hon, lekin beech me koi jodne wala lafz na ho. The rain stopped; the match began again. Dono hisse apni jagah mukammal jumle hain. Chaaho to full stop laga kar inhe alag bhi kar sakte ho, lekin rishta gehra ho to lekhak semicolon se jod deta hai. Dusri surat ye hai ke jab jodne wala lafz however, therefore ya moreover jaisa ho to us se pehle semicolon aur us ke baad comma ata hai. He was ill; however, he attended the office. Teesri jagah wo lambi fehrist hai jis ki har cheez ke andar pehle se comma ho. Wahan cheezon ko semicolon se alag karte hain, warna sab gadh madh ho jayega. The delegation visited Lahore, Punjab; Karachi, Sindh; and Quetta, Balochistan.

Colon, darwaza khulne ka nishan

Colon ka kaam bilkul aur hai. Ye kehta hai ke ruk jao, aage tafseel, fehrist, misaal ya wajah aa rahi hai. Colon ke baad ane wala hissa mukammal jumla ho bhi sakta hai aur nahi bhi. He had only one aim in life: to serve his country. Fehrist se pehle colon sab se aam hai. Please bring the following items: a pen, a notebook and a file. Aur wajah ya natija pesh karna ho tab bhi colon ata hai. She was sure of one thing: she would not give up.

Ab farq aik sath dekho. Semicolon do barabar ke jumlo ko jodta hai, is ke dono taraf mukammal jumla lazmi hai. Colon jumle ko aage kholta hai, is ke baad sirf tafseel bhi aa sakti hai. Semicolon kehta hai baat abhi judi hai, colon kehta hai lo, wo cheez pesh hai jis ka waada tha. Fehrist ginani ho to colon lagega, semicolon kabhi nahi. Aur do mukammal jumlo ko sirf comma se jodna comma splice hai, sakht ghalat.

Aik aham hidayat aur yaad kar lo. Such as ya including ke baad colon kabhi nahi lagta. He likes fruits such as apples and mangoes. Yahan colon lagana paper ki mashhoor ghalti hai. Waqt me bhi colon lagta hai, jaise 10:30 aur 6:45 a.m.

Sawal wala nishan aur hairani wala nishan

Question mark seedhe sawal ke aakhir me lagta hai. Where do you live? Lekin khabardar, ghumaye hue sawal, yani indirect question, ke aakhir me question mark nahi lagta, full stop lagta hai. He asked me where I lived. Paper is par har saal phansata hai, kyunke jumla khud bayan hota hai. Exclamation mark hairani, khushi, dukh ya sakht hukum ke liye ata hai. What a beautiful garden! Stop! Is ka istemal formal aur sarkari likhai me bohat kam hota hai.

Apostrophe, do kaam wala nishan

Apostrophe chhota sa nishan hai lekin is ke do bare kaam hain aur paper dono par sawal banata hai. Pehla kaam hai milkiyat batana. Ali's book. Ladke ki kitaab. Aik malik ho to apostrophe s se pehle lagta hai. Malkaan zyada hon aur lafz pehle se s par khatam ho to apostrophe s ke baad lagta hai. The boys' school. Dusra kaam hai alfaaz ko sikorna, yani contraction. Do not ka don't, it is ka it's, I have ka I've. Yahan apostrophe us jagah lagta hai jahan se harf gayab hua hai.

Ab wo jaal samjho jis me aadhe students phanste hain. Its aur it's ka farq. Its baghair apostrophe ke milkiyat hai. The dog wagged its tail. It's apostrophe ke sath it is ka sikora hua roop hai. It's raining. Jab bhi shak ho to jumle me it is rakh kar dekho. Matlab ban jaye to apostrophe wala sahi hai, na bane to baghair wala. Aur aik sakht hidayat: jama banane ke liye apostrophe kabhi nahi lagta. Apples sahi hai, jama ke matlab me apple's ghalat hai, aur paper me ye number kaat deta hai.

Inverted commas, kisi ke asal alfaaz

Quotation marks ya inverted commas kisi ke munh se nikle hue asal alfaaz ko gherne ke liye lagte hain. The teacher said, "Open your books." Bolne wale ke alfaaz commas ke andar hain aur un se pehle comma aya hai. Sawal andar ho to question mark commas ke andar rahega. She asked, "Where is my file?" Aik baat pakki kar lo ke apne alfaaz me kisi ki baat bayan karna ho, yani reported speech ho, to inverted commas nahi lagte. He said that he was ready. Paper me yehi farq aksar ata hai.

Hyphen aur dash, jodne wala aur todne wala

Hyphen chhota hota hai aur alfaaz ko aapas me jodta hai. Well-known, twenty-one, mother-in-law, a two-hour journey. Dash is se lamba hota hai aur is ka kaam jodna nahi, jumle ko beech me tod kar achanak khayal ya izafi baat ghusana hai. He came to the office — and surprised everyone. Hyphen alfaaz ko jodta hai, dash jumle ko todta hai. Ginti wale alfaaz paper me bar bar ate hain. Twenty-one, thirty-five. Aur jo lafz noun se pehle sifaat ban kar aye wahan bhi hyphen ata hai. A well-known poet. Lekin wahi lafz jumle ke aakhir me aye to hyphen aksar gir jata hai. The poet is well known.

Brackets aur capital letters

Brackets jumle ke andar apni taraf se di hui izafi maloomat ko alag karte hain. He (the new manager) signed the file. Brackets wala hissa nikaal do to bhi jumla mukammal rehta hai. Ab capital letters ki baat. Jumle ka pehla harf hamesha capital hota hai. Naam, jagah, din, mahina, zubaan, qaum aur mazhab ke alfaaz capital se shuru hote hain. Ali, Lahore, Monday, January, Urdu, English, Pakistani. Aur English ka I, chahe jumle me kahin bhi aye, hamesha capital hota hai. Kitabo, filmo aur akhbaro ke naam ke aham alfaaz bhi capital hote hain. Do ghaltiyan aam hain. Mosam ke naam par capital lagana, summer aur winter par capital nahi lagta. Aur aam cheezo par capital thopna, school, college aur book tab hi capital honge jab kisi khaas naam ka hissa hon.

Board par qanoon Angrezi me

Board par ustad in qanoon ko yun likhta hai. A full stop ends a complete sentence. A comma shows a short pause. A semicolon joins two complete and closely related sentences without a joining word. A colon introduces a list, an explanation or an example, and what follows it need not be a complete sentence. Never join two complete sentences with a comma alone. An apostrophe shows possession or a missing letter, never a plural. Use a question mark only after a direct question. Aur sab se aham line ye hai: every mark has one job, and the right mark is the one that does that job.

Misaalein board se

Ab board par chand misaalein dekho, har aik ke neeche us ki wajah bhi hai.

1. He had only one aim in life: to pass the CSS examination.
Colon aaya hai kyunke aage maqsad ki tafseel hai, mukammal jumla nahi.

2. The rain stopped; the match began again.
Semicolon ne do mukammal aur jure hue jumlo ko baghair kisi jodne wale lafz ke joda hai.

3. She bought three things: a register, a pen and a file.
Fehrist se pehle colon lagta hai, semicolon nahi.

4. Let us eat, Grandma.
Comma ne matlab badal diya. Daadi ko bulaya ja raha hai, khaya nahi ja raha.

5. The dog wagged its tail when it saw its master.
Dono jagah its milkiyat hai, is liye apostrophe nahi.

6. He asked me where the office was.
Ye ghumaya hua sawal hai. Jumla bayan hai, is liye aakhir me full stop hai, question mark nahi.

7. The students' hostel is near the college.
Bohat se students ki milkiyat hai aur lafz s par khatam hai, is liye apostrophe s ke baad aya hai.

8. She is a well-known writer, and her new book is well known in the city.
Pehli jagah lafz sifaat ban kar noun se pehle aya to hyphen laga. Dusri jagah jumle ke aakhir me aya to hyphen gir gaya.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Nishanaat sirf paper ke sawal me kaam nahi aate. Darkhwast, khat, essay aur precis, har likhai me yehi nishan tumhari English ka chehra hain. Aur paper me sentence correction wale hisse me aadhi ghaltiyan hi nishano ki hoti hain.

Ab paper wale andaaz ke do jumle dekho.

1. He visited three cities: Lahore, Karachi and Quetta; each city gave him a new lesson.
2. "Do not waste time," the teacher said, "for time is money."

Pehle me fehrist se pehle colon aur do jumlo ke beech semicolon hai. Dusre me asal alfaaz do hisso me inverted commas me hain.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab wo trick jo har nishan ko us ki jagah bitha degi. Isay Signal wala qanoon kehte hain. Zehen me sadak ke signal banao. Full stop laal batti hai, baat khatam. Comma halki si break hai, speed kam hoti hai lekin gaari rukti nahi. Semicolon amber batti hai, aadha rukna hai kyunke aage wali baat isi se judi hai. Aur colon wo darwaza hai jo khul raha hai, aage fehrist, tafseel ya misaal aa rahi hai. Bas paper me nishan dekh kar pehle ye socho ke rukna kitna hai. Poora rukna hai to full stop, aadha to semicolon, halka sa to comma, aur darwaza khulna hai to colon.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ab wo ghaltiyan dekho jo Pakistani students nishano ke sawalo me karte hain.

Ghalat: The cat drank it's milk.
Sahi: The cat drank its milk.
Wajah: yahan milkiyat hai. It's ka matlab it is hota hai jo yahan nahi banta.

Ghalat: He came late, he missed the bus.
Sahi: He came late; he missed the bus.
Wajah: do mukammal jumlo ko sirf comma se jodna comma splice hai. Semicolon ya full stop lagega.

Ghalat: Please bring the following items; a pen, a notebook and a file.
Sahi: Please bring the following items: a pen, a notebook and a file.
Wajah: fehrist se pehle colon ata hai, semicolon kabhi nahi.

Ghalat: I bought three apple's from the market.
Sahi: I bought three apples from the market.
Wajah: apostrophe jama nahi banata. Jama ke liye sirf s lagta hai.

Ghalat: The boys school is closed today.
Sahi: The boys' school is closed today.
Wajah: bohat se ladko ki milkiyat hai. Lafz s par khatam hai is liye apostrophe s ke baad ayega.

Ghalat: i am a pakistani and i live in lahore.
Sahi: I am a Pakistani and I live in Lahore.
Wajah: I hamesha capital hota hai, aur qaum aur jagah ke naam bhi capital se shuru hote hain.

Jawab aur un ki wajah

1. Jawab B hai. Colon ke baad maqsad ki tafseel hai aur wo mukammal jumla nahi, is liye semicolon nahi lag sakta. Colon darwaza hai.
2. Jawab B hai. Do mukammal jumle hain, beech me jodne wala lafz nahi. Sirf comma comma splice hota, is liye semicolon sahi hai.
3. Jawab B hai, its. Milkiyat me apostrophe nahi lagta. It's ka matlab it is hai jo yahan nahi banta.
4. Jawab B hai. Ye ghumaya hua sawal hai, jumla bayan hai, is liye full stop lagega.
5. Jawab C hai. Fehrist se pehle colon ata hai.
6. Jawab B hai. Well-known sifaat ban kar noun se pehle aya hai, is liye hyphen lagega. Teacher ke sath hyphen nahi lagta.
7. Jawab C hai. Dono jagah bohat se malkaan hain aur alfaaz s par khatam hain, is liye apostrophe s ke baad ayega.
8. Jawab B hai. However se pehle semicolon aur us ke baad comma, yehi sahi joda hai.
9. Jawab B hai. Asal sawal inverted commas ke andar hai aur question mark bhi andar hi lagega.
10. Jawab B hai. I hamesha capital hota hai aur zubaanon ke naam Urdu aur English bhi capital se shuru hote hain.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye pehli tasveer Signal wale qanoon ki ho. Aik traffic signal ki tasveer ho jis ke sath nishan bane hon. Laal batti ke sath full stop, amber ke sath semicolon aur halki break wali jagah comma. Sath me aik khula darwaza bana ho jis par colon likha ho aur darwaze ke peeche fehrist nazar aa rahi ho. Neeche aik line ho ke pehle ye socho, rukna kitna hai.

Doosri tasveer apostrophe ke do kaamo ki ho. Tasveer ke do hisse hon. Aik hisse me Ali ki kitaab par Ali's likha ho, aur dusre hisse me do not se harf gayab hokar don't ban raha ho. Beech me its aur it's ka joda bara bara likha ho, taake ye jaal hamesha yaad rahe.
