Lesson 48: Conditional Sentences
If wale jumle. Agar ye hota, to wo hota

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me if wale jumle har saal ate hain. Kabhi aadha jumla de kar baqi aadha poocha jata hai, kabhi chaar jumlo me se sahi wala chunna hota hai, aur kabhi jumla theek karna hota hai. In sawalo ki khaas baat ye hai ke in ka jawab andaze se nahi nikalta, formula se nikalta hai. Har if wale jumle ki aik qism hoti hai aur har qism ka apna formula hota hai. Jo student chaar formulay pakke kar le, us ke liye ye sawal paper ke sab se asaan number ban jate hain. Aur jo formula nahi janta, wo har option ko sahi samajh kar ghalat par nishan laga deta hai.

Shart aur natija, pehle ye joda samajh lo

Dekho, har if wale jumle ke do hisse hote hain. Aik hisse me shart hoti hai, yani agar wali baat. Dusre hisse me natija hota hai, yani phir kya hoga. Urdu me suno. Agar baarish hui, to match ruk jayega. Agar wali baat shart hai aur match rukna natija hai. English me bhi yehi do hisse hain. If it rains, the match will stop. If wala hissa shart hai, baqi natija hai.

Ab asal baat ye hai ke shart kitni asal hai aur kitni khayali, is par jumle ki shakal badal jati hai. Agar shart aam sachai ho to aik shakal hai. Agar mustaqbil ka asal imkan ho to dusri shakal. Agar shart khayali ho, jo asal me hai hi nahi, to teesri shakal. Aur agar shart guzre hue waqt ki ho, jo puri ho hi nahi saki, to chauthi shakal. Inhi chaar soraton ko hum zero, type 1, type 2 aur type 3 kehte hain. Naam ratne ki zaroorat nahi, har aik ki pehchan aur formula zehen me bitha lo.

Zero conditional, aam sachai wala jumla

Jab baat aam sachai ho, science ki baat ho ya aisi cheez jo hamesha aise hi hoti ho, to zero conditional ata hai. Is ka formula bilkul seedha hai. Dono hisso me present simple. If you heat ice, it melts. Baraf ko garam karo to wo pighalti hai. Ye na mustaqbil hai na khayal, ye har dafa ka usool hai. If the sun sets, it becomes dark. Zero conditional ki pehchan ye hai ke natije me will ya would nahi ata, seedha present simple ata hai. Paper me ye sab se kam ata hai, lekin ata zaroor hai, khaas taur par science wali misaalo me.

Type 1, asal imkan wala jumla

Jab shart asal me puri ho sakti ho aur baat mustaqbil ki ho, to type 1 ata hai. Is ka formula hai: if wale hisse me present simple, aur natije me will ke sath verb ki pehli form. If it rains tomorrow, we will stay at home. Baarish ka asal imkan hai aur natija mustaqbil me hoga. If you work hard, you will pass. Ab yahan paper ka sab se bara jaal suno. If wale hisse me will kabhi nahi ata. Students Urdu se soch kar likh dete hain: If he will come, I will help him. Ye ghalat hai. If ke baad present simple ayega. If he comes, I will help him. Ye aik line zehen me keel ki tarah garh lo. If wala hissa hamesha present me rehta hai, mustaqbil ka bojh natije wala will uthata hai.

Type 2, khayali duniya wala jumla

Jab shart khayali ho, matlab asal me wo baat hai hi nahi, sirf soch rahe hon, to type 2 ata hai. Formula hai: if wale hisse me past simple, aur natije me would ke sath verb ki pehli form. If I had a car, I would travel to Murree. Asal me mere paas car hai hi nahi, ye khayal hai. If he were rich, he would help the poor. Wo ameer nahi hai, hum soch kar baat kar rahe hain. Type 2 ki pehchan ye hai ke waqt haal ka hota hai lekin verb maazi ki shakal me ata hai. Ye maazi asal maazi nahi hai, ye khayal ki pehchan hai. Verb aik qadam peeche gaya, matlab baat khayali ho gayi.

Ab is lesson ka wo usool jo paper me sab se zyada ata hai. Type 2 me be verb tamam subjects ke sath were ata hai, was nahi. If I were you, I would accept the job. Ye jumla tum ne zaroor suna hoga. I ke sath aam tor par was ata hai, lekin if wali khayali duniya me I ke sath bhi were lagta hai. If he were here, he would solve the problem. Bol chal me was bhi sunai deta hai aur wahan chal bhi jata hai, lekin paper me sahi jawab hamesha were hota hai. Khaas taur par If I were you wala jumla paper ka pasandida jumla hai. Ye mashware ke liye istemal hota hai. Kisi ko mashwara dena ho to yun kehte hain. If I were you, I would work hard. Matlab: tumhari jagah main hota to mehnat karta. Is jumle ko aisay hi pakka kar lo.

Type 3, guzra hua afsos wala jumla

Jab shart guzre hue waqt ki ho aur puri ho hi na saki ho, to type 3 ata hai. Ye afsos wale jumle hain. Kaash wali baat. Formula hai: if wale hisse me had ke sath verb ki teesri form, yani past perfect, aur natije me would have ke sath verb ki teesri form. If you had worked hard, you would have passed. Asal me tum ne mehnat nahi ki aur tum pass nahi hue. Ab baith kar soch rahe ho ke agar ki hoti to ho jata. If she had left early, she would have caught the train. Type 3 ki pehchan sab se saaf hai. Dono hisso me teesri form hoti hai, if wale me had ke sath aur natije me would have ke sath. Maazi ki baat hai is liye har cheez sab se peeche wali shakal me chali jati hai.

Unless, chhupa hua if not

Ab aik chhota lafz jo paper me bohat phansata hai. Unless. Is ka matlab hai if not. Unless you work hard, you will fail. Is ka matlab hai: If you do not work hard, you will fail. Dekha, unless ne apne andar not chhupa rakha hai. Isi liye unless ke sath dobara not lagana sakht ghalat hai. Unless you do not work hard kehna double negative hai. Unless aya to not gayab. Unless it rains, we will play the match. Matlab ye ke agar baarish na hui to hum khelenge. Paper me unless wale jumle ko if not me badal kar dekho, jawab khud saaf ho jayega.

Tartib badalna aur comma ki baat

Aik akhri bareeki. Jumla natije se bhi shuru ho sakta hai. I will help you if you come to me. Matlab wahi hai. Farq sirf itna hai ke jab jumla if se shuru ho to beech me comma lagta hai, aur jab natije se shuru ho to comma nahi lagta. Chhoti si baat hai lekin punctuation wale sawal me yahi number banta hai.

Board par qanoon Angrezi me

Board par ustad chaaron formulay yun likhta hai. Zero conditional: If plus present simple, present simple. It states a general truth. Type 1: If plus present simple, will plus the base verb. It states a real future possibility, and will never comes after if. Type 2: If plus past simple, would plus the base verb. It states an unreal present wish, and be becomes were for every subject. Type 3: If plus had plus the past participle, would have plus the past participle. It states a past regret. Unless means if not. Aur sab se aham line ye hai: the further the wish moves from reality, the further the verb moves into the past.

Misaalein board se

Ab board par chand misaalein dekho. Har misaal ke sath us ki qism aur wajah bhi samjho.

1. If you boil water, it turns to steam.
Zero conditional hai. Ye science ki aam sachai hai, is liye dono hisso me present simple aya hai.

2. If it rains tomorrow, we will not go for a picnic.
Type 1 hai. If wale hisse me present simple hai aur natije me will. Baarish ka asal imkan hai.

3. If Ali works hard, he will get a good post.
Type 1 hai. If ke baad works aya hai, will nahi. Paper ke jaal se bachne ki yehi pehchan hai.

4. If I were a bird, I would fly to the mountains.
Type 2 hai. Main parinda nahi hun, ye khayal hai. I ke sath were aya hai, was nahi.

5. If I were you, I would not waste time.
Type 2 hai aur ye mashware wala mashhoor jumla hai. Paper me ye aisay hi ata hai.

6. If we had money, we would buy a house in the city.
Type 2 hai. Paisay asal me nahi hain, is liye if wale hisse me had past simple hai aur natije me would hai. Khabardar, yahan had ke baad koi teesri form nahi hai. Had khud yahan verb hai jis ka matlab hai paas hona.

7. If you had informed me, I would have reached the station.
Type 3 hai. If wale hisse me had informed hai aur natije me would have reached. Guzri hui baat ka afsos hai.

8. If the doctor had come in time, the patient would have survived.
Type 3 hai. Dono hisso me teesri form hai. Doctor waqt par nahi aya tha, ye maazi ka khayal hai.

9. Unless you run fast, you will miss the bus.
Unless ka matlab if not hai. Agar tum tez na bhage to bus nikal jayegi.

10. Unless he apologises, I will not forgive him.
Unless ke sath not nahi aya, kyunke not unless ke andar pehle se chhupa hai.

11. I would buy that house if I had enough money.
Type 2 hai lekin tartib ulti hai. Jumla natije se shuru hua hai is liye beech me comma nahi laga.

12. If she had taken the medicine, she would have recovered soon.
Type 3 hai. Had taken aur would have recovered, dono teesri form me hain.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

If wale jumle rozmarra ki English me har jagah hain. Mashwara dena ho to If I were you wala jumla. Darkhwast karni ho to ye jumle. Tanbeeh karni ho to bhi yehi. If you do not stop, you will be punished. Aur daftar ki likhai me bhi ye jumle aam hain. Paper me in ki pehchan is liye bhi zaroori hai ke narration aur jumla theek karne wale sawalo me bhi if wale jumle ghus ate hain.

Ab paper wale andaaz ke do jumle dekho.

1. If I had known his address, I would have visited him.
2. Unless the government takes action, the prices will keep rising.

Pehla type 3 hai, had known aur would have visited. Dusre me unless hai jis ka matlab if not hai.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab wo trick jo chaar formulon ko hamesha ke liye bitha degi. Isay Seerhi wali trick kehte hain. Zehen me aik seerhi banao. Jaise jaise hum seerhi par upar charhte hain, yani type 1 se type 2 aur type 2 se type 3 ki taraf jate hain, verb aik aik qadam peeche jata hai. Type 1 me if wale hisse me present hai. Type 2 me wahi baat past me chali gayi. Type 3 me past perfect me, sab se peeche. Aur natija bhi sath sath peeche jata hai. Will se would, aur would se would have. Bas ye jumla yaad kar lo: waqt aik qadam peeche, khayal aik qadam aage. Jitni baat sach se door, utna verb maazi me gehra. Aur type 2 ki seedhi par khade hokar aik baat hamesha sunai degi: were, was nahi.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ab wo ghaltiyan dekho jo Pakistani students if wale jumlo me karte hain.

Ghalat: If he will come, I will meet him.
Sahi: If he comes, I will meet him.
Wajah: if ke baad will kabhi nahi ata. If wala hissa present simple me rehta hai.

Ghalat: If I was you, I would accept this offer.
Sahi: If I were you, I would accept this offer.
Wajah: khayali duniya me har subject ke sath were ata hai. Paper me was ghalat mana jata hai.

Ghalat: If you had reached in time, you would catch the train.
Sahi: If you had reached in time, you would have caught the train.
Wajah: if wala hissa type 3 ka hai to natija bhi type 3 ka hoga. Would have ke sath teesri form ayegi.

Ghalat: Unless you do not hurry, you will be late.
Sahi: Unless you hurry, you will be late.
Wajah: unless ke andar not pehle se chhupa hai. Dobara not lagana double negative hai.

Ghalat: If I would be rich, I would help the poor.
Sahi: If I were rich, I would help the poor.
Wajah: would natije wale hisse me ata hai, if wale hisse me nahi. If wale hisse me past simple ayega.

Ghalat: If it rains tomorrow, we would stay at home.
Sahi: If it rains tomorrow, we will stay at home.
Wajah: kal ki asal mumkin baat type 1 hai. Us ka natija will se banta hai, would se nahi. Would khayali duniya ka hai.

Ghalat: If she had money, she would have bought the car yesterday.
Sahi: If she had had money, she would have bought the car yesterday.
Wajah: yesterday wali baat guzri hui hai, ye type 3 hai. If wale hisse me had ke sath teesri form ayegi, aur have ki teesri form had hai, is liye had had bana.

Jawab aur un ki wajah

1. Jawab A hai, works. Natije me will pass hai, ye type 1 hai. If wale hisse me present simple ayega aur he ke sath works lagega.
2. Jawab B hai, would help. Were dekh kar type 2 pehchano. Khayali duniya ka natija would se banta hai.
3. Jawab C hai, would have caught. Had left type 3 ki pehchan hai. Natije me would have ke sath teesri form ayegi.
4. Jawab B hai. If ke baad will aur would dono nahi ate. Comes hi sahi hai.
5. Jawab B hai, work hard. Unless ka matlab if not hai. Not us ke andar pehle se hai, is liye bahar se not wala option ghalat hai.
6. Jawab A hai, melts. Ye aam sachai hai, zero conditional. Dono hisso me present simple ata hai.
7. Jawab B hai. Type 2 me I ke sath bhi were ata hai. Was wala option paper me ghalat mana jata hai.
8. Jawab C hai, would not have fallen. Had taken care type 3 hai, is liye natija bhi would have wali shakal me hoga.
9. Jawab C hai, type 2. Yahan had rakhne ke matlab me past simple hai aur natije me would hai. Paisay asal me nahi hain, baat khayali hai.
10. Jawab C hai. Tomorrow ke sath ye type 1 hai. If wale hisse me rains aur natije me will stay.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye pehli tasveer Seerhi wali trick ki ho. Aik seerhi bani ho jis ki teen seedhiyan hon. Pehli seedhi par Type 1 likha ho, sath present simple aur will. Dusri par Type 2, sath past simple aur would. Teesri par Type 3, sath had wala past perfect aur would have. Har seedhi ke sath aik chhota jumla bhi ho, taake student aik nazar me dekhe ke jaise jaise seedhi upar jati hai, verb peeche jata hai. Side me zero conditional aik alag chhote khane me ho, aam sachai wala.

Doosri tasveer If I were you wale jumle ki ho. Do admi aamne saamne baithe hon aur aik dusre ko mashwara de raha ho. Upar bara bara likha ho: If I were you. Neeche chhoti si line ho ke khayali duniya me har subject ke sath were ata hai. Ye jumla paper me itna ata hai ke is ki tasveer alag banni chahiye.
