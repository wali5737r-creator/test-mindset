Lesson 49: Question Tags
Jumlay ke aakhir wale chhote sawal. Hai na wali aadat, lekin English ke usool se

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me question tag ka sawal taqreeban har saal ata hai. Kabhi jumla de kar us ka sahi tag poocha jata hai aur kabhi chaar tag de kar sahi wala chunna hota hai. Ye sawal sirf aik line ka hota hai aur number bhi aik hi milta hai, lekin ye un sawalo me se hai jin me aadhe students sirf is liye number kaatte hain ke unhe qanoon ka dusra hissa yaad nahi rehta. Khushkhabri ye hai ke question tag ke qanoon sirf do hain. Do qanoon aur chand khaas soratein pakki kar lo, to ye sawal tumhare liye paper ka sab se mehfooz number ban jayega.

Pehle ye samajh lo ke tag hota kya hai

Dekho, Urdu me hum har jumle ke aakhir me aik hi tag lagate hain. Tum kal ao ge, hai na? Wo kitaab parh raha hai, hai na? Ameer ho ya ghareeb, hamara hai na sab ke liye aik jaisa hai. English me aisa nahi hota. English me tag jumle ko dekh kar banta hai. Jumle me jo helping verb hai, wahi tag me ayega. Jumla positive hai to tag negative hoga aur jumla negative hai to tag positive hoga. Aur jumle ka subject tag me pronoun ban kar ayega. Bas yehi teen baatein hain. He is a teacher, isn't he? Dekho, helping verb is aya, positive jumle ka tag negative bana, aur subject he waisa ka waisa raha kyunke wo pehle se pronoun tha.

Ab do qanoon aik aik karke pakke kar lo

Pehla qanoon: ulta chalo. Jumla positive ho to tag negative banta hai. You are coming, aren't you? Jumla negative ho to tag positive banta hai. You are not coming, are you? Tag banane se pehle ye dekho ke jumla haan me hai ya na me. Dusra qanoon: helping verb wahi, subject pronoun ban kar. Jumle me jo helping verb hai, tag me wahi helping verb ayega. She has finished her work, hasn't she? Aur agar jumle ka subject koi naam ho to tag me us ki jagah pronoun ayega. Ali is honest, isn't he? Ali ki jagah he aya. Maryam is honest, isn't she? Naam nahi dohraya jata, pronoun ata hai.

Ab wo surat jo sab se zyada ati hai. Kabhi jumle me helping verb hota hi nahi. Ali plays cricket. Yahan is, has, will kuch nahi hai. Aisi surat me tag do, does ya did se banta hai. Qanoon ye hai ke jumla present simple me ho to do ya does ayega, aur past simple me ho to did ayega. Ali plays cricket, doesn't he? Plays par s dekh kar does chuna gaya. They play cricket, don't they? Aur maazi ho to: She sang a song, didn't she? Helping verb ghayab ho to ghabrana nahi. Waqt dekho, present ho to do khandan, past ho to did.

Helping verb ki pehchan

Ab un helping verbs ki fehrist zehen me bitha lo jo tag me seedha ate hain. Is, am, are, was, were. Has, have, had. Will, would. Can, could. Shall, should. May, might. Must. In me se jo jumle me hoga, wahi ulta hokar tag me ajayega. He can swim, can't he? We shall win, shan't we? She must work hard, mustn't she? Aur negative tag hamesha sikori hui shakal me ata hai. Is not ka isn't, are not ka aren't, do not ka don't, cannot ka can't. Paper me poora is not wala tag aksar option me hota hi nahi, sikori shakal hi sahi jawab hoti hai.

Ab khaas soratein, jahan paper phansata hai

Ab wo soratein aati hain jin par paper ka asal khel hota hai. Pehli khaas surat: I am wala jumla. I am late, aren't I? Sunne me ajeeb lagta hai lekin yehi sahi hai. Wajah ye hai ke am not ki koi sikori shakal English me hai hi nahi. Amn't koi lafz nahi hota. Is liye English ne are se kaam chala liya aur I am ka tag aren't I ban gaya. Ye paper ka sab se mashhoor tag hai, isay aisay hi rat lo.

Dusri khaas surat: Let's wale jumle. Let's go for a walk, shall we? Let's ka tag hamesha shall we hota hai. Will we ya don't we nahi. Let's dekhte hi shall we par nishan lagao. Teesri khaas surat: hukum aur darkhwast wale jumle, yani imperative. Close the door, will you? Sit down, will you? In jumlo me na subject hota hai na helping verb, lekin in ka tag hamesha will you hota hai. Narmi ke liye would you bhi chal jata hai, lekin paper me pehla jawab will you hota hai.

Chauthi khaas surat: wo jumle jin me negative lafz chhupa ho. Never, hardly, seldom, rarely, nobody, no one, nothing. Ye alfaaz jumle ko negative bana dete hain, chahe not nazar na aye. He never comes late, does he? Dekho, jumla dekhne me positive lagta hai lekin never ne usay negative kar diya, is liye tag positive bana. Nobody came to the meeting, did they? Nobody wala jumla negative hai aur us ka subject tag me they ban kar ata hai. Paanchvi khaas surat: everyone, everybody, someone, somebody, nobody, no one. In sab ka tag they se banta hai. Everyone is present, aren't they? Everyone aik lagta hai lekin tag me they ata hai. Aur everything, something, nothing ka tag it se banta hai. Everything is ready, isn't it? Chhati khaas surat: There wale jumle. There is a problem, isn't there? Yahan there khud tag me wapas ata hai, it nahi banta. Ye bhi paper me ata hai.

Board par qanoon Angrezi me

Board par ustad tag ke qanoon yun likhta hai. A positive statement takes a negative tag, and a negative statement takes a positive tag. The tag uses the same helping verb as the statement; if there is no helping verb, use do, does or did. The subject of the tag is always a pronoun. I am takes aren't I. Let's takes shall we. An order or request takes will you. Everyone and nobody take they, and everything takes it. Aur sab se aham line ye hai: the tag is the mirror of the statement, the same verb, the same subject, but the opposite sign.

Misaalein board se

Ab board par chand misaalein dekho. Har misaal ke sath us ki wajah bhi samjho.

1. She is a doctor, isn't she?
Positive jumla, negative tag. Helping verb is tag me wapas aya.

2. He does not like tea, does he?
Negative jumla, positive tag. Does not ka ulta does.

3. They will attend the meeting, won't they?
Will ka negative tag won't banta hai.

4. Ali plays football, doesn't he?
Helping verb ghayab tha. Plays par s dekh kar present ka does chuna gaya aur Ali he ban gaya.

5. She wrote a letter, didn't she?
Maazi ka jumla hai, helping verb nahi tha, is liye did aya.

6. I am your well-wisher, aren't I?
Ye khaas surat hai. I am ka tag aren't I hota hai. Amn't koi lafz nahi.

7. Let's play a match, shall we?
Let's ka tag hamesha shall we hota hai.

8. Open the window, will you?
Ye hukum wala jumla hai. Is ka tag will you banta hai.

9. Nobody helped him, did they?
Nobody ne jumle ko negative kar diya, is liye tag positive bana, aur subject they aya.

10. Everyone was happy, weren't they?
Everyone ka tag they se banta hai. Was ka ulta weren't ke sath joda gaya kyunke subject they hai.

11. He hardly ever misses the bus, does he?
Hardly ne jumle ko negative bana diya, is liye tag positive hai. Ye paper ka chhupa hua jaal hai.

12. There are two shops in this street, aren't there?
There wale jumle me there hi tag me wapas ata hai.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Tag rozmarra ki guftagu ki jaan hai. Tum kisi se baat karte hue us ki tasdeeq chahte ho to tag lagate ho. You will come tomorrow, won't you? Is se jumla sawal bhi ban jata hai aur narm bhi rehta hai. Daftar aur interview me bhi tag wale jumle aam hain. Paper me ye sawal aik line ka hota hai lekin is ke peeche poora system hai, helping verb ki pehchan, positive negative ka farq aur subject ki tabdeeli. Jo ye system samajh jaye, us ke liye tag banana bachon ka khel hai.

Ab paper wale andaaz ke do jumle dekho.

1. She has never visited Lahore, has she?
2. Let us start the work, shall we?

Pehle jumle me never ne jumle ko negative kar diya is liye tag positive has she bana. Dusra Let's wala hai is liye shall we aya. Haan, let us ko khol kar likha jaye to bhi tag wahi rehta hai.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab wo trick jo tag banana kabhi nahi bhulne degi. Isay Aaine wala qanoon kehte hain. Apne jumle ko aaine ke samne khada kar do. Aaina har cheez ko ulta dikhata hai. Jumla positive hai to aks negative ayega, jumla negative hai to aks positive. Lekin aaine me chehra wahi rehta hai, is liye helping verb wahi rahega aur subject bhi wahi, bas wo pronoun ban kar ayega. Pehle jumle ko aaine me dekho. Phir teen sawal poocho. Helping verb konsa hai? Jumla haan me hai ya na me? Subject ka pronoun kya banega? Teen jawab milte hi tag tayyar hai. Aur khaas sorato ko aaine ke kinare par likhi hui note samjho: I am ka aks aren't I, Let's ka shall we, hukum ka will you.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ab wo ghaltiyan dekho jo Pakistani students tag wale sawalo me karte hain.

Ghalat: He is a teacher, isn't it?
Sahi: He is a teacher, isn't he?
Wajah: tag me subject ka pronoun ata hai. He ka tag it nahi ban sakta.

Ghalat: You don't like coffee, isn't it?
Sahi: You don't like coffee, do you?
Wajah: tag jumle ki helping verb se banta hai. Yahan helping verb do hai, is nahi. Aur jumla negative hai is liye tag positive hoga.

Ghalat: I am late, amn't I?
Sahi: I am late, aren't I?
Wajah: amn't koi lafz nahi hota. I am ka tag hamesha aren't I hai.

Ghalat: She sang a song, doesn't she?
Sahi: She sang a song, didn't she?
Wajah: sang maazi hai. Maazi ke jumle ka tag did se banta hai, does se nahi.

Ghalat: Nobody came, didn't they?
Sahi: Nobody came, did they?
Wajah: nobody jumle ko negative bana deta hai. Negative jumle ka tag positive hota hai.

Ghalat: Let's go home, will we?
Sahi: Let's go home, shall we?
Wajah: Let's ka tag hamesha shall we hota hai.

Ghalat: Everyone is ready, isn't he?
Sahi: Everyone is ready, aren't they?
Wajah: everyone, somebody aur nobody ka tag they se banta hai, he ya she se nahi.

Jawab aur un ki wajah

1. Jawab B hai, isn't she. Positive jumla hai aur helping verb is hai, is liye us ka negative tag bana.
2. Jawab A hai, did he. Jumla negative hai is liye tag positive hoga, aur helping verb did wapas aya.
3. Jawab C hai, doesn't he. Helping verb ghayab tha, speaks par s dekh kar does chuna gaya aur Ali he ban gaya.
4. Jawab C hai, aren't I. Ye I am wali khaas surat hai. Amn't koi lafz nahi hota.
5. Jawab B hai, shall we. Let's ka tag hamesha shall we hota hai.
6. Jawab B hai, were they. Nobody jumle ko negative bana deta hai is liye tag positive hai, aur they ke sath were ata hai.
7. Jawab C hai, will you. Hukum aur darkhwast wale jumle ka tag will you banta hai.
8. Jawab A hai, haven't they. Have ka negative tag haven't hai aur subject they hai.
9. Jawab B hai, does she. Never ne jumle ko negative kar diya is liye tag positive bana. Jumla present simple hai aur helping verb ghayab tha, is liye does aya.
10. Jawab B hai. Can ka tag can't banta hai aur subject he ka pronoun he hi rahega. It wala option subject ki ghalti hai.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye pehli tasveer Aaine wale qanoon ki ho. Aik bare aaine ke samne aik jumla khada ho, She is a doctor, aur aaine ke andar us ka aks tag ki shakal me nazar aye, isn't she? Aaine ke neeche teen sawal likhe hon. Helping verb konsa hai? Jumla haan me hai ya na me? Subject ka pronoun kya hai? Student ye tasveer dekh le to tag banana us ke liye aks dekhna ban jayega.

Doosri tasveer khaas sorato ki chart ho. Chaar khane hon. Pehle me I am ke sath aren't I, dusre me Let's ke sath shall we, teesre me hukum wale jumle ke sath will you, aur chauthe me everyone aur nobody ke sath they. Har khane ka apna rang ho aur har khane me aik jumla misaal ke tor par likha ho. Ye chaar soratein hi paper ka asal khel hain, is liye in ki chart alag banni chahiye.
