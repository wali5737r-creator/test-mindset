Lesson: Ausat — Average
Ausat. Sab ko barabar kar dene wala hisaab, aur kul jama wala raaz

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me maths ke hisse me ausat ka sawal taqreeban har paper me ata hai. Kabhi seedha sawal ata hai ke chaar numberon ki ausat nikalo, aur kabhi ghuma kar ata hai ke class ki ausat umar itni thi, aik naya larka a gaya to ausat badal gayi, naye larke ki umar kitni hai. Seedha wala sawal to har koi kar leta hai, number ghumaye hue par kat-te hain. Is lesson ke baad tum ghumaya hua bhi nahi katoge, kyunke me tumhe formula ratta nahi marwa raha, me tumhe wo samajh de raha hun jis se har shakal ka sawal khud hal hota hai. Ausat asal me maths ka sab se asaan topic hai, bas aik baat pakki karni hai jo aage chal kar bata raha hun.

Pehle concept samajh lo, ausat hai kya

Dekho, ausat ka matlab sirf aik hai: sab ko barabar kar dena. Aik misaal se samjho. Teen dost hain. Aik ke paas 10 mithaiyan hain, dusre ke paas 20, teesre ke paas 30. Teeno ki mithaiyan aik jagah jama kar do, kul 60 ho gayin. Ab unhe barabar barabar baant do. Har aik ko 20 milen gi. Bas yehi 20 un teeno ki ausat hai. Ausat koi nayi cheez nahi hoti, ye wohi raqam hai jo har aik ko milti agar sab kuch jama karke barabar baant diya jata.

Ab cricket wali misaal lo, kyunke ausat ka sab se mashhoor istemal wahi hai. Aik batsman ne chaar matchon me 40, 60, 50 aur 30 runs banaye. Kul runs jama karo, 180. Ab ye runs chaaron matchon me barabar baant do, har match ke hisse me 45 aya. Yehi us ki batting average hai, 45. Jab akhbaar me likha ata hai ke falan batsman ki average 52 hai, to is ka matlab yehi hota hai ke us ke kul runs ko us ki innings par baant do to har innings me ausatar 52 bante hain. Na koi rocket science hai, na koi mushkil formula. Kul jama, aur phir barabar ki taqseem.

Class wali misaal bhi dekh lo. Aik class ke 5 bacho ne 60, 70, 80, 90 aur 100 number liye. Kul jama 400. Panchon me barabar baanto to har aik ke hisse 80 aya. Class ki ausat 80 hai. Gaur karo, kisi bache ne asal me 80 number nahi liye the, lekin ausat 80 hi hai. Ausat aik tasveeri number hota hai, jo poore group ki majmooi soorat aik hi number me bata deta hai. Yehi wajah hai ke ausat har jagah istemal hoti hai, mausam ki report me ausat darja hararat, mulk ki ausat amdani, factory me mazdoor ki ausat tankhwah.

To qanoon khud ban gaya. Ausat nikalni ho to pehle sab ko jama karo, phir jitni cheezain hain un par taqseem kar do. Aur is lesson ki sab se aham baat, jo paper ka asal khel hai, ye hai ke is qanoon ko ulta bhi chalaya ja sakta hai. Agar ausat pata ho aur tadaad pata ho, to kul jama nikal ata hai. Ausat ko tadaad se guna kar do, kul jama haath me hai. Aadhe se zyada ghumaye hue sawal isi ultay istemal se hal hote hain, aur aage misaalon me tum khud dekho ge.

Board par formula

Ustad jab board par ausat ka formula likhta hai to yun likhta hai. Average barabar hai Sum of values taqseem Number of values. Urdu me: Ausat = Kul Jama ÷ Tadaad. Aur is ka ulta, jo paper me sab se zyada kaam ata hai: Kul Jama = Ausat × Tadaad. Teesri baat, jab numbers aik hi farq se tartib me hon, jese 11, 13, 15, 17, 19, to ausat bilkul darmiyaan wala number hoti hai. Aur chauthi baat, jab do group milen to pehle dono ke kul jama alag alag nikalo, phir jama karke kul tadaad par taqseem karo. Do ausaton ko seedha seedha jama karke aadha karna sirf tab sahi hai jab dono groupon ki tadaad barabar ho.

Misaalein board se

Ab board par misaalein likhta hun. Asaan se shuru karte hain aur ahista ahista paper wali shakal tak jayen ge. Har misaal ke sath tareeqa bhi samjho, sirf jawab mat dekho.

1. 10, 20, 30 aur 40 ki ausat nikalo.
Kul jama 100 hai. Tadaad 4 hai. 100 ÷ 4 = 25. Ausat 25 hai. Gaur karo, 25 in chaaron me se koi number nahi, lekin barabar baanto to har aik ko 25 hi milega.

2. Aik batsman ne 4 innings me 35, 45, 60 aur 40 runs banaye. Us ki ausat kya hai?
Kul runs 180. Tadaad 4. 180 ÷ 4 = 45. Ausat 45 hai.

3. Panch numbers ki ausat 12 hai. Un ka kul jama kitna hai?
Ye ulta sawal hai. Ausat ko tadaad se guna karo. 12 × 5 = 60. Kul jama 60 hai. Dekha, jama kiye baghair kul jama nikal aya.

4. Aik class ke 20 students ke marks ki ausat 55 hai. Sab ke marks ka kul jama kitna hai?
55 × 20 = 1100. Kul marks 1100 hain.

5. Teen bhaiyon ki umaron ki ausat 12 saal hai. Aik chautha bhai aur shamil ho jata hai jis ki umar 20 saal hai. Ab chaaron ki ausat kya hogi?
Pehle purana kul jama nikalo. 12 × 3 = 36. Naye bhai ki umar jama karo, 36 + 20 = 56. Ab tadaad 4 hai. 56 ÷ 4 = 14. Nayi ausat 14 saal hai. Yehi paper ka sab se pasandida sawal hai, aur is ki jaan sirf kul jama hai.

6. Che mazdooron ki rozana mazdoori ki ausat 900 rupe hai. Aik mazdoor chala jata hai jo rozana 1200 rupe leta tha. Baqi panch ki ausat kya hogi?
Kul jama pehle nikalo. 900 × 6 = 5400. Jane wale ke 1200 ghatao, 4200 reh gaya. Ab tadaad 5 hai. 4200 ÷ 5 = 840. Baqi ki ausat 840 rupe hai. Dekho, jane wala ausat se zyada leta tha, is liye baqion ki ausat gir gayi. Ye samajh a jaye to jawab ka andaza pehle se lag jata hai.

7. Aik class ke 30 students ki ausat umar 13 saal hai. Jab teacher ko bhi shamil kar liya jaye to ausat 14 saal ho jati hai. Teacher ki umar kitni hai?
Students ka kul jama 13 × 30 = 390. Teacher samet kul tadaad 31, aur kul jama 14 × 31 = 434. Farq 434 − 390 = 44. Teacher ki umar 44 saal hai. Isi shakal ka sawal kabhi coach ke sath ata hai, kabhi captain ke sath, tareeqa aik hi hai.

8. 21, 23, 25, 27 aur 29 ki ausat nikalo.
Ruko, jama karne ki zaroorat nahi. Ye numbers aik hi farq, yani 2 ke farq se tartib me hain. Ausat bilkul darmiyaan wala number hai, 25. Paper me waqt bachana ho to ye trick sona hai.

9. 10, 20, 30 aur 40 ki ausat nikalo, lekin trick se.
Numbers tartib me hain lekin tadaad chaar hai, darmiyaan me aik number nahi, do numbers hain, 20 aur 30. In dono ki ausat lo, 25. Yehi jawab hai. Jori banane se bhi yehi nikalta hai, pehla aur aakhri 10 + 40 = 50, aadha 25.

10. Aik school ke do section hain. Pehle section ke 20 students ki ausat 45 hai, dusre section ke 20 students ki ausat 55 hai. Dono ki mili hui ausat kya hai?
Pehle kul jama alag alag. 45 × 20 = 900 aur 55 × 20 = 1100. Kul jama 2000, kul tadaad 40. 2000 ÷ 40 = 50. Yahan tadaad barabar thi is liye jawab 45 aur 55 ke bilkul darmiyaan aya. Agar tadaad alag hoti to jawab darmiyaan me na hota, zyada tadaad wale group ki taraf jhuka hota. Ye baat agli ghaltiyon me phir aye gi.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Rozmarra zindagi me ausat har jagah hai. Mausam wale kehte hain aaj ausat darja hararat itna raha. Coach team ki ausat age dekhta hai. Factory ka malik mazdooron ki ausat tankhwah nikalta hai. Lekin paper me ausat hamesha ghuma kar ati hai, aur do shaklain sab se zyada dohrai jati hain.

Pehli paper wali shakal dekho. Chaar doston ki ausat umar 15 saal hai. Aik panchwan dost shamil ho jata hai jis ki umar 20 saal hai. Ab panchon ki ausat kya hogi? Tareeqa wohi hai jo tum seekh chuke ho. Purana kul jama 15 × 4 = 60. Naye dost ki umar jama karo, 60 + 20 = 80. Ab tadaad 5 hai. 80 ÷ 5 = 16. Nayi ausat 16 saal hai. Dusri shakal ye hai. Panch doston ki ausat umar 24 saal hai. Un me se aik dost ki umar 30 saal hai aur wo group se alag ho jata hai to baqi chaar ki ausat umar 22.5 saal reh jati hai. Parkho: kul jama 120 tha, baqi ka kul 90, farq 30, bilkul wohi jo diya tha. Jab tum kul jama se sochte ho to har ghumaya hua sawal seedha ho jata hai.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab wo trick jo ausat ke har sawal ki chabi hai. Isay hum Kul Jama wala qanoon kehte hain. Jab bhi sawal me ausat dikhe, sab se pehle kul jama nikalo. Ausat dikhi nahi aur tum ne use tadaad se guna kiya nahi, to samjho aadha sawal wahi hal ho gaya. Naya banda aye to kul jama me us ki qeemat jama, banda jaye to ghata, aur aakhir me nayi tadaad par taqseem. Bas ye teen kadam har shakal par chalte hain. Aur dusri chhoti trick, tartib wale numbers me darmiyaan wala number hi ausat hai, jama karne ki mehnat hi nahi. Bas ye do baatein zehen me bitha lo: ausat ka matlab kul jama, aur tartib ka matlab darmiyaan.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ab wo ghaltiyan dekho jo students paper me karte hain. Main board par ghalat aur sahi sath sath likh raha hun, taake farq hamesha yaad rahe.

Ghalat: Aik group ki ausat 50 hai aur dusre ki 70, to dono ki mili hui ausat 60 hai.
Sahi: Pehle dono groupon ka kul jama alag alag nikalo, phir jama karke kul tadaad par taqseem karo.
Wajah: Ausat ka ausat sirf tab sahi hota hai jab dono groupon ki tadaad barabar ho. Tadaad alag ho to bari tadaad wala group jawab ko apni taraf kheenchta hai.

Ghalat: Naya student ane par purani ausat aur naye ki umar ko jama karke 2 par taqseem kar diya.
Sahi: Purana kul jama nikalo, naye ki umar jama karo, phir nayi tadaad par taqseem karo.
Wajah: Ausat numbers ki barabar taqseem hai, do numberon ki nahi. Kul jama ke baghair ghumaya hua sawal hal nahi hota.

Ghalat: Naya banda shamil hone ke baad bhi kul jama ko purani tadaad par taqseem kar diya.
Sahi: Jab banda barha to tadaad bhi barhi, taqseem nayi tadaad par hogi.
Wajah: Aadhe sawal yahin kat-te hain. Tadaad badalna bhool jana ausat ki sab se aam ghalti hai.

Ghalat: Batsman aik innings me 0 par out hua to use ginti me nahi liya aur ausat zyada nikal ayi.
Sahi: 0 bhi aik score hai, wo innings tadaad me shamil hogi.
Wajah: Ausat me har qeemat ginni jati hai, chahe wo zero hi kyun na ho. Zero ko chorna matlab tadaad kam karna, aur jawab ghalat.

Ghalat: 10, 20 aur 25 ki ausat darmiyaan wala number 20 keh di.
Sahi: Kul jama 55 hai, 55 ÷ 3 = 18.33 ke qareeb. Ausat 20 nahi hai.
Wajah: Darmiyaan wali trick sirf tab chalti hai jab numbers aik hi farq se tartib me hon. Yahan farq 10 aur 5 hai, tartib barabar nahi, trick nahi chalegi.

Jawab aur un ki wajah

1. Jawab B hai, 21. Kul jama 84 hai aur 84 ÷ 4 = 21.
2. Jawab C hai, 80. Kul jama hamesha ausat × tadaad hota hai, 16 × 5 = 80.
3. Jawab B hai, 50. Kul runs 200 hain, 200 ÷ 4 = 50.
4. Jawab C hai, 23 saal. Purana kul jama 14 × 8 = 112. Naya kul jama 15 × 9 = 135. Farq 135 − 112 = 23.
5. Jawab C hai, 29. Numbers aik hi farq se tartib me hain, ausat darmiyaan wala number hai.
6. Jawab C hai, 13,800 rupe. Kul jama 90,000 tha. 21,000 ghata kar 69,000 bacha, aur 69,000 ÷ 5 = 13,800.
7. Jawab B hai, 5. Kul jama 25 hai, 25 ÷ 5 = 5. Tartib wali trick se bhi darmiyaan wala number 5 hi hai.
8. Jawab B hai, 43 saal. Students ka kul jama 12 × 30 = 360. Teacher samet 13 × 31 = 403. Farq 403 − 360 = 43.
9. Jawab C hai, 31 saal. Teeno ka kul jama 75 hai. A aur B ka kul 44 hai. 75 − 44 = 31.
10. Jawab C hai, 35. Jab har number aik hi raqam se barhta hai to ausat bhi utni hi barhti hai. Har number me 5 barha, ausat me bhi 5 barha.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye do tasveerein bohat kaam ki hongi. Pehli tasveer barabar taqseem wali ho. Teen glass dikhaye jayen jin me pani alag alag unchai tak bhara ho, kam, zyada aur darmiyaan. Phir teer ke nishan se wahi teen glass dobara dikhaye jayen jin me pani teeno me bilkul barabar ho gaya ho. Neeche likha ho ke kul pani wohi hai, bas barabar ho gaya hai, yehi ausat hai. Student aik nazar me samajh jaye ke ausat koi jadui number nahi, barabar ki taqseem hai.

Doosri tasveer kul jama wale qanoon ki ho. Aik class ki tasveer ho jis me pehle 30 bache bethay hon aur un ke upar kul umar ka board laga ho. Phir teacher andar ata dikhaya jaye aur board par tadaad 31 aur nayi ausat likhi ho. Darmiyaan me bara sa formula ho, Kul Jama = Ausat × Tadaad. Ye tasveer dekh kar student ko hamesha yaad rahega ke naya banda aye ya purana jaye, pehla kadam kul jama hai.
