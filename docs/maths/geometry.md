Shaklain — Area, Perimeter aur Angles
Baarh ya farsh? Pehle ye pehchano, phir formula khud ayega

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me geometry ke sawal har dafa ate hain. Khet ki baarh, kamre ka farsh, gol mez ka kinara, triangle ka teesra kona, ye sawal ghuma phira kar har paper me hain. Aur aik ajeeb baat suno, aksar students formula jante hote hain lekin ghalat formula laga dete hain. Area wale sawal me perimeter nikaal liya, perimeter wale me area. Number concept par nahi, pehchan par kat-te hain. Is lesson ka pehla kaam yahi hai ke tum sawal ko parhte hi pehchan lo ke ye kinare ki baat kar raha hai ya andar ki jagah ki. Phir formula khud yaad ajaye ga.

Perimeter, chaaron taraf ki lambai

Pehle perimeter ko ghar se samajh lo. Tumhare ghar ke sahn ke chaaron taraf baarh lagani hai. Tum kya naapo ge? Sahn ke kinare kinare chal kar kul lambai. Bas yahi perimeter hai, kisi shakal ke chaaron taraf ki kul lambai. Race ke track par aik chakar lagana bhi perimeter hai. Khet ke gird taar lagana bhi perimeter hai.

Do baatein pakki kar lo. Perimeter sirf kinara hai, andar ki jagah se is ka koi lena dena nahi. Aur perimeter lambai hai, is liye is ki ikai metre, centimetre ya kilometre hoti hai, seedhi lambai wali.

Area, andar ki jagah

Ab area ko samajh lo. Kamre ke farsh par tiles lagani hain. Tum kya ginoge? Farsh kul kitni jagah gher raha hai. Bas yahi area hai, kisi shakal ke andar ki kul jagah. Carpet aur dari bichhana bhi area hai, aur zameen ka raqba bhi area hai.

Area ki ikai perimeter se alag hai, aur yehi farq paper ka phanda hai. Aik tile aik metre lambi aur aik metre chori hoti hai, to wo aik square metre jagah ghirti hai. Area hamesha square me ata hai, square metre ya square centimetre. Zehen me aik tasveer bana lo. Baarh kharidne jao to perimeter socho, tiles kharidne jao to area socho.

Square aur rectangle ke formula, bana kar

Ab formula ratne ki bajaye bana kar dekho. Square, yani murabba, ki chaaron sides barabar hoti hain. Agar aik side 5 metre hai to chaaron taraf ki lambai 5 chaar dafa, yani 4 × 5 = 20 metre. To perimeter barabar 4 guna side. Aur area ke liye socho, farsh par tiles ki 5 kataarein hain aur har kataar me 5 tiles hain, kul 25 tiles. To area barabar side guna side.

Rectangle, yani mustateel, me lambaai aur chorai mukhtalif hoti hain. Perimeter me lambaai do dafa ati hai aur chorai bhi do dafa, kyunke samne wali sides barabar hoti hain. Is liye perimeter barabar 2 guna (lambaai jama chorai). Aur area ke liye wahi tiles wali tasveer. Zameen 8 metre lambi aur 5 metre chori hai. Tiles ki 8 wali 5 kataarein bichengi, kul 40 khane. Isi liye area barabar lambaai guna chorai. Guna koi ratta nahi, ye kataaron ka hisaab hai.

Triangle, rectangle ka aadha

Triangle, yani masalas, ka perimeter to seedha hai, teeno sides ka jama. Lekin is ke area me aik raaz hai jo paper bohat azmata hai. Area barabar aadha guna base guna height. Ye aadha aya kahan se?

Aik rectangle lo aur use aik konay se dusre konay tak, yani diagonal par, kaat do. Do triangle bane, aur dono bilkul barabar ke. Matlab har triangle us rectangle ka aadha hisa hai. Rectangle ka area base guna height tha, to triangle ka area us ka aadha ho gaya. Bas, ye aadha kaatne wali tasveer se aya hai, ratne se nahi. Aur aik ehtiyaat, height hamesha wo seedhi lambai hai jo base par seedhi khari ho, yani perpendicular ho. Triangle ka jhuka hua kinara height nahi hota.

Circle aur pi ki kahani

Ab gol shakal par ao. Circle ke teen naam pakke kar lo. Markaz, yani centre, gol ke bilkul beech wala nuqta. Radius wo faasla hai jo markaz se kinare tak ho. Aur diameter wo lakeer hai jo aik kinare se dusre kinare tak markaz se guzar kar jati hai. Diameter hamesha radius ka dugna hota hai, yani d barabar 2r.

Circle ka perimeter, jise circumference kehte hain, barabar 2πr hai. Aur is ka area barabar πr² hai. Ab ye π, yani pi, hai kya? Ye is lesson ki sab se pyari baat hai. Koi si bhi gol cheez lo, thaali, plate, gaari ka pahiya. Us ke kinare ke gird dori lapait kar ghera naapo, phir us ka diameter naapo, aur ghere ko diameter se taqseem kar do. Jawab hamesha taqreeban 3.14 ayega. Chhoti thaali ho ya bara pahiya, ye hisaab nahi badalta. Bas isi musalsal jawab ko pi kehte hain, aur paper me is ki qeemat 22/7 li jati hai.

Aik pehchan bhi de dun. Jab paper me radius ya diameter 7, 14, 21 ya 28 diya ho, to samajh jao ke sawal 22/7 ke liye bana hai, 7 asaani se kat jaye ga aur hisaab saaf ho jayega.

Angles ki aqsaam

Ab konon, yani angles, ki baat. Kona do lakeeron ke milne se banta hai aur degree me naapa jata hai. Chaar aqsaam paper me bar bar ati hain.

Pehla acute angle, nuqeela kona. Ye 90 degree se chhota hota hai. Kainchi ke do hison ke darmiyan wala tang kona zehen me lao. Dusra right angle, theek 90 degree. Tumhari kitaab ka kona, meiz ka kona, yehi right angle hai. Teesra obtuse angle, phaila hua kona. Ye 90 se bara lekin 180 se chhota hota hai, jese zyada khuli hui kitaab ke do safhon ke darmiyan wala kona. Aur chaatha straight angle, theek 180 degree, jo bilkul seedhi lakeer banata hai.

Triangle ke teen konay aur 180 wala raaz

Aakhir me wo baat jo har paper me ati hai. Triangle ke teenon konon ka jama hamesha 180 degree hota hai. Ye bhi ratta nahi hai, is ki tasveer hai. Kagaz ka aik triangle banao. Us ke teeno konay kaat kar alag kar lo. Ab teeno ko aik jagah jor kar seedhi line par rakho. Teeno mil kar aik seedhi lakeer ban jayenge, na kuch zyada, na kuch kam. Aur seedhi lakeer ka kona 180 degree hota hai. Bas yahi se ye qanoon aya hai.

Is ka faida seedha hai. Agar triangle ke do konay maloom hon to teesra kona 180 me se dono ka jama ghatta kar nikal ata hai. Paper ka sab se asaan number yahi hota hai.

Board par formula

Ustad jab board par is lesson ka khulasa likhta hai to kuch yun likhta hai. Perimeter of a square = 4 × side. Area of a square = side × side. Perimeter of a rectangle = 2 × (length + width). Area of a rectangle = length × width. Perimeter of a triangle = sum of its three sides. Area of a triangle = 1/2 × base × height. Diameter = 2 × radius. Circumference of a circle = 2πr. Area of a circle = πr². π = 22/7. The three angles of a triangle always add up to 180 degrees.

Misaalein board se

Ab board par misaalein hal karte hain. Har misaal ke sath wajah bhi samjho, sirf jawab mat dekho.

1. Aik square ki side 5 metre hai. Us ke gird baarh lagani hai aur andar tiles bhi lagani hain. Dono ka hisaab nikalo.
Baarh perimeter hai, 4 × 5 = 20 metre. Tiles area hai, 5 × 5 = 25 square metre. Dekho, aik hi shakal, do sawal, aur dono ke jawab aur ikaiyan alag hain.

2. Aik bagh 8 metre lamba aur 5 metre chora hai. Us ka area aur perimeter nikalo.
Area barabar 8 × 5 = 40 square metre. Perimeter barabar 2 × (8 + 5) = 2 × 13 = 26 metre.

3. Aik khet 40 metre lamba aur 25 metre chora hai. Us ke chaaron taraf baarh lagani hai. Baarh kitni lambi hogi?
Baarh sirf kinara hai, yani perimeter. 2 × (40 + 25) = 2 × 65 = 130 metre. Area wala 1000 yahan lagana hi ghalti hai jo paper azmata hai.

4. Aik kamra 6 metre lamba aur 4 metre chora hai. Farsh par aik square metre wali tiles lagani hain. Kitni tiles lagengi?
Tiles jagah gherengi, yani area. 6 × 4 = 24 square metre, to 24 tiles lagengi.

5. Aik triangle ka base 10 centimetre aur height 6 centimetre hai. Us ka area nikalo.
Pehle poora rectangle socho, 10 × 6 = 60. Triangle us ka aadha hai, to area barabar 30 square centimetre. Formula se bhi yahi hai, 1/2 × 10 × 6 = 30.

6. Aik triangle ki teen sides 3, 4 aur 5 centimetre hain. Us ka perimeter nikalo.
Teeno ka jama, 3 + 4 + 5 = 12 centimetre. Perimeter me koi guna taqseem nahi, sirf chaaron, yani teeno taraf ki lambaiyon ka jama hai.

7. Aik gol thaali ka radius 7 centimetre hai. Us ke kinare ki lambai, yani circumference, nikalo.
Circumference barabar 2πr, yani 2 × 22/7 × 7. Neeche wala 7 upar wale 7 se kat gaya, jawab 44 centimetre.

8. Wohi gol shakal, radius 7 centimetre, us ka area nikalo.
Area barabar πr², yani 22/7 × 7 × 7. Aik 7 kat gaya, reh gaya 22 × 7 = 154 square centimetre. Gaur karo, circumference 44 centimetre thi aur area 154 square centimetre hai. Numbers bhi alag, ikaiyan bhi alag.

9. Aik pahiye ka diameter 70 centimetre hai. Wo aik chakar me kitna faasla tay karta hai?
Pehla qadam, radius nikalo. Diameter ka aadha, 70 / 2 = 35 centimetre. Aik chakar ka faasla circumference ke barabar hai, 2 × 22/7 × 35 = 220 centimetre.

10. Aik triangle ke do konay 50 degree aur 60 degree hain. Teesra kona kitna hoga?
Teeno ka jama 180 hota hai. Pehle do ka jama 110 hai. 180 − 110 = 70 degree. Teesra kona 70 degree ka hai.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Rozmarra me ye hisaab har jagah hai. Plot ki qeemat area par lagti hai, baarh aur taar perimeter par ati hai, kamre ka rang aur farsh area mangta hai. Paper me ye khaas teen suraton me ata hai, field ki baarh, kamre ka farsh, aur gol shakal ka kinara. Ab paper wale andaaz ke do sawal dekho.

1. Aik rectangle plot 25 metre lamba aur 15 metre chora hai. Us ke gird baarh lagani hai. Baarh kitni lambi hogi?
Hal zehen me karo. Baarh perimeter hai, 2 × (25 + 15) = 2 × 40 = 80 metre. Options me 375 bhi hoga, jo area hai. Wahi paper ka phanda hai.

2. Aik gol mez ka radius 14 centimetre hai. Us ke kinare par lace lagani hai. Lace kitni lagegi?
Lace kinare par lagegi, yani circumference. 2 × 22/7 × 14 = 88 centimetre.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab wo trick jo is poore lesson ko do lafzon me bandh degi. Baarh ya farsh? Sawal parhte hi apne aap se ye poochho. Agar baat kinare ki hai, baarh, lace, taar, chakar, to perimeter. Agar baat andar ki hai, tiles, carpet, rang, jagah, to area. Formula baad me ata hai, pehchan pehle ati hai.

Aur do chhoti lines aur bandh lo. Triangle dikhe to aadha kabhi na bhoolo. Aur circle dikhe to pehla qadam hamesha radius hai, diameter diya ho to use aadha karna hi pehla kaam hai.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ab wo ghaltiyan dekho jo Pakistani students paper me karte hain. Main board par ghalat tareeqa aur sahi tareeqa sath sath likh raha hun, taake farq hamesha yaad rahe.

Ghalat: Kamre ke farsh ka area nikaal kar jawab metre me likh diya.
Sahi: Area ka jawab hamesha square metre me hota hai.
Wajah: Area do lambaiyon ke guna se banta hai, lambaai guna chorai. Is liye us ki ikai bhi dugni, yani square, hoti hai.

Ghalat: Triangle ka area nikaalte hue base guna height karke chhor diya, aadha nahi kiya.
Sahi: Triangle ka area hamesha aadha guna base guna height hota hai.
Wajah: Triangle rectangle ka aadha hisa hai, kaat kar khud dekh sakte ho. Aadha bhoolna matlab dugna jawab likhna.

Ghalat: Circle wale sawal me diameter ko hi radius ki jagah rakh kar formula laga diya.
Sahi: Pehle diameter ko aadha karke radius nikalo, phir formula lagao.
Wajah: Dono formula radius mangte hain. Diameter rakhne se circumference dugni aur area chaar guna ho jata hai.

Ghalat: Rectangle ka perimeter sirf lambaai jama chorai kar diya.
Sahi: Perimeter barabar 2 guna (lambaai jama chorai) hota hai.
Wajah: Baarh chaaron taraf lagti hai. Lambaai bhi do dafa ati hai aur chorai bhi do dafa. Aik aik dafa lena matlab aadhi baarh lagana.

Ghalat: Square ka area 4 guna side kar diya.
Sahi: Square ka area side guna side hota hai. 4 guna side us ka perimeter hai.
Wajah: Chaar guna chaaron taraf ki lambai hai, andar ki jagah nahi. Yehi baarh aur farsh wali pehchan hai jo yahan kaam ati hai.

Jawab aur un ki wajah

1. Jawab B hai, 36 centimetre. Perimeter barabar 4 × 9 = 36. 81 wala option area tha, wahi phanda hai.
2. Jawab C hai, 48 square metre. Area barabar lambaai guna chorai, 12 × 4 = 48.
3. Jawab B hai, 20 square centimetre. 1/2 × 8 × 5 = 20. Aadha na karte to 40 ata, jo ghalat option A me mojood hai.
4. Jawab C hai, 88 centimetre. 2 × 22/7 × 14 = 88. 154 wala option area hai, kinare aur andar ko mix karna hi yahan ki ghalti hai.
5. Jawab A hai, 10 centimetre. Radius diameter ka aadha hota hai, 20 / 2 = 10.
6. Jawab D hai, obtuse angle. 95 degree 90 se bara hai lekin 180 se chhota, to ye phaila hua kona hai.
7. Jawab B hai, 50 degree. 180 − (70 + 60) = 180 − 130 = 50.
8. Jawab C hai, 121 square centimetre. Area barabar side guna side, 11 × 11 = 121. 44 wala option perimeter tha.
9. Jawab A hai, 100 metre. Baarh perimeter hai, 2 × (30 + 20) = 100. 600 wala option area hai.
10. Jawab C hai, 154 square centimetre. Area barabar πr², yani 22/7 × 7 × 7 = 154. 44 wala option isi circle ki circumference thi.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye do tasveerein bohat kaam ki hongi. Pehli tasveer aik hi rectangle ki ho jo do rangon me samjhai jaye. Us ke chaaron kinare par baarh bani hui dikhayi jaye aur kinare ke sath perimeter likha ho. Aur usi rectangle ke andar chhote chhote khano ka jaal, yani grid, bana ho jis par area likha ho. Aik nazar me student ke zehen me baith jaye ke kinara aur cheez hai aur andar ki jagah aur cheez hai.

Doosri tasveer triangle ke konon wali ho. Aik triangle ke teeno konay mukhtalif rangon me katay hue dikhayen jayen, aur phir wohi teen konay aik seedhi lakeer par jure hue dikhayen jayen, darmiyan me 180 degree likha ho. Teesri chhoti tasveer circle ki ho sakti hai jis par markaz se kinare tak radius ki lakeer aur kinare se kinare tak diameter ki lakeer alag rangon me bani ho, taake dono ka farq aur dugna wala rishta hamesha yaad rahe.
