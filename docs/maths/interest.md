Sood — Simple aur Compound Interest
Paisa kiraye par dene ka hisaab. Sood par sood wali baat, gin kar samjho

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me sood ka sawal ata rehta hai. Kabhi seedha simple interest pooch lete hain, kabhi compound ka ghumaya hua sawal ajata hai, aur kabhi dono ka farq pooch kar student ko parkha jata hai. Is topic me do cheezain alag karna hi asal kamaal hai. Aik seedha sood hai jisme hisaab har saal aik jaisa rehta hai. Dusra murakkab yani compound sood hai jisme sood par bhi sood lagta hai. Jo student ye farq gin kar samajh le, sirf formula se nahi, us ke liye is topic ka koi sawal mushkil nahi rehta. Is lesson me hum 1000 rupay ko teen saal tak chala kar dekhen ge, taake ankhon ke samne farq ajaye.

Pehle sood ko samajh lo

Dekho, sood asal me paise ka kiraya hai. Tum apna makan kiraye par do to har mahina kiraya milta hai. Isi tarah jab tum apna paisa bank ko dete ho, to bank us paisa ko istemal karta hai aur tumhen us ka kiraya deta hai. Ye kiraya hi sood hai. Aur jab tum bank se paisa lo, to kiraya tum dete ho. Baat seedhi hai, paisa bhi aik cheez hai aur us ke istemal ki bhi qeemat hoti hai.

Ab teen cheezon ke naam seekh lo, kyunke har sawal inhi teeno se banta hai. Pehli, asal raqam, jise principal kehte hain. Ye wo paisa hai jo shuru me diya ya liya gaya. Dusri, sharah, jise rate kehte hain. Ye feesad me hoti hai aur hamesha salana hoti hai, yani har saal asal ka itne feesad kiraya. Teesri, muddat, jise time kehte hain, saalon me. In teeno ke ilawa sawal me kuch nahi hota.

Simple sood aur wo seedha kyun hai

Ab simple interest ko samjho. Is me usool ye hai ke har saal sood sirf asal raqam par lagta hai, pehle mile hue sood par kabhi nahi. Farz karo 100 rupay par sharah 10 feesad salana hai. Aik saal ka sood 10 rupay hua. Dusre saal bhi sood 10 hi hoga, kyunke hisaab phir asal 100 par hi ban raha hai. Teesre saal bhi 10. Har saal brabar, is liye ise seedha ya simple kehte hain.

Ab dekho formula khud kese banta hai. 100 rupay par aik saal ka sood R rupay hota hai, jahan R sharah hai. To 1 rupay par aik saal ka sood R taqseem 100 hua. Aur P rupayo par aik saal ka sood P guna R taqseem 100 hua. Ab T saal hon to itna hi sood T dafa milega, yani P guna R guna T taqseem 100. Bas, yahi mashhoor formula hai: Simple Interest = PRT/100. Tum ne use rata nahi, tum ne use bante hue dekha hai. Ab ye kabhi nahi bhulega. Aik aur lafz bhi yaad rakho: kul raqam jo aakhir me milti hai use amount kehte hain, aur wo asal jama sood hoti hai.

Compound sood, yani sood par sood

Ab compound interest ki baat karte hain, aur isay gin kar dekhte hain, kyunke yehi wo jagah hai jahan ratta kaam nahi ata. Farz karo tum ne 1000 rupay bank me rakhe, sharah 10 feesad salana, compound.

Pehla saal: sood asal 1000 par lagega, 1000 ka 10 feesad barabar 100. Kul raqam 1100 ho gayi. Gaur karo, pehle saal compound aur simple brabar hain, koi farq nahi.

Dusra saal: ab bank tumhari raqam 1100 samjhta hai, kyunke pehle saal ka sood bhi tumhara hai aur wo bhi bank ke paas para hai. Sood 1100 par lagega, 1100 ka 10 feesad barabar 110. Kul raqam 1210 ho gayi. Ab farq dekho. Simple hota to dusre saal bhi 100 milta aur kul sood 200 hota. Compound me kul sood 210 hua. Ye zyada wale 10 rupay kya hain? Ye pehle saal ke sood 100 par laga hua sood hai. Isi ka naam hai sood par sood, aur yahi compound ki pehchan hai.

Teesra saal bhi gin lo taake baat pakki ho jaye. Sood ab 1210 par lagega, 121. Kul raqam 1331, kul sood 331. Simple hota to 300 hota. Farq barh kar 31 ho gaya. Dekha, farq har saal barhta hai, kyunke sood dene wali raqam khud barhti ja rahi hai. Ye tasveer zehen me bitha lo, compound ka har sawal isi tasveer se hal hoga.

Do saal ka tez tareeqa

Ab paper ke liye aik tez baat. Do saal ke liye compound aur simple ka farq nikalne ka seedha tareeqa hai: Farq barabar P guna (R taqseem 100) ka murabba. Upar wali misaal par parkho. P barabar 1000, R taqseem 100 barabar 0.10, is ka murabba barabar 0.01. 1000 guna 0.01 barabar 10. Wohi 10 rupay jo gin kar aye the. Aur aik dusri soorat bhi yaad rakho: do saal ki kul compound sharah is tarah banti hai, R jama R jama (R guna R taqseem 100). 10 feesad ke liye ye 10 jama 10 jama 1 barabar 21 feesad hai. Yani 2 saal me 1000 par compound sood 210, jo hum gin chuke hain.

Board par formula

Ab ustad board par formula likhta hai. Simple sood ke liye: Simple Interest = P × R × T ÷ 100, jahan P asal raqam, R salana sharah feesad me, aur T muddat saalon me hai. Kul raqam yani Amount = P + Interest. Compound ke liye board par yun likha hota hai: Amount = P × (1 + R ÷ 100)^T, aur compound sood barabar Amount manfi P. Us chhote se nishan ^T ka matlab hai ke (1 + R ÷ 100) ko T dafa apne aap se guna karo. Do saal hon to do dafa, teen saal hon to teen dafa. Formula dekh kar ghabrana mat, ye wohi hisaab hai jo hum ne 1000 rupay par ungliyon par gin kar kiya tha.

Misaalein board se

Ab board par misaalein hal karte hain. Pehle seedhi, phir ahista ahista ghumayi hui. Har misaal me pehle ye tay karo ke sawal simple hai ya compound, aadha sawal yahi tay ho jata hai.

1. 5000 rupay par 6 feesad salana ke hisaab se 2 saal ka simple sood kitna hai?
Formula lagayo, 5000 guna 6 guna 2 taqseem 100 barabar 600. Kul raqam 5600 hogi. Jawab 600 rupay sood.

2. 8000 rupay par 5 feesad salana se 3 saal ka simple sood kitna hai?
8000 guna 5 guna 3 taqseem 100 barabar 1200. Jawab 1200 rupay.

3. 10,000 rupay par 10 feesad se 2 saal ka simple aur compound sood nikalo aur farq batao.
Simple barabar 10,000 guna 10 guna 2 taqseem 100 barabar 2000. Compound gin kar, pehla saal 1000, kul 11,000, dusra saal 1100, kul sood 2100. Farq barabar 100. Jawab simple 2000, compound 2100, farq 100.

4. 2000 rupay par 10 feesad compound sood 2 saal ke liye kitna hoga?
Pehla saal sood 200, raqam 2200. Dusra saal sood 2200 ka 10 feesad barabar 220. Kul sood 200 jama 220 barabar 420. Jawab 420 rupay.

5. Kis raqam par 8 feesad salana se 2 saal me 800 rupay simple sood milega?
Ye ulta sawal hai. Sood barabar P guna 8 guna 2 taqseem 100, yani P ka 16 feesad. Agar 16 feesad barabar 800 hai to pura yani 100 feesad barabar 800 taqseem 0.16 barabar 5000. Jawab 5000 rupay.

6. 15,000 rupay par 12 feesad salana se 3 saal ka simple sood aur kul raqam batao.
Sood barabar 15,000 guna 12 guna 3 taqseem 100 barabar 5400. Kul raqam barabar 15,000 jama 5400 barabar 20,400. Jawab sood 5400, kul raqam 20,400.

7. 6000 rupay par 2 saal me 720 rupay simple sood mila. Sharah kitni thi?
720 barabar 6000 guna R guna 2 taqseem 100. Is se R barabar 720 guna 100 taqseem 12,000 barabar 6. Jawab 6 feesad salana.

8. 4000 rupay par 10 feesad se 1 saal ka simple aur compound sood nikalo.
Simple barabar 400. Compound bhi 400, kyunke pehle saal dono ka hisaab asal par hi banta hai. Jawab dono brabar, 400. Ye chhoti si baat paper me bare sawal ban kar ati hai.

9. 1000 rupay par 10 feesad compound sood 3 saal ke liye kitna hoga?
Gin kar chalo. Pehla saal 100, raqam 1100. Dusra saal 110, raqam 1210. Teesra saal 121, raqam 1331. Kul sood barabar 331. Jawab 331 rupay. Simple hota to 300 hota, farq 31 hai.

10. 12,000 rupay par 10 feesad salana se 6 mahine ka simple sood kitna hai?
Yahan jaal muddat ka hai. 6 mahine matlab aadha saal, T barabar 1/2. Sood barabar 12,000 guna 10 guna 1/2 taqseem 100 barabar 600. Jawab 600 rupay. Mahine dikhen to pehle saal me badlo.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Roz marra me ye hisaab bank ki bachat, qarzo aur installment wali cheezon me chhupa hota hai. Jab bank kehta hai ke hum compound dete hain to ab tum jante ho ke is ka matlab tumhare sood par bhi sood milega. Paper me is ki teen shaklain sab se zyada ati hain. Aik seedhi simple wali. Dusri do saal compound wali jisme gin kar ya farq wale tareeqe se jawab ata hai. Teesri ulti wali, jisme sood de kar asal ya sharah poochi jati hai. Ab paper wale andaaz ke do sawal dekho.

1. Find the simple interest on 7500 rupees at 8 percent per annum for 3 years. Hisaab, 7500 guna 8 guna 3 taqseem 100 barabar 1800.
2. What is the compound interest on 5000 rupees at 10 percent per annum for 2 years? Pehla saal 500, raqam 5500. Dusra saal 550, kul sood 1050.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Is topic ki trick aik jumla hai: pehla saal dost, dusra saal jaadu. Matlab pehle saal simple aur compound bilkul brabar hote hain, farq dusre saal se shuru hota hai. Jab bhi sawal 1 saal ka ho, be jhijhak simple wala hisaab kar do, jawab compound ka bhi wohi hoga.

Aur do chaalein aur bandh lo. Aik, do saal ke farq ke liye seedha formula: Farq barabar P guna (R taqseem 100) ka murabba. Dusri, 100 wali chaal. Sharah ya raqam ajeeb lage to pehle 100 rupay par gin lo. 100 par 10 feesad se 2 saal ka compound sood 21 hota hai. Ab jis raqam ka sawal ho, tanasub laga lo. 100 par 21, to 5000 par 1050. Bare numberon ka dar khatam.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ab wo ghaltiyan dekho jin par students number kaatte hain. Main ghalat tareeqa aur sahi tareeqa sath sath likh raha hun.

Ghalat: Compound sood bhi PRT taqseem 100 se nikal liya.
Sahi: Compound har saal nayi raqam par lagta hai, saal ba saal gin kar ya Amount wale formula se nikalo.
Wajah: PRT wala hisaab sirf simple ka hai. Do saal se zyada me is se jawab hamesha kam aur ghalat ata hai.

Ghalat: Compound me dusre saal bhi sood asal raqam par hi laga diya.
Sahi: Dusre saal sood asal aur pehle saal ke sood, dono par lagta hai.
Wajah: Yahi ghalti compound ko simple bana deti hai. Upar 1000 wali misaal me dusre saal ka sood 100 nahi 110 tha, yehi 10 ka farq pure topic ki jaan hai.

Ghalat: Sawal me muddat 6 mahine thi aur men ne T ki jagah 6 rakh diya.
Sahi: Muddat hamesha saalon me rakho. 6 mahine barabar 1/2 saal.
Wajah: Sharah salana hoti hai. Mahine ko saal me badle baghair hisaab dugna ho jata hai aur jawab ghalat.

Ghalat: Sawal sood pooch raha tha aur men ne kul raqam jawab me likh di.
Sahi: Sood aur kul raqam alag cheezain hain. Kul raqam me se asal nikal do to sood bachta hai.
Wajah: Paper jaan boojh kar dono options deta hai. Sawal ko aakhir tak parho, poocha kya hai, sood ya kul raqam.

Ghalat: Aik saal ke compound sawal me lambi ginoti shuru kar di aur waqt zaya hua.
Sahi: Aik saal ke liye simple aur compound brabar hote hain, seedha simple wala hisaab karo.
Wajah: Pehle saal dono ka sood asal par hi lagta hai. Ye pehchan paper me waqt bachati hai.

Jawab aur un ki wajah

1. Jawab B hai, 400. 4000 guna 5 guna 2 taqseem 100 barabar 400.
2. Jawab B hai. Sood barabar asal guna sharah guna muddat taqseem 100. Ye wohi formula hai jo hum ne 100 rupay wali misaal se bante hue dekha tha.
3. Jawab C hai, 1210. Pehla saal sood 100, raqam 1100. Dusra saal sood 110, kul raqam 1210. Gaur karo, sawal kul raqam pooch raha tha, sood nahi.
4. Jawab C hai, 1050. Pehla saal 500, raqam 5500. Dusra saal 550. Kul sood 500 jama 550 barabar 1050. Simple hota to 1000 hota.
5. Jawab C hai, 6000. 1200 barabar raqam guna 10 guna 2 taqseem 100, yani raqam ka paanchwa hissa. 1200 guna 5 barabar 6000.
6. Jawab C hai, 2000. 25,000 guna 8 guna 1 taqseem 100 barabar 2000.
7. Jawab B hai. Compound ka matlab hi ye hai ke har naye saal ka sood us kul raqam par lagta hai jo pichhle saal tak ban chuki ho, asal aur sood mila kar.
8. Jawab C hai, 4200. Do saal ki kul compound sharah 10 ke liye 21 feesad hoti hai. 20,000 ka 21 feesad barabar 4200.
9. Jawab B hai, 6 feesad. 1800 barabar 10,000 guna sharah guna 3 taqseem 100. Is se sharah barabar 1800 guna 100 taqseem 30,000 barabar 6.
10. Jawab C hai, dono brabar. Pehle saal compound ka sood bhi asal par hi lagta hai, farq dusre saal se paida hota hai. Pehla saal dost wala jumla yaad rakho.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye do tasveerein bohat kaam ki hongi. Pehli tasveer do seedhiyon wali ho. Aik taraf simple sood ki seedhi ho jisme har saal brabar uncha qadam ho, 1000, phir 1100, phir 1200, phir 1300. Dusri taraf compound ki seedhi ho jisme har qadam pehle se bara hota jaye, 1000, phir 1100, phir 1210, phir 1331. Neeche likha ho ke simple me qadam brabar rehta hai, compound me qadam barhta jata hai. Aik nazar me pura topic samajh ajayega.

Doosri tasveer aik chhote pode ki ho jo teen saal me barhta dikhaya gaya ho. Pehle saal poda chhota, dusre saal us ki tehniyon par nayi tehniyan, teesre saal aur ghana. Sath likha ho ke compound sood bhi yunhi barhta hai, jo barha hai us par mazeed barhta hai. Student jab bhi sood par sood sune ga, use ye poda yaad ayega aur concept kabhi nahi bhulega.
