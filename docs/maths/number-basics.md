Number ki Buniyad — Fractions, LCM, HCF aur Umar ke Sawal
Hisa, ghanti aur umar, teenon ka concept aik hi zuban me

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me fractions, LCM, HCF aur umar ke sawal har dafa ate hain. Ye wo sawal hain jo ratta bilkul nahi mangte, sirf concept mangte hain. Aur mazay ki baat suno, in me ghaltiyan hamesha aik hi jaisi hoti hain. Aadhe se zyada students fraction ke jama me neeche wale numbers bhi jama kar dete hain, ghanti wale sawal me LCM ki jagah HCF nikaal lete hain, aur umar wale sawal me waqt sirf aik bande par guzarte hain. Is lesson me hum teeno cheezain ghar ki misaalon se samjhen ge, taake paper me jesa bhi sawal ghuma kar aye, tum hal kar lo.

Pehle fraction ko roti se samajh lo

Ghar me ammi roti aadhi kar ke deti hain. Kabhi aadhi, kabhi chauthai hisa. Bas yahi fraction hai. Fraction ka matlab hai, barabar hison me se kuch hisay. Aik roti ko do barabar hison me kaato, aik hisa uthao, wo 1/2 hai. Chaar barabar hison me kaato, aik uthao, wo 1/4 hai.

Ab do numbers ke kaam pakke kar lo. Neeche wala number, jise denominator kehte hain, batata hai ke kul kitne barabar hisay hue. Upar wala number, jise numerator kehte hain, batata hai ke un me se kitne hisay liye ya kitne hisay hain. Aur aik shart hamesha yaad rakho, hisay barabar hona lazmi hai. Agar roti ke do tukre aik bara aur aik chhota kar diye, to chhote ko aadhi roti nahi keh sakte. Fraction ki neev hi barabar hisay hain.

Fractions ka jama aur ghatta, size aik karna

Ab wo sawal jahan sab se zyada number kat-te hain. 1/2 jama 1/4 kitna hua? Zehen kehta hai upar wale jama karo, neeche wale jama karo. Ruko. Pehle roti par dekho. Aadhi roti aik bara tukra hai, chauthai us se chhota tukra hai. Dono ke hison ka size mukhtalif hai, aur mukhtalif size ke hisay seedha jama nahi hote.

Hal ye hai ke pehle hison ka size aik karo. Aadhi roti ko beech se aik dafa aur kaat do. Ab wo do chauthai ban gayi. Roti utni hi hai, bas tukre chhote ho gaye. Yani 1/2 barabar 2/4. Ab jama asaan hai, 2/4 jama 1/4 barabar 3/4. Bas yahi qanoon hai. Jama ya ghatta karna ho to pehle denominators ko barabar karo, phir sirf upar wale numbers jama ya ghatta hote hain, neeche wala waisa hi rehta hai.

Denominator barabar karne ka matlab hai dono denominators ka wo mushtaraka number dhundna jo dono se pura taqseem ho jaye. Ye asal me agla topic, LCM, hi hai. Dekha, maths ke topic alag alag nahi hote, aik dusre se jure hote hain.

Guna aur taqseem ka concept

Guna me kahani badal jati hai. 1/2 guna 1/3 ka matlab hai, aadhe ka teesra hisa. Pehle aadhi roti lo, phir us aadhi ko teen barabar hison me baanto. Poori roti ke hisab se wo chhota sa tukra 1/6 hai. Isi liye guna me seedha qanoon chalta hai, upar wala upar wale se guna, neeche wala neeche wale se guna. Guna me denominator barabar karne ki koi zaroorat nahi.

Aur taqseem ka concept is se bhi pyara hai. 1/2 taqseem 1/4 ka matlab hai, aadhi roti me kitne chauthai hisay samate hain? Jawab hai do. Isi liye taqseem me dusri fraction ko ulta karke guna kar dete hain. Ulta karna koi jaadu nahi, ye "kitne hisay samate hain" wale sawal ka jawab nikalne ka tareeqa hai.

LCM kya hai, ghanti wali misaal

Ab LCM par ao. Do numbers lo, 4 aur 6. Har number ke zaeef, yani multiples, likho. 4 ke zaeef hain 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24. Aur 6 ke zaeef hain 6, 12, 18, 24. Dono safon me pehla mushtaraka number kon sa aya? 12. Bas yahi LCM hai, dono ke zaeef me sab se pehle milne wala mushtaraka number.

Ise ghanti se samjho to kabhi nahi bhoolo ge. School me do ghantiyan hain. Aik har 4 minute baad bajti hai, dusri har 6 minute baad. Agar dono abhi aik sath bajin, to dobara aik sath kab bajengi? Pehli 4, 8, 12 par bajegi, dusri 6, 12 par. Dono 12 minute par phir sath bajengi. Wahi 12 jo LCM tha. To jahan sawal me "aik sath", "dobara sath", "aik hi waqt par phir" jaisi baat aye, wahan LCM chhupa hota hai. Aur aik pehchan yaad rakho, LCM hamesha dono numbers ke barabar ya un se bara hota hai, kyunke waqt aage ki taraf jata hai.

HCF kya hai, ribbon wali misaal

HCF LCM ka ulta khayal hai. Yahan hum wo sab se bara number dhundte hain jo dono numbers ko pura pura taqseem kar de, kuch baqi na bache. 12 aur 18 lo. 12 ko pura baantne wale numbers hain 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12. Aur 18 ko baantne wale hain 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 18. Dono me mushtaraka hain 1, 2, 3, 6. In me sab se bara hai 6. Yahi HCF hai.

Ise ribbon se samajh lo. Tumhare paas do ribbons hain, aik 12 metre ka aur dusra 18 metre ka. Tum dono ko barabar tukron me kaatna chahte ho, aur koi ribbon zaya nahi hona chahiye. Sab se bare barabar tukre kitne lambe honge? 6 metre ke. Chhote tukre to bohat ban sakte hain, 3 ke bhi, 2 ke bhi, lekin sab se bare wala wahi hai jo HCF hai. To jahan sawal me "barabar hisay", "barabar tukre", "sab se bara hisa" aur "koi baqi na bache" aye, wahan HCF chhupa hota hai. Aur pehchan ye hai ke HCF hamesha dono numbers se chhota ya un ke barabar hota hai.

Farq aik line me zehen me bitha lo. Bajna aur sath milna LCM hai, baantna aur kaatna HCF hai. Aik aage ki taraf jata hai aur bara hota hai, dusra hisay karta hai aur chhota hota hai.

Decimal bhi fraction hi hai

Decimal ko alag cheez mat samjho. 0.5 ka matlab hai das hison me se paanch, yani 5/10, jo asaan kar ke 1/2 banta hai. Paison se yaad rakho, 50 paisa aadha rupiya hota hai. 0.25 matlab 25/100, yani 1/4. Aur 1.25 wohi hai jo hum kehte hain sawa rupiya, aik poora aur aik chauthai. Point ke baad pehli jagah dasven hison ki hai aur dusri jagah sauven hison ki. Bas itna samajh lo, decimal wala sawal paper me kabhi mushkil nahi hoga.

Umar ke sawal, x wala tareeqa

Ab umar ke sawal, jo har paper me aik do zaroor ate hain. In ka poora khayal sirf aik line hai. Jo umar na maloom ho, use x maan lo.

Agar abhi ki umar x hai, to 5 saal baad ki umar x + 5 hogi, kyunke umar barhti hai. Aur 5 saal pehle ki umar x − 5 hogi. Bas. Ab tareeqa suno. Pehla qadam, sab se chhoti ya bunyadi umar ko x mano. Dusra qadam, dusri umar ko x ki zuban me likho. Agar baap ki umar bete se teen guna hai aur beta x hai, to baap 3x hai. Teesra qadam, sawal ke jumle ko equation me badlo. Chaatha qadam, equation hal karo. Aur paanchva qadam, jo aksar log bhool jate hain, jawab ko asal jumle me rakh kar check karo ke baat banti hai ya nahi.

Aik baat ghanti ki tarah zehen me bandh lo. Waqt sab ke liye aik jaisa guzarta hai. Paanch saal guzren ge to baap ki umar me bhi paanch jama honge aur bete ki umar me bhi paanch. Waqt kisi aik par nahi guzarta.

Board par formula

Ustad jab board par is lesson ka khulasa likhta hai to kuch yun likhta hai. In a fraction, the number below the line is the denominator, the total equal parts. The number above the line is the numerator, the parts taken. To add or subtract fractions, first make the denominators equal, then add or subtract the numerators only. LCM is the smallest common multiple of two numbers. HCF is the largest common factor of two numbers. If the present age is x, then age after n years = x + n, and age n years ago = x − n.

Misaalein board se

Ab board par misaalein hal karte hain. Har misaal ke sath wajah bhi samjho, sirf jawab mat dekho.

1. 1/2 jama 1/4 kitna hua?
Pehle size aik karo. 1/2 ko 2/4 bana lo. Ab 2/4 jama 1/4 barabar 3/4.

2. 2/3 jama 1/6 kitna hua?
2/3 ko 4/6 bana lo, kyunke 6 dono ka mushtaraka denominator hai. Ab 4/6 jama 1/6 barabar 5/6.

3. 3/4 me se 1/2 ghattao.
1/2 ko 2/4 bana lo. Ab 3/4 me se 2/4 gaya to 1/4 reh gaya.

4. 2/3 guna 3/5 kitna hua?
Seedha guna karo. Upar 2 × 3 = 6, neeche 3 × 5 = 15. Jawab 6/15, jo asaan kar ke 2/5 banta hai. Jawab ko hamesha sab se asaan shakal me likho.

5. 6 aur 8 ka LCM nikalo.
6 ke zaeef hain 6, 12, 18, 24. Aur 8 ke zaeef hain 8, 16, 24. Pehla mushtaraka 24 hai. LCM barabar 24.

6. Do ghantiyan har 4 minute aur har 6 minute baad bajti hain. Agar dono abhi sath bajin to dobara sath kab bajengi?
Ye sath bajne wala sawal hai, yani LCM. 4 aur 6 ka LCM 12 hai. To 12 minute baad dono phir sath bajengi.

7. 16 aur 24 ka HCF nikalo.
16 ko baantne wale hain 1, 2, 4, 8, 16. Aur 24 ko baantne wale hain 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24. Dono me sab se bara mushtaraka 8 hai. HCF barabar 8.

8. Aik ribbon 12 metre ka hai aur dusra 20 metre ka. Dono ko barabar tukron me kaatna hai, kuch zaya na ho. Sab se bare tukre kitne lambe honge aur kul kitne tukre banenge?
Baantne wala sawal hai, yani HCF. 12 aur 20 ka HCF 4 hai. To sab se bare tukre 4 metre ke honge. Pehle ribbon se 3 tukre aur dusre se 5 tukre, kul 8 tukre.

9. Aik baap ki umar apne bete ki umar se teen guna hai. Dono ki umron ka jama 48 hai. Dono ki umar nikalo.
Bete ki umar x mano, to baap ki 3x. Jama wali baat equation bani, x + 3x = 48, yani 4x = 48, to x = 12. Beta 12 saal ka hai aur baap 36 saal ka. Check karo, 12 ka teen guna 36, aur jama 48, baat mil gayi.

10. Aik maa ki umar apni beti ki umar se teen guna hai. Paanch saal pehle maa ki umar beti ki umar se chaar guna thi. Dono ki mojooda umar nikalo.
Beti ki umar x, maa ki 3x. Paanch saal pehle dono ki umar me paanch kam honge, maa 3x − 5 aur beti x − 5. Jumla equation bana, 3x − 5 = 4 guna (x − 5). Hal karo, 3x − 5 = 4x − 20, to x = 15. Beti 15 ki hai aur maa 45 ki. Check karo, paanch saal pehle maa 40 ki aur beti 10 ki thi, aur 40 waqai 10 ka chaar guna hai.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Ghar ke hisaab me fractions har roz chalte hain. Aata, cheeni, doodh ke hisay, zameen ki taqseem, ye sab fractions hi hain. LCM waqt ke sath wale sawalon me ata hai aur HCF baantne wale sawalon me. Aur umar ke sawal to paper ki pehchan hain. Ab paper wale andaaz ke do sawal dekho.

1. Do ghantiyan har 5 minute aur har 7 minute baad bajti hain. Sath bajne ke baad dobara sath kab bajengi?
Hal zehen me karo. 5 aur 7 ka koi mushtaraka hissa nahi, to LCM dono ka guna hota hai, 35. Dono 35 minute baad phir sath bajengi. Ye chhoti si baat bhi yaad rakho, jab dono numbers me koi mushtaraka ajza na hon to LCM un ka guna hi hota hai.

2. Aik aadmi ki umar us ke bete ki umar se paanch guna hai aur dono ki umron ka jama 36 hai. Bete ki umar kya hai?
Beta x, aadmi 5x. Jama 6x = 36, to x = 6. Beta 6 saal ka hai.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab wo trick jo is poore lesson ko aik jumle me bandh degi. Bajna LCM, baantna HCF. Jab sawal me ghanti baje, sath mile, dobara aik ho, to LCM. Jab sawal me kaate, baante, barabar hisay kare, to HCF. Aur umar ke liye aik alag line, ghari sab ke liye aik. Waqt guzrega to har aik ki umar me utna hi badlega. Aur fraction ke jama ke liye, pehle size aik, phir jama. Teen chhote jumle, poora lesson in me band hai.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ab wo ghaltiyan dekho jo Pakistani students paper me karte hain. Main board par ghalat tareeqa aur sahi tareeqa sath sath likh raha hun, taake farq hamesha yaad rahe.

Ghalat: 1/2 jama 1/3 barabar 2/5 kar diya, upar wale jama kiye aur neeche wale bhi jama kar diye.
Sahi: Pehle denominators barabar karo. 1/2 barabar 3/6 aur 1/3 barabar 2/6, to jama 5/6 hua.
Wajah: Dono fractions ke hison ka size mukhtalif tha. Size aik kiye baghair jama karna aise hai jese bare tukre aur chhote tukre gin kar aik keh do.

Ghalat: Ghantiyon wale sawal me HCF nikaal liya.
Sahi: Sath bajne wale sawal me LCM nikalta hai.
Wajah: Jawab ko waqt me aage jana hai, is liye wo dono numbers se bara ya un ke barabar hoga. HCF hamesha chhota hota hai, wo yahan ho hi nahi sakta.

Ghalat: Umar wale sawal me paanch saal baad ki surat me sirf baap ki umar me paanch jama kiye, bete ki umar wohi rakhi.
Sahi: Paanch saal baad dono ki umar me paanch jama honge.
Wajah: Waqt sab ke liye aik jaisa guzarta hai. Ghari sab ke liye aik hai.

Ghalat: 0.5 ko 1/5 parh liya.
Sahi: 0.5 barabar 5/10, yani 1/2 hai.
Wajah: Decimal me point ke baad pehli jagah dasven hison ki hoti hai. 0.5 matlab das me se paanch, aadha.

Ghalat: Fraction ke guna me bhi pehle denominators barabar karne baith gaye.
Sahi: Guna me seedha upar se upar aur neeche se neeche guna hota hai.
Wajah: Denominators barabar karna sirf jama aur ghatta ki shart hai. Guna ka matlab hi hisa ka hisa lena hai.

Jawab aur un ki wajah

1. Jawab B hai, 5/6. Pehle size aik kiya, 3/6 jama 2/6 barabar 5/6. 2/5 wala option hi paper ka phanda tha.
2. Jawab A hai, 1/2. 1/3 ko 2/6 banaya, 5/6 me se 2/6 gaya to 3/6 bacha, jo asaan karke 1/2 hai.
3. Jawab C hai, 1/2. Upar 3 × 2 = 6, neeche 4 × 3 = 12, aur 6/12 asaan karke 1/2.
4. Jawab A hai, 20. 4 ke zaeef 4, 8, 12, 16, 20 aur 10 ke zaeef 10, 20. Pehla mushtaraka 20 hai. 40 bhi mushtaraka hai lekin wo pehla nahi, LCM hamesha sab se pehla, yani sab se chhota mushtaraka hota hai.
5. Jawab D hai, 9. 18 ko baantne wale 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 18 aur 27 ko baantne wale 1, 3, 9, 27. Sab se bara mushtaraka 9 hai.
6. Jawab C hai, 24 minute. Sath bajna yani LCM. 6 aur 8 ka LCM 24 hai.
7. Jawab D hai, 3/4. 0.75 matlab 75/100, jo asaan karke 3/4 banta hai. Teen chauthai, yani paune wala hisaab.
8. Jawab C hai, 36 saal. Teen guna ka matlab hai 12 × 3 = 36.
9. Jawab B hai, x − 8. Pehle ki umar me waqt minus hota hai, jama nahi.
10. Jawab C hai, 10 saal. Beta x aur baap 5x, jama 6x = 60, to x = 10. Baap 50 ka hai, check kar lo, 50 waqai 10 ka paanch guna hai.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye do tasveerein bohat kaam ki hongi. Pehli tasveer roti ke hison ki ho. Teen gol rotian aik saf me dikhayi jayen. Pehli do barabar hison me kati ho aur aik hisa rang se numayan ho. Dusri chaar hison me kati ho aur do hisay usi rang me hon. Teesri chhe hison me kati ho aur teen hisay rang me hon. Neeche likha ho ke 1/2 barabar 2/4 barabar 3/6. Student aik nazar me samajh jaye ke tukre chhote hue, roti utni hi rahi.

Doosri tasveer ghanti aur umar dono ko jode. Upar do ghantiyan aik time line ke upar dikhayi jayen, aik ke neeche 4, 8, 12 ke nishan aur dusri ke neeche 6, 12 ke nishan, aur 12 par dono ke nishan aik bare ghere me hon. Neeche aik baap aur bete ki umar wali line ho jis par teen nuqte hon, x − 5, x aur x + 5, aur teeron se dikhaya jaye ke waqt dono ke liye aik jaisa aage peeche hota hai.
