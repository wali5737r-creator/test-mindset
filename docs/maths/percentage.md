Feesad — Percentage
Har 100 par kitna. Bazaar ki discount se paper ke number tak

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me maths ke hisse me feesad yani percentage ka sawal na aye, aisa kam hi hota hai. Kabhi result ka sawal ata hai, kabhi qeemat barhne ghatne ka, kabhi abadi ka, kabhi discount ka. Aur khoobi ye hai ke ye saare sawal aik hi chhoti si samajh par khade hain. Jo student feesad ka asal matlab pakar le, wo in me se koi sawal nahi chhorta. Lekin jo student sirf formula ratta hai, wo ghumaye hue sawal me phans jata hai, kyunke paper wala formula nahi parkhta, samajh parkhta hai. Is lesson me hum pehle matlab samjhen ge, formula khud baad me apni jagah par ajayega.

Pehle feesad ko samajh lo

Dekho, feesad koi ajeeb si cheez nahi hai. Tum roz is se milte ho. Bazaar jao to board laga hota hai, 20 feesad discount. Is ka matlab kya hai? Matlab ye ke har 100 rupay ki qeemat par 20 rupay kam. Agar qameez 1000 ki hai to socho, is me das hisse hain 100 wale. Har hisse par 20 kam, to kul 200 kam. Dene kitne pare? 800. Bas, feesad yahi hai. Har 100 par kitna, is ka naam feesad hai. Feesad ka lafz hi ye kehta hai, fi sad, yani har sau par.

Ab test wali taraf ao. Tum ne 100 number ke paper me 60 liye. Kehte hain 60 feesad. Kyun? Kyunke kul 100 tha aur tum ne 60 liye, yani har 100 par 60. Ab socho agar paper 50 number ka ho aur tum 30 lo, to kya ye 30 feesad hai? Nahi. Yahan kul 100 nahi hai, 50 hai. 50 me 30 lena waisa hi hai jese 100 me 60 lena, kyunke dono me tum ne kul ka 60 feesad hissa liya. Yehi wo pehli samajh hai jo aadhe sawal hal kar deti hai. Feesad hamesha kul ke muqable me dekha jata hai, aur kul ko zehen me 100 bana kar socha jata hai.

Ab hisaab kese banta hai

Ab hisaab ki baat karte hain. Teen tarah ke sawal bante hain aur teeno isi aik samajh se niklen ge.

Pehla sawal: kisi number ka itne feesad kitna hota hai? Jese 200 ka 25 feesad. Matlab, 200 ko 100 brabar hisson me banto, har hissa 2 ka hua, aur un me se 25 hisse lo. 25 guna 2 barabar 50. Ya dusri tarah socho, 25 feesad matlab kul ka chautha hissa, aur 200 ka chautha hissa 50 hai. Dono raaste aik hi jagah jate hain.

Dusra sawal ulta hai: aik number dusre ka kitne feesad hai? Jese 45, 60 ka kitne feesad hai? Yahan hissa 45 hai aur kul 60 hai. Hisaab yun banta hai, pehle dekho 45 kul ka kitna hissa hai, 45 taqseem 60 barabar 0.75. Phir ise 100 par le jao, 0.75 guna 100 barabar 75. Jawab, 75 feesad. Jab bhi sawal puche ke itna, itne ka kitne feesad hai, to chhote ko bare se taqseem karo aur 100 se guna kar do.

Teesra sawal barhne ghatne wala hai, aur yahin sab se zyada ghalti hoti hai. Qanoon zehen me bitha lo: feesad izafa ya kami hamesha asal yani purani qeemat par nikala jata hai, nayi par kabhi nahi. Tankhwah 40,000 se 44,000 ho gayi. Izafa 4,000 hai. Ab ye 4,000 kis par nikle ga? Purani tankhwah 40,000 par. 4,000 taqseem 40,000 guna 100 barabar 10 feesad. Agar koi 44,000 par nikal le to jawab ghalat ajayega, kyunke izafa hua hi purani par tha.

Mashhoor fractions aur un ke feesad

Ab aik cheez jo tez students ka raaz hai. Kuch fractions roz ke feesad ban kar ate hain. Inhen yaad kar lo to aadha hisaab zehen me ho jayega. Aadha yani 1/2 barabar 50 feesad. Chautha hissa yani 1/4 barabar 25 feesad. Teen chauthai yani 3/4 barabar 75 feesad. Paanchwa hissa yani 1/5 barabar 20 feesad. Aathwa hissa yani 1/8 barabar 12.5 feesad. Daswa hissa yani 1/10 barabar 10 feesad. Aur aik mashhoor aur hai, 1/3 barabar 33.33 feesad, taqreeban aik teesra hissa. Ab dekho is ka kamaal. 480 ka 12.5 feesad nikalna hai. Formula lagao ge to waqt lagega. Lekin tum jante ho 12.5 feesad matlab 1/8. 480 ka aathwa hissa 60 hai. Jawab tayyar. Paper me waqt hi to bachana hota hai.

Board par formula

Ab ustad board par formula likhta hai, lekin gaur karo, formula wohi hai jo tum pehle se samajh chuke ho. Kisi number ka x feesad nikalna ho to number ko x se guna karo aur 100 se taqseem kar do. Feesad maloom karna ho to hissa ko kul se taqseem karo aur 100 se guna kar do. Feesad izafa maloom karna ho to izafe ko asal qeemat se taqseem karo aur 100 se guna kar do. Aur kami ka bhi yahi tareeqa hai, kami ko asal qeemat se taqseem karo. Angrezi me board par yun likha hota hai: Percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100. Aur x percent of a number = Number × x ÷ 100. Bas, is se zyada is lesson ka koi formula nahi hai.

Misaalein board se

Ab board par misaalein hal karte hain. Pehle seedhi, phir ahista ahista ghumayi hui. Har misaal ke steps gaur se parho, sirf jawab mat dekho.

1. 200 ka 25 feesad kitna hai?
25 feesad matlab chautha hissa. 200 ka chautha hissa 50. Jawab 50.

2. 480 ka 12.5 feesad kitna hai?
12.5 feesad matlab 1/8. 480 ko 8 se taqseem karo, 60. Jawab 60. Dekha, fraction wali samajh ne formula ko hara diya.

3. Ahmed ne 60 number ke test me 45 liye. Us ke feesad number kitne hain?
Hissa 45, kul 60. 45 taqseem 60 barabar 0.75, guna 100 barabar 75. Jawab 75 feesad.

4. Aik shirt 800 rupay ki hai aur us par 15 feesad discount hai. Kitne dene parein ge?
Pehle discount nikalo. 800 ka 15 feesad barabar 120. Ab 800 me se 120 ghatayo, 680. Jawab 680 rupay dene parein ge.

5. Aik mulazim ki tankhwah 40,000 hai. 10 feesad izafa hua. Nayi tankhwah kitni hai?
40,000 ka 10 feesad barabar 4,000. 40,000 jama 4,000 barabar 44,000. Jawab 44,000.

6. Aik mobile ki qeemat 500 se ghat kar 450 ho gayi. Kitne feesad kami hui?
Kami 50 hai aur wo asal qeemat 500 par nikle gi. 50 taqseem 500 guna 100 barabar 10. Jawab 10 feesad kami.

7. Aik gaon ki abadi 20,000 hai. Har saal 5 feesad barhti hai. Aik saal baad abadi kitni hogi?
20,000 ka 5 feesad barabar 1,000. 20,000 jama 1,000 barabar 21,000. Jawab 21,000.

8. Aik cheez ki qeemat pehle 20 feesad barhi, phir 20 feesad ghat gayi. Asal qeemat 100 thi. Aakhri qeemat kitni hai?
Gaur karo, ye paper ka pasandeeda jaal hai. Pehle 100 barh kar 120 hua. Phir kami 120 par nikli, nayi asal ab 120 hai. 120 ka 20 feesad barabar 24. 120 manfi 24 barabar 96. Jawab 96, yani kul 4 feesad kami. Barabar feesad upar neeche kabhi barabar nahi hota, kyunke dusri dafa hisaab nayi qeemat par banta hai.

9. Aik class me 50 students hain. 70 feesad pass hue. Kitne fail hue?
Pass 50 ka 70 feesad barabar 35. Fail 50 manfi 35 barabar 15. Aur feesad me fail 30 feesad hain. Jawab 15 students fail.

10. Kis number ka 30 feesad 90 hai?
Ye ulta sawal hai. Socho, 30 feesad barabar 90, to 10 feesad barabar 30, aur 100 feesad yani pura number barabar 300. Jawab 300.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Roz marra zindagi me feesad har jagah hai. Bazaar ki sale, bank ki peshkash, bijli ke bill par surcharge, aur tumhare apne result card par. Lekin paper me is ki teen shaklain sab se zyada ati hain. Aik result wali, kitne pass kitne fail. Dusri qeemat wali, pehle barhna phir ghatna. Teesri abadi aur tankhwah wali, salana izafa. Ab paper wale andaaz ke do sawal dekho.

1. In a class of 80 students, 45 percent are girls. How many boys are there? Ladkiyan 80 ka 45 feesad barabar 36, ladke 80 manfi 36 barabar 44.
2. The price of sugar increased by 25 percent. By how much is it now if it was 160 rupees per kg? Izafa 160 ka 25 feesad barabar 40, nayi qeemat 200 rupay.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab wo trick jo hisaab ko tez kar degi. Isay hum dus feesad wala jaadu kehte hain. Kisi bhi number ka 10 feesad nikalna ho to bas dashariya ko aik ghar peeche kar do. 350 ka 10 feesad 35, khatam. 1 feesad nikalna ho to dashariya do ghar peeche. Ab koi bhi feesad in tukron se ban jata hai. 15 feesad chahiye to 10 feesad aur us ka aadha jo 5 feesad hai, jod do. 350 ka 15 feesad barabar 35 jama 17.5 barabar 52.5. Na calculator, na lambi guna.

Aur aik dusri chaal bhi yaad rakho, ulta feesad wali. X feesad of Y hamesha Y feesad of X ke barabar hota hai. 8 feesad of 50 mushkil lagta hai? Ulta kar do, 50 feesad of 8 barabar 4. Jawab wohi hai, hisaab asaan ho gaya. Paper me jab number ajeeb lage to use ulta kar ke dekho.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ab wo ghaltiyan dekho jin par students number kaatte hain. Main ghalat tareeqa aur sahi tareeqa sath sath likh raha hun.

Ghalat: Qeemat 80 se 100 ho gayi, izafa 20 hai, aur men ne 100 par nikal kar kaha izafa 20 feesad hai.
Sahi: Izafa 25 feesad hai.
Wajah: Izafa hamesha asal yani purani qeemat par nikalta hai. 20 taqseem 80 guna 100 barabar 25. Nayi qeemat par nikalna sab se aam ghalti hai.

Ghalat: Pehle 10 feesad qeemat barhi, phir 10 feesad kam hui, to koi farq nahi para.
Sahi: Kul 1 feesad kami hui.
Wajah: 100 barh kar 110 hua, phir kami 110 par nikli jo 11 hai. 110 manfi 11 barabar 99. Dusri dafa ka hisaab nayi qeemat par banta hai, is liye farq parta hai.

Ghalat: Aik cheez par pehle 20 feesad discount, phir mazeed 10 feesad discount, to kul discount 30 feesad hua.
Sahi: Kul discount 28 feesad hai.
Wajah: 100 par pehla discount 20, bacha 80. Dusra discount 80 par nikla, 8. Kul kami 28, na ke 30. Discount kabhi jode nahi jate, aik ke baad dusra nayi qeemat par lagta hai.

Ghalat: 1/3 ko 33 feesad likh diya aur hisaab usi se kar diya.
Sahi: 1/3 barabar 33.33 feesad hota hai.
Wajah: Farq chhota hai lekin baray numberon par jawab badal deta hai. Jahan sawal me 1/3 ka ishara ho wahan fraction se hi hisaab karo.

Ghalat: 0.05 ko 50 feesad samajh liya.
Sahi: 0.05 barabar 5 feesad hai, aur 0.5 barabar 50 feesad.
Wajah: Dashariya ko feesad me badalna ho to 100 se guna karo. 0.05 guna 100 barabar 5. Aik ghar ki ghalti pure jawab ko kha jati hai.

Jawab aur un ki wajah

1. Jawab B hai, 60. 300 ka paanchwa hissa 60 hai aur 20 feesad matlab 1/5. Ya 300 guna 20 taqseem 100 barabar 60.
2. Jawab C hai, 12.5 feesad. 1/8 matlab 100 ko 8 se taqseem karo, 12.5. Ye fraction wali list yaad rakhna, paper me bohat kaam ati hai.
3. Jawab C hai, 85 feesad. 68 taqseem 80 barabar 0.85, guna 100 barabar 85.
4. Jawab A hai, 2250. Discount 2500 ka 10 feesad barabar 250. 2500 manfi 250 barabar 2250.
5. Jawab C hai, 500. Ulta hisaab karo. 40 feesad barabar 200, to 10 feesad barabar 50, aur pura number yani 100 feesad barabar 500.
6. Jawab B hai, 10 feesad. Izafa 5,000 hai aur wo asal abadi 50,000 par nikle ga. 5,000 taqseem 50,000 guna 100 barabar 10.
7. Jawab B hai, 1 feesad kami. 100 barh kar 110, phir 110 ka 10 feesad yani 11 kam hua, bacha 99. Barabar upar neeche kabhi barabar nahi hota.
8. Jawab B hai, 40. 6.25 feesad matlab 1/16. 640 ko 16 se taqseem karo, 40. Ye bhi fraction wali chaal hai.
9. Jawab C hai, 90. 150 ka 60 feesad barabar 150 guna 60 taqseem 100 barabar 90.
10. Jawab A hai, 100. Pehle 100 barh kar 125 hua. Phir 125 par 20 feesad discount barabar 25. 125 manfi 25 barabar 100. Dekho, jawab asal par wapas aya lekin wajah feesadon ka farq hai, barabari nahi.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye do tasveerein bohat kaam ki hongi. Pehli tasveer bazaar wali ho. Aik dukaan ki tasveer jisme qameez par bara sa tag laga ho, 1000 rupay par kaat kar 800 likha ho, aur sath 20 feesad discount ka board ho. Neeche aik lakeer me dikhaya ho ke har 100 par 20 kam, das 100 par 200 kam. Student bazaar dekh kar hi feesad ka matlab kabhi nahi bhulega.

Doosri tasveer 100 khano wali jaali ho, yani aik bara sa murabba jisme 100 chhote khane bane hon. Kuch khane rang se bhare hon, jese 25 khane bhare hon to sath likha ho 25 feesad. Is aik tasveer se student ko nazar ajayega ke feesad asal me 100 me se hissa ginne ka naam hai. Jab bhi feesad ka sawal aye, zehen me yehi jaali ghume gi aur kul ko 100 samajhna asaan ho jayega.
