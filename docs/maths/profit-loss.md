Nafa Nuqsan — Profit and Loss
Dukandaar ki zabaan me hisaab. Lagat, farokht, aur wo jaal jo har paper me ata hai

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me nafa nuqsan ka sawal taqreeban pakka hota hai. Kabhi seedha sawal ata hai ke itne me khareeda, itne me becha, nafa kitna. Kabhi ghumaya hua ata hai, discount ke sath, ya do cheezon ke sath. Is topic ki khoobi ye hai ke is ka hisaab mushkil nahi hai, lekin is ka aik usool hai jo agar zehen me baith jaye to saare sawal aik jaise lagne lagte hain. Aur wo usool ye hai ke nafa aur nuqsan hamesha lagat par nikalta hai. Aadhe se zyada students yahin phanste hain, kyunke wo farokht ki qeemat par nikal lete hain. Is lesson me hum dukandaar ban kar sochen ge, kyunke ye topic dukaan ka hai, kitab ka nahi.

Pehle dukaan ko samajh lo

Dekho, har dukandaar do qeematon ke darmiyan jeeta hai. Aik wo qeemat jis par wo cheez khareed kar lata hai. Use kehte hain lagat, angrezi me cost price. Dusri wo qeemat jis par wo cheez bechta hai. Use kehte hain qeemat-e-farokht, angrezi me selling price. Ab socho, dukandaar ne 100 rupay ki cheez li aur 120 me bech di. Us ki jeb se 100 nikle aur 120 wapas aye. 20 zyada aye, ye us ka nafa hai. Agar 80 me bechta to 20 kam aye, ye nuqsan hai. Bas, itni si baat hai. Nafa matlab farokht lagat se zyada, nuqsan matlab farokht lagat se kam.

Ab wo aham sawal jo is pure topic ki jaan hai. Nafa 20 rupay hai, lekin feesad me kitna? Dukandaar se poochho, wo kya kahega? Wo kahega, men ne 100 lagaye the aur 20 kamaye. Yani wo apne lagaye hue paiso se naapta hai. 20 taqseem 100 barabar 20 feesad nafa. Yahi usool hai aur yahi paper ka sab se bara jaal hai. Nafa aur nuqsan ka feesad hamesha lagat par nikalta hai, kyunke naap hamesha us raqam se hoti hai jo pehle lagi thi. Farokht wali qeemat to natija hai, naap nahi.

Ab discount wali baat bhi yahin samajh lo, kyunke yehi doosra jaal hai. Dukano par tum ne dekha hoga, cheez par aik qeemat likhi hoti hai aur us par kaat kar kam qeemat di jati hai. Likhi hui qeemat ko kehte hain marked price. Us par jo kami di jati hai use kehte hain discount, urdu me riyayat. Aur discount hamesha likhi hui qeemat par nikalta hai, lagat par nahi. To do usool zehen me bandh lo. Nafa nuqsan lagat par, discount likhi qeemat par. In do ko uljha diya to sawal gaya.

Do cheezon wala mashhoor jaal

Ab aik kahani suno jo paper me bar bar ati hai. Aik admi ne do ghariyan bechín, har aik 600 rupay me. Aik par use 20 feesad nafa hua, dusri par 20 feesad nuqsan. Dil kehta hai ke nafa nuqsan brabar kat gaya, na kuch mila na gaya. Lekin hisaab kuch aur kehta hai. Chalo gin kar dekhte hain.

Pehli ghari par 20 feesad nafa hua aur wo 600 me biki. Matlab farokht lagat ka 120 feesad hai. To lagat hui 600 taqseem 1.20 barabar 500. Dusri ghari par 20 feesad nuqsan hua aur wo bhi 600 me biki. Matlab farokht lagat ka 80 feesad hai. To lagat hui 600 taqseem 0.80 barabar 750. Ab kul lagat dekho, 500 jama 750 barabar 1250. Aur kul farokht, 600 jama 600 barabar 1200. 50 rupay ka nuqsan ho gaya. Wajah samjho, nuqsan hamesha bari lagat wali cheez par hota hai aur nafa chhoti lagat wali par. Feesad brabar hon tab bhi raqam brabar nahi hoti. Ye jaal pehchan lo, paper me ye sawal pehchan se hi hal hota hai.

Board par formula

Ab ustad board par formula likhta hai, aur tum dekho ge ke ye wohi baatein hain jo tum dukaan wali zabaan me pehle se samajh chuke ho. Nafa barabar farokht manfi lagat. Nuqsan barabar lagat manfi farokht. Nafa feesad barabar nafa taqseem lagat guna 100. Nuqsan feesad barabar nuqsan taqseem lagat guna 100. Agar lagat aur nafa feesad maloom ho to farokht barabar lagat guna (100 jama nafa feesad) taqseem 100. Nuqsan ho to 100 manfi nuqsan feesad ayega. Discount barabar likhi qeemat manfi farokht, aur discount feesad hamesha likhi qeemat par nikalta hai. Angrezi me board par yun likha hota hai: Profit = SP − CP, Loss = CP − SP, Profit percent = Profit ÷ CP × 100. Bas, is topic ke saare formula inhi chand lakeeron me band hain.

Misaalein board se

Ab board par misaalein hal karte hain. Pehle seedhi, phir ahista ahista ghumayi hui. Har misaal me pehle ye dekho ke lagat kya hai, kyunke wahi is topic ka raja hai.

1. Aik dukandaar ne cheez 500 me khareedi aur 600 me bech di. Nafa aur nafa feesad kitna hai?
Nafa barabar 600 manfi 500 barabar 100. Feesad lagat par, 100 taqseem 500 guna 100 barabar 20. Jawab 100 rupay nafa, 20 feesad.

2. Aik cheez 800 me khareed kar 680 me bechi gayi. Nuqsan feesad kitna hai?
Nuqsan barabar 800 manfi 680 barabar 120. Feesad lagat par, 120 taqseem 800 guna 100 barabar 15. Jawab 15 feesad nuqsan.

3. Lagat 400 hai. 25 feesad nafe par bechna hai. Farokht qeemat kya hogi?
Nafa 400 ka 25 feesad barabar 100. Farokht barabar 400 jama 100 barabar 500. Jawab 500 rupay.

4. Aik cheez 900 me bechi gayi aur 10 feesad nuqsan hua. Lagat kitni thi?
Socho, nuqsan 10 feesad hai yani farokht lagat ka 90 feesad hai. To lagat barabar 900 taqseem 0.90 barabar 1000. Jawab 1000 rupay. Ye ulta sawal hai, paper me bohat ata hai.

5. Aik jacket par 2000 rupay likhe hain aur 15 feesad discount mil raha hai. Kitne dene parein ge?
Discount likhi qeemat par, 2000 ka 15 feesad barabar 300. Dene parein ge 2000 manfi 300 barabar 1700. Jawab 1700 rupay.

6. Aik cheez ki likhi qeemat 1500 hai. 20 feesad discount ke baad bechi gayi. Lagat 1000 thi. Nafa feesad kitna hua?
Pehle farokht nikalo. Discount 1500 ka 20 feesad barabar 300, farokht 1200. Phir nafa, 1200 manfi 1000 barabar 200. Feesad lagat par, 200 taqseem 1000 guna 100 barabar 20. Jawab 20 feesad nafa.

7. Do ghariyan har aik 600 rupay me bechi gayin. Aik par 20 feesad nafa, dusri par 20 feesad nuqsan. Kul natija kya hai?
Ye wohi jaal hai jo hum ne kahani me samjhaya. Pehli ki lagat 500, dusri ki lagat 750. Kul lagat 1250, kul farokht 1200. Jawab 50 rupay nuqsan, jo lagat ka 4 feesad hai.

8. Aik dukandaar cheez ki lagat 500 hai. Wo lagat par 20 feesad barha kar qeemat likhta hai, phir grahak ko 10 feesad discount deta hai. Use kitna nafa hua?
Likhi qeemat barabar 500 jama 100 barabar 600. Discount 600 ka 10 feesad barabar 60. Farokht barabar 540. Nafa barabar 40, aur feesad me 40 taqseem 500 guna 100 barabar 8. Jawab 8 feesad nafa. Dekho, discount dene ke bawajood nafa bacha, kyunke likhi qeemat pehle hi barhai gayi thi.

9. Aik admi ne cycle 1200 me khareedi, 200 rupay marammat par lagaye, phir 1540 me bech di. Nafa feesad kitna hai?
Yahan jaal ye hai ke marammat bhi lagat hai. Asal lagat barabar 1200 jama 200 barabar 1400. Nafa barabar 1540 manfi 1400 barabar 140, aur feesad 140 taqseem 1400 guna 100 barabar 10. Jawab 10 feesad nafa.

10. Aik phal wale ne 40 kilo aam 50 rupay kilo ke hisaab se khareede. 10 kilo kharab ho gaye. Baqi 30 kilo us ne 80 rupay kilo beche. Natija kya hua?
Lagat barabar 40 guna 50 barabar 2000. Farokht barabar 30 guna 80 barabar 2400. Nafa barabar 400, feesad 400 taqseem 2000 guna 100 barabar 20. Jawab 20 feesad nafa. Kharab hisse ki lagat bhi tumhari hi jeb se gayi thi, is liye lagat pure 40 kilo ki ginni.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Roz marra me ye hisaab har dukaan, har sale aur har online offer me chhupa hota hai. Jab board par 50 feesad off likha dekho to ab tum jante ho ke ye likhi qeemat par hai, aur asal sawal ye hai ke likhi qeemat barhai to nahi gayi thi. Paper me is ki teen shaklain sab se zyada ati hain. Aik seedhi lagat farokht wali. Dusri discount ke sath uljhi hui. Teesri do cheezon wali, jisme nafa nuqsan brabar lagta hai lekin hota nahi. Ab paper wale andaaz ke do sawal dekho.

1. A shopkeeper buys an article for 750 rupees and sells it for 900 rupees. Find his profit percent. Nafa 150, lagat par nikalo, 150 taqseem 750 guna 100 barabar 20 feesad.
2. An article is sold at 10 percent loss for 720 rupees. What was its cost price? Farokht lagat ka 90 feesad hai, lagat barabar 720 taqseem 0.90 barabar 800.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Is topic ki trick aik sawal hai jo tum har sawal me pehle khud se poochho ge: feesad kis par nikal raha hai? Jawab hamesha tay hai. Nafa nuqsan ho to lagat par. Discount ho to likhi qeemat par. Bas ye aik sawal aadhi ghaltiyan wahi rok dega jahan hoti hain.

Aur dusri chaal, 100 wali chaal. Jab number baray aur ajeeb hon to zehen me lagat ko 100 maan lo. 100 lagaye, 20 feesad nafa yani 120 me biki. Ab tanasub se asal number par ajayo. Baray numberon ka dar khatam ho jata hai aur hisaab chhota ho jata hai. Aik aur nishani yaad rakho: jahan do cheezain aik hi qeemat par biki dikhen aur nafa nuqsan ke feesad brabar hon, wahan jawab hamesha nuqsan hota hai. Gin ne ki zaroorat nahi, pehchan hi kaafi hai.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ab wo ghaltiyan dekho jin par students number kaatte hain. Main ghalat tareeqa aur sahi tareeqa sath sath likh raha hun.

Ghalat: Lagat 100, farokht 120, nafa 20 hai. Men ne nafa feesad farokht par nikal kar 16.67 feesad likh diya.
Sahi: Nafa 20 feesad hai.
Wajah: Nafa hamesha lagat par nikalta hai. 20 taqseem 100 guna 100 barabar 20. Farokht par nikalna is topic ki sab se aam ghalti hai.

Ghalat: Discount lagat par nikal liya.
Sahi: Discount hamesha likhi hui qeemat par nikalta hai.
Wajah: Discount grahak ko dikhayi gayi qeemat par milta hai, dukandaar ki lagat se grahak ko koi matlab nahi. Dono ke adday alag hain, inhen milana sawal ko bigaar deta hai.

Ghalat: Do cheezain aik qeemat par bechín, aik par 20 feesad nafa aur dusri par 20 feesad nuqsan, to na nafa hua na nuqsan.
Sahi: Hamesha nuqsan hota hai.
Wajah: Nuqsan wali cheez ki lagat zyada hoti hai aur nafe wali ki kam. Feesad brabar hon tab bhi raqam brabar nahi hoti. Upar wali misaal me 50 rupay ka nuqsan gin kar dikhaya gaya tha.

Ghalat: Cycle 1200 me li, 200 marammat ke lagaye, 1540 me bechi, aur nafa 1540 manfi 1200 barabar 340 gin liya.
Sahi: Nafa 140 hai.
Wajah: Marammat aur le jane ka kharcha bhi lagat me shamil hota hai. Asal lagat 1400 hai. Jo paisa cheez ko bechne ke qabil banane me laga, wo lagat hai.

Ghalat: Nuqsan feesad farokht par nikal liya ke chalo nafa lagat par tha to nuqsan farokht par hoga.
Sahi: Nuqsan feesad bhi lagat par hi nikalta hai.
Wajah: Dono ka adda aik hi hai, lagat. Nafa ho ya nuqsan, naap hamesha us raqam se hoti hai jo pehle lagi thi.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye do tasveerein bohat kaam ki hongi. Pehli tasveer aik dukaan ke counter ki ho. Counter par aik cheez rakhi ho, us ke neeche do tag lage hon. Aik tag par likha ho lagat 100, dusre par farokht 120, aur darmiyan me teer bana kar upar likha ho nafa 20, aur ye nafa hamesha lagat wale tag ki taraf ishara kar raha ho. Student ye tasveer dekh kar kabhi nahi bhulega ke feesad kis par nikalta hai.

Doosri tasveer do ghariyon wali ho. Do brabar ghariyan aik hi 600 wale tag ke sath dikhayi jayen. Pehli ke neeche chhoti lagat 500 aur nafe ka sabz nishan ho, dusri ke neeche bari lagat 750 aur nuqsan ka surkh nishan ho, aur aakhir me kul khata 50 nuqsan par band ho raha ho. Ye wohi jaal hai jo paper me sab se zyada ata hai. Aik nazar dekhne se student ki ankhon me ye baat baith jayegi ke brabar feesad ka matlab brabar raqam nahi hota.
