Lesson: Nisbat aur Hissa — Ratio, Proportion aur Partnership
Nisbat aur Hissa. Mithai baantne se partnership ke munafe tak, aik hi concept

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me nisbat, yani ratio, aur partnership ke sawal har saal ate hain. Kabhi do logo ki tankhwah ka ratio de kar asal raqam poochi jati hai, kabhi zameen ya jaidad ki taqseem, kabhi do sharikon ke munafe ka hissa. Ye sawal un students ko phansate hain jo ratio ko seedhi raqam samajh bethte hain. Aur partnership ka sawal to aadhe paper me is liye kat-ta hai ke log waqt ko bhool jate hain, sirf paisa dekhte hain. Is lesson me me tumhe ratio ka woh concept dunga jo mithai baantne se shuru hota hai aur partnership ke munafe tak jata hai, kyunke asal me ye teeno aik hi cheez hain: kul cheez ko hison me baantna. Concept pakka ho jaye to ratio ka koi sawal mushkil nahi rehta.

Pehle concept samajh lo, nisbat hai kya

Dekho, nisbat ka matlab hai hison me taqseem ka hisaab. Aik misaal se samjho. Tumhare paas 25 mithaiyan hain aur tum ne do bhaiyon ko is tarah baantni hain ke chhote ko har 2 mithaiyon par bare ko 3 milen. Yani bare ka hissa kuch zyada hai, lekin kitna zyada, ye nisbat batati hai. Mithaiyon ko pehle hison me socho. Chhote ke 2 hisse, bare ke 3 hisse, kul 5 hisse. Ab 25 mithaiyon ko 5 hison me barabar baanto, aik hissa 5 mithaiyon ka hua. Chhote ko 2 hisse, yani 10 mithaiyan. Bare ko 3 hisse, yani 15 mithaiyan. Khatam. Jab hum kehte hain mithaiyan 2:3 ke ratio me baanti gayin, to is ka matlab yehi hai, 2 hisse aik ko, 3 hisse dusre ko.

Gaur karo, 2:3 ka matlab 2 rupe aur 3 rupe bilkul nahi hai. Ye hisse hain. Agar kul mithaiyan 25 ki jagah 50 hotin to hisse wohi 2 aur 3 rehte, lekin aik hissa 5 ki jagah 10 mithaiyon ka ho jata, aur taqseem 20 aur 30 ho jati. Ratio hamein sirf ye batata hai ke taqseem kis hisaab se hogi, asal raqam kul cheez par munhasir hai. Ye aik jumla zehen me bitha lo to ratio ke aadhe sawal wahi hal ho jate hain.

Ab proportion, yani tanasub, ko samjho. Ye ratio ki hi agli shakal hai. Jab do ratio barabar hon to use proportion kehte hain. Jese 2:3 aur 10:15 aik hi baat hai, kyunke 10 aur 15 ko 5 se taqseem karo to 2 aur 3 hi bachta hai. Dukaan par aksar yun hota hai. Agar 3 kilo cheeni 300 rupe ki hai to 1 kilo 100 ki, aur 7 kilo 700 ki. Qeemat aur wazan ka ratio hamesha aik rehta hai. Paper me proportion ka sawal yun ata hai ke 4:5 = 12:x, to x nikalo. Tareeqa ye hai ke dekho 4 se 12 kaise bana, 3 se guna karke. To 5 ko bhi 3 se guna karo, 15. X 15 hai. Ratio apna hisaab dono taraf aik jaisa rakhta hai.

Phir partnership par ao, kyunke ye bhi ratio hi hai, bas hisse banane ka tareeqa thora gehra hai. Do doston ne mil kar dukaan kholi. Aik ne zyada paisa lagaya, dusre ne kam. Saal ke aakhir me munafa kis hisaab se bante ga? Seedhi baat hai, jis ne zyada paisa lagaya use zyada hissa milega. Lekin aik aur baat hai jo aksar nazar andaz hoti hai, waqt. Socho, aik dost ne paisa saal ke shuru me lagaya aur wo paisa pure 12 mahine karobar me ghumta raha. Dusra dost 6 mahine baad shamil hua, us ka paisa sirf 6 mahine ghuma. Kya dono ka dawa barabar hoga agar raqam bhi barabar ho? Bilkul nahi. Jis ka paisa zyada dair karobar me raha, us ka hissa zyada hona chahiye. Isi liye partnership me hissa sirf sarmaye se nahi banta, sarmaya × waqt se banta hai. Paisa jitna zyada aur jitni dair, hissa utna bara. Ye concept samajh lo, partnership ka har sawal is ki hi aik shakal hai.

Board par formula

Ustad jab board par nisbat ka formula likhta hai to yun likhta hai. Ratio a:b ka matlab hai kul hisse a + b, aur har aik ka hissa nikalne ke liye kul raqam ko kul hison par taqseem karke aik hisse ki qeemat nikalo. Pehle hisse ki raqam = kul raqam × a ÷ (a + b). Aur proportion me, a:b = c:d ho to a × d = b × c, yani kone wale numberon ka guna darmiyaan walon ke guna ke barabar hota hai. Partnership ke liye board par ye likha hota hai: Munafe ka ratio = Sarmaya × Waqt ka ratio. Agar sarmaya alag ho lekin waqt aik ho to sirf sarmaye ka ratio, aur agar sarmaya aik ho lekin waqt alag ho to sirf waqt ka ratio.

Misaalein board se

Ab board par misaalein likhta hun. Pehle ratio ki buniyad, phir proportion, phir partnership. Har misaal ke sath tareeqa bhi samjho.

1. 25 mithaiyan do bhaiyon me 2:3 ke ratio me baanto.
Kul hisse 2 + 3 = 5. Aik hissa 25 ÷ 5 = 5. Chhote ko 2 × 5 = 10, bare ko 3 × 5 = 15. Jama karke dekho, 10 + 15 = 25, hisaab pura.

2. 12:18 ko sab se asaan shakal me likho.
Dono ko 6 se taqseem karo. 12 ÷ 6 = 2 aur 18 ÷ 6 = 3. Jawab 2:3 hai. Ratio ko hamesha sab se chhoti shakal me lana paper ki aadat hai, aur options bhi chhoti shakal me hi hote hain.

3. Aik raqam 900 rupe do logo me 4:5 ke ratio me baanti gayi. Har aik ko kitna mila?
Kul hisse 9. Aik hissa 900 ÷ 9 = 100. Pehle ko 4 × 100 = 400, dusre ko 5 × 100 = 500.

4. Do bhaiyon ki tankhwah ka ratio 5:3 hai aur un ki tankhwahon ka farq 4,000 rupe hai. Dono ki tankhwah nikalo.
Ye farq wala sawal hai, kul wala nahi. Hison ka farq 5 − 3 = 2 hisse, aur ye 2 hisse 4,000 ke barabar hain. Aik hissa 2,000. Pehle ki tankhwah 5 × 2,000 = 10,000, dusre ki 3 × 2,000 = 6,000. Parkho, farq waqai 4,000 hai.

5. Agar 3:4 = x:20 ho to x kitna hai?
3 se x bana hai aur 4 se 20, yani dusri taraf 5 guna barha hai. To pehli taraf bhi 5 guna, x = 3 × 5 = 15. Dusra tareeqa, kone wala guna: 3 × 20 = 4 × x, to x = 60 ÷ 4 = 15. Dono se aik hi jawab.

6. Teen doston ne aik raqam 2:3:5 ke ratio me baanti. Kul raqam 1,000 rupe hai. Har aik ka hissa nikalo.
Kul hisse 2 + 3 + 5 = 10. Aik hissa 100. Hisse 200, 300 aur 500. Teen wala ratio bhi bilkul isi tarah chalta hai, bas hisse teen hain.

7. Aik class me larkon aur larkiyon ki tadaad ka ratio 3:2 hai. Kul students 45 hain. Larkiyan kitni hain?
Kul hisse 5. Aik hissa 45 ÷ 5 = 9. Larkiyan 2 hisse, 2 × 9 = 18. Larkay 27. Jama 45, hisaab pura.

8. Ali ne karobar me 50,000 rupe lagaye aur Bilal ne 25,000. Dono ka paisa poora saal raha. Saal ke aakhir me munafa 9,000 rupe hua. Har aik ka hissa nikalo.
Waqt dono ka barabar hai, is liye sirf sarmaye ka ratio chalega. 50,000 : 25,000 = 2:1. Kul hisse 3, aik hissa 3,000. Ali ko 6,000, Bilal ko 3,000.

9. Ali ne 20,000 rupe 12 mahine ke liye lagaye. Bilal ne 30,000 rupe lagaye lekin wo 6 mahine baad shamil hua, yani us ka paisa sirf 6 mahine raha. Munafa 14,000 rupe hai. Har aik ka hissa nikalo.
Ab waqt alag hai, to sarmaya × waqt nikalo. Ali: 20,000 × 12 = 240,000. Bilal: 30,000 × 6 = 180,000. Ratio 240,000 : 180,000 = 4:3. Kul hisse 7, aik hissa 2,000. Ali ko 8,000, Bilal ko 6,000. Dekho, Bilal ne paisa zyada lagaya lekin waqt aadha tha, is liye hissa kam mila. Yehi is sawal ki jaan hai.

10. Aik bartan me doodh aur pani ka ratio 4:1 hai. Kul mixture 25 litre hai. Doodh aur pani alag alag kitna hai?
Kul hisse 5. Aik hissa 5 litre. Doodh 4 × 5 = 20 litre, pani 1 × 5 = 5 litre. Mixture ke saare sawal isi buniyad par khade hote hain, doodh pani wala ratio pehle pakka kar lo.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Rozmarra zindagi me ratio har jagah hai. Rasoi me sharbat banate hue cheeni aur pani ka hisaab ratio hai. Naqsha banane wala zameen ko ratio me chhota karta hai. Karobar me shareekon ka hissa ratio se banta hai. Paper me is ki teen shaklain sab se zyada ati hain.

Pehli shakal tankhwah aur raqam wali hai. Ahmed aur Bilal ki tankhwah ka ratio 3:5 hai aur kul tankhwah 24,000 rupe hai. Kul hisse 8, aik hissa 3,000, to Ahmed ko 9,000 aur Bilal ko 15,000. Dusri shakal mixture wali hai, jese oper doodh pani me dekhi. Teesri aur sab se zyada phansane wali shakal partnership me waqt wali hai. Do log barabar paisa lagate hain, lekin aik ka paisa 12 mahine rehta hai aur dusre ka 4 mahine. Ratio paisa ka nahi, paisa × waqt ka bane ga, yani 12:4 = 3:1. Jo student yahan sirf paisa dekh kar munafa aadha aadha kar de, us ka number kat gaya. Paper hamesha yehi parkhta hai ke tum waqt ko dekhte ho ya nahi.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab wo trick jo ratio ke har sawal ki chabi hai. Isay hum Aik Hissa wala qanoon kehte hain. Ratio dikha nahi aur tum ne kul hisse gine nahi, to samjho pehla kadam reh gaya. Kul hisse gin lo, phir kul raqam ko un par taqseem karke aik hisse ki qeemat nikalo, phir jis ka jitna hissa hai use utne se guna kar do. Kul diya ho to kul se, farq diya ho to hison ke farq se aik hissa nikalo. Aur partnership ke liye is ke sath aik jumla aur bitha lo: paisa × mahine = asal hissa. Jitna paisa jitni dair, hissa utna bara. Ye do baatein zehen me hon to ratio aur partnership ka koi sawal rukta nahi.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ab wo ghaltiyan dekho jo students paper me karte hain. Main board par ghalat aur sahi sath sath likh raha hun, taake farq hamesha yaad rahe.

Ghalat: 3:5 ka matlab liya ke pehle bande ko 3 rupe aur dusre ko 5 rupe milen ge.
Sahi: 3 aur 5 hisse hain, raqam nahi. Asal raqam kul cheez ko hison par baant kar nikalti hai.
Wajah: Ratio sirf taqseem ka hisaab batata hai. Kul raqam badal jaye to hison ki qeemat badal jati hai, ratio nahi badalta.

Ghalat: Partnership me sirf sarmaya dekh kar munafa baant diya, waqt nazar andaz kar diya.
Sahi: Munafa sarmaya × waqt ke ratio me bant-ta hai.
Wajah: Jo paisa zyada dair karobar me ghumta hai wo zyada munafa kamata hai. Aadhe partnership sawal isi aik bhool par kat-te hain.

Ghalat: Ahmed aur Bilal ka ratio 3:5 tha, jawab likhte hue Bilal ka hissa pehle aur Ahmed ka baad me likh diya.
Sahi: Jis ka naam pehle, us ka hissa pehle. Tartib ratio ki jaan hai.
Wajah: Ratio tartib se zinda hai. 3:5 aur 5:3 bilkul alag baatein hain. Naam dekh kar hisa lagaao, ulti tartib pura sawal ghalat kar deti hai.

Ghalat: 2:3:5 ke kul hisse 9 gin liye.
Sahi: Kul hisse 2 + 3 + 5 = 10 hain.
Wajah: Aik hisse ki qeemat kul hison se nikalti hai. Hisse ghalat gine to har jawab ghalat. Jama karte hue ungliyon par ginna bhi bura nahi, jaldi me yehi ghalti hoti hai.

Ghalat: Farq wale sawal me aik hissa kul raqam se nikal liya.
Sahi: Farq diya ho to aik hissa hison ke farq se nikalo, kul diya ho to kul hison se.
Wajah: Sawal jo deta hai, aik hissa usi se banta hai. Farq ko kul par taqseem karna hisaab ko ulta kar deta hai.

Jawab aur un ki wajah

1. Jawab B hai, 3:4. Dono ko 5 se taqseem karo, 15 ÷ 5 = 3 aur 20 ÷ 5 = 4.
2. Jawab C hai, 30,000 rupe. Kul hisse 5, aik hissa 10,000. B ke 3 hisse, 3 × 10,000 = 30,000.
3. Jawab B hai, 15. 4 se 12 bana, yani 3 guna. 5 ko bhi 3 guna karo, 15.
4. Jawab C hai, 10,000 rupe. Kul hisse 12, aik hissa 2,000. Sab se bara hissa 5 hai, 5 × 2,000 = 10,000.
5. Jawab A hai, 10,000 rupe. Sarmaye ka ratio 40,000:60,000 = 2:3 hai. Aik hissa 5,000. Ali ke 2 hisse, 10,000.
6. Jawab C hai, 1:1. Ali ka sarmaya × waqt 30,000 × 12 = 360,000. Bilal ka 45,000 × 8 = 360,000. Dono barabar, is liye munafa aadha aadha bante ga. Ye sawal sirf paisa dekhne walon ko phansata hai.
7. Jawab B hai, 10 litre. Kul hisse 7, aik hissa 5 litre. Pani 2 hisse, 2 × 5 = 10 litre.
8. Jawab B hai, 15,000 rupe. Hison ka farq 2 hai aur wohi 6,000 hai, to aik hissa 3,000. A ke 5 hisse, 15,000.
9. Jawab B hai, 18. Kul hisse 5, aik hissa 9. Larkiyan 2 hisse, 18.
10. Jawab A hai, 8:15. B ko barabar karne ke liye pehle ratio ko 4 se aur dusre ko 3 se guna karo. A:B = 8:12 aur B:C = 12:15, to A:C = 8:15.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye do tasveerein bohat kaam ki hongi. Pehli tasveer mithai baantne wali ho. Aik thaal me 25 mithaiyan dikhai jayen. Phir teer ke nishan ke baad do tokriyan hon, chhoti tokri me 10 mithaiyan aur upar 2 hisse likha ho, bari tokri me 15 mithaiyan aur upar 3 hisse likha ho. Darmiyaan me bara sa jumla ho ke ratio raqam nahi, hisse hain. Student aik nazar me dekh le ke 2:3 ka matlab 2 aur 3 mithaiyan nahi, 2 aur 3 hisse hain.

Doosri tasveer partnership wali ho. Do dukaan ke shareek dikhaye jayen. Aik ke neeche us ki raqam aur pure 12 mahine ka calendar ho, dusre ke neeche us ki raqam aur sirf 6 mahine ka aadha calendar ho. Darmiyaan me formula ho, Hissa = Sarmaya × Waqt. Is tasveer ka maqsad ye hai ke student ki ankh waqt ko dekhna seekh jaye, kyunke paper me number paisa dekhne se nahi, waqt bhoolne se kat-ta hai.
