Raftaar, Faasla aur Waqt — Speed, Distance, Time
Train, safar aur kashti ke sawal, bina ratte, sirf concept se

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me maths ke hisse me raftaar, faasle aur waqt ke do teen sawal taqreeban har paper me ate hain, aur train wala sawal to har dusre paper ki zeenat hai. Khushi ki baat ye hai ke is poore topic ka formula sirf aik hai. Paper usi aik formula ko ghuma phira kar, kabhi train bana kar, kabhi kashti bana kar, kabhi do gaariyan amne samne la kar poochta hai. Jo student concept samajh le, us ke liye ye sawal paper ke sab se asaan number hain. Aur jo sirf formula ratta hai, wo platform wale sawal me hamesha phansta hai. Is lesson ke baad tum nahi phanso ge.

Pehle raftaar ko ghar se samajh lo

Safar par nikalne se pehle ghar me sab se zyada poocha jane wala sawal yahi hota hai, Lahore se Multan kitni der lagegi? Is sawal ka jawab asal me raftaar hi deti hai. Raftaar ka matlab hai, aik ghante me kitna faasla tay hua. Bus 60 kilometre fi ghanta par ja rahi hai, is ka matlab hai ke agar wo isi raftaar se chalti rahe to har ghante 60 kilometre aage barhe gi. Gaari ke speedometer par jo number nazar ata hai, wo yahi raftaar hai.

Teen cheezon ke naam pakke kar lo. Faasla, yani distance, kul safar kitna lamba hai, jese ghar se station tak 12 kilometre. Waqt, yani time, wo safar karne me kitni der lagi. Aur raftaar, yani speed, wo tezi jis se faasla kata. Ye teen dost hain, aur poora lesson inhi teeno ki dosti hai.

Formula aur us ka ghumana

Ab formula ko ratne ki bajaye bana kar dekho. Bus har ghante 60 kilometre jati hai. Do ghante me wo 60 aur 60, yani 120 kilometre jaye gi. Teen ghante me 60 teen dafa, yani 180. Yehi kaam guna tez karke kar deta hai. To pehla formula khud ban gaya, Faasla barabar Raftaar guna Waqt.

Ab isi ko ulta ghumao. Tumhe 240 kilometre jana hai aur bus 60 ki raftaar se ja rahi hai. Kitne ghante lagen ge? Har ghante 60 kat-te hain, to 240 ko 60 se taqseem kar do, 4 ghante. Ye dusra formula hai, Waqt barabar Faasla taqseem Raftaar. Aur agar faasla aur waqt dono maloom hon aur raftaar chahiye ho, to Raftaar barabar Faasla taqseem Waqt. Teen formulae nahi hain, aik hi hai, bas jis cheez ko dhundna ho use akela kar lo.

Units ka masla aur 5/18 ki kahani

Paper ka pasandeeda phanda units ka hai. Raftaar kilometre fi ghanta me di hoti hai, lekin train wale sawal ka waqt seconds me poocha jata hai. Dono ko aik zuban me lana padta hai, warna jawab ghalat ho jata hai.

Kilometre fi ghanta ko metre fi second me badalna seekho, aur ratta nahi, wajah ke sath. Aik kilometre me 1000 metre hote hain, aur aik ghante me 3600 second. To 1 kilometre fi ghanta ka matlab hai 1000 metre tay karna 3600 second me, yani 1000 taqseem 3600 metre fi second. Ab 1000 aur 3600 dono ko 200 se taqseem karo to upar 5 aur neeche 18 reh jata hai. Bas, yahi se 5/18 aya hai. Ye koi jadui number nahi, ye 1000 aur 3600 ki asaan shakal hai.

To qanoon ye bana. Kilometre fi ghanta ko metre fi second me badalna ho to 5/18 se guna karo. Metre fi second ko kilometre fi ghanta me badalna ho to ulta, 18/5 se guna karo. Pehchan ye hai ke metre fi second wala number hamesha chhota hota hai. 72 kilometre fi ghanta sunne me tez lagta hai, metre fi second me wo sirf 20 hai. Number chhota karna hai to 5/18, number bara karna hai to 18/5.

Train wale sawal aur dum wala raaz

Ab paper ke sab se pyare sawal par ao. Gaari aur bus ko hum aik nuqta samajh kar chala dete hain, lekin train ki apni lambai hoti hai, aur yahi lambai is sawal ki jaan hai.

Pehli surat, train kisi khambe, signal ya khare hue aadmi ko par kare. Khamba aik nuqta hai, us ki apni lambai na hone ke barabar hai. Train tab usay par karti hai jab us ki dum bhi khambe se aage nikal jaye. Naak se dum tak, train ko sirf apni lambai jitna chalna padta hai. To is surat me faasla barabar train ki lambai.

Dusri surat, train kisi platform, pul ya surung ko par kare. Ab samne wali cheez ki bhi lambai hai. Train tab puri guzarti hai jab us ki dum platform ke dusre kinare se bhi bahar nikal jaye. Is liye train ke agle hisse ko platform ki lambai ke upar apni lambai bhi chalni padti hai. To is surat me faasla barabar train ki lambai jama platform ki lambai.

Zehen me aik tasveer bana lo. Train guzarne ka matlab naak ka guzarna nahi, dum ka guzarna hai. Jahan dum yaad rahi, wahan ye sawal kabhi ghalat nahi hoga.

Do gaariyan, amne samne ya aik hi simt

Jab do gaariyan amne samne a rahi hon to faasla dono ki tezi se khatam hota hai. Tum ne khud mehsoos kiya hoga ke samne se ane wali bus kitni tezi se paas se guzar jati hai. Isi liye amne samne ki surat me dono ki raftaar jama hoti hai. Ise relative speed kehte hain, yani aik dusre ke hisab se raftaar.

Aur jab dono aik hi simt me ja rahi hon, to peeche wali tez gaari agle wali ko tabhi pakregi jab wo apni izafi tezi se darmiyan ka faasla khatam kare. To is surat me raftaarain minus hoti hain, tez me se sust nikalti hai. Jama aur minus ka faisla sirf simt karti hai, samne se milna hai to jama, peeche se pakarna hai to minus.

Kashti aur darya ki dhara

Aakhir me kashti wala sawal, jo asal me yahi do gaariyon wala khayal pani me hai. Darya ki dhara kashti ki madad bhi karti hai aur rok bhi. Jab kashti dhara ke sath jati hai, yani downstream, to dhara use dhakelti hai, is liye raftaar dono ka jama hoti hai, kashti ki raftaar jama dhara ki raftaar. Aur jab kashti dhara ke khilaf jati hai, yani upstream, to dhara use peeche dhakelti hai, is liye raftaar minus hoti hai.

Is ka ulta sawal bhi paper me ata hai. Agar dhara ke sath wali raftaar aur khilaf wali raftaar dono maloom hon, to kashti ki asal raftaar dono ke jama ka aadha hoti hai, aur dhara ki raftaar dono ke farq ka aadha. Wajah saaf hai, jama karne se dhara aik dafa jama aur aik dafa minus hokar khatam ho jati hai, kashti dugni reh jati hai.

Board par formula

Ustad jab board par is lesson ka khulasa likhta hai to kuch yun likhta hai. Speed = Distance / Time. Distance = Speed × Time. Time = Distance / Speed. To change km/h into m/s, multiply by 5/18. To change m/s into km/h, multiply by 18/5. For a train crossing a pole, distance = length of the train. For a train crossing a platform, distance = length of the train + length of the platform. Opposite direction, relative speed = sum of speeds. Same direction, relative speed = difference of speeds. Downstream = boat speed + stream speed. Upstream = boat speed − stream speed.

Misaalein board se

Ab board par misaalein hal karte hain. Har misaal ke sath wajah bhi samjho, sirf jawab mat dekho.

1. Aik bus 60 kilometre fi ghanta ki raftaar se 3 ghante chalti hai. Faasla kitna hua?
Faasla barabar Raftaar guna Waqt, yani 60 × 3 = 180 kilometre.

2. Aik car 240 kilometre ka faasla 4 ghante me tay karti hai. Uski raftaar kya hai?
Raftaar barabar Faasla taqseem Waqt, yani 240 / 4 = 60 kilometre fi ghanta.

3. Aik cycle sawaar 15 kilometre fi ghanta se 45 kilometre jata hai. Kitna waqt lagega?
Waqt barabar Faasla taqseem Raftaar, yani 45 / 15 = 3 ghante.

4. 72 kilometre fi ghanta ko metre fi second me badlo.
72 × 5/18 = 20 metre fi second. Dekho, number chhota ho gaya, yahi pehchan hai.

5. 15 metre fi second ko kilometre fi ghanta me badlo.
15 × 18/5 = 54 kilometre fi ghanta. Ab number bara ho gaya.

6. Aik train 200 metre lambi hai aur 72 kilometre fi ghanta se ja rahi hai. Wo aik khambe ko kitni der me par karegi?
Pehle raftaar badlo, 72 kilometre fi ghanta barabar 20 metre fi second. Khambe wale sawal me faasla sirf train ki lambai hai, yani 200 metre. Waqt barabar 200 / 20 = 10 second.

7. Wohi train 300 metre lambe platform ko kitni der me par karegi?
Ab faasla train jama platform, yani 200 + 300 = 500 metre. Waqt barabar 500 / 20 = 25 second. Dekho, wohi train, wohi raftaar, sirf surat badli aur jawab 10 se 25 ho gaya. Yehi farq paper par number deta hai.

8. Do trains amne samne a rahi hain. Pehli 150 metre lambi aur 40 kilometre fi ghanta par hai, dusri 100 metre lambi aur 50 kilometre fi ghanta par hai. Wo aik dusre ko kitni der me par karengi?
Amne samne hain, raftaar jama hogi, 40 + 50 = 90 kilometre fi ghanta, yani 25 metre fi second. Aik dusre ko par karne me kul faasla dono ki lambaiyon ka jama hai, 150 + 100 = 250 metre. Waqt barabar 250 / 25 = 10 second.

9. Do basein aik hi simt me ja rahi hain, aik 60 aur dusri 45 kilometre fi ghanta par. Har ghante un ke darmiyan ka faasla kitna kam hoga?
Aik hi simt hai, raftaar minus hogi, 60 − 45 = 15 kilometre fi ghanta. Yani tez bus har ghante 15 kilometre ka faasla khatam karegi.

10. Aik kashti dhara ke sath 15 kilometre fi ghanta aur dhara ke khilaf 9 kilometre fi ghanta chalti hai. Kashti ki asal raftaar aur dhara ki raftaar nikalo.
Kashti barabar dono ke jama ka aadha, (15 + 9) / 2 = 12 kilometre fi ghanta. Dhara barabar farq ka aadha, (15 − 9) / 2 = 3 kilometre fi ghanta. Check kar lo, 12 + 3 = 15 aur 12 − 3 = 9, hisaab mil gaya.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Rozmarra me ye hisaab har safar me chhupa hai. Ghar se daftar kitni der lagegi, petrol kitna lagega, train station kab pohnche gi. Lekin paper me ye khaas teen suraton me ata hai, train aur platform, do gaariyan, aur kashti aur dhara. Ab paper wale andaaz ke do sawal dekho.

1. Aik train 180 metre lambi hai aur 54 kilometre fi ghanta se chal rahi hai. Wo 270 metre lambe platform ko kitni der me par karegi?
Hal zehen me karo. 54 kilometre fi ghanta barabar 15 metre fi second. Faasla 180 + 270 = 450 metre. Waqt 450 / 15 = 30 second.

2. Aik kashti dhara ke sath 14 kilometre fi ghanta aur khilaf 8 kilometre fi ghanta chalti hai. Kashti ki asal raftaar kya hai?
Jama ka aadha, (14 + 8) / 2 = 11 kilometre fi ghanta.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab wo trick jo har sawal ko teen second me khol degi. Isay hum Teen dost wala masalas kehte hain. Aik bara masalas, yani triangle, banao. Upar wale khane me Faasla likho, neeche ke do khanon me Raftaar aur Waqt. Jo cheez chahiye, us par ungli rakh kar chhupa do. Neeche wale do dost reh jayen to guna kar do, upar wala aur neeche wala reh jayen to taqseem kar do. Formula yaad nahi karna padega, masalas khud bata dega.

Aur units ke liye aik chhoti si line zehen me bitha lo. Number chhota karna hai to paanch atharah, number bara karna hai to atharah paanch. Aur train ke har sawal me sirf aik lafz, dum. Train tab guzri jab dum guzri.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ab wo ghaltiyan dekho jo Pakistani students paper me karte hain. Main board par ghalat tareeqa aur sahi tareeqa sath sath likh raha hun, taake farq hamesha yaad rahe.

Ghalat: Platform wale sawal me faasla sirf platform ki lambai liya.
Sahi: Faasla train ki lambai jama platform ki lambai liya jata hai.
Wajah: Train tab puri guzarti hai jab us ki dum bhi platform se bahar nikal jaye. Dum ko bhi to safar karna hai.

Ghalat: Kilometre fi ghanta ko metre fi second me badalne ke liye 18/5 se guna kar diya.
Sahi: 5/18 se guna hota hai.
Wajah: Metre fi second wala number hamesha chhota hota hai. Agar number bara ho gaya to samjho ulta formula lag gaya.

Ghalat: Amne samne ane wali trains ki raftaar minus kar di.
Sahi: Amne samne ki surat me raftaar jama hoti hai.
Wajah: Dono aik dusre ki taraf barh rahe hain, is liye darmiyan ka faasla dono ki tezi se khatam hota hai.

Ghalat: Aik aadmi 60 kilometre 40 ki raftaar se gaya aur wapas 60 ki raftaar se aya, to ausat raftaar (40 + 60) / 2 = 50 keh di.
Sahi: Ausat raftaar kul faasla taqseem kul waqt se nikalti hai. Jane me dedh ghanta, ane me aik ghanta, kul 120 kilometre taqseem dhai ghante = 48 kilometre fi ghanta.
Wajah: Aadmi sust raftaar par zyada waqt raha, is liye ausat 50 se neeche aati hai. Jitna faasla dono taraf barabar ho, seedha ausat kabhi nahi hota.

Ghalat: Train wale sawal me raftaar kilometre fi ghanta me rakhi aur waqt seconds me nikal liya, units aik nahi kiye.
Sahi: Pehle raftaar ko metre fi second me badlo, phir waqt nikalo.
Wajah: Metre wale faasle ke sath ghante wali raftaar nahi chalti. Pehla qadam hamesha units ko aik karna hai.

Jawab aur un ki wajah

1. Jawab B hai, 135 kilometre. Faasla barabar Raftaar guna Waqt, 54 × 2.5 = 135.
2. Jawab C hai, 60. Raftaar barabar Faasla taqseem Waqt, 300 / 5 = 60.
3. Jawab D hai, 25. 90 × 5/18 = 25. Number chhota hua, yahi sahi pehchan hai.
4. Jawab A hai, 36. 10 × 18/5 = 36.
5. Jawab C hai, 15 second. 36 kilometre fi ghanta barabar 10 metre fi second. Khambe ke liye faasla sirf train ki lambai, 150 / 10 = 15.
6. Jawab B hai, 20 second. Faasla 240 + 160 = 400 metre. Raftaar 72 kilometre fi ghanta barabar 20 metre fi second. 400 / 20 = 20.
7. Jawab A hai, 100. Amne samne hain, is liye raftaar jama hogi, 45 + 55 = 100.
8. Jawab D hai, 20. Aik hi simt hai, is liye farq niklega, 70 − 50 = 20.
9. Jawab B hai, 15. Kashti ki raftaar dono ke jama ka aadha hoti hai, (18 + 12) / 2 = 15.
10. Jawab A hai, 48. Jane me 3 ghante, ane me 2 ghante. Kul faasla 240, kul waqt 5 ghante. 240 / 5 = 48. Seedha ausat 50 wala option hi paper ka phanda tha.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye do tasveerein bohat kaam ki hongi. Pehli tasveer Teen dost wale masalas ki ho. Aik bara masalas teen khanon me banta hua dikhaya jaye, upar wale khane me FAASLA, neeche ke do khanon me RAFTAAR aur WAQT. Sath me aik ungli aik khane ko chhupati hui dikhayi jaye, taake student ko hamesha yaad rahe ke jo chahiye use chhupao, baqi do khud formula bata denge.

Doosri tasveer train wali ho, do hison me. Upar wale hisse me train aik khambe ko par karti hui dikhayi jaye aur sirf train ki lambai rang se numayan ho. Neeche wale hisse me wohi train aik platform ko par karti hui ho aur train ki lambai ke sath platform ki lambai bhi dusre rang me juri hui dikhayi jaye. Aik nazar me pole aur platform ka farq zehen me baith jaye, yehi is tasveer ka maqsad hai. Teesri chhoti tasveer me do trains amne samne teeron ke sath dikhayi ja sakti hain, aik teer 40 ka aur dusra 50 ka, aur darmiyan me bara sa 90 likha ho.
