Lesson: Waqt aur Kaam — Time and Work
Waqt aur Kaam. Aik din ka kaam samajh lo, har sawal khul jayega

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC ke one paper me waqt aur kaam ke sawal har saal ate hain. Kabhi do mazdooron ke mil kar kaam karne ka sawal, kabhi tanki bharne aur khali hone ka, kabhi aik banda darmiyaan me kaam chor kar chala jata hai. Ye topic un students ko sab se zyada phansata hai jo dinon ko seedha jama kar dete hain ya zyada bande dekh kar zyada din likh dete hain. Lekin sach ye hai ke is poore topic ki jaan sirf aik chhoti si baat hai: aik din ka kaam. Wo baat samajh a jaye to tanki ho ya deewar, mazdoor hon ya pipe, har sawal aik hi tareeqe se hal hota hai. Is lesson me me tumhe wo concept dunga, formula baad me khud banta jayega.

Pehle concept samajh lo, aik din ka kaam kya hai

Dekho, is topic me sab se pehli baat ye zehen me bithani hai ke kaam ko hum aik pura hisa samajhte hain. Poori deewar banana aik kaam hai, poori tanki bharna aik kaam hai. Is pure kaam ko hum 1 kehte hain. Na 100, na koi aur number, bas 1. Ab socho, aik mazdoor akela aik deewar 10 din me banata hai. Yani 10 din me wo 1 pura kaam karta hai. To aik din me wo kitna kaam karega? Seedhi si baat hai, kaam ka daswaan hisa, yani 1/10. Ye fraction koi mushkil cheez nahi, ye wohi barabar wali taqseem hai jo ausat me seekhi thi. Kul kaam 1 hai, din 10 hain, har din ke hisse me 1/10 aya.

Ab tanki wali misaal se is ko aur pakka karo. Aik pipe aik tanki ko 6 ghante me bharta hai. Yani 6 ghante me wo 1 poori tanki bharta hai. To aik ghante me kitna bharega? Tanki ka chhata hisa, 1/6. Agar tanki par nishan lage hon to tum dekh sakte ho ke har ghante pani aik hisa barhta hai, aur 6 ghante me tanki puri. Bas, yehi is topic ki buniyad hai. Banda jitne din me kaam karta hai, us ka aik din ka kaam us number ka ulta fraction hota hai. 10 din wala 1/10, 15 din wala 1/15, 6 ghante wala 1/6.

Ab asal sawal par ao. Do bande mil kar kaam karein to kya hoga? A akela kaam 10 din me karta hai, B akela 15 din me. Dono mil kar kitne din me karen ge? Yahan dimagh pehle kehta hai ke shayad 10 aur 15 ke darmiyaan ka koi number hoga, aur kuch log to 25 bhi likh dete hain. Lekin socho zara. A aik din me 1/10 kaam karta hai. B aik din me 1/15 kaam karta hai. Dono mil kar aik din me 1/10 + 1/15 kaam karen ge. Hisab karo, 1/10 aur 1/15 ko jama karne ke liye makhraj 30 karo. 3/30 + 2/30 = 5/30, yani 1/6. Dono mil kar aik din me kaam ka chhata hisa karte hain. To poora kaam, yani 1, kitne din me hoga? Chhe din me. Jawab 6 din. Gaur karo, dono ke akele dinon se kam hai, aur hona bhi chahiye, kyunke do bande mil kar tez hote hain. Yehi parkh har sawal me rakho: mil kar wala jawab hamesha dono ke akele dinon se chhota hoga. Agar tumhara jawab bare number se bara a jaye to samjho hisaab ghalat hai.

Aik aur rishta samajh lo, bande aur din ka ulta rishta. Deewar wohi hai, mazdoor barh jayen to din ghat jayenge. 6 mazdoor aik kaam 10 din me karte hain. Kul mehnat gin lo: 6 mazdoor × 10 din = 60 mazdoor-din ka kaam hai. Ab agar 12 mazdoor laga do to yehi 60 wala kaam 60 ÷ 12 = 5 din me ho jayega. Bande dugne, din aadhe. Isi ko kitabon me M1D1 = M2D2 likha jata hai, lekin ratta lagane ki zaroorat nahi. Bas ye yaad rakho ke kul kaam mazdoor × din se banta hai, aur kaam wohi rehta hai, bande badlo to din usi hisaab se badalte hain, lekin ulta. Zyada bande, kam din. Kam bande, zyada din.

Board par formula

Ustad jab board par waqt aur kaam ka formula likhta hai to yun likhta hai. Agar aik banda kaam ko D din me karta hai to us ka aik din ka kaam = 1/D. Do bande mil kar kaam karein to un ka aik din ka kaam dono ke aik din ke kaamon ka jama hota hai, aur kul din = 1 ÷ (aik din ka mila hua kaam). Bande aur din ke liye: M1 × D1 = M2 × D2, yani pehle mazdoor × pehle din = dusre mazdoor × dusre din, kyunke kul kaam aik hi rehta hai. Aur pipe wale sawalon ke liye khaas baat: bharne wala pipe jama hota hai aur khali karne wala pipe ghata hota hai, minus lagta hai.

Misaalein board se

Ab board par misaalein likhta hun. Har misaal ke sath tareeqa bhi samjho, sirf jawab mat dekho.

1. A akela aik kaam 8 din me karta hai. Us ka aik din ka kaam kitna hai?
Kaam 1 hai, din 8 hain. Aik din ka kaam 1/8. Bas, yehi pehla kadam har sawal me uthega.

2. A aik kaam 6 din me karta hai aur B wahi kaam 12 din me. Dono mil kar kitne din me karen ge?
Aik din ka mila hua kaam 1/6 + 1/12 = 2/12 + 1/12 = 3/12, yani 1/4. Poora kaam 4 din me hoga.

3. A aik kaam 10 din me aur B 15 din me karta hai. Dono mil kar?
Ye wohi sawal hai jo concept me hal kiya tha. Aik din ka kaam 1/10 + 1/15 = 1/6. Jawab 6 din. Dekho, tareeqa har dafa aik hi hai.

4. Panch mazdoor aik deewar 12 din me banate hain. Das mazdoor wahi deewar kitne din me banayen ge?
Kul kaam 5 × 12 = 60 mazdoor-din. Das mazdooron ke sath 60 ÷ 10 = 6 din. Bande dugne hue, din aadhe ho gaye.

5. Aath bande aik kaam 15 din me karte hain. Wahi kaam che bande kitne din me karen ge?
Kul kaam 8 × 15 = 120. Che bandon ke sath 120 ÷ 6 = 20 din. Bande kam hue, din barh gaye. Ulta rishta phir sabit ho gaya.

6. A aik kaam 12 din me karta hai. Us ne 4 din kaam kiya aur chala gaya. Baqi kaam B ne sambhala jo wahi kaam akela 9 din me karta hai. B ko baqi kaam kitne din me karna parega?
Pehle dekho A ne kitna kaam kar liya. A ka aik din ka kaam 1/12, to 4 din me 4/12, yani 1/3 kaam ho gaya. Baqi kaam 1 − 1/3 = 2/3 bacha. B ka aik din ka kaam 1/9 hai. B ko din lagen ge (2/3) ÷ (1/9) = 2/3 × 9 = 6 din. Chor kar jane wale sawal ki jaan yehi hai: pehle bacha hua kaam nikalo, phir dusre bande ki raftaar se taqseem karo.

7. Do pipe aik tanki ko alag alag 4 ghante aur 6 ghante me bharte hain. Dono aik sath khol diye jayen to tanki kitne ghante me bhare gi?
Aik ghante ka mila hua kaam 1/4 + 1/6 = 3/12 + 2/12 = 5/12. Poori tanki 12/5 ghante me, yani 2.4 ghante me bhare gi. Pipe wale sawal bilkul mazdooron jese hain, bas din ki jagah ghante hain.

8. Aik pipe tanki ko 8 ghante me bharta hai. Dusra pipe usi tanki ko 12 ghante me khali karta hai. Tanki khali ho aur dono pipe khol diye jayen to kya hoga?
Bharne wala jama, khali karne wala minus. Aik ghante ka asal natija 1/8 − 1/12 = 3/24 − 2/24 = 1/24. Yani har ghante tanki ka 24waan hisa bharta hai. Poori tanki 24 ghante me bhare gi. Bharne wala tez hai is liye tanki bharti hai, lekin ahista. Agar khali karne wala tez hota to tanki kabhi na bharti.

9. A aur B mil kar aik kaam 12 din me karte hain. A akela wahi kaam 20 din me karta hai. B akela kitne din me karega?
Dono ka aik din ka kaam 1/12. A ka aik din ka kaam 1/20. B ka aik din ka kaam 1/12 − 1/20 = 5/60 − 3/60 = 2/60, yani 1/30. B akela 30 din me karega. Ye ghata wala sawal hai, mila hua de kar aik ko nikalna hota hai.

10. A, B se dugna tez kaam karta hai. Dono mil kar aik kaam 12 din me karte hain. A akela kitne din me karega?
B ka aik din ka kaam x samajh lo, to A ka 2x. Dono mila kar 3x = 1/12, to x = 1/36. A ka aik din ka kaam 2/36, yani 1/18. A akela 18 din me karega. Tezi dugni thi, din B ke 36 ke muqable me aadhe, 18. Hisaab mil gaya.

Ye kahan kaam aata hai

Rozmarra zindagi me ye hisaab har jagah hai. Thekaydaar andaza lagata hai ke 20 mazdoor lagayen to building kitne din me khari hogi. Ghar ki tanki ka pipe badal jaye to bharne ka waqt badal jata hai. Khet me tube well se pani lagana ho to yehi hisaab chalta hai. Paper me is ki teen shaklain sab se zyada ati hain.

Pehli shakal mazdooron wali hai. 15 mazdoor aik kaam 20 din me karte hain, 25 mazdoor kitne din me karen ge? Kul kaam 300 mazdoor-din, 300 ÷ 25 = 12 din. Dusri shakal chor kar jane wali hai jo oper misaal 6 me dekhi, aur ye paper ki sab se pasandida shakal hai kyunke is me do kadam hain aur aik kadam bhoolne par poora sawal kat-ta hai. Teesri shakal tanki wali hai jisme aik pipe khali bhi karta hai. Wahan minus wala khayal rakhna hai. Teenon shaklon ki jaan aik hi hai, aik din ka kaam. Jo student aik din ka kaam nikalna seekh gaya, us ke liye ye topic paper ke sab se asaan number ban jata hai.

Yaad rakhne ki trick

Ab wo trick jo is poore topic ki chabi hai. Isay hum Ulta Fraction wala qanoon kehte hain. Din dikhe nahi aur tum ne use ulta kiya nahi, to samjho pehla kadam reh gaya. Banda jitne din me kaam kare, us ka aik din ka kaam us ka ulta fraction hai. Phir teen usool hain. Mil kar kaam ho to aik din ke kaam jama. Aik ko nikalna ho ya pipe khali karta ho to ghata. Aur bande badlein to kul kaam, yani mazdoor × din, hamesha wohi rehta hai. Bas ye jumla zehen me bitha lo: din ko ulta karo, kaam ko jama karo, khali karne wale ko ghata do. Is aik jumle me poora topic sama gaya hai.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ab wo ghaltiyan dekho jo students paper me karte hain. Main board par ghalat aur sahi sath sath likh raha hun, taake farq hamesha yaad rahe.

Ghalat: A 10 din me aur B 15 din me kaam karta hai, to dono mil kar 25 din me karen ge.
Sahi: Dono ka aik din ka kaam jama karo. 1/10 + 1/15 = 1/6, to dono mil kar 6 din me karen ge.
Wajah: Din kabhi jama nahi hote, aik din ke kaam jama hote hain. Aur mila hua jawab hamesha akele dinon se chhota hota hai, ye parkh har sawal me lagao.

Ghalat: 6 mazdoor 10 din me kaam karte hain, to 12 mazdoor 20 din me karen ge.
Sahi: Kul kaam 60 mazdoor-din hai. 12 mazdoor use 5 din me karen ge.
Wajah: Bande aur din ka rishta ulta hai. Bande dugne hon to din aadhe hote hain, dugne nahi. Seedha guna karna is topic ki sab se bari ghalti hai.

Ghalat: Tanki bharne wale pipe ke sath khali karne wale pipe ka kaam bhi jama kar diya.
Sahi: Khali karne wala hamesha ghata hota hai, minus lagta hai.
Wajah: Khali karne wala kaam ko aage nahi, peeche le jata hai. Use jama karna matlab dono ko bharne wala samajhna, aur poora hisaab ulta ho jata hai.

Ghalat: A kaam chor kar gaya to baqi poora kaam B ke aik din ke kaam se taqseem kar diya.
Sahi: Pehle A ke kiye hue kaam ko ghata kar bacha hua kaam nikalo, phir use B ki raftaar se taqseem karo.
Wajah: B ko sirf bacha hua kaam karna hai, poora nahi. Pehla kadam bhoolna matlab B ko woh kaam bhi de dena jo ho chuka tha.

Ghalat: Aik sawal me mazdoor 8 ghante wale din ke hisaab se diye the, jawab pure dinon me nikal liya.
Sahi: Sawal jis ikai me ho, ghante hon ya din, hisaab usi ikai me rakho aur aakhir me wazeh karo.
Wajah: Waqt aur kaam me ikai ka khulasa sab se zyada number kata-ta hai. Din aur ghante ko milana hisaab ko kharab kar deta hai.

Jawab aur un ki wajah

1. Jawab A hai, 1/15. Kaam 1 hai aur din 15, to aik din ka kaam us ka ulta fraction 1/15 hai.
2. Jawab B hai, 4 din. Aik din ka mila hua kaam 1/12 + 1/6 = 1/12 + 2/12 = 3/12, yani 1/4. Poora kaam 4 din me.
3. Jawab B hai, 5 din. Kul kaam 10 × 8 = 80 mazdoor-din. 80 ÷ 16 = 5.
4. Jawab B hai, 3 ghante 20 minute. Aik ghante ka kaam 1/5 + 1/10 = 3/10. Poori tanki 10/3 ghante me, yani 3 ghante 20 minute me bhare gi.
5. Jawab A hai, 6 din. Aik din ka kaam 1/18 + 1/9 = 1/18 + 2/18 = 3/18, yani 1/6. Poora kaam 6 din me.
6. Jawab C hai, 7/10. A ne 3 din me 3/10 kaam kar liya. Baqi 1 − 3/10 = 7/10 reh gaya.
7. Jawab C hai, 15 ghante. Bharne wala minus khali karne wala: 1/6 − 1/10 = 5/30 − 3/30 = 2/30, yani 1/15. Poori tanki 15 ghante me bhare gi.
8. Jawab C hai, 18 bande. Kul kaam 6 × 9 = 54 banda-din. 3 din me karna hai to 54 ÷ 3 = 18 bande chahiye.
9. Jawab C hai, 24 din. B ka aik din ka kaam 1/8 − 1/12 = 3/24 − 2/24 = 1/24. B akela 24 din me karega.
10. Jawab B hai, 15 din. Tezi dugni ho to din aadhe ho jate hain. B ke 30 din ka aadha 15 din.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye do tasveerein bohat kaam ki hongi. Pehli tasveer tanki wali ho. Aik bari tanki dikhai jaye jis par 6 barabar nishan lage hon. Aik pipe use bhar raha ho aur ghari par aik ghanta guzra dikhaya gaya ho, aur pani pehle nishan tak, yani 1/6 tak, charha ho. Neeche likha ho ke 6 ghante me bharne wale ka aik ghante ka kaam 1/6 hai. Student fraction ko ankh se dekh le to wo kabhi nahi bhoolta ke ulta fraction ata kahan se hai.

Doosri tasveer mazdooron wali ho. Aik deewar aadhi bani hui dikhai jaye. Aik taraf 6 mazdoor aur 10 din ka board ho, dusri taraf wohi deewar 12 mazdooron ke sath aur 5 din ka board ho. Darmiyaan me bara sa jumla ho ke bande dugne, din aadhe. Ye tasveer ultay rishte ko zehen me bitha degi, aur paper me koi student zyada bande dekh kar zyada din nahi likhega.
