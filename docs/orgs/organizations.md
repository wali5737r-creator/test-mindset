Aalami Tanzeemain
Tanzeemain aur Muahide. Duniya ke mulk mil kar kese kaam karte hain

Ye hissa kyun zaroori hai

One paper me GK ke hisse me har saal tanzeemon par sawal ata hai. Kabhi pooch liya jata hai ke UNO ka headquarter kahan hai, kabhi IMF kab bani, kabhi Security Council ke mustaqil members kitne hain. Ye sawal mushkil nahi hote, bas facts boht sare hain aur student unhe aapas me mila deta hai. Geneva, New York, Washington aur Paris, ye chaar sheher har dusri tanzeem ke sath jure hue hain, aur paper hamesha yahin phansata hai. Is lesson me hum har tanzeem ko is tarah parhen ge ke pehle asaan matlab samajh aye ke ye tanzeem hai kis liye, phir us ke fix facts zehen me beth jayen, aur aakhir me wo trap bhi pata chal jaye jo paper lagata hai. Jo student ye tartib pakar le, wo tanzeemon wale sawal kabhi ghalat nahi karega.

Tanzeem hoti kya hai

Pehle ye samajh lo ke tanzeem hai kya. Jab kuch mulk mil kar kisi aik kaam ke liye aik idara bana lein, usay tanzeem kehte hain. Bilkul wese hi jese mohallay ke log mil kar anjuman bana lete hain ke gali ki safai aur pani ka masla sab mil kar hal karenge. Aik mulk akela apne maslay hal kar sakta hai, lekin kuch maslay aisay hote hain jo poori duniya ke hote hain. Jang rukwana, bemari khatam karna, ghareeb mulkon ko qarz dena, tijarat ke qawaaid banana, ye kaam koi aik mulk akela nahi kar sakta. Isi liye mulkon ne mil kar ye bare idaray banaye hain. Har tanzeem ka aik headquarter hota hai jahan us ka daftar hota hai, aik buniyad ki tareekh hoti hai, aur kuch member mulk hote hain. Paper bas yehi teen cheezain ghuma phira kar poochta hai.

UNO. Duniya ki sab se bari tanzeem

Ab sab se bari tanzeem se shuru karte hain, United Nations, jisay short me UN ya UNO kehte hain. Urdu me isay Aqwam-e-Muttahida kehte hain. Ye 1945 me bani. Wajah suno, dusri jang-e-azeem me karoron log mare gaye, sheher ke sheher tabah ho gaye. Jang khatam hui to duniya ke mulkon ne socha ke aisa idara banna chahiye jo ainda jangain rok sake aur mulkon ke jhagre baat cheet se hal hon. America ke sheher San Francisco me 50 mulkon ne mil kar is ke charter, yani is ke buniyadi qanoon, par dastakhat kiye. Phir 24 October 1945 ko ye tanzeem baqaida amal me a gayi. Isi liye har saal 24 October ko UN Day manaya jata hai. Paper me aksar poocha jata hai ke UNO kab amal me ayi, aur jawab 24 October 1945 hai, sirf 1945 likhna kaafi nahi hota jab options me tareekh di gayi ho.

Is ka headquarter America ke sheher New York me hai. Is waqt is ke 193 member mulk hain, yani taqreeban poori duniya. Pakistan 30 September 1947 ko is ka member bana, yani azadi ke dedh mah baad. Ye tareekh bhi paper me ati hai.

UNO ke chhe aham hisse

UNO aik bari imarat ki tarah hai jis ke chhe aham hisse hain. Har hisse ka kaam alag hai, aur paper inhi chhe ko ghuma kar poochta hai.

Pehla hissa General Assembly hai. Ye sab se bari majlis hai. Is me tamam 193 member mulk bethte hain aur har mulk ka sirf aik vote hota hai. America jesa bara mulk ho ya koi chhota sa jazira mulk, vote dono ka aik hi hai. Yehi is ki sab se khaas baat hai.

Dusra hissa Security Council hai, aur suno, ye poore UNO ka sab se taqatwar hissa hai. Is me kul 15 members hote hain. In me se 5 mustaqil, yani permanent members hain, aur 10 ghair mustaqil hote hain jo do saal ke liye chune jate hain. Mustaqil paanch ye hain: America, Russia, China, France aur Britain. In paanchon ke paas aik khaas taqat hai jisay veto power kehte hain. Veto ka matlab hai rok dena. Agar koi faisla ho raha ho aur in paanchon me se aik bhi na kar de, to faisla ruk jata hai, chahe baqi 14 members haan keh dein. Paper ki jaan yehi sawal hai, veto wale paanch mulk konse hain aur veto ka matlab kya hai. Aik aur baat, Security Council hi woh hissa hai jis ke faislay manna har member ke liye lazmi hota hai, aur yehi jang rukwane ke liye aman foj, yani peacekeeping force, bhejta hai.

Teesra hissa Secretariat hai. Ye UNO ka daftar hai jahan roz ka kaam hota hai, files chalti hain, meetings hoti hain. Is ke sarbarah ko Secretary General kehte hain, jo poori tanzeem ka sab se bara ohda hai. Is waqt Secretary General Antonio Guterres hain, jo Portugal ke hain. Secretary General paanch saal ke liye chuna jata hai.

Chautha hissa International Court of Justice hai, yani Aalami Adalat-e-Insaaf. Yahan mulkon ke darmiyan jhagre sunay jate hain. Ab wo baat jo paper har dafa poochta hai aur aadhe students ghalat karte hain. Ye adalat New York me nahi hai. Ye Netherlands ke sheher The Hague me hai. UNO ke saare hisse New York me hain siwa is adalat ke. Ye aik line apne zehen me pathar par likh lo, International Court of Justice, The Hague, Netherlands.

Paanchwa hissa ECOSOC hai, jis ka poora naam Economic and Social Council hai. Ye duniya ke maashi aur samaji maslon par kaam karta hai, ghareebi, taleem, sehat jese mamlaat.

Chhetha hissa Trusteeship Council hai. Ye un ilaqon ki dekh bhaal ke liye bana tha jo kisi mulk ke qabze se nikal kar UNO ke hawale hue the. Ab us ka kaam taqreeban khatam ho chuka hai, lekin chhe hison ki list me is ka naam ata hai, is liye yaad rakho.

UNO ke mashhoor idaray

UNO ke neeche kuch khaas idaray kaam karte hain, har aik aik hi kaam ka mahir hai. In ke headquarter alag alag shehron me hain, aur paper inhi shehron me phansata hai, is liye har idare ke sath us ka sheher bhi yaad karo.

WHO, World Health Organization, sehat ki tanzeem hai. Urdu me isay Aalami Idara-e-Sehat kehte hain. Ye 1948 me bani aur is ka headquarter Geneva, Switzerland me hai. Duniya bhar ki bemarion se larna, vaccine ke programs chalana, ye is ka kaam hai. Corona ke dino me tum ne is ka naam har khabar me suna tha.

UNESCO taleem, science aur saqafat ki tanzeem hai. Ye 1945 me bani aur is ka headquarter Paris, France me hai. Jo jagahain duniya ki virsa, yani World Heritage, kahlati hain, un ki list yehi tanzeem banati hai. Mohenjo Daro aur Taxila Pakistan me UNESCO ki list me shamil hain.

UNICEF bachon ke liye kaam karta hai. Ye 1946 me bana. Bachon ki sehat, taleem aur khurak is ka kaam hai. Polio ke qatray pilane wali teams ka naam tum ne UNICEF ke sath suna hoga.

FAO khurak aur ziraat ki tanzeem hai. Is ka headquarter Rome, Italy me hai. Duniya me bhook khatam karna aur kheeti bari ko behtar karna is ka kaam hai.

ILO mazdooron ki tanzeem hai. Ye sab se purani idaron me se hai aur Geneva me hai. Mazdooron ke huqooq, kaam ke ghante, mazdoori ke qawaaid, ye is ka kaam hai.

UNHCR mahajireen, yani refugees, ke liye kaam karta hai. Jo log jang ya zulm ki wajah se apna mulk chor kar dusre mulk chale jate hain, un ki madad ye idara karta hai. Pakistan me Afghan mahajireen ke mamle me is ka naam ata rehta hai.

IMF. Paison wali tanzeem

Ab us tanzeem ki baat jis ka naam Pakistan me har dusre din khabron me ata hai, IMF, International Monetary Fund. Is ka asaan matlab samjho. Jab kisi mulk ke paas dollars khatam ho jayen, wo cheezain bahir se na khareed sake, to wo IMF ke paas jata hai. IMF usay qarz deta hai aur sath maashi mashware bhi deta hai ke kharche kese kam karo, tax kese barhao. Bilkul wese hi jese koi shakhs mushkil waqt me bank se qarz leta hai aur bank shartein lagata hai.

IMF ka faisla 1944 me America ki aik jagah Bretton Woods me hone wali conference me hua, aur 1945 me ye baqaida amal me aya. Is ka headquarter Washington DC, America me hai. Is waqt is ke 190 members hain. Is ki aik khaas currency bhi hai jisay SDR kehte hain, Special Drawing Rights. Ye koi asli note nahi hota, bas IMF ke hisaab kitab ki ikai hai. Paper me SDR ka poora naam aur IMF ka headquarter boht ata hai. Pakistan kai dafa IMF ke programs le chuka hai, aur har program ke sath qarz ki qistain aur shartein jurri hoti hain. IMF aur World Bank dono Washington me hain aur dono 1944 ki Bretton Woods conference se jure hain, is liye in dono ko judwa idaray bhi kaha jata hai. Ye jor paper ka pasandida trap hai.

World Bank

World Bank bhi 1944 me bana aur is ka headquarter bhi Washington DC me hai. Ab IMF aur World Bank ka farq saaf kar lo, kyunke paper yahin confuse karta hai. IMF chhoti muddat ke liye qarz deta hai jab mulk ki tijarat ka hisaab bigar jaye, yani fori mushkil door karne ke liye. World Bank lambi muddat ke liye qarz deta hai, taraqqi ke kaamon ke liye, jese dam banana, sadak banana, school aur hospital banana. World Bank ka sab se mashhoor hissa IBRD hai. To yaad rakho, fori maashi mushkil, IMF. Lamba taraqqiyati kaam, World Bank.

WTO. Tijarat ke qawaaid wali tanzeem

WTO, World Trade Organization, aalmi tijarat ki tanzeem hai. Ye 1995 me bani aur is ka headquarter Geneva me hai. Is ka kaam hai mulkon ke darmiyan tijarat ke qawaaid banana aur tijarati jhagre hal karna, taake aik mulk dusre ki cheezon par bila wajah pabandi ya zyada tax na laga sake. Is se pehle yehi kaam GATT nam ke muahide ke tehat hota tha jo 1948 se chala araha tha. WTO ne GATT ki jagah li hai. Paper me ye sawal ata hai ke WTO ne kis ki jagah li, jawab hai GATT.

NATO. Fouji ittehad

NATO aik fouji ittehad hai. Ye 1949 me bana aur is ka headquarter Brussels, Belgium me hai. Is waqt is ke 32 member mulk hain, jin me America, Britain, France, Germany aur Turkiye shamil hain. Is ki sab se mashhoor shart Article 5 hai. Is ka matlab hai ke agar kisi aik member mulk par hamla ho, to wo hamla tamam members par hamla samjha jayega, aur sab mil kar us ki madad karenge. Paper me NATO ka headquarter aur Article 5 boht poocha jata hai. Ye tanzeem UNO se alag hai, ye sirf fouji muahida hai, is ka UNO se koi lena dena nahi.

OIC. Musalman mulkon ki tanzeem

OIC, Organization of Islamic Cooperation, musalman mulkon ki tanzeem hai. Ye 1969 me bani. Is ki buniyad ki wajah aik dukh bhara waqia tha. 1969 me Masjid-e-Aqsa ko aag lagayi gayi, jis ke baad musalman mulkon ne mehsoos kiya ke un ka aik mushtarka idara hona chahiye. Is ki pehli summit Morocco ke sheher Rabat me hui. Is ka headquarter Saudi Arab ke sheher Jeddah me hai. Is waqt is ke 57 members hain. Ye UNO ke baad duniya ki dusri sab se bari tanzeem hai. Paper me OIC ki buniyad ka saal 1969, headquarter Jeddah, aur members 57, ye teenon facts bar bar ate hain. Pakistan is ka buniyadi member hai.

SAARC. Apne ilaqay ki tanzeem

SAARC, South Asian Association for Regional Cooperation, hamare apne ilaqay, yani South Asia, ke mulkon ki tanzeem hai. Ye 1985 me Bangladesh ke sheher Dhaka me bani. Is ke kul 8 members hain: Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Nepal, Bhutan, Maldives aur Afghanistan. Is ka headquarter Nepal ke sheher Kathmandu me hai. Paper me SAARC ke members ki tadaad 8 aur headquarter Kathmandu boht ata hai. Aik bareek baat yaad rakho, Afghanistan 2007 me shamil hua tha, us se pehle members 7 the. Purani kitabon me 7 likha mil sakta hai, lekin sahi jawab is waqt 8 hai.

ASEAN

ASEAN South East Asia ke mulkon ki tanzeem hai. Ye 1967 me bani aur is ka headquarter Indonesia ke sheher Jakarta me hai. Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand, Singapore aur Philippines is ke mashhoor members hain. Ye ilaqa tijarat me boht tez taraqqi kar raha hai, is liye is tanzeem ka naam khabron me ata rehta hai.

SCO. Shanghai wali tanzeem

SCO, Shanghai Cooperation Organization, China aur Russia ki sarbarahi me bani hui tanzeem hai. Is ka headquarter China ke sheher Beijing me hai. Pakistan 2017 me is ka mustaqil, yani permanent member bana. India bhi usi saal member bana. Paper me ye sawal boht ata hai ke Pakistan SCO ka mustaqil member kab bana, jawab hai 2017.

EU. Europe ki tanzeem

EU, European Union, Europe ke mulkon ki tanzeem hai. Is ke members ne mil kar aik hi currency apna li hai jisay euro kehte hain, aur aik dusre ke mulkon me baghair visa ke ana jana asan hai. Is ka markaz Brussels, Belgium me hai. Britain 2020 me is se nikal gaya tha, is waqie ko Brexit kehte hain. Ye lafz paper me ata hai.

Commonwealth

Commonwealth un mulkon ki tanzeem hai jo kabhi Britain ke qabze me rahe the. Pakistan azadi ke baad is ka member tha, phir 1972 me is se nikal gaya, aur 1989 me dobara shamil ho gaya. Paper me yehi sawal ata hai ke Pakistan Commonwealth me wapas kab shamil hua, jawab hai 1989.

Red Cross aur Red Crescent

Aakhir me aik aisi tanzeem jo na hukumat hai na mulkon ki majlis, Red Cross. Ye 1863 me Geneva me bani. Is ke bani Henry Dunant the. Is ka kaam hai jang aur afat me zakhmion aur musibat zadon ki madad karna, baghair kisi farq ke. Musalman mulkon me isay Red Crescent kehte hain, Urdu me Hilal-e-Ahmer. Pakistan ki Hilal-e-Ahmer bhi yehi kaam karti hai. Paper me Henry Dunant ka naam aur 1863 ka saal ata hai.

Fix facts ki recap

Ab aakhir me wo facts aik saath dohra lo jo paper me sab se zyada ate hain. UNO 24 October 1945 ko amal me ayi, headquarter New York, members 193. Security Council ke kul 15 members, 5 mustaqil jin ke paas veto hai: America, Russia, China, France, Britain. International Court of Justice The Hague, Netherlands me hai, New York me nahi. WHO Geneva, UNESCO Paris, FAO Rome. IMF aur World Bank dono Washington me aur dono 1944 ki Bretton Woods conference se jure. IMF ke members 190, us ki khaas currency SDR hai. WTO 1995 me bani, GATT ki jagah li, headquarter Geneva. NATO 1949, headquarter Brussels, members 32, mashhoor shart Article 5. OIC 1969, headquarter Jeddah, members 57. SAARC 1985, headquarter Kathmandu, members 8. Pakistan SCO ka mustaqil member 2017 me bana. Pakistan Commonwealth me wapas 1989 me shamil hua. Red Cross 1863 me bani, bani Henry Dunant.

Tasveer ka khayal

Is lesson ke liye aik tasveer boht kaam ki hogi. Duniya ke naqshe par un shehron par nishan lage hon jahan bare idaron ke headquarter hain: New York par UNO, Washington par IMF aur World Bank, Geneva par WHO aur WTO, Paris par UNESCO, Rome par FAO, Brussels par NATO, Jeddah par OIC aur Kathmandu par SAARC. Student aik nazar me dekh le ke konsa idara kis sheher me hai, kyunke paper ka aadha khel hi shehron ka hai.
