Mashhoor Muahide aur Agreements
Muahide aur Agreements. Jab mulk kagaz par waada karte hain

Ye hissa kyun zaroori hai

Tanzeemon ke sath sath one paper me muahidon par bhi har saal sawal ata hai. Indus Waters Treaty kab hua, Simla Agreement me kya tay hua, Tashkent me kon saalis tha, ye sawal ghuma phira kar ate rehte hain. Muahidon ka mushkil hissa ye hai ke in ke naam, saal aur jagah aapas me mil jate hain. Karachi, Tashkent, Simla, Lahore, ye sheher aur 1960, 1966, 1972, 1999, ye saal student ke zehen me garbar ho jate hain. Is lesson me har muahide ko aik kahani ki tarah parhen ge: pehle ye ke jhagra kya tha, phir muahida kab aur kahan hua, phir us me tay kya hua, aur aakhir me wo baat jo paper poochta hai. Kahani yaad reh jati hai, sirf tareekh ratni nahi parti.

Muahida hota kya hai

Pehle lafz saaf kar lo. Jab do ya zyada mulk kisi maslay par baith kar baat karte hain aur phir kagaz par likh kar dastakhat kar dete hain ke hum is baat ke paband hain, us kagaz ko muahida kehte hain. English me isay treaty kehte hain. Agreement bhi taqreeban yehi cheez hai, aam tor par agreement muahide se halka aur chhota samjha jata hai, lekin paper me dono lafz aik hi maane me ate hain. Jab koi muahida sirf ailan ki surat me ho, dastakhaton ke sath lekin qanooni pabandi halki ho, to usay declaration kehte hain, jese Tashkent Declaration aur Lahore Declaration. Ye bareek farq zehen me rakho, paper kabhi kabhi naam par hi phansa deta hai.

Aik aur baat. Jab do mulkon ke darmiyan koi teesra mulk ya idara beech me par kar sulah karwaye, usay saalisi kehte hain, English me mediation. Kai muahidon me saalis ka naam paper ka fix sawal hota hai. Indus Waters Treaty me World Bank saalis tha, Tashkent me Soviet Union saalis tha. Ye joray khaas taur par yaad karna.

Indus Waters Treaty 1960. Daryaaon ki taqseem

Ye Pakistan ke liye sab se aham muahida hai aur paper me sab se zyada ata hai. Kahani ye hai. Azadi ke waqt Punjab ke darya dono mulkon me bat gaye. Daryaaon ke headworks, yani jahan se nahrain nikalti hain, un me se kuch India me reh gaye aur khet Pakistan me. 1948 me India ne Pakistan ki taraf ane wala pani rok diya, nahrain sookh gayin, faslain tabah hone lagin. Ye masla barhta gaya. Phir World Bank beech me aya aur nau saal ki lambi baat cheet ke baad 19 September 1960 ko Karachi me muahida hua. Pakistan ki taraf se President Ayub Khan ne aur India ki taraf se Prime Minister Jawaharlal Nehru ne dastakhat kiye. World Bank ne bhi is par dastakhat kiye kyunke wo saalis tha.

Ab wo baat jo is muahide ki jaan hai aur paper har dafa poochta hai, chhe daryaaon ki taqseem. Punjab ke chhe bare darya hain. Teen mashriqi darya India ko de diye gaye: Ravi, Beas aur Sutlej. In teenon ke pani par India ka poora haq tay hua. Teen maghribi darya Pakistan ko mile: Indus, yani Darya-e-Sindh, Jhelum aur Chenab. In teenon ka pani Pakistan ka haq tay hua, bas India ko in par mehdood istemal ki ijazat mili, jese bijli banana waghera, lekin pani rokna ya rukawat dalna mana hai. Is taqseem ko yaad karne ka asaan tareeqa ye hai ke naqshe par dekho, jo teen darya mashriq ki taraf hain wo India ke, jo teen maghrib ki taraf hain wo Pakistan ke. Maghribi teenon me Indus sab se bara hai, isiliye muahide ka naam hi Indus Waters Treaty para. Paper me aksar options me Chenab ko India ki taraf aur Ravi ko Pakistan ki taraf likh kar phansaya jata hai, is liye ye taqseem pathar par likh lo: mashriqi teen Ravi, Beas, Sutlej India ke; maghribi teen Indus, Jhelum, Chenab Pakistan ke.

Tashkent Declaration 1966. Jang ke baad sulah

1965 me Pakistan aur India ke darmiyan bari jang hui. Jang band hui to sulah ki baat cheet ke liye dono mulk Soviet Union ke sheher Tashkent me ikatthe hue. January 1966 me muahida hua jisay Tashkent Declaration kehte hain. Pakistan ki taraf se President Ayub Khan aur India ki taraf se Prime Minister Lal Bahadur Shastri sharik hue. Saalis Soviet Union ke Prime Minister Alexei Kosygin the. Ye saalis wala sawal paper ka fix sawal hai. Muahide me tay hua ke dono mulkon ki fojain wapas un jagahon par chali jayengi jahan jang se pehle thi, aur maslay baat cheet se hal honge.

Is muahide ke sath aik mashhoor aur dukh bhara waqia jura hai. Muahide ke agle hi din, 11 January 1966 ko, Lal Bahadur Shastri ka Tashkent me hi achanak inteqal ho gaya. Paper me ye bhi poocha jata hai ke Shastri ki wafat kahan hui, jawab hai Tashkent.

Simla Agreement 1972. LoC ka naam kese para

1971 ki jang ke baad 2 July 1972 ko India ke sheher Simla me muahida hua. Pakistan ki taraf se President Zulfikar Ali Bhutto aur India ki taraf se Prime Minister Indira Gandhi ne dastakhat kiye. Is muahide ki do baatein paper ki jaan hain.

Pehli baat, Kashmir me jo purani ceasefire line thi, us ka naam badal kar Line of Control, yani LoC, rakh diya gaya. Yaad rakho, LoC koi sarhad nahi hai, ye sirf woh lakeer hai jahan dono fojain ruki hui hain. Paper me aksar poocha jata hai ke ceasefire line ko LoC ka naam kis muahide me mila, jawab hai Simla Agreement 1972.

Dusri baat, dono mulkon ne tay kiya ke apne tamam maslay, bashamool Kashmir, do-tarfa baat cheet se hal karenge, yani kisi teesre ko beech me nahi layenge. Ye do-tarfa wali shart is muahide ki pehchan hai. Jang bandi ke qaidi bhi is ke baad wapas hue.

Lahore Declaration 1999. Bus wala safar

February 1999 me India ke Prime Minister Atal Bihari Vajpayee bus par sawaar ho kar Lahore aye. Ye aik tareekhi safar tha, jisay bus diplomacy bhi kehte hain. Lahore me un ka istaqbal Prime Minister Nawaz Sharif ne kiya aur dono ne Lahore Declaration par dastakhat kiye. Is me tay hua ke dono mulk aman se rahenge, Kashmir samait tamam maslay baat cheet se hal karenge aur atom bomb ki jang ka khatra kam karne ke liye aitemaad barhaya jayega. Isi safar me Vajpayee Minar-e-Pakistan bhi gaye the, ye baat bhi paper me ati hai. Afsos ke kuch maah baad Kargil ki jang ho gayi aur ye amal ruk gaya, lekin muahida apni jagah tareekh ka hissa hai.

Geneva Conventions 1949. Jang ke bhi qawaaid hain

Ye muahida mulkon ke darmiyan sarhad ka nahi, jang ke doran insani rawaiye ka hai. 1949 me Switzerland ke sheher Geneva me ye conventions bane. In me tay hua ke jang ke doran zakhmion ka ilaaj hoga, jo sipahi hathyar dal de ya girftar ho jaye us ke sath acha salook hoga, usay mara nahi jayega, aam shehrion ko nishana nahi banaya jayega. Qaidi ke huqooq inhi conventions me likhe hain. Red Cross ka idara inhi qawaaid ki nigrani se jura hai. Paper me aksar poocha jata hai ke jang ke qaidi ke huqooq kis muahide me hain, jawab hai Geneva Conventions 1949.

NPT 1968. Atom bomb na phailane ka muahida

NPT ka poora naam Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty hai. Ye 1968 me bana. Is ka maqsad tha ke atom bomb mazeed mulkon tak na phailay, jin paanch mulkon ke paas pehle se hai bas unhi tak rahe. Duniya ke zyada tar mulkon ne is par dastakhat kar diye. Pakistan ne is par dastakhat nahi kiye. Wajah ghair-janibdarana tor par ye hai ke Pakistan ka maanna hai ke ye muahida ghair munsefana hai, kyunke ye kuch mulkon ko atom bomb rakhne ki ijazat deta hai aur baqion ko rokta hai, aur Pakistan apni hifazat ke paish-e-nazar is pabandi ko us waqt tak qubool nahi karta jab tak ilaqa is se pak na ho. India ne bhi is par dastakhat nahi kiye. Paper me ye sawal ata hai ke Pakistan ne NPT par dastakhat kyun nahi kiye, aur ye bhi ke NPT kab bana, 1968.

Paris Agreement 2015. Mausam ka muahida

Ye muahida kisi jang ya sarhad ka nahi, poori duniya ke mausam ka hai. December 2015 me France ke sheher Paris me duniya ke taqreeban tamam mulk ikatthe hue aur tay kiya ke duniya ka darja hararat barhne se rokenge, factoryon aur gariyon ka dhuan kam karenge, warna garmi, sailaab aur khushk saali poori duniya ko nuqsan pohchayegi. Pakistan bhi is ka hissa hai. Paper me Paris Agreement ka saal 2015 aur maqsad mausamiyati tabdeeli, yani climate change, poocha jata hai.

Durand Line Agreement 1893. Sab se purana muahida

Ye is list ka sab se purana muahida hai. 1893 me British India aur Afghanistan ke darmiyan ye muahida hua. Afghanistan ki taraf se Ameer Abdur Rahman Khan the aur British ki taraf se Sir Mortimer Durand, jin ke naam par is lakeer ko Durand Line kehte hain. Yehi lakeer aaj Pakistan aur Afghanistan ki sarhad hai. Is ki poori tafseel borders wale portion me di gayi hai, yahan bas ye yaad rakho ke Durand Line koi muahida nahi jo aaj kal hua ho, ye 1893 ki baat hai aur ye angrezon ke dor ki sarhad hai.

CPEC 2015. China ke sath maashi raahdari

CPEC ka poora naam China Pakistan Economic Corridor hai. Ye 2015 me shuru hua. Ye aik muahide se barh kar bare maashi mansubon ka majmua hai. China ke sheher Kashgar se lekar Pakistan ke Gwadar tak sadkon, railway aur bijli ke mansubon ka jaal bichaya gaya, taake China ko samandar tak seedha rasta mile aur Pakistan me bijli, sadak aur rozgar barhe. Gwadar is ka dil hai. Paper me CPEC ka aaghaz 2015, Kashgar se Gwadar tak ka rasta, aur is ka China ke bare One Belt One Road mansube ka hissa hona, ye baatein ati hain.

Treaty of Versailles 1919. Pehli jang ka ikhtitam

Ye muahida Pakistan se seedha nahi jurta, lekin one paper me duniya ki tareekh ke hisse me ata hai. 1919 me France ke sheher Versailles me ye muahida hua jis se pehli jang-e-azeem ka baqaida ikhtitam hua. Haar jane wale Germany par sakht shartein lagayi gayin, us ki foj chhoti kar di gayi, ilaqay cheen liye gaye aur bhari tazwana, yani jurmana, dala gaya. Tareekh parhne wale kehte hain ke inhi sakht sharton ki narazgi ne dusri jang-e-azeem ki buniyad rakhi. Paper me is ka saal 1919 aur Germany ke sath hona poocha jata hai.

Camp David 1978. Misr aur Israel ki pehli sulah

1978 me America ke Camp David nam ke maqam par America ke President Jimmy Carter ne Misr aur Israel ko bithaya. Misr ki taraf se President Anwar Sadat aur Israel ki taraf se Prime Minister Menachem Begin the. Is mulaqat ke natije me 1979 me Misr aur Israel ke darmiyan baqaida sulah ka muahida hua. Misr wo pehla Arab mulk bana jis ne Israel ke sath sulah ki. Paper me Camp David ka saal 1978, saalis America, aur Misr ka pehla Arab mulk hona, ye baatein ati hain.

Fix facts ki recap

Ab aakhir me har muahide ki aik aik line zehen me bandh lo. Indus Waters Treaty 1960, Karachi, Ayub Khan aur Nehru, saalis World Bank, mashriqi teen darya Ravi, Beas, Sutlej India ko, maghribi teen Indus, Jhelum, Chenab Pakistan ko. Tashkent Declaration 1966, saalis Soviet PM Kosygin, Shastri ki wafat bhi Tashkent me. Simla Agreement 1972, Bhutto aur Indira Gandhi, ceasefire line ka naam LoC hua, maslay do-tarfa hal karne ki shart. Lahore Declaration 1999, Vajpayee bus par Lahore aye. Geneva Conventions 1949, jang ke qaidi aur zakhmion ke huqooq. NPT 1968, atom bomb na phailane ka muahida, Pakistan ne dastakhat nahi kiye. Paris Agreement 2015, mausamiyati tabdeeli. Durand Line 1893, Ameer Abdur Rahman aur Sir Mortimer Durand. CPEC 2015, Kashgar se Gwadar. Treaty of Versailles 1919, pehli jang ka ikhtitam, Germany par sakht shartein. Camp David 1978, America ki saalisi me Misr aur Israel ki sulah ki buniyad.

Tasveer ka khayal

Is lesson ke liye do tasveerein kaam ki hongi. Pehli tasveer Punjab ke chhe daryaaon ki ho, mashriq ke teen darya aik rang me aur maghrib ke teen dusre rang me, taake Indus Waters Treaty ki taqseem aik nazar me samajh ajaye. Dusri tasveer aik time line ho jis par 1893 ki Durand Line se lekar 2015 ke Paris Agreement aur CPEC tak tamam muahide saal ki tartib se lage hon, taake student ko har muahida apne saal ke sath jorta hua nazar aye.
