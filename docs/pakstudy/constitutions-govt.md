Aaeen aur Hukumat
Pakistan ke teen aaeen, hukumat ka dhancha, aur sadar aur wazeer-e-azam ki tartib

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

One paper me Pak Studies ke hisse me aaeen par har saal sawal ata hai. Kabhi poochte hain 1973 ka aaeen kab nafiz hua, kabhi poochte hain pehla aaeen kab bana, kabhi Objectives Resolution ki tareekh aur kabhi Senate ki seats ki tadaad. Ye saare sawal seedhe seedhe hain aur in ke number pakke hote hain, bas shart ye hai ke kahani tartib se zehen me bethi ho. Jo student aaeenon ko aik kahani ki tarah samajh le, ke pehle kya hua, phir kya hua aur kyun hua, wo ye sawal kabhi ghalat nahi karega. Is lesson me hum 1947 se shuru karke aaj tak ka poora safar chalte hain.

Pehla setup, 1947

14 August 1947 ko Pakistan bana. Naye mulk ke paas apna koi aaeen nahi tha, is liye shuru me Bharat ka 1935 wala Government of India Act hi chalta raha, kuch tabdeeliyon ke sath. Mulk ke pehle Governor General Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah bane aur pehle Wazeer-e-Azam Liaquat Ali Khan. Governor General us waqt riyasat ka sarbarah tha, jese aaj ka sadar hota hai, aur wazeer-e-azam hukumat chalata tha.

Is ke sath hi Aaeen Saaz Assembly, yani Constituent Assembly, bhi bani. Is assembly ke do kaam the. Aik, mulk ke liye qanoon banana, yani ye parliament bhi thi. Dusra, Pakistan ka apna aaeen likhna. Quaid-e-Azam is assembly ke pehle sadar muntakhab hue aur 11 August 1947 ko unhon ne is se apna mashhoor khitaab kiya, jisme unhon ne kaha tha ke tamam shehri barabar hain aur mazhab har shakhs ka apna zaati mamla hai. 11 September 1948 ko Quaid-e-Azam ka inteqal ho gaya aur aaeen banane ka kaam abhi shuru hi hua tha. 16 October 1951 ko Liaquat Ali Khan Rawalpindi me aik jalse ke doran shaheed kar diye gaye. Naya mulk apne do sab se bare rehnumaon se mehroom ho chuka tha, aur yahi aik bari wajah hai ke aaeen banne me itni der lagi.

Objectives Resolution, aaeen ki buniyad

Ab us dastaveez ki baat jo poore aaeen ki buniyad hai. 7 March 1949 ko Liaquat Ali Khan ne Aaeen Saaz Assembly me Objectives Resolution, yani Qarardad-e-Maqasid, pesh ki aur 12 March 1949 ko ye manzoor ho gayi. Paper me is ki tareekh har dusre saal ati hai, isay pakka yaad kar lo: 12 March 1949.

Is qarardad me kya tha? Khulasa ye hai. Poori kainat par ikhtedar Allah Taala ka hai aur Pakistan ke log us ikhtedar ko Allah ki di hui amanat samajh kar istemal karenge. Mulk jamhoori usoolon par chalega. Musalmanon ko is qabil banaya jayega ke wo apni zindagi Quran aur Sunnat ke mutabiq guzar saken. Bunyadi huqooq ki zamanat hogi. Aqaliton ko apne mazhab par chalne aur apni saqafat barqarar rakhne ki poori azadi hogi. Adliya azad hogi.

Ye qarardad sirf aik taqreer nahi thi. Baad me banne wale teeno aaeenon ke shuru me ye preamble, yani dibache, ke tor par shamil rahi. Aur 1985 me isay baqaida aaeen ka hissa bana kar Article 2-A bana diya gaya. Isi liye isay Pakistan ke aaeen ki buniyad aur rooh kaha jata hai. Paper me ye sawal bhi ata hai ke Objectives Resolution ko aaeen ka hissa kab banaya gaya, to jawab hai 1985 me, Article 2-A ki surat me.

1956 ka pehla aaeen

Aaeen banne me pure nau saal lag gaye. Wajah ye thi ke bare bare sawalon par ikhtelaf tha. Mulk ka nizam parlimani ho ya sadarti, sobon ko kitni khudmukhtari mile, mashriqi aur maghribi Pakistan ki numaindagi kesay barabar ho, aur Urdu aur Bengali ka mamla kya ho. Aakhir 23 March 1956 ko Pakistan ka pehla aaeen nafiz ho gaya.

Is aaeen ki khaas baatein ye hain. Pakistan ka naam Islamic Republic of Pakistan rakha gaya, yani Islami Jamhuria Pakistan. Nizam parlimani tha, asal ikhtedar wazeer-e-azam aur cabinet ke paas. Parliament aik aiwan par mushtamil thi, sirf National Assembly thi, Senate us waqt nahi bana tha. Mulk wafaqi tha, yani ikhtedar markaz aur sobon me taqseem tha. Iskander Mirza, jo is se pehle aakhri Governor General the, mulk ke pehle Sadar bane, kyunke aaeen ke baad Governor General ka ohda khatam ho gaya aur us ki jagah Sadar ka ohda aa gaya.

23 March ki tareekh zehen me is liye bhi bitha lo ke ye din do wajah se Pakistan Day hai. Aik, 23 March 1940 ko Lahore me Qarardad-e-Pakistan manzoor hui thi. Dusri, 23 March 1956 ko pehla aaeen nafiz hua. Paper me aksar dono me se koi aik wajah option me de kar phansaya jata hai, asal me dono hi durust hain.

Lekin ye aaeen sirf dhai saal chala. 7 October 1958 ko Sadar Iskander Mirza ne aaeen mansookh kar diya, assembly tor di aur mulk me pehla martial law laga diya. Unhon ne General Ayub Khan ko Chief Martial Law Administrator banaya. Lekin sirf bees din baad, 27 October 1958 ko, Ayub Khan ne Iskander Mirza ko hata kar unhe mulk se bahar bhej diya aur khud Sadar ban gaye.

1962 ka dusra aaeen

Ayub Khan ne apna aaeen banaya. 1 March 1962 ko is ka elan hua aur 8 June 1962 ko ye nafiz ho gaya. Ye aaeen 1956 wale se bilkul ulta tha. Nizam sadarti tha, yani saara ikhtedar Sadar ke haath me tha aur wazeer-e-azam ka ohda hi khatam kar diya gaya tha. Parliament phir bhi aik aiwan wali thi.

Is aaeen ki sab se mashhoor cheez Basic Democracies ka nizam tha. Mulk me Union Council ki satah par assi hazar bunyadi jamhooriyat ke members, jinhe BD members kehte the, muntakhab hue aur yahi assi hazar log sadar ko chunte the. Aik aur baat jo paper me ati hai: is aaeen me pehle mulk ka naam sirf Republic of Pakistan rakha gaya tha, Islam ka lafz hata diya gaya tha. Phir 1963 me pehli aaeeni tarmeem ke zariye naam dobara Islamic Republic of Pakistan kar diya gaya.

1965 ki jang, mashriqi Pakistan me bechaini aur awaami ehtijaj ke baad Ayub Khan kamzor par gaye. 25 March 1969 ko unhon ne iqtedar General Yahya Khan ke hawale kar diya. Yahya Khan ne dusra martial law lagaya aur 1962 ka aaeen bhi khatam ho gaya. Yun Pakistan ke do aaeen ban kar toot chuke the.

1970 ke intikhabat aur 1971 ki jang

Yahya Khan ne aik naik aur tareekhi kaam kiya, unhon ne mulk me pehle aam intikhabat karaye. 7 December 1970 ko ye intikhabat hue. Ye Pakistan ki tareekh ke pehle intikhabat the jo aik admi aik vote ki buniyad par hue the. Mashriqi Pakistan se Sheikh Mujibur Rahman ki Awami League ne wazeh aksariyat jeet li aur maghribi Pakistan me Zulfikar Ali Bhutto ki Pakistan Peoples Party sab se bari jamaat ban kar ubhri.

Lekin jeetne wali jamaat ko iqtedar na mila. Baat cheet nakam hui, March 1971 me mashriqi Pakistan me foji operation shuru ho gaya aur khana jangi phoot pari. December 1971 me Bharat ke sath baqaida jang chhir gayi. 16 December 1971 ko Dhaka me Pakistani fauj ne hathyar daal diye. Mashriqi Pakistan alag hokar Bangladesh ban gaya aur nabbe hazar se zyada Pakistani foji aur shehri Bharat ke qaidi ban gaye. Ye Pakistan ki tareekh ka sab se bara sanha tha.

20 December 1971 ko Yahya Khan ko hatna para aur Zulfikar Ali Bhutto mulk ke Sadar aur Chief Martial Law Administrator bane. July 1972 me Bhutto ne Bharat ki Wazeer-e-Azam Indira Gandhi ke sath Simla Muahida kiya, jis ke tehet jang bandi wali lakeer ko Line of Control, yani LoC, ka naam diya gaya aur qaidiun ki wapsi ki raah hamwar hui. Ab sab se bara kaam aik naya aur mutafiqqa aaeen banana tha.

1973 ka aaeen, sab se aham hissa

Ab is lesson ke sab se aham hisse par aao, kyunke one paper ke aaeen wale sawalon me se aadhe se zyada sirf isi aaeen se ate hain. Tamam siyasi jamaaton ke rehnumaon par mushtamil aik aaeen saaz committee ne mahinon ghaur karke masudah tayyar kiya. 10 April 1973 ko National Assembly ne ye aaeen mutafiqqa tor par manzoor kiya aur 14 August 1973 ko ye nafiz ho gaya. Tareekhon ka ye jora pakka yaad kar lo: manzoori 10 April 1973, nifaaz 14 August 1973. Paper me aksar April wali tareekh de kar August wala jawab ghalat karaya jata hai.

Is aaeen ki khaas baatein aik aik karke samjho.

Pehli baat, ye aik likha hua aaeen hai aur Pakistan ka sab se lamba chalne wala aaeen hai. Aaj bhi yehi aaeen nafiz hai, go ke is me kai tarameem ho chuki hain.

Dusri baat, nizam parlimani hai. Hukumat wazeer-e-azam aur us ki cabinet chalati hai. Sadar riyasat ka sarbarah hai lekin rozmarra ki hukumat us ka kaam nahi. Nifaaz ke waqt Zulfikar Ali Bhutto pehle Wazeer-e-Azam bane aur Fazal Ilahi Chaudhry Sadar bane.

Teesri baat, mulk wafaqi hai. Ikhtedar markaz aur chaar sobon, Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa aur Balochistan, ke darmiyan taqseem hai.

Chauthi baat, Article 2 ke tehet Islam Pakistan ka riyasati mazhab qarar paya aur mulk ka naam Islamic Republic of Pakistan rakha gaya. Objectives Resolution is aaeen ka preamble bani aur baad me 1985 me Article 2-A ban kar is ka baqaida hissa ban gayi.

Paanchvi baat, aur ye bohat aham hai, is aaeen ne pehli dafa parliament ko do aiwanon wala banaya. Aiwan-e-Bala ko Senate kehte hain, jisme tamam sobon ko barabar numaindagi milti hai. Aiwan-e-Zeerin ko National Assembly kehte hain, jiske members awaam seedha vote de kar chunte hain. Paper me ye sawal pakka ata hai ke Senate kis aaeen me pehli dafa bana, jawab hai 1973 ke aaeen me.

Chhati baat, Article 251 ke tehet Urdu ko Pakistan ki qaumi zubaan qarar diya gaya. Sath ye bhi likha gaya ke sarkari kaamon me English ka istemal pandrah saal tak jari reh sakta hai, jab tak Urdu us ki jagah na le le.

Saatvi baat, shehriyon ke bunyadi huqooq ki zamanat di gayi aur adliya ko azad rakha gaya. Aathvi baat, sadar aur wazeer-e-azam ke liye musalman hona shart rakha gaya. Aur nawi baat, ye aaeen sakht qism ka hai, is me tarmeem aasan nahi, dono aiwanon ki do tihai aksariyat chahiye hoti hai.

Aik mashhoor tarmeem bhi yaad kar lo. 1974 me dusri aaeeni tarmeem ke zariye Qadiyani jamaat ko aaeen me ghair Muslim aqaliyat qarar diya gaya. Aur 2010 ki atharvi tarmeem bhi bohat mashhoor hai, jis ke tehet sobon ko zyada khudmukhtari mili aur sadar ke bohat se ikhtedarat wazeer-e-azam ko wapas mil gaye.

Is list ke khaali hisse bhi samajh lo, kyunke yahi asal kahani hai. 1958 me Ayub Khan ne wazeer-e-azam ka ohda hi khatam kar diya tha, is liye 1958 se 1971 tak koi wazeer-e-azam nahi raha, sirf aakhir me Nurul Amin chand din ke liye bane. 1973 ke aaeen ke baad ye ohda wapas aya aur Bhutto pehle wazeer-e-azam bane. Nawaz Sharif teen dafa wazeer-e-azam banne wale pehle shakhs hain, ye baat bhi paper me ati hai.

Seats ki tadaad aur umar ki shartein

Aakhir me wo adad o shumar jo paper me sab se zyada ate hain. National Assembly ki kul seats 336 hain. In me 266 aam seats hain jin par awaam seedha vote dete hain, 60 seats khawateen ke liye makhsoos hain aur 10 seats ghair Muslim aqaliton ke liye makhsoos hain. National Assembly ki muddat paanch saal hoti hai.

Senate ki kul seats 96 hain. Har sobe ko 23 seats milti hain aur Islamabad ko 4 seats. Senator ki muddat chhe saal hoti hai aur har teen saal baad aadhe senator retire ho jate hain, is liye Senate kabhi poori ki poori khatam nahi hoti.

Yahan aik phanda wazeh kar dun. Purani kitabon me tumhe National Assembly ki 342 aur Senate ki 104 seats likhi milengi. Wo purani tadaad hai. FATA ke Khyber Pakhtunkhwa me zam hone ke baad, jo 2018 ki pachisvi aaeeni tarmeem se hua, seats kam ho gayein aur ab durust tadaad National Assembly 336 aur Senate 96 hai. Paper agar nayi kitaab se bana ho to yehi jawab durust hoga.

Umar ki shartein bhi teen numberon me yaad kar lo. Vote dene ka haq har us Pakistani ko hai jis ki umar 18 saal ho jaye. National Assembly ka member banne ke liye kam az kam umar 25 saal hai. Senator banne ke liye kam az kam umar 35 saal hai. Aur Pakistan ka Sadar banne ke liye kam az kam umar 45 saal hai. Teen number zehen me bitha lo: voter 18, MNA 25, senator 35, sadar 45. Bas ye chaar number aaeen wale hisse ke aadhe sawalon ka jawab hain.

Aakhir me dohrana

Chalo jaldi se poori kahani aik saans me dohra lo. 1949 me Objectives Resolution aaeen ki buniyad bani. 1956 me pehla aaeen aya aur Iskander Mirza pehle sadar bane, lekin 1958 me martial law ne use khatam kar diya. 1962 me Ayub Khan ka sadarti aaeen aya jo 1969 me khatam hua. 1970 me pehle aam intikhabat hue aur 1971 me mulk do tukre ho gaya. Phir 1973 me wo aaeen bana jo aaj tak chal raha hai, parlimani, wafaqi, do aiwanon wala, jisme Islam riyasat ka mazhab aur Urdu qaumi zubaan hai. Is kahani ko is tartib se yaad kar lo, phir koi tareekh idhar udhar nahi hogi.
