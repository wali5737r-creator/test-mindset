Bare Rehnuma
Jin logon ne Pakistan banaya

Ye hissa kyun zaroori hai

Dekho, Pak Studies ke one paper me jitne sawal tareekhon par bante hain, utne hi sawal shakhsiyaton par bante hain. Kis ki paidaish kab hui, kon pehla sadar tha, kon sa khitab kis ko mila, kon kahan dafan hai. Ye sawal mushkil nahi hote, lekin masla ye hai ke naam aur tareekhen apas me mil jati hain. Is liye har rehnuma ko hum us ki kahani ke sath parhenge. Pehle ye samjho ke ye shakhs tha kon aur us ne kiya kya, phir us ki tareekhen aur paper wale facts khud zehen me beth jayenge. Tartib bhi wazeh kar dun, pehle wo buzurg aayenge jinhon ne buniyad rakhi, phir Quaid aur un ke sathi, aur aakhir me wo rehnuma jo is safar ke aham moron par khare nazar ate hain.

Sir Syed Ahmad Khan, 1817 se 1898

Sir Syed Ahmad Khan 17 October 1817 ko Delhi me paida hue aur 27 March 1898 ko Aligarh me un ka inteqal hua. 1857 ki nakami ke baad jab Musalman har medan me pase hue the, Sir Syed ne unhen jadeed taleem aur Angrezon se behtar taluqat ka rasta dikhaya. Unhon ne 1858 me Asbab-e-Baghawat-e-Hind likh kar Angrezon ko bataya ke jang ki asal wajahen kya theen. Scientific Society, Tahzib-ul-Akhlaq aur Aligarh ka idara un ke bare karnamay hain. Hindu aur Musalman ko do alag qaumain kehne ki wajah se inhen do qaumi nazriye ke banion me shumar kiya jata hai.

Paper me Sir Syed se ye sawal bante hain. Paidaish 1817 Delhi me, wafat 1898 Aligarh me. MAO College kis ne banaya, Sir Syed ne. Aur Aligarh Tehreek ke bani kon hain, ye bhi Sir Syed hi hain. Inhen wo pehla shakhs samjho jis ne girri hui qaum ko uthne ka pehla sabq diya, taleem.

Allama Muhammad Iqbal, 1877 se 1938

Allama Iqbal 9 November 1877 ko Sialkot me paida hue aur 21 April 1938 ko Lahore me inteqal farma gaye. Pakistan banne se nau saal pehle hi wo is duniya se rukhsat ho gaye, lekin Pakistan ka khayal sab se pehle inhi ki zubaan se siyasi mutalbe ki surat me nikla. Iqbal ko Shair-e-Mashriq kaha jata hai, aur Pakistan ka tasawwur dene ki wajah se inhen Musawwir-e-Pakistan ka khitab mila. 9 November ko har saal Iqbal Day manaya jata hai.

Iqbal ki shaairi ne soi hui qaum ko jhijhora. Bang-e-Dra, Bal-e-Jibril aur Zarb-e-Kaleem un ke mashhoor majmooye hain. Khudi ka falsafa un ki pehchan hai. Paper me sawal bante hain. Iqbal ki paidaish kahan hui, Sialkot me. Allahabad Khutba kab diya, 1930 me. Iqbal Day kab manaya jata hai, 9 November ko. Aur Iqbal kahan dafan hain, Lahore me Badshahi Masjid ke pehlu me. Ye aakhri baat aksar paper me ati hai, pakki yaad rakho.

Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah, 1876 se 1948

Ab ate hain is kahani ke sab se bare naam par. Muhammad Ali Jinnah 25 December 1876 ko Karachi me paida hue. Qanoon ki taleem ke liye London gaye aur Lincoln's Inn se bar-at-law ki degree li, us waqt wo Hindustan ke sab se kam umr barristeron me se the. Watan wapas aa kar Bombay me wakalat shuru ki aur jald hi Hindustan ke maane hue qanoon dan ban gaye. 1906 me Congress join ki, 1913 me Muslim League me aye, aur Lucknow Pact 1916 ke asal mehmaan hone par inhen Hindu Muslim ittehad ka safeer kaha gaya.

Lekin jab Congress ka rawaiya badla aur Nehru Report ne Musalmanaan ke huqoq radd kar diye to Jinnah ne 1929 me apne 14 Nuqaat pesh kiye aur phir do qaumi nazriye ke sab se mazboot wakeel ban gaye. 1940 ki Qarardad-e-Lahore ke baad se wo Musalmanaan ke wahid aur sab se bare rehnuma the, isiliye qaum ne inhen Quaid-e-Azam, yani sab se bara rehnuma, ka khitab diya. 14 August 1947 ko wo Pakistan ke pehle Governor General bane. 11 August 1947 ko Aayeen Saaz Assembly se un ki taqreer aaj bhi Pakistan ke aayeen ki rooh samjhi jati hai. 11 September 1948 ko Karachi me un ka inteqal hua aur wo Karachi hi me dafan hain, un ke mazaar ko Mazar-e-Quaid kaha jata hai. Paper me sawal tay hain. Paidaish 25 December 1876 Karachi me, is din Quaid-e-Azam Day manaya jata hai. Pehle Governor General kon the, Quaid-e-Azam. Aur un ka mazaar kahan hai, Karachi me.

Liaquat Ali Khan, 1895 se 1951

Liaquat Ali Khan 1 October 1895 ko Karnal me paida hue. Taleem Aligarh aur Oxford se hasil ki. Quaid-e-Azam ke sab se qareebi aur bharose mand sathi the, isiliye unhen Quaid ka dast-e-rast kaha jata hai. Muslim League ke general secretary rahe, aburi hukumat me Wazir-e-Khazana bane, aur Pakistan banne ke baad mulk ke pehle Wazir-e-Azam muqarrar hue. 1949 me unhon ne Aayeen Saaz Assembly se Qarardad-e-Maqasid, yani Objectives Resolution, pass karayi, jo Pakistan ke har aayeen ki buniyad hai.

16 October 1951 ko Rawalpindi ke Company Bagh me aik jalse se khitab ke doran unhen goli maar kar shaheed kar diya gaya. Us bagh ka naam baad me un ke naam par Liaquat Bagh rakh diya gaya. Liaquat Ali Khan ko Shaheed-e-Millat ka khitab mila. Paper me sawal bante hain. Pakistan ke pehle Wazir-e-Azam kon the, Liaquat Ali Khan. Qarardad-e-Maqasid kab pass hui, 1949 me. Aur Liaquat Ali Khan kahan shaheed hue, Rawalpindi ke Liaquat Bagh me. Tareekh 16 October 1951 bhi sath yaad rakho.

Fatima Jinnah, 1893 se 1967

Fatima Jinnah Quaid-e-Azam ki chhoti behan theen. Wo 1893 me Karachi me paida huin aur pesha ke aitbar se dentist theen. Apni zindagi ka bara hissa unhon ne apne bhai ki khidmat aur un ke siyasi kaam me madad me guzara. Pakistan banne ke baad wo khawateen ki taleem aur huqoq ke liye kaam karti rahin. Qaum ne inhen izzat se Madar-e-Millat, yani qaum ki maan, ka khitab diya.

1965 me Fatima Jinnah ne us waqt ke sadar Ayub Khan ke muqable me sadarti intikhab lara aur pora mulk un ke gird ikatha ho gaya. Wo intikhab to na jeet sakin, lekin is muqabilay ne mulk me jamhoori tehreek ko nayi zindagi di. 9 July 1967 ko Karachi me un ka inteqal hua aur wo apne bhai ke pehlu me Karachi me dafan hain. Paper me sawal bante hain. Madar-e-Millat kis ko kaha jata hai, Fatima Jinnah ko. Unhon ne sadarti intikhab kab lara, 1965 me. Aur wo kis ki behan theen, Quaid-e-Azam ki. Un ki kitab My Brother bhi un ke bhai ki zindagi par aham maloomat ka zariya hai.

Sir Aga Khan III, 1877 se 1957

Sir Sultan Muhammad Shah Aga Khan III 2 November 1877 ko Karachi me paida hue. Wo Ismaili firqe ke imam the aur apne daur ke sab se asar-o-rasookh wale Muslim rehnumaon me se aik the. October 1906 me un ki qiyadat me Musalmanaan ke 35 rukni wafd, yani Simla Deputation, ne Viceroy Lord Minto se mulaqat karke alag intikhabat ka mutalba pesh kiya tha. December 1906 me jab Dhaka me Muslim League bani to unhen hi jamaat ka pehla aur mustaqil sadar muntakhib kiya gaya.

Aga Khan ne Aligarh ke idare ke liye dil khol kar atiyaat diye aur Musalmanaan ki taleem ke liye hamesha kaam kiya. 1937 me wo League of Nations ke sadar bhi bane, jo us daur ki duniya ki sab se bari tanzeem thi. 1957 me un ka inteqal hua. Paper me sawal bante hain. Muslim League ke pehle sadar kon the, Sir Aga Khan. Simla Deputation ki qiyadat kis ne ki, Aga Khan ne. Aur Simla Deputation kab gaya, 1906 me. In teeno sawalon ka jawab aik hi shakhs hai, is liye ye naam kabhi nahi bhoolta.

Maulana Muhammad Ali Johar, 1878 se 1931

Maulana Muhammad Ali Johar 10 December 1878 ko Rampur me paida hue. Aligarh aur Oxford ke taleem yafta the, lekin un ki pehchan Khilafat Tehreek ke aatish bayan rehnuma ki hai. Apne bhai Maulana Shaukat Ali ke sath mil kar unhon ne Khilafat aur Adam Taawun ki tehreekon ko gali gali pohncha diya. Unhon ne English akhbar Comrade aur Urdu akhbar Hamdard nikale, jin ki tehreerain logon me josh bhar deti theen.

1930 me wo pehli Gol Maiz Conference me sharik hone London gaye. Wahan unhon ne apni mashhoor baat kahi ke wo watan azaad ho kar lotenge ya yahin dafan honge. 4 January 1931 ko London hi me un ka inteqal ho gaya aur un ki wasiyat ke mutabiq unhen Bait-ul-Muqaddas, yani Jerusalem, me dafan kiya gaya. Paper me sawal bante hain. Khilafat Tehreek ke bare rehnuma kon the, Ali Brothers, yani Muhammad Ali Johar aur Shaukat Ali. Maulana Johar ka inteqal kahan hua, London me. Aur wo dafan kahan hain, Jerusalem me. Ye aakhri sawal paper ka pasandeeda hai, aksar students inhen Hindustan me dafan samajhte hain.

Maulana Shaukat Ali, 1873 se 1938

Maulana Shaukat Ali, Maulana Muhammad Ali Johar ke bare bhai the. Wo 1873 me Rampur me paida hue aur Aligarh se taleem hasil ki. Khilafat Tehreek me dono bhai aik dosre ke kandhe se kandha milaye rahe, isiliye tareekh unhen hamesha Ali Brothers ke naam se aik sath yaad karti hai. Shaukat Ali apni buland awaz, lambe qad aur purjosh taqreeron ki wajah se jalson ki jaan hote the. Jail ki sakhtiyan bhi dono bhaiyon ne sath bardasht kein.

Apne chhote bhai ki wafat ke baad bhi Maulana Shaukat Ali Muslim League aur Muslim maqasid ke liye sargarm rahe. 26 November 1938 ko Delhi me un ka inteqal hua. Paper me sawal aam taur par jori ki surat me banta hai. Ali Brothers se murad kon hain, Maulana Muhammad Ali Johar aur Maulana Shaukat Ali. Bara bhai kon tha, Shaukat Ali. Aur dono ka taluq kis tehreek se sab se zyada jura hai, Khilafat Tehreek se. Jori yaad rakhoge to koi sawal ghalat nahi hoga.

Chaudhry Rehmat Ali, 1897 se 1951

Chaudhry Rehmat Ali 16 November 1897 ko Punjab ke ilaaqe Balachaur me paida hue. Wo Cambridge University ke talib ilm the jab unhon ne wo kaam kiya jis ne un ka naam hamesha ke liye amar kar diya. 28 January 1933 ko unhon ne Cambridge se apna mashhoor pamphlet Now or Never nikala, jis me pehli dafa is naye mulk ke liye lafz Pakistan tajveez kiya gaya. Lafz Pakistan ka matlab hai pak logon ki sarzameen, aur ye naam unhon ne paanch ilaaqon ke pehle harfon ko jor kar banaya tha. P se Punjab, A se Afghania yani NWFP, K se Kashmir, S se Sindh aur TAN se Balochistan.

Chaudhry Rehmat Ali ne Pakistan National Movement ke naam se tanzeem bhi banai aur apni puri zindagi is khayal ki tableegh me guzari. Ajeeb baat ye hai ke jis shakhs ne mulk ko naam diya, wo khud Pakistan me bas na saka. 1951 me Cambridge hi me un ka inteqal hua aur wo wahin dafan hain. Paper me sawal tay hain. Lafz Pakistan kis ne tajveez kiya, Chaudhry Rehmat Ali ne. Kab tajveez kiya, 1933 me. Aur kis pamphlet me, Now or Never me. Teeno sawal aik hi kahani se nikalte hain.

Huseyn Shaheed Suhrawardy, 1892 se 1963

Huseyn Shaheed Suhrawardy 8 September 1892 ko Bengal ke ilaaqe Midnapore me paida hue. Oxford ke taleem yafta the aur Bengal ki siyasat ke bare rehnuma the. 1946 ke intikhabat ke baad wo Bengal ke Wazir-e-Aala bane, aur Direct Action Day ke fasadat ke waqt Bengal ki hukumat unhi ke pas thi. Quaid-e-Azam ke qareebi sathion me shumar hote the aur Muslim League ki aala qiyadat ka hissa the.

Pakistan banne ke baad Suhrawardy ne Awami League ki buniyad me aham kirdar ada kiya aur 1956 me Pakistan ke Wazir-e-Azam bane. Wo is ohde par taqreeban aik saal rahe. 5 December 1963 ko Beirut me un ka inteqal hua aur Dhaka me dafan hue. Paper me sawal bante hain. Suhrawardy kis sobe ke rehnuma the, Bengal ke. Wo Pakistan ke Wazir-e-Azam kab bane, 1956 me. Aur 1946 me Bengal ke Wazir-e-Aala kon the, ye bhi Suhrawardy hi the.

Khawaja Nazimuddin, 1894 se 1964

Khawaja Nazimuddin 19 July 1894 ko Dhaka me paida hue. Wo Bengal ke bare zaimindar khandan se taluq rakhte the aur Muslim League ke purane aur wafadar rehnumaon me se the. Pakistan banne ke waqt wo Mashriqi Bengal ke Wazir-e-Aala the. Quaid-e-Azam ke inteqal ke baad September 1948 me wo Pakistan ke dusre Governor General bane. Phir 1951 me Liaquat Ali Khan ki shahadat ke baad unhon ne Governor General ka ohda chor kar mulk ke dusre Wazir-e-Azam ki zimmedari sambhali aur 1953 tak is ohde par rahe.

Ye tartib khaas taur par yaad rakho, kyunke paper is me phansata hai. Pehle Governor General Quaid-e-Azam, dusre Khawaja Nazimuddin. Pehle Wazir-e-Azam Liaquat Ali Khan, dusre Khawaja Nazimuddin. Yani dono ohdon par dusra number aik hi shakhs ka hai. 22 October 1964 ko Dhaka me un ka inteqal hua. Paper me sawal bante hain. Pakistan ke dusre Governor General kon the, Khawaja Nazimuddin. Dusre Wazir-e-Azam kon the, ye bhi Khawaja Nazimuddin. Aik shakhs, do dusre number, yehi is sawal ki jaan hai.

A. K. Fazlul Haq, 1873 se 1962

Abul Kasem Fazlul Haq 26 October 1873 ko Bengal ke ilaaqe Barisal me paida hue. Apne ilaaqe ke ghareeb kisanon ke huqoq ke liye awaz uthane ki wajah se awam ne inhen Sher-e-Bangla, yani Bengal ka sher, ka khitab diya. Unhon ne Krishak Praja Party banai aur Bengal ke Wazir-e-Aala rahe. Muslim League aur Congress dono ke ijlason me un ki baat wazan rakhti thi.

Tareekh me un ka naam 23 March 1940 ki wajah se amar hai. Lahore ke Minto Park me Muslim League ke tareekhi ijlas me Qarardad-e-Lahore unhi ne pesh ki thi. Socho, qarardad Lahore me pesh hui, lekin pesh karne wala Bengal ka rehnuma tha, yehi is tehreek ki wusat ka saboot hai ke Pakistan ka mutalba kisi aik sobe ka nahi, pure Hindustan ke Musalmanaan ka tha. Paper me sawal tay hai. Qarardad-e-Lahore kis ne pesh ki, A. K. Fazlul Haq ne. Sher-e-Bangla kis ko kaha jata hai, Fazlul Haq ko. 27 April 1962 ko Dhaka me un ka inteqal hua.

Aakhir me aik nazar

Aao is pure khandan ko aik nazar me dohra lein. Sir Syed ne taleem di, 1817 se 1898. Iqbal ne khayal diya, 1877 se 1938, aur un ka din 9 November hai. Quaid-e-Azam ne khayal ko mulk bana diya, 1876 se 1948, un ka din 25 December hai. Liaquat Ali Khan pehle Wazir-e-Azam bane aur 1951 me Rawalpindi me shaheed hue. Fatima Jinnah Madar-e-Millat kahlayen aur 1965 me sadarti intikhab lara. Aga Khan League ke pehle sadar the aur Simla Deputation ke rehnuma. Ali Brothers ne Khilafat Tehreek chalai, Johar London me inteqal karke Jerusalem me dafan hue. Chaudhry Rehmat Ali ne 1933 me mulk ko naam diya. Suhrawardy Bengal ke rehnuma aur baad me Wazir-e-Azam bane. Khawaja Nazimuddin dono ohdon par dusre number par aye. Aur Fazlul Haq ne 1940 me qarardad pesh ki.

Agar tum har naam ke sath us ka aik khaas kaam jor kar yaad rakho, naam aur kaam, to shakhsiyaton ke sawal kabhi ghalat nahi honge. Yahi is pure hisse ka asaan sa raaz hai.
