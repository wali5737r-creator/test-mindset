Tehreek-e-Pakistan
1857 se 1947 tak, har waqia tartib se

Ye hissa kyun zaroori hai

Dekho, Pak Studies ke one paper me Tehreek-e-Pakistan wo khana hai jahan se sawal ana tay hai. Har saal koi na koi tareekh, koi na koi waqia, koi na koi qarardad paper me mojood hoti hai. Masla ye hota hai ke students waqiat ko alag alag ratte hain, aur jab do milti julti tareekhen samne ati hain, jaise 1906 aur 1909, ya 1929 aur 1930, to dimagh ulajh jata hai. Is liye hum is pure safar ko aik kahani ki tarah parhenge. 1857 ki haar se shuru karenge, phir dekhenge ke kesay aik khoi hui qaum ne pehle taleem pakri, phir apni alag siyasat banai, phir apna mutalba wazeh kiya, aur aakhir me 14 August 1947 ko apna mulk hasil kar liya. Kahani tartib se zehen me beth jaye to tareekhen khud yaad ho jati hain, kyunke har waqia apne pichle waqie ka jawab hota hai.

Aik mashwara pehle se de dun. Jab bhi koi waqia parho, teen cheezain pakki karo. Saal kya hai, hua kya tha, aur is ka nateeja kya nikla. Paper ka sawal hamesha inhi teen me se banta hai. Chalo shuru karte hain.

1857 ki Jang-e-Azadi

Ye kahani ki pehli aur sab se talkh tareekh hai. 10 May 1857 ko Meerut se baghawat shuru hui aur dekhte hi dekhte Delhi, Lucknow aur Kanpur tak phail gayi. Is jang me Hindu aur Musalman kandhe se kandha mila kar Angrez ke khilaf lare. Baghiyon ne Delhi par qabza karke budhe Mughal badshah Bahadur Shah Zafar ko apna rehnuma aur badshah-e-Hind bana liya. Yaad rakho, Bahadur Shah Zafar Mughal khandan ke aakhri badshah the. Jang ki fori wajah nayi Enfield rifle ke wo kartoos the jin par gay aur suar ki charbi ka shak tha, lekin asal wajahen barson ki na-insafi, Angrez ki tawsee pasand policy aur mazhabi dakhal andazi thi.

Jang nakam hui aur is ki qeemat sab se zyada Musalmanaan ne chukai. Angrez ne badla Musalmanaan se liya, kyunke wo inhen is baghawat ka asal zimmedar samajhta tha. Bahadur Shah Zafar ko giraftar karke Rangoon, yani Burma, jalawatan kar diya gaya, jahan 1862 me un ki wafat hui. Delhi me Musalmanaan par zulm ke pahar tore gaye, un ki jaidaden zabt huin aur sarkari mulazmaton ke darwaze un par band ho gaye. Paper me is waqie se teen sawal bante hain. Aakhri Mughal badshah kon tha, Zafar. Zafar ko kahan jalawatan kiya gaya, Rangoon. Aur Jang-e-Azadi kis saal hui, 1857. Teeno pakke kar lo.

Sir Syed Ahmad Khan aur Aligarh Tehreek

1857 ke baad Musalman taleem, rozgar aur izzat, har medan me sab se peeche reh gaye the. Us andhere me sab se pehle roshni Sir Syed Ahmad Khan ne dikhai. Unhon ne kaha ke Musalmanaan ko Angrezon se narazgi aur purani rivayaton se chipke rehne ke bajaye jadeed English taleem hasil karni chahiye aur Angrezon ke sath taluqat behtar karne chahiye, warna ye qaum hamesha peeche rahegi. Unhon ne 1864 me Scientific Society banai taake English kitabon ka Urdu me tarjuma ho, aur 1870 me apna mashhoor risala Tahzib-ul-Akhlaq nikala. Sir Syed ko do qaumon ke farq ka bhi ehsas hua aur yahin se do qaumi nazriye ki buniyad parti hai.

Aligarh Tehreek ka sab se bara karnama MAO wala idara hai, aur is ki teen tareekhen paper ki jaan hain. 1875 me Aligarh me MAO School qaim hua. 1877 me ye idara MAO College ban gaya, is ka sang-e-buniyad us waqt ke Viceroy Lord Lytton ne rakha tha. Aur 1920 me yehi college Aligarh Muslim University ban gaya. Tartib yaad rakho, pehle school, phir college, phir university. Paper me aam taur par poochha jata hai ke MAO College kab bana, jawab 1877, aur Muslim University kab bani, jawab 1920. Sir Syed ki wafat 1898 me Aligarh me hui.

1885, Congress ki buniyad

December 1885 me Bombay me Indian National Congress ki buniyad rakhi gayi. Is ke bani aik retire Angrez afsar A. O. Hume the, aur is ke pehle sadar W. C. Bonnerjee bane. Shuru me Congress ne daawa kiya ke wo tamam Hindustaniyon ki jamaat hai, lekin waqt ke sath ye wazeh hota gaya ke is par Hindu aksariyat aur un ke mufadat ka ghalba hai. Sir Syed Ahmad Khan ne Musalmanaan ko Congress se door rehne ka mashwara diya, kyunke un ka khayal tha ke jamaat me Musalman hamesha aqliyat me dab jayenge.

Paper me is se do seedhe sawal bante hain. Congress kab bani, 1885. Aur Congress kis ne banai, A. O. Hume ne. Is ke sath ye baat bhi zehen me rakho ke Congress ki buniyad ke baad hi Musalmanaan ko apni alag siyasat ki zaroorat mehsoos hona shuru hui, aur aage chal kar yahi ehsas Muslim League ki surat me samne aya.

1905, Bengal ki taqseem

16 October 1905 wo tareekh hai jis din Bengal ki taqseem amal me aayi. Viceroy Lord Curzon ne intizami asani ka bahana karke bare sobe Bengal ko do hisson me baant diya. Maghribi hissa Bengal hi raha, aur mashriqi hisson ko Assam ke sath mila kar naya soba Mashriqi Bengal-o-Assam banaya gaya, jis ka darul hukumat Dhaka tha. Is naye sobe me Musalman aksariyat me the, is liye Musalmanaan ne taqseem ka khair maqdam kiya. Dhaka ke Nawab Salimullah is ke bare hamiyon me the.

Lekin Hindu rehnumaon ne is ke khilaf shadeed tehreek chalai, Swadeshi Tehreek ke naam se Angrezi maal ka boycott hua aur pora Bengal hangamon ki zad me aa gaya. Paper me is waqie se sawal yun banta hai. Bengal ki taqseem kis Viceroy ke dor me hui, Lord Curzon. Taqseem kab amal me aayi, 16 October 1905. Aur naye sobe ka darul hukumat kya tha, Dhaka. Ye bhi yaad rakho ke taqseem ke is puray tajurbe ne Musalmanaan ko sikha diya ke apne huqoq ke liye unhen apni alag jamaat banana hi hogi.

1906, Muslim League ki buniyad

Ab ati hai wo tareekh jahan se Pakistan ki kahani asal me shuru hoti hai. 30 December 1906 ko Dhaka me All India Muslim League ki buniyad rakhi gayi. Ye ijlas Muhammadan Educational Conference ke salana ijlas ke mauqe par Dhaka ke Shahbagh me hua, aur nayi jamaat ki tajveez Nawab Salimullah ne pesh ki. Sir Aga Khan ko Muslim League ka pehla aur mustaqil sadar muntakhib kiya gaya, aur Mohsin-ul-Mulk jamaat ke secretary bane.

Muslim League ke maqasid wazeh the. Musalmanaan ke huqoq aur mufadat ki hifazat karna, hukumat ke sath wafadari ke taluqat barqarar rakhna, aur dosri qaumon ke sath dostana taluqat ko farogh dena. Isi saal, yani 1906 me, aik aur aham waqia hua tha. October 1906 me Aga Khan ki qiyadat me Musalmanaan ke 35 rukni wafd ne Simla me Viceroy Lord Minto se mulaqat ki, jisay Simla Deputation kehte hain, aur alag intikhabat ka mutalba pesh kiya. Paper me Muslim League se sawal har saal ata hai. League kab bani, 30 December 1906. Kahan bani, Dhaka me. Pehla sadar kon tha, Sir Aga Khan. Teeno jawab ratne ke bajaye kahani se jor kar yaad rakho, Dhaka ki sarzameen, jahan chand maah pehle Musalmanaan ko apna soba mila tha, wahi un ki jamaat ki paidayish ki jagah bani.

1909, Minto-Morley Islahat aur alag intikhabat

1909 me Indian Councils Act aya, jo is ke banane walon, Secretary of State Lord Morley aur Viceroy Lord Minto, ke naam par Minto-Morley Islahat kahlata hai. Is act ka Musalmanaan ke liye sab se aham tohfa judda gana intikhabat, yani separate electorates, tha. Is ka matlab ye hua ke Muslim ummeedwar ko sirf Musalman vote denge aur Muslim halqon se sirf Musalman hi muntakhib hoga. Simla Deputation ka mutalba poora ho gaya tha.

Alag intikhabat ki ahemiyat samajh lo, kyunke paper me is par sawal bar bar ata hai. Is usool ne pehli dafa qanooni taur par ye tasleem kar liya ke Hindustan ke Musalman aik alag qaum hain jin ke numainday alag muntakhib honge. Yahi woh buniyad hai jis par aage chal kar do qaumi nazriya khara hua. Sawal yun banta hai, Musalmanaan ko alag intikhabat kis act me mile, 1909 ke Minto-Morley Act me.

1911, Bengal ki taqseem ki wapsi

December 1911 me Delhi Darbar ke mauqe par Badshah George V ne khud Hindustan aa kar Bengal ki taqseem ki mansoookhi ka elan kar diya. Taqseem wapas le li gayi aur sath hi Hindustan ka darul hukumat Calcutta se Delhi muntaqil karne ka bhi elan hua. Musalmanaan ke liye ye bohat bara dhachka tha. Unhon ne mehsoos kiya ke Angrez sirf us ki sunta hai jo dabao dale, aur ye ke un ke mufadat ki koi zamanat nahi.

Is wapsi ka nateeja ye nikla ke Musalmanaan ka Congress aur Angrez dono par se bharosa hil gaya, aur apni alag siyasat par un ka yaqeen aur pukhta ho gaya. Paper me sawal seedha banta hai, Bengal ki taqseem kab mansoookh hui, 1911 me. Aik aur baat jo aksar poochhi jati hai ke darul hukumat Calcutta se Delhi kab muntaqil hua, ye bhi 1911 ke elan se hi jura hai.

1913, Muslim League ke maqasid me tabdeeli

1913 Muslim League ki tareekh ka mor hai. Is saal League ne apne maqasid me tabdeeli karke Hindustan ke liye khud ikhtiyari, yani self-government, ko apna hadaf bana liya. Ab League sirf huqoq ki hifazat nahi chahti thi, wo mulk ke nizam me apna hissa bhi chahti thi. Isi saal aik aur bohat aham waqia hua. Muhammad Ali Jinnah ne 1913 me Muslim League join kar li. Jinnah us waqt Congress ke bhi rukn the aur un ki sab se bari khwahish Hindu Muslim ittehad thi.

Paper me 1913 se do sawal bante hain. Muslim League ne self-government ko maqsad kab banaya, 1913 me. Aur Quaid-e-Azam ne Muslim League kab join ki, 1913 me. In dono ko aik sath yaad rakho, kyunke yehi wo saal hai jab mustaqbil ke Quaid aur mustaqbil ki jamaat pehli dafa aik saf par aye.

1916, Lucknow Pact

December 1916 me Lucknow ke maqam par Congress aur Muslim League ke ijlas aik hi waqt me hue aur dono jamaaton ke darmiyan aik tareekhi muahida tay paya, jisay Lucknow Pact kehte hain. Is muahide me Congress ne pehli dafa Musalmanaan ke alag intikhabat ko tasleem kar liya, aur dono jamaaton ne mil kar Angrezon se islahat ka mushtarka mutalba pesh kiya. Ye Hindu Muslim ittehad ka sab se uncha nuqta tha.

Is muahide ke asal mehmaan Muhammad Ali Jinnah the. Unhon ne hi dono jamaaton ko aik table par bithaya tha. Isi koshish par mashhoor Hindu rehnuma Sarojini Naidu ne Jinnah ko Ambassador of Hindu-Muslim Unity ka khitab diya. Paper me sawal yun ata hai, Lucknow Pact kab hua, 1916 me. Congress ne alag intikhabat kab tasleem kiye, Lucknow Pact me. Aur Jinnah ko ittehad ka safeer kis ne kaha, Sarojini Naidu ne. Teeno baatein aik hi kahani ki karriyan hain.

1919, Islahat, Rowlatt Act aur Jallianwala Bagh

1919 teen bare waqiat ka saal hai. Pehla, Montagu-Chelmsford Islahat ke tehet Government of India Act 1919 aya, jis ne sobon me do amli nizam, yani dyarchy, mutarif karaya. Dusra, hukumat ne Rowlatt Act pass kiya, jis ke tehet kisi ko bhi baghair muqadma chalaye jail me rakha ja sakta tha. Awam ne isay kala qanoon kaha aur is ke khilaf pora mulk uth khara hua. Teesra aur sab se khooni waqia Jallianwala Bagh ka hai.

13 April 1919 ko Amritsar ke Jallianwala Bagh me aik pur aman jalse par General Dyer ne baghair kisi warning ke goliyan chalwa dein. Bagh ke chaaron taraf unchi deewarein thin aur nikalne ka sirf aik tang rasta tha, jo fouj ne band kar diya tha. Sainkadon log shaheed aur hazaron zakhmi hue. Is waqie ne Angrez raaj par awam ka aitmaad hamesha ke liye khatam kar diya. Paper me tareekh ke sath naam bhi poochha jata hai, Jallianwala Bagh kahan hai, Amritsar me. Zimmedar officer kon tha, General Dyer. Aur waqia kab hua, 13 April 1919 ko.

Khilafat Tehreek, 1919 se 1924

Pehli Aalmai Jang me Turkey, yani Saltanat-e-Usmania, Angrezon ke khilaf lara tha. Jang ke baad Angrezon ne Turkey ke sath sakht sulook kiya aur Khilafat khatam karne ki baten hone lagin. Hindustan ke Musalmanaan ke liye Khalifa mazhabi rehnuma tha, is liye Khilafat ki hifazat ke liye 1919 me Khilafat Tehreek shuru hui. Is ke rehnuma do bhai the, Maulana Muhammad Ali Johar aur Maulana Shaukat Ali, jinhen Ali Brothers kaha jata hai. Mahatma Gandhi ne bhi is tehreek ki himayat ki aur is ke sath apni Adam Taawun, yani Non-Cooperation, tehreek jor di.

1920 me Hijrat Tehreek bhi chali, jis me hazaron Musalman Hindustan ko dar-ul-harb samajh kar Afghanistan ki taraf hijrat kar gaye, lekin wahan se mayus lotna para. 1922 me Chauri Chaura ke waqie ke baad Gandhi ne apni tehreek wapas le li, jis se Musalman bohat mayus hue. Aur 1924 me Mustafa Kemal Ataturk ne Turkey me Khilafat ka idara hi khatam kar diya, jis ke sath ye tehreek bhi apne anjaam ko pohnchi. Paper me sawal bante hain, Khilafat Tehreek ke rehnuma kon the, Ali Brothers. Tehreek kab se kab tak chali, 1919 se 1924 tak. Aur Khilafat kis ne khatam ki, Mustafa Kemal Ataturk ne.

1927, Delhi Proposals

March 1927 me Delhi ke maqam par Muslim rehnumaon ne aik ijlas me chaar aham tajaveez pesh kein, jinhen Delhi Proposals kaha jata hai. Jinnah is koshish ke ruh-e-rawan the. Tajaveez ye theen. Sindh ko Bombay se alag karke alag soba banaya jaye. Sarhadi sobe, yani NWFP, aur Balochistan me dosre sobon jaisi islahat di jayen. Punjab aur Bengal me Musalmanaan ko un ki abadi ke tanasub se numaindagi mile. Aur markazi assembly me Musalmanaan ko aik tihai, yani one third, nashistain milen.

In tajaveez ki ahemiyat ye thi ke Musalman is ke badle me alag intikhabat chorne par bhi tayyar the, bashart ye mutalbat man liye jayen. Ye ittehad ki aakhri sanjeeda koshishon me se aik thi. Paper me sawal banta hai ke Delhi Proposals kab pesh hue, 1927 me, aur in me total kitni tajaveez theen, chaar. Sindh ki alahdagi ka mutalba is me pehli dafa baqaida tor par samne aya tha, ye bhi yaad rakho.

1928, Nehru Report

1927 me Angrez hukumat ne Simon Commission bheja tha jis me aik bhi Hindustani shaamil nahi tha, is liye tamam jamaaton ne is ka boycott kiya. Jawab me Congress ne Motilal Nehru ki sarbarahi me aik committee banai jis ne 1928 me apni report pesh ki, yehi Nehru Report hai. Is report ne Musalmanaan ke alag intikhabat ko radd kar diya aur mushtarka intikhabat ki sifarish ki. Delhi Proposals ki shartein bhi is me manzoor na huin. Sindh ki alahdagi aur markaz me aik tihai numaindagi jaise mutalbat nazar andaz kar diye gaye.

Nehru Report Musalmanaan ke liye bohat bara dhachka sabit hui. Jinnah ne isay rahain juda hone ka nuqta, yani parting of the ways, kaha. Paper me sawal yun banta hai. Nehru Report kab ayi, 1928 me. Is committee ke sarbarah kon the, Motilal Nehru. Aur is report ne kis cheez ko radd kiya, alag intikhabat ko. Nehru Report ke baad hi Jinnah ne apne mashhoor Chauda Nuqaat pesh kiye, dono ko hamesha jor kar yaad rakho, pehle report, phir us ka jawab.

1929, Quaid-e-Azam ke 14 Nuqaat

March 1929 me Delhi ke maqam par Muslim League ke ijlas me Muhammad Ali Jinnah ne apne mashhoor Chauda Nuqaat, yani Fourteen Points, pesh kiye. Ye asal me Nehru Report ka jawab aur Musalmanaan ke mutalbat ka mukammal manshoor tha. Aham nuqaat ye the. Hindustan ka aayeen wafaqi ho aur ikhtiyarat sobon ke pas hon. Tamam sobon ko barabar khud mukhtari mile. Har assembly me Musalmanaan ki munasib numaindagi ho aur koi bhi bill us waqt pass na ho jab us qaum ke teen chothai arakeen us ke khilaf hon. Alag intikhabat barqarar rahen. Markazi assembly me Musalmanaan ko aik tihai nashistain milen. Sindh ko Bombay se alag kiya jaye, NWFP aur Balochistan me islahat hon, aur sarkari mulazmaton me Musalmanaan ka hissa muqarrar ho.

Paper me 14 Nuqaat se sawal tay hai. Ye kis ne pesh kiye, Muhammad Ali Jinnah ne. Kab pesh hue, 1929 me. Aur ye kis ke jawab me the, Nehru Report ke. Aik baat aur zehen me bitha lo, 1929 ke baad se Musalmanaan ke mutalbat ka markaz hamesha yehi 14 Nuqaat rahe, aur yehi do qaumi nazriye ki siyasat ki sab se wazeh dastaveez hain.

1930, Iqbal ka Allahabad Khutba

29 December 1930 ko Allama Iqbal ne Allahabad ke maqam par Muslim League ke salana ijlas ki sadarat ki aur apna tareekhi Khutba-e-Allahabad pesh kiya. Is khutbe me Iqbal ne pehli dafa baqaida aur wazeh alfaz me mutalba kiya ke shimal maghribi Hindustan ke Muslim aksariyati ilaaqon, Punjab, NWFP, Sindh aur Balochistan, ko mila kar aik alag Muslim riyasat banayi jaye. Iqbal ne kaha ke Islam aik mukammal zabta-e-hayat hai aur Musalmanaan ko apni tehzeeb ke mutabiq zindagi guzarne ke liye apni alag riyasat chahiye.

Is khutbe ki ahemiyat ye hai ke Pakistan ka khayal sab se pehle aik wazeh siyasi mutalbe ki surat me yahin samne aya. Isi liye Allama Iqbal ko Musawwir-e-Pakistan kaha jata hai, yani Pakistan ka tasawwur dene wala. Paper me sawal bante hain. Allahabad Khutba kis ne diya, Allama Iqbal ne. Kab diya, 29 December 1930 ko. Aur is me kis cheez ka mutalba tha, shimal maghrib me alag Muslim riyasat ka. Tareekh samet teeno baatein pakki kar lo, ye one paper ka sab se pasandeeda sawal hai.

Gol Maiz Conferenceain, 1930 se 1932

Hindustan ke aayeeni masle ko hal karne ke liye London me teen Gol Maiz Conferenceain, yani Round Table Conferences, huin. Pehli 1930 me hui, Congress ne is ka boycott kiya aur Gandhi sharik na hue, lekin Muslim League ke rehnuma, Aga Khan, Jinnah aur Muhammad Ali Johar, is me sharik hue. Maulana Muhammad Ali Johar ka inteqal isi doran London me hua. Dusri conference 1931 me hui, Gandhi Irwin muahide ke baad Gandhi is me Congress ke wahid numainday ki haisiyat se sharik hue, lekin ye bhi kisi nateeje par na pohnchi. Teesri conference 1932 me hui aur wo bhi nakam rahi.

Paper me aam sawal ye banta hai ke Gol Maiz Conferenceain kul kitni huin, teen. Kahan huin, London me. Aur dusri conference me Congress ki numaindagi kis ne ki, Mahatma Gandhi ne. Conferenceaon ki nakami ne ye sabit kar diya ke Hindustan ka aayeeni masla beth kar tay nahi hoga, aur yehi nakami aage ke sakht faislon ki zameen bani.

1932, Communal Award

August 1932 me Bartanwi Wazir-e-Azam Ramsay MacDonald ne Communal Award ka elan kiya. Is ke tehet Musalmanaan ke alag intikhabat ko barqarar rakha gaya aur ye sahoolat dosri aqliyaton tak bhi barha di gayi. Gandhi ne is ke khilaf marne tak ki bhook hartal ki dhamki di, khaas taur par achedule caste ke alag intikhabat par, jis ke baad Poona Pact hua.

Paper me sawal seedha hota hai. Communal Award kab aya, 1932 me. Kis ne diya, Bartanwi Wazir-e-Azam Ramsay MacDonald ne. Aur is ne Musalmanaan ko kya diya, alag intikhabat ki zamanat. Isay Nehru Report se mila kar dekho to kahani saaf hoti hai, Congress alag intikhabat khatam karna chahti thi, aur Communal Award ne unhen phir se pukhta kar diya.

1935 ka Act

Government of India Act 1935 Tehreek-e-Pakistan ke is dor ki sab se aham qanoon sazi hai. Ye us waqt ka sab se lamba act tha. Is ke tehet sobon ko khud mukhtari, yani provincial autonomy, di gayi aur sobon me do amli nizam khatam kar diya gaya. Markaz me aik wafaq, yani federation, banane ki tajveez thi, lekin wo hissa kabhi nafaz nahi ho saka kyunke riyasaton ne shamooliyat se inkar kar diya. Sobai hissa nafaz hua aur isi act ke tehet 1937 ke intikhabat hue.

Paper me sawal yun banta hai. 1937 ke intikhabat kis act ke tehet hue, 1935 ke Act ke tehet. Is act ne sobon ko kya diya, khud mukhtari. Aur ye bhi yaad rakho ke Pakistan aur Bharat dono ke pehle aayeen isi 1935 ke act ki buniyad par chale, is liye ye act dono mulkon ki tareekh me aham hai.

1937 ke intikhabat aur Congress ki wazaratain

1935 ke Act ke tehet 1937 me sobai intikhabat hue. Congress ne bare paimane par kamyabi hasil ki aur aath sobon me apni wazaratain, yani hukumatein, bana lein. Muslim League in intikhabat me achhi karkardagi na dikha saki. Congress ne Muslim League ke sath iqtedar me shirkat se inkar kar diya aur League ke ummeedwaron ko Congress me zam hone ki shart rakhi. Yahan se dono jamaaton ke darmiyan talkhi apne urooj par pohnch gayi.

Congress ki wazaraton ka dor 1937 se 1939 tak raha, aur Musalmanaan ke liye ye dor talkh tajurbon se bhara tha. Bande Mataram ko qaumi tarana bana kar lazmi karna, Hindi ko farogh dena, taleemi idaron me Hindu tehzeeb ki jhalak aur Wardha Scheme jaise iqdamat ne Musalmanaan ko mehsoos karaya ke Congress ki hukumat me un ki tehzeeb aur mazhab mehfooz nahi. Muslim League ne in zulm-o-ziyadation ko Jama karne ke liye Raja Muhammad Mehdi ki sarbarahi me committee banai, jis ki mashhoor Pirpur Report 1938 me samne aayi. Paper me sawal bante hain. Congress ki wazaratain kitne arse rahin, 1937 se 1939 tak. Pirpur Report kis bare me thi, Congress ke dor me Musalmanaan par hue zulm ke bare me. Aur is ne League ko ye kehne ka mauqa diya ke Musalman Congress ke raaj me kabhi mehfooz nahi reh sakte.

1939, Youm-e-Nijat

1939 me Dusri Aalmai Jang shuru hui to Viceroy ne Hindustan ki jamaaton se poochhe baghair mulk ko jang me sharik karne ka elan kar diya. Ehtejajan Congress ki wazaraton ne October aur November 1939 me istefay de diye. Musalmanaan ke liye ye 1937 se chale ane wale talkh dor ka khatima tha. Muhammad Ali Jinnah ne 22 December 1939 ko Youm-e-Nijat, yani Day of Deliverance, manane ka elan kiya. Us din pora Hindustan ke Musalmanaan ne Congress ki wazaraton ke khatme par Allah ka shukar ada kiya.

Paper me tareekh wala sawal tay hai. Youm-e-Nijat kab manaya gaya, 22 December 1939 ko. Kis ke elan par manaya gaya, Quaid-e-Azam ke. Aur kis khushi me manaya gaya, Congress ki wazaraton ke istefon par. Ye tareekh 1940 ki Lahore Resolution se chand maah pehle ki hai, dono ko tartib me jor kar yaad rakho, pehle Nijat, phir mutalba.

23 March 1940, Qarardad-e-Lahore

Ab ati hai is puri kahani ki sab se aham tareekh. 22 se 24 March 1940 tak Lahore ke Minto Park me Muslim League ka tareekhi ijlas hua, jis ki sadarat Quaid-e-Azam ne ki. 23 March 1940 ko Bengal ke Wazir-e-Aala A. K. Fazlul Haq, jinhen Sher-e-Bangla kaha jata hai, ne wo mashhoor qarardad pesh ki jo tareekh me Qarardad-e-Lahore ke naam se amar ho gayi. Qarardad ka mutalba ye tha ke shimal maghribi aur mashriqi ilaaqon me jahan Musalman aksariyat me hain, un ki alag aur khud mukhtar riyasatain qaim ki jayen.

Aik baat khaas taur par samajh lo, kyunke paper is par phansata hai. Qarardad ke asal matn me lafz Pakistan istemal hi nahi hua tha. Hindu akhbarat aur press ne isay tanzen Pakistan Resolution ka naam diya, aur yehi naam tareekh me chal para. Baad me isi tareekh ki yaad me 23 March ko Pakistan Day manaya jata hai. Paper me sawal bante hain. Qarardad kis ne pesh ki, A. K. Fazlul Haq ne. Kahan pesh hui, Lahore ke Minto Park me, jisay aaj Iqbal Park kaha jata hai. Aur kab pesh hui, 23 March 1940 ko. Teeno sawal har paper me ghumte hain.

1942, Cripps Mission aur Quit India

March 1942 me Dusri Aalmai Jang ke dabao me Bartania ne Sir Stafford Cripps ko aik tajveez ke sath Hindustan bheja, jisay Cripps Mission kaha jata hai. Tajveez ye thi ke jang ke baad Hindustan ko dominion ka darja diya jayega aur sobon ko ye ikhtiyar hoga ke wo naye aayeen ko qabool karen ya na karen. Is akhri shart me alag riyasat ke imkan ka pehla ishara mojood tha. Gandhi ne is tajveez ko post-dated cheque kaha aur Congress ne isay radd kar diya. Muslim League ne bhi isay is liye radd kiya ke is me Pakistan ka wazeh elan nahi tha.

Isi saal, August 1942 me, Congress ne Quit India, yani Hindustan Chhor Do, tehreek shuru ki aur Gandhi ne Do or Die ka nara diya. Hukumat ne fori taur par Congress ki qiyadat ko giraftar kar liya aur tehreek dab gayi. Muslim League ne is tehreek se alag rehne ka faisla kiya. Paper me sawal bante hain. Cripps Mission kab aya, 1942 me. Cripps tajveez ko post-dated cheque kis ne kaha, Gandhi ne. Aur Quit India tehreek kab chali, August 1942 me.

1944, Gandhi Jinnah Muzakarat

September 1944 me Bombay me Mahatma Gandhi aur Muhammad Ali Jinnah ke darmiyan barah-e-rast muzakarat hue. Ye mulaqatein Rajagopalachari Formula ki buniyad par theen, jis me jang ke baad Muslim aksariyati ilaaqon me rai shumari, yani plebiscite, ke zariye faisle ki tajveez thi. Dono rehnuma kayi dafa mile, lekin koi muahida na ho saka. Gandhi Pakistan ko jang se pehle tasleem karne par tayyar nahi the aur Jinnah ka mauqif tha ke Pakistan ka usool pehle mana jaye, tafseel baad me tay hogi.

Paper me sawal aam taur par itna banta hai. Gandhi Jinnah muzakarat kab hue, 1944 me. Kahan hue, Bombay me. Aur ye kis formula ki buniyad par the, Rajagopalachari Formula par. In muzakarat ki nakami ke baad ye wazeh ho gaya ke Pakistan ka faisla ab muzakarat se nahi, siyasi taqat aur intikhabat se hoga.

1945, Simla Conference

June 1945 me Viceroy Lord Wavell ne Simla me tamam jamaaton ki conference bulai, jisay Simla Conference kehte hain. Wavell Plan ke tehet markazi executive council me Hindustaniyon ko sharik karna tha aur Hindu Muslim numaindagi barabar rakhni thi. Conference is nuqte par nakam hui ke Muslim League ka mutalba tha ke tamam Muslim arakeen ki namzadgi sirf League karegi, kyunke wahi Musalmanaan ki wahid numainda jamaat hai. Congress is par raazi na hui aur conference kisi nateeje ke baghair khatam ho gayi.

Paper me sawal banta hai. Simla Conference kis Viceroy ne bulai, Lord Wavell ne. Kab hui, 1945 me. Aur nakami ki wajah kya thi, Muslim arakeen ki namzadgi ka tanaza. Ye waqia is liye bhi aham hai ke is ke baad Muslim League ne apna yehi daawa intikhabat me sabit karke dikhaya.

1945 aur 46 ke intikhabat

1945 ke aakhir me markazi assembly ke aur 1946 ke shuru me sobai assemblies ke intikhabat hue. Muslim League ne in intikhabat ko Pakistan par rai shumari bana diya. Nateeja tareekhi tha. Markazi assembly me Muslim League ne Musalmanaan ki tamam tees nashistain jeet lein. Sobai intikhabat me bhi League ne Muslim nashiston ki bohat bari aksariyat hasil ki. Jo jamaat 1937 me kamzor thi, wo ab Musalmanaan ki wahid awaz ban chuki thi.

In intikhabat ke baad bane wali aburi hukumat, yani Interim Government, me Muslim League bhi sharik hui aur Liaquat Ali Khan Wazir-e-Khazana bane. Unhon ne apne pehle budget se Congress ko khaasa pareshan kiya, aur dono jamaaton ka ikathhe chalna na mumkin ho gaya. Paper me sawal bante hain. 1945-46 ke intikhabat me Muslim League ne markaz me Muslim nashistain kitni jeetin, tamam tees ki tees. Aur aburi hukumat me League ki taraf se Wazir-e-Khazana kon the, Liaquat Ali Khan. Ye intikhabat Pakistan ke qiyam ki sab se mazboot siyasi buniyad hain, ye jumla zehen me bitha lo.

1946, Cabinet Mission

March 1946 me Bartania ki hukumat ne teen wazeeron par mushtamil aik mission Hindustan bheja, jisay Cabinet Mission kaha jata hai. Is ke arakeen Lord Pethick-Lawrence, Sir Stafford Cripps aur A. V. Alexander the. Mission ne aik aisa hal pesh kiya jis me Pakistan ka mutalba baqaida taur par radd kar diya gaya, lekin sobon ko teen groups me baant kar Muslim aksariyati sobon ko apne group me reh kar bohat si khud mukhtari dene ki tajveez di gayi. Markaz ke pas sirf defa, kharja aur mawasilat jaise chand shobay rehne the.

Dono jamaaton ne pehle is mansube ko mashroot taur par qabool kiya, lekin phir ikhtilafat ubhar aye aur Muslim League ne apni razamandi wapas le li. Paper me sawal bante hain. Cabinet Mission kab aya, 1946 me. Is me kitne arakeen the, teen. Aur is ne Pakistan ke mutalbe ke sath kya kiya, usay radd karke grouping ka mansuba diya. Mission ki nakami ke baad seedhi karwai ke elawa koi rasta na bacha tha, aur kahani apne aakhri mor par aa gayi thi.

16 August 1946, Direct Action Day

Jab Muslim League ne Cabinet Mission Plan ki razamandi wapas le li to Quaid-e-Azam ne 16 August 1946 ko Direct Action Day, yani Youm-e-Barah-e-Rast Iqdaam, manane ka elan kiya. Maqsad Bartanwi hukumat aur Congress par dabao dalna tha ke Musalmanaan ko un ka haq, yani Pakistan, diya jaye. Bengal me Muslim League ki hukumat thi aur wahan is din aam chutti ki gayi. Calcutta me shadeed fasadat phoot pare, jinhen Great Calcutta Killing kaha jata hai, aur hazaron log mare gaye. Fasadat phir Bihar aur dosre ilaaqon tak phail gaye.

Paper me tareekh wala sawal tay hai. Direct Action Day kab manaya gaya, 16 August 1946 ko. Kis ne elan kiya, Quaid-e-Azam ne. Aur sab se bare fasadat kahan hue, Calcutta me. Is din ke baad ye baat sab par wazeh ho gayi ke Hindu aur Musalman ab aik mulk me aman se nahi reh sakte, aur taqseem ke baghair koi hal nahi.

3 June 1947 ka Mansuba

February 1947 me Bartanwi hukumat ne Lord Mountbatten ko Hindustan ka aakhri Viceroy bana kar bheja aur June 1948 tak iqtedar muntaqil karne ka elan kiya. Mountbatten ne tamam rehnumaon se mulaqaton ke baad 3 June 1947 ko apna mansuba pesh kiya, jisay 3 June Plan ya Mountbatten Plan kehte hain. Is ke tehet Bengal aur Punjab ki taqseem ka faisla hua, NWFP aur Assam ke Sylhet zila me referendum hona tha, aur dono nayi riyasaton ki sarhadon ke liye Boundary Commission bana jis ke sarbarah Sir Cyril Radcliffe the.

Paper me sawal bante hain. 3 June Plan kis ne pesh kiya, Lord Mountbatten ne. Punjab aur Bengal ki sarhad bandi kis ne ki, Radcliffe ne. Aur NWFP me Pakistan ya Bharat ke liye faisla kis zariye hua, referendum ke zariye, jis me aksariyat ne Pakistan ke haq me vote diya. Is mansube ko Congress aur Muslim League dono ne qabool kar liya, aur is ke sath Pakistan ka banna tay ho gaya. Bas tareekh ka elan baqi tha.

July 1947, Indian Independence Act

July 1947 me Bartanwi Parliament ne Indian Independence Act pass kiya, jisay 18 July 1947 ko shahi manzoori mili. Is act ke tehet 15 August 1947 se Hindustan ko do azaad dominionon, Pakistan aur Bharat, me taqseem karne ka qanoon ban gaya. Har dominion ko apna Governor General muqarrar karne ka ikhtiyar mila aur Bartanwi Parliament ki Hindustan par hukumat khatam ho gayi. Yehi wo qanoon hai jis ki ro se Pakistan qanooni taur par wajood me aya.

Paper me sawal seedha banta hai. Indian Independence Act kab pass hua, July 1947 me. Is act ne kitni riyasatain banayen, do, Pakistan aur Bharat. Aur ye baat bhi yaad rakho ke is act ke baad Quaid-e-Azam Pakistan ke pehle Governor General muqarrar hue aur Bartania ke badshah ne un ki taqrari ki manzoori di.

14 August 1947, Pakistan ban gaya

Aur phir wo din aa gaya jis ke liye ye puri sadi ki jaddojehad thi. 14 August 1947 ko Pakistan duniya ke naqshe par aik azaad mulk ki surat me ubhra. Karachi ko pehla darul hukumat banaya gaya. Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah Pakistan ke pehle Governor General bane aur Liaquat Ali Khan pehle Wazir-e-Azam. 11 August 1947 ko Quaid-e-Azam ne Karachi me Pakistan ki pehli Aayeen Saaz Assembly se apni tareekhi taqreer ki thi, jis me unhon ne kaha tha ke har shehri ko apne mazhab ke mutabiq zindagi guzarne ki mukammal azaadi hogi aur riyasat ki nazar me sab barabar honge.

Aik khaas baat jo har Pakistani ko yaad hai aur paper me bhi ati hai, 14 August 1947 ki raat Islami tareekh ke hisab se 27 Ramzan-ul-Mubarak thi, wo mubarak raat jo Laylat-ul-Qadr ki raaton me se hai. Isi liye kaha jata hai ke Pakistan Ramzan ke mubarak maah me aur 27 Ramzan ki raat ko wajood me aya. Taqseem ke sath lakhon logon ki hijrat aur fasadat ka talkh silsila bhi chala, aur naye mulk ko khali khazane, na mukammal daftar aur mehajireen ke samandar jaisi mushkilat ka samna tha. Lekin is sab ke bawajood 1857 se shuru hone wala safar apni manzil par pohnch chuka tha. Paper me aakhri sawal tay hai. Pakistan kab bana, 14 August 1947 ko. Pehla darul hukumat konsa tha, Karachi. Pehle Governor General kon the, Quaid-e-Azam. Aur pehle Wazir-e-Azam, Liaquat Ali Khan.

Aakhir me puri kahani aik nazar me

Aao aakhir me is pure safar ko aik sans me dohra lein, taake tartib kabhi na bhoole. 1857 me haar hui aur Zafar Rangoon gaye. Sir Syed ne taleem ka rasta dikhaya, 1875 me school, 1877 me college aur 1920 me university bani. 1885 me Congress bani to Musalmanaan ko apni siyasat ki zaroorat mehsoos hui. 1905 me Bengal taqseem hua, 1906 me Dhaka me Muslim League bani, 1909 me alag intikhabat mile aur 1911 me taqseem ki wapsi ne sab ko chaunka diya. 1913 me League ne self-government ka hadaf apnaya aur Jinnah League me aye. 1916 me Lucknow Pact hua, 1919 me Jallianwala aur phir Khilafat Tehreek chali. 1927 me Delhi Proposals aye, 1928 me Nehru Report ne unhen radd kiya, 1929 me Jinnah ne 14 Nuqaat diye aur 1930 me Iqbal ne alag riyasat ka tasawwur pesh kiya. 1935 ke Act ke baad 1937 ke intikhabat aur Congress ki wazaraton ke talkh tajurbe ne 1939 me Youm-e-Nijat tak pohnchaya. 1940 me Lahore me Pakistan ka mutalba qarardad bana. Phir Cripps, Gandhi Jinnah muzakarat, Simla Conference aur 1945-46 ke intikhabat me League ki faisla kun kamyabi ayi. 1946 me Cabinet Mission nakam hua aur Direct Action Day ke baad taqseem tay ho gayi. 3 June 1947 ke mansube aur July ke Independence Act ke baad 14 August 1947 ko Pakistan ban gaya.

Is tartib ko do teen dafa bol kar dohra lo. Phir paper me koi bhi tareekh aye, tum use is kahani ki sahi karri par rakh kar jawab de sako ge. Yehi is pure hisse ka asal faida hai, tareekhen ratna nahi, kahani yaad karna.
