Qaumi Nishanaat, Pehle Pehle, aur Khaas Facts
Pakistan ke parcham, tarane aur nishan, tareekh ke pehle pehle, jangain aur qaumi din

Ye file kyun zaroori hai

One paper me Pak Studies ke sawalon ka aik bara hissa isi file jaisi baaton se ata hai. Qaumi phool konsa hai, pehla Nobel kis ko mila, pehla Nishan-e-Haider kis ko mila, 23 March kyun manaya jata hai. Ye sawal mushkil nahi hote, bas in ki tadaad bohat hai aur student aksar do milti julti cheezon me ulajh jata hai. Is liye har cheez ko wajah ke sath samjho. Jab wajah yaad ho jati hai to jawab khud yaad rehta hai. Is file ke teen hisse hain: pehle qaumi nishanaat, phir Pakistan ke pehle pehle, aur aakhir me jangain, Nishan-e-Haider aur qaumi din.

Qaumi Parcham

Pakistan ke parcham ko Sabz Hilali Parcham kehte hain. Is ka design Syed Amiruddin Kidwai ne tayyar kiya tha aur 11 August 1947 ko Aaeen Saaz Assembly ne is ki manzoori di. Isi din Liaquat Ali Khan ne ye parcham assembly me pesh kiya tha. Ye tareekh yaad rakho, 11 August 1947, kyunke paper me aksar 14 August de kar phansaya jata hai. Parcham manzoor azadi se teen din pehle hua tha.

Ab parcham ki shakal ko samjho. Parcham ke chaar hisse hain. Teen hisse sabz rang ke hain aur aik hissa, yani aik chauthai, safed patti ki surat me dande wali taraf hai. Sabz hisse ke darmiyan safed hilal, yani chaand, aur paanch nokon wala sitara bana hai. Parcham ki lambai aur chorai ka tanasub teen aur do ka hai.

Har rang aur nishan ka matlab hai. Sabz rang mulk ki Muslim aksariyat, taraqqi aur khushhali ki alamat hai. Safed patti ghair Muslim aqaliton ki alamat hai aur aman ka pegham hai, ke Pakistan me rehne wali aqaliton ko barabar ke huqooq hasil hain. Hilal taraqqi ki alamat hai, ke mulk aage barhta rahega. Sitara roshni aur ilm ki alamat hai. Paper me aksar poochte hain ke safed rang kis ki alamat hai, jawab hai aqaliton ki.

Qaumi Tarana

Qaumi tarane ke alfaaz Abu Al-Asar Hafeez Jalandhari ne likhe the aur is ki dhun Ahmed Ghulam Ali Chagla ne banayi thi. Dhun pehle bani, 1949 me, aur alfaaz baad me muntakhab hue. 1954 me qaumi tarana baqaida tor par ikhtiyar kiya gaya. Tarana Pak sarzamin shad bad se shuru hota hai aur is ke teen band hain.

Aik dilchasp baat jo kitabon me bohat ati hai. Tarane ke alfaaz zyada tar Farsi zubaan ke hain aur kaha jata hai ke pure tarane me Urdu ka sirf aik lafz istemal hua hai, aur wo hai "ka". Ye baat is liye mashhoor hai ke tarane ki zubaan ko Urdu aur Farsi dono ka sangam dikhana maqsood tha. Naam aur kaam dono yaad rakho: alfaaz Hafeez Jalandhari, dhun Ahmed Chagla. Paper me aksar dono ko ulta de kar phansaya jata hai.

Qaumi Nishan, yani State Emblem

Pakistan ka qaumi nishan, jisay angrezi me state emblem kehte hain, 1954 me ikhtiyar kiya gaya. Is ki shakal gaur se dekho to har cheez ka matlab samajh ata hai. Sab se upar hilal aur sitara hai, jo parcham ki tarah mulk ki pehchan hai. Darmiyan me aik dhaal hai jis par Pakistan ki chaar aham faslain bani hain: kapas, gandum, chai aur patsan, yani jute. Ye chaar faslain us waqt ki Pakistani maeeshat ki buniyad thin. Dhaal ke dono taraf chambeli ke phoolon ka haar hai. Aur sab se neeche aik patti par Quaid-e-Azam ka mashhoor farmaan likha hai: Iman, Ittehad, Tanzeem. Yani faith, unity aur discipline. Paper me ye sawal ata hai ke qaumi nishan par konsi faslain hain aur neeche kya likha hai, dono ka jawab ab tumhe ata hai.

Baqi qaumi nishanaat

Ab baqi nishanaat aik aik karke, wajah ke sath.

Pakistan ka qaumi janwar Markhor hai. Ye aik jangli pahari bakra hai jo mulk ke shimali aur maghribi pahari ilaqon me paya jata hai. Is ke bal khaye hue bare seengh is ki pehchan hain. Markhor Farsi zubaan ka lafz hai jis ka matlab hai sanp khane wala, aur is janwar ke bare me yehi riwayat mashhoor hai.

Qaumi parinda Chakor hai. Ye bhi pahari ilaqon ka parinda hai aur Pakistan ki sarzameen se is ka purana talluq hai.

Qaumi phool Chambeli hai, jisay angrezi me jasmine kehte hain. Safed rang aur mithi khushboo wala ye phool qaumi nishan ke haar me bhi shamil hai, is liye emblem wale sawal se isay jor kar yaad rakho.

Qaumi darakht Deodar hai. Ye unche pahari ilaqon me ugne wala lamba aur mazboot darakht hai, jis ki lakri imaraton me bohat kaam ati hai.

Qaumi khel Hockey hai. Nayi nasal ko ye jaan kar hairat hoti hai, lekin Pakistan hockey me duniya ki sab se kamyab teamon me raha hai. Pakistan ne hockey ke chaar World Cup jeete, 1971, 1978, 1982 aur 1994 me, aur teen dafa Olympic gold medal bhi jeeta, 1960, 1968 aur 1984 me. Isi liye hockey ko qaumi khel ka darja mila.

Qaumi zubaan Urdu hai, jaisa ke 1973 ke aaeen ke Article 251 me likha hai. Qaumi mazhab Islam hai, jo Article 2 ke tehet riyasat ka mazhab hai. Qaumi rehnuma Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah hain, jinhe Bani-e-Pakistan kaha jata hai. Qaumi shair Allama Muhammad Iqbal hain, jinhe Musawwir-e-Pakistan bhi kehte hain, kyunke 1930 ke Allahabad khutbe me unhon ne alag Muslim riyasat ka tasavvur pesh kiya tha.

Qaumi airline PIA hai, yani Pakistan International Airlines, jo 1955 me qayam hui. Is se pehle 1946 me Orient Airways bani thi, jo baad me PIA me zam ho gayi. Mulk ka markazi bank State Bank of Pakistan hai, jis ka iftatah 1 July 1948 ko Quaid-e-Azam ne khud kiya tha aur is ke pehle Governor Zahid Hussain the. Qaumi libaas Shalwar Qameez hai, qaumi phal Aam hai aur qaumi mashroob ganne ka ras mashhoor hai. Ye chand aakhir wali baatein kitabon me aam taur par isi tartib se milti hain.

Pakistan ke pehle pehle

Ab tareekh ke pehle pehle dekh lo. Ye sawal one paper ke sab se zyada dohraye jane wale sawal hain.

Pakistan ke pehle Governor General Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah the. Unhon ne 14 August 1947 ko ohda sambhala aur 11 September 1948 ko wafat tak is par rahe. Un ke baad Khawaja Nazimuddin dusre Governor General bane.

Pakistan ke pehle Sadar Iskander Mirza the. Governor General ka ohda 1956 ke aaeen ke sath khatam hua aur 23 March 1956 ko Iskander Mirza pehle sadar bane. Ye farq yaad rakho: Jinnah pehle Governor General the, Mirza pehle Sadar. Paper yahi farq phansata hai.

Pakistan ke pehle Wazeer-e-Azam Liaquat Ali Khan the. Wo Quaid ke sab se qareebi saathi the aur unhe Shaheed-e-Millat ka khitab mila. 16 October 1951 ko Rawalpindi ke Company Bagh me unhe shaheed kar diya gaya. Us bagh ka naam baad me Liaquat Bagh rakh diya gaya.

Duniya bhar me mashhoor pehli baat ye hai ke Pakistan ki pehli aurat wazeer-e-azam Benazir Bhutto thin. Wo 1988 me wazeer-e-azam banein aur wo poori Muslim duniya ki pehli aurat wazeer-e-azam thin. Wo do dafa is ohde par rahein.

Pakistan ke pehle Chief Justice Justice Abdul Rashid the. Wo 1949 me mulk ki sab se barri adalat ke pehle sarbarah bane, jab adalat ko Federal Court kaha jata tha.

Pakistan ko pehla Nobel inaam Dr Abdus Salam ko mila. Unhon ne 1979 me tabiyaat, yani physics, ka Nobel inaam jeeta. Wo Pakistan ke pehle aur science me ab tak ke wahid Nobel yafta hain. Dusra Nobel inaam Malala Yousafzai ko 2014 me aman ka mila. Malala ki khaas baat ye hai ke wo Nobel inaam pane wali duniya ki sab se kam umar shakhsiyat hain, inaam ke waqt un ki umar sirf satrah saal thi.

Pakistan ka pehla Nishan-e-Haider Captain Muhammad Sarwar ko mila. Wo 1948 ki Kashmir ki jang me shaheed hue the. Nishan-e-Haider Pakistan ka sab se bara foji aizaz hai, is ki tafseel isi file me aage ati hai.

Pakistan ka pehla satellite Badr-1 tha, jo 16 July 1990 ko China se khala me bheja gaya. Pakistan ka khalai idara SUPARCO is se bohat pehle, 1961 me, ban chuka tha. Ye do tareekhein jor kar yaad rakho: idara 1961, pehla satellite 1990.

Jangain, mukhtasar

Ab un jangon ka mukhtasar jayeza jo Pakistan ne lari hain. Har jang ki wajah aur natija aik saans me samajh lo.

Pehli jang 1947 aur 1948 me Kashmir par hui. Taqseem ke waqt Kashmir ki taqseem par tanaza khara ho gaya tha. Qabaili lashkar aur Kashmiri mujahideen ki ladai ke baad Bharat ye mamla Aqwaam-e-Muttahida le gaya. 1 January 1949 ko jang bandi ho gayi aur jahan faujain rukin, wahan ceasefire line ban gayi. Yehi lakeer baad me 1972 ke Simla Muahide ke baad Line of Control, yani LoC, kahlai.

Dusri jang 1965 me hui. 6 September 1965 ki subah Bharat ne Lahore ki janib achanak hamla kar diya. Jang satrah roz jari rahi. Is jang me poori qaum fauj ke sath khari ho gayi, is liye 6 September ko har saal Youm-e-Difa, yani Defence Day, manaya jata hai. Aakhir 10 January 1966 ko Ayub Khan aur Bharat ke Wazeer-e-Azam Lal Bahadur Shastri ke darmiyan Tashkent Muahida hua aur jang ka baqaida khatma hua.

Teesri jang 1971 me hui, jis ka zikr aaeen wale lesson me bhi aya hai. Is ke natije me 16 December 1971 ko Dhaka ka suqoot hua aur mashriqi Pakistan Bangladesh ban gaya. Ye jang Pakistan ki tareekh ka sab se talkh waqia hai.

Chauthi barri jhadap Kargil ki jang hai jo 1999 me hui. Kargil ki unchi barfeeli chotiyon par May se July 1999 tak ladai hoti rahi. Isay duniya ki sab se unchi jagah par lari jane wali jangon me gina jata hai. July 1999 me ye ladai khatam hui. Isi jang ke do sipahiyon ko Nishan-e-Haider mila, Captain Karnal Sher Khan aur Havildar Lalak Jan ko.

Nishan-e-Haider pane wale

Nishan-e-Haider Pakistan ka sab se bara foji aizaz hai. Is ke bare me teen baatein pehle yaad kar lo. Aik, ye ab tak sirf gyarah fojion ko mila hai. Dusri, ye tamam ke tamam shaheedon ko un ki shahadat ke baad mila, koi aik bhi zinda shakhs ko nahi mila. Teesri, in gyarah me se das ka talluq Pakistan Army se hai aur aik ka, Pilot Officer Rashid Minhas ka, Pakistan Air Force se. Ab tartib se naam dekho.

1. Captain Muhammad Sarwar, 1948, Kashmir ki jang
2. Naik Saif Ali Janjua, 1948, Kashmir ki jang. Inhe pehle Hilal-e-Kashmir mila tha jo baad me Nishan-e-Haider ke barabar qarar diya gaya
3. Major Tufail Muhammad, 1958, mashriqi Pakistan ki sarhad par
4. Major Raja Aziz Bhatti, 1965, Lahore ke qareeb Burki ka muhaz
5. Major Muhammad Akram, 1971, mashriqi Pakistan ka muhaz
6. Pilot Officer Rashid Minhas, 1971, Pakistan Air Force ke wahid Nishan-e-Haider
7. Major Shabbir Sharif, 1971, Sulemanki ka muhaz
8. Sowar Muhammad Hussain, 1971, Zafarwal Shakargarh ka muhaz
9. Lance Naik Muhammad Mahfuz, 1971, Wagah Attari ka muhaz
10. Captain Karnal Sher Khan, 1999, Kargil
11. Havildar Lalak Jan, 1999, Kargil

In naamon ko jangon ke sath jor kar yaad karo. 1948 wale do, 1965 wala aik, 1971 wale paanch, Kargil wale do, aur darmiyan me 1958 ka aik. Yun gyarah ka hisaab bhi yaad rahega aur tartib bhi.

Qaumi din

Aakhir me wo din jo har saal manaye jate hain. Har din ke sath us ki wajah di hai, kyunke paper me din nahi, wajah phansati hai.

1. 5 February, Youm-e-Yakjehti-e-Kashmir. Kashmiri awam ke sath yakjehti ke izhar ka din.
2. 23 March, Pakistan Day, yani Youm-e-Pakistan. Is din ki do wajah hain. 23 March 1940 ko Lahore ke Minto Park me Qarardad-e-Pakistan manzoor hui thi aur 23 March 1956 ko Pakistan ka pehla aaeen nafiz hua tha.
3. 1 May, Youm-e-Mazdoor. Mazdooron ka aalmi din, Labour Day.
4. 14 August, Youm-e-Azadi. 1947 me isi din Pakistan bana tha.
5. 6 September, Youm-e-Difa. 1965 ki jang ki yaad me Defence Day manaya jata hai.
6. 9 November, Iqbal Day. Allama Muhammad Iqbal 9 November 1877 ko Sialkot me paida hue the.
7. 25 December, Quaid-e-Azam Day. Quaid-e-Azam 25 December 1876 ko Karachi me paida hue the.

Do paidaish wale din jor kar yaad kar lo: Iqbal 9 November ko aur Quaid 25 December ko. Aur do tareekhi din bhi jor kar rakho: azadi 14 August ko aur difa 6 September ko. Aik aakhri baat dohra lo. Is file ke sab se zyada dohraye jane wale sawal ye hain: parcham ka designer Amiruddin Kidwai, tarane ke alfaaz Hafeez Jalandhari aur dhun Ahmed Chagla, pehle sadar Iskander Mirza, pehle Nobel Dr Abdus Salam, pehla Nishan-e-Haider Captain Sarwar, aur Nishan-e-Haider ki kul tadaad gyarah. Ye chhe baatein pakki kar lo to is file ke aadhe sawal wahin khatam ho jate hain.
