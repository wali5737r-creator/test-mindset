Zameen ka Ghilaaf — Atmosphere aur us ki Layers
Hawa ka wo samandar jisme hum sab rehte hain, aik aik layer ke sath

Ye topic kyun zaroori hai

Dekho, one paper ke Everyday Science aur GK hisse me atmosphere aik fix topic hai. Layers ki tartib, kis layer me kya hota hai, ozone kahan hai, ozone hole kyun bana, Montreal Protocol kab hua — ye sawal har paper me ghum phir kar ate hain. Khushkhabri ye hai ke ye topic mushkil bilkul nahi hai. Bas aik tasveer zehen me bitha lo ke zameen ke upar hawa ki paanch tehain (layers) hain, aur har teh ka apna aik khaas kaam hai. Tartib aur har layer ki pehchan pakki ho jaye to ye number kahin nahi jate.

Atmosphere hai kya

Matlab ye hai. Zameen ke chaaron taraf lapeta hua gaso ka ghilaaf hi atmosphere hai. Asaan misaal se samjho. Jese sardi me tum kambal orhte ho aur kambal tumhe bahar ki thand se bachata hai, bilkul wese hi zameen ne hawa ka aik bohat bara kambal orha hua hai. Farq sirf itna hai ke ye kambal sirf thand se nahi bachata, ye zindagi ki har zaroorat poori karta hai. Zameen ki kashish (gravity) is hawa ko pakre hue hai, warna ye kab ki space me ur gayi hoti.

Is hawa me hai kya. Sab se zyada nitrogen hai, taqreeban 78 feesad. Doosre number par oxygen hai, taqreeban 21 feesad, yehi wo gas hai jo hum sans me lete hain. Baqi aik feesad me argon, carbon dioxide, pani ke bukharat aur dusri gasain shamil hain. Yahan pehla paper wala trap samajh lo. Zyada tar students samajhte hain ke hawa me sab se zyada oxygen hai, lekin asal me sab se zyada nitrogen hai. Ye sawal bar bar ata hai, pakka yaad rakho.

Atmosphere kyun zaroori hai

Pehli wajah sans hai. Oxygen ke baghair insaan, janwar, koi zinda cheez jee nahi sakti, aur poday (plants) carbon dioxide le kar khana banate hain aur oxygen wapas dete hain. Ye lena dena atmosphere ke andar hi hota hai.

Dusri wajah kambal wala kaam hai. Atmosphere din ki garmi ka kuch hissa rok kar zameen ko na bohat garam hone deta hai na raat ko bohat thanda. Agar ye ghilaaf na hota to din me jhulsane wali garmi aur raat me jama dene wali sardi hoti. Chaand ki misaal samne hai. Chaand par atmosphere nahi hai, is liye wahan din aur raat ke darja hararat me soin (hundreds) degree ka farq par jata hai. Zameen par ye farq is ghilaaf ki wajah se bohat kam hai.

Teesri wajah hifazat hai. Suraj ki khatarnak ultraviolet roshni ko ozone wali teh rok leti hai, jis ki tafseel aage arahi hai. Aur space se girne wale chhote pathar (meteor) bhi isi hawa me jal kar khatam ho jate hain, zameen tak bohat kam pohanchte hain.

Chauthi wajah mausam hai. Badal, barish, barf, hawa ka chalna, ye sab atmosphere ke andar hota hai. Pani ka pura chakkar, samandar se bukharat uthna, badal banna, barish girna, isi ghilaaf ki badaulat hai.

Aik aakhri baat zehen me rakho. Atmosphere ki koi wazeh kinara wali sarhad nahi hai. Neeche hawa ghani (dense) hai aur upar jate jate patli hoti jati hai, hatta ke ahista ahista space me ghul jati hai. Atmosphere ki zyada tar hawa zameen ke qareeb pehle chand kilometre ke andar hi hai. Aam taur par 100 kilometre ki bulandi ko space ki shuruaat mana jata hai, isay Karman Line kehte hain.

Layers ki tartib pehle yaad kar lo

Scientists ne darja hararat ke badalne ke andaaz ki bunyad par atmosphere ko paanch layers me baanta hai. Neeche se upar ki tartib ye hai. Troposphere, Stratosphere, Mesosphere, Thermosphere aur Exosphere. Ionosphere koi alag layer nahi hai, wo Thermosphere ke andar ka aik khaas charged hissa hai, us ki baat Thermosphere ke sath hi ayegi.

Tartib yaad karne ki trick ye hai. Ye jumla ratt lo: Tum Se Mila Tha Eid par. Tum yani Troposphere, Se yani Stratosphere, Mila yani Mesosphere, Tha yani Thermosphere aur Eid yani Exosphere. Bas, tartib kabhi nahi bhulegi. Ab har layer ko aik aik karke samajhte hain.

Troposphere

Matlab ye hai. Ye zameen se sab se qareeb, sab se neeche wali layer hai. Is ka naam Greek lafz tropos se aya hai jis ka matlab hai badlao ya milawat (mixing), aur is layer me hawa lagatar milti aur badalti rehti hai. Ye layer zameen ki satah se shuru ho kar taqreeban 12 kilometre ki bulandi tak jati hai. Khutut-e-istawa (equator) ke upar ye 16 se 18 kilometre tak unchi hai aur poles par sirf 8 kilometre ke qareeb hai, kyunke garam ilaaqon me hawa phail kar zyada upar tak jati hai.

Is layer ka sab se bara kaam ye hai ke saara mausam yahin hota hai. Badal, barish, barfbaari, aandhi, toofan, bijli kadakna, jo kuch bhi mausam ke naam par hota hai wo sab Troposphere ke andar hota hai. Wajah samajh lo. Pani ke bukharat taqreeban sare ke sare isi layer me hain aur hawa bhi yahin sab se ghani hai, to badal aur barish ka saara samaan yahin mojood hai. Atmosphere ki kul hawa ka taqreeban teen chauthai hissa, yani 75 feesad se zyada, isi chhoti si layer ke andar samaaya hua hai. Insaan, janwar, parinde, hatta ke duniya ki sab se unchi choti Mount Everest (taqreeban 8.8 kilometre) bhi isi layer ke andar hai.

Ab wo baat jo paper me sab se zyada ati hai. Is layer me bulandi ke sath darja hararat ghatta hai. Har aik kilometre upar jane par darja hararat taqreeban 6.5 degree Celsius kam ho jata hai, isay lapse rate kehte hain. Wajah bari asaan hai. Suraj ki roshni pehle zameen ko garam karti hai, phir garam zameen apne qareeb wali hawa ko garam karti hai. Bilkul wese jese tawa pehle garam hota hai phir roti ko garam karta hai. To jo hawa zameen ke sab se qareeb hai wo sab se garam, aur jese jese upar jao garam karne wale taway se doori barhti jati hai aur thand barhti jati hai. Isi liye paharon ki chotiyon par barf parti hai aur Murree jesi jagahen maidanon se thandi hoti hain. Is layer ki upar wali sarhad ko Tropopause kehte hain.

Paper ki pehchan wali baat ye hai ke saara mausam Troposphere me hota hai aur bulandi ke sath darja hararat ghatta hai. Aur haan, lambi parwaz wale jahaaz mausam ki kharabiyon se bachne ke liye taqreeban 10 se 12 kilometre ki bulandi par urte hain, yani is layer ke bilkul aakhir me, jahan se agli layer shuru hoti hai.

Stratosphere

Matlab ye hai. Ye Troposphere ke upar wali dusri layer hai, taqreeban 12 kilometre se 50 kilometre tak. Is layer ki sab se khaas baat ye hai ke yahan darja hararat ka hisaab ulta ho jata hai. Yahan bulandi ke sath darja hararat ghatne ke bajaye barhta hai. Wajah aage arahi hai, ozone. Ozone Suraj ki ultraviolet roshni ko jazb karti hai aur is amal se ye layer upar ki taraf garam hoti jati hai.

Is layer me hawa bohat pur-sukoon (calm) hai. Yahan na badal bante hain, na barish hoti hai, na aandhi chalti hai, kyunke pani ke bukharat yahan taqreeban nahi hote. Yehi wajah hai ke lambi parwaz wale jahaaz is layer ke neeche wale hisse me urna pasand karte hain. Mausam ki koi kharabi nahi, hawa ke jhatke nahi, safar hamvaar aur pursukoon rehta hai aur fuel bhi kam lagta hai.

Isi layer me wo cheez hai jis ki wajah se zameen par zindagi mehfooz hai, yani OZONE LAYER. Ozone gas ki ye teh taqreeban 15 se 35 kilometre ki bulandi ke darmiyan phaili hai aur sab se ghani 20 se 30 kilometre ke aas paas hai. Ozone ki puri tafseel is file ke aakhir me alag se di gayi hai, kyunke ye one paper ka sab se pasandeeda topic hai. Is layer ki upar wali sarhad ko Stratopause kehte hain.

Paper ki pehchan wali baat ye hai ke ozone layer Stratosphere me hai. Ye jumla har doosre paper me kisi na kisi surat me ata hai. Aur dusri pehchan ye ke jahaaz Stratosphere me is liye urte hain kyunke wahan mausam nahi hota.

Mesosphere

Matlab ye hai. Ye darmiyan wali, teesri layer hai, taqreeban 50 kilometre se 85 kilometre tak. Mesos ka matlab hi darmiyan hai. Ye atmosphere ki sab se thandi layer hai. Yahan darja hararat dobara ghatna shuru ho jata hai aur is layer ke sab se upar ja kar taqreeban minus 90 degree Celsius tak gir jata hai, jo pure atmosphere ka sab se kam darja hararat hai.

Is layer ka kaam zameen ki hifazat hai, lekin dusre andaaz me. Space se jo chhote pathar aur gard ke zaray (meteor) zameen ki taraf ate hain, wo is layer me tez raftaar se hawa se ragar khate hain, garam ho kar jal uthte hain aur aksar yahin khatam ho jate hain. Raat ko asmaan me jo roshni ki lakeer aik lamhe ke liye chamak kar gayab ho jati hai, jise awam tootta sitara kehte hain, wo asal me yehi meteor hai jo Mesosphere me jal raha hota hai. Meteor ki puri tafseel Space Objects wale topic me milegi.

Aik mazay ki baat ye hai ke ye layer scientists ke liye sab se mushkil hai. Jahaaz aur mausami ghubare (weather balloons) itni bulandi tak ja nahi sakte, aur satellites ke liye yahan hawa phir bhi itni hai ke wo yahan tik nahi sakte. Is liye is layer ko sab se kam explore kiya gaya hai. Is ki upar wali sarhad ko Mesopause kehte hain aur yehi atmosphere ka sab se thanda nuqta hai.

Paper ki pehchan wali baat ye hai ke meteor Mesosphere me jalte hain aur sab se thandi layer Mesosphere hai. Dono baatein fix sawal hain.

Thermosphere aur Ionosphere

Matlab ye hai. Ye chauthi layer hai, taqreeban 85 kilometre se 600 kilometre tak. Thermo ka matlab hai garmi, aur is naam ki wajah ye hai ke yahan darja hararat bohat zyada barh jata hai, hazaar degree Celsius se bhi upar napajata hai. Lekin ghabrane ki baat nahi. Yahan hawa itni patli hai ke gas ke zaray bohat door door hain, is liye itna zyada darja hararat honay ke bawajood koi cheez yahan aam maano me garam mehsoos nahi karti. Darja hararat zarayon ki raftaar ka naam hai, aur jab zaray hi na honay ke barabar hon to garmi ka asar bhi na honay ke barabar hota hai.

Ab Ionosphere ko samjho, ye is file ka aik aur paper wala hissa hai. Thermosphere ke andar, aur Mesosphere ke upar wale hisse me, Suraj ki tez roshni hawa ke zarayon ko charge (ionize) kar deti hai. Is charged hisse ko Ionosphere kehte hain. Is ka sab se bara kaam radio waves ko wapas morna hai. Zameen se bheji gayi radio waves jab Ionosphere se takrati hain to aainay se takra kar wapas ane ki tarah zameen ki taraf lot ati hain. Yehi wajah hai ke radio signals aik sheher se dusre mulk tak, samandaron ke paar tak, pohanch jate hain. Agar Ionosphere na hota to lambay faasle ki radio communication bohat mushkil ho jati.

Isi layer me wo roshni wala tamasha bhi hota hai jise Aurora kehte hain. Poles ke qareeb raat ko asmaan me sabz, surkh aur jamni rang ki leherti roshniyan nazar ati hain. Ye Suraj se ane wale charged zarayon ke hawa se takrane se banti hain. North Pole ki taraf wali ko Aurora Borealis aur South Pole ki taraf wali ko Aurora Australis kehte hain.

Aik aur aham baat. Insaan ki banayi hui sab se bari space station, International Space Station yani ISS, taqreeban 400 kilometre ki bulandi par isi layer me zameen ke gird ghoomti hai. Aur space ki mashhoor sarhad, Karman Line (100 kilometre), bhi isi layer ke neeche wale hisse se guzarti hai.

Paper ki pehchan wali baat ye hai ke radio waves Ionosphere se reflect ho kar wapas ati hain, aur ISS Thermosphere me hai. Ye dono jumle rat lo.

Exosphere

Matlab ye hai. Ye atmosphere ki sab se aakhri, sab se bahar wali layer hai. Ye taqreeban 600 kilometre se shuru hoti hai aur hazaron kilometre tak, taqreeban 10,000 kilometre tak, phaili hui hai, aur phir ahista ahista khala (space) me ghul jati hai. Is ki koi wazeh kinara wali sarhad nahi hai.

Yahan hawa khatam honay ke barabar hai. Sirf sab se halki gaso, hydrogen aur helium, ke chand zaray bohat door door mojood hain. Kuch zaray itne door nikal jate hain ke wo zameen ki kashish se nikal kar space me chale jate hain. Samajh lo ke ye wo jagah hai jahan atmosphere khamoshi se khatam hota hai aur asal space shuru hota hai.

Is layer ki pehchan ye hai ke zyada tar masnooi satellites (artificial satellites) isi layer aur is ke aas paas ke orbital ilaqay me zameen ke gird ghoomte hain. Mausam batane wale satellites, communication satellites, GPS satellites, sab inhi bulandiyon par apne apne orbit me hain. Masnooi satellites ki tafseel Space Objects wale topic me hai.

Paper ki pehchan wali baat ye hai ke sab se bahar wali layer Exosphere hai aur satellites Exosphere me hote hain. Aur tartib aik dafa phir dohra lo: Troposphere, Stratosphere, Mesosphere, Thermosphere, Exosphere — Tum Se Mila Tha Eid par.

Ozone — Zameen ki dhaal, aur us me surakh

Ab us cheez ki tafseel jo is pure topic ka sab se zyada poocha jane wala hissa hai, yani ozone. Pehle matlab samjho. Ozone oxygen ki aik khaas shakal hai. Aam oxygen, jo hum sans me lete hain, us ke har molecule me do atom hote hain, use O2 kehte hain. Ozone ke molecule me teen atom hote hain, use O3 kehte hain. Ye halki neeli (pale blue) rang ki gas hai aur is ki boo tez hoti hai. Bijli kadakne ke baad hawa me jo ajeeb si boo ati hai, wo bijli se bani hui thori si ozone hi ki hoti hai.

Ozone banti kese hai. Stratosphere me Suraj ki ultraviolet roshni jab aam oxygen (O2) ke molecule se takrati hai to use tor kar alag alag atom bana deti hai. Phir yehi atom kisi dusre O2 molecule se jud jata hai aur O3 yani ozone ban jati hai. Ye tootne aur banne ka amal lagatar chalta rehta hai, aur is amal ke doran ultraviolet roshni jazb (absorb) hoti rehti hai. Yehi jazb hona hi hamari hifazat hai.

Ozone ka kaam kya hai. Ozone layer Suraj se ane wali khatarnak ultraviolet, yani UV, rays ka taqreeban 97 se 99 feesad hissa rok leti hai. Ye koi chhoti baat nahi hai. Agar ye UV roshni seedhi zameen tak pohanchti to insaanon me jild ka cancer barh jata, ankhon me motia (cataract) aam ho jata, faslon ki paidawar kam ho jati, samandar ki satah ke qareeb rehne wali chhoti zindagi tabah ho jati aur insaan ke jism ki bimariyon se ladne ki taqat (immunity) kamzor par jati. Isi liye ozone ko zameen ki dhaal aur zindagi ka sun-screen kaha jata hai. Aur yaad rakho, ye ozone Stratosphere me hai, taqreeban 15 se 35 kilometre ki bulandi par.

Ab ozone hole ki baat. Hole ka matlab asal surakh nahi hai. 1980 ki dahayi me scientists ne dekha ke Antarctica, yani South Pole wale bar-e-azam, ke upar har saal wahan ki bahar (spring) ke mausam me, September se November ke darmiyan, ozone bohat patli ho jati hai. Is patle honay ko hi ozone hole kaha jata hai. Ye baat paper me is liye ati hai ke sawal poochta hai ozone hole kahan hai, aur sahi jawab Antarctica hai, Arctic ya North Pole nahi.

Hole bana kyun. Is ki sab se bari wajah CFC hai, jis ka pura naam chlorofluorocarbons hai. Ye chemicals purane zamane ke fridge, air conditioner, spray cans aur foam banane me aam istemal hote the. CFC ke molecules bohat dair tak kharab nahi hote, wo ahista ahista upar Stratosphere tak pohanch jate hain. Wahan tez UV roshni unhe torti hai aur un me se chlorine nikalta hai. Ye chlorine ozone ke molecules ko tor tor kar khatam karta hai. Aur sab se khatarnak baat ye hai ke chlorine ka aik atom is amal me khud khatam nahi hota, wo taqreeban aik lakh ozone molecules ko tor sakta hai. Isi liye thori si CFC bhi bohat bara nuqsan karti hai.

Phir duniya ne kya kiya. Is masle ka hal Montreal Protocol hai, aur ye one paper ka sab se fix sawal hai. 16 September 1987 ko Canada ke sheher Montreal me duniya bhar ke mulkon ne aik muahide par dastakhat kiye ke ozone ko nuqsan pohanchane wale chemicals, khaas taur par CFC, ahista ahista band kar diye jayenge. Ye muahida 1989 se nafiz hua aur itna kamyab raha ke ise duniya ke sab se kamyab maholiyati (environmental) muahidon me gina jata hai. CFC ka istemal taqreeban khatam ho chuka hai aur scientists ke mutabiq ozone layer ahista ahista theek ho rahi hai. Isi 16 September ko har saal duniya bhar me Ozone Day, yani International Day for the Preservation of the Ozone Layer, manaya jata hai.

Paper wali baatein aakhir me aik sath dohra lo. Ozone Stratosphere me hai aur us ke molecule me teen oxygen atom hain. Ozone hole Antarctica ke upar banta hai. Us ki wajah CFC hai. Montreal Protocol 1987 me Canada ke sheher Montreal me hua. Aur 16 September Ozone Day hai. Ye paanch baatein pakki kar lo, in me se koi na koi har paper me mojood hoti hai.
