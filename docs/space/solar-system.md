Nizam-e-Shamsi — Suraj aur Aath Siyare
Suraj ka khandan, har siyare ka naam, us ke laqab ki wajah, aur paper ke pakke facts

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

One paper ka GK wala hissa ho ya Everyday Science ka, Nizam-e-Shamsi se sawal ana tay hai. Sab se bara siyara konsa hai, sab se garam konsa hai, Red Planet kise kehte hain, kis siyare ke sab se zyada chaand hain — ye sawal har doosre paper me ghum phir kar ajate hain. Aur mazay ki baat ye hai ke ye pure number hain, kyunke in ka jawab aik hi hota hai aur wo badalta nahi. Jo student ye lesson samajh kar parh le, wo in sawalon me kabhi nahi atkay ga. Lekin aik shart hai. Naam ke sath wajah bhi samajhni hai. Sirf ye ratna ke Mars Red Planet hai kaafi nahi. Paper kabhi kabhi wajah wale pehlu se bhi phansata hai. Is lesson me har siyare ka naam, us ka laqab, us laqab ki wajah aur us ke paper wale facts, sab sath sath chalenge.

Pehle buniyadi baat samajh lo

Dekho, raat ko asmaan par jo kuch chamakta hai wo sab aik jesa nahi hota. Kuch cheezain apni roshni khud banati hain, unhe sitara kehte hain. Hamara Suraj aik sitara hai. Aur kuch cheezain khud thandi aur tareek hoti hain, wo kisi sitare ki roshni se chamakti hain aur usi sitare ke gird chakkar lagati hain, unhe siyara kehte hain. Chaand bhi khud tareek hai, wo siyare ke gird ghoomta hai. Bas yehi teen darjay zehen me bitha lo. Sitara sab se bara aur khud roshan, siyara sitare ke gird, aur chaand siyare ke gird.

Hamari Zameen aik siyara hai aur Suraj ke gird ghoomti hai. Suraj ke gird kul aath siyare ghoomte hain, aur is pure khandan ko Nizam-e-Shamsi kehte hain. Shamsi ka matlab hi hai Suraj se taluq rakhne wala. Ab aik aik karke pure khandan se milte hain. Pehle khandan ke sarbarah se, yani Suraj se.

Suraj — hamara sitara

Sab se pehli aur sab se pakki baat. Suraj siyara nahi hai, sitara hai. Ye hamare Nizam-e-Shamsi ke bilkul darmiyan me hai aur baqi tamam siyare is ke gird chakkar lagate hain. Is ki wajah is ka hajm hai. Pure Nizam-e-Shamsi ke kul wazan ka takreeban tamam hissa, yani 99.86 feesad, akelay Suraj me hai. Baqi aath siyare, un ke chaand, pathar aur gard, sab mil kar bhi aik feesad se kam bante hain. Yehi wajah hai ke Suraj ki kashish sab ko apne gird bandhe rakhti hai, jese aik bara markaz apne chhote sathiyon ko sambhale rakhta hai.

Suraj kitna bara hai, is ka andaza yun lagao. Is ka qutar takreeban 13 lakh 90 hazar kilometre hai, yani Zameen ke qutar se takreeban 109 guna bara. Agar Suraj ko khokhla ghubaara samjho to is ke andar takreeban 13 lakh Zameenein sama sakti hain. Ye aisi misaal hai jo paper me bhi ati hai aur zehen me bhi reh jati hai.

Ab sawal ye hai ke Suraj itna garam aur roshan kyun hai. Is ke markaz me hydrogen gas shadeed dabao se helium me badalti rehti hai, aur is amal me bohat zyada tawanai banti hai jo roshni aur garmi ki surat me bahar nikalti hai. Isi liye Suraj ke markaz ka darja-e-hararat takreeban dedh crore degree centigrade hai, jabke is ki satah ka darja takreeban 5,500 degree hai. Suraj ki roshni ko Zameen tak pohnchne me takreeban 8 minute aur 20 second lagte hain. Yani tum jo Suraj dekh rahe ho, wo asal me 8 minute purana manzar hai. Suraj ki umar takreeban 4 arab 60 crore saal hai, aur Zameen par zindagi ki buniyad hi is ki roshni aur garmi hai. Paper ke liye ye lines pakki kar lo: Suraj aik sitara hai, Nizam-e-Shamsi ka markaz hai, sab se bara hissa hai, aur is ki roshni 8 minute se zyada waqt me hum tak pohnchti hai.

Pehla siyara: Mercury — Utarid

Suraj se sab se qareeb siyara Mercury hai, jise Urdu me Utarid kehte hain. Ye Nizam-e-Shamsi ka sab se chhota siyara hai, itna chhota ke hamare Chaand se bas thora sa bara hai. Aur ye sab se tez siyara bhi hai. Tez se murad ye hai ke Suraj ke gird is ka chakkar sab se jaldi pura hota hai, sirf 88 din me. Socho, jahan Zameen apna saal 365 din me pura karti hai, wahan Mercury ka saal 88 din ka hai. Faasla sab se kam hai, is liye safar bhi sab se chhota aur raftar bhi sab se zyada, takreeban 47 kilometre fi second. Lekin apne gird ghoomne me ye sust hai, is ka aik din Zameen ke 59 dinon ke barabar hota hai. Ye farq zehen me rakho, paper yahin phansata hai. Sab se tez chakkar Suraj ke gird Mercury ka hai, lekin sab se chhota din Mercury ka nahi, Jupiter ka hai. Wo baat aage ayegi.

Mercury ka koi chaand nahi hai. Is ke gird koi faza, yani atmosphere, bhi nahi hai. Isi liye wahan din aur raat ke darja-e-hararat me zameen asmaan ka farq hai. Din ko satah 430 degree tak garam ho jati hai aur raat ko minus 180 degree tak thandi. Faza hoti to garmi ko rok kar rakhti, faza na honay se garmi foran ur jati hai. Paper facts ye hain: sab se chhota siyara, Suraj ke sab se qareeb, sab se tez chakkar, saal sirf 88 din, chaand aik bhi nahi.

Dusra siyara: Venus — Zohra

Dusra number Venus ka hai, Urdu me Zohra. Ye hamara sab se qareebi parosi siyara hai aur Zameen ki judwa behen kahlata hai, kyunke is ka size aur wazan Zameen se bohat milta julta hai. Lekin tabiyat me ye Zameen se bilkul ulta hai. Venus is pure nizam ka sab se garam siyara hai. Sunne me ajeeb lagega ke Mercury Suraj ke zyada qareeb hai, phir garam Venus kyun? Wajah is ki faza hai. Venus ke gird carbon dioxide gas ki bohat moti aur ghani faza hai jo Suraj ki garmi ko andar to ane deti hai lekin bahar nikalne nahi deti, bilkul us greenhouse ki tarah jisme sabziyan garam mahol me rakhi jati hain. Isi greenhouse effect ki wajah se Venus ki satah ka darja takreeban 465 degree centigrade rehta hai, jo Mercury ke din se bhi zyada hai. Paper ka sab se mashhoor trap yehi hai: sab se qareeb Mercury hai, lekin sab se garam Venus hai.

Venus asmaan ka sab se chamakdar siyara bhi hai. Subah suraj nikalne se pehle ya sham ko doobne ke baad jo sab se roshan tara nazar ata hai, aksar wahi Venus hota hai. Isi liye ise Bhor ka tara aur Sham ka tara kehte hain, halanke ye tara hai hi nahi, siyara hai. Ab is ki sab se ajeeb baat suno. Venus ulta ghoomta hai, yani baqi siyaron ke bar-aks simt me. Aur apne gird itna ahista ghoomta hai ke is ka aik din is ke pure saal se lamba hai. Apne gird aik chakkar me 243 din, aur Suraj ke gird aik chakkar me 225 din. Yani Venus par din, saal se bara hai. Is ka bhi koi chaand nahi. Paper facts: sab se garam, sab se chamakdar, Zameen ki judwa behen, ulta ghoomta hai, din saal se lamba, chaand koi nahi.

Teesra siyara: Earth — Zameen

Teesra number hamari apni Zameen ka hai. Aath siyaron me ye wahid siyara hai jahan zindagi mojood hai, aur ye baat is ki har pehchan se bari hai. Ise Neela Siyara kehte hain. Wajah saaf hai, is ki satah ka takreeban 71 feesad hissa pani se dhaka hai, aur khala se dekhne par samandaron ka neela rang sab par ghalib nazar ata hai. Yehi pani, munasib faasla aur saans lene ke qabil faza, teeno cheezain mil kar zindagi ko mumkin banati hain. Zameen Suraj se na itni qareeb hai ke sab kuch jal jaye, na itni door ke sab jam jaye. Is munasib faasle wale hisse ko hi zindagi ke liye mozoon ilaqa samjha jata hai.

Zameen Suraj ke gird aik chakkar 365 din aur takreeban 6 ghanton me pura karti hai, yehi hamara aik saal hai, aur un extra ghanton ki wajah se har chaar saal baad February 29 din ka ata hai, jise leap year kehte hain. Apne gird ye 24 ghanton me ghoomti hai, jis se din aur raat bante hain. Zameen apne mehwar par takreeban sadhe 23 degree jhuki hui hai, aur yehi jhukao mausamon ki asal wajah hai. Is ka sirf aik chaand hai, jo raat ke asmaan ki sab se roshan cheez hai. Aik aur dilchasp paper fact ye hai ke Zameen wahid siyara hai jis ka naam kisi devi devta ke naam par nahi rakha gaya, baqi saton ke naam purani tehzeebon ke devtaon par hain. Paper facts: wahid zindagi wala siyara, Neela Siyara, 71 feesad pani, aik chaand, saal 365 din, jhukao sadhe 23 degree.

Chautha siyara: Mars — Mareekh

Chautha siyara Mars hai, Urdu me Mareekh. Ise duniya bhar me Red Planet, yani Surkh Siyara, kehte hain. Ab wajah samajh lo, kyunke paper wajah wala pehlu bhi parkhta hai. Mars ki satah aur is ki gard me iron oxide ki bharmaar hai. Iron oxide wahi cheez hai jise hum aam zuban me zang kehte hain, jo lohe par lagta hai aur use surkh bhura kar deta hai. Mars ki mitti asal me zang lagi hui mitti hai, is liye poora siyara door se surkh nazar ata hai aur is ki faza me urti gard use surkh asmaan bhi deti hai. Bas ye jumla pakka kar lo: Mars is liye surkh hai kyunke is ki mitti me lohe ka zang, yani iron oxide, mila hai.

Mars Zameen se chhota hai, is ka saal 687 din ka hota hai, lekin is ka din Zameen ke din jesa hi hai, takreeban sadhe 24 ghante. Is ke do chhote se chaand hain, jin ke naam Phobos aur Deimos hain. Nizam-e-Shamsi ka sab se bara aatish fashan, yani volcano, bhi Mars par hai, jis ka naam Olympus Mons hai, aur wo Zameen ke sab se unche pahar Mount Everest se takreeban dhai guna uncha hai. Mars ke dono qatbon par barf ki safed topiyan bhi mojood hain. Aaj kal ki khabron me Mars is liye rehta hai ke insan wahan pohnchne ki tayyari kar raha hai, aur wahan bheje gaye roboti jahaaz is ki satah par ghum phir kar maloomat bhej rahe hain. Paper facts: Red Planet, wajah iron oxide yani zang, do chaand Phobos aur Deimos, sab se bara aatish fashan Olympus Mons, saal 687 din.

Mars aur Jupiter ke darmiyan patti

Aage barhne se pehle aik zaroori jagah par ruk jao. Mars ke baad Jupiter se pehle, darmiyan me patharon ki aik bohat bari patti hai jise Asteroid Belt kehte hain. Is me lakhon chhote bare pathar Suraj ke gird ghoom rahe hain, jese aik adhoora siyara jo kabhi ban hi na paya ho. Paper me ye sawal ata hai ke Asteroid Belt kin do siyaron ke darmiyan hai, aur jawab hai Mars aur Jupiter. Is patti ke baad jo chaar siyare ate hain wo pehle chaaron se bilkul mukhtalif hain. Pehle chaar chhote aur pathreelay hain, baad wale chaar bohat bare aur gas ke bane hain. Inhen gas ke dev, yani gas giants, kaha jata hai. Ye taqseem bhi zehen me bitha lo: andar wale chaar pathreelay, bahar wale chaar gas wale.

Paanchwa siyara: Jupiter — Mushtari

Paanchwa number Jupiter ka hai, Urdu me Mushtari, aur ye pure Nizam-e-Shamsi ka sab se bara siyara hai. Kitna bara, is ka andaza yun lagao ke is ke andar takreeban 1,300 Zameenein sama sakti hain, aur baqi tamam saath siyaron ko mila lo to bhi Jupiter un sab se zyada wazan rakhta hai. Ye gas ka bana hai, is ki koi thos satah nahi jis par khara hua ja sake.

Jupiter ki sab se mashhoor pehchan is ka Great Red Spot hai, yani Bara Surkh Nishan. Ye asal me aik azeem toofan hai jo is ki faza me soiyon saalon se chal raha hai. Toofan kitna bara hai, suno: is ke andar hamari poori Zameen sama sakti hai. Yani jis cheez ko hum nishan keh rahe hain, wo hamari duniya se bari hai. Ab wo baat jo upar wada ki thi. Jupiter apne gird sab se tez ghoomta hai, is ka din sirf takreeban 10 ghanton ka hai, jo tamam siyaron me sab se chhota din hai. Lekin Suraj ke gird is ka chakkar sust hai, takreeban 12 saal, kyunke faasla bohat zyada hai. Jupiter ke chaandon ki tadaad bhi bohat zyada hai, tasdeeq shuda counts ke mutabiq 95 ke qareeb, aur in me Ganymede sab se khaas hai. Ganymede pure Nizam-e-Shamsi ka sab se bara chaand hai, itna bara ke wo Mercury siyare se bhi bara hai. Paper facts: sab se bara siyara, Great Red Spot aik azeem toofan hai, sab se chhota din takreeban 10 ghante, saal takreeban 12 saal, sab se bara chaand Ganymede.

Chhata siyara: Saturn — Zohal

Chhata siyara Saturn hai, Urdu me Zohal, aur khubsoorti me is ka koi muqabla nahi. Ise dekhte hi jo cheez nazar ati hai wo is ke gird bane hue roshan chhallay, yani rings, hain. Ye chhallay koi thos plate nahi hai. Ye barf aur pathar ke be-shumar chhote bare tukre hain jo Saturn ke gird chakkar laga rahe hain, aur door se aik khoobsurat thaali ki surat nazar ate hain. In chhallon ki khoobsurati ki wajah se Saturn ko aksar Nizam-e-Shamsi ka sab se khoobsurat siyara kaha jata hai.

Saturn Jupiter ke baad dusra sab se bara siyara hai. Aik mazay dar fact ye hai ke ye tamam siyaron me sab se halka hai, is ki kasafat, yani density, pani se bhi kam hai. Agar kahin itna bara samandar mil jaye ke is me Saturn dala ja sake, to ye doobega nahi, terne lagega. Ab sab se aham paper fact. Is waqt ke tasdeeq shuda counts ke mutabiq sab se zyada chaand Saturn ke hain, 146 ke qareeb, aur is hisaab se ye Jupiter se bhi aage hai. Ye wala sawal paper me bar bar ata hai aur bohat se students purani kitabon ki wajah se Jupiter likh ate hain. Tum ye pakka kar lo: sab se zyada chaand Saturn ke hain. Saturn ka aik chaand Titan bhi khaas hai, kyunke us ke gird ghani faza mojood hai. Saturn ka saal takreeban sadhe 29 saal ka hota hai aur is ka din takreeban sadhe 10 ghante ka. Paper facts: khoobsurat chhallay jo barf aur pathar ke tukron se bane hain, dusra sab se bara siyara, sab se halka siyara, sab se zyada chaand, saal takreeban sadhe 29 saal.

Saatwa siyara: Uranus

Saatwa number Uranus ka hai. Ye wo siyara hai jise leta hua siyara kaha jata hai, aur wajah bohat dilchasp hai. Baqi tamam siyare apne mehwar par takreeban seedhe khare ghoomte hain, jese lattu ghoomta hai. Lekin Uranus takreeban 98 degree jhuka hua hai, yani ye aik taraf ludhka hua hai aur is surat me ghoomta hai jese koi gend ludhakti hui ja rahi ho. Mahireen ka khayal hai ke kisi zamane me kisi bohat bari cheez se takrao ne ise is halat me kar diya hoga. Uranus bhi Venus ki tarah ulta ghoomta hai. Is ka rang halka neela sabz hai, aur ye rang is ki faza me mojood methane gas ki wajah se hai. Ye Suraj se bohat door hai, is liye wahan shadeed thand hoti hai, aur is ki faza me Nizam-e-Shamsi ka sab se kam darja-e-hararat record hua hai, minus 224 degree ke qareeb. Is ka saal takreeban 84 saal ka hota hai, yani jo bacha Uranus ke aik saal pehle paida ho, wo apni pehli salgirah par 84 saal ka hoga. Is ke chaandon ki tadaad 28 ke qareeb hai. Paper facts: leta hua siyara, 98 degree jhukao, halka neela sabz rang methane ki wajah se, ulta ghoomta hai, saal 84 saal.

Aathwa siyara: Neptune

Aathwa aur aakhri siyara Neptune hai. Ye Suraj se sab se door siyara hai, is liye is tak Suraj ki roshni bohat madham ho kar pohnchti hai aur wahan sakht tareen thand hoti hai. Neptune ka rang gehra neela hai, aur ye neela rang bhi is ki faza ki methane ki wajah se hai, bilkul Uranus wali wajah, bas rang us se gehra hai. Ise yaad rakhne ka tareeqa ye hai ke neele rang wale dono behn bhai aakhir me ate hain, pehle halka neela sabz Uranus, phir gehra neela Neptune.

Neptune ki aik aur pehchan is ki tez hawaein hain. Pure Nizam-e-Shamsi ki sab se tez hawaein yahin chalti hain, jin ki raftar 2,000 kilometre fi ghanta se bhi zyada ho sakti hai. Is ka saal tamam siyaron me sab se lamba hai, takreeban 165 saal. Is ka matlab samjho: Neptune 1846 me daryaft hua tha, aur is ne Suraj ke gird apna pehla chakkar 2011 me ja kar pura kiya. Yani daryaft ke baad ise aik saal pura karne me 165 saal lag gaye. Is ke chaandon ki tadaad 16 ke qareeb hai, jin me Triton sab se bara hai. Paper facts: sab se door siyara, gehra neela rang, sab se tez hawaein, sab se lamba saal 165 saal, sab se bara chaand Triton.

Pluto ka kissa

Ab aik chhota sa kissa jo paper me zaroor ata hai. Purani kitabon me tumhe nau siyare likhe milenge, aur nauwa naam Pluto hoga. 2006 me duniya bhar ke mahireen ne siyare ki tareef tay ki, aur Pluto us tareef par pura na utra. Tab se Pluto ko siyara nahi, bona siyara, yani dwarf planet, kaha jata hai. Isi liye ab siyare aath hain, nau nahi. Agar paper me sawal aye ke Pluto ko siyaron ki fehrist se kab nikala gaya, to jawab hai 2006. Aur agar kitabon me nau siyare parho to ghabrana nahi, wo 2006 se pehle ki kitabein hain.

Tartib yaad karne ki trick

Ab aath siyaron ki tartib hamesha ke liye zehen me bithane ka tareeqa. Suraj se faasle ki tartib ye hai: Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune. Ise yaad karne ke liye Angrezi ka aik purana jumla hai: My Very Educated Mother Just Served Us Noodles. Is jumle ke har lafz ka pehla harf hi siyare ka pehla harf hai. My se Mercury, Very se Venus, Educated se Earth, Mother se Mars, Just se Jupiter, Served se Saturn, Us se Uranus aur Noodles se Neptune. Apni ammi ke hath ke noodles wali tasveer bana lo, tartib kabhi nahi bhulegi.

Aik dusri madad ye hai. Andar wale chaar, yani Mercury, Venus, Earth aur Mars, chhote aur pathreelay hain. Beech me Asteroid Belt hai. Bahar wale chaar, yani Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus aur Neptune, bare aur gas wale hain. Aur size ki tartib alag hai, use faasle wali tartib se mix mat karna. Size me sab se bara Jupiter, phir Saturn, phir Uranus, phir Neptune, phir Zameen, phir Venus, phir Mars, aur sab se chhota Mercury. Paper in dono tartibon ko mila kar phansata hai, tum dono ko alag alag zehen me rakho.

Sab se wali recap — paper ke pakke sawal

Aakhir me wo tamam baatein aik sath dohra lo jin par paper ke sawal bante hain. Sab se bara siyara Jupiter hai. Sab se chhota siyara Mercury hai. Suraj ke sab se qareeb Mercury hai. Sab se door Neptune hai. Sab se garam siyara Venus hai, greenhouse effect ki wajah se. Sab se chamakdar siyara bhi Venus hai, Bhor ka tara. Surkh siyara Mars hai, mitti ke zang, yani iron oxide, ki wajah se. Zindagi wala wahid siyara Zameen hai, Neela Siyara. Sab se zyada chaand Saturn ke hain. Sab se bara chaand Jupiter ka Ganymede hai. Sab se chhota din Jupiter ka hai, takreeban 10 ghante. Sab se lamba saal Neptune ka hai, 165 saal, aur sab se chhota saal Mercury ka, 88 din. Sab se khoobsurat chhallay Saturn ke hain. Leta hua siyara Uranus hai. Sab se tez hawaein Neptune ki hain. Aur sab ke markaz me Suraj hai, jo siyara nahi, sitara hai. Agar ye recap tumhe bina dekhe sunai ja sake, to samjho Nizam-e-Shamsi ke sawal tumhare pakke ho gaye.

Aakhir me tasveer ke do khayal

Is lesson ke liye pehli tasveer aath siyaron ki aik lambi patti wali ho, jisme Suraj aik kinare par bara sa bana ho aur tamam siyare faasle ki tartib me khare hon. Har siyare ka size us ke asal size ke hisaab se chhota bara ho, taake student aik nazar me dekh le ke Jupiter ke samne Zameen kitni chhoti hai aur Mercury to us ke samne nuqta sa hai. Har siyare ke neeche us ka naam aur aik lafz me us ki pehchan likhi ho, jese Mars ke neeche Surkh, Saturn ke neeche Chhallay, Venus ke neeche Sab se Garam.

Dusri tasveer sab se wali chart ho. Aik taraf sawal ho, sab se bara, sab se garam, sab se door, sab se zyada chaand, aur dusri taraf us siyare ki chhoti si tasveer ke sath jawab. Ye chart revision ke liye hai, paper se aik raat pehle student is aik tasveer ko dekh le to pure lesson ka khulasa zehen me taaza ho jayega.
