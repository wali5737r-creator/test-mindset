Asmaan ki Cheezain — Star, Comet, Galaxy aur baqi
Har cheez pehle hai kya, phir us ki pehchan kya hai

Ye topic kyun zaroori hai

Dekho, one paper ke Everyday Science hisse me asmaan wala hissa har saal ata hai. Mushkil ye nahi ke sawal sakht hote hain, mushkil ye hai ke alfaaz bohat milte julte hain. Meteor, meteorite, asteroid, comet — student in me ulajh kar number kata deta hai. Is liye is topic me hum har cheez ko aik hi tartib se parhenge. Pehle matlab, ye cheez hai kya. Phir pehchan, paper me ya asmaan me use pehchanna kese hai. Aur aakhir me wo baat jo paper me sawal banti hai. Tartib ye zehen me bitha lo to ye uljhane wale alfaaz hamesha ke liye sulajh jayenge.

Sitara (Star)

Matlab ye hai. Sitara garam gaso ka aik bohat bara gola hai jo apni roshni aur apni garmi khud banata hai. Is ke andar sab se zyada hydrogen hoti hai, us ke baad helium. Sitare ke markaz me dabao aur garmi itni shadeed hoti hai ke wahan nuclear fusion hota hai, yani hydrogen ke atom jud jud kar helium bante hain aur is amal se bohat bari miqdar me roshni aur garmi bahar nikalti hai. Bas yehi roshni lakho kilometre ka safar karke hum tak pohanchti hai.

Pehchan ye hai. Sitare raat ko timtimate (twinkle) nazar ate hain. Mazay ki baat ye hai ke timtimana sitare ka kaam nahi hai, ye hamari atmosphere ki karistani hai. Sitare ki roshni jab hawa ki tehno se guzarti hai to bar bar terhi hoti hai, is liye humein lagta hai ke sitara jhapak raha hai. Sitaron ka rang bhi un ki garmi batata hai. Neelay sitare sab se garam hote hain, safed darmiyan me, aur laal sitare sab se thande.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Suraj hamara sab se qareebi sitara hai. Ye jumla itna ata hai ke ab to isay GK ki bunyad samjho. Aur Suraj ke baad sab se qareebi sitara Proxima Centauri hai, jo taqreeban 4.2 light year door hai. Light year kya hai, wo aage isi topic me araha hai.

Siyara (Planet)

Matlab ye hai. Siyara wo jism hai jo kisi sitare ke gird aik muqarrar raaste me ghoomta hai, jise orbit kehte hain, aur us ki apni koi roshni nahi hoti. Wo sitare ki roshni ko reflect karke chamakta hai. Planet lafz ka matlab bhi bhatakne wala hai, kyunke purane zamane ke log dekhte the ke ye roshniyan baqi sitaron ke muqable me asmaan me apni jagah badalti rehti hain. Asal me wo sitare nahi, hamare apne nizam ke siyare the jo Suraj ke gird ghoomte hue idhar udhar hote dikhte the.

Pehchan sitare se farq me hai, aur ye farq paper ka pasandeeda hai. Siyare timtimate nahi hain. Un ki roshni mustaqil (steady) rehti hai. Wajah ye hai ke siyare hum se bohat qareeb hain aur humein aik nuqte ki bajaye aik chhoti si gol shakal (disc) ki surat me dikhte hain, is liye un ki roshni hawa me utni nahi bikhar ti. To usool yaad rakho: timtimaye to sitara, mustaqil chamke to siyara ho sakta hai.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Hamare nizam-e-shamsi (solar system) me aath siyare hain. Jupiter sab se bara siyara hai, Mercury Suraj ke sab se qareeb hai aur Neptune sab se door. Venus sab se zyada chamakne wala hai, use subah ka sitara aur sham ka sitara kaha jata hai, lekin naam ke bawajood wo sitara nahi, siyara hai — yehi paper ka trap hai. Aur Pluto ko 2006 me siyaron ki list se nikal kar dwarf planet (chhota siyara) kar diya gaya tha, is liye siyare nau nahi, aath hain.

Qudrati Satellite (Chaand)

Matlab ye hai. Jo jism kisi siyare ke gird ghoomta hai, use us siyare ka satellite kehte hain, aur jo satellite qudrat ne banaya ho use qudrati (natural) satellite kehte hain. Zameen ka qudrati satellite sirf aik hai, aur wo hamara Chaand hai. Dusre siyaron ke chaand bohat zyada hain, masalan Saturn ke 140 se zyada chaand maloom ho chuke hain, lekin zameen ke hisse me sirf aik aya hai.

Chaand ki asal baatein ye hain. Chaand zameen se taqreeban 3 lakh 84 hazar kilometre door hai aur zameen ke gird aik chakkar taqreeban 27.3 din me pura karta hai. Is ki apni roshni koi nahi, ye Suraj ki roshni reflect karke chamakta hai. Aik khaas baat ye hai ke Chaand apne mehwar (axis) par ghoomne me bhi utna hi waqt leta hai jitna zameen ke gird chakkar me, yani 27.3 din. Is pur-asrar barabari ki wajah se hum zameen se hamesha Chaand ki aik hi taraf dekhte hain, dusri taraf hamesha hum se chhupi rehti hai. Aur Chaand ki kashish hi samandaron me madd-o-jazr (tides) paida karti hai, yani pani ka charhna aur utarna.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Chaand par pehla qadam rakhne wala insaan Neil Armstrong tha, 20 July 1969 ko, Apollo 11 mission me. Ye GK ka sab se mashhoor sawalon me se aik hai. Aur ye bhi yaad rakho ke Chaand par atmosphere nahi hai, is liye wahan na awaaz safar kar sakti hai na mausam hota hai.

Masnooi Satellite (Artificial Satellite)

Matlab ye hai. Masnooi ka matlab hai insaan ka banaya hua. To masnooi satellite wo machine hai jo insaan ne bana kar rocket ke zariye space me pohanchai, aur ab wo zameen ke gird aik muqarrar orbit me ghoom rahi hai. Qudrati satellite qudrat ne banaya, masnooi satellite insaan ne — bas yehi dono ka bunyadi farq hai.

In ka kaam kya hai, ye samjho to satellite kabhi nahi bhulega. Communication satellites TV channels, mobile phone aur internet ke signals ko aik jagah se dusri jagah pohanchate hain. Weather satellites badalon aur toofanon ki tasveerein bhej kar mausam ki peshangoi mumkin banate hain, sailab aur samandari toofan ki warning inhi ki wajah se pehle mil jati hai. GPS satellites tumhare mobile ko batate hain ke tum is waqt khare kahan ho. Is ke ilawa zameen ki nigrani karne wale satellites faslon, janglon, barf aur sheheron ki tabdeeli par nazar rakhte hain aur science ki research ke satellites alag hain.

Paper wali baatein teen hain aur teeno fix hain. Duniya ka pehla masnooi satellite Sputnik 1 tha, jo Soviet Union (maujooda Russia) ne 4 October 1957 ko space me bheja. Pakistan ka pehla satellite Badr-1 tha, jo 1990 me China ki madad se bheja gaya. Aur Pakistan ki space agency ka naam SUPARCO hai, jo 1961 me bani thi. SUPARCO ka pura naam hai Space and Upper Atmosphere Research Commission. Sputnik ka 1957, SUPARCO ka 1961 aur Badr-1 ka 1990, ye teen saal apni jagah pakke kar lo.

Dum-daar Sitara (Comet)

Matlab ye hai. Comet barf, gard (dust) aur pathar ke tukron ka bana hua aik gola hai jo Suraj ke gird bohat lambe, ande ki shakal wale (elliptical) orbit me ghoomta hai. Scientists isay aksar ganda barf ka gola, yani dirty snowball, kehte hain, kyunke asal me ye jami hui gaso, barf aur mitti pathar ka mila jula dhair hai. Urdu me isay dum-daar sitara kehte hain, lekin naam ke bawajood ye sitara nahi hai.

Pehchan is ki dum hai, aur dum ka qissa hi asal concept hai. Jab comet apne lambe orbit me ghoomte hue Suraj ke qareeb ata hai to Suraj ki garmi se is ki barf bhaap ban kar urne lagti hai. Is ke gird roshni wala dhundla sa ghilaaf banta hai jise coma kehte hain, aur peeche ki taraf aik lambi chamakti dum (tail) nikal ati hai jo lakhon kilometre lambi ho sakti hai. Ab sab se aham baat, jo paper ka mashhoor trap hai. Dum hamesha Suraj se ulti simt (opposite direction) me hoti hai. Comet Suraj ki taraf ja raha ho ya Suraj se door wapas ja raha ho, dum har haal me Suraj ke opposite hi rahegi, kyunke Suraj ki roshni aur Suraj se ane wali hawa (solar wind) use peeche dhakelti rehti hai. Dum peeche ki taraf is liye nahi hoti ke comet aage barh raha hai, dum Suraj se door is liye hoti hai ke Suraj use dhakel raha hai.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Sab se mashhoor comet Halley ka comet hai. Ye har taqreeban 76 saal ke baad wapas nazar ata hai. Aakhri dafa ye 1986 me nazar aya tha aur agli dafa 2061 me ayega. Is ka naam scientist Edmond Halley ke naam par hai, jis ne is ke purane zahoor ka hisaab laga kar is ki wapsi ki peshangoi ki thi. 76 saal, 1986 aur 2061, ye teen number Halley ke sath jor kar yaad rakho.

Asteroid

Matlab ye hai. Asteroid Suraj ke gird ghoomne wale chhote pathreele jism hain. Ye asal me wo pathar aur dhat (metal) ke tukre hain jo nizam-e-shamsi ke bante waqt siyare na ban sake aur yunhi ghoomte reh gaye. In ki koi tay shuda gol shakal nahi hoti, aksar ye be-tarteeb, aalu jaisi shakal ke hote hain.

Pehchan aur jagah ye hai. Zyada tar asteroids aik patti (belt) ki surat me Mars aur Jupiter ke darmiyan ghoomte hain, isay Asteroid Belt kehte hain. Comet se farq yahan pakka kar lo. Asteroid pathar aur dhat ka hota hai, comet barf ka hota hai. Asteroid ki koi dum nahi hoti, comet ki dum hi us ki pehchan hai. Ye do farq paper me bar bar ate hain.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Asteroid Belt Mars aur Jupiter ke darmiyan hai, ye jumla ratt lo. Aur sab se bara asteroid Ceres hai, jise ab dwarf planet bhi kaha jata hai.

Meteor (Tootta Sitara, Shooting Star)

Matlab ye hai. Space se ane wala koi chhota sa pathar ya gard ka zarra jab zameen ki atmosphere me bohat tez raftaar se dakhil hota hai to hawa ki shadeed ragar (friction) se garam ho kar jal uthta hai aur asmaan me aik lamhe ke liye roshni ki lakeer si ban jati hai. Bas yehi jalti hui lakeer meteor hai. Awam isay tootta sitara ya shooting star kehte hain, lekin naam ke bawajood is ka sitaron se koi taluq nahi. Koi sitara nahi tootta, aik chhota sa zarra jalta hai.

Pehchan aur kaam ye hai. Zyada tar meteors itne chhote hote hain ke wo atmosphere ki Mesosphere layer me hi jal kar raakh ho jate hain aur zameen tak pohanchte hi nahi. Jese Atmosphere wale topic me parha tha, ye layer zameen ki hifazati dhaal ka kaam karti hai. Kabhi kabhi aik sath bohat se meteors nazar ate hain, jese asmaan se roshni ki barish ho rahi ho, use meteor shower kehte hain. Ye tab hota hai jab zameen kisi comet ke chhore hue gard ke badal se guzarti hai.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Shooting star asal me sitara nahi hota, ye jumla statement wale sawal me ata hai. Aur meteor ka jalna atmosphere me hota hai, space me nahi.

Meteorite

Matlab ye hai. Jo meteor itna bara ho ke atmosphere me pura na jale aur bach kar zameen ki satah tak pohanch jaye, use meteorite kehte hain. Ye zameen par gir kar kabhi kabhi aik bara garha (crater) bhi bana deta hai.

Ab teeno ka farq aik sath samajh lo, kyunke ye silsila paper ka sab se pasandeeda hai. Aik hi pathar ke teen marahil hain aur har marhale ka naam alag hai. Jab wo pathar space me ghoom raha ho to use meteoroid kehte hain. Jab wo atmosphere me dakhil ho kar jalti hui lakeer banaye to wo meteor hai. Aur jab wo bach kar zameen par gir jaye to wo meteorite ban jata hai. Tartib is jumle se yaad rakho: space wala meteoroid, jala to meteor, gira to meteorite.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Duniya ke mashhoor craters me America ke Arizona ka Meteor Crater hai. Aur aik bohat mashhoor GK baat ye bhi hai ke taqreeban 6 crore 60 lakh saal pehle aik bare asteroid ya meteorite ke zameen se takrane ko hi dinosaurs ke khatam honay ki sab se bari wajah mana jata hai. Wo takrao Mexico ke ilaqe me hua tha.

Galaxy (Kahkashan)

Matlab ye hai. Galaxy sitaron ka aik bohat bara sheher hai. Karoron aur arbon sitaron, un ke siyaron, gas aur gard ka wo wasee majmua jo kashish (gravity) ki wajah se aik sath bandha hota hai, galaxy kahlata hai. Aam galaxy me 100 arab (100 billion) se zyada sitare hote hain, aur har sitare ke gird us ke apne siyare ho sakte hain.

Hamari galaxy ka naam Milky Way hai, Urdu me use Kahkashan kehte hain. Kisi saaf aur andheri raat me asmaan me jo safed dhundli si patti aik kinare se dusre kinare tak phaili dikhti hai, wo hamari apni galaxy hi hai jise hum andar se, kinare ki taraf se, dekh rahe hote hain. Hamara Suraj is galaxy ke arbon sitaron me se sirf aik aam darjane ka sitara hai. Milky Way ki shakal spiral hai, yani ghumao-daar, jese ghoomta hua chakkar.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Hamari sab se qareebi bari galaxy Andromeda hai, jo taqreeban 25 lakh light year door hai. Aur pure universe me arbon galaxies hain, har galaxy me arbon sitare. Sawal aksar is tartib se ata hai ke Suraj kis galaxy me hai, jawab Milky Way hai.

Universe (Kainat)

Matlab ye hai. Jo kuch bhi mojood hai, sab ka sab universe hai. Tamam galaxies, tamam sitare, tamam siyare, khala, waqt, roshni, energy, har cheez ka kul majmua universe kahlata hai. Is se bahar ki koi cheez is tareef me nahi ati, kyunke universe ka matlab hi sab kuch hai.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Aaj ki science ke mutabiq universe taqreeban 13.8 arab saal pehle aik bohat hi chhote aur ghane nuqte se aik bare dhamake se shuru hua, is nazariye ko Big Bang kehte hain. Tab se universe lagatar phail raha hai aur galaxies aik dusre se door ja rahi hain. Big Bang aur phailta hua universe, ye dono baatein fix sawal hain.

Light Year (Noori Saal)

Matlab ye hai, aur pehle is topic ka sab se bara trap samajh lo. Light year waqt ki ikai nahi hai, faasle ki ikai hai. Ye wo faasla hai jo roshni pure aik saal me tay karti hai. Naam me saal honay ki wajah se bohat se students use waqt samajh lete hain, aur paper bilkul yehi trap lagata hai.

Hisab is tarah hai. Roshni duniya ki sab se tez cheez hai, wo aik second me taqreeban 3 lakh kilometre chalti hai. Ab socho, jo cheez aik second me 3 lakh kilometre jati hai, wo pure saal me kitna jayegi. Jawab hai taqreeban 9.46 trillion kilometre, yani saade nau hazar arab kilometre se bhi zyada. Bas yehi aik light year hai.

Is ka kaam ye hai ke asmaan ke faasle itne bare hain ke aam kilometre me likhne se number sambhalta nahi. Is liye light year istemal hota hai. Do misaalein zehen me bitha lo. Suraj ki roshni zameen tak pohanchne me taqreeban 8 minute lagti hai, is ka matlab hai ke hum Suraj ko hamesha 8 minute purani halat me dekh rahe hote hain. Aur sab se qareebi sitara Proxima Centauri 4.2 light year door hai, yani us ki roshni ko hum tak ane me 4.2 saal lagte hain.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Light year faasle ki ikai hai, waqt ki nahi. Aur roshni ki raftaar 3 lakh kilometre per second hai. Ye do baatein har doosre paper me ghumti hain.

Black Hole

Matlab ye hai. Black hole khala me wo jaga hai jahan kashish (gravity) itni shadeed hai ke koi cheez wahan se bahar nahi nikal sakti, hatta ke roshni bhi nahi. Isi liye ye kala hai. Jo cheez roshni bahar hi nahi ane deti, wo nazar kese ayegi. Scientists use seedha dekh nahi sakte, us ke aas paas ki cheezon ke bartao se pehchante hain.

Banta kese hai. Jab koi bohat bara sitara apni zindagi ke aakhir me apna fuel khatam kar deta hai to wo apne hi wazan ke neeche andar ki taraf dhas (collapse) jata hai. Agar sitara bohat bara ho to ye dhasna black hole par ja kar rukta hai. Us ke gird aik sarhad hoti hai jise event horizon kehte hain. Us sarhad ke andar jo gaya, us ki wapsi mumkin nahi, roshni ki bhi nahi.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Har bari galaxy ke markaz me aik bohat bara, supermassive, black hole hota hai. Hamari Milky Way ke markaz wale black hole ka naam Sagittarius A Star hai. Aur kisi black hole ki pehli asal tasveer 2019 me li gayi thi, jo M87 galaxy ke markaz ke black hole ki thi. Ye haal hi ke saalon ka mashhoor GK sawal hai.

Suraj Grehan (Solar Eclipse)

Matlab ye hai. Grehan ka matlab hai kisi roshni wali cheez ka chhup jana. Suraj grehan tab hota hai jab Chaand ghoomte hue Suraj aur Zameen ke darmiyan a jata hai. Chaand Suraj ki roshni ko rok leta hai, zameen ke kuch hisse par Chaand ka saya par jata hai aur din ke waqt kuch dair ke liye andhera sa chha jata hai.

Kab hota hai. Sirf naye chaand, yani new moon, wale din, jab Suraj, Chaand aur Zameen aik seedh me ajayein. Lekin har naye chaand par grehan nahi hota, kyunke Chaand ka orbit zara sa terha hai, is liye aksar Chaand ka saya zameen ke upar ya neeche se guzar jata hai. Grehan usi din hota hai jab teeno bilkul seedh me hon.

Is ki qismein bhi samajh lo. Kul grehan (total eclipse) me Suraj pura chhup jata hai aur din ke waqt asmaan me sitare nazar ane lagte hain. Juzwi grehan (partial) me Suraj ka sirf aik hissa chhupta hai. Aur aik qism me Chaand zameen se door honay ki wajah se chhota dikhta hai, to wo Suraj ke markaz ko chhupata hai lekin kinaron par roshni ki aik angothi (ring) reh jati hai, use annular eclipse kehte hain.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Suraj grehan me darmiyan me Chaand hota hai, ye position pakki yaad rakho. Aur suraj grehan ko nangi ankh se dekhna ankhon ke liye khatarnak hai, ye bhi sawal banta hai. Khaas glasses ke baghair seedha Suraj ki taraf grehan ke doran bhi nahi dekhna chahiye.

Chaand Grehan (Lunar Eclipse)

Matlab ye hai. Chaand grehan tab hota hai jab Zameen Suraj aur Chaand ke darmiyan a jati hai. Ab Zameen ka saya Chaand par parta hai, aur Chaand, jo Suraj ki roshni se chamakta tha, madham ho jata hai. Farq sirf darmiyan wali cheez ka hai. Suraj grehan me darmiyan me Chaand tha, chaand grehan me darmiyan me Zameen hai.

Kab hota hai. Sirf pure chaand, yani full moon, wali raat ko, jab teeno aik seedh me hon. Chaudhvin ke chaand wali raat hi chaand grehan ki raat ho sakti hai, is ke ilawa nahi.

Aik khaas baat ye hai ke kul chaand grehan me bhi Chaand pura gayab nahi hota. Zameen ki atmosphere se chhan kar aur mur kar ane wali halki laal roshni Chaand tak pohanch jati hai, is liye grehan wala Chaand aksar gehra laal ya tambay (copper) jaisa rang le leta hai. Isay Blood Moon kaha jata hai. Aur suraj grehan ke bar-aks, chaand grehan ko nangi ankh se dekhna bilkul mehfooz hai. Chaand grehan ghanto tak chal sakta hai aur jahan raat ho wahan ke bare hisse se aik sath dikhta hai, jabke suraj grehan chhote ilaqe me sirf chand minute ka hota hai.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Dono grehanon ka asal sawal position ka hai. Suraj grehan: Suraj, Chaand, Zameen. Chaand grehan: Suraj, Zameen, Chaand. Aur waqt ka farq bhi yaad rakho, suraj grehan naye chaand par, chaand grehan pure chaand par.

Chaand ki Shaklain (Phases of the Moon)

Matlab ye hai, aur yahan aik mashhoor ghalat fehmi pehle door kar lo. Chaand apni shakel asal me badalta hi nahi. Badalta sirf ye hai ke us ka roshan hissa humein kitna aur kis taraf se nazar ata hai. Chaand Suraj ki roshni reflect karta hai. Jab wo zameen ke gird ghoomta hai to kabhi us ka roshan hissa pura hamari taraf hota hai, kabhi aadha, aur kabhi bilkul hamari taraf nahi hota. Bas yehi badlao chaand ki shaklain kahlata hai.

Tartib ye hai. Pehle naya chaand ata hai, jab roshan hissa bilkul hum se ulti taraf hota hai aur chaand nazar hi nahi ata. Phir patla sa hilal (crescent) dikhta hai — Eid ka chaand yehi hilal hai. Phir aadha chaand banta hai, phir roshan hissa barhta jata hai, aur aakhir me pura chaand (full moon) chamakta hai, jise chaudhvin ka chaand kehte hain. Is ke baad roshan hissa isi tartib se ghatna shuru ho jata hai, hatta ke dobara naya chaand a jata hai. Ye pura chakkar taqreeban 29.5 din ka hai, aur yehi qamri (lunar) maheena hai. Ramzan, Eidain aur islami tareekhain isi cycle se tay hoti hain.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Chaand ki shaklain zameen ke saye ki wajah se nahi banti. Saya wali baat chaand grehan ki hai, jo saal me kabhi kabhar hota hai, jabke shaklain har maheene badalti hain aur un ki wajah Chaand ka zameen ke gird ghoomna aur Suraj ki roshni ka zawiya (angle) hai. In do cheezon ko mix karna paper ka mashhoor trap hai, is se bachna.

Aakhir me farq wali joriyan dohra lo

Aakhir me wo joriyan aik sath rakh kar yaad kar lo jin me paper phansata hai. Sitara apni roshni khud banata hai aur timtimata hai, siyara roshni reflect karta hai aur mustaqil chamakta hai. Comet barf ka hai aur us ki dum hoti hai, asteroid pathar ka hai aur us ki koi dum nahi hoti. Meteor wo hai jo atmosphere me jala, meteorite wo hai jo bach kar zameen par gira. Qudrati satellite qudrat ne banaya jese Chaand, masnooi satellite insaan ne banaya jese Badr-1. Aur light year waqt nahi, faasla hai. Ye paanch joriyan pakki ho jayein to asmaan wala hissa tumhara pakka ho gaya.
