Asnaaf-e-Sukhan — Nazm o Nasr ki Qismein
اصنافِ سخن. Ghazal, nazm, qasida, marsiya, masnavi, rubai aur nasr ki tamam qismein

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

One paper me urdu adab ke hisse me asnaaf-e-sukhan se har saal sawal ata hai. Paper ka andaaz ye hota hai ke kisi sanf ki tareef de kar us ka naam poocha jata hai, ya do asnaaf ka farq poocha jata hai, ya kisi mashhoor sanf ke mashhoor shair ka naam. Masla ye hai ke asnaaf bohot si hain aur students unhe aik dusre se mila dete hain. Marsiya ko qasida aur masnavi ko nazm keh dena aam ghalti hai. Is lesson me har sanf ko us ki pehchan wale nukton ke sath samjhaya gaya hai, taake paper me jo tareef bhi aye tum foran sanf ka naam pehchan lo. Har sanf ko us ki sab se mazboot pehchan ke sath yaad karo, baqi tafseel khud zehen me beth jayegi.

Pehle ghazal ka khulasa

غزل

Ghazal urdu shairi ki sab se maqbool sanf hai. Ye ashaar ka majmooa hoti hai jis ke tamam ashaar aik hi behr, aik hi qafiye aur aik hi radeef me bandhe hote hain, lekin har sher apna alag mazmoon rakhta hai. Pehle sher ko matla aur takhallus wale aakhri sher ko maqta kehte hain. Ghazal ke asal mazmoonat ishq, mehboob, hijr o wisal aur zindagi ke falsafiyana khayalat hain. Mir, Ghalib, Iqbal, Faiz aur Parveen Shakir ghazal ke bare naam hain. Paper me ghazal ko nazm se mila kar poocha jata hai, farq ye hai ke nazm me aik hi mazmoon musalsal chalta hai aur ghazal ke ashaar alag alag mazmoon rakhte hain. Ghazal ki mukammal istilahaat alag lesson me tafseel se di gayi hain.

Nazm aur us ki teen qismein

نظم

Nazm wo sanf hai jis me aik hi mazmoon ya khayal ko shuru se aakhir tak musalsal bayan kiya jata hai. Nazm ka koi aik unwan hota hai aur tamam ashaar usi unwan ke gird ghoomte hain. Nazm ki teen mashhoor qismein hain aur paper in teeno ka farq lazmi poochta hai. Paband nazm wo hai jis me behr, qafia aur radeef ki mukammal pabandi ho, jese Iqbal ki "شکوہ" aur Hali ki musaddas. Azad nazm me behr ki pabandi nahi hoti, misray chote bare ho sakte hain, lekin lehja aur aahang barqarar rehta hai, Noon Meem Rashid aur Meeraji is ke bare naam hain. Muarra nazm behr ki pabandi karti hai lekin qafia aur radeef ki pabandi nahi karti. Bas ye teen line ka farq yaad rakho: paband me sab pabandi, azad me wazan ki pabandi nahi, muarra me wazan hai lekin qafia radeef nahi.

Qasida tareef aur satayish ki sanf

قصیدہ

Qasida wo lambi nazm hai jis me kisi shakhsiyat, badshah ya sardar ki tareef aur madah bayan ki jaye, ya kisi ki burai me hajv kahi jaye. Tareef wale qaside ko qasida-e-madhiya aur burai wale ko qasida-e-hajviya kehte hain. Qasida ghazal ki tarah aik hi behr aur qafiye me hota hai aur is ka pehla sher bhi matla hota hai, lekin ghazal se farq ye hai ke qaside ka aik hi mazmoon hota hai aur ye bohot lamba hota hai. Qasida asal me arbi sanf hai aur wahan se farsi aur urdu me aya. Urdu me Sauda aur Zauq qasida-goi ke sab se bare naam hain, Mirza Rafi Sauda ko qaside ka badshah kaha jata hai. Paper me ye sawal ata hai ke urdu ke sab se bare qasida-nigar kon hain aur qasida kisay kehte hain. Pehchan ye hai ke jahan kisi ki shaan me lambi satayish nazar aye, wo qasida hai.

Marsiya Karbala ka gham

مرثیہ

Marsiya wo nazm hai jis me Hazrat Imam Hussain aur shuhada-e-Karbala ki shahadat ka zikr aur un ke gham ka izhar kiya jata hai. Marsiya ghazal ki tarah ashaar me nahi hota balke bandon me hota hai, aur urdu me ye aam taur par musaddas ki surat me likha gaya, yani har band me chhe misray hote hain. Marsiye ke mashhoor ajza hain, chehra yani shahadat ka pas-e-manzar, rukhsat, jang aur aakhir me bain yani gham ka izhar. Urdu marsiye ko Mir Anees aur Mirza Dabeer ne apne urooj tak pohanchaya, aur mashhoor kahawat hai ke Anees ka marsiya aur Dabeer ka marsiya apni apni jagah lajawab hain. Is ke ilawa Josh Malihabadi ne bhi marsiya likha. Paper me marsiya ka sawal Karbala, Anees aur Dabeer ke gird ghoomta hai. Ye bhi yaad rakho ke marsiya kisi bhi marne wale ki yaad me kaha ja sakta hai, lekin urdu me is se murad aam taur par Karbala ka marsiya liya jata hai.

Masnavi lambi dastaan

مثنوی

Masnavi wo lambi nazm hai jis me aik mukammal kahani ya dastaan musalsal bayan ki jati hai. Masnavi ki pehchan us ke qafiye se hoti hai. Is ke har sher ke dono misray apas me hum-qafia hote hain, aur har agle sher ka qafia pichle sher se badal jata hai. Yehi surat kahani ko lambai tak le jane me madad deti hai, kyunke shair ko aik hi qafiye ki pabandi nahi karni parti. Masnavi ke mazmoonat ishqia dastaanain, akhlaqi waqiat aur tasawwuf hote hain. Urdu ki sab se mashhoor masnavi Mir Hasan ki "سحر البیان" hai jisay مثنوی سحر البیان kehte hain, is ke ilawa Daya Shankar Naseem ki "گلزارِ نسیم" bhi bohot mashhoor hai. Farsi me Maulana Rumi ki masnavi duniya bhar me mashhoor hai. Paper me masnavi ki pehchan qafiye wale nuktay se poochi jati hai ke kis sanf me har sher ka qafia badal jata hai, jawab masnavi hai.

Rubai chaar misron ki sanf

رباعی

Rubai chaar misron par mushtamil chhoti si sanf hai jis me aik mukammal khayal ya falsafiyana baat bayan ki jati hai. Rubai ka apna khaas wazan hota hai jo har rubai me lazmi barqarar rehta hai. Is ke pehle, dusre aur chauthe misray hum-qafia hote hain, jabke teesra misra azaad hota hai. Rubai ki jaan chautha misra hota hai jahan baat apne urooj par pohanchti hai. Farsi me Umar Khayyam ki rubaiyan duniya bhar me mashhoor hain aur Edward FitzGerald ne un ka angrezi tarjuma kiya. Urdu me Amjad Hyderabadi ko rubai ka badshah kaha jata hai aur Mir Anees ne bhi rubaiyan likhi hain. Paper me rubai ki pehchan chaar misron aur hum-qafia wali tartib se hoti hai. Qata se is ka farq ye hai ke qata kisi bari nazm ya ghazal ka hissa ho sakta hai aur us ka wazan muqarrar nahi, jabke rubai hamesha chaar misron ki hoti hai aur apne khaas wazan me hoti hai.

Qata aik mazmoon ke ashaar

قطعہ

Qata do ya do se zyada ashaar ka aisa majmooa hai jis me aik hi mazmoon musalsal bayan ho. Qata ka matlab hai kata hua tukra, yani kisi bari cheez se kata hua hissa. Qata azaad taur par bhi likha jata hai aur kisi ghazal ya nazm ka hissa bhi ho sakta hai. Is ke ashaar me qafia aur radeef ki pabandi hoti hai aur dusra aur chautha misra hum-qafia hona is ki mashhoor surat hai. Akbar Allahabadi ke qataat bohot mashhoor hain. Paper me qata aur rubai ko mila kar poocha jata hai, farq ye hai ke rubai hamesha chaar misron ki hoti hai aur us ka apna khaas wazan hai, qata ke ashaar do se zyada bhi ho sakte hain aur wazan koi bhi ho sakta hai. Aik aur farq ye ke ghazal ke ashaar alag mazmoon rakhte hain, qata ke tamam ashaar aik hi mazmoon bayan karte hain.

Hamd Allah ki tareef

حمد

Wo nazm jis me Allah Taala ki tareef, us ki qudrat aur us ki nematon ka bayan ho, use hamd kehte hain. Hamd aam taur par deewan aur majmooon ke shuru me ati hai. Urdu ke taqreeban har bare shair ne hamd likhi hai, Hali aur Iqbal ki hamdiya shairi mashhoor hai. Paper me hamd aur naat ka farq lazmi poocha jata hai. Pehchan ka asaan tareeqa ye hai ke tareef Allah ki ho to hamd hai. Hamd me Allah ki sifaat, kainat ki takhleeq aur insan par us ke ihsanat bayan hote hain.

Naat Rasool ki tareef

نعت

Wo nazm jis me Hazrat Muhammad Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam ki tareef, ap ke akhlaq, ap ki seerat aur ap ki azmat ka bayan ho, use naat kehte hain. Naat urdu shairi ki muqaddas tareen sanf hai aur mehfilon me aqeedat se parhi jati hai. Maulana Ahmed Raza Khan Barelvi ki naat "مصطفیٰ جانِ رحمت پہ لاکھوں سلام" duniya bhar me mashhoor hai, aur Altaf Hussain Hali aur Iqbal ne bhi naatein likhi hain. Paper me naat ki tareef aur mashhoor naat-khawan shair pooche jate hain. Hamd se farq zehen me rakho, hamd Allah ki tareef hai aur naat Rasool Allah ki tareef hai. Ye aik line ka farq paper me har saal kaam ata hai.

Munqabat ahl-e-bait ki madah

منقبت

Munqabat wo nazm hai jis me Hazrat Ali Karamallahu Wajhahu ya ahl-e-bait aur aaimma ki tareef aur un ke fazail bayan kiye jate hain. Munqabat aur naat me farq ye hai ke naat me sirf Rasool Allah ki tareef hoti hai aur munqabat me ahl-e-bait ki madah hoti hai. Mir Anees aur Mirza Dabeer ne marsiyon ke sath munqabatein bhi likhi hain. Paper me munqabat ki tareef kam ati hai lekin jab ati hai to naat aur hamd ke options ke sath mila kar ati hai, is liye teeno ka farq aik sath yaad rakho. Hamd Allah ke liye, naat Rasool ke liye, munqabat ahl-e-bait ke liye.

Geet gana banne wali nazm

گیت

Geet wo chhoti nazm hai jo gaye jane ke liye likhi jati hai. Is me lehja ki narmi, alfaaz ki sada-pan aur musiqui hoti hai, aur aam taur par aik misra ya tukra baar baar dohraaya jata hai jise tek kehte hain. Geet ke mazmoonat mohabbat, fitrat ki khoobsurti aur josh o jazba hote hain. Urdu filmon ke geeton ne is sanf ko aam logon tak pohanchaya, Qateel Shifai aur Ahmed Faraz ke geet mashhoor hain. Is ke ilawa Hafeez Jalandhari ka likha hua Pakistan ka qaumi tarana bhi isi lehje ki mashhoor misal hai. Paper me geet ki pehchan us ke gaye jane aur dohraaye jane wale hisse se hoti hai.

Ab nasr ki asnaaf mukhtasar magar mukammal

Nazm ke baad nasr ki bari ati hai. Nasr wo tehreer hai jo shairi ke wazan aur qafiye ke baghair aam guftagu wale andaaz me likhi jaye. Novel, afsana, drama aur mazmoon sab nasr ki qismein hain. Paper me nasr ki asnaaf se tareef aur mashhoor musannif ke sawal ate hain, is liye har sanf ko us ke sab se bare naam ke sath yaad karo.

Novel lambi kahani

ناول

Novel nasr ki sab se lambi sanf hai jis me aik wasee kahani, bohot se kirdar aur zindagi ke mukhtalif pehlu tafseel se bayan kiye jate hain. Novel me kirdar waqt ke sath badalte aur nikharty hain. Mirza Hadi Ruswa ka "امراؤ جان ادا" urdu ke ibtedai aur mashhoor novelon me shumar hota hai, aur Deputy Nazir Ahmad ki "مراۃ العروس" ko aam taur par urdu ka pehla novel kaha jata hai. Is ke ilawa Qurratulain Hyder ka "آگ کا دریا" urdu adab ka shahkar novel hai. Paper me pehle novel aur mashhoor novel-nigaron ke naam pooche jate hain.

Afsana chhoti kahani

افسانہ

Afsana aik mukhtasar kahani hai jis me zindagi ka koi aik waqia, koi aik kirdar ya koi aik jazba bayan kiya jata hai. Afsana novel se is liye chota hota hai ke ye zindagi ke aik pehlu ko leta hai aur asar ki wahdat barqarar rakhta hai, yani parhne wale par aik hi majmooi asar parta hai. Urdu afsane ko Premchand ne bulandi di, un ke afsane "کفن" aur "نمک کا داروغہ" duniya bhar me parhe jate hain. Is ke ilawa Saadat Hasan Manto, Krishan Chandar, Ismat Chughtai aur Rajinder Singh Bedi urdu afsane ke bare naam hain. Paper me afsana aur novel ka farq aur mashhoor afsana-nigaron ke naam ate hain.

Drama stage ki sanf

ڈرامہ

Drama wo tehreer hai jo stage par pesh karne ke liye likhi jati hai. Is me kahani kirdaron ki guftagu aur amal ke zariye aage barhti hai, bayaniye ki tarah koi sunane wala nahi hota. Drama ke buniyadi hisse plot, kirdar, makalme aur manzar hain. Urdu drama ko Agha Hashr Kashmiri ne urooj diya aur unhe urdu ka Shakespeare kaha jata hai. Is ke ilawa Imtiaz Ali Taj ka "انار کلی" urdu ka mashhoor tareen drama hai. Paper me drama ki pehchan stage aur makalmon se hoti hai aur Agha Hashr ka naam is sanf ke sath lazmi ata hai.

Mazmoon kisi mozua par tehreer

مضمون

Mazmoon kisi aik mozua par likhi hui wo nasri tehreer hai jis me musannif apne khayalat tartib aur dalail ke sath pesh karta hai. Mazmoon ki qismein mazhabi, akhlaqi, samaji, tanqeedi aur mizahiya ho sakti hain. Urdu mazmoon ko Sir Syed Ahmad Khan ne aik baqaida sanf banaya aur un ke mazameen "تہذیب الاخلاق" me shaye hue. Maulana Shibli Nomani aur Maulana Abul Kalam Azad bhi bare mazmoon-nigar hain, aur mizahiya mazmoon me Patras Bukhari ka "کتے" aur Mushtaq Ahmed Yusufi ki tehreerein mashhoor hain. Paper me mazmoon-nigari aur mazameen ke majmooon ke sawal ate hain.

Safarnama safar ki rudaad

سفرنامہ

Safarnama wo tehreer hai jis me musannif apne safar ke waqiat, mashahidat aur tajurbat bayan karta hai. Is me jagah jagah ke logon, maahol aur tehzeeb ka zikr hota hai. Urdu ke mashhoor safarnamon me Mustansar Hussain Tarar ke safarname bohot maqbool hain, aur Ibn-e-Insha ka mizahiya andaaz safarnamon me alag pehchan rakhta hai. Qadeem safarnamon me Yusuf Khan Kambalposh ka safarnama aham mana jata hai. Paper me safarnama ki tareef aur mashhoor safarnama-nigaron ke naam pooche jate hain.

Aap beeti aur khud-nawisht

آپ بیتی

Aap beeti wo tehreer hai jis me koi shakhs apni zindagi ke waqiat khud bayan karta hai. Jab ye zindagi ki mukammal kahani ki surat me likhi jaye to use khud-nawisht ya sawaaneh-umri kehte hain, angrezi me isay autobiography kehte hain. Aap beeti aur khud-nawisht me halka sa farq ye hai ke aap beeti me chune hue waqiat aur tajurbat hote hain jabke khud-nawisht me zindagi ka musalsal aur mukammal bayan hota hai. Is ke ilawa dusre shakhs ki zindagi par likhi gayi tehreer ko sawaaneh hayat kehte hain. Paper me in teeno ka farq poocha jata hai. Asaan pehchan ye hai, apni zindagi khud likhi to khud-nawisht, kisi aur ki zindagi likhi to sawaaneh.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ab wo ghaltiyan jama kar lo jo in asnaaf me number katwati hain. Pehli ghalti marsiya aur qasida me hoti hai, dono lambi nazmein hain lekin qasida kisi ki tareef ya hajv hai aur marsiya Karbala ke gham ki sanf hai. Dusri ghalti masnavi aur nazm me hoti hai, masnavi ki pehchan ye hai ke us ke har sher ka qafia badal jata hai. Teesri ghalti rubai aur qata me hoti hai, rubai hamesha chaar misron ki hoti hai aur us ka khaas wazan hota hai, qata ke ashaar is se zyada bhi ho sakte hain. Chauthi ghalti hamd, naat aur munqabat me hoti hai, teeno tareef hain lekin hamd Allah ki, naat Rasool ki aur munqabat ahl-e-bait ki. In chaar farqon ko pakka kar lo to asnaaf wala hissa tumhare liye numberon ki zamanat hai.
