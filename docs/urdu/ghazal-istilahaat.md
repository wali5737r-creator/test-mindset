Ghazal ki Istilahaat
غزل کی اصطلاحات. Matla, maqta, radeef, qafia — har lafz ki pehchan ashaar ke sath

Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai

PPSC aur FPSC ke one paper me Urdu adab ka hissa hamesha hota hai, aur us hisse me ghazal ki istilahaat sab se zyada dohrayi jati hain. Paper ka tareeqa seedha hai. Ghazal ka aik sher de diya jata hai aur poocha jata hai ke is sher me radeef kya hai, qafia kya hai, ye matla hai ya maqta. Kabhi sirf istilah ki tareef pooch li jati hai. Ye sawal un students ke number kaatte hain jo ghazal parhte to hain lekin us ke hison ke naam nahi jante. Is lesson ke baad tum kisi bhi ghazal ko khol kar us ka har hissa alag alag pehchan sako ge. Bas har istilah ko us ki misal ke sath zehen me bitha lo, tareef khud yaad ho jayegi.

Sab se pehle sher ko samajh lo

شعر

Sher ghazal ki sab se chhoti aur sab se buniyadi ikai hai. Do misron ke jor ko sher kehte hain. Har sher apne andar aik mukammal khayal pesh karta hai, aur ghazal ki sab se khaas baat yehi hai ke us ka har sher aksar apna alag mazmoon rakhta hai. Aik sher ishq par ho sakta hai, dusra duniya ki be-subati par, teesra khuda ki tareef par. Isi liye ghazal ko motiyon ki lari kaha jata hai, har moti apni jagah alag aur chamakta hua.

Misra bhi samajh lo

مصرع

Sher ki aik satar, yani aik line ko misra kehte hain. Har sher me do misray hote hain. Pehle misray ko مصرعِ اول kehte hain aur dusre misray ko مصرعِ ثانی. Paper me kabhi ye pooch liya jata hai ke misra kya hota hai, to bas ye yaad rakho ke sher aadha misra nahi, do misron ka jor sher hai. Ghalib ka ye mashhoor sher dekho, pehli satar misra-e-awwal hai aur dusri satar misra-e-saani.

نقش فریادی ہے کس کی شوخیِ تحریر کا
کاغذی ہے پیرہن ہر پیکرِ تصویر کا

Ab matla ki baat

مطلع

Ghazal ke sab se pehle sher ko matla kehte hain. Matla ki pehchan bohot asaan hai. Is ke dono misray hum-qafia hote hain, yani dono misron ke aakhir me aik hi tarz ke hum-awaaz alfaaz aur aik hi radeef ati hai. Baqi ghazal me sirf dusra misra hum-qafia hota hai, pehla misra azaad hota hai. Bas yehi farq matle ko ghazal ke baqi ashaar se alag karta hai. Matla ka matlab hota hai nikalne ki jagah, jese suraj ke nikalne ko matla kehte hain. Ghazal bhi yahin se nikalti hai, isi liye pehle sher ka naam matla para.

Ghalib ki ghazal ka matla dekho.

دلِ ناداں تجھے ہوا کیا ہے
آخر اس درد کی دوا کیا ہے

Dekho, pehle misray ke aakhir me "ہوا" aya aur dusre misray ke aakhir me "دوا". Ye dono hum-awaaz alfaaz qafia hain, aur in ke baad "کیا ہے" dono misron me dohraaya gaya, ye radeef hai. Dono misron me qafia aur radeef maujood hain, bas yehi matle ki pehchan hai. Paper me ghazal ka pehla sher de kar poocha jata hai ke ye konsi istilah hai, aur jawab matla hota hai.

Husn-e-matla kya hota hai

حسنِ مطلع

Agar ghazal me matle ke foran baad aik aur sher aisa ajaye jis ke dono misray bhi hum-qafia hon, to use husn-e-matla kehte hain. Yani dusra matla. Har ghazal me husn-e-matla hona zaroori nahi, lekin jahan ho wahan ghazal ki khoobsurti aur barh jati hai, isi liye is ka naam husn para. Paper me ye istilah tareef ki surat me ati hai. Sawal yun banta hai ke ghazal ke dusre matle ko kya kehte hain, aur jawab husn-e-matla hai. Ye bhi yaad rakho ke husn-e-matla bhi ghazal ke shuru me hi ata hai, darmiyan ya aakhir me aane wala sher husn-e-matla nahi kehlata.

Ab maqta samajh lo

مقطع

Ghazal ke sab se aakhri sher ko maqta kehte hain. Maqta ki sab se bari pehchan ye hai ke is me shair apna takhallus istemal karta hai. Maqta ka matlab hai kaatne ki jagah, yani jahan ghazal kaat kar khatam kar di jaye. Shair aksar maqte me apne aap se baat karta hai, kabhi apni tareef, kabhi shikwa, kabhi naseehat. Paper me aakhri sher de kar ya takhallus wala sher de kar poocha jata hai ke ye konsa sher hai, jawab maqta hota hai.

Mir ka maqta dekho, ghazal ke aakhri sher me "میر" saaf nazar aa raha hai.

میر ان نیم باز آنکھوں میں
ساری مستی شراب کی سی ہے

Aur Ghalib ka ye maqta to har jagah misal banta hai.

کعبے کس منہ سے جاؤ گے غالب
شرم تم کو مگر نہیں آتی

Takhallus kya hai

تخلص

Wo naam jo shair apni shairi ke liye ikhtiyar karta hai aur apne asal naam ki jagah ghazal ke maqte me istemal karta hai, use takhallus kehte hain. Mir Taqi ka takhallus "میر" tha, Mirza Asadullah Khan ka takhallus "غالب" tha, aur is se pehle wo "اسد" takhallus istemal karte the. Takhallus aam taur par maqte me ata hai, lekin kabhi kabhi shair ghazal ke kisi aur sher me bhi apna takhallus le ata hai. Paper me takhallus ki tareef bhi ati hai aur ye bhi poocha jata hai ke falan shair ka takhallus kya tha. Ye bhi yaad rakho ke har ghazal me maqta hona zaroori nahi, bohot si ghazalon me shair takhallus istemal hi nahi karta.

Radeef ko pakko

ردیف

Ghazal ke har sher ke dusre misray ke aakhir me, qafiye ke baad, jo lafz ya alfaaz bilkul aik hi surat me baar baar dohraye jate hain, unhe radeef kehte hain. Radeef ka matlab hai peeche sawaar hone wala, jese ghore ke peeche dusra sawaar beth jata hai. Qafiye ke peeche peeche jo alfaaz har sher me sawaar ho kar ate hain, wo radeef hain. Radeef aik lafz ki bhi ho sakti hai aur chand alfaaz ki bhi. Upar matle wale sher me "کیا ہے" radeef thi. Ghalib ke is sher me dekho.

ہزاروں خواہشیں ایسی کہ ہر خواہش پہ دم نکلے
بہت نکلے میرے ارماں لیکن پھر بھی کم نکلے

Is ghazal ke har sher ke aakhir me "نکلے" dohraaya jata hai, ye aik lafz ki radeef hai. Ab ye baat bhi zehen me rakho ke har ghazal me radeef hona zaroori nahi. Jis ghazal me radeef na ho use غیر مردف غزل kehte hain, muraddaf ka matlab hai radeef wali. Paper me ye sawal bohot ata hai ke ghair-muraddaf ghazal kisay kehte hain. Jawab yehi hai, jis ghazal me radeef na ho.

Qafia radeef se pehle ata hai

قافیہ

Radeef se bilkul pehle aane wale hum-awaaz alfaaz ko qafia kehte hain. Qafia ghazal ki awaaz ki buniyad hai. Har sher me qafia badalta hai lekin us ki awaaz aik hi rehti hai. Jese upar wale sher me "دم" aur "کم" qafia hain, dono ki aakhri awaaz milti hai. Matle me qafia dono misron me ata hai aur baqi ashaar me sirf dusre misray me. Qafia aik lafz bhi ho sakta hai aur lafz ka aakhri hissa bhi, asal cheez hum-awaazi hai. Paper me sher de kar poocha jata hai ke is me qafia konsa lafz hai, aur aksar students radeef ko qafia keh kar ghalti karte hain. Is ghalti se bachne ka tareeqa agle hisse me hai.

Radeef aur qafia ka farq aik hi misal se

Ye farq paper ka sab se pyara sawal hai, isay aik hi ghazal par samajh lo. Mir ki mashhoor ghazal ke do sher lete hain.

ہستی اپنی حباب کی سی ہے
یہ نمائش سراب کی سی ہے

نازکی اس کے لب کی کیا کہیے
پنکھڑی اک گلاب کی سی ہے

Ab gaur karo. Har sher ke aakhir me "کی سی ہے" har dafa bilkul waisa hi dohraaya gaya hai, lafz bhi wahi aur surat bhi wahi. Ye radeef hai. Is se bilkul pehle "حباب"، "سراب" اور "گلاب" aye hain. Ye alfaaz har sher me badal rahe hain lekin in ki awaaz aik hai, sab ke aakhir me "اب" ki awaaz hai. Ye qafia hain. Bas qanoon zehen me bitha lo. Jo har dafa lafz-ba-lafz dohraaya jaye wo radeef hai, aur jo badle lekin awaaz aik rakhe wo qafia hai. Radeef nahi badalti, qafia badalta hai lekin apni awaaz nahi badalta. Ye aik jumla paper ke do teen number pakke kar dega.

Behr yani ghazal ka wazan

بحر

Ghazal ke tamam ashaar aik hi wazan me hote hain, aur is wazan ko behr kehte hain. Behr ka matlab samandar hai. Jese samandar ki lehren aik tartib se ati hain, isi tarah sher ke alfaaz bhi aik muqarrar wazan ki tartib me bandhe hote hain. Har sher ke dono misray wazan me barabar hote hain. Wazan naapne ke ilm ko علمِ عروض kehte hain. Paper me behr ki tareef ati hai aur kabhi ye poocha jata hai ke ghazal ke tamam ashaar kis me aik jese hote hain. Jawab behr hai, yani wazan. Ye baat bhi yaad rakho ke ghazal ka pehla sher jis behr me ho, aakhri sher tak wohi behr rehti hai, darmiyan me behr nahi badalti.

Bait-ul-ghazal sab se acha sher

بیت الغزل

Ghazal ke tamam ashaar me se jo sher sab se acha, sab se umda aur sab se zyada pasandeeda ho, use bait-ul-ghazal kehte hain. Har ghazal ka aik na aik sher aisa hota hai jo sab ki zabaan par charh jata hai aur mehfilon me dohraaya jata hai. Paper me bait-ul-ghazal ki tareef seedhi pooch li jati hai. Isay matle aur maqte se mila kar confuse mat hona. Matla pehla sher hai, maqta aakhri sher hai, aur bait-ul-ghazal ghazal ke darmiyan ka wo sher hai jo sab se behtareen ho. Jagah se nahi, khoobsurti se pehchana jata hai.

Ghazal ki shartein aik jagah

Ab ghazal ki wo shartein jama kar lo jin par paper ghoomta hai. Ghazal ka har sher aam taur par apna alag mazmoon rakhta hai, ashaar ka mazmoon me aik dusre se jurna zaroori nahi. Tamam ashaar aik hi behr me hote hain. Tamam ashaar me aik hi qafia aur aik hi radeef ki pabandi hoti hai. Pehla sher matla hota hai jis ke dono misray hum-qafia hote hain. Aakhri sher maqta ho sakta hai jis me takhallus ata hai. Ashaar ki tadaad ke bare me standard baat ye hai ke ghazal me kam az kam paanch ashaar hote hain, aur aam taur par tadaad taaq hoti hai, jese paanch, saat, nau ya giyarah. Ghazal ke buniyadi mazmoonat ishq, mehboob ki tareef, hijr aur wisal ki kaifiyat, aur phir is ke sath zindagi, maut aur duniya ki be-subati par falsafiyana khayalat hain. Ghazal urdu shairi ki sab se maqbool sanf hai aur isay asnaaf-e-sukhan ki malika bhi kaha jata hai.

Poori ghazal par nishan laga kar samjho

Ab aik chhoti si ghazal lete hain aur us par saari istilahaat aik sath pehchante hain. Ye Mir Taqi Mir ki ghazal ke ashaar hain. Pehla sher parho.

ہستی اپنی حباب کی سی ہے
یہ نمائش سراب کی سی ہے

Ye ghazal ka pehla sher hai aur is ke dono misray hum-qafia hain, is liye ye matla hai. Is me "حباب" aur "سراب" qafia hain aur "کی سی ہے" radeef hai. Ab dusra sher dekho.

نازکی اس کے لب کی کیا کہیے
پنکھڑی اک گلاب کی سی ہے

Is sher ka pehla misra azaad hai, qafia aur radeef sirf dusre misray me aye hain, "گلاب" qafia hai aur "کی سی ہے" wahi radeef hai. Ye ghazal ke aam sher ki surat hai. Ab ghazal ka aakhri sher dekho.

میر ان نیم باز آنکھوں میں
ساری مستی شراب کی سی ہے

Ye ghazal ka aakhri sher hai aur is me "میر" shair ka takhallus aya hai, is liye ye maqta hai. Is me bhi "شراب" qafia aur "کی سی ہے" radeef hai. Dekho, poori ghazal me behr aik rahi, qafiye ki awaaz aik rahi, radeef nahi badli, pehla sher matla bana aur aakhri sher maqta. Bas yehi tartib har ghazal me dhoondni hai. Paper me jo sher bhi mile, pehle dekho ye ghazal ke shuru ka hai ya aakhir ka, phir dekho aakhir me dohraaya jane wala hissa konsa hai aur us se pehle hum-awaaz lafz konsa hai. Sawal khud hal ho jayega.

Paper ki aam ghaltiyan

Ab wo ghaltiyan dekho jo students paper me karte hain. Pehli ghalti ye ke radeef aur qafia ko ulta bata dete hain. Yaad rakho, aakhir me jo lafz-ba-lafz dohraaya jaye wo radeef hai, us se pehle badalne wala hum-awaaz lafz qafia hai. Dusri ghalti ye ke har aakhri sher ko maqta keh dete hain, halanke maqte ki shart takhallus hai, takhallus na ho to wo bas ghazal ka aakhri sher hai. Teesri ghalti ye ke matle ko sirf pehla sher samajh lete hain, halanke matle ki asal pehchan dono misron ka hum-qafia hona hai. Chauthi ghalti ghair-muraddaf ghazal me hoti hai, radeef na mile to students ghabra jate hain, halanke wahan sirf qafia dhoondna hota hai. In chaar ghaltiyon se bach gaye to ghazal wala hissa tumhara pakka hai.
