Mashhoor Mulk — Darul Hukumat aur Currency
Paper ke pasandeeda mulk, un ke darul hukumat aur sikke

Ye hissa kyun zaroori hai

One paper me har saal chand sawal mulkon par ate hain. Falan mulk ka darul hukumat kya hai, falan mulk ki currency kya hai, sab se bara mulk konsa hai. Ye sawal ratne se nahi, jor banane se yaad hote hain. Har mulk ko us ki aik pehchan ke sath bandh lo to darul hukumat aur currency khud yaad ane lagte hain. Aur aik khaas baat pehle se samajh lo, paper hamesha un mulkon me phansata hai jahan sab se mashhoor sheher hi darul hukumat nahi hota. Australia ka mashhoor sheher Sydney hai lekin darul hukumat Canberra hai. Turkey ka mashhoor sheher Istanbul hai lekin darul hukumat Ankara hai. Jahan jahan ye jaal hai, main wahan ruk kar tumhe wazeh karunga. Kul mila kar hum takreeban 30 mulk parhenge, wahi jo paper me ghumte rehte hain.

Asia ke mulk

Pehle apna continent. Asia ke wo mulk jo paper me sab se zyada ate hain, aik aik karke.

China

China ka darul hukumat Beijing hai aur currency Yuan hai, jisay Renminbi bhi kehte hain. China raqbe me duniya ke bare mulkon me hai aur abadi me ab India ke baad doosre number par hai. Is ki pehchan Great Wall of China hai, duniya ki sab se lambi deewar, jo hazaron kilometre phaili hai. China ka sab se lamba darya Yangtze hai jis ka zikr tum pichhle hisse me parh chuke ho.

India

India ka darul hukumat New Delhi hai aur currency Indian Rupee hai. Yahan pehla jaal samajh lo, darul hukumat Delhi nahi, New Delhi hai, ye bareek farq paper me ata hai. India ab abadi me duniya ka sab se pehla mulk ban chuka hai. Agra me waaqe Taj Mahal is ki duniya bhar me pehchan hai. Rupee ka lafz Pakistan se mila kar confuse mat hona, aage ye baat khulegi ke Rupee kai mulkon ki currency ka naam hai.

Japan

Japan ka darul hukumat Tokyo hai aur currency Yen hai. Japan Asia ke mashriq me jaziron ka mulk hai. Isay Land of the Rising Sun, yani ugte suraj ki sarzameen, kaha jata hai, kyunke Asia me suraj sab se pehle yahin nazar ata hai. Japan ke apne lafz Nihon ka matlab bhi suraj ka sar chashma hai. Mount Fuji yahan ki mashhoor choti hai. Suraj wala sawal aye to jawab Japan hai, pakka kar lo.

Saudi Arabia

Saudi Arabia ka darul hukumat Riyadh hai aur currency Saudi Riyal hai. Is mulk ki pehchan do cheezain hain, Makkah aur Madinah ke muqaddas sheher, aur duniya ke bare teel ke zakhair. Currency par khaas tawajjo do, Riyal ke hawalay se aage Iran ke Rial se is ka farq ayega, yahi paper ka jaal hai.

Iran

Iran ka darul hukumat Tehran hai aur currency Iranian Rial hai. Ab farq saaf sun lo. Saudi ki currency Riyal hai aur Iran ki Rial, dono ke bolne me halka sa farq hai lekin paper me yehi farq number deta hai. Aam bol chal me Iran me das Rial ko aik Toman bhi kaha jata hai, ye maloomat bhi kabhi kaam ati hai. Iran Pakistan ka maghribi parosi hai, ye baat Pak Studies se jurti hai.

United Arab Emirates

UAE ka darul hukumat Abu Dhabi hai aur currency Dirham hai. Yahan is hisse ka mashhoor jaal hai. Duniya bhar me mashhoor sheher Dubai hai, Burj Khalifa bhi Dubai me hai, lekin darul hukumat Dubai nahi, Abu Dhabi hai. Ye sawal paper me itni dafa aya hai ke isay alag se zehen me nashaan laga kar rakho.

Bangladesh

Bangladesh ka darul hukumat Dhaka hai aur currency Taka hai. Isay daryaaon ka mulk kaha jata hai kyunke Ganga aur Brahmaputra ke delta par waaqe hai, aur ye delta duniya ka sab se bara delta hai. 1971 se pehle ye Pakistan ka mashriqi hissa tha, ye baat Pak Studies ke liye khaas hai.

Sri Lanka

Sri Lanka ka darul hukumat Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte hai aur currency Sri Lankan Rupee hai. Yahan aik imandari wali baat sun lo. Purani kitabon me Sri Lanka ka darul hukumat Colombo likha milta hai. Asal soorat ye hai ke Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte qanoon saaz darul hukumat hai aur Colombo purana aur sab se bara sheher hai jahan bohat se sarkari dafatir ab bhi hain. Nayi kitabon me Kotte ata hai, paper me jo option mile usi hisab se jawab dena. Sri Lanka Indian Ocean me jazira mulk hai aur isay Hind ka moti bhi kaha jata hai.

Nepal

Nepal ka darul hukumat Kathmandu hai aur currency Nepalese Rupee hai. Nepal ki pehchan Everest hai, duniya ki sab se unchi choti isi mulk me hai, is liye Nepal ko duniya ki chhat bhi kaha jata hai. Ye Pakistan ki tarah landlocked nahi lekin samandar se ghira bhi nahi, paharon wala mulk hai. Rupee yahan teesri dafa aya, Pakistan, India aur Nepal teeno ki currency ka naam Rupee hai, hawalay se mulk pehchanna hota hai.

Afghanistan

Afghanistan ka darul hukumat Kabul hai aur currency Afghani hai. Ye Pakistan ka shimal maghribi parosi hai aur Durand Line wali sarhad isi ke sath hai, jis ki tafseel tum Pakistan wale hisse me parh chuke ho. Afghanistan landlocked mulk hai, yani is ke kisi taraf samandar nahi. Currency ka naam Afghani hai, admi ko bhi Afghan kehte hain aur sikke ko bhi Afghani, ye chhota sa point paper me ata hai.

Indonesia

Indonesia ka darul hukumat Jakarta hai aur currency Rupiah hai. Pichhle hisse me tum parh chuke ho ke ye duniya ka sab se bara archipelago mulk hai, 17 hazar se zyada jazire. Is ki doosri bari pehchan ye hai ke ye duniya ka sab se zyada Muslim abadi wala mulk hai. Java is ka sab se mashhoor aur sab se zyada abadi wala jazira hai, aur Jakarta isi par hai.

Malaysia

Malaysia ka darul hukumat Kuala Lumpur hai aur currency Ringgit hai. Aik chhoti si maloomat ye hai ke Malaysia ka intizami darul hukumat Putrajaya hai jahan bohat si wizaratain hain, lekin rasmi darul hukumat Kuala Lumpur hi hai. Malacca wali mashhoor guzargah isi mulk ke saahil ke sath hai, jis ka zikr tum pichhle hisse me parh chuke ho.

Thailand

Thailand ka darul hukumat Bangkok hai aur currency Baht hai. Thailand ki khaas baat jo paper me ati hai ye hai ke ye South East Asia ka wo mulk hai jo kabhi kisi European taqat ki colony nahi bana. Is ke purane naam Siam se bhi sawal banta hai.

South Korea

South Korea ka darul hukumat Seoul hai aur currency Won hai. Korea do mulkon me banta hai, South Korea aur North Korea, North Korea ka darul hukumat Pyongyang hai. Dono ko mila kar confuse hona aam ghalti hai. Paper me Korea ka sawal aye to pehle dekho ke shimal poocha hai ya janub.

Do bar-e-azamon par phailay mulk

Ab teen mulk aisay parho jo aik hi continent me nahi aate. Ye teen paper ke sab se pasandeeda hain kyunke in par do sawal aik sath bante hain, darul hukumat ka aur do continents wala.

Turkey

Turkey ka darul hukumat Ankara hai aur currency Turkish Lira hai. Sab se pehle jaal saaf kar lo, Istanbul Turkey ka sab se bara aur sab se mashhoor sheher hai lekin darul hukumat nahi, darul hukumat Ankara hai. Turkey do continents par phaila hai, is ka chhota hissa Europe me aur bara hissa Asia me hai, aur Istanbul sheher khud Bosphorus ke dono kinaron par honay ki wajah se dono continents me ata hai. Do continents wala sawal aye to pehla naam Turkey ka zehen me ana chahiye.

Russia

Russia ka darul hukumat Moscow hai aur currency Ruble hai. Russia raqbe me duniya ka sab se bara mulk hai, taqreeban 17.1 million square kilometre, yani duniya ki zameen ka aathwan hissa ke qareeb akela Russia hai. Ye bhi do continents ka mulk hai, Ural paharon ke maghrib wala hissa Europe me aur mashriq wala hissa Asia me, jahan Siberia ka mashhoor ilaaqa hai. Lake Baikal bhi isi mulk me hai. Is ke size ka andaza is se lagao ke is me gyarah time zones hain.

Egypt

Egypt ka darul hukumat Cairo hai aur currency Egyptian Pound hai. Egypt bhi do continents ka mulk hai, is ka bara hissa Africa me hai aur Sinai ka hissa Asia me, aur Suez Canal in dono ke darmiyan se guzarti hai. Nile darya aur qadeem pyramids is ki duniya bhar me pehchan hain. Pound yahan phir aya, England wale Pound se isay alag pehchanna, ye Egyptian Pound hai. Teen do-continent wale mulk aik line me bandh lo, Turkey, Russia, Egypt.

Europe ke mulk

United Kingdom

UK ka darul hukumat London hai aur currency Pound Sterling hai. London River Thames ke kinare waaqe hai. UK chaar mulkon ka majmooa hai, England, Scotland, Wales aur Northern Ireland, ye baat bhi GK me ati hai. England aur UK ko aik samajhna aam ghalti hai, England UK ka aik hissa hai, pura mulk nahi.

France

France ka darul hukumat Paris hai aur currency Euro hai. Paris me waaqe Eiffel Tower duniya ki mashhoor tareen imaraton me hai. France Europe ke bare mulkon me hai aur is ka purana sikka Franc tha, ab Euro chalta hai, purani kitabon wala Franc dekh kar confuse mat hona.

Germany

Germany ka darul hukumat Berlin hai aur currency Euro hai. Germany ko Europe ki sab se bari economy mana jata hai. Doosri Aalmai Jang ke baad Germany do mulkon me bant gaya tha, East aur West Germany, aur 1990 me dono phir aik ho gaye, Berlin Wall ka girna isi kahani ka mashhoor waqia hai.

Italy

Italy ka darul hukumat Rome hai aur currency Euro hai. Rome qadeem Roman Empire ka markaz tha, aur wahan ka Colosseum, purana stadium, duniya bhar me mashhoor hai. Italy naqshay par boot, yani jootay, ki shakal ka nazar ata hai, ye pehchan bhi sawal me kaam ati hai. Duniya ka sab se chhota mulk Vatican City Rome ke andar hi waaqe hai.

Spain

Spain ka darul hukumat Madrid hai aur currency Euro hai. Spain Europe ke janub maghrib me hai aur Gibraltar wali mashhoor guzargah is ke bilkul janub me hai, jis ka zikr tum pichhle hisse me parh chuke ho.

Greece

Greece ka darul hukumat Athens hai aur currency Euro hai. Greece ko jamhooriyat ki janam jagah mana jata hai, aur Olympic Games bhi yahin se shuru hue. Europe ke in mulkon me aik mushtarka baat dekh lo, France, Germany, Italy, Spain aur Greece sab ki currency Euro hai. Euro walon ko aik group me yaad kar lo to aadha kaam ho jata hai.

America ke mulk

USA

USA ka darul hukumat Washington DC hai aur currency Dollar hai. Yahan bhi mashhoor jaal hai, New York USA ka sab se mashhoor sheher hai lekin darul hukumat nahi. Darul hukumat Washington DC hai, aur naam ke sath laga DC District of Columbia ka matlab hai, ye kisi riyasat ka hissa nahi, alag zila hai. USA pachaas riyaston ka mulk hai aur economy ke hisab se duniya ke sab se aage mulkon me hai.

Canada

Canada ka darul hukumat Ottawa hai aur currency Canadian Dollar hai. Jaal yahan bhi wahi hai, Toronto sab se bara sheher hai lekin darul hukumat Ottawa hai. Canada raqbe me Russia ke baad duniya ka doosra bara mulk hai. Dollar yahan phir aya, ye American Dollar se alag Canadian Dollar hai.

Brazil

Brazil ka darul hukumat Brasilia hai aur currency Real hai. Yahan jaal do hain. Aik ye ke Rio de Janeiro mashhoor sheher hai aur aik zamane me darul hukumat bhi tha, lekin ab darul hukumat Brasilia hai jo khaas is maqsad ke liye banaya gaya sheher hai. Dusra ye ke South America ke baqi mulk Spanish bolte hain lekin Brazil me Portuguese boli jati hai. Brazil South America ka sab se bara mulk hai aur Amazon ka jangal isi me hai.

Argentina

Argentina ka darul hukumat Buenos Aires hai aur currency Peso hai. South America ki sab se unchi choti Aconcagua isi mulk me hai, jis ka zikr Andes wale hisse me aya tha. Peso ka naam Spain ke asar se kai mulkon me milta hai, is liye mulk ka naam sath pehchanna zaroori hai.

Africa ka aik aur aham mulk

Nigeria

Nigeria ka darul hukumat Abuja hai aur currency Naira hai. Yahan bhi mashhoor sheher Lagos hai lekin darul hukumat Abuja hai, bilkul wahi purana jaal. Nigeria Africa ka sab se zyada abadi wala mulk hai, is liye is ka naam abadi wale sawalon me ata hai. Aur Egypt to tum upar parh hi chuke ho, Africa ke do naam pakke kar lo, Egypt aur Nigeria.

Oceania ke mulk

Australia

Australia ka darul hukumat Canberra hai aur currency Australian Dollar hai. Is pure hisse ka sab se mashhoor jaal yahi hai. Sydney Australia ka sab se mashhoor sheher hai, Opera House bhi wahin hai, lekin darul hukumat Sydney nahi, Canberra hai. Ye sawal itni dafa paper me aya hai ke is par alag se nashaan laga lo. Australia khud sab se chhota continent bhi hai aur mulk bhi, ye doosri pehchan bhi yaad rakho.

New Zealand

New Zealand ka darul hukumat Wellington hai aur currency New Zealand Dollar hai. Ye Australia ke janub mashriq me jaziron ka mulk hai. Iski pehchan kiwi parinda hai jo yahin milta hai. Dollar yahan teesri surat me aya, American, Canadian, Australian aur New Zealand, chaar mulkon ki currency ka naam Dollar hai lekin har aik apni alag hai.

Currency wale jaal, aik nazar me

Ab aakhir me wo ghaltiyan bandh lo jo paper me sab se zyada hoti hain. Pehli, Dollar sirf USA ka nahi, Canada, Australia aur New Zealand ka bhi hai. Dusri, Rupee sirf Pakistan ka nahi, India, Nepal aur Sri Lanka ka bhi hai. Teesri, Saudi ke Riyal aur Iran ke Rial me confuse hona, dono alag mulk, alag sikke. Chauthi, Pound UK ka bhi hai aur Egypt ka bhi. Aur paanchvi, Europe ke zyada tar mashhoor mulkon ki currency aik hi hai, Euro. Agar ye paanch baatein saaf hon to currency wala sawal tumhara kabhi ghalat nahi hoga.

Aakhir me poora khaka zehen me bandh lo

Har mulk ko us ki pehchan ke sath jor kar yaad karo. Mashhoor sheher wala jaal Canberra, Ankara, Abu Dhabi, Ottawa, Brasilia aur Abuja me hai, in chhe ko aik sath rat lo ke ye wo darul hukumat hain jo mashhoor shehron ke peeche chhupe hain. Do continents wale teen hain, Turkey, Russia aur Egypt. Sab se bara mulk Russia hai. Euro walon ka group alag hai, Dollar walon ka group alag. Jab mulk ka naam dekh kar us ki pehchan wali tasveer zehen me ane lagay to samajh lo ye hissa pakka ho gaya, aur paper me ye number tum se koi nahi cheen sakta.
