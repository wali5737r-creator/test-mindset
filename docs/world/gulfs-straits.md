Khaleejain, Aabnayen aur Mashhoor Samandar
Har guzargah ki jagah, us ke kinare ke mulk, aur us ki ahmiyat ki kahani

Ye topic kyun zaroori hai

Dekho, duniya ki tijarat ka sab se bara hissa inhi paniyon se guzarta hai. Teel inhi khaleejon se nikalta hai, jahaz inhi aabnayon se hokar dusre samandar me jate hain, aur tareekh ki bari jangein bhi inhi tang darwazon par lari gayi hain. Is liye one paper me har saal koi na koi khaleej ya aabnaay lazmi ati hai. Sawal kabhi jagah poochta hai, kabhi kin mulkon ke darmiyan hai, aur kabhi ye ke ye mashhoor kyun hai.

Har feature ko teen sawalon se parho ge. Kahan hai. Kin mulkon ya kin paniyon ke darmiyan hai. Aur kyun aham hai, teel ki wajah se, tijarat ki wajah se ya jang ki tareekh ki wajah se. Teeno jawab ajayen to wo feature tumhara pakka ho gaya. Shaklon ki pehchan tum istilahaat wali file me seekh chuke ho, yahan unhi shaklon ke asal naam aur kahaniyan hain.

Persian Gulf (Khaleej-e-Faris)

Arabia aur Iran ke darmiyan zameen me ghusi hui ye khaleej duniya ki sab se aham khaleej hai, aur us ki wajah aik hi lafz hai, teel. Is ke kinare aath mulk hain. Iran, Iraq, Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, Bahrain, Qatar, United Arab Emirates aur Oman. In mulkon ke paas duniya ke teel ke zakhair ka bohat bara hissa hai aur wo teel jahazon me bhar kar isi khaleej se bahar bheja jata hai. Khaleej ka pani nisbatan uthla aur garam hai aur bahar jane ka is ka akela bara darwaza Strait of Hormuz hai, is liye jo is darwaze par asar rakhe wo duniya ki teel ki farahmi par asar dal sakta hai. Tareekh me bhi ye pani garam raha hai, 1980 ki dahai me Iran Iraq jang me yahan ke teel wale jahazon par hamle hue aur 1991 ki Gulf War bhi isi ilaqay ke gird ghoomi. Pakistan ke liye is ki do ahmiyatain hain, aik ye ke faasla bohat kam hai, dusri ye ke lakho Pakistani inhi khaliji mulkon me kaam karte hain aur un ki bheji hui rakam yahin se ati hai.
Pin: 26.50, 52.00

Gulf of Oman

Persian Gulf ke darwaze Hormuz se bahar nikalte hi jo pani ata hai wo Gulf of Oman hai. Is ke janub me Oman aur United Arab Emirates hain, shimal me Iran hai, aur mashriq ki taraf Pakistan ka Makran sahel is se lagta hai. Gwadar isi ke kinare ke qareeb hai, is liye ye khaleej Pakistan ke liye seedhi aur khaas ahmiyat rakhti hai. Persian Gulf se nikalne wala har teel wala jahaz pehle isi khaleej se guzarta hai aur phir Arabian Sea me dakhil hota hai, yani ye andar wali khaleej aur khule samandar ke darmiyan wala darmiyani hissa hai. Ise sea nahi gulf is liye kehte hain ke is ki shakal bhi zameen ke andar ki taraf ghusi hui hai, jese tum istilahaat wali file me parh chuke ho. Duniya ke naqshay par jab tum Gwadar se maghrib ki taraf dekho ge to pehle yehi khaleej milegi, phir Hormuz, phir Persian Gulf. Ye tartib zehen me bitha lo.
Pin: 24.70, 58.00

Arabian Sea

Sab se pehle ye farq pakka kar lo ke Arabian Sea khaleej nahi hai, ye aik sea hai aur Indian Ocean ka shumal maghribi hissa hai. Is ke kinare Pakistan, India, Oman, Yemen aur Somalia ate hain. Karachi aur Gwadar isi ke kinare par hain aur Pakistan ki tamam bahri tijarat isi se guzar kar duniya tak jati hai. Ye samandar monsoon hawaon ki wajah se mashhoor hai, purane zamane me Arab tajir inhi hawaon ke sahare Arabia, Bharat aur Africa ke darmiyan safar karte the, isi lambi tareekh ki wajah se is ka naam Arabian Sea para. Aaj bhi Persian Gulf ka teel, Europe aur mashriqi Asia ke darmiyan chalne wale container jahaz, aur Pakistan ki import export yahin se guzarti hai. Khaleej se is ka farq wazeh hai, ye khula aur wasee hai, zameen is ke andar nahi ghusi, balke ye khud teen continents ke kinaron tak phaila hai. Pakistan ke hisab se ye hamara akela samandar hai, is liye is ka har sawal hamare liye aham hai.
Pin: 15.00, 65.00

Red Sea

Africa aur Arabia ke darmiyan phasi hui lambi aur tang pani ki patti Red Sea hai. Is ke maghribi kinare par Egypt, Sudan, Eritrea aur Djibouti hain aur mashriqi kinare par Saudi Arabia aur Yemen. Is ke shumal me Suez Canal hai jo ise Mediterranean Sea se milati hai, aur janub me Bab el-Mandeb hai jo ise Gulf of Aden aur phir Indian Ocean se milati hai. Yani ye sea Europe aur Asia ke darmiyan sab se chhote samandari raste ka darmiyani hissa hai. Is ka pani duniya ke garam aur namkeen paniyon me ginna jata hai aur is ki coral reefs bohat mashhoor hain. Naam ke bare me kaha jata hai ke kinare ke surkh paharon ya pani me kabhi kabhi nazar ane wale surkh rang ki wajah se ye naam para. Tijarat ke hisab se duniya ke jahazon ka bohat bara hissa Suez se hota hua yahin se guzarta hai, is liye is raste ki bandish ka asar poori duniya ki tijarat par parta hai.
Pin: 21.00, 38.50

Mediterranean Sea

Ye wo samandar hai jo teen continents ke darmiyan phasa hua hai. Shimal me Europe, janub me Africa aur mashriq me Asia, aur is ke kinare Spain, France, Italy, Greece, Turkey, Syria, Lebanon, Egypt aur Libya jese mulk hain. Ye bahar ki duniya se sirf do jagah se jurta hai, maghrib me Strait of Gibraltar ke zariye Atlantic Ocean se, aur mashriq me insaan ki banayi hui Suez Canal ke zariye Red Sea se. Raqbe me ye takreeban pachis lakh square kilometre hai aur duniya ke bare seas me ginna jata hai. Purani duniya ki tareekh ka bara hissa isi ke kinaron par likha gaya, Greek aur Roman tehzeebein yahin phalein aur baad me Muslim duniya ka bara hissa bhi isi ke gird raha. Is ke andar Sicily, Cyprus aur Crete jese mashhoor jazire hain. Paper me ye teen continents wale point aur Gibraltar wale darwaze ke sath ata hai, ye dono baatein pakki kar lo.
Pin: 36.00, 18.00

Bay of Bengal

Bharat ke mashriq aur Myanmar ke maghrib me waaqe Bay of Bengal duniya ki sab se bari bay hai, itni bari ke bohat se mashhoor seas is se chhote hain. Is ke kinare India, Bangladesh aur Myanmar hain aur janub ki taraf Sri Lanka ka jazira is ke munh par hai. Ganga aur Brahmaputra jese bare darya isi me girte hain aur Bangladesh ke kinare par un ka mashhoor delta banta hai jo duniya ka sab se bara delta hai. Ye bay apne samandari toofanon yani cyclones ki wajah se bhi badnaam hai jo taqreeban har saal kinare ke ilaaqon me tabahi late hain. Is ke andar Bharat ke Andaman aur Nicobar jazire hain. Pakistan ke hisab se ye hamare mashriqi samandari pados me hai aur monsoon ka nizam yahin se banta hua hamari taraf ata hai. Bay aur gulf ke farq me ye sab se achi misaal hai ke naam hamesha size se tay nahi hota, shakal aur tareekh se bhi hota hai.
Pin: 14.00, 88.00

Gulf of Mexico

North America ke janub mashriq me zameen se ghiri hui ye bari khaleej hai jis ke kinare America, Mexico aur Cuba ka jazira hain. Mississippi, jo America ka sab se bara darya hai, isi khaleej me girta hai. Is ke teh me teel aur gas ke bare zakhair hain aur kinare par America ki bari refineries aur bandargahen hain, jin me Houston mashhoor hai. Ye khaleej apne sakht samandari toofanon yani hurricanes ki wajah se bhi khabron me rehti hai jo har saal kinare ke shehron ko nuqsan pohanchate hain. Garam current Gulf Stream yahin ke garam pani se paida hokar Florida ke paas se Atlantic ki taraf nikalti hai, jis ka zikr tum istilahaat wali file me parh chuke ho. Florida ki aabnaay aur Yucatan ke raste se ye khaleej Caribbean Sea aur Atlantic Ocean se jurti hai. Naqshay par America ke neeche bani hui ye bari jeb tumhe foran nazar ayegi.
Pin: 25.00, -90.00

Gulf of Aden

Arabia ke janub aur Africa ke mashriqi sing, jisay Horn of Africa kehte hain, ke darmiyan waaqe khaleej hai. Is ke shimal me Yemen hai aur janub me Somalia aur Djibouti. Bab el-Mandeb ke zariye ye Red Sea se jurti hai aur dusri taraf Arabian Sea me khulti hai, yani Suez se hokar Europe se Asia jane wala har jahaz yahan se lazmi guzarta hai. Chand saal pehle tak ye ilaqa Somalia ke samandari dakuon yani pirates ki wajah se duniya bhar ki khabron me raha, jahazon ko aghwa kiya jata tha, aur bohat se mulkon ki navies yahan gasht karti rahi hain. Aden ka sheher aur us ki purani bandargah Yemen ke janubi kinare par is khaleej ki pehchan hai. Tijarati raste ke hisab se ye chhoti si khaleej duniya ke aham tareen paniyon me hai, kyunke ye Red Sea wale raste ko khule samandar se jorti hai.
Pin: 12.50, 45.50

South China Sea

Pacific Ocean ka wo hissa hai jo China ke janub, Vietnam ke mashriq, Philippines ke maghrib aur Malaysia aur Indonesia ke shumal me phasa hua hai. Duniya ki samandari tijarat ka bohat bara hissa isi sea se guzarta hai, kyunke Malacca se ane wale jahaz yahin se hokar China, Japan aur Korea ki bandargahon tak jate hain. Is ke teh me teel aur gas ke zakhair ka andaza hai aur machhliyon ke bare zakhair bhi hain, is liye kinare ke tamam mulk is par apna haq jatate hain. Aaj kal ye sea khabron me is liye rehta hai ke is ke chhote jaziron aur paniyon par China aur aas paas ke mulkon, khaas taur par Philippines aur Vietnam, ke darmiyan ikhtilaf chal raha hai aur China yahan masnooi jazire bhi bana raha hai. Paper me ye sea tijarati raste aur is ikhtilaf dono hisab se ata hai. Naqshay par China ke neeche phaila hua ye neela hissa tumhe Malacca wali file ke sath jor kar yaad rahega.
Pin: 14.00, 115.00

Caribbean Sea

America ke janub mashriq me jaziron se ghira hua garam sea hai jo Atlantic Ocean ka hissa hai. Is ke shimal me Cuba, Jamaica aur Hispaniola jese bare jazire aur chhote jaziron ki lambi qataar hai jinhe mil kar West Indies kehte hain, aur janub me Venezuela aur Colombia ka sahel hai. Panama Canal ka mashriqi darwaza isi sea me khulta hai, is liye Atlantic se Pacific jane wale jahaz yahan se guzarte hain. Ye ilaqa apne hurricanes, khoobsurat sahilon aur purane zamane ke samandari dakuon ki kahani ki wajah se mashhoor hai. Cricket khelne wale West Indies ke mulk bhi yahi hain, aur un ka naam is sea ke kinare wale jaziron ki wajah se para hai. Paper me Caribbean aksar Panama Canal aur West Indies ke sath jor kar ata hai.
Pin: 15.50, -76.00

Bering Strait

Asia aur North America ke darmiyan wo tang aabnaay hai jo Pacific Ocean ko Arctic Ocean se milati hai. Is ke maghrib me Russia ka Chukotka hai aur mashriq me America ka Alaska, aur tang tareen jagah par dono kinaron ke darmiyan faasla takreeban bayasi kilometre hai. Is ke darmiyan do chhote jazire hain jinhe Diomede kehte hain, aik Russia ka hai aur dusra America ka, aur un ke darmiyan se waqt ki woh lakeer guzarti hai jisay International Date Line kehte hain, yani aik jazire par aaj hai to dusre par kal. Mahireen ke khayal me barf ke zamane me jab samandar ki satah neechi thi to yahin aik zameeni pul tha jisay paar karke pehle insaan Asia se America pohunche the. Aaj ye ilaqa sakht sardi aur barf ki wajah se tijarat me kam istemal hota hai, lekin do azeem continents ko sab se qareeb se jodne wali yehi jagah hai.
Pin: 65.78, -168.99

Strait of Gibraltar

Mediterranean Sea ko Atlantic Ocean se milane wali ye aabnaay Europe aur Africa ke darmiyan hai. Shimal me Spain hai aur janub me Morocco, aur tang tareen jagah par dono kinaron ke darmiyan faasla sirf takreeban terah kilometre hai, itna kam ke saaf din me khade hokar dusra kinara nazar ajata hai. Jo jahaz Atlantic se Mediterranean me jayega wo yahin se guzre ga, aur Mediterranean ke tamam mulkon ke liye ye maghrib ki taraf akela qudrati darwaza hai. Tareekh me 711 esvi me Tariq bin Ziyad ne yehi aabnaay paar karke Spain me qadam rakha tha, aur Gibraltar ka naam bhi Jabal Tariq, yani Tariq ke pahar, se bana hai. Ye naam wali baat paper me bohat ati hai, isay pakka kar lo. Naqshay par Spain ke neeche aur Morocco ke upar bani ye tang gali Mediterranean ka darwaza hai.
Pin: 35.97, -5.49

Bab el-Mandeb

Red Sea ko Gulf of Aden se milane wali ye tang aabnaay Arabia aur Africa ke darmiyan hai. Aik taraf Yemen hai aur dusri taraf Djibouti aur Eritrea. Is ke naam ka matlab aansuon ka darwaza hai, kaha jata hai ke purane zamane me yahan ke khatarnaak patharon aur tez lehron ki wajah se bohat se jahaz doobte the, is liye ye naam para. Suez Canal se guzar kar Europe aur Asia ke darmiyan chalne wala har jahaz yahan se lazmi guzarta hai, is liye ye duniya ki chand aham tareen guzargahon me ginni jati hai. Is ke darmiyan Perim ka jazira ise do raston me bant deta hai. Iski ahmiyat ka andaza is se lagao ke agar ye rasta band ho jaye to jahazon ko poora Africa ghoom kar jana parega, jo waqt aur kharche dono ko bohat barha deta hai. Naqshay par Red Sea ke janubi siray par bani ye tang jagah is poore raste ki dusri chaabi hai, pehli chaabi Suez hai.
Pin: 12.60, 43.40

English Channel

England aur France ke darmiyan waaqe ye channel Atlantic Ocean ko North Sea se milati hai. Is ke sab se tang hisse ko Strait of Dover kehte hain jahan dono kinaron ke darmiyan faasla sirf takreeban chontees kilometre hai. Duniya ke masroof tareen jahazrani raston me ye shamil hai, rozana sainkadon jahaz yahan se guzarte hain aur dono kinaron ke darmiyan ferry ka silsila bhi chalta hai. Sann 1994 me is ke neeche Channel Tunnel bani jo England ko France se train ke zariye milati hai, aur is se pehle England hamesha aik jazire ki tarah alag raha tha. Tareekh me is channel ne England ko bohat se bare hamlo se bachaya, kyunke hamla awar ko pehle ye pani paar karna parta tha aur mausam aksar us ke khilaf hota tha. Paper me ye channel apni tunnel aur masroof raste ki wajah se ata hai.
Pin: 50.20, -1.50

Strait of Malacca

Malaysia aur Indonesia ke jazire Sumatra ke darmiyan ye lambi aur tang guzargah Indian Ocean ko Pacific Ocean, yani South China Sea, se milati hai. Iski tijarati ahmiyat duniya ki tamam guzargahon se zyada hai, kyunke duniya ke teel aur samaan ka bohat bara hissa isi tang raste se guzarta hai. China, Japan aur Korea ko jane wale jahazon ke liye ye sab se chhota rasta hai, aur mashriqi Asia ki factoryon ko duniya se jodne wali yehi naali hai. Singapore ki bandargah isi ke kinare honay ki wajah se duniya ki aham tareen bandargahon me hai, jahan jahaz ruk kar teel aur samaan badalte hain. Pakistan se mashriqi Asia jane wale jahaz bhi yahin se guzarte hain, is liye hamari tijarat se bhi is ka seedha taluq hai. Iski tangi ki wajah se yahan kabhi samandari dakuon ka masla bhi raha hai aur kinare ke teenon mulk, Malaysia, Indonesia aur Singapore, mil kar is ki hifazat karte hain. Agar ye rasta kabhi band ho jaye to jahazon ko Indonesia ke gird bohat lamba chakkar lagana parega, yehi wajah hai ke isay duniya ki sab se aham guzargah kaha jata hai.
Pin: 4.20, 100.10

Dardanelles aur Bosphorus, Istanbul ki do aabnayen

Turkey ke shumal maghrib me do tang aabnayen hain jo asal me aik hi kahani ke do hisse hain. Pehli Dardanelles hai jo Aegean Sea ko Sea of Marmara se milati hai, phir Marmara ke dusre siray par Bosphorus hai jo Black Sea tak pohanchati hai. In dono se guzar kar hi koi jahaz Mediterranean se Black Sea ja sakta hai, yani Russia, Ukraine, Romania aur Bulgaria jese Black Sea wale mulkon ka ye akela samandari darwaza hai. Istanbul ka sheher Bosphorus ke dono kinaron par basta hai, is ka aik hissa Europe me hai aur dusra Asia me, is liye Istanbul ko do continents par waaqe sheher kaha jata hai. Pehli Jang-e-Azeem me 1915 me Gallipoli ki mashhoor larai Dardanelles par hi hui thi, jahan Usmani fauj ne ittehadiyon ko andar ghusne se roka tha. Sann 1936 ke Montreux Muahide ke tehat in aabnayon se guzarne ke usool tay hain aur in ka intizam Turkey ke paas hai. Paper me ye jora Istanbul, do continents aur Gallipoli ke sath ata hai.
Pin: 40.70, 27.80

Aakhri baat

Ab tum duniya ke pani ke bare darwaze pehchante ho. Har feature ko teen sawalon se parkho, kahan hai, kin ke darmiyan hai, aur kyun aham hai. Teel ka rasta ho to Persian Gulf aur Hormuz zehen me ayenge, Europe Asia ka chhota rasta ho to Suez, Red Sea aur Bab el-Mandeb ki poori zanjeer samne ajayegi, aur tijarat ki sab se masroof naali ka naam Malacca hoga. Ye zanjeerain hi asal pehchan hain, akeli jagah ratne se zyada ye jor yaad rakhna kaam ata hai.
