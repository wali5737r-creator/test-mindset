Duniya ka Naqsha — Continents, Oceans aur Mashhoor Jagahen
Puri duniya aik nazar me, bar-e-azamon se mashhoor guzargahon tak

Ye hissa kyun zaroori hai

Pakistan ke darya aur pahar tum parh chuke ho. Ab naqsha thora peeche se dekho, puri duniya ka. PPSC, FPSC aur NTS ke one paper me duniya par sawal har saal ate hain. Sab se bara continent konsa hai, sab se lamba darya konsa hai, Suez Canal kin do samandaron ko milati hai, Everest kis mulk me hai, duniya ki sab se neechi jagah kahan hai. Ye sawal mushkil nahi hote, lekin masla ye hota hai ke student duniya ko bikhre hue tukron me yaad karta hai. Aaj hum puri duniya ko aik tartib me sajayenge. Pehle continent aur ocean ka matlab, phir saat continents raqbe ki tartib me, phir paanch oceans, aur aakhir me wo mashhoor jagahen jin par paper ka sawal banta hai. Tartib zehen me beth jaye to naqsha khud bolne lagta hai.

Pehle do lafz samajh lo, continent aur ocean

Continent kise kehte hain? Zameen ke bohat bare aur musalsal tukre ko continent kehte hain, Urdu me isay bar-e-azam kaha jata hai. Duniya ki khushk zameen saat bar-e-azamon me banti hui hai. Har continent ke andar bohat se mulk hote hain, bare bare pahar, darya aur registran hote hain. Pakistan Asia continent ka aik mulk hai.

Ocean kise kehte hain? Khaare pani ke bohat bare zakhire ko ocean, yani behra, kehte hain. Duniya ki satah ka taqreeban 71 feesad hissa pani hai, aur ye pani paanch bare oceans me taqseem samjha jata hai. Ab aik bareek farq bhi samajh lo jo paper me phansata hai. Ocean aur sea me farq hai. Sea ocean se chhota hota hai aur aksar zameen ke qareeb ya zameen se ghira hota hai. Masalan Arabian Sea koi alag ocean nahi, wo Indian Ocean ka hi hissa hai jo Pakistan ke janub me hai. Isi tarah jazira us zameen ko kehte hain jis ke chaaron taraf pani ho, aabnaye yani strait pani ki us tang patti ko kehte hain jo do bare panion ko milati hai, aur canal wo nehar hai jo insaan ne khud bana kar do samandaron ko jor diya ho. Ye chaar paanch lafz saaf ho jayen to aage ka sara hissa asaan ho jayega.

Saat continents, raqbe ki tartib me

Ab saat continents ko bare se chhote ki tartib me parho. Ye tartib hi paper ka pehla sawal hai, isay aik saans me bolna seekh lo. Asia, Africa, North America, South America, Antarctica, Europe, Australia.

Asia, sab se bara continent

Asia duniya ka sab se bara continent hai. Is ka raqba taqreeban 44.6 million square kilometre hai, yani puri duniya ki khushk zameen ka taqreeban aik tihai hissa sirf Asia hai. Abadi me bhi Asia sab se aage hai, duniya ki aadhi se zyada abadi yahin rehti hai. Pakistan isi continent ka mulk hai. China, India, Japan, Saudi Arabia aur Indonesia jaise bare mulk yahin hain. Duniya ki sab se unchi choti Everest isi continent me hai, aur Asia ka sab se lamba darya Yangtze bhi yahin behta hai. Asia teen oceans se lagta hai, shimal me Arctic, mashriq me Pacific aur janub me Indian Ocean. Itna bara continent honay ki wajah se yahan garam registran bhi hain aur barf wale ilaaqe bhi.

Africa, doosra bara continent

Africa raqbe me doosra bara continent hai, taqreeban 30.3 million square kilometre. Isay dekhne ka asaan tareeqa ye hai ke equator, yani khat-e-istawa, is ke bilkul darmiyan se guzarta hai, is liye is ka zyada tar hissa garam hai. Duniya ka sab se bara garam registran Sahara isi me hai, aur duniya ka sab se lamba mashhoor darya Nile bhi yahin behta hai. Egypt, Nigeria, South Africa aur Kenya is ke mashhoor mulk hain. Africa ke mashriq me bara jazira Madagascar hai, jis ka zikr aage ayega. Aik zamane me Africa ko tareek continent kaha jata tha kyunke is ke andar ke ilaaqon ki maloomat duniya ko kam thin, ye purani baat bhi kabhi kabhi paper me aa jati hai.

North America, teesra bara continent

North America teesra bara continent hai, taqreeban 24.7 million square kilometre. Is ke teen sab se mashhoor mulk hain, Canada, America yani USA, aur Mexico. Canada raqbe me duniya ka doosra bara mulk isi continent me hai. Yahan ka mashhoor pahari silsila Rockies hai, aur mashhoor darya Mississippi hai, dono ka zikr aage tafseel se ayega. Is continent ke janub me aik tang si patti hai jahan Panama Canal bani hai, aur wahi patti North America ko South America se jorti hai. To yaad rakho, do America hain, shimali aur janubi, aur dono ke darmiyan rabta Panama ke ilaaqe se hai.

South America, chautha bara continent

South America chautha bara continent hai, taqreeban 17.8 million square kilometre. Is ki pehchan do cheezain hain. Aik Amazon darya aur us ka ghana jangal, jo duniya ka sab se bara barishi jangal hai, aur dusra Andes pahari silsila, jo duniya ka sab se lamba silsila hai. Brazil is continent ka sab se bara mulk hai aur duniya ke bare mulkon me ata hai. Argentina, Peru aur Chile bhi yahin hain. Is continent ki zyada tar abadi Portuguese aur Spanish bolti hai, Brazil me Portuguese aur baqi aksar mulkon me Spanish, ye chhoti si baat bhi GK me aa jati hai.

Antarctica, barf wala continent

Antarctica paanchwan bara continent hai, taqreeban 14.2 million square kilometre, aur ye sab se mukhtalif hai. Ye South Pole par waaqe hai aur saal bhar barf se dhaka rehta hai. Yahan koi mulk nahi, koi mustaqil abadi nahi, sirf mukhtalif mulkon ke research station hain jahan scientist rehte hain. Ye duniya ka sab se thanda, sab se khushk aur sab se tez hawaon wala continent hai. Mazay ki baat ye hai ke raqbe me ye Europe se bara hai lekin sawal hamesha is ki barf aur South Pole par banta hai. Penguin yahin ka mashhoor janwar hai.

Europe, chhota lekin mashhoor continent

Europe raqbe me chhata continent hai, taqreeban 10.2 million square kilometre. Ye Asia ke maghrib me hai aur asal me Asia se jura hua hai, is liye kuch log Asia aur Europe ko mila kar Eurasia bhi kehte hain. Europe chhota hai lekin tareekh aur siyasat me is ka naam bohat bara hai. UK, France, Germany, Italy aur Spain yahin ke mulk hain. Yahan ka mashhoor silsila Alps hai. Russia ka aik hissa Europe me hai aur dusra Asia me, ye do bar-e-azamon wala point aage mulkon wale hisse me phir ayega.

Australia, sab se chhota continent

Australia saatwan aur sab se chhota continent hai, taqreeban 7.7 million square kilometre. Iski khaas baat ye hai ke ye aik hi mulk ka continent hai, pura continent hi Australia mulk hai. Isay jazira continent bhi kehte hain kyunke chaaron taraf samandar hai. Kangaroo yahin ka duniya bhar me mashhoor janwar hai, aur Great Barrier Reef, duniya ki sab se bari moonga chattan, is ke mashriqi saahil ke qareeb hai. Australia ke aas paas ke jaziron aur New Zealand ko mila kar is pure ilaaqe ko Oceania bhi kaha jata hai, ye lafz paper me ata hai is liye pehchan lo.

Paanch oceans, bare se chhote tak

Ab pani ki taraf ao. Paanch oceans ki tartib bhi raqbe se hi yaad hogi. Pacific, Atlantic, Indian, Southern, Arctic. Teen bare to aam tor par sab ko yaad hote hain, asal phansane wale aakhri do hain.

Pacific Ocean, sab se bara aur sab se gehra

Pacific duniya ka sab se bara ocean hai, taqreeban 165 million square kilometre. Itna bara ke baqi tamam oceans ko mila lo to bhi ye un ke brabar ya un se bara nikle ga. Ye Asia aur Australia ke mashriq me aur dono America ke maghrib me phaila hai. Duniya ki sab se gehri jagah isi me hai, Mariana Trench, jo Philippines ke mashriq me waaqe hai aur us ki gehraai taqreeban 10,935 metre hai, aam tor par 11,000 metre ke qareeb kaha jata hai. Socho, Everest ko utha kar is khaai me daal do to us ke upar bhi pani reh jayega. Pacific ke kinaron par aatish fishan paharon aur zalzalon ki aik puri patti hai jisay Ring of Fire kehte hain, ye naam bhi paper me ata hai.

Atlantic Ocean, doosra bara

Atlantic doosra bara ocean hai, taqreeban 106 million square kilometre. Is ki pehchan is ke kinare hain. Aik taraf North aur South America hain, dusri taraf Europe aur Africa. Yani purani duniya aur nayi duniya ke darmiyan yahi samandar hai. Duniya ki masroof tareen tijarati guzargahen isi me hain, aur Suez aur Panama canals asal me Atlantic ko hi dusre oceans se jorne ke liye bani hain. Naqshay par Atlantic S ki shakal ka nazar ata hai, ye chhota sa ishara bhi yaad rakhne me madad deta hai.

Indian Ocean, Pakistan wala ocean

Indian Ocean teesra bara ocean hai, taqreeban 70 million square kilometre. Hamare liye ye sab se aham hai kyunke Pakistan isi ke kinare par hai. Pakistan ke janub me jo Arabian Sea hai, wo isi Indian Ocean ka hissa hai, aur Karachi aur Gwadar ki bandargahen isi par hain. Is ocean ki aik niri pehchan ye hai ke ye duniya ka wahid ocean hai jis ka naam kisi mulk ke naam par hai, India ke naam par Indian Ocean. Is ke kinare Asia, Africa aur Australia teenon ate hain, aur Hormuz aur Malacca jaisi mashhoor guzargahen isi se jurti hain, jin ka zikr aage ayega.

Southern Ocean, Antarctica ke gird

Southern Ocean chautha ocean hai, taqreeban 20 million square kilometre. Ye Antarctica continent ke chaaron taraf ghumta hai, is liye isay Antarctic Ocean bhi kehte hain. Ye wala ocean aksar students ki fehrist se reh jata hai, kyunke isay alag ocean ki haisiyat nisabatan nayi mili hai, saal 2000 ke aas paas isay baqaida alag ocean tasleem kiya gaya. Paper me paanch oceans ginane ka sawal aye to Southern ko bhoolna hi sab se aam ghalti hai, khaas khayal rakho.

Arctic Ocean, sab se chhota

Arctic Ocean paanchwan aur sab se chhota ocean hai, taqreeban 14 million square kilometre. Ye North Pole ke ird gird hai, yani duniya ki chhat par. Saal ka bara hissa is ki satah par barf jami rehti hai. Is ke kinare Russia, Canada aur Greenland ate hain. Yaad rakhne ka tareeqa seedha hai, janub me Southern Ocean South Pole ke gird hai aur shimal me Arctic Ocean North Pole ke gird. Dono ke naam hi un ki jagah bata dete hain.

Duniya ke mashhoor darya

Ab un features ki taraf ate hain jin par paper ka seedha sawal banta hai. Pehle chaar darya, phir registran, pahar, aur aakhir me guzargahen aur jheelain. Har feature ke aakhir me us ki jagah ka pin diya hai taake tum naqshay par wo jagah foran pehchan sako.

Nile, duniya ka sab se lamba darya

Nile ko duniya ka sab se lamba darya mana jata hai, is ki lambai taqreeban 6,650 kilometre hai. Ye Africa me behta hai aur is ki kahani do rangon se banti hai. White Nile mashriqi Africa ki Lake Victoria se ata hai, aur Blue Nile Ethiopia ke paharon se, aur dono Sudan ke darul hukumat Khartoum ke qareeb mil jate hain. Wahan se Nile shimal ko chalta hai, Egypt se guzarta hai, aur Cairo ke aage aik bare delta ki surat me Mediterranean Sea me ja girta hai. Egypt ki tehzeeb isi darya ki den hai, is liye kaha jata hai ke Egypt Nile ka tohfa hai. Aswan Dam isi par bana hai. Lambai wala sawal aye to jawab Nile hai, ye pakka kar lo.
Pin: 30.04, 31.24

Amazon, pani ke hisab se sab se bara darya

Amazon South America ka darya hai aur yahan aik bareek baat hai jo paper bohat phansata hai. Lambai me Amazon Nile se peeche samjha jata hai, is ki lambai taqreeban 6,400 kilometre hai, lekin behene wale pani ki miqdar me Amazon duniya ka sab se bara darya hai. Itna pani ke baad wale kai daryaaon ko mila kar bhi is ke brabar na ho. Ye Peru ke Andes paharon se nikalta hai, Brazil se guzarta hai, aur Atlantic Ocean me girta hai. Is ke ird gird duniya ka sab se bara barishi jangal hai jisay Amazon Rainforest kehte hain aur usay duniya ke phephre kaha jata hai. To do sawal alag alag yaad rakho, sab se lamba Nile, sab se zyada pani wala Amazon.
Pin: -3.12, -60.02

Yangtze, Asia ka sab se lamba darya

Yangtze China ka darya hai aur Asia ka sab se lamba darya hai, is ki lambai taqreeban 6,300 kilometre hai. Ye Tibet ke pahari ilaaqe se nikalta hai, pure China ko mashriq ki taraf paar karta hai, aur Shanghai ke qareeb East China Sea me girta hai. Duniya ka sab se bara bijli ghar, Three Gorges Dam, isi darya par bana hai. China ke dosre mashhoor darya Yellow River se isay confuse mat karna. Tartib yaad rakho, duniya me Nile pehla, Amazon doosra, Yangtze teesra, aur teeno alag alag continents me hain, Africa, South America aur Asia.
Pin: 31.23, 121.47

Mississippi, America ka mashhoor darya

Mississippi USA ka sab se mashhoor darya hai. Akela Mississippi taqreeban 3,730 kilometre lamba hai, lekin is ke madadgar Missouri ko mila kar Mississippi Missouri system taqreeban 6,275 kilometre ban jata hai, aur system wala figure bhi kitabon me ata hai. Ye America ke shimal se nikalta hai, pure mulk ko darmiyan se kaatta hua janub ko jata hai, aur New Orleans ke qareeb Gulf of Mexico me girta hai. America ki tareekh aur tijarat me is darya ka bara kirdar hai. Paper me sawal aam tor par is ke girne ki jagah ya Missouri wale system par banta hai.
Pin: 29.15, -89.25

Duniya ke mashhoor registran

Sahara, duniya ka sab se bara garam registran

Sahara Africa ke shimal me phaila duniya ka sab se bara garam registran hai. Is ka raqba taqreeban 9.2 million square kilometre hai, yani takreeban pura America jitna ilaaqa sirf ret hai. Ye koi aik mulk me nahi, Libya, Egypt, Algeria, Mali aur Niger samait kai mulkon me phaila hai. Yahan din ko sakht garmi aur raat ko thand hoti hai, aur barish taqreeban na honay ke brabar. Aik note zehen me rakho, duniya ka sab se bara registran majmooi tor par Antarctica hai lekin wo barf ka registran hai. Jab paper me sab se bara registran poocha jaye aur garam ka lafz na ho to bhi aam textbook jawab Sahara hi hota hai, kyunke sawal garam registran ke maanon me hi banta hai.
Pin: 23.00, 10.00

Gobi, thanda registran

Gobi Asia ka mashhoor registran hai, jo Mongolia aur China ke darmiyan phaila hai. Is ka raqba taqreeban 1.3 million square kilometre hai. Sahara se is ka farq ye hai ke Gobi garam registran nahi, ye thanda registran hai jahan sardion me barf bhi parti hai aur ret ke sath pathreeli zameen bhi bohat hai. Ye China ki mashhoor Great Wall ke shimal me waaqe hai, aur purane zamane me Silk Road ke qafilay is ke kinaron se guzarte the. To do registran do mausamon ke sath yaad rakho, Sahara garam, Gobi thanda.
Pin: 42.59, 103.43

Duniya ki mashhoor chotiyan aur silsile

Everest, duniya ki sab se unchi choti

Everest duniya ki sab se unchi choti hai, is ki unchai 8,849 metre hai. Purani kitabon me 8,848 metre likha milta hai, lekin nayi pemaish ke baad 8,849 hi durust figure hai, paper me dono me se jo option mile usi ko dekh kar chunna, aam tor par naya figure hi ata hai. Everest Nepal me hai, Himalaya silsile me, aur Nepal aur China ki sarhad par khari hai. Nepal me isay Sagarmatha aur Tibet me Chomolungma kehte hain. Aur aik baat jo Pakistani student ko khaas yaad rakhni hai, duniya ki doosri unchi choti K2 hai, 8,611 metre, aur wo Pakistan me hai. Uski puri tafseel, Karakoram silsila aur us ke aas paas ki chotiyan, tum Pakistan wale hisse me parh chuke ho, yahan sirf tartib pakki kar lo, pehle Everest phir K2.
Pin: 27.99, 86.93

Kilimanjaro, Africa ki sab se unchi choti

Kilimanjaro Africa ki sab se unchi choti hai, is ki unchai 5,895 metre hai. Ye Tanzania mulk me hai aur is ki do khaasiyat paper me ati hain. Aik ye ke ye kisi silsile ka hissa nahi, akeli khari aatish fishan choti hai, aur dusri ye ke equator ke bilkul qareeb honay ke bawajood is ki choti par saal bhar barf rehti hai. Garam ilaaqe me barf wali choti, yehi is ki pehchan hai.
Pin: -3.07, 37.35

Alps, Europe ka mashhoor silsila

Alps Europe ka sab se mashhoor pahari silsila hai, jo France, Switzerland, Italy aur Austria samait kai mulkon me phaila hai. Is ki sab se unchi choti Mont Blanc hai, 4,808 metre, jo France aur Italy ki sarhad par hai. Barf, skiing aur khoobsurat wadiyon ki wajah se ye silsila duniya bhar me mashhoor hai. Europe ke bare darya, Rhine aur Danube, ke sar chashmon ka taluq bhi isi ilaaqe se hai. Paper me Europe ka silsila poocha jaye to jawab Alps hai.
Pin: 46.56, 8.03

Andes, duniya ka sab se lamba silsila

Andes duniya ka sab se lamba pahari silsila hai, jo South America ke maghribi kinare ke sath sath shimal se janub tak phaila hai. Is ki lambai taqreeban 8,900 kilometre batayi jati hai, kuch kitabon me 7,000 kilometre ke qareeb bhi milta hai, lekin sab se lamba silsila honay par koi ikhtilaf nahi. Ye saat mulkon se guzarta hai, Venezuela se Chile tak. Is ki sab se unchi choti Aconcagua hai, 6,961 metre, jo Argentina me hai. Himalaya unchai me aage hai lekin lambai me Andes hi pehle number par hai, ye farq paper me bohat ata hai.
Pin: -20.00, -68.50

Rockies, North America ka silsila

Rockies North America ka bara pahari silsila hai, jo Canada se shuru ho kar USA ke maghribi hisse tak taqreeban 4,800 kilometre phaila hai. Is ka pura naam Rocky Mountains hai. Barf posh chotiyan, national parks aur jangli zindagi is ki pehchan hai. To teen silsile teen continents ke sath bandh lo, Asia me Himalaya, South America me Andes, North America me Rockies, aur Europe me Alps. Ye chaar naam hi paper ke sawalon ka khazana hain.
Pin: 44.43, -110.59

Mashhoor canals aur aabnayen, yani straits

Ab pani ke un raaston par ao jin par duniya ki tijarat chalti hai. Canal insaan ki banayi hui nehar hai aur strait qudrati tang patti hai, lekin kaam dono ka aik hai, samandaron ko jorna. In par sawal har saal ata hai ke ye kin do panion ko milati hai, bas wahi jor yaad karna hai.

Suez Canal, Mediterranean aur Red Sea ka rabta

Suez Canal Egypt me hai aur ye Mediterranean Sea ko Red Sea se milati hai. Ye 1869 me khuli aur is ki lambai taqreeban 193 kilometre hai. Is ke bannay se Europe se Asia ane wale jahazon ko pura Africa chakkar lagane se chutkara mil gaya, safar hazaron kilometre kam ho gaya. Is ke shimal me Port Said aur janub me Suez sheher hai. Aik aur baat, ye canal Africa aur Asia ko alag bhi karti hai, Egypt ka bara hissa Africa me hai aur Sinai ka hissa Asia me. Sawal hamesha yahi banta hai, Suez kin do samandaron ko milati hai, Mediterranean aur Red Sea.
Pin: 30.42, 32.34

Panama Canal, Atlantic aur Pacific ka rabta

Panama Canal Central America ke mulk Panama me hai aur ye Atlantic Ocean ko Pacific Ocean se milati hai. Ye 1914 me khuli aur is ki lambai taqreeban 82 kilometre hai. Is se pehle America ke aik kinare se dusre kinare jane wale jahaz ko pura South America ghumna parta tha. Suez aur Panama ko aksar mila kar poocha jata hai is liye jor alag alag pakke kar lo. Suez purani duniya me hai, Mediterranean aur Red Sea ke darmiyan. Panama nayi duniya me hai, Atlantic aur Pacific ke darmiyan.
Pin: 9.08, -79.68

Strait of Malacca, masroof tareen guzargah

Strait of Malacca Malaysia aur Indonesia ke jazire Sumatra ke darmiyan waaqe hai. Ye Indian Ocean ko Pacific Ocean, yani South China Sea, se milati hai. China, Japan aur mashriqi Asia ki tijarat ka bara hissa isi tang raaste se guzar kar Indian Ocean me ata hai, is liye isay duniya ki masroof tareen guzargahon me ginah jata hai. Singapore ki bandargah isi ke kinare honay ki wajah se duniya ki aham bandargahon me hai. Pakistan se mashriq ki taraf jane wale jahaz bhi yahin se guzarte hain.
Pin: 4.20, 100.10

Strait of Hormuz, teel ki guzargah

Strait of Hormuz Persian Gulf ko Gulf of Oman aur phir Arabian Sea se milati hai. Is ke aik taraf Iran hai aur dusri taraf Oman. Ye guzargah hamare liye khaas hai kyunke Pakistan ke Makran saahil ke bilkul qareeb hai, Gwadar yahan se door nahi. Is ki duniya bhar me ahmiyat teel ki wajah se hai. Khaliji mulkon se nikalne wale teel ka taqreeban paanchwan hissa duniya bhar ke hisaab se yahin se guzar kar jata hai, is liye akhbaron me is ka naam bar bar ata hai aur paper me bhi. Sawal banta hai ke Hormuz kis cheez ki wajah se mashhoor hai, jawab hai teel ki naqal o harkat.
Pin: 26.56, 56.27

Bering Strait, Asia aur America ke darmiyan

Bering Strait Asia aur North America ke darmiyan hai. Is ke aik taraf Russia ka Siberia hai aur dusri taraf America ki riyasat Alaska. Ye tang patti taqreeban 85 kilometre chauri hai aur Bering Sea ko Arctic Ocean se jorti hai. Sardi me yahan barf jam jati hai. Tareekh ki aik dilchasp baat ye hai ke purane zamane me jab samandar ki satah neechi thi to yahin se insaan paidal chal kar Asia se America pohuncha tha. Paper me sawal is ki jagah par banta hai, do bar-e-azamon ke darmiyan wali aabnaye.
Pin: 65.78, -168.99

Strait of Gibraltar, Europe aur Africa ke darmiyan

Strait of Gibraltar Mediterranean Sea ko Atlantic Ocean se milati hai. Is ke shimal me Spain yani Europe hai aur janub me Morocco yani Africa, aur tang tareen jagah par dono kinaron ke darmiyan sirf taqreeban 14 kilometre ka faasla hai. Purane zamane se ye Mediterranean ki duniya ka darwaza hai, jo jahaz Atlantic se Mediterranean me jaye ga wo yahin se guzre ga. To teen aabnayen teen joron ke sath yaad rakho, Gibraltar Europe Africa, Bering Asia America, Hormuz khalij aur khule samandar ke darmiyan.
Pin: 35.97, -5.49

Mashhoor jheelain aur neechi jagah

Caspian Sea, duniya ki sab se bari jheel

Naam ke aakhir me Sea hai lekin asal me Caspian duniya ki sab se bari jheel hai, yehi is ka sab se bara trap hai. Is ka raqba taqreeban 371,000 square kilometre hai aur is ka pani namkeen hai. Ye Europe aur Asia ki sarhad par waaqe hai aur is ke kinare paanch mulk hain, Russia, Kazakhstan, Turkmenistan, Iran aur Azerbaijan. Naam dekh kar isay ocean ya sea samajhna hi wo ghalti hai jo paper karwana chahta hai. Pakka kar lo, sab se bari jheel Caspian hai.
Pin: 42.30, 50.30

Lake Baikal, duniya ki sab se gehri jheel

Lake Baikal Russia ke ilaaqe Siberia me hai aur ye duniya ki sab se gehri jheel hai, is ki gehraai taqreeban 1,642 metre hai. Ye meethay pani ki jheel hai aur duniya ke meethay pani ka sab se bara zakhira isi me samjha jata hai, itna pani ke kai bare daryaaon ke brabar. Ye duniya ki qadeem tareen jheelon me bhi ginah jati hai. To jheelon ke do sawal alag alag bandh lo, sab se bari Caspian lekin namkeen, sab se gehri Baikal aur meethi.
Pin: 53.79, 108.16

Dead Sea, duniya ki sab se neechi jagah

Dead Sea Jordan aur Israel ke darmiyan waaqe hai aur is ke kinare ki zameen duniya ki sab se neechi jagah hai, samandar ki satah se taqreeban 430 metre neeche. Asal me ye bhi aik jheel hai, sea sirf naam ka hai, bilkul Caspian ki tarah. Is ke pani me namak itna zyada hai ke is me zinda cheezain taqreeban nahi miltin, isiliye isay Dead Sea kaha gaya, aur namak ki zyadti ki wajah se insaan is me dobta nahi, satah par tairta rehta hai. Ye dono baatein, sab se neechi jagah aur na dobne wala pani, paper ke sawal hain.
Pin: 31.50, 35.55

Mashhoor jazire

Greenland, duniya ka sab se bara jazira

Greenland duniya ka sab se bara jazira hai, is ka raqba taqreeban 2.16 million square kilometre hai. Ye North America ke shimal mashriq me hai lekin siyasi tor par Denmark ke sath jura hai. Is ka zyada tar hissa saal bhar barf ki moti teh se dhaka rehta hai, is liye is ki abadi bohat kam hai. Naam ke aakhir me green hai lekin rang safed hai, ye chhoti si baat bhi sawal ban jati hai. Sab se bara jazira poocha jaye to jawab Greenland hai, Australia nahi, kyunke Australia ko continent ginah jata hai, jazira nahi.
Pin: 71.71, -42.60

Madagascar, Africa ke qareeb bara jazira

Madagascar duniya ka chautha sab se bara jazira hai, is ka raqba taqreeban 587,000 square kilometre hai. Ye Africa ke mashriqi saahil ke samne Indian Ocean me waaqe hai aur khud aik alag mulk hai. Is ki pehchan yahan ke ajeeb janwar hain, khaas tor par lemur, jo duniya me kahin aur nahi milte. Pehle teen bare jazire bhi tartib se sun lo, Greenland pehla, New Guinea doosra, Borneo teesra, phir Madagascar chautha.
Pin: -19.00, 47.00

Indonesia, sab se bara archipelago mulk

Indonesia Asia ka wo mulk hai jo hazaron jaziron par phaila hai, is ke jaziron ki tadaad 17 hazar se zyada batayi jati hai, aur yehi duniya ka sab se bara archipelago, yani jaziron ke majmooe wala mulk hai. Ye equator par waaqe hai, is ke bare jazire Sumatra, Java, Borneo aur Sulawesi hain. Abadi ke hisab se ye duniya ke bare mulkon me hai aur sab se zyada Muslim abadi wala mulk bhi yahi hai, ye doosri baat Pak Studies aur GK dono me ati hai. Is ka darul hukumat Jakarta hai, jis ka zikr mulkon wale hisse me ayega.
Pin: -2.50, 118.00

Aakhir me puri duniya aik nazar me

Ab is pure hisse ko chand lineon me bandh lo. Continents ki tartib, Asia, Africa, North America, South America, Antarctica, Europe, Australia. Oceans ki tartib, Pacific, Atlantic, Indian, Southern, Arctic. Sab se lamba darya Nile, sab se zyada pani wala Amazon, Asia ka sab se lamba Yangtze. Sab se unchi choti Everest Nepal me, doosri K2 Pakistan me. Sab se lamba silsila Andes, sab se bara garam registran Sahara. Suez Mediterranean ko Red Sea se, Panama Atlantic ko Pacific se milati hai. Sab se bari jheel Caspian, sab se gehri Baikal, sab se neechi jagah Dead Sea, sab se bara jazira Greenland. Agar ye chand linein tumhe naqsha dekh kar zehen me ane lag jayen to samajh lo duniya ke GK ka khazana tumhare hath lag gaya, aur is topic ke number kahin nahi jayenge.
