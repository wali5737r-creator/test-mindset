Naqshay ki Istilahaat — Pani aur Zameen ki Shaklain
Har shakal ka asaan matlab, naqshay par pehchan, duniya aur Pakistan ki misaal, aur paper wali baat

Ye topic kyun zaroori hai

Dekho, duniya ke naqshay par pani aur zameen ne ajeeb ajeeb shaklain bana rakhi hain. Kahin pani zameen ke andar ghusa hua hai, kahin zameen pani me nok banaye khari hai, kahin do samandar aik tang gali se juray hain. One paper ka sawal inhi shaklon par banta hai. Sawal poochta hai ke khaleej kise kehte hain, aabnaay aur channel me kya farq hai, falana jagah peninsula hai ya cape. Student ko jagah ka naam yaad hota hai lekin shakal ki pehchan nahi hoti, aur wahi number kat jata hai.

Is liye har shakal ko chaar hisso me parho ge. Pehle asaan alfaaz me matlab, jese ustad class me samjhata hai. Phir ye ke naqshay par us shakal ko dekh kar kesay pehchano ge, kyunke paper me pehchan hi asal imtihan hai. Phir duniya ki aik misaal aur jahan mumkin ho Pakistan ki misaal, taake shakal hawa me na rahe. Aur aakhir me wo baat jo paper me sawal banti hai. Bas yehi tartib zehen me bitha lo, phir poora naqsha tum se baatein karega.

Gulf (Khaleej)

Matlab ye hai. Samandar ka wo bara hissa jo zameen ke andar door tak ghusa hua ho aur jis ka munh baqi samandar ki nisbat tang ho, use gulf yani khaleej kehte hain. Asaan tasveer ye hai ke jese pani ne zameen ke andar aik bari jeb bana li ho, jeb andar se chauri aur us ka munh tang.

Naqshay par pehchan ye hai. Pani ki aik bari aur gehri jeb zameen ke andar ghusi hui nazar ayegi. Teen taraf zameen hogi aur sirf aik taraf tang sa rasta khule samandar ki taraf khulega. Kinare andar ki taraf narm ghulay hue honge, nokile nahi honge.

Duniya ki misaal ye hai. Persian Gulf, jisay Khaleej-e-Faris kehte hain, Iran aur Arabia ke darmiyan zameen me ghusi hui hai. Gulf of Mexico America ke janub me hai. Pakistan ki misaal ye hai ke Gwadar ke qareeb bhi chhoti khaleej numa shaklain banti hain jinhe wahan khor kaha jata hai, lekin asal bari khaleej hamare pados me Persian Gulf hi hai.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Khaleejain aksar teel aur tijarat ke sath jor kar ati hain, khaas taur par Persian Gulf. Aur ye bhi yaad rakho ke khaleej ka munh tang hota hai, yehi us ki sab se wazeh pehchan hai.

Bay (Khaari)

Matlab ye hai. Bay bhi samandar ka zameen ke andar ghusa hua hissa hai, lekin gulf se chhota hota hai aur us ka munh khula hota hai. Asaan alfaaz me ye sahil ke andar bana hua aik khula sa kataav hai, jese kinare ne halka sa andar ki taraf jhukao liya ho.

Naqshay par pehchan ye hai. Sahil me aik gol sa kataav nazar ayega, lekin ye kataav zameen ke andar zyada door tak nahi gaya hoga aur us ka darwaza chaura hoga. Zameen use poori tarah gher nahi rahi hogi, bas aik taraf se halka sa ghera hoga. Gulf wali jeb yahan nahi banti.

Duniya ki misaal ye hai. Bay of Bengal Bharat ke mashriq me hai aur ye duniya ki sab se bari bay hai, itni bari ke bohat se seas is se chhote hain. Pakistan ki misaal Karachi ke maghrib me Sonmiani Bay hai, jo aik khuli si khaari hai.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Bay of Bengal ko duniya ki sab se bari bay ke tor par pakka kar lo. Aur ye zehen me rakho ke naamon ka faisla hamesha size se nahi hota, tareekhi wajah bhi hoti hai, is liye paper me jo naam mashhoor hai wahi jawab hota hai.

Strait (Aabnaay)

Matlab ye hai. Do bare paniyon ko milane wali tang pani ki patti, jis ke dono taraf zameen ho, use strait yani aabnaay kehte hain. Aabnaay asal me do samandaron ke darmiyan wala tang darwaza hai, aur darwaze ki tarah hi is ki ahmiyat hoti hai.

Naqshay par pehchan ye hai. Do bare neelay hisse aik tang si gali se juray hue nazar ayenge, aur gali ke dono taraf zameen hogi. Tasveer yun banao ke do bare kamron ke darmiyan aik tang darwaza hai, jo guzre ga yahin se guzre ga.

Duniya ki misaal ye hai. Strait of Hormuz Persian Gulf ko Gulf of Oman aur phir khule Arabian Sea se milati hai. Strait of Gibraltar Mediterranean Sea ko Atlantic Ocean se milati hai. Pakistan se is ka taluq yun hai ke hamari bandargahon se nikalne wala har jahaz mashriq ya maghrib jate hue kisi na kisi aabnaay se lazmi guzarta hai.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Aabnaay hamesha jora bana kar ati hai. Konsi aabnaay kin do paniyon ko milati hai, aur kin mulkon ke darmiyan hai. Ye jore pakke kar lo, kyunke seedha sawal yahi banta hai.

Channel

Matlab ye hai. Channel bhi do paniyon ko milane wala pani ka rasta hai, lekin strait se zyada khula, chaura aur lamba hota hai. Kaam wahi darwaze wala hai, farq bas tangi ka hai. Strait tang gali hai, channel chaura rasta.

Naqshay par pehchan ye hai. Do zameenon ke darmiyan pani ki chauri si patti door tak jati nazar ayegi. Ye tang gali nahi lagegi, balke khula beh raha pani lagega jis me jahaz asani se dono simton me chal sakte hain.

Duniya ki misaal ye hai. English Channel England aur France ke darmiyan hai aur Atlantic ko North Sea se milati hai. Mozambique Channel Africa aur Madagascar jazire ke darmiyan hai. Pakistan ke sahil ke sath koi mashhoor channel nahi, lekin Karachi ke qareeb Manora Channel bandargah ke andar jahazon ka rasta hai.

Paper wali baat ye hai. English Channel ko us ke neeche bani Channel Tunnel ke sath yaad rakho jo England ko France se milati hai. Aur farq wala sawal aye to jawab yehi hai, channel strait se chaura hota hai.

Peninsula (Jazira-numa)

Matlab ye hai. Zameen ka wo hissa jis ke teen taraf pani ho aur sirf aik taraf wo bari zameen se juda ho, use peninsula yani jazira-numa kehte hain. Asaan lafzon me ye aadha jazira hai, poori tarah kata hua nahi, peeche se zameen ke sath jurra hua hai.

Naqshay par pehchan ye hai. Zameen ki aik bari nok ya zabaan pani ki taraf nikli hui nazar ayegi. Teen taraf neela pani hoga aur peeche ki taraf zameen ka bara hissa. Jazire se farq ye hai ke jazira chaaron taraf se kata hota hai, peninsula aik taraf se jurra hota hai.

Duniya ki misaal ye hai. Arabia duniya ka sab se bara peninsula hai, is ke gird Persian Gulf, Red Sea aur Arabian Sea hain. Bharat bhi aik bara peninsula hai. Pakistan ki misaal bohat khoobsurat hai, Gwadar aik hammer yani hathori jaisi shakal wale peninsula par basta hai jis ke gird gehra pani hai, aur yehi shakal usay bari bandargah ke liye behtareen banati hai.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Arabia ko duniya ka sab se bara peninsula pakka kar lo. Korea aur Florida bhi mashhoor peninsula hain, aur Bharat ko bhi aksar peninsula mulk kaha jata hai.

Isthmus

Matlab ye hai. Do bari zameenon ko milane wali tang zameeni patti, jis ke dono taraf pani ho, use isthmus kehte hain. Ye aabnaay ka bilkul ulta hai. Wahan pani ki tang patti zameenon ko alag karti thi, yahan zameen ki tang patti do zameenon ko milati hai.

Naqshay par pehchan ye hai. Do bare zameeni hisse aik tang si gardan se juray hue nazar ayenge, aur gardan ke dono taraf pani hoga. Jese do bare kamre aik tang raahdari se juray hon.

Duniya ki misaal ye hai. Panama ka isthmus North America aur South America ko milata hai, aur Panama Canal isi tang jagah ko kaat kar bani hai. Egypt me Suez wala isthmus Africa aur Asia ko milata hai, aur Suez Canal bhi yahin kati hai. Pakistan me aisi koi mashhoor isthmus nahi, hamari zameen wasee hai.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Jahan isthmus hoga wahan aksar canal hogi, kyunke tang zameen ko kaatna sab se asaan hota hai aur do samandaron ka faasla sab se kam ho jata hai. Panama aur Suez ka jora isthmus ke sath hi yaad rakho.

Cape (Ras)

Matlab ye hai. Zameen ki wo nokili nok jo samandar ke andar ghusi hui ho, use cape kehte hain, Urdu aur Arabi me isay ras kehte hain. Ras zameen ka pani ki taraf barha hua kona hai, peninsula se bohat chhota.

Naqshay par pehchan ye hai. Sahil ki lakeer se aik nokili nok pani me nikli hui nazar ayegi. Ye peninsula wali bari zabaan nahi hogi, bas aik tez kona hoga jahan sahil achanak simt badalta hai.

Duniya ki misaal ye hai. Cape of Good Hope Africa ke janubi konay par hai, 1498 me Vasco da Gama yehi rasta pakar kar Bharat pohuncha tha. Pakistan ki misaal Karachi ke qareeb Ras Muari hai jisay Cape Monze bhi kehte hain, aur Makran ke sahil par Ras Jaddi bhi mashhoor hai.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Cape of Good Hope tareekhi samandari raste ki wajah se ata hai. Aur Cape Horn South America ke janubi siray par hai, jahan do oceans milte hain aur mausam sakht rehta hai.

Delta

Matlab ye hai. Jahan darya samandar me girne se pehle sust ho jata hai, apne sath layi hui mitti aur ret wahan bicha deta hai aur kai chhoti shakho me bant kar samandar me milta hai, us zameen ko delta kehte hain. Darya yahan aik nahi rehta, phail jata hai.

Naqshay par pehchan ye hai. Darya ke munh par aik takon yani triangle ya pankhe jaisi shakal nazar ayegi, aur us ke andar chhoti chhoti lakeerain shakho ki surat me phaili hongi. Is ka naam hi Greek harf Delta ki takon wali shakal se para hai.

Duniya ki misaal ye hai. Nile Delta Egypt me hai jahan Nile Mediterranean me girta hai, aur Ganga ka delta Bangladesh me duniya ka sab se bara delta hai. Pakistan ki misaal Indus Delta hai jo Thatta aur Keti Bandar ke qareeb Arabian Sea me banta hai.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Delta hamesha darya ke aakhri siray par hota hai aur apni zarkhez mitti ki wajah se mashhoor hota hai. Sawal ata hai ke falana darya ka delta kahan hai, jawab us ke samandar wale siray par hoga.

Estuary

Matlab ye hai. Estuary delta ki ulti shakal hai. Yahan darya shakho me nahi bantta, balke us ka munh aik chauri keertay yani funnel ki surat me khula rehta hai, jahan samandar ka jowar bhata andar tak ata hai aur mitti ko jama nahi honay deta. Pani yahan darya aur samandar ka mila jula hota hai.

Naqshay par pehchan ye hai. Darya ka munh andar ki taraf tang aur samandar ki taraf jate hue chaura phaila hua nazar ayega. Pankha nahi hoga, shakhein nahi hongi, bas aik khuli hui keerta numa khaari hogi.

Duniya ki misaal ye hai. England ki Thames aur America ki Hudson ke munh mashhoor estuary hain, aur in par bari bandargahen bani hain kyunke gehra pani andar tak ajata hai. Pakistan ki misaal se farq samjho, Indus ne estuary nahi banaya, delta banaya hai.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Paper delta aur estuary ko aamne saamne rakh kar phansata hai. Darya bant kar phailay to delta, munh keertay ki tarah khula ho aur jowar andar aye to estuary. Bas ye aik line ka farq pakka kar lo.

Lagoon

Matlab ye hai. Sahil ke sath wo uthla yani kam gehra thehra hua pani jo ret ki patti, patharon ya coral ki deewar ki wajah se khule samandar se alag ho gaya ho, use lagoon kehte hain. Ye samandar se bilkul kata nahi hota, kisi tang raste se us se jurra rehta hai.

Naqshay par pehchan ye hai. Sahil ke bilkul sath aik lambi patli jheel si nazar ayegi, lekin gaur karo to wo samandar se aik patli zameeni ya ret ki patti se alag hogi aur kahin se us me samandar ka pani araha hoga.

Duniya ki misaal ye hai. Maldives ke gole jaziron, jinhe atoll kehte hain, ke darmiyan lagoon bante hain aur un ka pani saaf aur uthla hota hai. Pakistan ki misaal Balochistan ke sahil par Miani Hor hai, jo Sonmiani ke qareeb aik mashhoor lagoon hai aur wahan mangrove ke jangal hain. Kalmat Hor bhi isi tarah ki lagoon hai.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Lagoon ko jheel se alag pehchano. Jheel chaaron taraf zameen se band hoti hai, lagoon samandar ke sath jurri hoti hai aur us ke aur samandar ke darmiyan bas aik patli patti hoti hai.

Ocean aur Sea ka farq

Ab wo farq jo paper ka sab se pasandeeda sawal hai, ocean aur sea ka farq. Ise alag se aur tafseel se samjho, kyunke aadhe students yahin ulajhte hain.

Ocean duniya ke pani ke wo azeem zakhair hain jo continents ke darmiyan phailay hain. Kul paanch ocean hain, Pacific, Atlantic, Indian, Southern aur Arctic. Zameen ki satah ka takreeban ikhattar feesad hissa pani hai aur us ka sab se bara hissa yahi oceans hain. Ocean ki pehchan teen hai. Ye bohat wasee hota hai, ye bohat gehra hota hai, aur zameen use band nahi karti, balke continents us ke kinaron par khade hote hain. Ocean khula hota hai, us ka apna koi band kinara nahi.

Sea ocean ke muqable me chhota hota hai. Ye asal me kisi ocean ka hi wo hissa hai jo zameen ke kinare ki taraf phasa hua ho ya zameen ke andar ghusa hua ho. Sea aksar teen taraf se ya zyada taraf se zameen se ghira hota hai, ocean se kam gehra hota hai, aur us ka pani aur mausam qareebi zameen se zyada mutaassir hota hai. Sab se aham baat ye hai ke har mashhoor sea asal me kisi na kisi ocean ka hissa hai. Arabian Sea Indian Ocean ka hissa hai. Mediterranean Sea Strait of Gibraltar ke zariye Atlantic Ocean se jurta hai. Caribbean Sea bhi Atlantic ka hissa hai.

Naqshay par pehchan ye hai. Ocean khula aur wasee neela phaila hoga jis ka koi band kinara nazar nahi ayega. Sea zameen ke andar ghusa hua ya kinare se laga hua nisbatan chhota neela hissa hoga jis ke gird zameen ka ghera saaf dikhega.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Farq teen nuqton me band kar lo. Size me ocean bara, gehrai me ocean gehra, aur sea zameen se ghira hota hai jabke ocean nahi. Aur tadaad bhi yaad rakho, ocean kul paanch hain, seas darjanon hain. Ye chaar baatein hi sawal ke chaar options banti hain.

Sea aur Lake ka farq, Caspian wala jaal

Ab is se bhi bare jaal ki baat. Kuch paniyon ke naam ke sath Sea laga hua hai lekin wo asal me sea hain hi nahi, wo jheelein hain. Pehle farq samajh lo. Sea asal me samandar ka hissa hoti hai aur kisi na kisi surat ocean se jurti hai, chahe tang aabnaay se hi sahi. Lake chaaron taraf zameen se ghiri hoti hai aur ocean se us ka koi rabta nahi hota, us me darya girte hain lekin wo kahin bahar nahi nikalti ya nikalti bhi hai to kisi darya ki surat me.

Duniya ki misaal ye hai. Caspian Sea Russia, Iran aur darmiyani Asia ke mulkon ke darmiyan hai, lekin ye asal me duniya ki sab se bari jheel hai, kyunke ye har taraf zameen se ghiri hai aur kisi ocean se nahi milti. Isi tarah Dead Sea aur Aral Sea bhi naam ke sea hain, asal me jheelein hain.

Naqshay par pehchan ye hai. Pani ko dekho, agar wo har taraf zameen se band hai aur us se bahar ocean ki taraf jane ka koi rasta nahi, to wo lake hai, chahe naqshay par us ke sath Sea hi kyun na likha ho.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Caspian ko duniya ki sab se bari lake ke tor par pakka kar lo. Sawal seedha ata hai ke Caspian Sea asal me kya hai, aur sahi jawab lake hai. Naam par mat jao, shakal aur rabte par jao.

Island aur Archipelago

Matlab ye hai. Island yani jazira chaaron taraf pani se ghiri hui zameen hai. Aur jab bohat se jazire aik hi ilaqay me jhund ki surat me phailay hon to us pure jhund ko archipelago kehte hain. Aik jazira akela hota hai, archipelago jaziron ka majmua hai.

Naqshay par pehchan ye hai. Akela jazira pani ke darmiyan aik alag zameeni nuqta sa nazar ayega. Archipelago me chhoti chhoti zameenain aik silsile ya jhund ki surat me door tak phaili hongi, jese pani par nishan paray hon.

Duniya ki misaal ye hai. Indonesia duniya ka sab se bara archipelago mulk hai jo hazaron jaziron par phaila hai, aur Philippines bhi mashhoor archipelago hai. Maldives chhote jaziron ka archipelago hai. Pakistan ka sab se bara jazira Astola hai aur Karachi ke qareeb Bundal aur Manora ke jazire hain.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Indonesia ko sab se bara archipelago mulk pakka kar lo. Aur ye bhi ke jazira chaaron taraf pani se ghira hota hai, teen taraf pani ho to wo peninsula hai, jazira nahi. Ye farq pehle bhi aya tha, yahan pakka ho gaya.

Coast (Sahil)

Matlab ye hai. Wo kinara jahan zameen khatam aur samandar shuru hota hai, use coast yani sahil kehte hain. Sahil par hi bandargahen banti hain, machhli pakri jati hai, aur lagoon, cape aur bay jaisi tamam shaklain bhi sahil par hi banti hain jin ka zikr is file me aya hai.

Naqshay par pehchan ye hai. Sahil wo lakeer hai jahan zameen ka rang khatam aur pani ka neela rang shuru hota hai. Jitni lambi ye lakeer kisi mulk ke hisse me aye, utna hi lamba us ka sahil ginna jata hai.

Duniya ki misaal ye hai. Duniya ka sab se lamba sahil Canada ka hai, kyunke us ke shumal me hazaron jazire hain aur har jazire ka apna sahil hai. Pakistan ki misaal pakki hai, hamara sahil takreeban 1,046 kilometre lamba hai jo sirf do sobon, Sindh aur Balochistan, ke sath lagta hai.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Pakistan ke sahil ki lambai 1,046 kilometre aur ye ke Punjab aur Khyber Pakhtunkhwa ka koi sahil nahi, ye do baatein paper me dohrayi jati hain. Aur Canada ko sab se lambe sahil ke sath yaad rakho.

Reef

Matlab ye hai. Pani ke andar chattanon ya coral ki bani hui wo lambi patti ya deewar jo satah ke qareeb hoti hai, use reef kehte hain. Coral reef zinda mojoodon ki kaloni se banti hai, chhote chhote coral janwar saalon me aik dusre ke upar ghar banate rehte hain. Reef sirf garam, saaf aur uthle pani me banti hai.

Naqshay par pehchan ye hai. Sahil ya jazire ke gird aik halki si lakeer ya kinara nazar ata hai, aur aksar usi reef ke andar ki taraf lagoon banta hai. Bare naqshon par mashhoor reefs ke naam likhe hote hain.

Duniya ki misaal ye hai. Australia ki Great Barrier Reef duniya ki sab se bari coral reef hai, takreeban 2,300 kilometre lambi, aur zinda mojoodon ki banayi hui duniya ki sab se bari cheez kahi jati hai. Pakistan me Churna Island aur Astola ke gird chhoti coral reefs payi jati hain.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Great Barrier Reef ko Australia aur sab se bari reef ke jore ke sath pakka kar lo. Aur ye bhi ke reef jahazon ke liye khatarnaak hoti hai kyunke wo satah ke bilkul qareeb chhupi hoti hai.

Ocean Trench

Matlab ye hai. Samandar ki teh me bani hui bohat gehri aur lambi khaai ko ocean trench kehte hain. Ye wahan banti hai jahan samandar ke farsh ki aik bari plate dusri plate ke neeche dhasti hai. Ye khaaiyan samandar ki sab se gehri jagahen hain, zameen ke sab se neeley nuqte.

Naqshay par pehchan ye hai. Ye aksar jaziron ki qataar ke sath sath chalti hui aik gehri lakeer ki surat me dikhti hai, aur bare naqshon par is ka naam wazeh likha hota hai. Aam naqshay par gehrai rang se pata chalti hai, jitna gehra neela utni gehrai.

Duniya ki misaal ye hai. Mariana Trench Pacific Ocean me hai aur is ka sab se gehra nuqta Challenger Deep hai jo takreeban gyarah kilometre gehra hai. Is ka andaza yun lagao ke duniya ki sab se unchi choti Mount Everest ko is me dala jaye to us ki choti bhi pani ke neeche rahe gi. Pakistan ke qareeb koi mashhoor trench nahi, hamara samandar nisbatan uthla hai.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Mariana ko sab se gehri trench aur Pacific Ocean ke sath pakka kar lo. Sab se gehra nuqta wala sawal bar bar ata hai aur us ka jawab hamesha yehi hota hai.

Ocean Current

Matlab ye hai. Samandar ke pani ka aik tay shuda simt me musalsal behne wala wasee bahao ocean current kehlata hai, jese samandar ke andar behta hua aik bara darya. Kuch currents garam ilaaqon ka pani le jati hain, unhe garam current kehte hain, aur kuch thande ilaaqon ka pani lati hain, wo thandi current hoti hain. In ka asar kinare ke mulkon ke mausam par parta hai.

Duniya ki do mashhoor misaalein hi paper ke liye kaafi hain. Gulf Stream garam current hai. Ye Gulf of Mexico ke garam pani se nikal kar Atlantic paar karti hai aur Europe ke maghribi sahilon tak garmi pohanchati hai, yehi wajah hai ke England aur Norway itne shumal me honay ke bawajood sakht barfeelay nahi hote. Labrador Current thandi current hai jo Canada ke mashriqi sahel ke sath thanda pani laati hai. Jahan ye garam Gulf Stream se takrati hai wahan sakht dhund banti hai aur machhliyon ki mashhoor jagah Grand Banks hai.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Garam aur thandi current ka jora bana kar ata hai. Gulf Stream garam, Labrador thandi. Bas ye jora aur ye baat ke currents kinare ke mausam badal deti hain, itna pakka kar lo.

Tide (Jowar Bhata)

Matlab ye hai. Samandar ke pani ka din me do dafa upar charhna aur do dafa utarna jowar bhata kehlata hai. Charha hua pani jowar hai aur utra hua pani bhata. Is ki sab se bari wajah Chaand ki kashish yani gravity hai. Suraj ka asar bhi hota hai lekin kam, kyunke wo bohat door hai.

Naqshay par ye shakal ki tarah nahi dikhta, ye waqt ke sath badalne wala amal hai. Is ki pehchan ye hai ke bandargahon par jahazon ki amad aur raft jowar ke auqaat dekh kar tay hoti hai. Karachi ki bandargah par bhi roz jowar bhata hota hai aur jahaz aksar unche pani ke waqt andar ate hain.

Duniya ki misaal ye hai. Canada ki Bay of Fundy me duniya ka sab se uncha jowar ata hai, pani takreeban solah metre tak upar charh jata hai. Pakistan me itna uncha jowar nahi hota, lekin Karachi aur Gwadar dono par ye amal roz hota hai.

Paper wali baat ye hai. Wajah wala sawal ata hai. Jowar bhate ki sab se bari wajah Chaand ki kashish hai. Chaand chhota hai lekin qareeb hai, Suraj bara hai lekin door hai, is liye jeet Chaand ki hoti hai. Ye wajah wala jumla pakka kar lo.

Aakhri baat

Ab naqshay par koi shakal dekho to pehle us ka naam apne zehen me lo. Pani zameen ke andar ghusa hai to khaleej ya khaari, pani ki tang gali hai to aabnaay, zameen pani me nikli hai to peninsula ya ras. Naam sahi ajaye to sawal aadha wahi hal ho jata hai. Agli file me yehi shaklain asal naam wali jagahon ke sath milengi, duniya ki mashhoor khaleejain, aabnayen aur samandar, har aik apni kahani ke sath.
